## Targets

| Letter | Intended pronunciation | Review focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eta | connected `AY-tuh` | Long A as in *day*, never EYE; smooth two-syllable word with no large pause. |
| Mu | `MYOO`, like *mew* | Clear initial M; one short syllable. |
| Nu | `NOO`, like *new* | Short, plain, natural delivery; no drawn-out vowel. |
| Xi | `ZYE`, rhyming with *eye* | One syllable; current American-English default remains revisable. |
| Upsilon | `OOP-sih-lon` | Stress the first syllable and keep all three syllables intelligible. |
| Phi | `FYE`, rhyming with *pie* | One syllable; do not say *fee*. |
| Chi | `KYE`, rhyming with *sky* | One syllable with a clear K sound. |
| Psi | `SYE`, like *sigh* | One syllable with silent P. |
