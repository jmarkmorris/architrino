# Cosmology Claim Map

## Purpose

This page maps cosmology concepts to the observable domains they are meant to describe, while development remains theory-first.

## Observable Domains

- Expansion history domain
- CMB structure domain
- Early-universe chemistry domain
- Structure-growth domain
- Cluster/merger phenomenology domain

## Cross-Domain Bridges to Keep Explicit

- Neutrino/sea coupling bridge:
  keep $N_{\text{eff}}$ language tied to medium-coupled neutrino-sector dynamics in the same parameter ledger as CMB and BBN quantities.
- Spectroscopy/redshift bridge:
  connect cosmological redshift interpretation to local spectral behavior through medium-state dependence of atomic transition scales.
- Background/growth bridge:
  keep $H_0$ and $S_8$ under one relaxation-and-coupling narrative rather than parallel disconnected narratives.
