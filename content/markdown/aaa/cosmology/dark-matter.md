# Dark Matter

## Core Question

What does dark-matter phenomenology correspond to in Architrino assembly ontology?

## Working Interpretation Space

- Neutral assembly populations with weak ordinary coupling
- Medium-response effects that alter effective gravitational behavior
- Hybrid descriptions combining both

## Conceptual Requirements

A coherent theory-level account should explain, in one framework:

- galaxy-scale behavior,
- cluster-scale behavior,
- large-scale structure role,
- compatibility with early-universe medium history.

## Theory Priorities

- Clarify whether dark matter is primarily a sector of assemblies, a medium-response regime, or a principled hybrid.
- Define a minimal ontology with no duplicate mechanisms.
- Keep language consistent with structure-formation and CMB interpretations.
