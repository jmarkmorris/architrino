# Structure Formation

## Scope

This document defines large-scale structure growth requirements for Architrino cosmology.

## Core Observables

- Expansion history $H(z)$
- Growth proxy $f\sigma_8(z)$
- Lensing-derived $S_8$
- Cluster-scale mass distribution consistency

## Baseline Dynamics

Use effective perturbation growth as comparison surface:

$$
\ddot\delta + 2H\dot\delta - 4\pi G_{\text{eff}}\rho_m\delta = 0,
$$

with Architrino modifications represented in $H(z)$, $G_{\text{eff}}$, and medium-coupling terms.

## Architrino Interpretation

- Growth suppression/enhancement can arise from medium-response effects.
- Dark-sector behavior may be modeled as weakly coupled neutral assembly populations.
- Scale dependence is expected to be diagnostic, not nuisance.

## Required Tests

- Fit low-redshift growth data without breaking CMB-era constraints.
- Reconcile $S_8$ behavior with lensing and clustering simultaneously.
- Validate cluster/lensing consistency (including merger-separation phenomenology).

## Falsifiers

- Growth fits require parameter choices incompatible with expansion data.
- Claimed $S_8$ relief fails under joint lensing+RSD constraints.
- Cluster-scale lensing signatures contradict model assumptions.

## Immediate Work

1. Build a two-sector growth toy model (baryonic + neutral assemblies).
2. Evaluate redshift and scale dependence of suppression signatures.
3. Produce a single consistency panel for $H(z)$, $f\sigma_8(z)$, and $S_8$.
