# Quantum Summary

This page is the entry hub for the quantum branch of $\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}$. The standard quantum formalism remains the observer-level comparison target for spectra, interference, statistics, and measurement frequencies. The proposed mapping treats the formal quantum state as an effective summary of assembly dynamics and causal wakes, including phase information, basin weights, and measurement channels. Recovering that summary and its predictions from the substrate dynamics remains open.

Read this hub as a map of what must be recovered. An assembly is an organized configuration of [architrinos](../foundations/architrino.md), the polarity-bearing point entities of the theory; their wakes are the expanding causal structures fixed by their earlier paths. On an admissible history where the [Master Equation](../dynamics/master-equation.md) is well posed, received wake contributions determine each architrino's acceleration. A detector is itself an assembly network. A measurement channel specifies its preparation, coupling, retained information, observation window, and record criterion. A basin groups initial histories that lead to the same outcome under that channel; a basin measure assigns weights to those histories. The physical derivation must explain both those weights and the phase-sensitive response across different channels: one outcome histogram does not determine a quantum state.

## Open Quantum Obligations

- **Finite measurement time is open:** [Measurement Ontology](./measurement-ontology.md) defines elapsed apparatus-clock times $\tau_{\mathrm{meas}}$ for crossing an outcome boundary, $\tau_{\mathrm{split}}$ for the onset of independently recordable alternatives, and $\tau_{\mathrm{rec}}$ for a persistent record. They are distinct from substrate absolute time and require an explicit clock map when compared with it. Defining these first-passage times proves neither that their conditions are reached in a finite window nor that an apparatus class has a positive minimum duration $\tau_{\min}$. A derived lower bound is contradicted by an independently measured upper bound below it.
- **The Born square is open:** the branch requires a selected physical basin measure, convergence of repeated preparation frequencies to its weights, and an independently extracted complex envelope $\psi$. For a declared position-record chart, the pushed-forward basin measure must have density $\rho_{\mathrm{env}}=|\psi|^2$ with respect to the declared chart measure, normalized so its integral is one. Other measurements require their own derived apparatus response or effective operator; a position density is not their outcome law. These obligations are stated but not discharged in [Wavefunction Ontology](./wavefunction-ontology.md).
- **The photon route is referent-pending:** no retained equilibrium branch has established the coaxial contra-rotating polarity-conjugate planar pair. Gate A therefore remains a kinematic theorem target, and Gate B has not derived Malus' law or the squared-amplitude rule.
- **Stern-Gerlach supplies a worked measurement scaffold:** in the spin-one-half comparison, the apparatus separates a beam into two spin-dependent records. First-order record kernels, which map unresolved histories to outcomes, and the ideal half-angle probability comparison are specified in [Angular Momentum and Spin](../philosophy-history/theory-bridges/angular-momentum-and-spin.md#stern-gerlach-like-measurement-response). The substrate spinor coordinate, physical record-cycle measure, independently calibrated heralding efficiency, and a computed Born residual remain open. Here a spinor is the effective rotation-sensitive state coordinate, heralding efficiency is the probability of a valid trial-selection record, and the Born residual measures disagreement with the target outcome law.
- **No-go constraints remain active:** Bell, Pusey-Barrett-Rudolph, Kochen-Specker, and observed-observer consistency are replacement constraints or rejection conditions, not philosophical side notes. Their applicability and replacement burdens are owned by [No-Go Theorems](../validation/no-go-theorems.md).
