$$ \frac{\|\mathbf{r}\|_1^2}{\|\mathbf{r}\|_2^2} = \frac{(r_I + r_{II} + r_{III})^2}{r_I^2 + r_{II}^2 + r_{III}^2} = \frac{3}{2} $$
