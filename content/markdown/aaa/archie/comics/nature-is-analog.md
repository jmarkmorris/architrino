# Nature Is Analog

Discrete quantum records can be implemented by analog underlying dynamics.

[![Nature Is Analog](../../../../assets/images/comics/nature-is-analog.png)](../../../../assets/images/comics/nature-is-analog.png)

The drawn ket labels and numbers are schematic, not a valid qubit probability calculation. Actual probabilities require a specified preparation and measurement basis; the comic illustrates the distinction between dynamics and records without deriving quantum statistics.
