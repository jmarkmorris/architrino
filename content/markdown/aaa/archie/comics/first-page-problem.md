# First Page Problem

The first page of a reconstruction does not by itself establish a cosmic beginning or an earlier history. The cartoon’s unbounded history is a candidate, and its evidence labels are schematic: in the [standard chronology](https://science.nasa.gov/universe/overview/), big-bang nucleosynthesis (BBN) precedes the cosmic microwave background (CMB) last-scattering epoch.
