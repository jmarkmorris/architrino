# Signals From Earlier

The present record carries signals from earlier events, so interpretation has to respect [path history](../../foundations/absolute-time.md#path-history-and-non-markovian-memory)—the past trajectory record that delayed dynamics still consult—instead of treating now as self-explanatory.
