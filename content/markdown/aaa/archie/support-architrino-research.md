# Support Architrino Research

Architrino Assembly Architecture is an independent research program in fundamental physics. Independent research depends on uninterrupted time to write, calculate, simulate, revise, answer criticism, and keep the public project usable. Readers who find the work valuable can help preserve that continuity through monthly or one-time financial support.

## Why Support Matters

Sustained time supports:

- research and development
- long-form writing and revision,
- simulation design and interpretation,
- editorial cleanup and document organization,
- public discussion and response to criticism,
- and continued improvement of the webapp as a reader-facing research environment.

If this project is useful to you, financial support helps preserve that continuity.
