# Support Architrino Research

## Purpose

This page explains how readers can support continued work on the Architrino project.

The goal is not to gate access, sell certainty, or turn the webapp into a fundraising surface first. The goal is to make it possible for readers who find the work valuable to help sustain ongoing research, writing, simulations, editing, and public discussion.

## Why Support Matters

Architrino is being developed as an independent research program in fundamental physics.

That means the work depends on sustained time for:

- long-form writing and revision,
- simulation design and interpretation,
- editorial cleanup and document restructuring,
- public discussion and response to criticism,
- and continued improvement of the webapp as a reader-facing research environment.

If this project is useful to you, financial support helps preserve that continuity.

## What Support Means

Support for this project should be understood plainly.

- It helps keep the research active and public.
- It does not purchase agreement or editorial control.
- It does not imply that the framework is finished or beyond criticism.
- It does not place core theory content behind a paywall.

The standard remains the same: claims should be evaluated on clarity, derivation, explanatory power, and contact with evidence.

## Suggested Reader Frame

If you want the shortest version, it is this:

Architrino is an independent research program. If you find the work valuable and want to help keep it moving, you can support it either monthly or with a one-time contribution.
