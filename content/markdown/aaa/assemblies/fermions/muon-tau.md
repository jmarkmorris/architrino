# Muon and Tau

## Purpose

This chapter is a placeholder for the heavier charged lepton excitation states in $\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}$.

## Current framing

Muon and tau states are treated as higher-excitation charged lepton assemblies that share the same broad charge pattern as the [electron](./electron.md) while differing in shielding, excitation, and dissociation-accessible relaxation channels. In the present architecture they are the heavier charged-lepton branches of the same shielding ladder used in [Nested Binaries and the Noether Core](../noether-core.md) and [Particle Masses: Emergent Inertia in the Noether Sea](../particle-masses.md).
