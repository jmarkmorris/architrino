Filters combine to narrow the collection. Counts show unique braid entries; related exact configurations share one entry until opened. Any includes records with missing assignments; choosing a value shows only confirmed matches. 1D includes lines and points.

Circle occupancy counts complete geometric circles in one declared assembly frame. Multiple per circle means every identified circle has at least two architrinos; One per circle means every identified circle has one; Both occupancy types means both occur. Different circles may have different radii.

Two-occupant antipodal circle: a red half-turn tail and a blue half-turn tail. A co-rotating multi-occupant circle reaches back to the preceding member. A single-occupant circle shows a full turn in its architrino's color. Trails use only available past motion, so they fill in at the beginning of a record.

About this library

Live previews of the sealed catalog are served through the indexed assembly registry. The page lists braid entries and separately counts exact configurations linked to active source-owned findings. It runs no simulation and does not treat an unindexed finding as a negative. The million-entry benchmark measures registry engineering only; synthetic rows supply no scientific evidence.

Assembly radii compares every architrino’s distance from the declared center of the whole assembly at the same time. Iso-radii means equal distances throughout the record; Hetero-radii means unequal distances at some time. Equal radii may breathe together. This is not the radius about an individual orbit center. Missing source information remains unassigned. Spindle classifications are recorded separately from the sealed motion.
