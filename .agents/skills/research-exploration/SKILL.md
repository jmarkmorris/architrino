# Research Exploration

Use this skill for provisional research discussion and idea capture.

1. Follow the repository [startup instructions](../../../AGENTS.md).
2. Read the live [brainstorming prompt](../../../reference/research-office/cto/prompts/brainstorming.md) completely before responding.
3. Follow that owner for exploration, claim discipline, and capture, and its linked standards for responses and exposition.

Use [corpus convergence](../aaa-corpus-advancement/SKILL.md) for an explicitly requested convergence campaign, including its exploration mode. Use [the review workflows](../corpus-review-workflow/SKILL.md) to judge, integrate, or verify supplied feedback. Operational workflow discussions belong in [operator brainstorming](../../../reference/op/brainstorming.md).
