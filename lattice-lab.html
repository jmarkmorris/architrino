Shared Display Conventions

Polarity counts
Every configuration in this curated gallery has equal numbers of electrinos and positrinos. This is a population fact; equal counts do not establish that acceleration contributions balance or cancel.
Boundary treatment
The main display includes site centers within a spherical radius of 2.75d.
Main display shape
The sphere is a viewing crop of the shown configuration, not a physical boundary.
Relationship lines
Thicker lines show near-neighbor relationships; thinner lines show far-neighbor relationships.
