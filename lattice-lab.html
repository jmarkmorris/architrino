Lattice Primer Simple cubic

Equal rows of sites meet at right angles. Each interior site has six nearest neighbors: left, right, up, down, forward, and back.

The checkerboard polarity rule changes red to blue at every one-step move. Its smallest polarity pattern repeats every two site spacings. The main view includes every lattice site center that fits inside the dotted spherical crop.

The geometric density n = 1/d³ counts sites per volume for spacing d. It is not mass density and does not establish a physical medium.
