## Overall Finding

The first honest object is not a positive-width invariant cell. It is a validated finite-memory relative-periodic point enclosed by a validation box. At fixed memory level $N$ and drift $u$, define

$$
F_{N,u}(x)
=
P_{N,u}(x)-g\mathbin{\cdot}x,
$$

where $P_{N,u}$ is one declared first-return map on a regular section chart and $g$ contains only symmetries proved neutral on the complete stored delayed state. A Krawczyk or interval-Newton operator belongs to the square equation $F_{N,u}=0$:

$$
\mathcal K_{F_{N,u}}(\bar x,B_{N,u})
\subset
\operatorname{int}(B_{N,u}).
$$

Under the declared differentiability, chart, derivative-enclosure, and symmetry hypotheses, this can prove that one relative-periodic point lies in $B_{N,u}$. Positive box width does not prove forward invariance, attraction, or positive basin measure.

Plainly: the box is the error enclosure used to prove that one complete delayed history returns. It does not say that every history in the box remains there or approaches the returned history.

The second decisive correction concerns no-retune transport. On the one-dimensional drift interval $I_u$, curvature vanishes automatically and forward-and-back loop transport is trivial. Drift-only holonomy therefore cannot show that clock, envelope, energy, momentum, mass-shell, phase, or Noether sea rows were not separately tuned. The surviving test is frozen-source held-out parallelism: construct a source-identified connection from declared retained source data, freeze the construction, and predict independently extracted row sections on unused rows and an unused drift block with a validated identification margin.

Plainly: a real no-retune test hides the answers from the constructor, proves the source data leave no invisible tuning direction, and then predicts data that did not shape the construction.

The correct proof order is:

1. one validated finite-memory relative-periodic point;
2. controlled lift to one full delayed history;
3. validated continuation over drift when family-level transport is required;
4. gamma-free pointwise coframe extraction or drift transport at the strength supported;
5. frozen-source held-out no-retune testing;
6. separate stability, trapping, or basin work only when claimed;
7. downstream mass-shell and Koide diagnostics only after the retained mass map exists.

Plainly: later residual matches cannot repair a missing returned history, and a point at one drift cannot support a connection across drift.

### Finding 2: The First Existence Equation Is The Group-Reduced Return Residual

The early schematic inclusion

$$
\mathcal K_{P_N}(B_N)\subset B_N

$$
F_{N,u}(x)=P_{N,u}(x)-g\mathbin{\cdot}x=0,

with either an exact symmetry-reduced section or an augmented system carrying return time and group parameters plus one pinning condition per neutral generator.

Plainly: validated root methods solve equations. The equation is “returned history minus the allowed symmetry-adjusted starting history equals zero.”

The finite-memory state must retain or rigorously reconstruct every coordinate that can change the next return:

Plainly: two states that look identical in the proposed coordinates but produce different next returns show that the return state is incomplete.

Plainly: the proof follows the same path and root strands through the entire return. Matching names at the two endpoints is not proof of identity.
