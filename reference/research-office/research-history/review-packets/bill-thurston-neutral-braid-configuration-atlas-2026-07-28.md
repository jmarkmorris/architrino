## Research framing and executive finding

The configuration atlas should not be one finite-dimensional moduli space. The smallest justified model is a **stratified topological symmetry groupoid of polarity-labeled histories**, represented computationally by a pro-system of certified finite-history approximation groupoids. Exact-binary descriptions belong in matching-decorated subcharts. Causal-root and spatial-topology rows are derived, validity-scoped diagnostics; they are not free coordinates and do not establish dynamical retention.

Plainly: the mathematical object is the six recorded paths and the boundary information needed to evaluate delayed interactions. A numerical parameterization is only one controlled view of that object. Searching that view cannot support a claim about histories outside it.

The research gives a decisive counterexample to the current provisional invariant families. In the bounded sub-field-speed class, the elementary causal-root counts and signed degrees can remain fixed while one spatial support component changes from an unknot to a nontrivial knot. Therefore neither $(N_s,M_p,c_1)$ nor $(D_{\mathrm{self}},D_p,c_1)$, even with $D_{\mathrm{plane}}$ and one chirality bit, can classify the topology of six histories.

Plainly: causal-root topology and spatial knot topology measure different structure. Both may be useful, but neither can stand in for the other.

The analysis supplies one exact nontrivial sharp-root tail theorem. If the complete declared transmitter inventory remains inside one fixed region of diameter $D$, every sharp causal-root delay is at most $D$. A retained horizon $H>D$ therefore contains every sharp causal root. A uniform speed ceiling $\|\mathbf V_i\|\le1-\varepsilon$ is not needed for sharp-root exclusion, but it also makes any admitted root unique, simple, and positive-sign while excluding nontrivial self-hits. Existence of a nontrivial partner root additionally requires positive receiver–transmitter separation at reception.

Plainly: on this bounded class, the sharp causal-root inventory can be exact. The EOM solver’s finite-width Gaussian acceleration kernel has noncompact tails, so $H>D$ does not make its omitted acceleration contribution exactly zero. Neither result establishes a returned or stable branch.

## Claim-grade boundary

### Derived in this research synthesis

- Simultaneous positions alone cannot determine delayed acceleration because active causal roots depend on retained history.
- Pointwise centered Gram data reconstruct each labeled rank-three simultaneous shape only up to an independently chosen orthogonal transformation at each time; they do not reconstruct a history modulo one constant rotation.
- Centered vector history $Q(T)$, or the cross-time kernel $K(T,S)=Q(T)^{\mathsf T}Q(S)$ with one rank-three oriented anchor, restores the missing temporal orientation relation.
- Every opposite-polarity perfect matching supplies algebraic midpoint and half-separation rows, so the mere existence of those rows does not define a persistent binary locus.
- A generic simple root persists locally under a positive transversality margin, and an ordinary root fold changes unsigned root count by two while preserving signed degree.
- A complete transmitter inventory confined for all relevant past times to one fixed region of diameter $D$ has no sharp causal-root delay greater than $D$.
- In bounded histories with all speeds strictly below $1$, every admitted root is unique, simple, and positive-sign; a distinct ordered pair has one nontrivial partner root when its receiver–transmitter separation at reception is positive; and no path has a nontrivial self-hit.
- Spatial knot or link type is preserved under collision-free ambient isotopy with a fixed closure convention.

Plainly: these are mathematical consequences of the supplied setup and stated regularity assumptions. They do not show that the equation of motion realizes any proposed braid.

### Proposed architecture

- a stratified topological symmetry groupoid;
- certified finite-horizon and finite-resolution pro-presentations;
- prehistory as a boundary fiber with an explicit tail contract;
- matching-decorated exact-binary charts;
- a nested motion/deformation hierarchy with refinement witnesses;
- a derived invariant hierarchy combining causal-root, closure, knot, link, framing, and higher-order rows;
- a minimum chart-to-instrument contract;
- a regular-stratum persistence and reconstruction lemma.

## Independent analytical audit

An independent read-only analysis rederived the central mathematics and compared it with the live EOM solver contracts and implementation. Its result is a partial acceptance with two required corrections. This is an analytical audit, not an independently authored numerical instrument or measured acceptance record.

### Verified as derived

- **Sharp-root delay bound:** for a complete declared inventory contained through all relevant times in one fixed region $\Omega$ of diameter $D$, every sharp causal-root delay is at most $D$.
- **Sub-field-speed regularity:** $\|\mathbf V_j\|\le1-\varepsilon$ implies $D_{t,ij}\ge\varepsilon$, so any root is simple, positive-sign, and unique for its ordered pair.
- **Partner-root existence with qualification:** exactly one nontrivial partner root exists when the ordered pair has positive receiver–transmitter separation at reception and the bounded-history old-boundary sign is negative.
- **Self-hit exclusion:** a uniformly sub-field-speed path has no nontrivial self-root.
- **Cross-time reconstruction:** pointwise Gram histories plus orientation signs are incomplete; $Q(T)$ is the direct fixed-frame coordinate, while $K(T,S)=Q(T)^{\mathsf T}Q(S)$ plus a rank-three oriented anchor reconstructs centered history modulo one constant proper rotation. The barycenter history remains separate.
- **Scalar-label incompleteness:** the unlink/square-knot construction verifies that $(N_s,M_p,c_1)$ and $(D_{\mathrm{self}},D_p,c_1)$ are not complete spatial knot or link classifiers for prescribed closed histories.

Plainly: the audit confirms the sharp-root, reconstruction, and classifier-scope corrections. It does not produce retained EOM branches with different knot types and therefore does not establish a realized assembly classification.

### Refuted or narrowed

- The statement that $H>D$ omits exactly zero acceleration contribution is false for finite regulator width. A Gaussian kernel has noncompact support, so the omitted tail may be tolerance-small but is not identically zero.
- The statement that every distinct ordered pair has a partner root is false at a receiver–transmitter collision unless a separate nontrivial-root convention and collision treatment supply it.
- The horizon-clearance margin must not use $\eta$, which already denotes regulator width; this packet uses $\delta_H>0$ instead.

For two static paths separated by $1$, with $H=2$ and a Gaussian regulator of width $\eta>0$, the omitted factor contains

$$
\int_2^\infty
\delta_\eta(1-u)\,du
=
\frac12
\operatorname{erfc}
\left(
\frac{1}{\sqrt2\,\eta}
\right)
>
0.
$$

Plainly: the finite-width contribution can be very small, but it is not exactly zero.

The live [evolution contract](../../../priorities/app-solver/contracts/evolution-contract-v1.md) integrates the finite-width kernel only over retained history and does not claim earlier contributions are zero. The EOM solver fails closed with `insufficient_history_depth` when the stored boundary is inadequate. The [master EOM binding](../../../priorities/app-solver/contracts/master-eom-binding-v1.md) defines the noncompact Gaussian regulator relevant to this distinction.

Plainly: the sharp causal-root theorem and the finite-width acceleration-tail problem are different obligations. The former is accepted with hypotheses; the latter remains unresolved.

## Research findings

### 1. Use a stratified topological symmetry groupoid with certified pro-presentations

Fix an evaluation interval $J=[T_0,T_1]$, regularity $r\ge1$, and persistent identity set $I_6=\{1,\ldots,6\}$. A full object is

$$
B
=
\left(
\mathbf X,
\mathbf q,
\mathcal A,
\mathcal E,
\chi
\right),
$$

where $\mathbf X_i:(-\infty,T_1]\to\mathbb R^3$ is a $C^r_{\mathrm{loc}}$ path, $\mathbf q$ is the neutral polarity row, $\mathcal A$ is the complete declared ambient Noether sea record, $\mathcal E$ contains boundary and retained-history declarations, and $\chi$ contains chart and evidence metadata.

Plainly: a full object is the six labeled histories plus every declared external record capable of changing their delayed evaluation. The causal-root ledger is computed from the object; it is not an independent coordinate.

On collision-free objects, the path component lies in

$$
C^r_{\mathrm{loc}}
\left(
(-\infty,T_1],
\operatorname{Conf}_{3,3}(\mathbb R^3)
\right)
$$

with the compact-open $C^r$ topology.

Plainly: two histories are close when they and their required derivatives are close on every finite past interval.

Let $\Gamma$ contain only declared combinations of constant Euclidean translations and proper rotations, permitted constant shifts of absolute-time origin, polarity-preserving persistent-label permutations, and any separately proved full-record conjugation. An arrow $B\to B'$ is a $\gamma\in\Gamma$ satisfying

$$
\gamma\mathbf X=\mathbf X',
\qquad
\gamma\mathbf q=\mathbf q',
\qquad
\gamma\mathcal A=\mathcal A',
\qquad
\gamma\mathcal E=\mathcal E',
$$

and preserving every causal-root row and delayed acceleration contribution under the induced identity map.

Plainly: an action becomes a symmetry arrow only after it preserves the entire delayed record. A time-dependent rotation, drift change, prehistory change, ambient-state change, or root-identity reassignment is not an arrow by default.

The resulting action groupoid is

$$
\mathscr G
=
\Gamma\ltimes\mathscr H
\rightrightarrows
\mathscr H,
\qquad
\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathscr G}(B)
=
\{\gamma\in\Gamma:\gamma B=B\}.
$$

Plainly: the groupoid retains the stabilizer of a symmetric history rather than flattening it into a singular quotient coordinate.

For horizon $H>0$ and approximation depth $N$, a certified finite presentation has the form

$$
B_{H,N}
=
\left(
P_N\mathbf X|_{[T_0-H,T_1]},
\mathcal U_{H,N},
\beta_H,
\mathcal A_H,
\chi_{H,N}
\right).
$$

Here $P_N$ is the declared finite-rank representation, $\mathcal U_{H,N}$ is a rigorous reconstruction-error set, and $\beta_H$ is the tail contract.

Plainly: a finite coefficient row represents a controlled set of possible histories. Its error tube and missing-past contract are part of the mathematical record.

Compatible restriction maps must satisfy

$$
\rho_{\alpha\to\alpha}
=
\operatorname{id},
\qquad
\rho_{\beta\to\alpha}
\mathbin{\circ}
\rho_{\gamma\to\beta}
=
\rho_{\gamma\to\alpha}
$$

for $\alpha\preceq\beta\preceq\gamma$, while conservatively mapping coefficient enclosures, tail contracts, ambient records, arrows, and stabilizers.

Plainly: restricting a longer, finer record directly must agree with restricting it through any intermediate presentation.

A compatible family represents a unique full history only if its horizons are cofinal, its resolutions increase without bound, and its reconstruction uncertainty closes:

$$
H\longrightarrow\infty,
\qquad
N\longrightarrow\infty,
\qquad
\operatorname{diam}_{C^r}\mathcal U_{H,N}\longrightarrow0.
$$

Plainly: nested finite records may otherwise converge only to a nontrivial set of possible histories.

**Proposed change.** Use “stratified topological symmetry groupoid with certified pro-presentations” as the current global description. Reserve “orbifold” or “differentiable stack” for a finite-dimensional stratum only after the necessary properness, smoothness, and slice results are proved. Keep evidence status—prescribed, candidate, retained, or certified—as metadata rather than a geometric stratum.

Every finite presentation must either supply complete prehistory, prove exact root exclusion, or bound every omitted root and acceleration contribution below declared tolerances.

### 2. Centered labeled histories are the neutral-base coordinates

Before binary grouping, use an arithmetic center and centered labeled vectors:

$$
\mathbf b(T)
=
\frac16\sum_{i=1}^6\mathbf X_i(T),
\qquad
\mathbf z_i(T)
=
\mathbf X_i(T)-\mathbf b(T),
\qquad
\sum_{i=1}^6\mathbf z_i(T)=0.
$$

Plainly: $\mathbf b$ records translation relative to the Euclidean void, while the $\mathbf z_i$ record internal shape without assuming three binaries.

Five centered vectors determine the sixth:

$$
Q(T)
=
\begin{bmatrix}
\mathbf z_1(T)&\cdots&\mathbf z_5(T)
\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
\mathbf z_6(T)
=
-\sum_{i=1}^5\mathbf z_i(T).
$$

Their pointwise Gram history is

$$
G(T)=Q(T)^{\mathsf T}Q(T).
$$

Plainly: when $Q(T)$ has rank three, $G(T)$ determines the labeled shape at that time up to an orthogonal transformation. It does not say how the orientations chosen at different times fit together.

The pointwise record is incomplete even after adding an orientation sign. Let $Q_0$ have rank three and let $R(T)\in SO(3)$ be nonconstant:

$$
Q_1(T)=Q_0,
\qquad
Q_2(T)=R(T)Q_0.
$$

Then

$$
Q_1(T)^{\mathsf T}Q_1(T)
=
Q_2(T)^{\mathsf T}Q_2(T)
$$

at every time, and all oriented-minor signs agree, yet no one constant rotation maps the first history to the second.

Plainly: instantaneous shape data cannot tell whether the complete configuration is rotating as time passes. The two histories can have different velocities, causal roots, and delayed accelerations.

The corrected intrinsic alternative is the cross-time kernel

$$
K(T,S)=Q(T)^{\mathsf T}Q(S).
$$

Plainly: cross-time inner products retain how the orientations at different times relate to each other.

If $Q(T_\ast)$ has rank three at an anchor time, equality of $K(T,S)$ for two histories determines them up to one constant orthogonal transformation. To select $SO(3)$ rather than all of $O(3)$, retain one nonzero oriented anchor such as

$$
\omega_{abc}(T_\ast)
=
\operatorname{sign}
\det
\begin{bmatrix}
\mathbf z_a(T_\ast)&\mathbf z_b(T_\ast)&\mathbf z_c(T_\ast)
\end{bmatrix}.
$$

Plainly: the cross-time kernel fixes one temporal orientation relation, and the anchor sign removes the remaining reflection ambiguity.

**Counterexamples to narrower coordinates.**

- Reflecting a generic rank-three labeled configuration preserves every pairwise distance and Gram entry but reverses its orientation. No proper rotation identifies the two records.
- Applying a nonconstant $R(T)$ preserves every pointwise Gram matrix and orientation sign while changing the history modulo constant full-record rotations.
- Traversing identical support curves with different cadence functions preserves the unparameterized curves while changing velocities, causal roots, and $D_{t,ij}$.

**Proposed change.** Require neutral-base charts to carry $\mathbf b(T)$ and centered $Q(T)$ directly, or the cross-time kernel $K(T,S)$ with a rank-three oriented anchor. Pointwise Gram-plus-orientation data are not an equivalent history coordinate. Also require the derivatives used by the instrument, persistent identities and polarities, boundary fibers, and reconstruction-error flags. Treat Fourier and spline coefficients as representations of these histories, not as intrinsic coordinates, and treat causal-root ledgers as derived rows with provenance.

### 3. Exact binaries form a matching-decorated stratum

Let

$$
P=\{i:q_i>0\},
\qquad
M=\{j:q_j<0\},
\qquad
|P|=|M|=3.
$$

Plainly: $P$ and $M$ are the persistent identity sets for the three positive and three negative architrinos.

There are six opposite-polarity perfect matchings:

$$
\mathfrak M
=
\operatorname{Bij}(P,M),
\qquad
\left|
\mathfrak M
\right|
=3!=6.
$$

Plainly: every matching produces three algebraic pair rows, whether or not any pair persists dynamically.

For one matching $\mu$,

$$
\mathbf c_a^\mu
=
\frac{
\mathbf X_{a,+}
+
\mathbf X_{\mu(a),-}
}{2},
\qquad
\mathbf d_a^\mu
=
\frac{
\mathbf X_{a,+}
-
\mathbf X_{\mu(a),-}
}{2}.
$$

Plainly: midpoint and half-separation formulas are coordinate identities, not binary-retention certificates.

The proposed binary cover is

$$
\widehat{\mathscr B}
=
\left\{
(B,\mu):
\mu
\text{ satisfies the declared binary-coherence predicates on }B
\right\}
\longrightarrow
\mathscr H.
$$

Plainly: one history may support no admissible matching, one unique matching, or several competing matchings.

One proposed interval functional is

$$
S_\mu(B;I)
=
\sum_{a=1}^{3}
\left[
w_d\operatorname{osc}_I\log\|\mathbf d_a^\mu\|
+
w_v\int_I
\left\|
\frac{d}{dT}
\frac{\mathbf d_a^\mu}{\|\mathbf d_a^\mu\|}
\right\|^2dT
+
w_c\mathcal C_a^\mu
\right],
$$

where the weights, normalizations, and root-ledger or phase-chart incoherence term $\mathcal C_a^\mu$ are fixed before evaluation.

Plainly: the score asks whether a proposed pair keeps a usable separation, coherent relative direction, and the declared root or phase structure throughout the interval. The score is an atlas predicate, not a derived dynamical binary theorem.

A matching is locally coherent with margins $(\delta,\gamma)$ when

$$
\inf_{T\in I}\|\mathbf d_a^\mu(T)\|\ge\delta>0,
\qquad
S_\nu-S_\mu\ge\gamma>0
\quad
\text{for every }\nu\ne\mu,
$$

and every required pair phase and partner-root row remains valid.

Plainly: a certified matching must be nonsingular, keep its pair charts, and beat every alternative by a positive declared margin.

**Proposed change.** Use the positive coherence and uniqueness margins to distinguish algebraic pair rows, a coherent pair chart, matching degeneracy, bookkeeping reassignment, and gain or loss of a pair certificate. “Pair creation” should be recorded only when a previously absent coherence certificate closes.

On the fully labeled history space, the matching bundle is a subspace of the trivial finite product $\mathscr H\times\mathfrak M$. If $\gamma>0$ is maintained along a connected loop, the unique matching is locally constant and therefore cannot return as a different labeled matching.

Plainly: persistent identities and a positive uniqueness gap exclude genuine matching monodromy. Apparent monodromy can arise only after quotienting labels or crossing a matching-degeneracy stratum, where the regular certificate has already failed.

### 4. Use one nested motion and deformation hierarchy

Represent motion classes as nested restrictions of the same centered path history:

1. common rigid return;
2. center drift;
3. independent cadence;
4. finite-basis precession, breathing, and phase modulation;
5. general controlled deformation.

At the general finite level,

$$
\mathbf z_i^{(N)}(T)
=
\sum_{k=1}^{N}
\mathbf a_{ik}B_k(T),
\qquad
\sum_{i=1}^6\mathbf a_{ik}=0.
$$

Plainly: every finite campaign searches a declared coefficient domain in one representation. A negative at depth $N$ applies only to that domain and its proved error envelope.

**Proposed change.** A scoped negative at depth $N$ must declare:

- the coefficient domain $K_N$;
- basis and regularity norm;
- a $C^1$ or stronger projection-error bound;
- the root-transversality margin after that error is included;
- comparison with at least one larger depth $N'>N$;
- a larger history horizon;
- persistence of the negative conclusion across the declared refinement envelope.

The $C^1$ requirement is material because the causal-root equation depends on position while $D_t$ depends on transmitter velocity. Basis density in an infinite-dimensional regularity class does not turn a finite search into an exhaustion claim.

The resulting scoped-negative theorem target is:

> If a validated enclosure covers every coefficient cell in compact domain $K_N$, includes its $C^1$ reconstruction-error tube, has a valid tail contract and complete root inventory, and proves an operator-checkable predicate $P$ false uniformly on that covered set, then the campaign may conclude only that no history in that explicitly declared set satisfies $P$.

It may not exclude histories outside $K_N$, at unresolved singular walls, at greater basis depth without a coverage theorem, under another prehistory or ambient state, or in another motion class. It cannot convert a prescribed-history negative into a retained-branch or stability conclusion.

Plainly: the negative applies exactly where the campaign supplied an enclosure proof. Unsearched or unresolved regions stay unresolved.

Uniform positional approximation alone is insufficient. For

$$
x_n(T)
=
\frac{\epsilon}{n}\sin(nT),
$$

one has

$$
\|x_n\|_{C^0}\longrightarrow0,
\qquad
\|\dot x_n\|_{C^0}=\epsilon.
$$

Plainly: arbitrarily small position wiggles can retain non-small velocity errors, changing $D_t$ and moving a near-fold root across its transversality wall.

A valid negative therefore requires interval enclosures of $g$ and $D_t$, root isolation plus a nonzero $g$-floor outside root boxes, a tail theorem or horizon refinement, basis refinement unless a coverage theorem replaces it, and separate treatment of every box meeting a declared wall.

### 5. Quotient only full-record symmetries

The default treatment of candidate transformations is:

| Transformation | Default treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Global translation | Symmetry only when the void boundary and ambient record are translation invariant |
| Global rotation | Symmetry only when boundary and ambient records rotate with the history |
| Absolute-time shift | Symmetry only for an autonomous law with shifted prehistory and ambient state |
| Common return-phase shift | A restricted time-shift action only on a retained phase-locked branch |
| $S_3$ pair relabeling | Available only in a matching-decorated binary chart |
| Polarity conjugation | Symmetry only when all polarity-sensitive records are conjugated |
| Within-pair endpoint exchange | Not generally neutral because the endpoints have opposite polarity and persistent identity |
| Drift-velocity change | Not a gauge transformation |

Plainly: a transformation is removable only when it preserves every field used by the delayed return map. Geometric familiarity is not enough.

Before binary decomposition, the candidate label action lies in the subgroup of $S_3^+\times S_3^-$ permitted by the persistent-identity contract. A diagonal $S_3$ action on pair records appears only after choosing a matching.

**Proposed change.** Search in labeled coordinates and deduplicate only after evaluation with a full-record equivalence test. Report both labeled multiplicity and groupoid-orbit multiplicity. Retain stabilizer data at equal-cadence, equal-geometry, rotational-symmetry, short-period, matching-degeneracy, coplanar, and collinear strata rather than forcing a singular canonical ordering.

### 6. Separate geometric, causal, phase, and chart walls

The principal wall catalogue is:

| Wall | Generic status under stated regularity | Local effect |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| $\max_T\|\mathbf V_i(T)\|=1$ | Codimension one at a nondegenerate maximum | Opens the possibility, not the necessity, of self-hits |
| Root fold $g=0$ and $\partial g/\partial T_e=0$ | Codimension-one discriminant after eliminating $T_e$ | Creates or removes an opposite-sign root pair |
| $D_{\mathrm{plane}}=0$ | Codimension one under transverse crossing | Invalidates the rank-three plane chart |
| Phase-lock boundary | Often codimension one in a declared return-map family | May make $c_1$ undefined |
| Collision $\mathbf X_i(T)=\mathbf X_j(T)$ | Typically codimension two after event time is allowed | May change spatial-link type or invalidate the point-path chart |
| Framing failure | Depends on the framing construction | May make framing and chirality rows undefined |
| Oriented-minor zero | Coordinate wall | Requires a Gram-orientation chart transition |

Plainly: some walls change causal channels, some permit spatial topology to change, and some only require a new coordinate chart. They must not be collapsed into one generic degeneracy flag.

The signed causal degrees can remain fixed across a root fold even while unsigned counts change. A collision can change spatial knot or link type while the signed causal degrees remain unchanged. The intersections that need separate normal forms include:

1. a root fold at the field-speed wall;
2. a root fold at collision;
3. $D_{\mathrm{plane}}=0$ with phase-chart failure;
4. collision with framing failure;
5. matching degeneracy with root reassignment.

**Proposed change.** Give each wall its own defining function, margin, event type, and allowed surgery. Do not assign a universal codimension to phase-lock or framing loss until their defining functions are fixed.

### 7. The present invariant labels are incomplete

Consider bounded periodic histories satisfying

$$
\sup_{i,T}\|\mathbf V_i(T)\|<1.
$$

Plainly: every transmitter remains strictly below the normalized wake speed throughout the history.

For $i\ne j$,

$$
\frac{\partial g_{i\leftarrow j}}{\partial T_e}
=
1-\hat{\mathbf r}_{ij}\mathbin{\cdot}\mathbf V_j(T_e)
>
0.
$$

Plainly: the causal equation is strictly increasing in emission time. Bounded infinite prehistory gives one and only one positive-sign simple root for each ordered distinct pair.

For $i=j$ and $T_e<T$,

$$
\left\|
\mathbf X_i(T)-\mathbf X_i(T_e)
\right\|
<
T-T_e.
$$

Plainly: a strict sub-field-speed path cannot catch its own earlier wake, so it has no nontrivial self-hit.

This broad class can therefore share elementary root counts and signed degrees while containing different spatial knot types.

**Explicit non-equivalence control.**

- Record $A$: six disjoint periodic support curves forming the six-component unlink.
- Record $B$: the same construction except one component is a disjoint square knot with zero framing.
- Give both a sufficiently long common period so every speed remains below $1$.
- Choose the same return-degree row and the same three derived angular-momentum directions.

The records can share $(N_s,M_p,c_1)$, $(D_{\mathrm{self}},D_p,c_1)$, $D_{\mathrm{plane}}$, and one chirality bit, yet their support-curve complements are inequivalent because $B$ contains a nontrivial knotted component.

Plainly: this counterexample rules out the current scalar rows as a complete topological classifier, even before asking whether either history is dynamically retained.

**Proposed invariant hierarchy.**

1. component knot types or declared computable surrogates;
2. the framed pairwise linking matrix

   $$
   \Lambda_{ij}
   =
   \operatorname{lk}(K_i,K_j),
   \qquad
   \Lambda_{ii}
   =
   \text{self-framing};
   $$

   Plainly: this records ordinary pairwise linking and framing but misses higher-order links such as the Borromean rings.

3. a closure graph with persistent identities and root-edge labels;
4. a higher-order link row when pairwise linking vanishes;
5. complement or braid-group data for cases left ambiguous by cheaper invariants.

Persistent homology may remain a numerical shape diagnostic, but it is not a complete knot or link invariant. None of these rows establishes retention, stability, or EOM closure.

Topology rows are conditional on a valid closure class:

| Closure class | Mandatory rows | Optional rows | Fail-closed boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open finite history | Complete causal-root ledger, signs, and singular margins | Persistent homology as shape metadata | No knot, link, braid, or framing claim |
| Prescribed closed support curves | Closure identity, embedding validity, component-knot diagnostic, pairwise linking matrix | Higher linking and complement data | Applies only to the prescribed curves |
| Returned history with declared closure | Return map, closure rule and provenance, knot and pairwise-link rows | Higher linking, braid, or complement data | Applies only to that returned closure |
| Framed returned history | Returned-history rows, nonvanishing framing, self-framing integers | Framed complement data | Framing undefined when its norm margin closes |
| Matching-decorated binary chart | Matching identity and margin, pair-chart validity, root-edge closure graph | Linking among closed pair carriers | No binary topology after coherence fails |

Plainly: root information is meaningful whenever the ledger is complete. Knot and link information requires a valid closure, and framing requires an additional nonvanishing normal field.

The topology inventory should escalate only when a cheaper row leaves an ambiguity relevant to the claim. Component polynomials are not complete knot invariants; pairwise linking misses the Whitehead link and Borromean rings; higher-link rows can miss a local knot tied into one component; distinct braid words can have isotopic closures; and persistent homology is a shape diagnostic rather than a knot classifier.

Plainly: every topology row must state its known blind spot and abstain outside its closure and recognition domain.

## Exact finite-history result

### Bounded-history tail theorem

Let the complete declared transmitter inventory remain, for every relevant past time, inside one region $\Omega\subset\mathbb R^3$ of finite diameter

$$
D
=
\sup_{\mathbf x,\mathbf y\in\Omega}
\|\mathbf x-\mathbf y\|
<
\infty.
$$

Plainly: no two declared transmitter or receiver locations in the complete history are farther apart than $D$.

At any causal root,

$$
0<T-T_e
=
\|\mathbf X_i(T)-\mathbf X_j(T_e)\|
\le D.
$$

Therefore a retained horizon $H>D$ contains every sharp causal root from the declared transmitter inventory.

Plainly: a sharp wake arriving now cannot have traveled longer than the maximum separation allowed by the complete spatial enclosure. This conclusion does not make the omitted tail of a finite-width Gaussian acceleration kernel exactly zero.

For evaluation interval $[T_0,T_1]$, the exact finite record must begin at $T_0-H$. The theorem’s inequality then places every required emission time inside $[T_0-H,T_1]$.

Plainly: the horizon is measured backward from the earliest time at which the campaign makes a claim.

This exact exclusion does not require a speed ceiling. If the histories are also $C^1$ and satisfy

$$
\|\mathbf V_j(T)\|
\le
1-\varepsilon,
\qquad
\varepsilon>0,
$$

then

$$
\frac{\partial g_{i\leftarrow j}}{\partial T_e}
=
1-\hat{\mathbf r}_{ij}\mathbin{\cdot}\mathbf V_j(T_e)
\ge
\varepsilon.
$$

Plainly: every admitted root is simple and positive-sign. A distinct ordered pair with positive receiver–transmitter separation at reception has exactly one nontrivial partner root under the bounded-history boundary signs, and no path has a nontrivial self-hit.

The theorem applies only when one fixed enclosure covers the complete relevant past and the complete transmitter inventory. A bounded simultaneous diameter in a drifting enclosure is insufficient. Undeclared ambient transmitters, an enclosure known only on the retained segment, a receiver–transmitter collision, or unresolved regulator behavior invalidates the corresponding stronger conclusion.

**Required certificate fields.** Record the fixed enclosure, $D\le D_{\max}$, $H\ge D_{\max}+\delta_H$ with $\delta_H>0$, the transmitter inventory, the ambient-state declaration, and—when uniqueness and self-hit exclusion are claimed—the bound $\|\mathbf V_j\|_\infty\le1-\varepsilon$. Record a separate finite-width omitted-tail bound whenever the EOM solver’s regulated acceleration is evaluated.

## Corrected local theorem target

### Regular-stratum persistence and reconstruction lemma

Let $B$ be a full or tail-certified $C^1$ history on compact interval $I$ satisfying:

1. a centered vector history $Q(T)$, or cross-time kernel $K(T,S)$, with a rank-three oriented anchor;
2. a positive collision margin;
3. a complete finite root inventory;
4. a uniform root-transversality floor;
5. a root-boundary gap and positive $|g|$ floor outside disjoint root neighborhoods;
6. complete closed embedded support curves or one fixed returned-history closure for every topology claim;
7. positive intercomponent separation and tubular-neighborhood radius;
8. a nonvanishing framing field with positive norm margin for every framing claim;
9. a valid phase map on a fixed return circle for every phase-degree claim.

Then a sufficiently small $C^1$ neighborhood preserves:

- reconstruction modulo one declared constant translation and proper rotation;
- the number and transmitter-side signs of simple roots;
- component-knot and link type under the fixed closure;
- framing homotopy class;
- phase-return degree.

Plainly: the local conclusions are available only after temporal orientation, root-boundary control, valid closures, curve thickness, and framing and phase margins are supplied.

The proof route uses cross-time Gram realization, the implicit-function theorem for each simple root, compactness plus the off-root $|g|$ floor to exclude new roots, isotopy extension inside disjoint tubular neighborhoods, homotopy of nonvanishing framing fields, and homotopy invariance of circle-map degree.

A finite open path segment has no intrinsic knot type. The statement that a history can leave a stratum only through declared walls is valid only when the stratum is defined as the connected component on which all listed margins and discrete invariants remain valid; it is not a completeness theorem for an arbitrary wall list.

## Decisive controls and falsifiers

### Bounded-tail control

Use six prescribed $C^1$ histories with a positive collision margin, enclosed through all relevant times in one verified fixed region of diameter $D$, with speed ceiling $1-\varepsilon$, no ambient transmitters, and horizon $H=D+\delta_H$. An independent reference must establish:

- exactly one partner root for every ordered $i\ne j$;
- no nontrivial self-root;
- $D_{t,ij}\ge\varepsilon$;
- no ledger change under extension to substantially longer horizons;
- agreement with the analytic delay and boundary bounds.

Plainly: this control tests the exact sharp-root theorem, root completeness, sign convention, and horizon restriction maps without testing finite-width tail truncation or retention.

The separate-thread derivation grades the sharp-root tail contract as derived on this bounded class. A passed independently referenced control would grade only the instrument’s compliance as measured, scoped to the fixture and its tolerances. Neither result changes the claim grade of existence, retention, stability, or physical realization.

### Three-way negative control

Evaluate one prescribed six-history fixture in three forms:

1. the original labeled record;
2. a true full-record symmetry transform;
3. a near-copy differing only in omitted prehistory, chosen to introduce one additional remote causal root.

The instrument must identify the first two modulo the declared symmetry and reject equivalence with the third.

Plainly: failure means either the quotient discarded physical information or the tail/root contract omitted relevant history.

### Topology benchmark control

Use at least the unlink, square-knot insertion, Hopf link, Whitehead link, and Borromean rings. Every claimed invariant row must state which pairs it is expected to distinguish and must fail closed when the required closure or framing record is absent.

Plainly: these examples expose the known blind spots of component, pairwise-linking, chirality, and higher-link diagnostics.

### Architecture falsifier

The finite-presentation architecture is falsified on a purportedly certified class if one record with a proved tail contract and vanishing reconstruction diameter still admits two full-history completions with different causal-root ledgers or delayed accelerations on the certified interval.

Plainly: certified finite data must determine the delayed evaluation on the exact class claimed. If it does not, the certification architecture cannot support candidate or scoped-negative claims there.
