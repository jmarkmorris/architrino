A wake emitted by transmitter $t$ at absolute time $T_t$ reaches receiver $r$ at time $T_r$ when

$$
\|\mathbf X_r(T_r)-\mathbf X_t(T_t)\|=c_f(T_r-T_t),\qquad T_t<T_r.
$$

Here $\mathbf X_r$ and $\mathbf X_t$ are the receiver and transmitter paths, and $c_f$ is wake speed. On a simple root, let $\hat{\mathbf r}$ point from the emission position toward the reception position and let $\mathbf V_t$ and $\mathbf V_r$ be the corresponding velocities. Define

$$
D_t=c_f-\hat{\mathbf r}\cdot\mathbf V_t(T_t),\qquad
D_r=c_f-\hat{\mathbf r}\cdot\mathbf V_r(T_r),\qquad
W^{\mathrm{acc}}=\frac{c_f}{|D_t|},\qquad
\frac{dT_t}{dT_r}=\frac{D_r}{D_t}.
$$

The nonzero transmitter derivative $D_t$ makes the root locally trackable and determines the acceleration weight $W^{\mathrm{acc}}$. The receiver derivative $D_r$ enters signed root playback as reception time advances; it does not multiply instantaneous acceleration. Each calculation must declare the retained roots, separation and derivative bounds, memory domain, and any regulator. Numerical instantiations use $c_f=1$.
