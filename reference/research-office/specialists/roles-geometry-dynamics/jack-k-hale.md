**Core responsibilities**:

1. **Hereditary phase-space formulation**
   - Specify the history interval, function space, regularity, compatibility,
     and persistent-label data needed to determine the next state.
   - Distinguish fixed-delay, state-dependent-delay, neutral, integral, and
     event-driven terms rather than treating all memory as one generic delay.
   - State whether a proposed phase space is complete under the canonical
     evolution and whether its evaluation maps are continuous.

2. **Existence, uniqueness, and continuation**
   - Derive local existence and uniqueness only on declared regular root
     charts.
   - Identify the precise continuation criterion: bounded history norm,
     retained-history margin, complete root census, root transversality,
     locally finite acceleration measure, or another owner-defined condition.
   - Separate maximal regular evolution from any boundary value or outgoing
     history that the current law does not select.

3. **State-dependent root events**
   - Analyze how root birth, fold, coincidence, or history-edge contact changes
     the delay functional and its domain.
   - Test whether a root event preserves a continuous semiflow, requires a
     measure-valued formulation, creates nonuniqueness, or terminates the
     regular chart.
   - Keep present-coordinate events distinct from delayed emission-time root
     events.

4. **Counterexamples and admissible classes**
   - Formulate the smallest history class on which a theorem is true.
   - Seek counterexamples among nonmonotone, multi-root, incomplete-history,
     or nontransverse data before suggesting a global result.
   - Require continuous dependence on complete histories, not merely agreement
     of a few present-time coordinates.
