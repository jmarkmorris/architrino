# Brainstorming Session (Capture-First)

Use this prompt for a working brainstorm: the operator wants to think out loud across the theory, and the session's job is both to reason well and to capture relentlessly. The failure mode to avoid is a good conversation that leaves no durable trace.

## Posture

- Engage deeply and physically. Take each question as a sincere inquiry; reason from the substrate law and geometry, connect across lanes (master-equation, braid, sea, Lorentz, cosmology, quantum, reactions), and use examples, limits, and thought experiments.
- Be honest about provenance. Distinguish what the corpus already says (cite the chapter) from what is a new synthesis this session; own ideas that get refuted rather than defending them.
- Hunt the leap patterns: an identity hiding as a coincidence; one lane's result resolving another lane's open target; a missing-structure argument; a substrate mechanism for an effective-level postulate; a one-line unification. For each, state the trigger, the strongest defensible claim, the assumptions and proof burden, and one concrete next artifact.

## Capture (the point of the session)

- Capture every worthwhile insight immediately, at the correct canonical level, into the owning lane's `brainstorming.md` (loose ideas working toward promotion) or into a memory file (a durable project, user, or feedback fact). Over-capture rather than under-capture.
- Each captured item carries: a claim level (ontology, derivation, effective, or speculation), the assumptions and proof burden, a concrete promotion target (which existing or new chapter it may support), and a one-line next artifact.
- Do not promote into `content/markdown/aaa` during a brainstorm unless explicitly asked — capture is to the priority lane. Corpus promotion is a separate, deliberate pass; see [corpus-promotion.md](corpus-promotion.md).
- Do not link `content/markdown/aaa` to `reference/priorities`. Keep Research Office workflow and Specialist names out of any corpus prose.

## Discipline

- KaTeX `$...$` with balanced delimiters in captured notes. Convert relative dates to absolute when writing memory.
- Keep the write set scoped to `reference/priorities/**` and memory; do not touch the solver, generators, or canon during a brainstorm.
- Before asserting that the corpus does or does not say something, check the relevant chapter rather than relying on memory.

## Close

End the session with a `Closure goal:` line summarizing the captured threads and the single most valuable next artifact or build to pursue.
