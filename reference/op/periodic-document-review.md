# Periodic corpus and priority review

## Purpose and authority

This procedure owns the recurring scans requested on 2026-09-13 and tracked as [OPS-031 and OPS-032](../priorities/aaa-operations/work-queue.md#ops-031--periodic-reader-facing-corpus-scan). Operations owns scheduling, coverage and evaluation; the existing corpus and priority owners retain scientific, editorial and implementation authority. A scan identifies warranted work. It is not a standing instruction to rewrite every document it visits. Findings use the live [corpus reviewer](../office-of-research/cto/prompts/corpus-reviewer.md), accepted repairs use the [integrator](../office-of-research/cto/prompts/integrator-reviewer.md), and verification uses the [closure verifier](../office-of-research/cto/prompts/review-closure-verifier.md), with their current evidence and source requirements.

The initial cadence is an operational judgment, not a measured optimum. Priority states can become stale when ownership or execution changes; scientific exposition needs deeper evidence before revision. Frequent broad rewrites can introduce errors, obscure useful history and consume the same attention needed for scientific progress. Sparse reviews can leave real contradictions undiscovered. The compromise is frequent inexpensive status checks, bounded substantive sampling, and prompt review of affected dependencies after material advances. A model release alone is not evidence that another global rewrite would help.

## Change-triggered review and model advances

A newly accepted derivation, counterexample, primitive/terminology decision, changed runtime contract, failed validation, or credible reader report triggers a targeted review of the affected claim and its direct consumers before relying on or publishing the changed claim. Name the changed premise, source snapshot and dependency path. A typo or stylistic preference does not trigger a new scientific campaign. Route a material defect immediately to its owner and coordinate with active editors; a scan must not overwrite unfinished work.

When adopting a materially different model for reviews, first run a report-only comparison on a fixed small set of six previously reviewed passages: three with independently established defects in their historical snapshots and three valid passages for which no change is expected. Include two additional current passages whose answers have not been supplied to the reviewer. Preserve inputs, model/settings where available, prompts and reference explanations; evaluate both missed defects and invented corrections. Known historical cases test review behavior and may be familiar to the model; they do not establish general superiority. A model that does well earns one bounded current-document pilot, followed by ordinary adjudication and verification. Record a model's useful new finding even when its overall pilot is weak. Model age, confidence, verbosity and agreement with another model are not acceptance criteria.

Broaden a scan when a reproduced defect affects a common definition or dependency, or when two independently substantiated substantive findings in the batch suggest the same failure across related documents. Expand first to that affected family. The standing corpus review cycles above authorize report-only coverage of their due populations. A broader repair campaign or additional priority-assets campaign requires a recorded scope, reason, owner and review capacity under the existing campaign authority; calendar passage or a new model name does not authorize substantive edits. These escalation thresholds are trial operating choices, not statistical tests.

## From findings to changes

Freeze the inspected snapshot or record its exact hashes before review. Each finding must identify the passage, the specific error or missing explanation, the independent reference or counterexample, its consequence, the smallest justified correction, and an observation that would overturn the finding. Distinguish demonstrated defects, unresolved scientific questions, evidence gaps and optional preferences. A cleaner-sounding paraphrase is not by itself an improvement. Check original intent and accepted definitions before proposing a different explanation; preserve useful positive insights at their strongest supported claim level.

Scans are report-only for substantive text. Routine control-record reconciliation may proceed when the live owner's evidence unambiguously establishes the state and existing authority permits it. Substantive corrections enter the existing owning queue with a concrete disposition, then proceed under its accepted edit scope; uncertain scientific or policy choices go to the operator. This prevents discovery from automatically becoming an expanding rewrite and avoids asking again for an already accepted repair. There is no edit quota, and a no-change outcome is valid.

Before a repair, inventory relevant hashes, links, generated consumers and declared reproduction dependencies. After a repair, reread the whole affected document and direct claim consumers; check mathematics, claim grades, source support and rendering as applicable. A different reviewer checks consequential scientific corrections against an independent reference, not merely the author's explanation. Reconcile disagreements explicitly; majority agreement is not proof. A confirmed substantive regression introduced by the repair pauses expansion of that repair family until the owner resolves it and rechecks related changes. Preserve the before/after evidence and concurrent work; do not use destructive Git operations as an automatic recovery step. Generated writes and publication follow their existing authorization owners.

## Measuring whether the trend is positive

Each pass adds one compact dated entry under its OPS ID in the [operations work log](../priorities/aaa-operations/work-log.md), linking detailed findings to existing subject owners. Record scope, source versions, selection method, reviewer/model when known, substantive findings confirmed and rejected, accepted changes, unresolved disputes, confirmed introduced regressions, review time and operator review burden when measured. Report changed-file/line counts as churn indicators only. Do not compare raw finding counts across different sample sizes or risk mixes as if they were an error rate.

At the next substantive pass, within the currently due priority classes, recheck two previous accepted corrections and one previous no-change disposition against the preserved before/after claim and its independent reference. Add these three claim-level checks to the ordinary batch; they need not become three more full-document reviews. Distinguish a regression actually introduced by an edit from an old defect missed by both versions, and leave causal attribution unresolved if history cannot establish the transition. Compare substantive correctness, preserved meaning and explanatory usefulness; report each dimension separately rather than manufacturing a single quality score. Evidence that a repair survives later checking is useful, while the unchanged sample helps detect false reassurance.

Give the operator a short selection of the most consequential correction, any disputed or regressed claim, and a rotating ordinary/no-change example, each with before/after text and its justification. Operator sampling supplements independent verification; it must not be the only way introduced errors are detected. The small samples do not certify corpus-wide correctness or provide a defensible prevalence estimate.

Low yield means no confirmed substantive defect in the inspected set, not proof of a clean corpus. Prioritize resolving repair regressions or an unmanageable verification backlog before expanding review volume. The balance is overturned by observed missed consequential changes, introduced regressions or burdensome low-yield work, all inspectable in the dated records.
