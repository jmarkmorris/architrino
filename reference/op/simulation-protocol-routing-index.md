The table above answers one question: where a simulation request goes, and what the receiving owner must check before accepting it. It does not say that a simulation ran, and it does not say that any result was accepted. No result may claim authority from this index. The EOM solver owns numerical evolution; the scientific lane owns the campaign; the proof lane owns acceptance; Borg may display sealed records but does not certify them.
