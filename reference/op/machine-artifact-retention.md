# Machine Artifact Retention

Responses and working-document capture follow the [operator explanation standard](operator-explanation-standard.md); explanatory prose follows the [academic style guide](../../content/markdown/aaa/archie/academic-style-guide.md).

Tracked machine records must retain their provenance and stated research or operational purpose. Reproducibility is assessed, not presumed or required as a condition for preserving unique evidence.

Keep the operator-facing research account current and preserve underlying evidence whenever practical regeneration has not been demonstrated. Storage thresholds trigger research storage review, never evidence loss or a halt to research. The [research checkpoint contract](operator-explanation-standard.md#research-checkpoint-and-handoff-completion) owns current-account updates; this procedure owns preservation.

The repository retains authored sources, protocols, evidence needed to understand and verify results, and independently necessary fixtures. Historical observations, corrections, failed hypotheses, inconclusive experiments and independent checks can justify retention without a current program consuming their rows. Bulk output may move to approved durable storage only after the preservation requirements below are satisfied. Ignored `.local-data/` is working storage, not a backup. A compact evidence receipt binds an untracked artifact by SHA-256 and records its byte and line counts, original source/protocol identity, claim boundary, reproduction status and retrieval instructions; it does not replace the artifact. Pure runtime build outputs remain a separate class: keep their canonical sources and generators and reconstruct the declared outputs during setup and deployment.

## Preservation from creation through closeout

At meaningful research checkpoints and before closeout, verify that required evidence remains present and accessible, and record its location, identity and reproduction status in the existing evidence or work record. The operator accepts the established ignored `.local-data/` owners as retained evidence storage for now, including for new evidence of unknown or expensive regeneration cost. A separate backup is not a prerequisite for research handoff, administrative closeout or PR publication while the evidence remains intact there. This is local retention, not independently backed-up preservation: Git pushes do not include ignored files, and machine failure remains an accepted risk. Do not claim remote preservation or verified backup without the corresponding evidence. Missing or damaged required evidence remains a readiness problem; a hash or summary cannot replace its payload.

“Easily regenerated” requires available source versions, inputs, dependencies, seeds or other necessary run conditions, a demonstrated reproduction against declared comparison criteria, and measured time/resource cost acceptable to the operator. A command alone is not evidence of practical regeneration. Distinguish reproducing the scientific result from recovering the exact historical bytes; volatile metadata and nondeterministic searches may prevent the latter. Unknown cost or reliability means preserve by default. Do not launch an expensive replay merely to classify storage without the required compute authority.

Retain evidence in its established Git or `.local-data/` owner; the absence of a separate archive does not require force-adding ignored payloads or creating a storage service. Do not omit intended tracked evidence from the publication candidate just to satisfy a size check. Preserve original observations and frozen independent reports. Update the current synthesis and append corrections or supersession explanations without overwriting historical evidence.

Before an approved relocation or deletion, inventory consumers and byte bindings. Unless the reproducible-output exception below applies, preserve the exact original in the approved durable destination, retrieve it and verify its hash, and record the destination, retrieval command, verification date/result and retention conditions. Update links and consumers and verify them before retiring the old location. An automatically expiring archive is not sufficient permanent preservation. Neither passing a size check nor a compact receipt authorizes removal; disposition needs explicit scoped authority. Periodic review checks continuing accessibility and recovery gaps without authorizing cleanup.

### Deleting verified reproducible output

Bulk derived output may be deleted without archiving its full payload when all of the following conditions are established in the existing evidence or work record:

- The output meets the demonstrated, measured definition of easily regenerated above, using retained source versions, inputs, dependencies and necessary run conditions. Preserve those prerequisites durably; a recipe that depends on the files being deleted is insufficient.
- The current research account retains the findings, limitations and consequential negative results. Retain the reproduction command, comparison criteria, known-case controls and reproduction receipt, including measured resource cost and the operator's acceptance of that cost.
- Inspection of scientific and operational consumers establishes that no original-observation, frozen-independent-reference, historical-correction or exact-byte obligation requires the payload. A repeatable scientific result does not establish that its original evidence is disposable. Required fixtures and active-job inputs remain available.
- The operator approves an exact deletion scope after reviewing the classification, measured payload size, expected reclaimable space and recovery method. Record the deleted identities and disposition, update affected consumers and links, and verify them. A failed reproduction or an unmet preservation obligation makes the affected output ineligible.

This exception permits retiring genuinely replaceable output rather than moving every payload into permanent archives. It does not authorize deletion by age, size, supersession or directory alone. Irreplaceable, expensive or uncertain evidence retains the archive-and-retrieval safeguard before destructive disposition; accepting local retention does not authorize removing its last valuable copy. Redundant working copies may be retired only after verifying the retained copy. Caches and intermediate files are eligible only when they contain no required evidence and serve no active job or consumer. Git history reclamation remains a separate operation.

## Recurring retention review

[OPS-033](../priorities/aaa-operations/work-queue.md#ops-033--periodic-retention-review) owns a monthly report-only lifecycle review, first due October 14, 2026, then on the 14th at 09:00 app-local time. The active task heartbeat `architrino-retention-review`, titled “Architrino retention review”, schedules it. Monthly sampling is an initial operating choice; reassess burden and coverage after three completed passes. This complements the [periodic document reviews](periodic-document-review.md): those assess correctness and current status; retention review assesses continued purpose, reproduction and preservation obligations.

Read the [September 8 baseline](../priorities/development-process-review/analysis/retention-health-review.md) and live subject owners before each pass. Run the retention validator under the current compute procedure and measure repository/local-output allocation using named commands such as `du` and `git count-objects -vH`, stating scope, date and measurement limits. Budget compliance does not establish backup recovery or justify deletion. Inventory artifact families and maintain a coverage cursor; inspect three bounded families per pass: one changed or growing, one completed or superseded, and one rotating older family. If a class has no eligible family, record that fact and select another uncovered family. Include related documents and compact receipts when tracing an artifact's purpose; do not convert this into prose trimming or a duplicate scientific review.

For each selected family, identify current consumers, ownership, reproduction commands and measured cost when available, unique evidence, superseded copies, compact receipt bindings and archive/retrieval evidence. Propose keep/archive/rebuild/delete dispositions with reasons and exact paths, distinguishing verified reproducible output from protected evidence and redundant copies. Report measured payload size and expected reclaimable space with filesystem and Git-history limitations; do not count an archive move as reducing total retained storage. Label unavailable consumers, unmeasured costs and untested recovery honestly. A small read-only retrieval check may verify an existing accessible archive, but bulk retrieval, recomputation, new storage services or copying sensitive material requires its own scope. Preserve source identities, claim grades and historical evidence.

The circular-root pilot family's detailed lifecycle review remains deferred until the operator explicitly selects a research milestone, as recorded in the baseline. Broad allocation inventory may include it; record the deferral without reactivating its consumer/disposition investigation. This recurring review authorizes reporting and control-record maintenance only, not artifact deletion, movement, compaction, Git garbage collection/history rewriting, budget increases, authored regeneration or publication.

Record each pass under OPS-033 in the operations work log: exact coverage, commands/results and limits, comparison with prior measurements, proposed dispositions and receiving owners, recovery gaps, deferred/uncovered families, coverage cursor and next due date. Reuse existing subject records instead of duplicating evidence. Merge overdue work into one current review; a missing measurement is not a clean result. Record unchanged outcomes durably and notify only meaningful findings, failures or required decisions. A review completes only its recorded scope; recommended cleanup remains a separate decision.

## Runtime Build Contract

[`generated-runtime-assets.json`](../../scripts/config/generated-runtime-assets.json) declares the generated Borg catalogue and certified-budget identity modules and record family, the equation-mapping corpus registry, the full-corpus source index and the internal reference surface. The Borg catalogue and budget identity modules are prepared from their authored JSON before later consumers, preserving synchronous runtime imports. Budget identities retain their authored allocation applicability; projection generation does not accept a scientific budget. Output paths remain stable for app consumers. Tracking is forbidden, including forced additions; no runtime-transition exception remains. Every Borg record comes from the existing generator's canonical target list; this is not a per-example cleanup.

Run `node scripts/prepare-runtime-assets.mjs --write` to create or refresh only those runtime outputs, and `node scripts/prepare-runtime-assets.mjs --check` to verify them without writing. Local server startup, the PR gate, and Content Integrity prepare them automatically. Explicit test runs through `npm test -- <files>` or `node --test <files>` require preparation only when the selected tests consume runtime outputs that are absent or stale. The full-corpus MCP launcher prepares its index before freezing the startup snapshot; the request loop remains read-only. The equation generator's artifact-only `--build` mode rejects missing canonical links rather than modifying Markdown; its explicit source-repair `--write` mode remains separately authorized.

Pages validates PRs through [`pages.yml`](../../.github/workflows/pages.yml) and publishes main through [`pages-deploy.yml`](../../.github/workflows/pages-deploy.yml). Both call the same [build action](../../.github/actions/build-pages/action.yml), which uses [`build-static-site.mjs`](../../scripts/build-static-site.mjs) to produce the generated deployment directory. Preserve the existing custom-domain and HTTPS settings. The [publishing decision and first live acceptance](../architectural-decisions/pages-generated-runtime-build.md) record why the generated files no longer belong in Git.

The effect of that contract is that a fresh clone builds what it needs before it can test or serve anything. Git keeps the recipe; the local server and the website receive the cooked output. The automatic rebuild covers only the runtime outputs declared as ignored — not tracked textbook documents, iOS packages, or children's-book exports, which are authored or on-demand artifacts and must be produced deliberately.

## Actions publishing and recovery

GitHub builds the website's data from source and publishes it only after that build passes. Require the exact-state local PR gate and both remote checks: Content Integrity and the Pages build. The Pages build includes a source-only reconstruction test that starts without any declared runtime output, then requires every Borg record byte digest to match both the tracked registry and assembly-view collection before the upload step. PR builds never deploy. Post-merge Pages acceptance remains a deployment obligation; it does not prevent ordinary development from resuming after the publication runner verifies cleanup and releases the checkout. After a publishing-path change merges, require a successful deployment tied to the expected `main` commit, verify HTTPS responses and byte hashes for every path enumerated by `runtimeAssetPaths()` against that run's deployment artifact, and exercise data loading in the homepage, Borg, and Equation Mapping. A passing homepage alone is insufficient; record the run URL, deployed commit, verification counts, and app checks in the PR evidence. Hash agreement checks packaging fidelity, not scientific correctness.

The deployment job requires the repository Actions variable `ARCHITRINO_PAGES_DEPLOY_ENABLED` to equal `true`, `main`, a push or manual-dispatch event, and a successful build. A preflight read of the Pages API must return `build_type: workflow` before either configure or deploy actions execute; legacy mode, missing metadata, and API errors fail closed. Missing variables evaluate to empty strings in GitHub Actions and therefore leave deployment disabled. See [GitHub's variables context](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/reference/workflows-and-actions/contexts#vars-context). Builds and tests remain enabled regardless of this switch. Leave the repository variable enabled for ordinary automatic deployments from `main`.

To pause publishing during recovery, set the repository variable to `false` and cancel or wait for in-flight Pages deployments; changing the variable does not cancel an already-running deployment. Repair the source or build path through an explicitly approved PR, validate it, then re-enable publishing and start a fresh run on the verified `main` commit. If recovery requires an earlier source state, inspect the repository branches and commits and select that source explicitly; do not predeclare or automate a target. Branch-mode Pages lacks the generated runtime files and is not a substitute for the verified workflow. Restoring legacy publisher inputs requires an explicitly approved repair PR. Never force-add ignored outputs or rewrite repository history as a routine repair. Changing a setting cannot substitute for missing files: a repair has to restore a working build.

The static builder rejects a deployment payload above 1,000,000,000 bytes before copying publication files, matching the [documented Pages site-size limit](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/github-pages-limits). A successful reconstruction test alone is not deployment acceptance. If the complete repository payload exceeds this budget, determine and approve the public deployment scope before publishing; do not silently drop existing public downloads or remove web-used assets from an otherwise development-only tree.

The operator approved these deployment-only exclusions: PowerPoint presentation, template, and slide-show originals by extension; `reference/design/` production material; `reference/op/agent-chats/` conversation records and accompanying files (approved October 9, 2026); `apps/ios/` development files except `apps/ios/ArchitrinoReader/ArchitrinoReader/ReaderAssets/katex/`; and unused images in `content/assets/images/`. All excluded files remain tracked and unchanged in the repository. PDF exports and web-referenced artwork remain published. The shared KaTeX JavaScript, CSS, fonts, and license retain their existing web URLs and bytes. The source-only integration test verifies the exclusions, repository-original preservation, actual image links, and shared KaTeX preservation in the build output.

[`pages-image-assets.mjs`](../../scripts/pages-image-assets.mjs) selects image-library files from the root web shells, application code and styles, scenes, reader Markdown, generated reading copies, and their supported dependencies. Linked priority Markdown follows the reader's supported namespace. Catalog entries, `usedBy` annotations, tests, code examples, and unlinked production documents are not proof of website use. Direct download and attribution links count as use, not only embedded images. SVG dependencies are followed. Literal runtime directory prefixes and template prefixes conservatively retain matching images; dynamically selected images must have literal paths or a complete image-library prefix in a web consumer. Arbitrarily assembled paths cannot be inferred by this static scan. Missing image references and undeclared runtime image-catalog consumers fail the build. Future catalog-driven image galleries require an explicit selection policy before deployment.

The deployment copy of `images.json` contains only entries for included images, preserving each retained entry's provenance and attribution without editing the full repository catalog. Image bytes are never transformed by the Pages builder. New literal references automatically include their images on the next build; removing the last website reference omits the image from Pages without deleting it from Git. For agreed changes to this behavior, `npm test -- tests/pages-image-assets.test.js tests/runtime-asset-build.test.js` checks selection and packaging fixtures. The Pages workflow uses `node scripts/check-pages-build.mjs --out .tmp/site` to build once from its source checkout and verify that payload; it does not repeat reconstruction inside a fresh-checkout test.

## Storage review thresholds and blocking checks

[`validate-machine-artifact-retention.mjs`](../../scripts/validate-machine-artifact-retention.mjs) checks JSON, JSONL, NDJSON, CSV, and TSV in both the staged index and the working tree, including nonignored new files. The ordinary file review thresholds in [`machine-artifact-retention-registry.v1.json`](machine-artifact-retention-registry.v1.json) are:

- at least `100000` physical lines; or
- at least `10485760` bytes.

Machine records under a `reference/priorities/**/evidence/` directory use the earlier review threshold of `25000` physical lines or `1048576` bytes. Their path does not establish reproducibility, scientific value or disposability.

The same `100000`-line or `10485760`-byte threshold applies to an entire machine-file collection. Asset and generated collections are grouped by family, evidence by its owning evidence directory (including nested priority lanes), and other files by directory; nesting below an evidence directory does not bypass collection review. Explicit collection thresholds remain recorded in the registry. The iOS package remains on demand and is not rebuilt by this workflow.

Across the branch, `100000` added machine-file lines or `10485760` bytes of positive file-size growth triggers review. Comparison uses the merge base with `origin/main` (or an explicit `--base`); a missing base fails closed. Deletions do not offset additions. The index is read directly, so shrinking an unstaged file cannot hide a large staged payload.

For machine records under `reference/priorities/`, file and collection size findings are advisory `REVIEW` warnings, not publication failures. This conservative routing also covers research inputs outside `evidence/`; it does not assign scientific authority. Branch totals including these records are advisory, while positive growth outside that subtree is measured separately and retains its existing blocking budget. Non-research file/collection limits, missing registered records, malformed registry/audit inputs, unavailable comparison bases, and forbidden tracked runtime outputs remain blocking. Genuine deployment limits remain separate. Do not relocate a file merely to change checker classification; assess evidence outside the normal priority owner explicitly.

Research warnings require an assessment recorded with the existing research/publication record, not a new registry entry solely to silence the warning. Preserve evidence pending that review; size alone neither blocks publication nor authorizes cleanup. Registry entries name an owner, retention reason, consumers (including human review or historical verification), and why a compact alternative is insufficient. A regeneration command field may explicitly state that reproduction is unavailable or unverified; it must not invent a command for unique evidence. Threshold changes require an explicit storage decision. Passing this checker establishes only its structural and storage checks, not current research accounts, practical reproduction, durable backup or scientific correctness.

## Version and evidence handling

- Supersession alone does not justify removing full outputs. Preserve originals supporting corrections, failed hypotheses, independent verification or costly experiments; any disposition follows the preservation requirements above.
- Raw results may remain tracked for present or historical research verification even without a current runtime consumer. Compact only when retained evidence and a verified archive preserve the actual obligation, or the [verified reproducible-output exception](#deleting-verified-reproducible-output) is satisfied, under explicit authority.
- Tests should consume the smallest fixture that exercises their contract. A test that reads only summary values does not itself justify retaining unrelated raw ledgers, but independent research or historical preservation obligations may do so.
- A producer that records runtime, timestamps, host paths, or other volatile metadata must not claim byte-identical regeneration. Its receipt should identify the stable comparison fields.
- Moving a raw record out of Git must not silently upgrade or weaken its scientific authority. Preserve its original claim boundary and falsifier. Storage form and evidence grade are independent: compacting a diagnostic neither validates nor invalidates its scientific conclusion.

- Ignored local storage is accepted retained evidence storage, not a durable backup. Separate backup verification is not a checkpoint or publication prerequisite; the relocation/deletion safeguards above still apply.
- Removing a payload from the tracked tip prevents future generated churn; it does not purge earlier Git objects or reclaim all historical GitHub storage. History rewriting is a separate, explicitly authorized operation and is not part of runtime preparation.
