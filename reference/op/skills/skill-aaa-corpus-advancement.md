# Corpus Convergence and Source Mining

This skill selects the live procedure for actively reconciling the corpus and developing mathematical material from corpus, priority, or source signals.

1. Follow the repository [startup instructions](../../../AGENTS.md).
2. Read [convergence-campaign.md](../../../reference/office-of-research/cto/prompts/convergence-campaign.md) and select the mode supported by the request, including its audit/report mode when edits are excluded.
3. When source mining is requested, also read [source-mining-best-practice.md](../../../reference/op/source-mining-best-practice.md).
4. Follow those owners for scope, claim discipline, edits, and validation, and their linked standards for responses and exposition.

An external link alone does not select source mining. A request to judge, apply, or verify supplied review feedback belongs to [the review workflows](skill-corpus-review-workflow.md); open-ended discussion and provisional idea capture belong to [research exploration](skill-research-exploration.md). A specifically requested convergence exploration remains governed by the convergence procedure.
