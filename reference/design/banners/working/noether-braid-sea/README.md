# Noether Braid Sea Banner

This working package owns the first Noether Braid banner family.

- Working master: `noether-braid-youtube-banner-sea-2048x1152.png`
- Deployed directory: `../../../../../content/assets/images/brand/banners/noether-braid-sea/`
- Platform-size manifest: `../../../logo-exports/noether-braid-ribbon-social-platforms/manifest.json`
- Builder: `../../../../../scripts/art/build-noether-braid-banner-assets.py`
- Review contact sheet: `noether-braid-sea-platform-contact-sheet.jpg`

The master is a dense field of small Noether Braid marks on the project purple sea background. Use it when the surface needs a texture-like brand field rather than a title card.

## Design Review

Strengths:

- The repeated Noether Braid marks create a recognizable project-owned texture.
- The purple field works across wide and tall platform crops.
- The image stays usable when heavily cropped because it has no single fragile focal point.

Limits:

- It reads as a background pattern, not a finished brand header.
- The repeated marks compete with any overlaid title, QR code, or profile avatar.
- Very shallow crops reduce the braid marks to a busy stripe.

Retired deployed crops:

- LinkedIn company cover, LinkedIn profile background, and Reddit small/medium/large community banners were removed because those crops got dark in the middle instead of preserving the darker-corner tint.

## Better Candidate Directions

1. Focal ribbon over quiet sea: keep the small Noether Braid field as a background, but add one large softened Noether Braid ribbon mark near the safe center and fade the surrounding pattern.
2. Horizon field: keep the purple sea, reduce the braid density, and create a darker top/bottom falloff so platform chrome and profile avatars do not fight the artwork.
3. Logo-and-QR campaign banner: use the deployed logo-plus-QR asset as a controlled focal group over a quieter Noether Braid field for campaign, flyer, and support-page sharing.
