The master is a dense field of small Noether Braid marks on the project purple sea background. Use it when the surface needs a texture-like brand field rather than a title card.

## Design Review

Strengths:

- The repeated Noether Braid marks create a recognizable project-owned texture.
- The purple field works across wide and tall platform crops.
- The image stays usable when heavily cropped because it has no single fragile focal point.

Limits:

- It reads as a background pattern, not a finished brand header.
- The repeated marks compete with any overlaid title, QR code, or profile avatar.
- Very shallow crops reduce the braid marks to a busy stripe.

Retired deployed crops:

- LinkedIn company cover, LinkedIn profile background, and Reddit small/medium/large community banners were removed because those crops got dark in the middle instead of preserving the darker-corner tint.
