# Noether Braid QR Logo Composites

This package contains simple QR-code plus Noether Braid logo composites for print, flyer, and social placement tests.

- QR source: `../architrino-qr-code/qr.png`
- Logo source: `../../../../content/assets/images/brand/noether-braid-ribbon-app-icon-1024.png`
- QR background: `#ece4f2`, the selected 75% standard-purple tint
- Horizontal exports are `1200 x 600` PNG files.
- Vertical exports are `600 x 1200` PNG files.
- The logo and QR artwork abut with no divider line and no backing card.
- Reader-facing landscape copy: `../../../../content/assets/images/brand/architrino-logo-qr-landscape.png`
