### 2026-09-06 — Symbols and source moved into a right rail

- Replaced the floating `Symbols & source` panel, and the lozenge beside the equation title that opened it, with a collapsible right rail. The lozenge sat to the right of the title inside the same centring box, so the title read as skewed left; the title now carries the title alone.
- The rail is a third grid column mirroring the equations rail, not an overlay: `grid-template-columns: var(--index-width) minmax(0, 1fr) var(--reference-width)`, collapsing to 72 px. The equation therefore re-centres in the remaining canvas instead of sitting behind a panel. A `--rail-width` custom property is the sum of both rails, and everything sized against the canvas subtracts it.
- The rail carries its own collapse control, so source context stays reachable on an equation that defines no symbols — the case that would have been unreachable had the lozenge simply been deleted. Pressing a symbol beneath the equation expands the rail, marks that definition active, and scrolls it into view. The collapsed state persists with the other display settings.
- Two consequences of the new column, both found by measurement rather than by reading: the control strip is positioned against the viewport's top-right, which is now the rail's corner, and it painted over the rail; and the settings panel, anchored inside the canvas column, drifted 340 px left of the button that opens it, so the panel layer is now anchored to the viewport instead.
- Resolved the strip-over-rail collision by hanging the rail below the strip — `margin-top: var(--panel-top)` with a matching height reduction — rather than reserving the band inside the rail's header, which left the strip's buttons sitting on the rail's own surface, or shifting the strip left, which would move shared chrome for one app only. The strip now sits on the canvas here exactly as it does on every other page. Measured at 1440x900 and 390x844: rail top 56 px against a strip bottom of 48 px and 44 px respectively, in both collapsed and expanded states, with the collapse control the topmost element at its own centre.
- In the compact layout neither rail is a column, so both now contribute stage padding when collapsed, and the equation shell and overlay layer are sized against the stage's content box rather than the viewport. The left strip had the same overlap before this change; it is fixed here because the same rule covers both sides.
- Browser verification at 1440x900 measured three columns of 276 / 1092 / 72 px collapsed and 276 / 824 / 340 px expanded, the equation clearing the rail in both, the collapse control reachable rather than behind the settings button, a symbol chip expanding the rail with its definition active and in view, and the settings panel flush with its button. At 390x844 with both rails collapsed the equation spanned 87 to 309 px, clearing the 72 px left strip and the 318 px right strip.
