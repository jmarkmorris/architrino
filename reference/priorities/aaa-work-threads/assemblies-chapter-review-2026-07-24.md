## Errors and internal inconsistencies

**E1. `electron.md` inverts the shielding-coherence ordering in prose.** Lines ~5 and ~15 say the electron is "built from the **lowest shielding-coherence** version" / "in its lowest shielding-coherence class." The electron's class is $(1,1,1)$ — *full* coherence, the *highest*-coherence class; Gen III $(1,0,0)$ is the lowest. The intended meaning is clearly "lowest-excitation, fully shielded." As written it asserts the opposite of the generation mechanism in `quantum-number-mapping.md` and `muon-tau.md`. Fix wording in both places.

**E2. Legacy H/M/L axis labels survive in `gluons.md` and `weak-mixing-angle.md`.** `gluons.md` uses $|u_H\rangle,|u_M\rangle,|u_L\rangle$, sector pairs $(HM),(HL),(ML)$ (~33–35, 70, 123), and "Red: H-exceptional" (~182); `weak-mixing-angle.md` (~46) defines $\mathcal{F}_{\text{core}}=\{\hat{\mathbf e}_H,\hat{\mathbf e}_M,\hat{\mathbf e}_L\}$. Everywhere else the chapter uses persistently indexed axes $(1,2,3)$ and repeatedly states that indices carry no radius/role meaning ("These source-record roles do not encode a radius order," `quarks.md` ~28). H/M/L is precisely the retired High/Middle/Low radius-order naming. Sweep both files to $(1,2,3)$; keep $H_1,H_2$ only for the diagonal Gell-Mann generators, where the collision with the axis label makes the current text actively confusing (`gluons.md` ~124 uses both meanings of "H" in one paragraph).

**E3. Broken sentence in `muon-tau.md` (~29–41).** "The observer-level channels $$\mu^-\to\dots$$ and $$\tau^-\to\dots$$ Here $\ell\in\{e,\mu\}$…" — the opening clause never gets a verb before the next sentence starts. Rewrite: "The observer-level channels are … and …, where $\ell\in\{e,\mu\}$ in the tau channel."

**E4. Symbol collision on $M_0$ inside `particle-masses.md`.** Three distinct meanings: $M_0(A)$ the rest/internal branch invariant (~68), $M_0^{\mathrm{src}}(A)$ the quotient-visible exposed source (~300), and $M_0\sim\hbar_{\mathrm{act}}c_f/(\kappa\varepsilon^2)$ the universal substrate mass *unit* (~552). The first and third are flatly incompatible uses of the same glyph in the same chapter. Rename the unit ($M_\star$ or $M_{\mathrm{unit}}$). Same section also silently switches the polarity unit from $\epsilon$ to $\varepsilon$.

**E5. Referent-pending number asserted for the neutrino ζ.** `particle-masses.md` Critical Unknowns (~842) writes "nearly perfect shielding ($\zeta\sim10^{-12}$)" for the neutrino. Under `neutrinos.md` §Referent Status, every quantity defined as a residual about the photon lock is referent-pending: "no property of $\delta\Omega_{\mathrm{bin}}$ — its magnitude, its spectrum… — can be measured or argued about before a lock closes." A numeric ζ for the near-photon pair is exactly such a magnitude. Either mark it as an illustrative order-of-magnitude target with the referent-pending flag, or remove the number.

**E6. Z-corridor payload contradiction in `electroweak-bosons.md`.** The corridor provenance table (~510) says the neutral current imports "no charged axial inventory… No net charge payload," but the summary table (~613) lists the Z payload as "Neutral ($3\epsilon_+ + 3\epsilon_-$)," which reads as a carried balanced six-unit inventory. Change the summary row to "Neutral (no axial payload)" or gloss what the $3\epsilon_+ + 3\epsilon_-$ notation means for a corridor that imports nothing.

**E7. Delta lifetime inconsistent within `mesons.md`.** Prose (~124, ~159) says the Delta dissociates in $\sim10^{-24}$ s; the cheat sheet (~181) says $\Gamma\approx120$ MeV ($\sim10^{-23}$ s). $\hbar/\Gamma\approx5.5\times10^{-24}$ s, so both roundings are defensible alone but they disagree with each other, and the ρ ($\Gamma\approx150$ MeV, $\tau\approx4.4\times10^{-24}$ s) is listed as $\sim10^{-24}$ s — a faster label for a nearly identical lifetime. Use $\sim5\times10^{-24}$ s for both.

**E8. `weak-mixing-angle.md` electron-limit claim vs its own moment analysis.** ~56 says for a symmetric lepton axial layer "the axial moment record is isotropic." For six same-polarity charges on three orthogonal axes, $M_{ij}$ is indeed proportional to the identity and $d_i=0$ — isotropy holds for the *joint moment record defined in the file*, but only because the sites are assumed at ideal antipodal positions on orthogonal axes. The later §Minimal Geometric Parameterization correctly says the misalignment diagnostic belongs to the *relaxed* equilibrium. Add one clause noting the electron isotropy statement is also a relaxed-record claim, not an ideal-position identity, or the $\alpha=0$ assignment inherits the same idealization it warns against at ~117–123.

## Clarifications

**C23. The "two-same + one-different" rule is a theorem for charged quarks, not a postulate — say so.** `color-charge-su3.md` (~170–172) presents the admissibility rule as a dynamical-stability assumption and "forbids" three-different configurations with the example "1 positive dyad, 2 negative dyad, 3 mixed dyad." But that example sums to $3\epsilon_+,3\epsilon_-$ — a *neutral* inventory, not a down-type quark. Check the arithmetic: for the up-type inventory $5\epsilon_+,1\epsilon_-$ the only dyad decomposition is {two positive, one mixed}; for the down-type $4\epsilon_-,2\epsilon_+$ the only decompositions are Family I and Family II; a three-different pattern (one positive dyad, one negative dyad, one mixed) forces the neutral $3{:}3$ inventory. So for the charged quark inventories the rule is *forced by counting* — the dynamical exclusion is needed only in the neutral $3{:}3$ sector. Restating this upgrades a postulate to a two-line lemma and simultaneously identifies the neutral sector as the natural home of the excluded three-different transients (which connects to the `quantum-number-mapping.md` $3\epsilon_+,3\epsilon_-$ row's "not automatically a stable neutrino axial layer" caveat).

**C24. Undefined "Gate 1" in `gauge-structure-emergence.md`** (~453). Define or link the gate taxonomy where "Gate 1-level deviation" lives; currently the falsification section's quantitative teeth reference an unnamed scale.

**C25. Stating the sign and size makes the falsifiability concrete: the dressing derivation must produce a small negative shift, not just "a correction."

(b) `electron.md` detection map (~44): $\Theta_{\mathrm{atom}}$ appears in the conditioning but is never related to the four listed arguments — one clause ("$\Theta_{\mathrm{atom}}$ bundles $\mathcal A_{\mathrm{nuc}},\theta^{(\ell)}_{\mathrm{sea}},\dots$") fixes it. ## Recap (plain language)

This chapter maps the Standard Model's particle zoo onto the theory's building blocks. The core moves all check out arithmetically: charges come in sixths because stable particles carry six polarity slots; color is "which of three axes is the odd one out"; generations are how many shielding layers remain; and the anomaly sums — the Standard Model's most delicate consistency conditions — were recomputed here from the chapter's own tables and all come out exactly zero, as they must. The biggest problem is honesty synchronization, not physics: the corpus recently *measured* that the proposed photon structure does not hold together as declared, and only the neutrino file says so — the photon's own file and four others still describe it as if nothing happened. Second, the electroweak section starts from an action integral that the dynamics canon has explicitly declined to promote (varying it would require knowing the future). Third, two files still use old High/Middle/Low axis names that the rest of the corpus retired in favor of neutral indices. Alongside the fixes, the review found a few genuine upgrades hiding in the text: the "two same + one different" color rule is actually *provable by counting* for charged quarks, every generation-changing decay must trade exactly one binary with the surrounding sea (a rule worth naming), and the theory should be asked why the strong force's medium response amplifies at short distance while the electric one weakens — a sharp qualitative test no file currently owns.

Closure goal: synchronize the photon-lock referent status across the five unmarked files, re-anchor the electroweak bridge off the unpromoted action, and bank the counting-lemma and ΔN-scaffold rules as named chapter content.
