#### The side effect, and a correction to an earlier attribution

The layout pass rewrote 4,478 links. Rewriting a link changes the file's bytes, and several evidence and methodology documents are bound elsewhere by a pinned SHA-256. Those pins break on a link edit exactly as they would on a substantive edit, because a content hash cannot distinguish the two. This consequence was not anticipated when the pass was designed, and it is the significant residual risk of the campaign.

Measured: the reorg commit `51ee4b84f` content-modified 361 files beyond its 237 renames, and the Stage B commit `965c1a3b4` content-modified 81. Two pins were traced to those commits and repaired here by first verifying the substance and only then refreshing the digest: the Borg scientific-status projection, whose 28 adjudication relations were all confirmed present in the current owner text, and the Borg Platonic relationship assignments, whose owner document differed by exactly one link path. A third, the analytical methodology coverage contract in `src/prescribed-path-analysis/analytical-measure-coverage.v2.json`, was **not** repaired: it pins `content/markdown/aaa/noether-braid/braid-analysis-methodology.md`, that document was last changed by `c973402b9` rather than by this campaign, and its gate demands an explicit analytical coverage impact review rather than a digest refresh.

Correction to the 2026-09-05 Stage A entry and to statements made in the operator thread: `51ee4b84f`, whose message reads "Converge corpus documents to canonical academic style", is the commit that carried this layout pass, not an unrelated corpus-style commit. An earlier attribution of the Platonic pin's staleness to operator corpus work was wrong; that pin broke in the layout pass. The projection and methodology pins remain correctly attributed to `c973402b9`.
