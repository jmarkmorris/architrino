# Corpus Dragnet Brainstorming

## Candidate Passes

- Duplicate and near-duplicate concept map across published and internal material, preserving independent evidence and claim grades.
- Terminology and canonical-name drift sweep.
- Published-to-internal routing map: published assertions lacking an obvious supporting owner, and internal results lacking a clearly appropriate future destination.
- Priority, brainstorming, and work-queue overlap map.
- Stale link and generated-versus-canonical-source audit.

## Open Design Questions

- Which corpus roots should the first bounded scan include, and which archives should remain excluded?
- What confidence thresholds prevent vague semantic resemblance from entering the durable ledger?
- When, if ever, should a finding propose a new priority lane rather than routing to an existing owner?
