# Scientific Explanation Without Claim Drift

## 1. Readability and preservation are different obligations

A scientific explanation must help a reader understand both the argument and its limits. Replacing a cautious sentence with a decisive one may improve its apparent fluency while changing what it asserts. Preserving every equation does not prevent that change: the surrounding prose may turn a conditional result into a universal one, confuse a diagnostic with a physical response, or identify a proposed mechanism with a recovered observation. Conversely, a smoothly rewritten paragraph can preserve an existing mathematical error. Readability, fidelity and correctness therefore require different checks.

The local Corpus Explanation Rewrite record contains two related activities. Its [conversion ledger](evidence/conversion-ledger.md) records conversion of an existing online corpus to an explanatory standard while preserving its scientific content. Its [substantive review record](work-queue.md) identifies and corrects particular mathematical, conceptual and source-scope defects under separate acceptance. A rule that forbids changing an equation during conversion is compatible with a later authorized correction to that equation. The two operations have different purposes and must retain different records.

The intended reader can arrive at any page without prior knowledge of either Architrino theory or the established physics it seeks to recover. A term introduced elsewhere therefore needs enough local explanation to make the current argument intelligible, together with a route to its full treatment. An appeal to established physics must explain what that comparison supplies. It cannot import a higher-level law into the primitive theory merely by naming it. Within $\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}$, substrate ontology, conditional derivation, observer-level recovery, measurement and hypothesis remain distinct.

This audience rule does not require writing every passage twice. A technical paragraph followed by a simpler restatement can usually become one paragraph in which the symbols, reasoning and interpretation are explained together. Removing the restatement without integrating its information defeats that purpose. Some explanations belong before a list because they explain why the list exists; others contain several ideas that should be distributed among their actual subjects. The relevant invariant is explanatory substance, not the position of a label.

An edition records which explanatory standard a document was assessed against. It does not establish that the document satisfies a later standard, has received independent scientific review, or is free from error. The retained conversion ledger records 199 documents at edition 1.0 in September 2026. A separate assurance program used a denominator of 190 after excluding nine already independently reviewed Foundations documents; its recorded fourteen-document first packet left 176. Later substantive Foundations review used its own nine-document denominator and reached seven accepted and verified chapter corrections. Those are source-time descriptions of different activities, not interchangeable completion percentages or a fresh census of the present corpus.

The preservation contract covers claims, grades, falsifiers, equations, internal links and equation-viewer anchors. It also preserves the paragraph structure needed by a viewer that associates a link with the equation above it. Mechanical equality can establish that a formula or anchor survived; it cannot establish that the formula is true or the prose adequately explains it. Length was expressly removed as a conversion-quality measure. The relevant question is whether the reader can follow the preserved argument, not whether an explanation grew or shrank.

## 2. A reader's navigation problem is not necessarily a definition problem

### 2.1. Reachable chapters and discoverable meanings

The initial vocabulary audit was interpreted too broadly. Finding a document that uses foundational terms without linking directly to Foundations does not show that its reader has no navigation route. The retained application audit described a persistent table-of-contents route and page navigation. Its narrower problem was discoverable term lookup: scene search indexed metadata rather than document bodies, the glossary required knowledge of its existence, and two important names were absent from the glossary's first column.

A first-column audit asks a different question from a full-text search. A definition of a causal root buried inside another row does not make “Causal Root” a term a reader can scan for. The recorded repair added first-class entries for Wake, Causal Root, Complete State / Universe State, Polarity, Physical Observer and Worldline. Their meanings retained their source layers: a causal root is a delayed-interaction record, not an acceleration or stability result; a Physical Observer is an access role; and a complete state is not the constrained projection available to that observer.

The selected access route was a persistent Glossary action in the optional document-entry position after Search. The source acknowledges the cost in toolbar density rather than describing another permanent control as free. Heading links retained the intended section, and closing a document-only reading scene returned to useful history or Home instead of revealing an empty canvas. A document overlay on a populated scene could still close in place. These are reported interface results, not results newly tested by this manuscript.

A persistent lookup route does not replace every local clue. A passage may depend immediately on understanding a causal-root ledger, polarity or a causal-wake formula. In that case an in-place explanation serves the argument better than asking the reader to leave it. The recorded selective intervention covered two already-converted opening passages. It was not a mandate to add a link at every occurrence of every term.

### 2.2. A successful negative result for automatic decoration

The proposed render-time decorator would have linked a term's first occurrence automatically. Its literal matching prototype excluded headings, tables, fenced and inline code, mathematics and authored links. It matched exact forms without plural or possessive expansion, preferred longer labels, and excluded matches inside hyphenated or alphanumeric compounds. These rules address syntax and overlapping strings; they do not decide what a word means in context.

The [retained prototype report](evidence/crw-002-term-lookup-result.md) parsed 113 usable glossary labels. In twelve documents chosen at evenly spaced positions in a sorted 199-document list, it found 143 candidate first-occurrence decorations spanning 49 labels. This measured the reach of that rule, not the correctness of those links. A separate ambiguity-stratified test read thirty contexts, five for each of six deliberately difficult words:

| Term | Eligible source contexts | Contexts inspected | False links |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| assembly | 1,496 | 5 | 0 |
| binary | 463 | 5 | 2 |
| wake | 945 | 5 | 0 |
| field | 567 | 5 | 5 |
| reaction | 261 | 5 | 0 |
| background | 273 | 5 | 3 |
| Total inspected | — | 30 | 10 |

The ten false links were one third of the inspected contexts, reported as 33.3%. “Binary outcomes” and “binary completeness ratios” did not identify a binary assembly. Academic fields, effective fields and reduced continuum variables did not all carry the glossary's substrate meaning. Cosmological background and background subtraction were not a fixed ontological background. Longest-match ordering cannot resolve these distinctions. Exact-form matching also misses valid inflections, so its omissions do not establish safe extensibility.

This sample was deliberately stratified for ambiguity. Its result supports declining that literal decorator; it is not an unbiased estimate of the error rate across every corpus word or of every possible language model. The retained decision declined autonomous render-time linking. A possible offline, context-aware assistant would propose a particular link, no link or a terminology correction for human review. Its benefit, benchmark and error threshold remain unmeasured and unaccepted for implementation. Rejecting the tested design converted uncertainty into a useful boundary without requiring a replacement feature.

### 2.3. Noncomparable counts cannot measure progress

The original 70-document vocabulary result lacked a preserved term list, parser, command and file list. Its exact value could not be reproduced from the retained repository evidence. A later scan used an explicit 149-document domain outside the reference and Foundations sections, a stated ten-term list and matching exclusions, and returned 113 candidates with no authored Foundations link. The two numbers do not show an increase from 70 to 113: the instruments and selected populations are not established as identical.

The same distinction applies to validation counts. A test of 23 registered equation links does not certify every viewer anchor in a corpus containing thousands of links. A scan that distinguishes isolated source lines from complete Markdown paragraphs can find defects missed by a line-only check. The record reports 661 viewer links that needed paragraph separators, including 23 in quotations, without changing their equations or anchor identities. These are statements about the recorded instruments and versions. They do not license a new claim about current generated files or current runtime behavior.

## 3. A claim must retain the information on which it depends

### 3.1. The exact factorization test

Many apparent explanatory improvements conceal an information-loss assumption. Suppose a complete state is $H$, a reduced description is $C(H)$ and a selected observable is $O(H)$. The assertion that the observable can be computed entirely from the reduced description means that a map $f$ exists with $O=f\circ C$. The necessary and sufficient condition on the realized reduced states is

$$
C(H_1)=C(H_2)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad O(H_1)=O(H_2).
$$

Necessity follows by applying $f$ to equal inputs. Conversely, define $f(C(H))=O(H)$; the implication makes that definition independent of which complete state represents the reduced value. A map that retains an apparatus's smooth surroundings but discards its recorded bit need not determine that bit. Two abstract states $(b,z)$ with the same retained $z$ and different $b$ demonstrate the logical gap. They are not a pair of exhibited physical histories.

If an approximate map satisfies $\|O(H)-f(C(H))\|\leq\eta$ for each admitted history, equal reduced inputs must have outputs separated by at most $2\eta$. That follows from two applications of the triangle inequality. An output separation above $2\eta$ falsifies this uniform approximation. A claim merely to upper-bound a residual has a different test: unequal residuals do not refute it unless one exceeds its stated bound.

Several measurements can share one physical history without sharing one scalar summary or one identical response map. Clock duration, spatial length and signal arrival time ask different questions and can even have different units. Their common origin becomes meaningful through derived, jointly consistent measurement maps. Independently adjusting one coefficient for each channel can fit agreement without deriving that origin.

### 3.2. A moment is not a physical response law

Let a declared normalized average of unit framing directions define

$$
S=\langle\hat{\mathbf n}\hat{\mathbf n}^{\mathsf T}\rangle,
\qquad Q=S-I/3.
$$

The tensor $Q$ is symmetric and trace-free. For a unit probe direction $\mathbf e$, substitution gives

$$
\left\langle(\mathbf e\cdot\hat{\mathbf n})^2\right\rangle
=\frac13+\mathbf e^{\mathsf T}Q\mathbf e.
$$

Thus $Q=0$ means isotropy of this second directional moment. It does not mean that every directional statistic is isotropic. Equal mass on the six signed Cartesian axes and a uniform sphere both have $Q=0$, but their fourth moments along the first axis are respectively $1/3$ and $1/5$. Even within a three-frame representation, equal weights on an orthogonal Cartesian triad give fourth moment $1/3$, while rotating the triad by $\pi/4$ about its third axis gives $1/6$ along the same fixed probe. Both triads remain orthogonal and have $Q=0$.

These are exact geometric comparisons. They neither establish that those distributions occur as retained assemblies nor show that an actual clock couples to the omitted moment. They establish that second-moment equality alone cannot exclude such dependence. Rotational covariance also does not exclude a fourth-moment response: the fourth-power probe expression respects a simultaneous rotation of probe and frame.

For three unit normals collected as columns of $N$, with normalized weights $w_a$ summing to one, the retained decomposition is

$$
Q=\frac13(NN^{\mathsf T}-I)
+\sum_{a=1}^{3}(w_a-1/3)
\left(\hat{\mathbf n}_a\hat{\mathbf n}_a^{\mathsf T}-I/3\right).
$$

An orthogonal triad gives zero only when its weights are equal. Since each unit projector minus $I/3$ has Frobenius norm $\sqrt{2/3}$, the triangle inequality gives

$$
\|Q\|_F\leq\frac13\|NN^{\mathsf T}-I\|_F
+\sqrt{\frac23}\sum_a|w_a-1/3|.
$$

The Frobenius norm is the square root of the sum of squared matrix entries. Unit columns lie in a compact set; equality in their determinant bound requires orthogonality. Consequently $|\det N|\to1$ and equalizing weights force $Q\to0$. This remains a statement about the tensor, not stability or a physical suppression theorem. Nonnegative weights are additionally required when the average is interpreted probabilistically. Action, energy, geometric and causal-hit weighting prescriptions cannot be interchanged without defining their extraction and normalization.

The record supplies a useful sufficient lemma. If a channel's response has actually been reduced to

$$
O_k(\mathbf e)=a_k+b_k\left\langle(\mathbf e\cdot\hat{\mathbf n})^2\right\rangle
+\rho_k(\mathbf e),
\qquad \sup_{\|\mathbf e\|=1}|\rho_k(\mathbf e)|\leq\eta_k,
$$

with direction-independent coefficients and fixed comparison conditions, then

$$
\sup_{\|\mathbf e\|=1}\left|O_k(\mathbf e)-a_k-b_k/3\right|
\leq |b_k|\|Q\|_{\mathrm{op}}+\eta_k.
$$

The operator norm here is the largest absolute eigenvalue. The proof is the preceding moment identity and the uniform remainder bound. The physical burden lies in deriving the quadratic response, its gain and its remainder. A small input moment does not imply a small response with an uncontrolled gain. For a differentiable exact reduction, every admitted variation with $DQ[\delta H]=0$ must also have $DO_k[\delta H]=0$; passing that local test is necessary, not sufficient for a global reduction or autonomous reduced evolution.

### 3.3. Different scalar summaries preserve different properties

A response tensor needs a declared physical meaning before its trace can be called a cosmological scale or a laboratory residual. Isotropic linear stretch records $aI$, while its squared-length metric records $a^2I$. Their trace means are $a$ and $a^2$ even though they describe the same stipulated length change. Contraction requires the tensor and its reference metric to be expressed on the same space and in the same chart.

For a positive symmetric stretch $S$ with principal length multipliers $s_1,s_2,s_3$, the mean principal stretch and the volume-equivalent length factor are

$$
a_{\mathrm{tr}}=\frac13\operatorname{tr}S,
\qquad a_V=(\det S)^{1/3}.
$$

If $\gamma=S^{\mathsf T}hS$ is the squared-length metric, the same volume factor is $(\det\gamma/\det h)^{1/6}$. The arithmetic-geometric mean inequality gives $a_V\leq a_{\mathrm{tr}}$, with equality at isotropy. For $S=\operatorname{diag}(2,1,1)$ the values are $\sqrt[3]{2}$ and $4/3$. The trace also equals the spherical mean of the longitudinal projection $h(\mathbf n,S\mathbf n)$; it generally differs from the mean actual length $\|S\mathbf n\|_h$. Along $(1,1,0)/\sqrt2$, those two quantities are $3/2$ and $\sqrt{5/2}$.

These identities distinguish valid summaries; they do not select a physical ruler map or derive expansion of the fixed void. A cosmological distance relation and an orientation-sensitive clock measurement need their own response and calibration maps. A measurement map can have a null space, so a small recorded signal need not bound every underlying tensor component.

## 4. Local geometry does not certify an entire history

### 4.1. Units, roots and finite windows

For the retained causal-root convention, a gap $F=r-c_f(T_r-T_t)$ has units of length. Dividing by speed gives a duration, not a dimensionless quantity. Its emission-time derivative after that division is dimensionless. Choosing normalized wake-speed units $c_f=1$ does not erase the distinction. All numerical illustrations in this manuscript use that normalization.

A nonzero derivative at a root permits local continuation. A positive numerical floor additionally controls local conditioning. Crossing that chosen floor need not make the derivative zero. Nor does a floor stop a root from leaving a retained interval. For a stationary transmitter and receiver separated by two units, the gap on emission interval $s\in[0,1]$ is

$$
F(T,s)=2-T+s,
\qquad s_*(T)=T-2,
\qquad \partial_sF=1.
$$

The retained count is one at $T=5/2$ and zero at $T=7/2$, even though the full-history root remains simple. No physical wake disappears in this example; its root crosses the chosen window boundary. At fixed reception $T=3$, comparing emissions $1/2,1,3/2$ compares sphere radii $5/2,2,3/2$ at that same reception. Only the middle sphere directly hits the receiver. This differentiation across past emission labels does not use future support.

On a compact retained interval, smooth dependence, no boundary zeros and a nonzero emission derivative at every zero prevent boundary loss and local singular creation. The implicit-function theorem continues each root; compactness and simplicity exclude an accumulating family. The finite count is then locally constant, and constant on a connected parameter range. The pair set and retained history domain must remain controlled. Counting roots at each reception time is also different from counting sampled rows over a cycle, which would depend on sampling density.

### 4.2. A singular root need not be a fold

The equations $F=0$ and $F_s=0$ identify singular roots. A nondegenerate fold additionally needs $F_{ss}\ne0$ and a transverse unfolding parameter. The prescribed local path $X(s)=s-s^3$, receiver position 1 and reception time $1+v$ give, where separation stays positive,

$$
F(1+v,s)=s^3-v.
$$

At the origin of this local comparison the first and second derivatives vanish while the third equals six. The root is triple; its real solution does not create a pair as $v$ changes sign. This refutes the universal fold classification for that smooth prescribed-history class. It does not produce an EOM trajectory, a physical caustic transition or a continuation through the singularity.

Likewise, point support does not prove dynamical admissibility of coincidence. Point coordinates can have their coincidence diagonal excluded without possessing a geometric radius. The model's absence of a primitive hard core is an ontological statement; whether a lawful history reaches and continues through equal coordinates is a separate question. A regularized kernel value does not supply that missing continuation.

### 4.3. Direct support, indirect dependence and time orientation

A single emitted sphere supplies direct support on its boundary. Its filled cone records locations the sphere has passed by a given time, not repeated direct reception at every later interior event. Indirect dependence can instead pass through an altered intermediate trajectory and that source's later continuous emissions. The original sphere is not redirected and emission need not be switched on by reception.

The geometry alone is not transitive. In normalized units, an emission at time zero and position zero can reach a relay at time one and position one; a later emission from that position at time two reaches position zero at time three. The last event is not on the original radius-three sphere. A real transmitted perturbation would still require an actual dynamical response between the two relay events. No operational relay or faster-than-wake-speed signal is demonstrated by this construction.

Wake-only chains whose every segment is bounded by the wake speed satisfy a cone bound by the triangle inequality. A claim about all history-mediated influence must also account for transport along constituent paths; the primitive model does not universally impose a sub-wake-speed constituent bound. The retained super-speed transport example is therefore a counterexample to an unsupported geometric inference, not a certified signalling channel.

The product manifold and a position graph over time do not themselves select causal direction. The future-directed admissibility rule and the delayed support condition do. Every link in a finite admitted causal chain strictly increases absolute time, so the chain cannot return to its initial event. Instants or simultaneity slices are totally ordered; distinct simultaneous events instead give a total preorder under comparison of their time coordinates.

### 4.4. Symmetry and sign identities retain their domains

The primitive acceleration kernel is acceleration-first and has no primitive mass. Holding the reception event, complete admitted external transmitter histories, receiver polarity magnitude and summation prescription fixed, its polarity product gives matched external responses $\mathbf A_+=\epsilon\mathbf B$ and $\mathbf A_-=-\epsilon\mathbf B$. The even external component vanishes. A stationary external source at the origin and receiver at position two, received at time three with unit coupling and polarity magnitudes, gives emission time one and accelerations $\pm(1/4,0,0)$.

Here $\mathbf B$ is only a coefficient of the prescribed external record, not a primitive magnetic field. Reversing a physical receiver also reverses its own transmitter polarity, so a fixed-history self-hit has an even squared-polarity factor. Recomputing coupled histories or a dressed assembly changes the comparison. The external identity therefore does not derive total self-plus-partner parity, Maxwell behavior or observer-field normalization.

Other small corrections protect similarly important domains. The bundle with fiber $SO(3)$ contains orientation-compatible frames, whereas all orthonormal frames have fiber $O(3)$. Spherical and cylindrical formulas fail as charts at their axes or origin and require angular seams or overlaps; this is a coordinate-rank failure, not a singularity of Euclidean space. A fixed affine time scale already supplies a duration metric and compatible flat connection on the time factor, without introducing dynamical time geometry; the full product connection remains additional data. Finally, three translations, three rotations and one time translation give seven continuous substrate generators. Adding three boosts gives the ten-generator Poincare group; the Lorentz subgroup has six, and reflections are disconnected. Counting generators does not derive their physical recovery or conserved charges.

## 5. Effective clock comparisons require a consistent measurement rule

### 5.1. A clock formula must agree with its declared metric

The substantive review found a concrete algebraic mismatch between two descriptions intended to assign elapsed time to the same effective clock. With rest-clock factor $A>0$ and $q=B_{ij}w^iw^j/c_0^2$, the original clock target was $A\sqrt{1-q}$, whereas the unchanged metric gave

$$
r^2=A^2-q,
\qquad r=A\sqrt{1-q/A^2},
\qquad q<A^2.
$$

Here $r$ is clock time per effective coordinate time, $B$ is the declared ruler tensor, $\mathbf w$ the relative effective velocity, and $c_0$ the effective speed calibration. Factoring $A^2$ out of the square root requires dividing the velocity term by $A^2$. The two earlier squared rates differ by $(1-A^2)q$; they agree at rest or when $A=1$, so those controls alone miss the error.

For the explicitly illustrative effective choices $c_0=1$, $A=1/2$, $B=I$ and $\mathbf w=(1/4,0,0)$, the two rates are $\sqrt{15}/8\approx0.484123$ and $\sqrt3/4\approx0.433013$. The correction selects the latter to match the existing metric convention. The example also has primitive $c_f=1$ but does not establish a general equality between primitive and effective speeds. It is direct algebra, not a simulated assembly clock.

The discrepancy is second order in velocity at fixed $A$, although it can be a mixed higher-order term in a joint weak-field expansion. It does not invalidate the separately correct first-order weak-field signs. Changing the spatial tensor convention could instead reconcile the old formula, but would require corresponding changes to the metric and signal expressions. The recorded correction preserved the shared convention and its recovery-target status. Positive rest lapse alone does not certify a timelike moving path or an advancing physical clock phase.

### 5.2. A symmetry action is not its observed contraction factor

A full boost action can have an exponential representation on a declared event or state space. A scalar length ratio obtained by projection and simultaneity selection need not inherit that multiplication law. In the explicitly observer-level Lorentz comparison, choose rapidity $\varphi$ with $\cosh\varphi=2$. Then

$$
\operatorname{sech}\varphi=\frac12,
\qquad \operatorname{sech}(2\varphi)=\frac17\ne\frac14
=(\operatorname{sech}\varphi)^2.
$$

This does not violate boost composition. It shows why the scalar contraction factor is not the full transformation. Different observable spaces may also carry different representations of one symmetry. The meaningful common-history obligation is to derive their compatible extraction maps, not to demand identical scalar functions or matrices without defining what they act on.

A shape comparison needs a reference normalization too. A body whose rest radii are two and one has rest aspect ratio two; ideal longitudinal contraction gives ratio $2/\gamma$, not $1/\gamma$. A test of deformation therefore compares the moving-to-rest ratio in each direction, holding branch, excitation, medium and calibration fixed. An unnormalized aspect-ratio test can reject a shape that exactly follows its intended comparison law. Neither the normalization nor the boost algebra establishes that physical assemblies realize that law.

### 5.3. Repeatable phase is not the same as symplectic preservation

A clock phase and a Hamiltonian representation answer different questions. The retained comparison system

$$
\dot\theta=\Omega,
\qquad \dot r=-\kappa(r-r_0),
\qquad \Omega,\kappa>0
$$

has constant period $2\pi/\Omega$ while its area form $dr\wedge d\theta$ shrinks by $e^{-\kappa t}$. It is an abstract system showing that failure to preserve that area form does not generally imply phase-frequency drift. It is not a derived dissipative architrino assembly or an evaluation of the proposed memory-boundary term.

The delayed-Hamiltonian construction in the source record leaves its memory kernel and boundary identity open. A retained history-window boundary is not by itself destruction of physical history or a measured energy flux. Missing memory might shift a phase, alter calibration, generate transient error, destroy recurrence or have no relevant effect; its influence must be derived for the branch. A phase certificate cannot be equated to an unconstructed symplectic condition, while a stable phase alone does not certify Hamiltonian completeness or universality across clock species.

### 5.4. A return map needs both full turns and elapsed time

Phase on a circle records position modulo a full turn. Advancing one quarter turn or five quarters gives the same circle map. Repeating a quarter turn every one or two time units also gives the same map but different frequencies. The full physical lift and the return durations retain the two kinds of information that this reduction loses.

Let $\theta\in\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$ measure cycles, let $\widetilde P$ be the physical lift with $\widetilde P(x+1)=\widetilde P(x)+1$, and let $\delta t(\theta)>0$ be the elapsed effective time to the next return. When the mean phase advance exists and the mean duration has a finite positive limit,

$$
\overline\nu=\lim_{n\to\infty}
\frac{\widetilde P^{\,n}(\theta)-\theta}
{\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\delta t(P^k\theta)},
\qquad \overline\Omega=2\pi\overline\nu.
$$

The numerator counts full cycles; the denominator measures elapsed time. A quarter cycle per unit time, a quarter per two units, and five quarters per unit time give frequencies $1/4$, $1/8$ and $5/4$ despite identical modulo-one maps. A claim of one frequency across a branch also needs initial-phase independence and suitable uniformity. Mean frequency does not establish bounded timing jitter or an everywhere advancing instantaneous phase. The source's correction supplies this missing information requirement without deriving a physical clock or its universal rate.

## 6. Finite reconstruction and observer comparisons

### 6.1. Exact center recovery differs from a noisy inverse fit

Four noncoplanar points on a sphere determine its center by subtracting squared-distance constraints. With $\mathbf d_\alpha=\mathbf Y_\alpha-\mathbf Y_0$, the unknown center $\mathbf z$ satisfies

$$
2\mathbf d_\alpha\cdot\mathbf z
=\|\mathbf Y_\alpha\|^2-\|\mathbf Y_0\|^2,
\qquad \alpha=1,2,3.
$$

The radius cancels. A full-rank displacement system supplies exact uniqueness; a noisy fit additionally needs quantitative conditioning, coordinate scale and an error model. Four unit-sphere tetrahedral vertices at signed coordinates $1/\sqrt3$ have displacement Gram determinant $256/27>0$, although their finite direction set has spherical area zero. A positive continuous aperture can be a sufficient instrument policy, but is not a universal mathematical necessity for exact finite recovery.

The radius assumption changes the local sensitivity matrix. At an exact radial fit, unit directions $\hat{\mathbf n}_k$ and positive normalized weights give

$$
\delta r_k=-\hat{\mathbf n}_k\cdot\delta\mathbf z-\delta R,
\qquad G=\sum_k w_k\hat{\mathbf n}_k\hat{\mathbf n}_k^{\mathsf T}.
$$

Known radius sets $\delta R=0$, leaving $G$ as the local center matrix. If radius is fitted, minimizing over $\delta R$ instead gives

$$
C=G-\bar{\mathbf n}\bar{\mathbf n}^{\mathsf T},
\qquad \bar{\mathbf n}=\sum_k w_k\hat{\mathbf n}_k.
$$

This centered matrix detects the tradeoff between center and radius. Uncertain tagged times similarly require propagation of radius uncertainty rather than an assumption that it vanishes.

The source's exact ambiguity example uses four coplanar cardinal points at radius $a=\sqrt{2/3}$. Both centers at heights $\pm1/\sqrt3$ fit a unit sphere. Equal weights give $G=I/3$ at either center, so local known-radius rank does not choose a global solution. The free-radius matrix is $C=\operatorname{diag}(1/3,1/3,0)$ and detects the continuous center-radius ambiguity. The displacement determinant correctly rejects the coplanar four-point uniqueness test. A small noncoplanar perturbation can restore exact uniqueness while leaving the fit arbitrarily ill-conditioned.

### 6.2. Tagged motion and a finite rest diagnostic

A center history includes its time labels, not just its spatial trace. The prescribed straight path

$$
X(T)=\frac{T+T^2}{2},
\qquad V(T)=\frac12+T,
\qquad A(T)=1
$$

has zero spatial curvature on a regular interval above $T=-1/2$ but nonzero acceleration. At reception one and emission zero its displacement and delay are both one. Locally its self-root gap is $F(1,s)=s(1-s)/2$, with derivative $1/2$ at zero and ordinary weight two. This is a simple positive-delay root on a straight variable-speed prescribed path, not a demonstration that the complete interacting EOM realizes that path. Constant straight velocity below or above wake speed has no positive-delay self-root; exactly wake speed gives the degenerate riding equality. Curvature is therefore not a necessary condition of the geometric root equation.

Exact tagged spheres recover their unique centers on the declared emission interval. Equal absolutely continuous center curves have equal velocities almost everywhere. Identity and polarity tags cannot be recovered from untagged geometry alone. A zero center diameter means exact rest over that interval; uniform motion gives diameter equal to speed times interval duration. These statements assume the fixed spatial identification and absolute time, rather than deriving them from a finite observer record.

For finite estimated centers with error at most $\eta_z$, pairwise triangle inequalities give

$$
\max(0,\widehat D-2\eta_z)\leq D_{\mathrm{sample}}
\leq\widehat D+2\eta_z.
$$

An estimated diameter above $2\eta_z$ certifies sampled non-rest. A small value does not certify exact rest. If an independent speed bound $L$ holds, the sample includes interval endpoints and its largest gap is $h$, every time is within $h/2$ of a sample, giving the interval bound

$$
\max(0,\widehat D-2\eta_z)\leq D(I)
\leq\widehat D+2\eta_z+Lh.
$$

Without temporal regularity, excursions between identical samples can be missed. The record proposes this conditional uncertainty extension; it does not supply an apparatus enclosure or a physical speed bound.

### 6.3. A leakage bound needs a specified comparison

A deterministic matched-history comparison, a distribution of records and an estimator of that distribution are different output types. Identical unbiased binary-output laws can produce one zero and one one; their individual outcomes then differ although the laws agree. Conversely, matching sampled outcomes can conceal distinct laws. Neither observation alone tests invariance of a preparation ensemble.

An observer-hiding target must therefore fix preparation and apparatus, admitted environment and nuisance variation, retained and erased provenance, calibration, output type and a metric appropriate to that output. Replacing a scalar readout $f$ by $\alpha f$ changes its numerical diameter by $|\alpha|$, so a metric and tolerance must transform consistently or be fixed through a dimensionless residual. A velocity range alone cannot specify this experiment.

This distinction also governs imported benchmarks. Differential accelerometry does not measure clock-composition response; an orientation-sensitive resonator result is not a universal bound on every material or clock. A forecast using simulated clusters and observed input properties is not a newly measured temperature-evolution relation. The retained review corrected a source title and preserved that forecast boundary. It also distinguished a published review's parameter table from an earlier draft and from a current-limit claim. This manuscript relies on those local attribution records and has not reexamined the external papers or experiments.

An unproved calibrated hiding bound remains open. A verified above-bound residual in its admitted comparison family would falsify that particular recovery claim. Absence of a derivation is not itself an observed violation, and exact reconstruction from complete tagged history does not prove that an embedded observer has access to those tags.

## 7. Population limits require more than local decay

An inverse-square contribution and a root-conditioning floor do not ensure convergence over an unlimited population. In a homogeneous three-dimensional shell, the number of potential contributors grows like $r^2dr$, canceling the inverse-square radial decay in an absolute-magnitude estimate. Cancellation, root multiplicity, transmitter weights and a summation prescription therefore matter.

The retained conditional repair defines each centered cell contribution as the complete received acceleration from its admitted root ledger. One sufficient covariance hypothesis is

$$
\left|\mathbb E[\delta\mathbf a_k\cdot\delta\mathbf a_l]\right|
\leq C r_k^{-2}r_l^{-2}e^{-d_{kl}/\ell},
$$

Here $r_k$ is the distance of cell $k$ from the receiver, $d_{kl}$ the separation of two cells, $\ell>0$ a fixed correlation length, and $C$ a uniform positive bound. Assume bounded cell-packing density and shells of fixed thickness comparable to $\ell$. For a shell $I_n$ at distance comparable to $n\ell$, packing bounds the sum of the exponential correlation factor around each cell. Since the shell contains $O(n^2)$ cells, covariance expansion yields

$$
\mathbb E\left\|\sum_{k\in I_n}\delta\mathbf a_k\right\|^2
\leq C' n^{-4}|I_n|=O(n^{-2}).
$$

A separation-only covariance bound on unit-variance received cells would instead allow shell variance of order $n^2$. The radial factors must therefore apply to the full received contribution, including the root multiplicity and weight burden, or follow from an explicitly equivalent undiluted representation.

If the centered shell increments additionally form a martingale-difference sequence, summable variances give bounded second moments of its partial sums and the stated mean-square and almost-sure martingale convergence. Mixing alone does not supply that conditional-mean assumption. The result belongs to the declared receiver-centered exhaustion; it does not establish arbitrary rearrangement independence or prove that the physical Noether sea satisfies the hypotheses. A correct finite calculation is not invalidated merely because a different infinite-population claim remains open.

The same discipline appears in the retained Bell discussion. Two-party degradation when a finite-speed connection fails is a possible prediction, but it does not by itself meet a multipartite consistency obstruction that already allows some disconnected correlations to degrade. A proposed response must satisfy the relevant joint-law and no-signalling requirements or identify a changed premise with its consequences. This source-scope correction neither constructs a Bell mechanism nor proves every finite-speed proposal impossible; the associated physical work remains deferred in its own records.

## 8. What the recorded corrections establish

The conversion ledger and later reviews support a general method: preserve an argument's inputs and claim level, test it on a small case that isolates the asserted implication, and repair only what that counterexample or derivation establishes. Exact arithmetic, a local geometric lemma, a source identity, a rendered paragraph and a physical measurement are different references. Agreement between two consumers of the same record demonstrates consistent consumption, not independent correctness of the producer.

Some accepted changes were small definitions: oriented versus full frames, regular chart domains, a group name, a time-valued residual, a neutral scaffold rather than a charged whole, and a default reading route. Others changed effective formulas or acceptance criteria materially: the moving-clock denominator, rest-normalized shape comparison, known-radius versus fitted-radius reconstruction and calibrated observer comparison. Describing all of them as style alone would hide those consequences. Describing them as a replacement of primitive dynamics would claim more than the record establishes.

The retained six-chapter impact assessment found no identified change required in the sampled primitive acceleration accumulation path. That was a bounded source inspection, not an exhaustive solver conformance or downstream consumer audit. The later straight-history self-hit repair changed a linked explanatory requirement in the Master Equation without changing its acceleration kernel. Reading that dependency did not count as a complete Dynamics review. No code inspection or solver run is newly performed here.
