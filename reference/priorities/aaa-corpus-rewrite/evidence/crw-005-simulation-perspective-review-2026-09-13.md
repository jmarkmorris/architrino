## Independent mathematical checks and boundaries

For SP-05, place an equal-radius opposite-polarity binary on the circle of radius $a$ in the plane $Z=0$ and a receiver of polarity $q_r$ at rest at $(0,0,z)$, with $c_f=1$. Every point of the circle is at distance $R=\sqrt{z^2+a^2}$ from the receiver, so both transmitters have the same causal root $T_t=T_r-R$ and are at antipodal points $\pm a\hat{\mathbf u}$ at that time. The tangential velocity is perpendicular to both $\hat{\mathbf u}$ and $\hat{\mathbf z}$, so $\hat{\mathbf r}_t\cdot\mathbf V_t=0$ and $D_t=c_f$ for both, whatever the speed. The potential contributions are $\pm\epsilon/R$ with equal weights and cancel exactly. The per-hit accelerations are $\kappa\epsilon\lvert q_r\rvert\sigma_i\hat{\mathbf r}_i/R^2$ with $\sigma_2=-\sigma_1$ and $\hat{\mathbf r}_{1,2}=(\mp a\hat{\mathbf u}+z\hat{\mathbf z})/R$, so the sum is $-2\sigma_1\kappa\epsilon\lvert q_r\rvert a\hat{\mathbf u}/R^3$: transverse, of magnitude $2\kappa\epsilon\lvert q_r\rvert a/(z^2+a^2)^{3/2}$, rotating with $\hat{\mathbf u}$ so its orbit average is zero. A Node witness first returned magnitude $2$ and axial component $0$ for the known case $z=0$, $a=1$, $\kappa=\epsilon=q_r=1$ (two collinear contributions of unit magnitude), then $0.70711$ against the formula's $2/2^{3/2}=0.70711$ at $z=a=1$ and $0.11413$ against $0.11413$ at $z=2$, $a=1/2$, with zero potential and $D_t=1$ for both transmitters in every case. The check assumes a receiver at rest on the axis and equal orbit radii; it says nothing off-axis.

For SP-07, the per-hit acceleration has magnitude $\kappa\lvert q_tq_r\rvert W^{\mathrm{acc}}/r^2$, so $[\kappa\epsilon^2]=L^3T^{-2}$; dividing by $c_f^2=L^2T^{-2}$ leaves the unique length $\kappa\epsilon^2/c_f^2$ and the unique time $\kappa\epsilon^2/c_f^3$. A branch radius is therefore a dimensionless branch number times $\kappa\epsilon^2/c_f^2$, and changing $\kappa$ at fixed $\epsilon$ and $c_f$ rescales it. This establishes the dependence, not the branch number, and not that any retained branch exists.

For SP-03, with $E_k=\tfrac12\mu\|\mathbf V\|^2$ and $d\mathbf V/dT=\mathbf A$, $dE_k/dT=\mu\mathbf A\cdot\mathbf V$; if $\mu\mathbf A=-\nabla U(\mathbf X,T)$ on the interval then $dE_k/dT=-\nabla U\cdot\mathbf V$, while $dU(\mathbf X(T),T)/dT=\nabla U\cdot\mathbf V+\partial_TU$, so $\Delta E_k=-\Delta U+\int\partial_TU\,dT$. The identity $\Delta E_k=-\Delta U$ holds only when the explicit-time integral vanishes, which a potential built from moving wake surfaces does not guarantee.
