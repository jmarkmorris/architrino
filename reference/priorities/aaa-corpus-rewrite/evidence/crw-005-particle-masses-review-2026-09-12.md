## Independently checkable mathematics

### Trace and directional positivity

In an orthonormal Euclidean frame, let $Z$ denote the exposure tensor and $M$ the symmetric medium tensor. The chapter's ansatz is $I=\alpha_{\mathrm m}E(ZM+MZ)/2$, so cyclicity of the finite-dimensional trace gives $m_{\mathrm{tr}}=\alpha_{\mathrm m}E\operatorname{tr}(ZM)/3$. If $Z=\zeta\mathbf1+Z_{\mathrm{tf}}$ and $M=c_0^{-2}[(1+\delta M_0)\mathbf1+\delta M_{\mathrm{tf}}]$, both trace-free parts have zero trace, and the displayed composed trace follows. This algebra proves no constitutive law.

For a concrete algebraic counterexample, use normalized units with $c_f=1$ and a positive overall coefficient chosen as one:

$$
Z=\operatorname{diag}(1,9,1),\qquad
M=\begin{pmatrix}1&0.9&0\\0.9&1&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
I=\begin{pmatrix}1&4.5&0\\4.5&9&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Both $Z$ and $M$ are positive definite; $M$ has eigenvalues $0.1,1,1.9$. Yet $\operatorname{tr}(I)/3=11/3>0$ while for $u=(-3,1,0)/\sqrt{10}$, $u^TIu=(9-27+9)/10=-0.9$. Thus even positive input tensors do not guarantee positive directional inertia after the symmetrized product. This is a counterexample to a mathematical implication, not a proposed physical assembly or calibrated numerical simulation.

The separate reviewer tested its matrix calculator on commuting diagonal inputs $(2,3,4)$ and $(5,6,7)$, whose known product is $(10,18,28)$, and recorded that pass before calculating this counterexample. The independently checkable reference is the hand quadratic form above. A symmetric frequency-dependent response can also have phase lag; its spatial symmetry does not prove reversible work over a probe cycle.

### Exposure and conjugation

For $L(\hat R)=3\cos^2\theta-1$, the spherical mean is zero because $\langle\cos^2\theta\rangle=1/3$, while any genuine norm of this nonzero function is positive. A norm ratio therefore cannot replace the signed monopole without the chapter's additional scalar projection and positivity conditions. This is a derived analytic counterexample and does not supply a physical energy conversion.

Under full polarity reversal, $q_iq_j$ is unchanged. If only the assembly is reversed, a cross product with an unchanged sea constituent becomes $(-q_i)q_s=-q_iq_s$. A zero total sea polarity does not require cancellation at each delayed hit, since the geometric weights and emission times differ. Conjugation-invariant response therefore requires more than neutrality.

### Differential and dimensional domains

Write $S=\zeta E$ and suppress indices in the trace-free contraction. At a general medium state the exact first variation of the retained composed terms is

$$
\delta m=\frac{\alpha_{\mathrm m}}{c_0^2}
\left[(1+\delta M_0)\delta S+S\,\delta(\delta M_0)
+\frac13\delta(EZ_{\mathrm{tf}}):\delta M_{\mathrm{tf}}
+\frac13EZ_{\mathrm{tf}}:\delta(\delta M_{\mathrm{tf}})\right]+\delta R.
$$

At the unperturbed isotropic reference, the pre-existing medium perturbations vanish and the chapter's shorter pressure formula follows. Away from that reference, dropping the two product terms is not justified merely by calling the pressure step small. The representative defect is in energy units; its mass effect is $\Delta m=(\alpha_{\mathrm m}/c_0^2)\Delta_{\mathrm{tr}}+\Delta R$ at the same medium record.

For the declared density-only constitutive example, the chain rule gives $\partial m/\partial P=(\partial m/\partial n)s_n/K_{\mathrm{pack}}$. Vanishing headroom implies a vanishing derivative only with the stated uniform bounds. For example, $K_{\mathrm{pack}}=K_0s_n$ removes the vanishing factor even with constant finite mass sensitivity; a sensitivity proportional to $1/s_n$ also defeats the limit at fixed modulus. These are analytic constitutive counterexamples, not evidence about the actual sea.

With $[\kappa\epsilon^2]=L^3T^{-2}$ and $[c_f]=LT^{-1}$, the ratio $\kappa\epsilon^2/c_f^2$ has length units. These inputs have no mass dimension. Adding a separately calibrated physical action $[\hbar_{\mathrm{act}}]=ML^2T^{-1}$ permits $\hbar_{\mathrm{act}}c_f/(\kappa\epsilon^2)$ to have mass units. No dimensional manipulation supplies the missing action calibration or a retained radius. Likewise, integer action values alone do not imply energy equals action times frequency: the action-angle comparison gives an integral of frequency in general.

Defining an excitation gap as the fitted mass times the same effective speed squared makes their residual identically zero, including derivatives along that definition. The repaired gap must instead come from an independently defined excitation-energy difference. A finite positive split penalty can likewise always be traded against residual improvement; it cannot enforce exact shared parameters.
