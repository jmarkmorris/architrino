## Independent mathematical checks and boundaries

For FC-01 and FC-11, $\operatorname{Ext}_S(c)\subseteq\mathcal{C}_{\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}}\subseteq\mathcal{C}_S$ by definition, and every $\theta\in\operatorname{Ext}_S(c)$ satisfies $\pi_S(\theta)=c$, so a nonempty fiber places $c$ in $\pi_S(\mathcal{C}_S)$; the lemma's first condition is implied by its second. With each $\mathcal{G}_S$ carrying the null condition for channels in domain $S$, a record with a violating channel in any sector's domain fails that sector's $\mathcal{G}_S$ and is outside the intersection. A channel whose null data product lies in no sector's domain escapes, which is exactly the chapter's stated exemption for channels outside the validated comparison domain. These are set-theoretic facts about the declared definitions, not evidence that any $\mathcal{C}_S$ is nonempty.

For FC-02 and FC-03, a sum of two nonnegative terms is zero exactly when both are zero; with weight $0$ the split term vanishes identically, so a split of $1$ with no bound excess gives residual $0$, and with weight $1$ it gives $1$. Node arithmetic returned $[\log(0/1)]_+=0$ for an absent channel, $[\log(10/1)]_+=2.3026$ for a tenfold excess, and $[\log(0.1/1)]_+=0$ under the bound; a known control returned $\log 1=0$ first. The lifetime conversion is $\tau/B>X\Rightarrow B/\tau<1/X$ by reciprocal algebra.

For FC-04, in normalized wake-speed units with $c_f=1$, a dressed speed $c_{\mathrm{eff}}=0.8$ and group speed $v=0.48$ give $\beta_{\mathrm{eff}}=0.6$ and $\gamma_{\mathrm{eff}}=1.25$; the primitive denominator gives $\beta_f=0.48$ and $\gamma_f=1.1399$. For the incompatibility margin, if $\mathcal{C}_T\!\mid c\neq\varnothing$ some compatible record has distance at most $\epsilon_T$, so the infimum is at most $\epsilon_T$ and $\delta_T(c)\ge0$; the contrapositive is the stated certificate.
