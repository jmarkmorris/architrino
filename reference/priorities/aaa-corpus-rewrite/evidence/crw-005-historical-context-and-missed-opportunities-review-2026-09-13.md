## Independent mathematics

Their difference is (L/c)β²+O(β⁴). Thus the relevant leading anisotropy is second order. This is comparison geometry, not a substrate dynamics derivation. The scratch checker first passes the zero-drift known case, then checks the coefficient with signal speed and arm length both one.

For the effective harmonic oscillator, the phase-space ellipse has area A=2πE/ω. Comparing with E=(n+1/2)ℏω gives A/h=n+1/2, and distance to the nearest integer is 1/2. This observer-level counterexample limits the displayed integer target and does not import oscillator quantization as an architrino premise.
