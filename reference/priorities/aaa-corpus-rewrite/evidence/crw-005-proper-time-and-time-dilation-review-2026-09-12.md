## Independent reasoning and falsifiers

These are derived mathematical checks on declared inputs, not measured assembly behavior. Numerical examples use $c_f=1$; observer comparison constants are separately declared.

**Time conversion.** Phase counting gives $\tau=(\theta-\theta_0)/\omega_0$, hence $d\tau/dt_{\mathrm{eff}}=(d\theta/dT)/(\omega_0J)$. For the independently chosen chart $t_{\mathrm{eff}}=2T$, native ratio one exports to $1/2$. This is a counterexample to unconditional equality of the ratios. A universal $J=1$ needs its own derivation.

**Nonstationary clock path.** In a flat observer comparison, choose $x(t)=a\sin(\pi t/t_f)$, $0<\pi a/(c_0t_f)<1$, with fixed endpoints. Its interval integrates $\sqrt{1-(\pi a/(c_0t_f))^2\cos^2(\pi t/t_f)}$. The derivative of that integral with respect to positive $a$ is strictly negative. An accelerated timelike clock therefore accumulates valid proper time without stationarity. This observer counterexample imports no geodesic dynamics into the substrate.

**Normalization and coefficient identification.** With $c_f=1$ and declared $c_{\mathrm{eff},0}=1/2$, $\chi_0=2$. The old delay coordinate is $\ln2$ at reference; the repaired coordinate is zero. Choosing $b_\chi=1/2$ exposes the old nonzero reference $\ln\Gamma_N$. An arbitrary remainder proportional to $-\ln\xi$ can trade coefficient with $b_\xi$ without changing the response; excluding that term is necessary for identification. A bounded remainder alone does not bound its derivative. Evidence violating these assumptions reopens the coefficient claims.

**Spectral and pressure algebra.** With gap frequency $\Delta E/h=10$, stretch two, and independent residual one, the forward frequency is six. The old inverse gives $10/6$; the corrected inverse gives $10/(6-1)=2$. The pressure calculation $0.6\times0.5=0.3\ne1$ is exact on its assumed inputs. Changed residual/gap or an independently obtained pressure vector changes the inference; these are not physical hydrogen or Fe/Cr measurements.

**Kernel and root roles.** A length-valued causal constraint has $[\delta(g)]=\mathrm L^{-1}$ and $[dT_t]=\mathrm T$. Multiplication by $c_f$ cancels these units; $[\kappa|q_aq_b|]=\mathrm L^3/\mathrm T^2$ and the inverse-square spatial kernel then give acceleration. At an isolated simple root, delta substitution yields $c_f/|\partial g/\partial T_t|=c_f/|D_t|$. Separately differentiating $g(T_r,T_t)=0$ gives playback $dT_t/dT_r=D_r/D_t$, not an acceleration multiplier. The soft vector tends to the sharp inverse-square kernel at positive separation as $\epsilon_c\to0$. These are local identities, not complete-root, history, limit, or event certification.

**Phase witness.** A prescribed circle with two turns has lifted phase change $4\pi$ while its endpoint principal angles agree modulo $2\pi$. An independent numerical unwrap of 17 trigonometric samples recovered $4\pi$; endpoint subtraction returned zero to roundoff. The instrument first passed one complete known turn. This tests extraction on prescribed geometry, not EOM stability; aliased sampling remains inadmissible.

**PPN expansion.** Put $u=U/c_0^2$ and $v_2=v^2/c_0^2$. The retained observer radicand is $1-2u-v_2+2\beta u^2-2\gamma uv_2$. Its square root through degree two is $1-u-v_2/2+(\beta-1/2)u^2-(\gamma+1/2)uv_2-v_2^2/8$. Squaring the GR polynomial reproduces the radicand through degree two; replacing its mixed coefficient by zero fails. An independently written multiplication instrument checked this after passing $(1+x)^2$. Another chart, shift, potential convention, or uncontrolled PPN potential requires a new map.

**Visibility and statistics.** For balanced paths correlated with normalized pure internal states, tracing out the internal state gives path coherence proportional to their overlap. Ideal contrast is its magnitude; optimal equal-prior pure-state distinguishability gives $D^2=1-V^2$. Mixed states or apparatus losses do not inherit that equality for total visibility. Weighted-fit covariance requires full rank and an error covariance proportional to $W^{-1}$; singular designs or correlated/nuisance errors invalidate its unqualified use. Neither identity accepts a microscopic clock.
