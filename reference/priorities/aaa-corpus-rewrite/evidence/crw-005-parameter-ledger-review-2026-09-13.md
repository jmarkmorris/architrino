## Independent mathematical checks and boundaries

For PL-01, a length-normalized mollifier has units inverse length. Multiplying the old integral by the coupling and squared polarity gives units inverse time, not length per time squared. The added wake speed restores acceleration. For a simple causal root, the derivative of delayed separation minus wake radius with respect to emission time is $D_t=c_f-\hat{\mathbf R}\cdot\mathbf V_t$, so the corrected delta collapse gives $c_f/|D_t|$. A stationary transmitter gives unit weight. These are symbolic checks; no new numerical calculation uses a wake speed other than one. They do not justify tangent roots or a global regulator limit.

For PL-03, the dimensional exponent matrix of the triplet has determinant one, so no nontrivial dimensionless monomial is constructed solely from it. The declared length and time scales make the acceleration prefactor one. Initial histories divided by those scales remain functions supplied to the delayed problem; dimensional analysis does not solve them.

For PL-04, if an observable is proportional to a nonzero parameter raised to power $a$, infinitesimal fractional response is $a$ times the fractional input change, and the displayed quotient tends to $1/a$. Thus a large response corresponds to a small magnitude of the quotient. This derivation checks the interpretation without changing the diagnostic's identity.

For PL-10 and PL-11, dividing the speed by its asymptotic value normalizes the delay independently of primitive speed matching. Squaring a clock law $1-u+C_2u^2$ gives $1-2u+(1+2C_2)u^2$ through second order. Inverting the declared PPN coefficient relations $\Xi_1=\alpha_1-2\alpha_2$, $\Xi_2=\alpha_2$, and $\Xi_3=\alpha_1-\alpha_2-\alpha_3$ yields the repaired dictionary and $\Xi_4=2\alpha_3-\alpha_1$. These checks establish algebra conditional on the effective PPN comparison, not a constitutive derivation from architrinos.

For PL-14, circumference identification contributes a factor $1/(4\pi^2)$ to squared radius and cycle-to-radian action contributes $1/(2\pi)$. Together with the observer Planck-length definition, these give $G_{\text{eff}}/G\approx(c_f/c)^3/(8\pi^3)$. The source's conjectural estimate therefore retains an unresolved factor even after speed calibration. No geometric factor was fitted or invented to remove it.
