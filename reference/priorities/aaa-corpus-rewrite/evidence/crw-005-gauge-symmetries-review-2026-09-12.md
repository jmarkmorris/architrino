## Checkable derivations, counterexamples, and falsifiers

**GS-01: local versus delayed Noether theorem.** Claim grade: derived, conditional on a differentiable first-order effective action. For fields $\Phi^A$, define the Euler derivative $E_A=\partial\mathcal L/\partial\Phi^A-\partial_\mu(\partial\mathcal L/\partial(\partial_\mu\Phi^A))$. If an internal generator $X^A$ changes the density by $\delta_X\mathcal L=\partial_\mu K^\mu$, the current is $J^\mu=(\partial\mathcal L/\partial(\partial_\mu\Phi^A))X^A-K^\mu$. The product rule gives $\partial_\mu J^\mu=-E_AX^A$. Thus it vanishes on the effective Euler–Lagrange solutions. Integrating over a spatial volume gives charge change equal to minus outward current flux; local continuity alone does not conserve an open subsystem's charge. A nonzero $E_AX^A$, unaccounted boundary term, or nonzero outward flux overturns the corresponding conservation claim. Look at the action, its admitted variations, and its boundary data. None of this constructs the delayed action or its history boundary charge.

**GS-02: quotient and projection.** Claim grade: derived for the specified group action. Identity, inverse and composition give reflexivity, symmetry and transitivity of $c\sim g\cdot c$. This is distinct from two histories having the same coarse image under $\Pi$. A gauge transformation that changes a retained observable, escapes the admitted boundary/domain, or fails group composition falsifies the asserted redundancy. A representative choice that meets one orbit twice without accounting for residual freedom falsifies the corresponding uniqueness claim. The chapter does not assert a global smooth selector or an injective history map. Constraints and their evolution must be proved for any proposed effective dynamical action; no particular Hamiltonian constraint algebra has been derived here.

**GS-03: hypercharge covariance.** Claim grade: derived. Writing $u=\exp(i y_r\alpha)$ gives $\partial_\mu u=i y_r(\partial_\mu\alpha)u$. With $A'_\mu=A_\mu+(1/g_1)\partial_\mu\alpha$, the corrected derivative obeys $D_\mu^{(r)\prime}(u\Psi_r)=uD_\mu^{(r)}\Psi_r$. Combining a weight-$y_r$ field transformation with the old unit-weight derivative instead leaves the defect $i(y_r-1)(\partial_\mu\alpha)u\Psi_r$. This is nonzero for generic nonunit weights. The photon benchmark also requires its own connection because hypercharge and electric charge are different representation generators. Reopen if the recovered representations, charge lattice, or mixing convention differs; substitute every admitted representation and verify cancellation using the same coupling normalization.

**GS-04: invariance without a connection.** Claim grade: derived counterexample at effective comparison level. Let $\rho=\psi^\dagger\psi$ and take $\mathcal L=\partial_\mu\rho\,\partial^\mu\rho-V(\rho)$ for an arbitrary scalar function $V$. Every local unitary change of $\psi$ leaves $\rho$ unchanged, so the action is locally invariant without a compensating connection. The corrected hypothesis instead asks to differentiate the charged field itself covariantly. Reopen any unrestricted necessity claim unless it excludes this counterexample by a stated hypothesis. No substrate field is introduced by this comparison.

**Preserved transformation signs.** Claim grade: derived by substitution. For $D_\mu=\partial_\mu-igA_\mu$, insert $A'_\mu=UA_\mu U^{-1}+(i/g)U\partial_\mu U^{-1}$ and use $U\partial_\mu U^{-1}=-(\partial_\mu U)U^{-1}$: the two derivatives of $U$ cancel and $D'_\mu(U\psi)=UD_\mu\psi$. Expanding the commutator yields $[D_\mu,D_\nu]=-ig(\partial_\mu A_\nu-\partial_\nu A_\mu-ig[A_\mu,A_\nu])$. Hermitian Pauli generators therefore give the displayed positive $g_2\epsilon^{abc}W_\mu^bW_\nu^c$ term. An uncancelled derivative or opposite coefficient would falsify this sign check. This is an independent algebraic identity, not a solver run.

**GS-05: phase representatives.** Claim grade: derived. Phases $0$ and $2\pi$ describe the same point on the circle. Their old absolute difference is $2\pi$, while $\inf_{N\in\mathbb Z}|0-2\pi-2\pi N|=0$. For any phase difference $d$, replacing it by $d+2\pi k$ merely relabels the minimizing integer, so the repaired residual is invariant. Reopen if shifting either phase representative changes acceptance. The calibrated arm action and flux dependence remain recovery obligations; no interference measurement was performed.

**GS-06: integrated force is insufficient for pointwise suppression.** Claim grade: derived at effective comparison level. On an arm, take a smooth force bump of fixed peak magnitude $M$ supported in an arclength interval of width $\delta$. Its squared integral is at most $M^2\delta$, which tends to zero while the maximum remains $M$. Hence the aggregate residual can be small without pointwise force suppression. Separate limits fix this failure. Reopen if acceptance still permits a force peak above the declared bound or fits force and phase from different branch records. This is a symbolic functional counterexample; no numerical wake-speed value is instantiated.

**GS-07: curvature and global holonomy.** Claim grade: derived. On a punctured plane, the locally defined one-form $A^{\mathrm{em}}=a\,d\varphi$ has zero curvature away from the puncture, but its integral around the puncture is $2\pi a$. The loop does not bound a surface in that domain, so applying a surface integral there would erase the holonomy. For two admissible spanning surfaces, equality of exponentiated phases requires their flux difference times $q_{\mathrm{eff}}/\hbar$ to lie in $2\pi\mathbb Z$. This does not require an arbitrary single solenoid flux to be quantized. Reopen if a branch discards nonbounding-loop holonomy, uses curvature outside its declared domain, or gives different phases from admissible alternative surfaces. The example is mathematical effective-connection geometry, not a claim about a primitive field in the void.

**GS-08: wrong integer sector.** Claim grade: derived. Let a reference sector have label $n_{\mathrm{ref}}(s)$ and let the candidate return $n_{\mathrm{ref}}(s)+1$. The old nearest-integer term vanishes. If the compared local observables do not distinguish these sectors, the entire old residual can vanish despite the wrong topology. The repaired integer term equals one. Reopen if sector orientation/normalization is left unspecified, labels can be reassigned after inspecting the candidate, or a wrong fixed sector still passes. Selecting the reference topology cannot substitute for deriving the candidate's invariant from its own history.

**GS-09: algebra is not transport.** Claim grade: derived. Matrix units obey $[E_{ab},E_{cd}]=\delta_{bc}E_{ad}-\delta_{da}E_{cb}$. Traceless Hermitian $3\times3$ matrices have eight real dimensions and close under $-i[A,B]$. On that same space, an effective comparison generator $H=\operatorname{diag}(h_1,h_2,h_3)$ with $h_1\ne h_2$ gives $[H,T^{(x)}_{12}]=i(h_1-h_2)T^{(y)}_{12}\ne0$. Availability of the full generator algebra therefore does not make every color rotation a symmetry of the dynamics. Also, a nontrivial axis mixer sends one basis state to a superposition, so individual axis basis labels cannot simultaneously be invariant sectors of the full action. Reopen when a proposed history-derived transport map fails covariance, composition, or preservation of its claimed physical observables. The adjacent chapter's norm and determinant assumptions remain outside this repair scope.

**GS-10: kinetic action not fixed by gauge covariance.** Claim grade: derived counterexample. A gauge-invariant scalar density built only from $\Psi^\dagger\Psi$, or a kinetic density with independent electric and magnetic response coefficients on an effective preferred-frame chart, is compatible with the old broad field assumptions without giving the displayed Lorentz-normalized gauge action. Even after imposing Lorentz covariance, gauge symmetry alone does not fix all coefficients or remove scalar and permitted topological terms. The repaired display is therefore a conditional comparison ansatz. Reopen if a claimed derivation omits the metric, spinor, representation, normalization, or constitutive assumptions, or calls all omitted terms higher order without an operator analysis.
