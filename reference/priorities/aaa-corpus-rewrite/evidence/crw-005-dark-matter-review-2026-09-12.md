## Analytical checks and source limits

These are independent analytical references for corrected expressions. Inline Node assertions check arithmetic witnesses, not physical realizations:

1. A transformation error with entries `(1,2,3,4)` and identity covariance has scalar squared norm 30. Its two-by-two Gram product instead has rows `(10,14)` and `(14,20)`. Both were checked after the known case.
2. Lens Jacobians with diagonals `(2,3)` and `(4,5)` give a relative map with diagonal `(0.5,0.6)`. Scaling both by 0.6 leaves the map unchanged within absolute tolerance $10^{-12}$. Algebraically the scale factors cancel, unlike their effect on convergence integrals.
3. At normalized baryonic mass one, the two literal source forms give 0.934180721754538 and 0.7615941559557649. This disproves algebraic equivalence without deciding the intended fit.
4. Opposite charges at positions `+1` and `-1` have charge zero and comparison dipole two. This counterexample is not an electrostatic premise for primitive dynamics.
5. With transition scale one and enhancement four, the low-acceleration extrapolations meet at Newtonian acceleration 0.0625 and observed acceleration 0.25. Separately, inserting the exterior inverse-square and circular-orbit accelerations into deep MOND derives the fourth-power speed relation.
6. Adding the number balances gives $\dot N_{\mathrm{tot}}+3HN_{\mathrm{tot}}=0$, hence $d(a^3N_{\mathrm{tot}})/dt_{\mathrm{eff}}=0$. Doubling scale factor changes physical density eight to one while comoving count stays eight. Equal errors `(1,1)` give old difference zero and repaired squared norm two.
7. With $c_0=a=1$, squared sound speed $10^{-6}$ and comoving wavenumber $10^4$ give pressure coefficient 100. Small speed does not control all modes. In the separate Einstein–de Sitter reference, exponent $p=2/3$ gives $p(p-1)+4p/3=2/3$, matching the independently specified density coefficient.
8. Fixed permanent multipoles of orders two and two have electrostatic comparison energy proportional to $r^{-(2+2+1)}$ and its radial derivative proportional to $r^{-6}$. No native cross-section is calculated.
9. The flyby coefficient follows from $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}GMb(b^2+v^2t^2)^{-3/2}\,dt=2GM/(bv)$ for positive impact parameter and speed. One-sided isotropic acceptance follows from $\int_{\mathrm{hemisphere}}\cos\vartheta\,d\Omega/(4\pi)=1/4$. These symbolic integrals justify restricted formulas, not detections.
