These references establish effective comparison mathematics or experimental statements, not substrate premises.

- [Hensen et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.05949), abstract: actual CHSH statistic, 245 trials, and locality/detection claims with statistical interpretation. No new numerical empirical result was inserted into the chapter.
- [Giustina et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/1511.03190v2), main text Eq. (1), HTML lines 85–94: CH–Eberhard probability inequality, non-detections, and trial definition. [Shalm et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/1511.03189v2), setting/detection discussion, HTML lines 63–67: RNG assumptions, Eberhard detection treatment, finite statistical inference. These support distinguishing photon probability inequalities from a universal CHSH report.
- [Rauch et al., cosmic Bell test](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.05966), abstract: explicit fair-sampling and quasar-wavelength assumptions. Past-light-cone provenance exclusions are not a total-variation bound for every hidden-state model.
- [Bancal et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.3795), abstract: finite-speed hidden-influence models face a multipartite signaling obstruction under the paper's hypotheses. Two-party marginal checks alone do not answer it.
- Direct CHSH proof: for four deterministic binary values, a(b-b')+a'(b+b') is plus or minus 2. Convex normalized mixtures preserve the bound. The same corner argument applies to stochastic conditional means in [-1,1]. A complete shared fair bit gives the coarse correlated table (1/2,0,0,1/2), whose TV distance from the product of its marginals is 1/2, while its complete-variable model is Bell-local.
- Direct tolerance proof: an observable bounded by 1 changes in expectation by at most twice TV. Four conditional contexts within epsilon of one common preparation therefore give 2+8epsilon, under otherwise fixed local response laws. Four outcome-cell errors of size delta give correlator error at most 4delta, and four CHSH contexts at most 16delta. Hence the preserved singlet/product floor is (sqrt(2)-1)/8, approximately 0.05177669529663689.
- Direct marginal proof: summing two cells gives marginal error at most 2delta. Exact singlet probabilities have fair marginals for every angle but setting-dependent correlation; this disproves full joint-law invariance as a no-signaling requirement.
