## Primary textual checks

- [Maimonides, Guide for the Perplexed II.25](https://sacred-texts.com/jud/gfp/gfp112.htm), Friedländer translation: the text considers philosophical arguments about creation and eternity, while defending creation. This supports distinguishing divine dependence, temporal beginning, and textual interpretation; it does not establish all Jewish schools' positions.
- [Averroes, Decisive Treatise](https://iberian-connections.yale.edu/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/Averroes-DecisiveTreatise.pdf), Butterworth translation, §§18–21: directly distinguishes the world's dependence on a cause from the temporal categories assigned to ordinary generated things and discusses continuing being/time. This is a concrete counterexample to the blanket temporal-beginning summary, not a replacement orthodoxy for all Islam.
- [Tattvartha Sutra 3.27 with Sarvarthasiddhi commentary](https://www.wisdomlib.org/jainism/book/tattvartha-sutra-with-commentary/d/doc1084698.html), Vijay K. Jain translation: the verse names Bharata and Airavata; the commentary assigns rise/fall to human conditions there. The repaired chapter does not relabel these cycles as universal destruction/recreation.
- [Udana 8.3](https://accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/kn/ud/ud.8.03.than.html), Thanissaro Bhikkhu translation: directly distinguishes unconditioned liberation from conditioned phenomena. The translator's note also distinguishes an undiscoverable beginning of transmigration from being unborn. This is not evidence for a creator, a physical substance, or uniform metaphysics across Buddhist schools.
- [Taishang Ganying Pian](https://www.sacred-texts.com/tao/sbe40/sbe4018.htm), Legge translation, main text §§2 and 4–5: describes spirits recording conduct and assigning consequences. The correction uses the translated primary text, not the translator's derogatory footnotes. It disproves the blanket judgment-free summary; this review makes no new claim to have measured the prevalence of apocalyptic texts across Daoist history.

These primary examples justify narrow corrections. Search snippets and general websites were used only for discovery, not as doctrinal authority. A source unavailable at one mirror was checked at the successfully opened primary-text edition named above. No translated passage is quoted at length.

## Logical check and limits

The displayed kalam implication is valid under ordinary first-order reading: instantiate the universal conditional at the universe, then apply the stated beginning premise to obtain an existential cause. That conclusion contains no uniqueness, personhood, immateriality, or transcendence predicate. Adding any of those requires additional premises. The chapter preserves the exact display and viewer identity.

Structural resemblance and incompatibility are independent: two theories may both use persistent constituents while disagreeing about irreducible selves; a physical model can omit a creator without its equations proving that no creator exists. The project may maintain a proposed complete physical ontology, but that additional commitment must be distinguished from the results of a physical calculation.

A primary passage contradicted by the revised scope would falsify the associated historical claim. Broader quantitative comparisons would require a defined textual corpus and method; none is claimed here. The review does not certify every theological or historical detail across all schools, adjudicate metaphysics, or change physical canon.
