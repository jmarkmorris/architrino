The references establishing mathematical correctness are the following arguments, independently checked rather than inferred from agreement between edited chapters:

1. **Snapshot and motion.** For seed position $R\mathbf e_a$ and body-diagonal unit axis $\hat{\mathbf n}=(1,1,1)/\sqrt3$, axial projection is $R/\sqrt3$ and transverse radius is $R\sqrt{2/3}$. These are the unchanged displayed values. Rotation with nonzero angular rate $\omega$ has initial velocity $\omega R\hat{\mathbf n}\times\mathbf e_a\ne0$. Rotation about the endpoint's own fixed axis at zero transverse radius leaves that endpoint stationary. Thus one instantaneous inventory admits distinct histories. The live [Spatial Braid Assemblies](../../../../content/markdown/aaa/noether-braid/3d-braid-assemblies.md), line 72, supplies the same explicit distinction in its prescribed fixture; that agreement checks terminology, while the velocity calculation proves the distinction.
2. **Axial-sign domain.** In the canonical radial acceleration kernel, the axial numerator of one hit is proportional to the polarity product times $z_i-z_j$, with positive ordinary-root weight and positive range cubed in the denominator. At two fixed heights $\pm h$ with polarity segregated by plane, same-plane contributions vanish and opposite-plane contributions all point inward. Unequal same-polarity heights can instead give an outward contribution to the higher receiver. This defeats extension of the all-sign proof, without supplying a balanced unequal-height example. The repair retains exactly that proof boundary and does not assert that the unexcluded planar limit is balanced.
3. **Polar and axial parity.** Inversion changes the sign of a nonzero determinant of three polar vectors. An axial vector formed from two polar vectors is unchanged because $(-\mathbf u)\times(-\mathbf w)=\mathbf u\times\mathbf w$. A frame of axial vectors alone therefore cannot supply the asserted parity-odd sign. Global polarity conjugation leaves path positions fixed. The current polar-frame definition consequently supports the stated $C$-even, $P$-odd orientation and its factorization with the separately declared polarity pseudoscalar. This agrees with [Mathematical Terminology](../../../../content/markdown/aaa/archie/mathematics-terminology.md), line 31, and [Terminology Usage](../../../../content/markdown/aaa/archie/terminology-usage.md), line 161. It remains conditional on a nondegenerate extraction and viewing convention; no universal extraction algorithm is claimed.
4. **Internal transit scale.** A stationary-center circle of radius $\rho$ and angular rate $\omega$ has internal speed $s=\omega\rho$ despite zero group speed. For an equatorial antipodal pair with delay $\Delta$ and swept angle $\delta=\omega\Delta$, its delayed chord gives $c_f\Delta=2\rho|\cos(\delta/2)|$, hence $\delta=2(s/c_f)|\cos(\delta/2)|$. The diameter estimate gives a rough scale, not the exact finite-angle solution. The revised paragraph states that limited role and uses the internal-speed convention of Spatial Braid Assemblies, lines 183–191, without overloading reserved group-speed notation.
5. **Frame rank versus support.** Unit normals $\mathbf e_x$, $\mathbf e_y$ and $(\mathbf e_x+\mathbf e_y)/\sqrt2$ have zero determinant. Complete circles in the first two normal planes contain the three independent coordinate directions, so their complete support is three-dimensional. Thus loss of the selected three-normal frame does not imply planar paths. Two nonparallel normals also give three independent frame directions after taking their cross product; the three-row construction is a declared coordinate choice rather than a general geometric lower bound. The modified table and downstream envelope sentence now preserve the [taxonomy's complete-path definition](../../../../content/markdown/aaa/noether-braid/braid-taxonomy.md#component-braid-dimensionality). This reference is geometric only, with no Master Equation solution asserted for the counterexample.
6. **Finite relabeling versus tangent neutrality.** A continuous curve in a finite group is locally constant, so the finite $S_3$ orbit has no infinitesimal tangent direction to remove from a Floquet spectrum. Continuous symmetry tangents require the derivative of the actual retained group action and an actual neutral multiplier or exponent check. The current paragraphs preserve finite equivalence of records while applying tangent removal only to verified continuous neutral directions. The neighboring [frequency-lock chapter](../../../../content/markdown/aaa/noether-braid/three-binary-4-2-1-frequency-lock.md), line 589, remains consistent; no computed spectrum is certified by this prose correction.
