## Independent mathematical checks

Claim grade: derived. These elementary calculations use declared mathematical or effective quantum premises; they are not substrate imports. A counterexample satisfying their premises or an error in the algebra below would overturn the result.

1. **Classical correlation is not mixed-state entanglement.** The equal mixture of the two product projectors for binary outcomes both zero and both one is separable by construction, but its joint distribution is not the product of its marginal distributions. The same common classical bit can be copied into any finite number of variables, giving perfect pairwise correlation with every copy. Classical provenance therefore supplies neither quantum entanglement nor quantum monogamy.
2. **CHSH local bound.** For deterministic binary responses, the pointwise CHSH expression is one local value times the sum of the two opposite-wing responses plus the other local value times their difference. Exactly one parenthesis vanishes and the other has magnitude two. Convex averaging over a common normalized setting-independent measure preserves the bound. Stochastic bounded responses are covered by convexity. This proof requires neither independent hidden coordinates nor predetermined values for every quantum observable.
3. **Joint approximation bound.** An entrywise error at most delta in a binary joint table changes a marginal probability by at most twice delta and a product correlation by at most four times delta. Comparing two remote settings doubles the marginal bound; summing the two outcomes gives a one-wing no-signaling residual at most eight times delta. Four CHSH correlations give an error at most sixteen times delta. Thus arbitrarily accurate Bell-local approximation precludes strict CHSH violation even when the infimum is not attained.
4. **Measurement-independence tolerance.** Under total variation defined as the supremum over events, the expectation of a function bounded in absolute value by one changes by at most twice the total-variation distance. If each of the four setting-conditioned preparation measures is within epsilon of one common measure, their four correlations differ from the common-measure local model by at most eight epsilon in CHSH. The resulting conservative local bound is two plus eight epsilon. This is not permission to call a finite tolerance exact independence.
5. **Post-setting trace counterexample.** Let a pre-setting hidden bit be independent of a freely sampled setting bit, and let a later detector trace explicitly record that setting. The pre-setting bit remains independent of the setting; the complete later trace plainly does not. Testing independence on that trace confuses dynamical response with setting-dependent preparation.
6. **Nonfactorization after compression.** Two outputs equal to the same unbiased hidden bit have a correlated, nonproduct joint distribution when that bit is omitted. Conditioned on the bit, their deterministic response probabilities factor. Nonproduct compressed data alone do not refute Bell-local causality.
7. **Conditional state and no-signaling.** In a normalized bipartite quantum state, summing an unread local measurement over Kraus operators uses the completeness identity. Partial trace over that measured subsystem leaves the other subsystem's reduced density operator unchanged. Conditioning on a selected result is a different normalized distribution and requires access to the result to identify its subensemble.
8. **Binary joint-law expansion.** The four functions one, first outcome, second outcome, and their product form a basis for tables on two binary variables. Expanding with normalization, local means, and product mean yields the chapter's displayed quarter-factor formula. Positivity of all four entries is an independent requirement; the formula alone does not prove the underlying response is physical.
9. **Causal event domain.** A wake emitted at one spacetime event reaches a second only when spatial separation equals wake speed times the positive absolute-time difference. Comparing separation at a common late instant does not constrain every earlier emission or moving endpoint. Effective photon-cone spacelike separation does not exclude the proposed faster wake channel.

No numerical wake-speed instantiation, Python computation, or EOM simulation was used.
