### Independent mathematical witnesses

**Inventory count preserved.** From $2n_++n_m=N_+$ and $2n_-+n_m=N_-$, the mixed count has the parity of both inventories. For $(N_+,N_-)=(5,1)$, nonnegativity forces $n_m=1$, hence $(n_+,n_-,n_m)=(2,0,1)$. For $(2,4)$, $n_m=0$ or $2$, giving $(1,2,0)$ or $(0,1,2)$. Polarity conjugation exchanges $n_+$ and $n_-$. Three different dyad classes give $(1,1,1)$ and neutral inventory $(3,3)$. This is a derived counting fact about the declared six sites; it supplies no binding, color amplitude, or generation dynamics.

**Phase-cancellation counterexample.** For orthonormal spatial vectors $n_1,n_2,n_3$ and phases $\phi_a=2\pi(a-1)/3$, the complex vector $\sum_a n_a e^{i\phi_a}$ has squared norm $\sum_a1=3$, although the scalar sum is zero. Its real oscillatory version $p(T)=p_0\sum_a n_a\cos(\Omega T+\phi_a)$ has $\|p(T)\|^2=3p_0^2/2$, not zero for $p_0\ne0$. These are algebraic response countermodels, not radiation or master-equation calculations. A common vector response is sufficient for the scalar cancellation to carry over; it is not the only possible weighted cancellation.

**Hermitian algebra correction.** Matrix-unit multiplication gives $[T^{(x)}_{12},T^{(y)}_{12}]=iH_1$, which is outside the real Hermitian span. Under $[A,B]_{\mathrm H}=-i(AB-BA)$ the same span closes. The map $\varphi(A)=-iA$ obeys $\varphi([A,B]_{\mathrm H})=[\varphi(A),\varphi(B)]$, so it is the stated isomorphism to the traceless anti-Hermitian realization. This direct calculation checks the rule independently of the chapter's claim; agreement between two agents is not its evidence.

**Singlet counterexample and positive witness.** A uniform vector $(1,1,1)$ is unchanged by coordinate permutations but changes under the SU(3) matrix $\operatorname{diag}(e^{i\theta},e^{-i\theta},1)$ for generic $\theta$. Permutation uniformity is therefore insufficient for a singlet. Likewise, the total $T^{(x)}_{12}$ acts on $|123\rangle$ as $(|223\rangle+|113\rangle)/2$. In contrast, the normalized epsilon sum is invariant by the determinant identity. The baseline already said that the displayed assignment was one component of an antisymmetrized state; the repair fixes its surrounding stronger geometric and multipole conclusions, rather than claiming that antisymmetrization was absent.

**Stability does not select populations.** Consider a purely mathematical two-sector countermodel with the same local equation $\dot x=-x$ in each disconnected sector and energies $E_I(x)=x^2/2$, $E_{II}(x)=\Delta+x^2/2$ for $\Delta>0$. Both equilibria are stable and Family I has lower energy, but no inter-sector transitions exist. Every initial mixture of the two equilibrium point measures remains stationary; in particular all occupation can remain in Family II. A temperature label cannot impose a Gibbs ratio on this dynamics. This is an implication counterexample, not an imported assembly dynamics.

**Conditional Gaussian calculation.** Under the chapter's common measure and well assumptions, integration of the quadratic energy gives $Z_F\simeq g_F e^{-E_F^\star/\vartheta}(2\pi\vartheta)^{n/2}/\sqrt{\det H_F}$. Taking $-\vartheta\log Z_F$ yields the displayed free energy, including its measure weight and common reference-energy term. Exact sector probabilities in that Gibbs ensemble satisfy $\pi_{II}/\pi_I=Z_{II}/Z_I$. Neither calculation derives the measure from delayed histories. A unit-modulus multiplier similarly supports no automatic instability verdict: the identity return map has multiplier one and neither contraction nor exponential growth.

**Flavor residual and spectral controls.** If an arbitrary eight-component correction is allowed, choose $\Delta=M_{\mathrm{obs}}-m_0\mathbf1-\alpha t_8-\beta d_8$ to force a zero residual. That fit is a tautology, not predictive evidence. In a positive effective spectral representation, a probe with nonzero vacuum expectation contributes the constant $|\langle O\rangle|^2$ to its raw correlator; subtracting it is required before a positive excitation exponent can dominate. Even then, a probe with zero overlap on a lower state misses that state. These independently checkable observations delimit the repaired comparison templates.
