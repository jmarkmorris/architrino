## Independent mathematical checks and boundaries

For MSG-10 and the worked bound, the comparison acceleration of a point mass at distance $R_{\mathrm{eff}}$ is $G_{\mathrm{eff}}M/R_{\mathrm{eff}}^2$; its derivative with respect to a line-of-sight source displacement is $2G_{\mathrm{eff}}M/R_{\mathrm{eff}}^3$ and with respect to a transverse displacement $G_{\mathrm{eff}}M/R_{\mathrm{eff}}^3$, the eigenvalues $2/R^3$ and $-1/R^3$ of the tidal kernel $(3R_iR_j-R^2\delta_{ij})/R^5$ displayed in the owner. The branches sit at $\pm d/2$, so their difference is the response to a displacement $d\le d_0$, giving the factor $2G_{\mathrm{eff}}Md_0/R_{\mathrm{eff}}^3$ as the largest leading-order bound. Squaring, dividing by $S_a$, and integrating over $T_W$ gives the displayed $4G_{\mathrm{eff}}^2M^2d_0^2T_W/(R_{\mathrm{eff}}^6S_a)$; inverting for $M$ at $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{grav}}=\varepsilon_{\mathrm{wp}}$ gives the displayed $M_{\mathrm{crit}}$. Units: $S_a$ carries $\mathrm{m^2\,s^{-4}\,Hz^{-1}}=\mathrm{m^2\,s^{-3}}$, so $(\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}})^2\,\mathrm{s}/(\mathrm{m^2\,s^{-3}})$ is dimensionless. Numerically, with CODATA 2022 $G=6.67430\times10^{-11}\,\mathrm{m^3\,kg^{-1}\,s^{-2}}$ and the stated benchmark inputs, $|\Delta h|\le1.335\times10^{-21}\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$, $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{grav}}\le1.78\times10^{-12}$, and $M_{\mathrm{crit}}=7.49\times10^{-9}\,\mathrm{kg}\,\varepsilon_{\mathrm{wp}}^{1/2}$, agreeing with the chapter's $1.8\times10^{-12}$ and $7.5\times10^{-9}\,\mathrm{kg}$ after rounding; every scaling exponent in the two scaled displays matches the formula. For the general $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{grav}}$, if $N(t,t')=S\delta(t-t')$ then $N$ has the units of $S$ per time, the operator inverse has units of one over $[h]^2\,\mathrm{time}^2$, and the double integral is dimensionless; the white-noise reduction to $\int\Delta h_A S^{-1}_{AB}\Delta h_B\,dt$ follows because the inverse of a delta kernel is the delta kernel with the inverse matrix.

For the response model, $\varphi(u-d/2)-\varphi(u+d/2)=-d^i\partial_i\varphi(u)+O(\|d\|^3)$ because the even-order Taylor terms cancel in the difference; a scaling check on a unit Gaussian at fixed argument gave a residual whose ratio to $d^3$ was constant ($2.29\times10^{-2}$) for $d=10^{-1},10^{-2},10^{-3}$.
