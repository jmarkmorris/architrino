## Independent mathematical checks and boundaries

For SO-01, the divergence theorem states that for a continuously differentiable field on a bounded region of the flat slice the outward flux equals the volume integral of the divergence, so the numerator of $R_G$ vanishes for every smooth field, including any smooth difference of two channels. The Node witness used a midpoint quadrature on the unit cube for fields of the form $(Y_x,0,0)$: it first returned $R_G=4.7\times10^{-11}$ for the known case $Y_x=x$, whose flux and divergence integral are both one, and then $R_G=1.2\times10^{-12}$ for the difference field $Y_x=10x^2$, whose relative size against the base field was $7.7$. The old residual therefore passed a discrepancy nearly eight times the channel. With a fixed root-channel defect of $10^{-3}$ in the numerator, the old ratio was $5\times10^{-4}$, $5\times10^{-3}$, and $5\times10^{-2}$ for discrepancy scales $1$, $0.1$, and $0.01$, growing as parity improved. The repaired $E_{\mathrm{op}}$ is the relative normalized $L^2$ norm, which is zero exactly when the channels agree on the window.

For SO-02, a smooth trajectory gives $\Delta z=\dot z\,\Delta T+O(\Delta T^2)$, so $\langle\Delta z^a\Delta z^b\rangle/(2\Delta T)=\dot z^a\dot z^b\,\Delta T/2+O(\Delta T^2)\to0$. The Node witness first returned $0.712$ for the known case of Brownian increments with $D=0.7$ at $\Delta T=0.1$ and $0.01$ (the estimator is step-independent for a genuine diffusion; the $0.012$ excess is sampling error over $2\times10^5$ draws), and then $5.6\times10^{-2}$, $5.6\times10^{-3}$, and $5.6\times10^{-4}$ for the deterministic flow $dz/dT=1+\tfrac12\sin z$ at $\Delta T=0.1$, $0.01$, and $0.001$, scaling linearly with the step. The repaired display evaluates the quotients at a declared coarse-graining interval; the plateau condition is a reporting requirement, not a theorem that a plateau exists for any given reduction.

For SO-09, the canonical wake-surface density is $\rho_{m,\eta}=q_m\,\delta_\eta(r_m-c_f\Delta_m)H(\Delta_m)/(4\pi r_m^2)$, and integrating over an annulus with $dV=4\pi r_m^2\,dr_m$ cancels the surface density exactly, leaving $q_mH(\Delta_m)\int_{R_-}^{R_+}\delta_\eta\,dr_m=Q^{\mathrm{ann}}_{m,\eta}$. The Node witness first returned $1.000000000000$ for the known case of the Gaussian mollifier integrated over a wide interval, then $R_{N,m}=1.7\times10^{-15}$ for an annulus containing the whole surface at $c_f\Delta_m=2$, $\eta=0.05$, $q_m=1/6$, and $7.1\times10^{-16}$ for a partial annulus holding $34\%$ of the surface, confirming that the identity does not require the annulus to contain the surface. For the curvilinear check, $r^{-2}\partial_r(r^2F_r)=\partial_rF_r+2F_r/r$ by the product rule, which is the omitted term named in the repair. Both checks are symbolic identities with $c_f$ retained; the numerical instantiation used $c_f=1$.

For SO-10, ram-pressure balance at the head, $\rho_j(v_j-v_h)^2R_j^2=\rho_av_h^2R_h^2$, solves to $v_h=v_j\big[1+(\eta_ja_h)^{-1/2}\big]^{-1}$ with $\eta_j=\rho_j/\rho_a$ and $a_h=(R_j/R_h)^2$.
