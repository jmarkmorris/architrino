## Checkable reasoning, claim grades, and falsifiers

**MT-1: information and dynamics boundary.** Claim grade: inferred, from complete-chapter inspection and the named braid/mass owners. A shielding tuple retains whether each indexed support is coherent, but not its complete positions, velocities, causal-root history, medium coupling, or preparation measure. The mass owner requires those additional inputs, and a survival distribution requires a measure over prepared histories and a clock/readout map. The chapter cannot supply these outputs by displaying the tuple alone. This is an insufficiency finding about the presented argument, not a theorem that a completed shielding theory can never determine them. Falsifier: an exhibited retained family and uniquely specified shared maps producing the mass and lifetime data from the declared inputs; inspect the mass and branch owners, including their domains and independent evidence.

**MT-2: signed inventory versus response.** Claim grade: derived for the conditional arithmetic and logical distinction. Six negative units sum to $-6\epsilon=-|e|$ under $|e|=6\epsilon$; reversing every sign reverses that sum. In contrast, the weak-coupling selection in Quantum Number Mapping depends on docking/exposure weights in addition to the counts. The same signed count does not specify those functions or their normalization. No physically realized pair of branches with unequal couplings was constructed here. Falsifier: a derived exposure and observer map proving equal corresponding couplings over the admitted family, with charge/spin/handed-sector conventions held fixed. The complete polarity inversion leaves products $q_iq_j$ unchanged at fixed worldlines; this algebraic symmetry does not prove a conjugate solution in an unchanged, polarity-asymmetric environment or prove antiparticle mass equality there.

**MT-3: scaffold arithmetic.** Claim grade: derived conditional on the dictionary's proposed count $N_{\mathrm{scaffold}}(g)=8-2g$. Its muon class has $g=2$ and four scaffold constituents, while the electron class has $g=1$ and six. The change is $+2$, one neutral support pair, in the charged branch. The six axial constituents remain unchanged. Tau-to-muon similarly gains one pair; tau-to-electron gains two pairs in that branch. These counts neither balance the complete event, including neutral outgoing products, nor specify a source, stable association mechanism, or transition probability. Falsifier: a revised accepted scaffold assignment or an explicit constituent record contradicting the conditional count. A standard observed leptonic reaction alone does not falsify or verify the unobserved constituent routing.

**MT-4: interface identity.** Claim grade: measured by `nl -ba` comparison of the complete target and Gauge Structure Emergence lines 568–605. The latter explicitly uses $R_{\mathrm{comp}}$ and separates the two ansätze. The supporting agent measured that owner at SHA-256 `91f4531aa3ae3a009002399abc0e82e8ad6296ef3eeb3337d7e0575b407ee83b`; this is a source snapshot, not causal attribution to an earlier edit. Falsifier: a changed live owner or an explicit retained $R_L$ conversion contract that makes the old reference valid. No history claim about which editor introduced the mismatch is made.

**MT-5: ranking counterexample.** Claim grade: derived within the stated illustrative leading-order ansatz $\Delta a_\ell=\mathcal C_\ell(m_\ell R_{\mathrm{comp}})^2+\cdots$. Its free coefficients permit $\mathcal C_\mu=0$ and $\mathcal C_e\ne0$, so a larger muon mass need not produce a larger leading correction. A sensitivity comparison also divides the relevant predicted response by the combined measurement and reference uncertainty. This counterexample limits the inference licensed by the ansatz; it is not a derived Architrino branch or an experimental model. Falsifier: a derived coefficient relation and uncertainty budget establishing a quantitative ranking on a specified domain, with higher-order terms controlled.

**Observer-level source check.** Claim grade: measured by documentary comparison against the Particle Data Group's PDG Live [muon S004](https://pdglive.lbl.gov/Particle.action?init=0&node=S004) and [tau S035](https://pdglive.lbl.gov/Particle.action?init=0&node=S035) listings, accessed 2026-09-12. This validates the notation and empirical-target identification only; no measured number is fitted or newly promoted as a native prediction.
