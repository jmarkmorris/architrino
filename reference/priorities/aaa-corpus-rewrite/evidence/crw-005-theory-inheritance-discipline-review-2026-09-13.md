## Independent mathematical references

The references for the mathematical repairs are direct analytic examples independent of the chapter's expressions; no simulator or same-source fixture is used as an oracle.

1. **Optimization:** minimizing the function equal to its argument over strictly positive real arguments has infimum zero but no minimizer. A constant-zero loss on two distinct candidate maps has two minimizers. An argmin set therefore needs attainment and a declared selection rule before it denotes one frozen map. Approximate minimization must state its tolerance rather than silently assert exact attainment.
2. **Dependence:** two separately identified rows can report the same random measurement. Their record sets are disjoint but their errors coincide. For prediction minus benchmark, expanding the square gives variance equal to the two variances minus twice their covariance. Disjoint identifiers do not remove that term.
3. **Apparatus:** clocks with readouts equal to independently calibrated scale factors times elapsed time can share one inverse calibration law while having different coefficients. This is consistent apparatus input, not a fitted change of physics. Two proposed velocity corrections proportional to squared velocity with distinct coefficients agree at rest and disagree away from rest, so stationary calibration does not identify the continuation.
4. **Finite evidence:** two functions can agree on a finite sample and differ elsewhere; a polynomial formed as the product of differences from the sampled arguments supplies an explicit nonzero discrepancy vanishing on every sample. A shared generator and sampled residual cannot alone establish a domain-wide effective theorem.
5. **Autonomous projection:** consider a two-coordinate system whose first derivative is the second coordinate and whose second derivative is zero. Retaining only the first coordinate maps initial states with first coordinate zero and second coordinate plus or minus one to the same retained state but opposite derivatives. No single autonomous derivative function of that retained state represents both histories.
6. **Derivative error:** an exact constant trajectory and an approximation with constant derivative defect epsilon and identical initial value differ by epsilon times elapsed time. Arbitrarily small instantaneous defect permits large error on a sufficiently long window. This is a kinematic example, not a substrate law or numerical wake-speed instantiation.
7. **Conservation:** adding to any differentiable energy proxy the negative integral of its derivative makes a constant by construction. This does not independently identify a conserved symmetry charge. The action owner's explicit same-support counterterm obstruction, rather than agreement with an edited expression, controls TI-08.

These examples need no external factual source; they are elementary constructions proved directly here. No external status census or standard-physics premise was introduced. Existing observer-level Lorentz formulas remain comparison targets, not inputs to substrate evolution.
