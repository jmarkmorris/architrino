## Independent mathematical checks

These references are analytically separate from the chapter, not same-source simulations. Standard quantum expressions below are comparison mathematics only.

- **Canonical commutator:** for finite square matrices, cyclicity gives zero trace of a commutator. The difference from the canonical target has trace magnitude equal to dimension times the action scale. Since trace magnitude is bounded by dimension times operator norm, uniform error is at least that action scale. Low-energy matrix elements can agree while the upper truncation boundary carries the obstruction.
- **Known-case-first matrix instrument:** an in-memory Node matrix product first passed multiplication by the identity and the known square of the Pauli exchange matrix. Only then it evaluated the three-state oscillator lowering matrix. The commutator diagonal was `[1, 1.0000000000000004, -2.0000000000000004]`, the expected `[1,1,-2]` up to roundoff. The same independently controlled algebra verified that conjugating `diag(0,1)` by the Pauli exchange gives `diag(1,0)`. Unitarity alone does not conserve target energy.
- **Missing phases:** the equal positive and equal opposite-sign two-state superpositions have identical probabilities in the computational basis but opposite exchange-operator expectations. A joint-probability table cannot determine those relative phases. Classical operations that reset a bit and flip it also fail to commute, without supplying quantum amplitudes.
- **Bell:** for each deterministic local assignment, the CHSH expression is one outcome times the sum of two remote outcomes plus the other outcome times their difference. For dichotomic outcomes one bracket is zero and the other is plus or minus two. Averaging over any normalized setting-independent complete hidden state preserves the bound. Direct enumeration of all 16 assignments returned maximum two. Retained history is part of that complete state, not an exception.
- **Mixture entropy:** the equal mixture of two normalized pure states has trace one and determinant on its two-dimensional support equal to one quarter of one minus their squared overlap. Its eigenvalues are therefore one half of one plus or minus the overlap magnitude. The independently written endpoint check passed orthogonal eigenvalues `[0.5,0.5]` and identical eigenvalues `[1,0]` before the target squared overlap `0.25` gave `[0.75,0.25]`. Classical label entropy remains one bit for a fair choice even when the states coincide.
- **One-trial law versus frequency law:** let every trial copy one fair Bernoulli draw. Every marginal is fair, but every empirical frequency is either zero or one for all sample sizes. Thus the displayed concentration target requires more than a one-trial measure. Translation of a point mass also gives a valid normalized, nonstationary preparation measure.
- **Entanglement evidence:** any one joint discrete distribution is realized by a diagonal density matrix that mixes product basis projectors with those probabilities. Additional calibrated incompatible contexts or a justified witness are required to rule out that separable realization.
- **Pole order:** for a nonzero residue, the product of displacement with a pole regulated by a fixed positive imaginary part tends to zero if displacement tends to zero first. Removing that regulator first away from the pole gives the nonzero residue. Stable-pole residue extraction and finite-width resonance evaluation are different operations.
- **Regularity:** at fixed nonzero separation the native branch weight is unbounded as the causal-root Jacobian tends to zero. A distance floor does not control that factor or an uncontrolled sum of branches.
- **Action and domains:** a finite action integral over a finite interval does not prevent an escaping trajectory; it contains no universal confinement theorem. For Berry connection, a normalized local eigenvector rephasing changes the connection by an exact differential; curvature is patch invariant, and global phase requires transition functions when no global section exists. For supersymmetric energy, the quadratic form is the sum of two nonnegative squared norms divided by two, provided the adjoints and common domain are admitted. These facts justify domain repairs without assuming substrate actions.
- **Reduced coherence:** a controlled unitary correlating a two-state target with distinguishable environment states suppresses the target's off-diagonal reduced matrix elements by the environment-state overlap. This effective comparison mechanism needs no empty-vacuum premise. A second-increment covariance is positive semidefinite by the nonnegativity of every projected squared increment; the existence of the Markov diffusion limit still needs proof.
