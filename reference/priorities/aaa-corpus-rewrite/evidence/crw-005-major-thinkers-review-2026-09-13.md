## Primary-source verification

The following sources were inspected for the specific claims named, not mined in full. Their role is historical attribution or a standard-theory constraint; none is imported as an architrino substrate law.

- **Newton:** [Bentley letter, Newton Project](https://newtonproject.ox.ac.uk/view/texts/normalized/THEM00258). Newton explicitly rejects innate, unmediated action at a distance and leaves the mediating agent's nature open. This directly falsifies the baseline attribution; it does not establish Newton's endorsement of this project's medium.
- **Hume and Kant:** [Enquiry VII](https://davidhume.org/texts/e/7) and [Prolegomena, §13 remarks](https://www.earlymoderntexts.com/assets/pdfs/kant1783.pdf). These support the respective inference/necessary-connection distinction and empirical-realist qualification. The Kant source is a modernized English presentation; no claim of a new philological interpretation is made.
- **Laplace:** [translated Essay excerpt](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Extras/Laplace_Probabilities/). The stipulated intelligence has comprehensive data and analytical capacity. The source supplies the historical ideal; the forward/inverse distinction below is an independent logical check.
- **Epicurean swerve:** [Lucretius, Book II](https://classics.mit.edu/Carus/nature_things.2.ii.html), the discussion of atomic swerving and agency. This supports the attribution to the surviving Epicurean tradition, not an exact quotation from an extant Epicurus manuscript.
- **Bohr:** [1928 Quantum Postulate paper](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/72800/72800-h/72800-h.htm), especially §1. The text treats phenomena and observational arrangements together and discusses classical description. It is more specific than the baseline's generic denial-of-ontology characterization.

## Independent logical and mathematical checks

Grade: derived under the stated comparison assumptions. These checks neither prove the substrate dynamics nor decide philosophical realism. Their falsifiers are explicit counterexamples satisfying the same assumptions or an error in the displayed algebra.

1. **Forward determination versus inverse reconstruction.** The discrete map sending a real number to its square has one next state for every input, yet positive and negative one have the same next state. Thus forward uniqueness does not imply a unique past. This logical counterexample is not a claim that the master equation has that map or is irreversible. For an actual delayed law, inverse reconstruction and admissible boundary history must be analyzed separately.
2. **Bell bound.** For dichotomic deterministic local outcomes, regroup the CHSH expression into the first outcome times the sum of remote outcomes plus the second outcome times their difference. One remote sum/difference vanishes and the other has magnitude two. Integration over one setting-independent normalized common measure preserves the bound. Extending the hidden history without changing these assumptions cannot evade it. Defining the hidden variable to include setting-dependent future history instead abandons the stated independence condition.
3. **Holonomy covariance.** A based non-Abelian loop matrix transforms by conjugation under a change of local representative. Cyclicity of the trace makes its trace invariant while its entries can change. A diagonal two-by-two matrix conjugated by a right-angle determinant-one rotation exchanges its diagonal entries and preserves the trace. This supports the sole Weyl clarification without changing his historical analysis.
4. **Complex interference versus positive mixtures.** Two unit amplitudes with opposite phase sum to zero, whereas the sum of their individual squared magnitudes is two. An ordinary sum of positive classical path weights does not implement destructive interference. A single-history ontology therefore still needs an effective complex-amplitude or equivalent joint-response derivation.
5. **Descriptor versus distribution overlap.** A coarse-grained descriptor can be a function of a complete state. Preparation distributions for distinct descriptor values can then have disjoint support, even though each descriptor leaves many microscopic coordinates unknown. The everyday phrase “incomplete knowledge” therefore does not imply the technical overlapping-support property of a psi-epistemic ontological model.
6. **Thermal arrow and dynamics.** A stationary equilibrium distribution can be coarse-grained without producing a monotonic entropy increase. A thermal arrow needs additional preparation and dynamical conditions. Likewise, multiple possible attractors or periodic modes do not alone determine quantum probabilities or action quantization.
