## Independent mathematical checks and boundaries

In the Einstein equation $G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=8\pi G T_{\mu\nu}/c^4$ the term $\Lambda$ shares the units of the Einstein tensor, inverse length squared. A direct identification therefore requires a factor with units of length squared that the functional does not supply. This is a dimensional argument independent of the owner's prose; it does not say what the missing map is.
