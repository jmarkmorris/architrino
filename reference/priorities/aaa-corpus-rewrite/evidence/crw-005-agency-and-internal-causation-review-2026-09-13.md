## Independent mathematical counterexamples

For total variation on a common finite outcome alphabet, identical distributions have distance zero and disjoint unit masses have distance one. The scratch checker tests those known cases before the target counterexamples.

First, preparations a and b can both produce probabilities (0.5, 0.5), while a third preparation c produces (1, 0). The selected a/b distance is zero, but a/c distance is 0.5. Thus a null result for one pair cannot prove global external determination.

Second, keep the same two basins and the same outcome rule while preparing probability weights (0.25, 0.75) and (0.75, 0.25) on them. Total variation is 0.5, although no basin boundary changes. This is a direct counterexample to inferring changed partition geometry from changed outcome weights.

These are abstract probability counterexamples, not architrino numerical instantiations, instrumented agency measurements, or a sweep of a stand-in medium. No propagation speed is instantiated. They independently validate the logical repairs without importing an observer law into substrate dynamics.

The fixed-context object is retained as a preparation-time record. A future-window experiment additionally requires matching incoming histories or their controlled law over the window; matching an instantaneous boundary value cannot impose future equality. An unresolved correlation between preparation and incoming histories can otherwise account for a distribution difference without a causal internal-control effect.
