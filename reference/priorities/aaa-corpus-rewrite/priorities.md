## Objective

Bring all 199 documents under `content/markdown/aaa` into line with edition 1.0 of the academic style guide, so that a reader who knows neither $\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}$ nor established physics can arrive at any page and follow it.

This is an editorial program, not a theory program. It changes how the corpus explains, never what it claims. A rewrite that alters a claim, a claim grade, a falsifier, or an equation has failed, and the physics content of a converted document must survive the conversion exactly.

### How the assurance corrections relate to the rewrite

The preservation rule above governs the original style conversion. Its purpose was to make the reader-facing Markdown understandable without silently changing its scientific content. CRW-005 was the subsequent assurance stage: review the converted documents for preservation, mathematical validity, source support, accurate claim boundaries and consistency with their technical owners. Accepted substantive corrections were explicitly adjudicated as repairs and recorded separately; they were not treated as harmless style changes.

Consequently, a completed conversion could still contain a pre-existing false inference, a missing hypothesis or an obsolete app description. The assurance repairs corrected those defects in the authored reader-facing Markdown. For example, the well-posedness chapter now distinguishes shrinking adjacent refinement differences from actual convergence; the Photon Guide states the implementation's actual fit-window and denominator rules. These corrections make the clearer explanations more reliable without claiming that an open physical mechanism has been derived.

The current completion boundary is the authored-document campaign: both the original conversion and the bounded assurance dispositions are recorded as complete in the unified board. Remaining scientific derivations and separately scoped runtime obligations retain their technical owners. Neither source-edit completion nor content validation establishes that every public rendered surface has been refreshed and visually audited. The review record also distinguishes defects already present in earlier sources from conversion-introduced errors; it does not attribute all findings to the rewrite or to any one editor.

## Why This Exists

Edition 1.0 settled three rules that most of the corpus does not currently follow.

The audience is a reader who holds neither the theory nor the established physics it must recover, so both are explained rather than named. Where a document uses a concept developed elsewhere, it gives a brief clue in place and links to the full treatment, rather than assuming the reader arrived through the earlier chapter. And length is not a constraint, because the corpus is published for online access with no printed edition, which leaves the prose free to be accessible and technical at once.

Measured on 2026-09-03, **70 documents use foundational vocabulary and link to `foundations/` zero times.** Most of the corpus currently assumes a reader who started at page one. Almost nobody does: readers arrive from a search result, an external link, or the application, on an arbitrary page.

Claim grade: `measured` by scanning every corpus document outside `archie/` and `foundations/` for foundational vocabulary against the presence of any `foundations/` link. Falsifier: a repeat scan returning materially fewer such files, which would mean the gap is being closed by other work.
