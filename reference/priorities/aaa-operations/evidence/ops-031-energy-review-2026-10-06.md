The opening retains symbolic $c_f$ in derivations and normalizes only numerical examples. This paragraph is a general regime statement with no local normalized-speed declaration. Its threshold should therefore name the wake speed, or explicitly use a speed ratio.

The opening currently defines dimensional velocity and distinguishes symbolic derivations from numerical instantiations. - **Adiabatic big-O units:** the estimate $dI_a/dT=O(\epsilon_{\mathrm{ad},a})+\mathcal R_{\mathrm{int},a}$ does not prove a dimensional error. In conventional big-O notation the bound constant may carry action/time units, so $|dI_a/dT-\mathcal R_{\mathrm{int},a}|\le C\epsilon_{\mathrm{ad},a}$ is dimensionally valid with $[C]=[I]/[T]$. An explicitly uniform reference scale would improve exposition but is optional; the chapter correctly labels the dynamical result a theorem target. No repair is routed for this suspicion.
- **Candidate-charge propagation:** the positive static like-polarity normalization and outer minus sign are not invalidated by the October 5 dynamic charge-balance counterexample. Energy already denies that the candidate kernel supplies a canonical charge and requires an independently derived action/update. The Master Equation's eight pending proposals remain separate. Static sign correctness does not validate the dynamic candidate, but neither does its failure reverse the static sign.
- **Instantaneous transverse motion:** the per-hit acceleration is parallel to the emission-to-receiver line, so its contribution has no component in the transverse plane frozen at that instant. This does not claim that a moving branch frame has time-independent transverse coordinates. No repair is warranted on that reading.
