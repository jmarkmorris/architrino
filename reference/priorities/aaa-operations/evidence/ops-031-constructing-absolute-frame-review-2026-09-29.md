## CAFR29-01 — Medium: a causal-root fold does not require ribbon framing to degenerate

**Exact passage:** line 187 says “a near self-hit or a fold crossing is exactly where the framing degenerates.” Line 189 then correctly says “A causal-root fold alone does not change $Lk$” and limits changes to replacement of retained closure, collision, reconnection or framing singularity. The first sentence identifies two mathematically distinct failures without a theorem relating them. Failure of the causal-root derivative is not failure of a ribbon's normal field or disjoint edges.

**Independent geometric counterexample:** choose a regular spatial circle $\gamma(\theta)=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta,0)$ with constant unit framing $\mathbf n=(0,0,1)$ and second edge $\gamma_\epsilon=\gamma+\epsilon\mathbf n$, $\epsilon>0$. Both edges remain smooth, closed and disjoint; their ribbon and zero linking number are independent of the speed at which a member traverses the circle. With $c_f=1$, circular transmitter/receiver histories can nevertheless have a causal fold. For receiver-minus-transmitter phase $\Delta=-\pi/3$ and positive angular rate, write the angular delay as $\varphi$, and set $(\Delta+\varphi)/2=v$. The causal equation is $\beta\sin v-v=\pi/6$. At the unique $0<\xi<\pi/2$ satisfying $\tan\xi-\xi=\pi/6$, take $\beta=\sec\xi$ and $v=\xi$. The root derivative vanishes because $\beta\cos\xi=1$; its second derivative is $-\beta\sin\xi\ne0$, and speed variation unfolds an ordinary pair birth. The framed circle has not changed at all. This is prescribed geometric data, not an asserted coupled solution, physical assembly or singular continuation.

The example separates the two definitions using the chapter's Euclidean setting and causal geometry. It does not claim that a particular dynamically constructed framing can never fail at a fold. Such dependence would require its explicit construction and proof. A nonzero geometric linking number is also not needed to refute a claim that a fold necessarily degenerates every admissible framing.

**Smallest proposed repair:** replace the last clause of line 187 with “A near self-hit or causal-root fold requires the framing and retained closure to be checked separately; neither by itself proves a ribbon singularity.” Preserve the closed-ribbon restriction, the identity, and the correct next paragraph. This is substantive proposal text only, not an implemented correction.

**Consequence:** readers would otherwise confuse failure of a root chart with a topological transition. **Falsifier:** a stated chapter-specific rule constructing this exact ribbon from causal roots, together with a proof that its framing necessarily degenerates at every admitted fold or near self-hit, would narrow or remove the finding. No such rule is supplied in the inspected chapter. Route to aaa-corpus-rewrite and the existing foundation/topological-branch owner; no new theoretical campaign is needed.
