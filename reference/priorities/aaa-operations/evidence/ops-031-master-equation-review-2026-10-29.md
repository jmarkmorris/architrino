For ME29-M1, the printed expansion separates the possibly time-dependent rest orbit from its group-speed derivative. Setting that derivative to zero leaves the rest orbit's time derivative unchanged. For ME29-M2, the exact uniform-circle gap is a function of the delay alone. Its reception derivative and emission derivative are opposite. At a degenerate root both are zero, whereas the ordinary reception-fold estimate at lines 332–374 requires a nonzero reception coefficient and positive separation. A transverse fold in a speed parameter can be valid without being a reception-time transit. At fixed threshold circular parameters, a positive-separation quadratic tangency persists under reception-time translation. For a Gaussian mollifier, changing the emission displacement variable by the square root of the width gives an emission integral proportional to the inverse square root of the width; a fixed positive reception window does not remove that persistent divergence.
