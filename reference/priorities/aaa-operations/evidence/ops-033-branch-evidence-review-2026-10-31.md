## Duplication and the producer's output format

### What the scientific audit and consistency companion check

Source inspection of the [fixed scenario](../../master-equation-closure/noether-sea-research/analysis/spherical-six-pole-path-continuation-plan.md#3-fixed-law-and-starting-point), [audit9](../../master-equation-closure/noether-sea-research/evidence/spherical-six-pole-turning-point-claude-review-audit9.py), inherited [audit8](../../master-equation-closure/noether-sea-research/evidence/spherical-six-pole-speed-maximum-feedback-claude-review-audit8.py) and audit10 distinguishes three purposes. The scientific subject is the identical six-pole rest-release history in a constrained great-circle reduction at radius 10 with $c_f=1$. It retains the selected tangent interaction, discards the canonical radial acceleration and requires the geometric normal acceleration. Its common coordinate is $q$, with signed rate $w=dq/dt$. These records concern that constrained experiment, not unrestricted six-architrino evolution or physical realization.

The scientific review checks the continuation's interval records and their joins, delayed-history lookups, delay-root brackets, declared geometry domains and acceleration evaluations. Its separately authored evaluations compute tangent and radial acceleration contributions, signed radial support and support derivatives; known and deliberately incorrect controls exercise those paths. Event rulings require bounded sign changes and, where claimed, whole-interval derivative signs for uniqueness. A path turn means $w$ changes sign and the common coordinate reverses direction; a support turn concerns an extremum of the signed normal-support requirement. The [review final report](../../master-equation-closure/noether-sea-research/analysis/spherical-six-pole-post-turn-claude-review-final-report.md#6-limitations-and-premises) retains material premises, including stored rate bounds and the absence of a known negative-source-speed control in the later return-path audit. This review is conditional numerical/mathematical evidence, not premise-free validation of every trajectory claim.

The extra `frozen_consistency` field has a narrower mechanical purpose: for every certified reconstructed step with an older frozen box record at the same step, audit10 tests whether the new and old endpoint intervals overlap for both $q$ and $w$. It records `meets_q`, `meets_w`, new interval widths, ratios to old widths and their aggregate `all_meet`. It does not demand equal endpoints, equal widths or containment. Wider or narrower bounds can both be compatible with the same trajectory. Disjoint intervals expose an inconsistency requiring investigation; overlapping intervals alone do not establish either enclosure's correctness or the independence of their derivations. In particular, changing the correlations inside a saved state can preserve its coordinate-wise interval hull, as the serializer correction account explains.

The present retention review is separate from both: it checks file identities, accessibility, consumers and preservation/reproduction obligations. Its manifest hash comparisons do not rerun the scientific audit or re-certify its events.
