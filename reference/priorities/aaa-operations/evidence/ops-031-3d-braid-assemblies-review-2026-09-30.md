## S3D30-01 — medium: reconcile the two-component chart's coordinate domain and indices

**Derived contradiction, first witness.** In the all-equatorial restriction, `h_ba=0`, every one of the six endpoints in component 1 has axial coordinate `-d_C/2`, and all six in component 2 have axial coordinate `+d_C/2`. They can be twelve distinct paths: choose nonzero transverse radii and six distinct phases on each plane. No relabelling makes their axial coordinates strictly increasing. Coordinate coincidence in height is not coincidence of positions. Consequently this explicitly included planar restriction is absent from the earlier strict-spacing domain.

**Derived contradiction, second witness.** Opposite component circulation does not imply opposite circulation of the lower and upper sorted halves when component axial extents overlap. Set the two centers to `-1` and `+1`, so `d_C=2`, take half-separations `(3,4,5)` for the first component and `(1/2,3/2,5/2)` for the second, and choose positive transverse radii and antipodal neutral partners. The first component occupies axial coordinates `{-6,-5,-4,2,3,4}`; the second occupies `{-3/2,-1/2,1/2,3/2,5/2,7/2}`. Every height is distinct. Sorted positions alternate component membership across the proposed half-block split: the first six contain both components and therefore both circulation signs. This satisfies the later independent-component geometry but not the claimed inherited sorted-block circulation relation. These are prescribed geometry witnesses only, not candidate EOM solutions.

The optional complete coincident-axis decomposition at lines 457, 479 and 505 is not itself a contradiction: complete component membership and membership in that special coordinate locus are different requirements. No separate defect is inferred from the component count or names.
