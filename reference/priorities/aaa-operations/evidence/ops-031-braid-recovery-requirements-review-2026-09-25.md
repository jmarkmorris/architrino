## BRR-01 — Specify observer-chart velocity in the weak clock row

**Disposition:** proposed, low severity; notation and domain clarity, not a demonstrated physical-clock error. **Location:** line 119, the “Effective metric and weak-field gravity” row. The equation uses $d\tau_{\mathcal A}/dt_{\mathrm{eff}}$ and calibrated $c_0$, but its speed is bare $\mathbf w$, defined only as the clock's group velocity through the local Noether sea.

The live [Proper Time and Time Dilation](../../../../content/markdown/aaa/spacetime/proper-time-and-time-dilation.md) distinguishes native $\mathbf w$ in its opening phase map from $\mathbf w_{\mathrm{eff}}=d\mathbf x_{\mathrm{eff}}/dt_{\mathrm{eff}}-\mathbf u_{\mathrm{sea,eff}}$ at line 38; it explicitly says the latter is not obtained by relabeling the former. Its weak-clock formula at lines 97–102 uses $\mathbf w_{\mathrm{eff}}$ and states the small-potential, small-speed domain. The requirements chapter links this owner but leaves the velocity layer implicit in the condensed row.

**Independent argument:** under a positive rescaling of effective spatial units, $\mathbf x_{\mathrm{eff}}\mapsto a\mathbf x_{\mathrm{eff}}$ with effective time unchanged, the numerical values of $\mathbf w_{\mathrm{eff}}$ and $c_0$ both scale by $a$, leaving their squared ratio invariant. A native velocity retained in its substrate coordinates does not acquire that factor. Inserting it directly against the rescaled observer $c_0$ changes the purported dimensionless clock correction by $a^{-2}$. Thus a velocity-layer declaration or an explicit conversion is substantive even though either velocity is informally described as motion through the sea. This is a coordinate-consistency argument, not an Architrino constitutive derivation or a claim that the row necessarily intended native velocity.

**Smallest proposal:** use $\mathbf w_{\mathrm{eff}}$ in the formula and define it as the clock velocity relative to the local sea in the declared effective observer chart, as in the linked owner. Retain the same negative potential and kinetic coefficients and the recovery-target grade. A short clause specifying $U/c_0^2\ll1$ and $\|\mathbf w_{\mathrm{eff}}\|^2/c_0^2\ll1$ would make the intended leading-order scope explicit without importing a new clock law.

**Falsifier:** an existing local convention that explicitly defines this row's bare $\mathbf w$ in effective observer coordinates, or a same-record speed/ruler conversion establishing its equality to $\mathbf w_{\mathrm{eff}}$ on the claimed domain. Because the intended observer reading is plausible, this finding is a bounded definition clarification rather than an assertion that the displayed benchmark is mathematically false. A later integration should check this direct consumer and the unchanged clock owner, not start a new clock-recovery campaign.
