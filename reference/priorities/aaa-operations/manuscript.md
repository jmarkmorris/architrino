# Operating the Architrino Public Surface

## 1. Purpose and authority

AAA Operations keeps the public applications, deployment path, generated artifacts, support routes and maintenance workflows reliable, inspectable and affordable. It is an operational lane, not a theory or solver-design lane. Its policies establish how an operational claim is measured and owned; its contracts and procedures remain the executable authorities; its evidence files retain dated measurements; and its work log preserves completed records and chronology.

The academic style guide governs explanatory quality, while the operator explanation standard governs operator-facing response and discussion capture. The EOM solver remains the sole solver authority. Operational work may protect product quality, deployment reliability and evidence integrity, but it may not create a second solver path, change a scientific claim grade, or turn a display result into a physical result.

## 2. Operating policies

GitHub Pages is treated as static hosting unless a service backend is explicitly introduced. Browser runtime compute, browser heap, GPU memory and browser storage are measured separately from Pages transfer, repository storage and GitHub Actions usage. Public and review output targets 4K UHD, 3840 by 2160, while display resolution remains separate from solver authority.

Hosting decisions are evidence-bound. A change of host requires measurements of site and bundle size, static transfer, build and deployment behavior, artifact retention, browser performance, service limits and total cost. A payload that fits the current host does not by itself prove that another host is unnecessary; a measured warning does not by itself authorize migration.

Every operational control needs a measurable risk, affected public surface, owner, completion condition, and failure-and-recovery behavior. Operational polish is not a substitute for a release gate. A negative control establishes only the paths it can inspect, and a successful subset does not establish whole-system health.

Public feedback is privacy-safe by construction. Diagnostic detail is optional and collapsed by default, and the accepted public route is the Webapp Feedback sphere reached through Home, Archie and User Interface. Client analytics remain disabled unless an explicit policy changes that decision. Website Statistics is a separate zero-data static operations utility, outside the product application catalogue and public scene search. External services are user-selected and disclosed; a product page must not silently transmit sensitive or diagnostic material.

Generated artifacts retain their source ownership and declared retention policy. The one-day Pages deployment handoff is the current artifact producer; future diagnostics, captures, benchmarks and review bundles require bounded classes, retention and size ceilings. Raw scientific output and sensitive material are prohibited by default. Generated files may be prepared by their owners, but an ordinary source-edit campaign does not silently regenerate or force-add them.

The internal reference surface uses maintainable section numbering for priority manuscripts, while queue identifiers remain distinct from reader-facing section numbers. The 1-based hierarchy is applied within each manuscript from `##` through deeper headings; the document title remains unnumbered. Project skills keep shared rules in maintained instruction owners; discovery packages point to those owners rather than becoming competing policy sources. The repository skills policy requires monthly and change-triggered maintenance. Recurring work remains a live queue item with its next due date so it remains visible; each completed pass is recorded in the work log, and this cadence does not create an automatic scheduled job.

## 3. Guidance and workflow decisions

Recurring document review balances new scientific understanding and reviewer capability against the risk of introducing errors through unnecessary revision. The [periodic review procedure](../../op/periodic-document-review.md) uses frequent priority-state checks, priority-ordered corpus cycles and change-triggered dependency review. Foundations and Dynamics receive full monthly review coverage, core theory quarterly coverage, supporting exposition six-monthly coverage, and validation/simulation annual coverage at the present project stage. Monthly priority-asset sampling remains separate. Coverage is spread across manageable batches; an unchanged chapter can receive a no-edit disposition. It measures independently supported corrections, later regressions and operator burden, preserves no-change outcomes, and treats new models as candidates for a bounded comparison rather than evidence that the corpus needs another rewrite. Operations owns cadence and coverage; subject owners retain claim acceptance. The first three months are a trial, after which observed benefit and failure modes determine the interval.

Operator-facing responses follow the operator explanation standard: answer first, explain at the needed depth, capture substantive discussion in the owning document, and close substantive responses with numbered possible actions and recommendations. The academic style guide owns explanatory writing. Working documents, reader-facing corpus files, application content and protected historical records retain separate owners and write boundaries. Routine discussion capture does not authorize reader-facing edits, application changes or publication.

The primary review surface is the document that owns the full treatment. A publication-ready correction may be written directly to its reader-facing owner when the task authorizes it; material still under development remains in working documents. Confidence does not replace evidence, claim grading or required review. Generated startup guidance and reusable prompts must point to maintained instruction owners rather than copy their rules.

The sequential Office Document Standards Review is complete for its declared population: 292 Markdown documents and 166 other files, with safe guidance updates to 41 Research Office and 8 Learning Office documents. Its historical evidence, append-only records, manuscripts, production prompts, artwork and QA decisions remain protected. The technical brief and ten legacy role documents were reconciled against their live scientific owners; the brief now distinguishes the full vector acceleration residual from radial, causal-root and playback diagnostics. These are completed records, not a current product or scientific acceptance claim.

Internal section numbering is complete for the 46-file priority-manuscript population. It keeps work-queue identifiers distinct from reader-facing numbering and leaves the existing publication pipeline unchanged. The adopted skills architecture has nine project-scoped skills, with maintained instruction owners, discovery pointers, and monthly or change-triggered review. That cadence is a policy boundary, not an automatic scheduled job.

The active operational queue owns detailed work such as GitHub Presence and Community guidance review, internal reference section numbering, release-gate profile coverage, binding-corruption review, periodic skill maintenance and other accepted controls. Queue entries, completion criteria and pending decisions remain authoritative in [work-queue.md](work-queue.md); they are not reproduced here as a second task ledger.

## 5. Decisions and boundaries

The current operational decisions are to retain static hosting, keep resource classes measured separately, target 4K public and review output, require evidence before hosting changes, and preserve one solver authority. These decisions are compatible with future change when a named trigger and owner accept it; they are not permanent claims that no change will ever be needed.

Completed work records belong in [work-log.md](work-log.md), not in this manuscript. Contracts, procedures, evidence receipts and the work queue retain exact commands, versioned schemas, task status, failure classifications and recovery steps. The manuscript explains why those owners exist and what their current boundaries mean; it does not duplicate every completion receipt.

The operations lane does not independently promote internal research into reader-facing theory, certify physical recovery, authorize publication, or close a scientific queue. Corpus Dragnet may supply read-only inventories and correlation findings, while Corpus Rewrite owns reader-facing conversion. Development Process Review retains incident evidence and recovery reasoning. App Solver may own a technical manuscript about numerical computation, but the physical law and its acceptance remain with the scientific owners.

The shelved idea of rendering agent responses in a side panel is not an active task. The file-based equation-mapping surface addresses the related need for readable, symbol-supported explanations written to a known path. Reopening a chat-response surface would require a measurable file-retention risk, an owner, a completion condition and a recovery behavior; it should not be treated as an automatic extension of the existing reference surface.

## 6. How to read operational evidence

An operational result must name its instrument, scope, version and boundary. A budget checker can establish that its declared profile stayed within its declared limits; it cannot establish production-user performance. A byte-identity audit can establish equality for the inspected registry and records; it cannot establish future deployment identity. A security inventory can establish observed controls and owner actions; it cannot prove the absence of every vulnerability outside its declared dependency and source population.

This separation keeps operations useful without overstating what measurements mean. The current baselines support continued guarded operation and targeted maintenance. They do not replace live contracts, reopen completed work, or create an automatic campaign beyond the accepted queue.

## 7. Testing philosophy and execution regimen

### 7.1. Purpose and ownership

The official [Testing Philosophy and Process](../../op/testing-regime.md) owns agreement, execution situations, scope changes and continued-value review for local and GitHub PR testing. This manuscript is its explanatory companion and dated decision record. The operator requires agreed testing; neither the inventory below nor the existence of a checker grants approval for regular execution.

Testing supports three distinct decisions: whether software behaves as intended, whether a candidate is safe to publish under its declared contracts, and whether a scientific calculation has sufficient independent support for its stated claim. A test result is useful when it makes one of these decisions more reliable. Counting passing assertions without identifying the decision obscures the difference between a correctly rendered equation, a correctly implemented calculation and a physically established result.

This section is the operations account of testing purpose, selection, cost and evidence. The [testing-by-situation review](../development-process-review/analysis/testing-by-situation-review.md) retains the detailed inventory, including when each effort runs, measured timing, value and limits. Its [per-file execution record](../development-process-review/evidence/testing-baseline-2026-09-14.json) retains the September 14 measurements and original log paths. Development Process Review owns that dated investigation and failure diagnosis; operations owns the cross-repository regimen. The [publication procedure](../../op/git/pr-lifecycle.md#4-run-the-exact-state-pr-gate), executable hooks and runners retain authority over publication. The [long-running job procedure](../../op/long-running-test-heartbeats.md) owns supervision. Solver and scientific owners retain their correctness and acceptance obligations.

### 7.2. Philosophy: what a test should establish

A useful test protects a named behavior, accepted mathematical condition, interface, or operational risk. Its expected result must have a defensible origin. An independently derived closed form or separately implemented numerical reference can support a correctness claim within its domain. A saved output from the implementation under test supports regression detection or deterministic reproduction; it does not independently validate the generating rule. Rejection tests matter because a system that returns plausible output after invalid inputs, incomplete work or failed cleanup can be more misleading than one that stops visibly.

Evidence is graded at the point of use. A measured result names its instrument and inspected scope; an inference states the additional reasoning and what observation would overturn it. New one-off measurement or extraction tools first pass a known case before use on the target. A passed subset, an unavailable test, and a test that ran but failed remain distinct outcomes. File counts, test-method counts, subtest failures and assertion counts are different units and must not be combined as if they measured one coverage percentage.

The same distinction applies to operations. Checking saved browser-performance evidence establishes compliance of the recorded observation and its declared applicability; it is not a fresh browser measurement. A source-only build checks reconstruction and deployment packaging; it does not establish production-user usability. A validation receipt avoids rerunning an unchanged verified candidate; it certifies only the named checks and recorded boundaries. Passing these controls does not promote a scientific claim.

Cost is measured rather than inferred from names, file counts or problem size. Timing records identify the host, execution mode, setup/build inclusion, selected population and whether other work could interfere. Nested test durations are components of suite totals, not additional costs to add again. Deadline values bound failure handling; they are not expected runtimes. A slow but independent numerical check may be more valuable than many fast fixture comparisons. Selection should follow the consequence of failure, relevance to the change and quality of the evidence, with measured cost informing cadence.

Test maintenance should preserve useful behavioral and scientific coverage without recreating the retired Option A/B enforcement framework. A changed source hash is not itself a behavioral defect. When a test fails after a contract changes, inspect the live caller, expected behavior and reference before deciding whether to repair the implementation, repair the test, renew an explicitly owned artifact, or retire an obsolete obligation. Preserve original scientific evidence under its owner; do not strengthen claims or change a reference merely to obtain a pass.

#### 7.3.1. Interpreting failures and establishing a baseline

To disposition a failure means to determine what failed, whether the protected requirement still applies, and what action follows. The completed teardown refers to finishing the operator-approved removal of Option A/B enforcement and its obsolete machinery; it does not mean removing every test that happens to fail during that work. The following distinctions guide investigation rather than preassigning any particular failure to a category.

| What investigation establishes | Appropriate response |
| --- | --- |
| The test enforces a requirement explicitly retired by the operator | Remove that obsolete test or obsolete assertion while preserving any still-required behavior it also covers |
| The requirement remains valid, but the test setup, fixture or interface expectation is outdated | Repair the test to exercise the current contract without weakening its intended check |
| The requirement remains valid and the implementation violates it | Repair the implementation and retain a regression test that demonstrates the defect |
| Required input or execution capability is unavailable | Record the missing prerequisite and unexecuted coverage; do not report a pass |
| Files changed or disappeared during the run, so the outcome cannot be assigned to one source state | Retain the observation and rerun against a stable selection before drawing a candidate-level conclusion |

For example, an assertion demanding rejection solely because a retired source pin changed may be obsolete; an assertion requiring rejection of incomplete numerical output can remain essential. A cancellation test reporting failure is evidence to inspect, not proof that cleanup is broken or that its test is obsolete. Several failing assertions can share one missing fixture or changed interface, so 75 Node failures do not establish 75 independent defects.

Sources have settled for a baseline when the intended edits are complete and the relevant source files, fixtures, test selection and generated prerequisites remain unchanged through the run. This does not require a pristine repository or a new commit: intentional uncommitted work can be the measured candidate. First rerun each retained failing test after its cause is resolved, then run the ordinary sweep to detect interactions or regressions beyond those focused tests. A stable failing run is still a useful baseline if the failures and skips are explicitly accounted for; a healthy passing baseline requires the applicable checks to pass. Repeating the same failure against unchanged inputs does not by itself diagnose it.

The September 14 ordinary and slow Node rows report runner test outcomes. The Python row reports file outcomes: a failing file can contain many passing methods alongside one or more failures or errors. Its 83 files reported 1,698 test methods in total. The rows therefore cannot be compared as pass percentages with a common denominator. The 18 skipped Node outcomes did not establish the skipped behavior. Their reasons need review just as failures do, although an intentional skip may have a valid owner and prerequisite. Timings show the cost of those particular runs, including failed work and setup; they are evidence for planning, not a settled cost for the future test population.

#### 7.3.3. Retention must be justified by consequences

A description of a test's activity is not a value statement. The reason to keep a test is that it detects a consequential mistake in software or a calculation someone currently uses, before that mistake costs more to discover or misleads a decision. Runtime alone does not establish that value. Neither does the existence of an elaborate reporting pipeline establish that the pipeline deserves continued maintenance. The recommendation below concerns useful coverage; it does not defend every assertion, fixture, dependency or current implementation in a test file.

The concrete examples below are prospective failure consequences inferred from inspection of the linked tests in §7.3.2 and their callers. They are not claims that these exact defects have occurred, nor measured estimates of money or researcher time saved. The September 14 run measures execution time and outcomes; it does not measure defect-detection history, false alarms, maintenance burden or unique coverage relative to every other test. #### 7.3.4. Operator decision: four suites removed from rotation

On September 14, 2026, the operator removed the EQ-21 nonlinear transfer, halo/cluster transfer, shear/RSD transfer and prescribed structural-root ledger suites from regular testing. They remain available only for explicitly selected work. Their filenames now end in `.manual.js`, preserving their test bodies and relative imports while excluding them from Node's default discovery, `npm test`, the ordinary sweep and the slow sweep. Their entries have been removed from the slow manifest. The historical tables and receipts above retain the measured ten-file baseline; the current slow selection has six files.

Explicit invocation uses `node --test --test-concurrency=1 <path>` with one of these paths:

- `tests/eq21-nonlinear-transfer-child-residual.manual.js`
- `tests/eq21-halo-cluster-transfer-child-residual.manual.js`
- `tests/eq21-shear-rsd-transfer-child-residual.manual.js`
- `tests/coincident-midpoint-common-frequency-prescribed-structural-root-ledger.manual.js`

Do not restore these suites to regular discovery, a sweep, a publication gate or a scheduled rotation merely because their code changes or they pass. Reintroduction requires a concrete current consumer and decision, a consequential error the test detects, evidence that existing simpler coverage does not suffice, measured execution and maintenance cost, and a justified trigger or cadence accepted by the operator. This decision preserves the research implementations and evidence; it does not declare them physically valid or retire their pipelines.

### 7.4. Official process and approval reconciliation

The [Testing Philosophy and Process](../../op/testing-regime.md) is the canonical process owner. It covers the purpose of different kinds of testing, the need for operator agreement, local and GitHub PR execution, timing and maintenance burden, failure handling and retirement. Its decision-table requirements include value, current consumer, scope, evidence quality, trigger, timing, blocking status and approval source. The publication procedure retains execution authority over its hooks and receipts; the scientific owners retain their claim requirements.

The operator reports that uncontrolled testing may have cost three to seven weeks of progress. This is the operator's estimate, not a measured attribution of project delay. It motivates a scope constraint: tests must serve agreed decisions, and their presence or good intentions do not justify recurring obligations. Existing agreements persist within scope, but a one-time diagnostic does not become routine testing and a useful local check does not automatically become a required PR gate.

The four removed suites remain explicit-use only. Other efforts require justification within the scope in which they are proposed; their existence does not require a comprehensive approval audit, repair campaign or replacement. The operator's review of “Remove nonsense processes” emphasizes that maintaining and investigating process machinery must not displace the project. No clean broad baseline or completed inventory is a prerequisite to resuming theory or development. Address an actual blocking PR check in that publication task, and keep the decision scoped to that check. The policy is documented; no new automated approval machinery or CI setting is introduced by this documentation change.
