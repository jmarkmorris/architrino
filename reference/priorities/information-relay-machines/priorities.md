# Information Relay Machines

## Current

`Information Relay Machines` is the selected working name for the cross-domain idea that communication, industrial processes, technological workflows, and institutional procedures can be mapped as record-preserving transduction chains. The technical subtitle is `record-preserving transduction chains`.

This packet is priority-stage material. It is not yet corpus canon. It should preserve the idea, split paper and outreach work cleanly, and keep claim levels visible until individual examples are strong enough for promotion.

## Objective

Develop at least one paper and a reusable mapping method for how selected information survives through chains of physical carrier handoffs, encoding changes, filtering, error correction, calibration, and reconstruction.
