## Classification rules

- **Geometry-retained:** root existence, history coverage, interval geometry, or architecture remains valid independently of acceleration strength.
- **Diagnostic-only:** implementation, cost, display, or failure behavior remains informative but cannot support the proposed equation.
- **Old-law-only:** a measured acceleration, evolution, event impulse, or acceptance result consumed the receiver-weighted law and does not transfer.
- **Rerun-eligible:** retained inputs or architecture may be reused only after a new accepted evaluator, schema, and independent control exist.

One artifact may have different dispositions for different claims. The disposition attaches to the claim, not merely the filename.
