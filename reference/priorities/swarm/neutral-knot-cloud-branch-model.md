# Neutral Knot-Cloud Branch Model

Promotion status: `priority-only`. This packet generalizes the tri-binary architecture from three assumed binary slots to a neutral six-architrino free-support branch. The old binary-slot, exact-antipodal, same-level, nested, and hybrid variable-radius packets become sector charts of this broader branch family rather than mandatory ontology.

The working label `neutral knot-cloud` means a time-dense, possibly knotted support branch made from three positive and three negative architrinos. It is not a probabilistic quantum cloud by default. The cloud is the coarse-grained occupancy measure generated by fast closed paths in a support band. Pairing, binary slots, antipodal symmetry, and common radius are optional reductions that must be certified when used.

This packet refines [hybrid-variable-radius-model-card.md](hybrid-variable-radius-model-card.md), [free-support-bounded-speed-dynamics.md](free-support-bounded-speed-dynamics.md), [bounded-speed-factor-finite-mode-branch-system.md](bounded-speed-factor-finite-mode-branch-system.md), [bounded-speed-factor-branch-search-certificate.md](bounded-speed-factor-branch-search-certificate.md), [attraction-repulsion-inventory-theorem.md](attraction-repulsion-inventory-theorem.md), and [central-inventory-and-event-ledgers.md](central-inventory-and-event-ledgers.md). It does not retain a branch or authorize corpus migration.

## 1. General Neutral Branch

Let

$$
I=\{1,\ldots,6\}
$$

index the Noether-core architrinos. A neutral branch supplies a polarity map

$$
\sigma:I\to\{+1,-1\},
\qquad
\#\{i:\sigma_i=+1\}=3,
\qquad
\#\{i:\sigma_i=-1\}=3,
$$

so that

$$
Q_{\mathrm{core}}
=
\epsilon\sum_{i\in I}\sigma_i
=0.
$$

No pairing relation is part of the base definition. The branch variables are six closed arclength curves

$$
\mathbf{Y}_i:\mathbb{R}/L_i\mathbb{Z}\to\mathbb{R}^3,
\qquad
\|\mathbf{Y}_i'(\lambda)\|=1,
$$

positive bounded speed factors

$$
0<\nu_-\le\nu_i(\lambda)\le\nu_+<\infty,
$$

and causal-time maps

$$
\chi_i(\lambda)
=
\int_0^\lambda\frac{d\xi}{\nu_i(\xi)},
\qquad
\Lambda_i=\chi_i^{-1},
\qquad
H_i=\chi_i(L_i).
$$

The physical trajectory in the normalized center-time chart is

$$
\mathbf{x}_i(u)=\mathbf{Y}_i(\Lambda_i(u)).
$$

The equality or winding period row is still required:

$$
H_i=H_*,
$$

or

$$
m_iH_i=H_{\mathrm{com}}.
$$

The base branch is therefore

$$
\mathfrak{K}_\nu
=
\left(
I,\sigma,
\mathbf{Y},
\nu,
\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{supp}},
\mathcal{A}_\nu,
\mathsf{Action}^{\nu},
\mathsf{Event}^{\nu},
\mathsf{Inventory}
\right).
$$

The old tri-binary notation $i=(a,\sigma)$ with $a\in\{1,2,3\}$ is allowed only after a packet declares a partition of $I$ into three two-site blocks. Without that partition, the correct source sums range over all $j\ne i$.

## 2. Hollow Support Band

Let $\mathbf{C}$ be the center-gauge point and define

$$
r_i(\lambda)=\|\mathbf{Y}_i(\lambda)-\mathbf{C}\|.
$$

A general knot-cloud support descriptor is an annular or hollow support band

$$
\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{supp}}^{\mathrm{knot}}
=
\left(
\mathbf{C},
R_{\mathrm{in}},
R_{\mathrm{out}},
B_i^-,
B_i^+,
\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{cent}},
\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{sector}}
\right),
$$

with

$$
0<R_{\mathrm{in}}<R_{\mathrm{out}}.
$$

The support inequalities are

$$
B_i^-(\lambda)
=
r_i(\lambda)-R_{\mathrm{in}}
\ge0,
\qquad
B_i^+(\lambda)
=
R_{\mathrm{out}}-r_i(\lambda)
\ge0.
$$

For the example band suggested by the operator,

$$
R_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac12R_*,
\qquad
R_{\mathrm{out}}=3R_*.
$$

The hollow center condition is the exclusion row

$$
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{hole}}
=
\max_i
\sup_\lambda
\left(R_{\mathrm{in}}-r_i(\lambda)\right)_+.
$$

This row says only that the six neutral-core architrinos do not enter the central hollow. It does not decide what lives inside the hollow. A charged branch, central-inventory representative, or other inner occupant must carry its own noncollision, action, root, event, and provenance rows.

The band-width ratio is

$$
\beta_{\mathrm{band}}
=
\frac{R_{\mathrm{out}}}{R_{\mathrm{in}}}.
$$

A narrow same-level sector has $\beta_{\mathrm{band}}\approx1$ after radius normalization. A general knot-cloud branch may have $\beta_{\mathrm{band}}\gg1$ while still remaining bounded and noncolliding.

## 3. Cloud As Coarse-Grained Occupancy

The cloud is the causal-time occupancy measure of the closed paths. For a Borel region $A\subset\mathbb{R}^3$, define

$$
\mu_i^\nu(A)
=
\frac1{H_i}
\int_0^{H_i}
\mathbf{1}_A(\mathbf{Y}_i(\Lambda_i(u)))\,du.
$$

The signed and unsigned coarse-grained densities at smoothing scale $\delta$ are

$$
n_{\mathrm{occ},\delta}(\mathbf{x})
=
\sum_{i\in I}
\frac1{H_i}
\int_0^{H_i}
K_\delta(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{Y}_i(\Lambda_i(u)))\,du,
$$

and

$$
n_{\mathrm{sgn},\delta}(\mathbf{x})
=
\sum_{i\in I}
\sigma_i
\frac1{H_i}
\int_0^{H_i}
K_\delta(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{Y}_i(\Lambda_i(u)))\,du.
$$

These are occupancy densities, not probabilities unless a separate normalization and observer rule is declared. A branch may look cloud-like to a coarse observer when $n_{\mathrm{occ},\delta}$ varies slowly compared with the path recurrence time, even though the primitive branch is six deterministic closed paths.

A coverage claim is optional. If the branch claims that the Noether core forms an enclosing support cloud over an annular region

$$
\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{supp}}
=
\{\mathbf{x}:R_{\mathrm{in}}\le\|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{C}\|\le R_{\mathrm{out}}\},
$$

it must emit coverage rows such as

$$
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{cover}}^{\delta}
=
\sup_{\mathbf{x}\in\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{supp}}}
\left(
n_{\min}-n_{\mathrm{occ},\delta}(\mathbf{x})
\right)_+,
$$

and signed-balance rows such as

$$
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{sgn}}^{\delta}
=
\sup_{\mathbf{x}\in\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{supp}}}
|n_{\mathrm{sgn},\delta}(\mathbf{x})|.
$$

These rows are not required for every branch. They are required only when the branch claims cloud-like containment, shielding, or near-neutral coarse exposure.

## 4. Optional Pairing And Binary Slots

A pair structure is an involution

$$
\iota:I\to I,
\qquad
\iota^2=\mathrm{id},
\qquad
\iota i\ne i,
\qquad
\sigma_{\iota i}=-\sigma_i.
$$

It is a derived row, not base ontology. If a packet declares paired behavior, it must emit pair residuals. For exact antipodality around $\mathbf{C}$,

$$
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{anti},i}
=
\sup_\lambda
\left\|
\mathbf{Y}_{\iota i}(\lambda+\Delta_i)
+
\mathbf{Y}_i(\lambda)
-2\mathbf{C}
\right\|,
$$

with the speed parity row

$$
\mathcal{R}_{\nu\mathrm{pair},i}
=
\sup_\lambda
|\nu_{\iota i}(\lambda+\Delta_i)-\nu_i(\lambda)|.
$$

A binary-slot structure is a partition

$$
\mathcal{P}
=
\{P_1,P_2,P_3\},
\qquad
|P_a|=2,
\qquad
\sum_{i\in P_a}\sigma_i=0.
$$

Once $\mathcal{P}$ is declared, the old slot notation $i=(a,\sigma)$ becomes available. The slot radii are then derived from [hybrid-support-radius-functional.md](hybrid-support-radius-functional.md), not assumed:

$$
R_a=\mathscr{R}_a(\mathbf{Y},\nu,\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{supp}},\mathcal{P}).
$$

The binary-slot reduction row is

$$
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{slot}}
=
\left(
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{partition}},
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{anti}},
\mathcal{R}_{\nu\mathrm{pair}},
\mathcal{R}_{R\mathrm{def}},
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{slot\text{-}ledger}}
\right).
$$

If $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{slot}}$ is absent or fails, the branch remains a general neutral knot-cloud branch. It is not a failed Noether-core branch merely because no clean binary pairing exists.

## 5. Topological And Knotted Support Data

Let

$$
\Gamma=\bigcup_{i\in I}\operatorname{im}\mathbf{Y}_i
$$

be the labeled path union. A topological statement is meaningful only when the branch has a tubular noncollision floor:

$$
d_{\min}
=
\inf_{i\ne j,\lambda,\lambda'}
\|\mathbf{Y}_i(\lambda)-\mathbf{Y}_j(\lambda')\|
>2\epsilon_{\mathrm{tube}}.
$$

Then the branch may attach a labeled link or knot invariant

$$
\mathcal{K}_{\mathrm{knot}}
=
\left(
\operatorname{Link}_{ij},
\operatorname{Wr}_i,
\operatorname{Braid}_{\Pi},
\operatorname{Framing}_i,
\operatorname{Provenance}
\right),
$$

with the understanding that these are branch classifiers until the dynamics closes. The topology cannot replace the force, action, root, support, or event rows.

An event that changes the labeled link class must be ledgered. Typical topology-changing events are:

| Event | Required row |
| --- | --- |
| path close approach | noncollision floor and event normal form |
| self-hit window | self-hit return and exchange closure |
| root-sheet fold | bounded-speed root-fold normal form |
| support-boundary contact | support multiplier or event exchange row |
| central-hollow contact | central exclusion event and provenance row |

## 6. Root Ledger And Dynamics

For every ordered source pair $i\ne j$, retained causal roots solve

$$
G_{ij,\alpha}^{\nu}(u,\eta)
=
\left\|
\mathbf{Y}_i(\Lambda_i(u))
-
\mathbf{Y}_j(\Lambda_j(u-\eta))
\right\|
-\eta
=0.
$$

The bounded-speed Jacobian is

$$
J_{ij,\alpha}^{\nu}
=
1-\nu_j^-\mathbf{T}_j^-\cdot\widehat{\mathbf{R}}_{ij,\alpha}.
$$

The general force ledger is

$$
F_i^\nu(u)
=
\sum_{j\ne i}
\sum_{\alpha\in\mathcal{A}_{ij}^{\nu}(u)}
\sigma_i\sigma_j
\frac{\widehat{\mathbf{R}}_{ij,\alpha}(u)}
{\eta_{ij,\alpha}(u)^2|J_{ij,\alpha}^{\nu}(u)|}
+
F_{i,\mathrm{self}}^\nu
+
F_{i,\mathrm{med}}^\nu
+
F_{i,\mathrm{supp}}^\nu.
$$

The structural attraction/repulsion inventory survives without pairing. For each receiver $i$,

$$
N_{\mathrm{attr},i}
=
\#\{j\ne i:\sigma_i\sigma_j=-1\}
=3,
$$

and

$$
N_{\mathrm{rep},i}
=
\#\{j\ne i:\sigma_i\sigma_j=+1\}
=2.
$$

The weighted force implication is still a delayed projection problem:

$$
F_i^\nu
=
F_{i,\mathrm{attr}}^\nu
+
F_{i,\mathrm{rep}}^\nu
+
F_{i,\mathrm{self}}^\nu
+
F_{i,\mathrm{med}}^\nu
+
F_{i,\mathrm{supp}}^\nu.
$$

The bounded-speed dynamics rows are

$$
R_{T,i}^\nu
=
\nu_i\nu_i'
-
\Gamma_B^\nu\mathbf{T}_i\cdot F_{i,\mathrm{tot}}^\nu,
$$

and

$$
R_{N,i}^\nu
=
\nu_i^2\mathbf{K}_i
-
\Gamma_B^\nu P_i^\perp F_{i,\mathrm{tot}}^\nu.
$$

The speed ODE must close over the declared period:

$$
\int_0^{H_i}
\mathbf{T}_i(u)\cdot F_{i,\mathrm{tot}}^\nu(u)\,du
=0,
$$

with primitive excursion inside the speed band.

## 7. Containment And Central Inventory

The word `containment` is not a primitive force law. In this packet it means one or more of the following certified rows:

1. hollow support exclusion for the six neutral-core paths;
2. support-band viability under the dynamics and support multipliers;
3. coarse-grained occupancy coverage if cloud-like enclosure is claimed;
4. Noether-Sea exchange and response rows if the branch claims a medium-supported boundary;
5. central-inventory provenance rows if charged or inner architrinos occupy the hollow region.

Let $\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{cent}}$ denote any central inventory placed inside the hollow region. Its exclusion from the neutral-core paths is

$$
\inf_{i\in I,\,a\in\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{cent}},\,u,v}
\|\mathbf{x}_i(u)-\mathbf{x}_a(v)\|
\ge
d_{\mathrm{cent}}>0.
$$

Its charge and provenance rows remain separate:

$$
Q_{\mathrm{total}}
=
Q_{\mathrm{core}}+Q_{\mathrm{cent}},
\qquad
Q_{\mathrm{core}}=0.
$$

Thus a neutral knot-cloud core can provide a hollow support band around a central inventory, but it does not by itself explain a charged fermion. The charged branch must still supply the central-inventory ledger in [central-inventory-and-event-ledgers.md](central-inventory-and-event-ledgers.md).

## 9. Sector Reductions

The old architectures are recovered as reductions of the general branch:

| Sector | Added rows |
| --- | --- |
| exact-antipodal binary | involution $\iota$, antipodal geometry, speed parity, pair-root parity |
| same-level tri-binary | partition $\mathcal{P}$, small slot-radius spread, common support band |
| nested tri-binary | partition $\mathcal{P}$, ordered slot radii, persistent radius gaps |
| hybrid variable-radius | partition $\mathcal{P}$ plus support-radius functional and transition rows |
| free knot-cloud | no partition; hollow support band, occupancy density, topology, and all-pairs root ledger |

The proof direction is one-way:

$$
\text{general retained branch with optional slot rows}
\Longrightarrow
\text{sector branch}.
$$

The reverse implication is false unless the sector certificate also proves that no required general-case rows were hidden by the symmetry ansatz.
