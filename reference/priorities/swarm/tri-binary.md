## Scope

This workstream now owns development of the general neutral knot-cloud branch model, the candidate hybrid variable-radius tri-binary sector, and the eventual user-facing corpus merge decision. The broad base case is a neutral six-architrino Noether-core branch: three positive and three negative architrinos on closed free-support bounded-speed paths in $\mathbb{R}^3$, with no required binary pairing until a branch certificate proves it. The candidate model treats nested and same-level tri-binary geometries as sectors of this broader branch family rather than as mutually exclusive ontologies. Fixed radial nesting becomes a sector condition; same-level choreography becomes a small radius-spread sector; exact-antipodal binaries become a high-symmetry chart; and a wide hollow support band may remain a general knot-cloud sector. The direction for the hybrid model is to use neutral slot labels, preferably $a\in\{1,2,3\}$, only after a partition into three binary slots is declared and certified. Roman labels `I,II,III` are not preferred because `I` collides with the old inner-binary label and the sequence can still imply hierarchy. Transition rows decide whether `I/M/O`, `H/M/L`, `HML/HLM`, `inner`, `middle`, and `outer` survive only as sector-local geometric-order, continuation-history, weak-stress-role, ordered-frame/chirality, or rejected labels. This does not yet authorize corpus-wide replacement or merge edits. User-facing migration must wait until the general and sector forms have been stated with enough precision to preserve charge bookkeeping, conservation ledgers, Standard Model-facing mappings, Lorentz / photon closure targets, strong-field behavior, and simulation reproducibility.

The first priority is not migration. It is to develop the hybrid architecture documents in this directory while the architecture is under review. Corpus migration, priority-ledger rewrites outside this bucket, simulations, and web/app asset changes should stay deferred until the hybrid user-facing corpus merge gate passes. During this phase, identify affected theory surfaces, decide what survives as a theorem target, and keep both the same-level path and nested tri-binary fallback path legible while the merged architecture is derived.
