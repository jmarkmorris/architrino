- Record constraints that shaped the search, especially parsimony constraints, rejected alternatives, pruning decisions, and failed branches.

- Preserve the role of dissatisfaction with existing theory as a search driver without overclaiming that dissatisfaction proves the replacement architecture.
- Keep $\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}$ proof burdens separate from the personal path that made the architecture discoverable.
- Translate legacy vocabulary into current terminology internally, but preserve legacy wording when it is needed to identify or interpret a source artifact.
