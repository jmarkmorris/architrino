**G1 — Projection domains need an explicit identification before the common-history expression is a fully typed mathematical contract.** The canonical Formal Audit defines bridge source/target domains as D-i/D-j, but at line 125 declares P-i from H to O-i and then applies the bridge to P-i(H) at line 131. An intended identification of each observer-record space with the relevant bridge domain, or a stated embedding into it, makes the intended comparison intelligible; it is not supplied explicitly. This is inherited notation incompleteness, not a defect created by the new coverage. A future mathematical completion should state the domains and admissible overlap; this editorial disposition does not certify that completion.

**G2 — The exact scale composition and inventory-flow proposal remains guessed, with its algebra and physical accounting unspecified.** Canonical lines 152–168 expressly grade the equations as a candidate target. They do not yet define the types of coarse-graining maps on histories, an additive or signed inventory space for subtraction, the sign convention for boundary flow, or the condition separating a pure resolution change from actual physical crossings. Those definitions matter before one can test whether boundary transport has been counted once or whether exact composition is appropriate. The coverage correctly preserves “guessed” rather than presenting an achieved conservation or semigroup theorem. Acceptance of a chapter destination does not remove this substantive mathematical burden.

**G3 — The exact scale fiber and protected-factorization statement are conditional structures, not reconstruction results.** The set written at canonical lines 170–188 is a well-defined fiber once its history space, record space and projection are fixed. Calling it the zero-tolerance specialization of the earlier bridge fiber additionally requires identifying the earlier source with the history space and its map with the scale projection, and requires a residual whose zero distinguishes the intended records (or an explicit observational quotient). The preceding text permits a generic distance or residual. The protected quantity must be constant on each fiber for the displayed factorization to exist. The chapter states that condition and denies unique reconstruction, so no strengthening is introduced by the coverage; the needed domain and separation assumptions remain unproved or unstated in this bounded review.

**G4 — The primary literature and outside historical/scientific owners remain beyond this review's reading.** No external source was opened for this assignment; no scientific runtime, data analysis or historical Git transition was examined. The Weyl episode, Major Thinkers, gauge chapters and other domain owners were not read in full. The bridge matrix's PDG 2020 reference differs from the chapter's 2024 reference; edition equivalence is not established here. The reviewer encountered a bounded PDG 2024 numerical passage in the preceding electromagnetic assignment, but that does not verify this lane's Fermi bridge or count as a full review of the paper. Historical source support and scientific mechanisms therefore retain their original limits. The eight-row sample remains nonexhaustive.
