# OpenAlex Baseline For Breather Certificate And Planar Bridge

## Status

This note records the May 18, 2026 OpenAlex baseline for priority rank 2,
`proof-programs`: the finite collinear-breather certificate and the queued
planar bridge closure. It is priority material, not authored AAA prose.

Method: use the OpenAlex Works API with title-focused searches and sort by
`cited_by_count:desc`. Broad full-text searches were noisy and pulled in
high-citation works outside the proof-program subject, so they are rejected as
baseline evidence here. Citation counts below are OpenAlex `cited_by_count`
values observed on May 18, 2026, not claims about mathematical importance.

## Subject Split

The workstream subject has four distinct literature lanes:

1. State-dependent delay differential equations, because the AAA return map is
   not an ordinary finite-dimensional ODE return map.
2. Periodic solutions and computation of delay-equation orbits, because the
   next AAA object is one instantiated candidate cycle plus a finite audit.
3. Breather solutions in nonlinear wave theory, because the word `breather`
   has a strong external meaning that must remain an analogy unless the AAA
   delayed system supplies its own return-map proof.
4. Direct-action or Fokker/Wheeler-Feynman electrodynamics, because the local
   AAA chapters already mention this as a comparison class, but the AAA law is
   delayed-only causal-wake dynamics rather than a direct import.

## Abstract-Confirmed Refinements

OpenAlex had abstracts for three especially useful records:

- [Finding periodic orbits in state-dependent delay differential equations as roots of algebraic equations](https://openalex.org/W2008866301) explicitly frames periodic boundary-value problems for delay differential equations as locally equivalent to finite-dimensional algebraic systems under regularity assumptions tied to the state-dependent-delay review literature. This directly supports the finite residual vector
  $$
  \mathcal R_{\mathrm{cand}}(\mathbf a)
  $$
  used below.
- [Collocation Methods for the Computation of Periodic Solutions of Delay Differential Equations](https://openalex.org/W2011033548) treats periodic DDE computation as a periodic two-point boundary-value problem, uses piecewise polynomial collocation, and discusses mesh-point convergence and adaptive mesh selection. This supports keeping the AAA candidate packet finite, mesh-explicit, and separator-refined.
- [Nonexistence of small-amplitude breather solutions in phi^4 theory](https://openalex.org/W1964131158) reports that an all-orders formal breather expansion can still fail to correspond to a true breather, with approximate breathers radiating slowly. This strengthens the AAA rule that an ansatz or residual fit is not enough; returned-history residuals, fold-layer budgets, and self-map closure must be certified before any breather claim is promoted.
- The state-dependent-delay baseline strengthens the certificate-level state-space requirement. [Functional Differential Equations with State-Dependent Delays: Theory and Applications](https://openalex.org/W1535375949) emphasizes that a state-dependent delay may be implicit, functional, integral, or differential, and may need to be treated as part of the state data. [The solution manifold and C1-smoothness for differential equations with state-dependent delay](https://openalex.org/W1988906184) then supplies the compatible local target: the return map and its variational row should be posed on histories satisfying endpoint compatibility, not on arbitrary $C^1$ histories. For the collinear certificate this means the packet must record the first-order state $Y=(x,u)$, the compatibility row $\dot X(0)=U(0)$ and $\dot U(0)=F_\eta(\Phi)$, and the tangent compatibility used by the monodromy diagnostic.
- The remaining periodic-orbit construction sources are exhausted as candidate-generation discipline. Continuation, finite auxiliary ODE systems, iteration, and bifurcation curves can discover or rank candidates, but the accepted proof object remains one frozen packet identity with projection/reconstruction data, separator/fold-layer budgets, branch-chart margins, interval residuals, and returned-history rows.
- The direct-action electrodynamics sources are exhausted as contrast-class discipline. They sharpen the exclusion that the breather certificate uses
  $$
  \mathcal{C}_{ij}(t)
  =
  \{\,t_0<t:\|\mathbf{x}_i(t)-\mathbf{x}_j(t_0)\|=c_f(t-t_0)\,\},
  $$
  so future-lightcone data, half-advanced terms, and absorber-response assumptions cannot become causal-root ledger rows.

## Claim Map

- Ontology: no external source changes AAA ontology. The architrino, causal wake, absolute time, and Euclidean void commitments remain internal.
- Derivation/closure target: the external state-dependent-delay and collocation literature strengthens the existing target that the breather proof must construct one finite candidate cycle, a same-domain causal pre-ledger, an authorized branch chart, returned-sample residuals, certified topology, and a closed convex tame self-map domain.
- Effective summary: `breather` is acceptable as a compact label for a bounded return-map fixed point, but external NLS or sine-Gordon breathers are comparison objects, not mechanisms.
- Speculation: direct-action electrodynamics may help compare action principles later, but it should not be attached to the current certificate unless the potential/action workstream explicitly asks for that comparison.

## Concrete Mathematical Advance

The OpenAlex baseline makes the next candidate-cycle route sharper: treat
the candidate construction as a finite residual problem before any theorem
promotion.

Let
$$
\mathbf a
=
\left(
T_{\mathrm{cyc}},
\mathcal S,
\mathcal P,
\mathbf c
\right)
$$
collect the period, section/symmetry data, parameter tuple, and finite
representation coefficients for
$$
\phi_{\mathrm{cyc}}(\cdot;\mathbf a).
$$
Define the candidate residual vector
$$
\mathcal R_{\mathrm{cand}}(\mathbf a)
=
\left(
G_{\mathrm{sec}},
G_{\mathrm{sym}},
G_{\mathrm{per}},
\{E_j\}_{j\in\Theta_{\mathrm{simple}}},
\{I_{\Sigma,k}\}_{k},
\{R_j^x,R_j^v\}_{j\in\Theta}
\right),
$$
where the terms enforce section anchoring, symmetry and periodicity, simple-root
equation residuals, fold-layer integral residuals, and returned-history sample
residuals. A numerical, quadrature, closed-form, or collocation candidate is
acceptable only when it supplies this residual vector on the same packet identity
tuple
$$
\mathfrak{I}_{\mathrm{seed}}
=
\left(
\mathcal{K},
T_{\mathrm{cyc}},
\mathcal{S},
\mathcal{P},
\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{rep}},
\Theta
\right).
$$

If an interval-Newton, Krawczyk, or equivalent interval-collocation enclosure is
used, the useful certificate target is
$$
K(X)\subset \operatorname{int}(X)
$$
for the finite coefficient box
$$
X\ni\mathbf a,
$$
together with strict pre-ledger gaps, branch-chart Jacobian floors, and
returned-sample residual budgets on the same mesh. This does not prove the
breather by itself; it only upgrades `candidate data absent` to a candidate
packet ready for the null-coordinate pre-ledger.

## Rejected Signals

- Broad OpenAlex full-text searches were rejected because citation sorting
  surfaced high-citation works with weak lexical overlap rather than subject
  relevance.
- The source's Hopf-bifurcation application and suggested branching extensions
  are rejected for the current mining pass. They are useful evidence that
  finite algebraic reductions can transfer bifurcation machinery, but the
  present proof-program bottleneck is earlier: one candidate cycle, projection
  and reconstruction data, a same-domain pre-ledger, branch-chart margins, and
  returned-history residuals.
- The collocation paper's software architecture, benchmark examples, and
  implementation-specific convergence orders are not promoted as AAA claims.
  The useful source signal is narrower: a collocation candidate must declare
  its piecewise polynomial representation, collocation nodes, section anchoring,
  and adaptive mesh policy before its residuals can feed the seed-chart packet.
- The $\phi^4$ nonexistence paper's specific Klein-Gordon equation, perturbation
  expansion, and radiation-rate calculation are not imported into AAA
  dynamics. Its useful signal is the proof standard: formal all-orders closure
  and approximate long-lived oscillation are insufficient unless the relevant
  leakage channels are closed by the native delayed-system certificate rows.
- The NLS and coupled-NLS breather sources are exhausted as terminology and
  failure-pressure comparators. Their Darboux constructions, explicit coherent
  profiles, rogue-wave limits, and degenerate-breather limits are not promoted.
  The useful source signal is the native requirement to declare candidate
  variables, limiting procedures, and separator/fold budgets inside the delayed
  architrino certificate.
- The perturbed sine-Gordon nonpersistence source is exhausted as planar-bridge
  pressure. It supports treating tangential leakage, gauge-reset discontinuity,
  and perturbation survival as real certificate burdens rather than prose gaps.
- The sine-Gordon rigidity source's equation, inverse-scattering or
  integrability structure, perturbation-space calculations, and external
  breather formulae are not promoted. Its useful signal is the stricter planar
  transport rule: a collinear candidate is not planar evidence until the native
  branch topology, gauge reset, fold/separator transit, and leakage-budget
  margins close on one certified packet.
- The direct-action sources are rejected as breather-certificate inputs. Their
  half-advanced terms, absorber/universe-response assumptions, future boundary
  segments, Minkowski/proper-time setup, point-charge ontology, and
  Wheeler-Feynman no-self-interaction premise are not AAA substrate
  assumptions. Their safe use is narrower: they sharpen the contrast between
  boundary-value/action formalisms and AAA delayed-only causal-wake history
  bookkeeping, and they may later support potential/action comparison
  residuals.
