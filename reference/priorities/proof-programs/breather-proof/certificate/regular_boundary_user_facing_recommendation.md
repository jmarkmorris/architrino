## Concrete Theorem Target

The future user-facing theorem should be introduced only after a same-packet proof supplies a predicate of the following form.

Fix the packet identity tuple
$$
\mathfrak{I}_{\mathrm{seed}}
=
\left(
\mathcal{K},
T_{\mathrm{cyc}},
\mathcal{S},
\mathcal{P},
\mathcal{B}_{\mathrm{rep}},
\Theta
\right)
$$
and fixed parameters
$$
\eta=0.02,\qquad \epsilon_c=0.05,\qquad \Gamma=1.
$$
For each separator
$$
\Sigma\in\{\Sigma_1,\Sigma_2,\Sigma_3,\Sigma_4\},
$$
define a finite same-packet family
$$
\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{reg}}_{\Sigma}
$$
of named residual equality cores. A core
$$
C\in\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{reg}}_{\Sigma}
$$
may instantiate the generic AAA phrase "another already accepted same-packet complement predicate" only if the certificate records:

1. the outward-rounded receiver/source rectangle and null-coordinate overlap for $C$;
2. the parent row, strip id, accepted simple-root boundary, and complement-boundary ownership convention;
3. exact same-packet inclusion
   $$
   C\in\overline{\mathcal{F}}_{\Sigma}^{\mathrm{bdry}},
   $$
   where $\overline{\mathcal{F}}_{\Sigma}^{\mathrm{bdry}}$ is the finite listed regular-boundary family, not an open-ended regular neighborhood;
4. preservation of
   $$
   \alpha_{\Sigma}>0,\qquad
   \nu_{\mathrm{exit},\Sigma}>0,\qquad
   \Delta N_\Sigma\in2\mathbb{Z},\qquad
   \Delta D_\Sigma=0;
   $$
5. a same-packet domination inequality, for example
   $$
   \sum_{B\in\mathcal{F}_{\Sigma}} I^{\mathrm{fold}}_{\eta,\epsilon_c,B}
   +
   \sum_{C\in\mathcal{C}^{\mathrm{reg}}_{\Sigma}}
   I^{\mathrm{reg\text{-}bdry}}_{\eta,\epsilon_c,C}
   \le
   I^{\mathrm{fold}}_{\eta,\epsilon_c,\Sigma},
   $$
   or a separately accepted enlarged finite ceiling on the same packet identity tuple;
6. a no-double-counting rule keeping $C$ out of strict simple-root branch sums and endpoint-excluded complements;
7. accepted closure of all non-core complement strips by strict gap, endpoint exclusion, exact fold-layer coverage, or another expressly accepted alternative.

If any field is missing, the regular-boundary predicate fails closed and the candidate remains rejected before branch-chart certification.
