## Current Decision

The selected immediate route was:

**Option A: proof-grade intervalization of the Hermite dual obstruction.**

Option A is now closed for this generic same-itinerary Hermite route. The
multiplier side is exact-rational at denominator cap $10^9$, the active row
backend reconstructs exact rational Hermite coefficients, encloses
trigonometric row bounds outward by rational intervals, repairs gamma
stationarity exactly without a gamma residual cap, and proves
$$
\gamma\le -2.20499517531647\times 10^{-10}.
$$

The continuous-collar lift then verifies that the active sampled gap rows and
retained speed-sign rows are embedded in the declared continuous collar/speed
target. Any continuous candidate satisfying that target would satisfy the active
sampled subset, so the sampled dual bound rules it out. This does not certify a
positive candidate or continuous row variation between samples; it closes the
obstruction route only. The current decision boundary is therefore to stop
enriching this generic same-itinerary Hermite family. Two bounded Option B
screens have now also been tested: period-coupled Hermite separator locking and
fixed-separator velocity-Bernstein sign corridors. Neither opens positive
sampled margin. Option C has now been opened at priority level by
`fresh_v10_higher_fold_itinerary_rebuild_target.v0.md`, which freezes the
12-root shifted-threshold rebuild target. The diagnostic successor seed packet
for that branch now also exists. The first binary64/Lipschitz root-tube attempt
has been translated into the exact-rational outward interval certificate
`fresh_v10_higher_fold_root_tube_interval_certificate.v0.md`, which closes the
12-root topology gate while leaving the null-coordinate preledger incomplete.
The proof-interval v1 sidecar classifies 270 coarse range-empty rows, the
proof-interval v2 sidecar adds row-specific trigonometric enclosures that
classify 1,062 range-empty rows, the proof-interval v3 sidecar uses the
root-count complement certificate to consume 26 same-interval diagonal rows,
the proof-interval v4 sidecar records 42 simple-root receiver subwindow
certificates without consuming their parent rows, and the proof-interval v5
sidecar certifies 571 receiver-grid cells while missing 773 cells and consuming
0 parent rows. It still leaves 162 rows requiring parent-complement,
endpoint/complement, and fold-layer closure. The
remaining constructive work is therefore either the larger nonlinear
fold-coordinate collocation target or residual proof-interval closure for
`fresh-v10-higher-fold-12-root-rebuild-v0`.

## Rejected Non-Option

Do not continue by merely adding more generic same-itinerary basis functions.
The three-coordinate, split-two, and Hermite screens already cover the obvious
linear enrichment ladder. Also do not repeat the bounded period-coupled Hermite
or fixed-separator velocity-corridor variants without changing the structural
law. Any same-itinerary continuation must now instantiate the nonlinear
fold-coordinate collocation target with hard field-speed itinerary constraints
built into the solve.

## Guardrails

- No branch chart may be built until a proof-interval pre-ledger passes.
- No sampled LP updates a live causal ledger.
- Same-itinerary routes must preserve field-speed itinerary constraints as hard
  constraints, not as post-hoc diagnostics.
- A higher-fold route must freeze a new packet identity before any proof-interval
  row consumption.
- The 10 v10 collars remain the strict-gap target unless the itinerary is
  explicitly replaced.
- `C_u_A4_A2_left_v10_7` remains highlighted as the controlling collar from
  the direct-integration and shifted-separator screens.
- Existing accepted or partial rows remain historical unless recomputed or
  explicitly proven persistent for the repaired or successor packet.
