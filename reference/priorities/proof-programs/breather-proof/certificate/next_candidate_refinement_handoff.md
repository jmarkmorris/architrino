1. every ordered arc-pair row is empty, simple-root, or fold-layer;
2. every simple-root parent complement is strict range-empty, endpoint-excluded, or exact fold-layer-covered;

1. accepted simple-root windows can be chosen so their complements have strict outward-rounded gaps;
2. fold-adjacent complements become exact accepted fold-layer rectangles;
3. an enlarged finite regular-boundary theorem supplies same-packet inclusion, domination, ownership, and non-core strict-gap fields.
