## Tier and severability rules used

- **T1 SURVIVES:** instantaneous delayed-force, force/torque-balance, explicit ledger, geometry, and equilibrium facts.
- **T2 RE-ANCHOR:** linear stability, flutter, restoring-spectrum, basin, or damping verdicts.
- **T3 RE-DERIVE:** release, dispersal, settling, locking, temporal growth, expansion, relaxation, or long-term fate.
- **VOID:** a T2 claim whose linearization point is not an equilibrium. No live claim in this register is newly assigned void; the void §97/§98 pencil did not reach current reader-facing corpus prose.
- **SEVERABLE:** a T1 clause carries the stated conclusion without the T2/T3 clause.
- **RE-GRADE:** the conclusion itself depends on T2/T3 evidence and cannot remain at its current strength merely by deleting provenance words.
