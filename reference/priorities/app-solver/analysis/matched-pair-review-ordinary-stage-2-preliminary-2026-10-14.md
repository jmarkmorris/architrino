## O2-R1: a mutable restore result issues a purportedly sealed ordinary continuation

`native_validated_continuation_from_replay(const ValidatedRestoreResult&)` checks the public `status` string and non-null continuation/record, then copies the public `replayed_rejected_steps` and record into `NativeValidatedContinuation`. A `ValidatedRestoreResult` is mutable caller data; this check establishes neither that its fields came from one complete replay nor that they still agree with that replay. The sealed driver continuation authenticates its own accepted boundary, not these newly attached fields.

The known case executes the supported two-path stationary exact origin, completes one step without rejection, restores its complete record under the externally selected record identity, and produces a genuine ordinary continuation with rejection count zero. Only after that recorded pass, the target copies the result and changes `replayed_rejected_steps` to 99. The public factory issues a `recorded` continuation with 99. The ordinary entry accepts it with matching caller restart counters, completes the second step and reports two accepted/99 rejected steps. `create_validated_provider_checkpoint` issues a 72,722-byte checkpoint with those counters. The retained raw output is `.local-data/eom-matched-pair/2026-10-14/ordinary-integration/reviewer-o2-replay-seal-probe.txt`.

A second target replaces only the public result's record with a different-coupling zero-leg record. The same factory issues a continuation labelled `recorded` whose driver accepted count is one while the attached record's count is zero. This independently demonstrates lack of common provenance; no mathematical state-containment failure is claimed.

Required correction: make ordinary continuation issuance consume authentic, inseparable replay provenance rather than trust public result labels/counters/record. An internal issuance seam that only the complete replay/ordinary checkpoint path can invoke, or a sealed replay boundary issued by the record module, can preserve the frozen public reporting result while preventing mutated reports from minting continuation authority. Recomputing a caller's checksum is insufficient. Revalidate complete actual provenance if exposed mutable results remain accepted. Counters and attached record must agree with the actual same replay. Preserve existing frozen record instruments and return a versioned scratch packet with exact differences.

Falsifier for closure: the unchanged probe must still record its genuine known pass first, then both mutations must refuse without issuing a usable ordinary continuation. Genuine worker-changing, rejected-leg and multi-leg restores must continue to work. Correcting only checkpoint serialization leaves the earlier forged continuation and cumulative accounting defect open.
