## Why the admission correction is acceptable

Source inspection of the candidate bridge shows that a new origin must pass a discarded certificate from the existing validated producer before cancellation or a memory-budget return. That producer supplies the actual common-history, speed, separation, root-window, enclosure and budget checks. The ordinary bridge does not manufacture true-history evidence or change the equation. Foreign evidence remains subject to the driver's independent population admission. A genuine resumed boundary still goes through the driver's continuation/population binding before any returned state.

Certification of this probe is a sufficient positive preflight for the origin. It is not necessary to prove that a refusal at the chosen length rules out every larger step in order to keep certified evolution sound: every actual evolution step still needs its own producer certificate. A conservative preflight can decline an otherwise usable configuration. Nor is `minimum_step` necessarily a length the halving controller actually reaches when it is not a dyadic subdivision of `initial_step`.

**Required narrow correction before live application:** remove the unsupported assertion that all interval tests are monotone in step length and that a refused origin therefore cannot advance at any other step. Describe the chosen length as `min(minimum_step, remaining_span)` and the check as a conservative preflight. Change the diagnostic's categorical “cannot step from this origin” to “origin preflight is not certified” or an equivalent precise statement. Make the same correction in source comments, header and a new companion receipt; preserve the version 2 historical packet. No new mathematical theorem, model rule or reference rewrite is needed. Return exact changed bytes and include them in the live source/build binders.

O1-R1 is closed on the retained independent controls. The live gate still requires fresh guarded preimages, exact final-source comparison, a separately built archive, the unchanged admission probe and exact reference check on live outputs, cumulative callback/resume/refusal controls, and default/Surface compatibility. A target that returns either continuation or a cancellation/resource accepted boundary for an unsupported cancelled origin reopens O1-R1.

## Resource and O2 design disposition

The new estimate uses exact decimal span arithmetic, bounded halving counts and explicit overflow refusal. Source inspection of `History.cpp` confirms shared segment storage and copied tail/index storage, and inspection of `ValidatedEvolution.cpp` confirms that retry attempts halve their step. The revised bound accounts for retained attempts, atomic copies and accepted history versions rather than charging full duplicated history contents for every copy. These are reviewed admission allowances; no measured resident-set bound or timing benefit is established.

The roughly 82.7 GB worst-case estimate for the receding-source request still exceeds the maintained wire ceiling. O3 must expose a useful bounded request domain through explicit per-invocation step/resource controls, larger declared minimum steps where chosen, or a separately justified retention bound. It must refuse requests outside that resource domain honestly. Do not raise the 2 GiB ceiling, remove retained scientific evidence, change defaults silently or claim broad long-run availability merely to admit this one control. Short independently checked runs are already within the revised bound; the large control does not block the correctness of that bounded route.

O2 version 2 resolves the reviewed identity map: the original preparation and `threadCount` remain bound, while a distinct admitted runtime worker override controls replay/new execution and is reported as actual execution metadata. Retain ordinary numerical/resource admission for that override, including the wire's worker ceiling in O3. The proposed replay-derived rejection count, sealed generating record with explicit record-failure status, exact-origin-only v9, guarded transport placement, complete replay before usable state and no-durability-before-directory-fsync rules are accepted design conditions.

Persistence runtime remains held until independent live O1 acceptance. On that completed join, release O2 scratch implementation immediately under this design, with actual leg/count/record/envelope copies included in resource admission and the existing immutable references kept separate from the subject.
