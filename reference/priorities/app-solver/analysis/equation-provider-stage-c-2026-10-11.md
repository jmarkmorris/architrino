## Independent controls and falsifiers

Thus $b=0.2$ is strictly admissible without uncertainty; $b=0.5$ violates both restricted domains inside despite safe endpoints; $b=0.25$ touches one and must fail strict admission. A first-axis velocity radius 0.06 makes the $b=0.2$ tube unsafe to certify. Exact constant velocity $(0.6,0.8,0)$ independently gives squared norm one, admitting inclusive and rejecting strict; the exact decimal `1.0000000000000000000001` exceeds one. A accepted mismatch or unequal restarted fingerprint falsifies the corresponding persistence guarantee; inspect the fixture assertions and named raw logs, rather than treating checksum equality alone as proof.
