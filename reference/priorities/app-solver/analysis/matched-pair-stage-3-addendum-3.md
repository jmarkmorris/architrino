# Generated-History Inclusion, Addendum 3: Admission Corrections to the Prototype Carrier

[Addendum 2](matched-pair-stage-3-addendum-2.md) reported a prototype of the validated far-step carrier. Its [independent review](matched-pair-review-stage-3-addendum-2-2026-10-11.md) accepted the mathematics conditionally and found two defects in what the prototype admits. This addendum records the corrections, made in a new version 2 file with version 1 preserved, and one clarification of addendum 2's text. Evidence is in the [version 2 receipt](../evidence/matched-pair-stage-3-validated-far-step-v2-receipt-2026-10-11.md). The prototype remains scratch evidence outside the solver, and Stage 3 of the [integration scope](../contracts/matched-pair-enclosure-integration-scope.md) is not released. Every numerical instance uses $c_f=1$.

## 1. VFS-R1: a shorter history must not erase another path's emissions

Version 1 took the latest first time among the paths as the start of the emission domain. With a receiver history on $[-5,0]$ and a static source history on $[-20,0]$ at distance $10$, it searched only $[-5,0]$, found both pairs absent, and certified zero acceleration. The source's supplied history contains the root at $-10$, whose contribution is exactly $-1/100000$ at $\kappa=10^{-3}$. The certificate was false for the data it was given. **Claim grade: measured**, by the reviewer's probe and reproduced by the author's.

Hypothesis H0 of addendum 1 speaks of "the retained window" as if all paths shared one. That was an unstated assumption, and version 1 enforced it by truncation instead of by refusal. The theorem's emission domain for a source is that source's own retained history; a receiver's history length has no bearing on it. The corrected statement of H0 is: every path's retained history starts at one common time $S_{\min}$, and every segment back to $S_{\min}$ is validated. Version 2 declines with `VFS-H0-01` when the starts differ. This is the narrower of the two corrections the review offered. The other, admitting unequal starts with a separate proof that each discarded prefix contains no root for any reception in the step, is the prefix-exclusion obligation that the snapshot stages already met for the grouped route; it is not implemented here. Equal shallow starts remain admissible as a finite retained domain, with absence conditional on that domain being complete.

## 2. VFS-R2: evidence must be bound to the data it describes

Version 1 stored each segment's speed bound in a field of the caller's segment and reused it whenever the field was present. After a genuine step, changing the source's cubic in place to one with peak speed $2$ left the stale bound in force; the step then certified without a budget. The sub-wake-speed hypothesis H1, on which the uniqueness of roots and the absence of self-hits both rest, was reported as holding for data that violate it. **Claim grade: measured**, by the reviewer's probe and reproduced by the author's.

Version 2 neither reads nor writes any field of caller data. Its speed cache is internal and keyed by the segment's times, all twelve coefficients and its velocity radii, which are everything the bound depends on. A changed coefficient is a different key. The same principle is applied to the provenance label, which addendum 2 listed as a limit: a segment labelled `validated_far_step` is admitted only if a digest of its complete payload is one the module recorded when it published that segment. A published segment whose radius or coefficient is later altered, and a segment that merely carries the label, are both refused with `VFS-H0-01`. Each certificate now carries a digest of its inputs, of each published segment and of itself.

This binding lives in one process's memory. It shows the admission rule a real carrier needs and it closes the reproduced defect; it is not the certificate chain that production would need, where the generating certificate must be retained and rechecked by the consumer, as the grouped snapshot certificate is.

## 3. Increment budget and total uncertainty

Addendum 2 called its budget "the validated counterpart of the solver's position and velocity tolerances". It is a budget on how much one step adds to the radius, and it says nothing about the radius a state has accumulated. In the long run each step added at most $1.71\times10^{-9}$ in velocity while the total reached $2.08\times10^{-7}$. Version 2 records both quantities for every step and accepts an optional budget on the total published radius, declining with `VFS-BUDGET-02` when it is exceeded even though the increment budget is met. How a total budget should relate to the solver's accepted-state ledger is an integration question that is not answered here.

## 4. A clarification of the root-window argument

Addendum 2 said the window intersection is repeated "until it stops shrinking". The prototype performs at most eight intersections and then recomputes the hull on the final window. Containment of the roots does not depend on convergence, since each intersection preserves every root; the cap affects only how tight the window is. When the window stays so wide that the distance floor is not positive, the step declines with `VFS-H4-01`. The reviewer's fast affine source is such a case: its real root is regular, and the refusal is conservative. Version 1's qualification did not exercise that code; the version 2 probes do.

## 5. Result and disposition

Run unchanged through an import adapter, the scientific qualification gives the same 45 result lines for version 2 as for version 1. The separate admission probes pass all 29 rows: the two findings are reproduced on version 1 and refused by version 2, label binding and the total budget behave as stated, the `VFS-H4-01` branch is exercised, and the long run's whole-bracket containment and its separation from the aliased interval are now blocking assertions. Exact rational receipts are retained for every step.

**Claim grade: measured** for those results, on the listed cases. The prototype's standing is unchanged in kind: a bounded demonstration outside the solver that the validated far step can be computed, with its admission rules now tested against the two ways version 1 could be made to certify falsely. What a production carrier still needs is stated in the receipt: solver arithmetic and types, certificate binding across the consumer boundary, a prefix proof for unequal starts, a ledger rule for total uncertainty, and a cost measurement. Stage 3 is not entered; evolution entry points still refuse the grouped policy and exact fallback remains in force.
