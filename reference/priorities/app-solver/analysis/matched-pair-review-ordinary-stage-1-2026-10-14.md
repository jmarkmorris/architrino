## O2 design dispositions

The [persistence design](matched-pair-ordinary-stage-2-design.md) is accepted as a direction of preparation with the following conditions, not as runtime release:

1. Add `replayed_rejected_steps` to the restore result, deriving it from actual fully replayed leg results, with overflow/resource admission and no partial state on failure. Do not change record bytes or frozen comparison instruments. This is required to verify the cumulative ordinary rejection count.
2. Carry the generating record in the sealed continuation. Report record open/append/storage failure explicitly; preserve accepted coverage and never promise restartability or durability after a dropped record. Account for the record and its copies in resource admission.
3. Limit v9 creation to exact-origin generating records. Supplied radii/evidence can continue within O1's admitted domain, but do not become a manufactured origin or an unaudited persistent seed.
4. Add guarded v9 functions beside v8 in the existing transport owner, preserving actual v8 bytes and obligations. Typed v9 remains unadvertised until O3 wire qualification.
5. Tighten memory admission against the actual retained attempts/history/record/replay shapes before setting a useful finite wire default. An estimate of approximately $7.2\times10^{14}$ bytes for a short bounded case would deny ordinary execution under the maintained wire's 2 GiB resource ceiling. It is not enough to call this harmless under an unlimited typed default. Exact span/minimum-step arithmetic must not undercount at a large epoch; saturation must mean refusal when a finite bound cannot be established.

Resolve one identity-map inconsistency before runtime: §3 declares worker count unbound metadata, but v8-style `encode_request` puts `threadCount` in preparation and in the binding's hashed numerical controls; §4 recomputes both from the caller's request. A changed worker count therefore cannot simultaneously preserve that preparation/binding without an explicit runtime override separate from the original preparation, or a reviewed canonicalization rule. Choose one coherent map, retain numerical/resource controls, and test actual changed-worker restart. Do not silently exclude arbitrary request fields to make the comparison pass.

Expected controls plus caller origin are not a selected original complete record. A different admitted full record with the same controls/origin can satisfy that identity basis after replay; the planned resealed-operation test must distinguish this legitimate alternative from inconsistent or false claims. Full-record expectations, where exposed, must refuse another complete identity. Corruption detection, caller selection and true-history replay remain distinct.
