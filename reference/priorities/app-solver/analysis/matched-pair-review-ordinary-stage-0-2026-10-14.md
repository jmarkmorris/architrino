## Decisions on the four questions

1. **Accept the separate `integration_method` axis.** Equation selection still describes the canonical numerical model. The integration method belongs to execution identity. Admit only the ordinary default and accepted validated token; refuse unknown selections and unconsumed nondefault validated controls under ordinary selection before public entry-point early returns. Do not imply a changed law or a new primitive model from the new method.
2. **Accept explicit v3 request/response and v9 validated persistence.** Preserve v2/v8 and v1/v7 semantics and admission. The existing CLI can multiplex a supported v3 document while readiness/progress retain their declared v2 schemas, if those channels' semantics remain correct. Add separate execution identity/inspection, rather than changing the model's evaluation axis. No existing checksum or schema is reinterpreted as issuing history evidence.
3. **Accept the advertised capabilities addition as an explicit expected default difference.** Retain every current field and value apart from named build identities. Advertise a validated wire/checkpoint selection only when the actual supported operations are implemented and qualified; O1 typed-only availability does not warrant advertising v3/v9 prematurely. Unknown v2 execution fields remain refused. Generic consumers must not treat a registry list as evidence of a wider domain.
4. **Map a positive `diagnostic_maximum_accepted_steps` to the validated driver's per-invocation accepted-step limit; zero retains its documented no-diagnostic-limit meaning.** The ordinary loop uses `accepted_count - initial_accepted_count`, so it is a per-invocation diagnostic cap, not a lifetime count. The validated driver also compares a local accepted count. If the driver's existing independent resource ceiling remains when the diagnostic is zero, expose and document it honestly; do not call it an unlimited run. Preserve completion at the requested end versus a diagnostic/resource halt, exact accepted prefix and resumable continuation. Test first and later invocations, including zero-step and already-completed boundaries.

The bridge may refuse meaningless ordinary nondefault controls by name; it must not silently ignore them. Maximum step equal to initial step is coherent here because the actual validated controller caps regrowth at initial step. Empty/default maximum-step handling must be explicit. Tests must use the actual ordinary convention, not an invented new sentinel.

## Required translation details

### Callbacks and counters

**Derived from the inspected code:** the validated driver's callback receives `ordinal_base + accepted` before the count increment, a zero-based accepted-step index. The ordinary callback receives `accepted_count` after increment, an accepted-step count. Directly forwarding the callable does not preserve its ordinary semantics. Wrap the validated callback to report the actual accepted-step count, including the genuine resumed ordinal; test first callback, later callbacks, changed-worker resume and cancellation triggered at a callback boundary. Do not change the frozen driver or its original fixtures to make an adapter comparison pass.

The validated `run` reports local accepted/rejected counts, while its sealed continuation holds cumulative accepted count. Ordinary requests/certificates and provider responses carry cumulative controller counters. The bridge/next persistence design must declare and implement the mapping: distinguish per-invocation attempts from cumulative accepted/rejected/controller state, reconstruct any required accumulated counters from actual issued/replayed history, and refuse inconsistent caller-supplied restart counters. Do not return a local count under a field promised as cumulative or trust unbound uploaded counters. The full validated certificate may remain available unchanged beside the truthful ordinary view. The O1 packet must resolve this mapping explicitly rather than relying on plain `run` copying.

Reject an unsupported reusable ordinary start snapshot with validated selection, or prove why it is semantically unused and expose that fact; it must not become whole-step evidence. Public atomic-step and snapshot entry-point refusal is acceptable and must be explicit before computation. Existing snapshot use with the ordinary selector remains unchanged.

### Resource and control admission

Ordinary typed/wire requests own memory/population/history limits; bypassing the ordinary controller must not bypass those obligations. Preserve resource admission on origins and genuinely resumed full populations, including working histories/evidence/replay and future publication growth as required by the actual owner. Report an estimate as an estimate, not a measured resident-set bound. A finite tiny resource budget cannot become ignored because the validated driver has no such field. Budget rejection returns no usable unadmitted validated continuation, including cancelled/zero-length/diagnostic-limit early returns.

Provide a complete disposition of ordinary fields: exact translation, explicitly supported default, or named refusal. Cover ordinary cumulative counters, adaptive/multirate/quarter/cost-feedback/event/joint/Surface state and callbacks, not only a short remembered list. Increment-budget token conversion must remain within its stated exact/rounding semantics; nonfinite/nonpositive/overflow tokens refuse. The accepted canonical carrier's stricter speed/separation/history hypotheses remain unchanged.

Consequently “the Node process client passes JSON through and needs no change” is not a complete consumer conclusion. Resolve this actual projection/retention seam in O3, including ordinary v3 raw retention/complete marker, view budget, evidence links, durability failures and browser schema/execution-digest comparison.
