## R0-3: exact eligibility and window coverage

**Charge tokens.** Two charge tokens are an admissible pair only when both parse as finite decimals of the form optional sign, digits with an optional point, and an optional exponent with optional sign; their normalized digit strings and exponents are identical; their signs are opposite; and the common magnitude is nonzero. Normalization strips leading and trailing zeros and folds the exponent, so `1`, `1.0`, `+1e0` and `10e-1` denote one magnitude. A zero magnitude, a zero receiver charge, equal signs or any difference in magnitude declines with `MPE-CHARGE-01`. Tokens that are not finite decimals decline.

**Window.** The window start must be strictly before the reception token, by exact decimal comparison, or the route declines with `MPE-WINDOW-01`. One cubic segment of each source must contain the whole window, by exact decimal comparison of segment bounds.

**Coverage of earlier retained history.** This is a correction to the packet, which accepted any window start inside a covering segment. No retained emission time before the window start may be left unexamined. The route admits exactly two cases. In the first, the window starts at the retained history start of both members, so nothing earlier is retained; the certificate records `whole_retained_history`. In the second, the window starts later and the group binds the snapshot's causal-prefix-exclusion certificate itself: its schema, a `certified_complete` status, its flag that the prefix is excluded for every ordered pair, a strictly negative residual upper bound, an active lower bound equal to the window start, a reception token equal to the group's, and an original lower bound equal to the retained history start of both members. The certificate then records `certified_prefix_exclusion` and carries a copy of that evidence. Any other situation declines with `MPE-WINDOW-02`. A status string alone is refused. The prefix certificate's scope is every ordered pair of the snapshot's histories, because it is computed from the largest receiver radius and the largest source radius over all of them; the equality of its original lower bound with both members' history starts is what ties that scope to these two members.

**Unsupported carriers.** The grouped route is not attempted when the chart policy is not `sharp`; when a Surface geometry is selected or any path carries a Surface continuation carrier; when an equation selection is present and is not the canonical, unrestricted, unconstrained numerical one; or when the request carries joint histories or joint root states. A source history with a uniform-circular analytic certificate, or with any nonzero position or velocity error token, declines with `MPE-HISTORY-01`. An unknown policy string is refused before any work.

## R0-4: published carrier and zero-width semantics

**Ownership cross-check.** The acceleration reconstruction verifies group ownership over the complete request matrix before any pair is evaluated. Every request whose chart is `matched_pair_enclosure` must carry a group certificate for its own receiver and must name that group's leader or follower. Each group certificate object must be referenced exactly once as leader and exactly once as follower, and no two certificate objects may share a group identity. A certificate attached to a request of another chart is refused. Any violation fails the reconstruction, so a missing, repeated or foreign reference cannot drop or duplicate the group's interval. Each member request is also rechecked individually: receiver and member identities and history fingerprints, reception coverage, membership dispositions consistent with the status, established window coverage, distinct members that are not the receiver, and emitted width within the two-slice threshold.

**Leader and follower.** The leader is the member earlier in canonical path order. Its row carries the group's interval and the acceptance status `consumed_certified_matched_pair_group`, or `consumed_certified_matched_pair_absence` for an absent group. The follower's row carries the additive identity and the status `matched_pair_group_reduction_identity`. Both rows name the group in their accumulation-group field. The follower's row is a reduction role and asserts nothing about the follower's own acceleration.

**Absent groups.** A both-absent group is counted as two certified exclusions and consumes no enclosure width. Its debit is assigned as exact zero. It is not measured with `Interval::width()`, which rounds upward and reports the smallest positive number for the point-zero interval; measuring would charge a spurious positive width. The group's interval is the point zero in each component.

**Reduction rounding.** The receiver total is the existing fixed pairwise sum over the unchanged source slots. Outward addition widens a partial sum by one unit in the last place per addition whether or not an addend is zero, exactly as it does today for every slot. The grouped route adds no slot and removes none, so reduction rounding stays in its existing budget and is not part of the enclosure debit.

**Disabled route.** With the policy disabled, no grouped code path executes and no existing output field changes. The comparison against the frozen baseline is made on complete outputs: the native and evolution `all` outputs byte for byte, the acceleration `all` output byte for byte, and the dispersal output on every field other than its wall-clock fields. No numerical field is filtered. Fields added to carriers by this work are not printed by the existing fixtures; if a later comparison meets a difference that is only an added schema field or a source-identity digest, it will be named as such and listed separately from any numerical difference.

## Refinement found in preparation: the transmitter-factor floor

The packet wrote $\nu=1-v^+$ evaluated downward. The radius is valid for any $\nu$ with $0<\nu\le1-v^+$, with $v^+$ unchanged, and this is what the module uses. The reason is that each use of $\nu$ in the theorem's proof is a lower bound: $D\ge\nu$ on the speed ball, the residual derivative is at least $\nu$, and the delayed separation bound $\ell^++v^+\ell^+/\nu\le\ell^+/\nu$ needs only $\nu+v^+\le1$. Rounding $1-v^+$ downward therefore gives an admissible $\nu$ without enlarging $v^+$.

**Claim grade: derived**, by inspection of the three places $\nu$ enters the reviewed proof. Falsifier: a step of that proof that requires $\nu=1-v^+$ exactly.
