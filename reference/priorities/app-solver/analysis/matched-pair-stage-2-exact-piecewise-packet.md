# Matched-Pair Enclosure, Stage 2 Packet: Exact Piecewise Windows

This packet disposes finding S2-1 of the [independent Stage 2 design review](matched-pair-review-stage-2-design-2026-10-11.md), corrects the general theorem of the [Stage 2 design record](matched-pair-stage-2-design.md), and presents the implementation and controls for the one domain the review released: windows spanning several error-free cubic segments of each source. The design record is preserved unchanged; where the two differ, this packet governs. The implementation is a candidate built and run in a scratch copy of the live tree. **It has not been applied to the live checkout.** The live Stage 1 route is unchanged. All numerical instances use $c_f=1$.

## 1. Finding S2-1 and the corrected hypotheses

The design record assumed a true source path that is absolutely continuous, with a derivative almost everywhere lying in the velocity tube. That is enough for the residual to be strictly increasing, and so for root uniqueness. It is not enough for the kernel. The canonical sharp weight of a hit is $1/|D|$ with $D=1-\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf V$ evaluated at the emission time itself, and "almost everywhere" allows the path to have a corner exactly at the root, where no velocity exists and the weight is undefined. The reviewer's counterexample, a source whose path has a corner at its own root time while every tube, speed, distance and membership condition holds, shows the gap is real.

The finding is accepted. The hypotheses of the general theorem are strengthened and replace H1 and H3 of the design record:

- **H1′, a continuously differentiable path.** On the window, each true source history $\mathbf X_\pm$ is continuously differentiable, including across segment joins, with derivative $\mathbf V_\pm$ defined at every emission time.
- **H2, the tubes**, unchanged, now read at every time: $\mathbf X_\pm(S)$ within the position radii of the nominal position and $\mathbf V_\pm(S)$ within the velocity radii of the nominal velocity.

Under H1′ and H2 every step of the design record's proof holds at every time: the residual has derivative at least $\nu$ everywhere, the delayed events have well-defined velocities, and the kernel difference bound applies to them. The statement and radius of the theorem are otherwise unchanged. The nominal velocity-jump allowance $J$ remains in the radius as a bound on the nominal curve; it never stood in for a derivative of the true path and is not claimed to.

**Claim grade: derived**, conditional on H1′ and H2. Falsifier: histories satisfying H1′, H2 and every stated bound and membership sign whose exact two-root acceleration has a component outside the interval.

## 3. The admitted theorem, stated for the admitted domain

For a window $[S_0,T_r]$ on which both sources are continuously differentiable piecewise cubics, let $\Omega$ be a convex set containing both curves at distance at least $r^->0$ from the receiver box, $v^+<1$ a bound on both speeds, $\ell^+$ and $w^+$ bounds on the equal-time separation and relative velocity, and $a^+$ a bound on the acceleration inside segments. Because each velocity is continuous on the window and has derivative of norm at most $a^+$ away from finitely many joins, it changes by at most $a^+|\Delta S|$ over any time interval. The reviewed single-slab proof then applies word for word with the window in place of the slab: the residual is continuously differentiable and strictly increasing, membership is decided by its sign at the window start, the upper endpoint is automatically positive, and the two-root acceleration lies in $[-B,B]^3$ with the Stage 1 radius. A join is not a special place: a root is an emission time, and nothing is assigned to one side of a join.

The implementation adds one term to that radius, an outward bound $J$ on the summed velocity jumps across the cell boundaries of one source. On this domain every such jump is exactly zero, so $J$ is the outward rounding of zero, of order $10^{-15}$ or smaller in the controls. It is kept so that the emitted radius is rigorous in floating point without relying on the exactness of the join in binary arithmetic.

**Claim grade: derived.** Falsifier: an admitted pair whose independently evaluated exact two-root acceleration has a component outside the emitted interval.

## 4. Candidate implementation

One new policy value, `canonical_snapshot_piecewise_exact/v1`, beside the unchanged Stage 1 value. Under it the module:

1. finds, by exact decimal comparison, the range of each source's segments that meets the window, and declines if the window is not inside the retained coverage;
2. splits the window at every segment boundary of either source that lies strictly inside it, so both sources are single cubics on each cell and the cells tile the window exactly;
3. declines unless every segment the window meets has zero error tokens and neither history carries an analytic certificate;
4. re-proves any earlier retained history root free from each member's own position hull, as in Stage 1;
5. reuses the Stage 1 re-expansion on each cell and takes the bounds over all cells: one box hull, the largest speed, separation, relative-velocity and acceleration norms, and the larger of the two sources' summed boundary jumps;
6. decides membership once at the window start, evaluates the radius, and applies the two-slice budget.

The certificate gains the cell boundary list, each member's last segment index, the jump bound and a history grade, `exact_piecewise_cubic`. All are covered by the existing field-by-field comparison with a fresh certification, and the jump bound enters the single-request recomputation of the radius. The Stage 1 code path is untouched: a request with the Stage 1 policy runs the same statements as before.

Outside the module, one statement changes in one existing file: the snapshot's policy check in `CoupledEvolution.cpp` accepts any known policy where it accepted the single Stage 1 value. Pairing, accounting, markers, the consumer and the evolution refusal are unchanged. The capability remains snapshot-only.

The diff of the candidate against the live tree touches that file, the module header and source, and the fixture. It also shows `README.md`, which a concurrent writer changed on the live tree after the scratch copy was taken; that file is not part of the candidate and the candidate's copy of it will not be applied.

## 5. Results in the scratch build

The scratch tree is a copy of the live tree with the candidate applied, built Release with two workers under the compute supervisor with the per-process Command Line Tools toolchain, no warning or error.

**Nothing earlier changes.** **Claim grade: measured**, by `cmp`. With the policy disabled, the native, acceleration and evolution `all` outputs are byte-identical to the frozen baseline, and the dispersal output differs only in its four wall-clock fields. Under the Stage 2 build, the Stage 1 module output, the evolution guard, the matrix negatives, the 47 Stage 1 snapshot cases and the 16 reviewer-input cases are each byte-identical to the accepted live Stage 1 outputs. The reviewer's containment comparison on the reviewer-input bytes therefore still applies.

**Known controls first.** A static source at distance two held as two segments gives a receiver total containing $-1/4$ on the exact route. The single-slab static pair run under the piecewise policy, as one cell, gives the Stage 1 radius.

**Module controls, sixteen certificates.** Eight enclosed: the one-cell static and quadratic pairs; the static pair with two segments each; the quadratic pair with a join between its two roots, with a join exactly at one root, and with unequal segmentation into four cells; a window starting at a join with the earlier segment re-proved as prefix; and a window whose prefix segment carries a radius while the window itself is exact. Eight declined: a position radius, a velocity radius, radii of $10^{-300}$, one later segment with a radius, and the uniform-circular factory history, each with `MPE-HISTORY-01`; a window starting before one member's coverage and a history ending before reception, with `MPE-WINDOW-01`; and a prefix the one-hull test cannot re-prove, with `MPE-WINDOW-02`. In the last case the prefix is in fact root free; the test is conservative and the exact route runs.

**Snapshot controls, fourteen cases.** The two-segment static population admits one group and gives the same decisions and intervals in six scheduling variants; the unequal-segmentation quadratic population agrees across both traversal modes. The Stage 1 policy declines the multi-segment windows with `MPE-WINDOW-01`, and the piecewise policy declines a history with radii, in both cases reproducing the disabled totals bit for bit.

**Complete matrix, piecewise.** With the leader in two segments and the follower in three, the unchanged matrix reproduces the snapshot totals bit for bit. Nine mutations are refused. A sweep of forty-five single-field falsifications is refused in full, including eight on the fields that exist only for piecewise windows: a boundary removed, a boundary moved, the jump bound zeroed, the jump bound removed, the history grade, each member's last segment index, and the policy relabelled as single-slab.

**Author's consistency instrument.** After its known cases and known target, twenty further checks pass, including containment of the exact rational totals $1/9.995^2-1/10.005^2$ and $17/10125$ in every admitted piecewise radius and snapshot total, containment of the disabled-route totals in the grouped totals, and agreement of the piecewise and single-slab radii to one part in $10^9$. Its first run failed one check, and the fault was the instrument's: it required the piecewise radius to be no smaller than the single-slab radius, whereas per-cell control hulls can be slightly tighter than a whole-slab hull. The expectation was corrected to a two-sided agreement; the first run's output is retained beside the corrected one.

**Claim grade: measured**, by the named fixture commands over the retained outputs, with the shared-environment interpreter for every Python step. The instrument and the sweeps are the implementer's and are development evidence; independent acceptance is the reviewer's.
