## Remaining prefix binding obligation

◐ Partial — R0-3's shortened-window branch remains open. A copied schema, status, negative residual, common start and reception token establish neither the originating history collection nor its receiver/member coverage. Different histories can have identical time bounds while differing in causal membership. Equality of the retained starts therefore does not establish that an imported prefix certificate covers these members.

**Measured carrier limitation:** bounded `sed` reads of `NativeCausalPrefixExclusionCertificate` in `CoupledEvolution.hpp` and its generator in `CoupledEvolution.cpp` show that the current certificate carries numerical/window fields and an all-pairs flag, with no history manifest or member fingerprints. The generator computes its separation bound from the actual supplied collection, so its immediate use within that same snapshot can be valid. The copied public evidence loses that relationship unless the new consumer binds it. A review of the current scratch `MatchedPairPrefixEvidence` and admission code shows only the listed window/status checks; scratch bytes are still under implementation and are not an accepted frozen target.

Before accepting this branch, either bind the prefix proof to its actual source collection and receiver/member histories and verify that binding at consumption, independently recompute the exclusion from those actual inputs, or decline shortened-window grouping in Stage 1 and preserve exact fallback. The latter is a valid bounded first increment. Do not treat a hash of the unbound numerical fields as a history binding.

**Operator-checkable falsifier/control:** construct a prefix certificate from a different collection with the same retained start, active lower bound and reception token, then present it with a member having a retained root in the purportedly excluded prefix. The grouped route must refuse the mismatched evidence or establish exclusion anew from the actual histories. This control is independent of whether the genuine in-snapshot generator normally supplies the right object.
