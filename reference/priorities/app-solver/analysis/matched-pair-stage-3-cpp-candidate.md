# Scratch C++ Candidate: Validated Far-Step Producer and Grouped Consumption of Its Tubes

The [independent review of version 3](matched-pair-review-stage-3-addendum-4-2026-10-11.md) accepted the Python prototype as bounded predecessor evidence and released a serial scratch candidate in C++ that uses the solver's own interval and history types: first a producer for hypotheses H0 to H5 of [addendum 1](matched-pair-stage-3-addendum-1.md) with reconstructible ancestry evidence, then grouped consumption of its tubes under the tube form of the grouped theorem stated in the [Stage 2 design](matched-pair-stage-2-design.md). This record describes that candidate, what was checked, what failed on the way, and the narrow live seam it suggests. Evidence is in the [candidate receipt](../evidence/matched-pair-stage-3-cpp-candidate-receipt-2026-10-11.md).

The candidate exists only in a scratch copy of the solver tree and in the files retained beside the receipt. No file under `src/eom` was edited. Stage 3 of the [integration scope](../contracts/matched-pair-enclosure-integration-scope.md) is not entered: production evolution still refuses the grouped policy, nothing is persisted or transported, and no Surface or Borg file is touched. Every numerical instance uses $c_f=1$.

## 1. The producer

The [producer](../evidence/matched-pair-stage-3-cpp-candidate/validated_far_step_candidate.hpp) is one header compiled into a fixture program and linked against the scratch library. It works on `RetainedHistory` and `CubicHistorySegment` objects with the solver's outward-rounded `Interval` arithmetic, and it performs the same step as the accepted prototype.

- **H0.** All histories must start at one common time, end at the step start, and carry no analytic certificate. If any segment declares a radius, the population's set of path identifier, charge and history fingerprint must equal the published state of a step this producer certified under the same coupling; otherwise the step declines. A population with no declared radius is exact prescribed data, whose joins the history layer has already validated exactly.
- **H1.** The past speed bound is taken per segment over sixty-four abutting pieces, cached under a key made of every token that determines it. Nothing is read from or written to caller data.
- **H2 and H3.** As in the prototype: step boxes farther apart than the step, and a uniform lower sign for every ordered pair, present at the earliest reception time or absent at the latest.
- **H4.** The complete root window is found by repeated intersection, up to sixty-four passes, and the kernel is evaluated in interval arithmetic over the receiver box and the source's position and velocity tubes on that window.
- **H5.** A checked inclusion, with re-evaluation after each enlargement of the boxes.
- **Publication.** The segment is the quadratic through the centres of the inherited sections with the centre of the acceleration box as acceleration. Its declared radii are $\rho_x+h\rho_v+\tfrac{h^2}{2}\rho_a$ and $\rho_v+h\rho_a$, rounded up and then raised by two units in the last place so that the decimal token cannot fall below the bound. They dominate the theorem's sets at every time in the step because each term is nonnegative and nondecreasing in elapsed time. The segment is then appended with the history layer's own `appended`, so the layer's join rule also applies, and a nine-point sampled comparison of the theorem's sets against the segment's own evaluation guards the construction.
- **Budgets.** A per-step growth budget and an optional total-radius budget, each with its own refusal code.

Two points differ from the Python prototype and both came from failures recorded in section 4. The history layer evaluates a cubic over a time interval with interval arithmetic, which overestimates on a wide window; the candidate therefore takes hulls as unions of the layer's enclosures on forty-eight abutting pieces, which is still an enclosure. And the candidate's evidence is a state record instead of a per-layer chain, described next.

## 2. The evidence, and what it does and does not bind

For each certified step the producer records the coupling and, for every path, the identifier, charge and history fingerprint before and after the step. A population with declared radii is admitted only if its whole state equals the after-state of one record under the same coupling. A record is created only by a step that admitted its own input, so the after-state of the latest step can only have come from a chain of admitted steps down to exact prescribed data. Appending requires that the base equal the record's before-state and that the appended result equal its after-state.

This binds the full population, the charges, the coupling and, through the fingerprint, the complete content of every history. It is weaker than the prototype's binding in one respect and equal in the others. The history fingerprint is the solver's existing provenance fingerprint, a sixty-four-bit state and not a cryptographic digest, so the binding is only as strong as that fingerprint. The record is in one process's memory. Both would have to change before production: the consumer must be able to recheck a retained certificate chain, with a digest strong enough to carry the claim.

## 3. The consumer

The consumer is the matched-pair module with the tube form of the piecewise policy restored from the retained Stage 2 preparation, under a new policy name, `canonical_snapshot_piecewise_validated_tube/v1`. The [module difference from live](../evidence/matched-pair-stage-3-cpp-candidate/matched-pair-module-live-to-candidate.diff) is the whole of the consumer change; no other solver file differs in the scratch tree.

Under that policy a window may meet segments with declared radii. The member boxes for position and velocity are widened by the declared radii, so the convex domain, the distance floor, the speed bound, the equal-time separation and the membership residual are taken over the tubes. The acceleration bound is taken from the nominal cubic only, and is used only for the change of a member's nominal velocity between the two emission times. The velocity allowance is the nominal equal-time relative velocity, plus the nominal join term, plus each member's largest declared velocity radius over the window. That is the Stage 2 formula $\omega^+=w_0+a_0\ell^+/\nu+J+\varepsilon^v_++\varepsilon^v_-$ with $\ell^+=\ell_0+\varepsilon^x_++\varepsilon^x_-$.

Declared radii are admitted only with evidence. The receiver and both members must belong to one population that a producer registered, with these identifiers, charges and history fingerprints, under the request's coupling; otherwise the group declines with a new code, `MPE-EVIDENCE-01`, and the pair is evaluated exactly. The certificate's history grade is then `validated_far_step_tubes`. The theorem's regularity condition, a continuously differentiable true path whose velocity is the derivative the velocity tube encloses, is what H0 supplies for validated segments.

Everything else is the accepted snapshot machinery, unchanged: the deterministic disjoint pairing, two logical identities and one numerical interval per group, the two-slice width debit, both scheduling routes, the per-request and whole-matrix rechecks, and atomic exact fallback. In the candidate the evidence registry is a process-wide list inside the module. That was the smallest way to test the rule without threading a new argument through the snapshot code, and it is not the seam proposed in section 6.

## 4. Failures on the way

Four runs failed or exposed a defect before the retained run. Each is kept with its outputs.

1. **Exact-policy values moved in the last digit.** The restored tube code widened boxes by the padded enclosure of a zero radius token, so the exact piecewise policy's fixture output differed from live in the last place although no radius was declared. The candidate now treats an exactly zero token as exactly zero. After the change all six existing fixture outputs are byte-identical to live.
2. **The root window stalled.** The first long run declined at step 25 on budget. Interval evaluation of the source's cubic over a window a full segment wide returned a hull wide enough that the window intersection had a second, wide fixed point. Taking hulls over subdivided windows removed it.
3. **A refusal case stopped being a refusal.** With the tighter hulls the reviewer's fast affine source is resolved and declined only for budget; without a budget it certifies, and its acceleration box contains the exact value $-199/10000000$. The chart refusal is now exercised by a source that passes through the receiver's position just before its regular root.
4. **The retained tube code under-allowed the velocity difference.** It bounded the two members' relative velocity cell by cell with that cell's radii. The two emission times can lie in different cells, and validated radii grow from segment to segment, so a pair of times could need one member's radius from a later cell than the bound used. This was a soundness gap in code that had never been accepted. The candidate uses nominal relative velocity plus window maxima of both declared velocity radii, as the Stage 2 formula says. The equal-time position separation is correctly cell by cell and was left alone.

## 5. What was checked

A [separate checker](../evidence/matched_pair_stage_3_cpp_candidate_check.py) reads the candidate's outputs, converts every binary value and decimal token to an exact rational, rebuilds each end tube from the published tokens, and compares with truth references that use no candidate code. All 68 rows pass, with the known cases first.

- **Known.** The static two-path step contains the closed-form brackets; the history layer's own end interval contains the rebuilt tube. The three-path static case, receiver at $0$, a like charge at $10$ and an opposite charge at $11$, has acceleration boxes containing the reviewer's exact initial values $-21/12100000$, $101/100000$ and $-122/121000$. The snapshot totals on exact histories contain the same three values under the disabled and the validated policy and under both scheduling routes.
- **Producer.** A second step inherits radii that do not shrink. Two small steps on the control's oscillating past are certified within the $10^{-8}$ budgets and contain the small-time true brackets. The adversarial step of $2$ without a budget contains the frozen bracket and with the budgets declines. The 512-step run to $t=2$ certifies every step; its end velocity tube, half-width $1.89\times10^{-7}$, and its end position tube contain the frozen true brackets, and the velocity tube is disjoint from the solver's aliased interval by $2.06\times10^{-7}$.
- **Refusals.** Unequal starts; a speed arch, here a decimal variant with peak speed $3$, after a genuine step; an altered prescribed ancestor that keeps its joins exact, so that only the evidence can refuse it; the reviewer's ancestor moved to $11$, which the history layer itself refuses as discontinuous; a segment transferred between populations; changed coupling; changed charge; a renamed path; and the hypothesis and budget codes. Appending is refused on a different base, for a declined certificate, for an altered segment and for a forged status. Each refusal publishes nothing.
- **Reception from generated segments.** With steps of $4$ from a static start, by the fourth step every root window lies wholly on generated segments. A zero-coupling run with a uniformly moving source stays on its exact straight lines at every step.
- **Cross-language comparison.** On the three-path case and on the large-step static case the C++ tubes and the Python version 3 tubes intersect at every step, with the C++ velocity radius at most $1.11$ times the Python one. This compares two implementations of one theorem. It is not a truth reference.
- **Consumer.** After four validated steps of the three-path population, under each scheduling route, exactly one group is certified from validated tubes, for the receiver with the opposite pair as members. The exact count falls by one and the enclosed count rises by one against the disabled route, with nine logical relationships in both. The receiver's total width equals the group's one emitted width. Grouped and disabled totals intersect for every receiver and axis. The two scheduling routes give identical totals and groups. The exact-only policy declines the same histories and reproduces the disabled totals. With the coupling changed, a charge changed, a member's radius altered, or the registry cleared, no group is certified and the snapshot falls back to exact evaluation.
- **Unchanged routes.** From the scratch build, the native, acceleration and evolution fixtures and the matched-pair module, piecewise and evolution-guard fixtures give output byte-identical to the retained live outputs.

One result deserves stating without overstating. For the receiver, the grouped total is $[-2.01,\,2.01]\times10^{-6}$ where the disabled route gives $[-18.26,\,-1.74]\times10^{-6}$, because the disabled route replaces the farther member by a zero-centred individual enclosure. The grouped interval is narrower here. That is one configuration and one tolerance, and no cost was measured.

## 6. A proposed narrow live seam

This is a proposal for review. Nothing below has been applied.

1. **Module.** Apply the retained module difference to `MatchedPairEnclosure.hpp` and `MatchedPairEnclosure.cpp`: the policy name, the two radius fields in member evidence and their comparison, the widened boxes with exact treatment of zero radii, the window-maximum velocity allowance, the evidence refusal and the new history grade. The policy stays unknown to the default configuration.
2. **Evidence argument in place of the registry.** Replace the process-wide list by an evidence object that the caller passes: one field on the module request and one on the snapshot request, carrying the producer's population record. The complete-matrix recheck already re-certifies each group from the request, so it would recheck the evidence with no new mechanism.
3. **Producer as its own unit.** Add the producer as a separate source file with its certificate type. It should not enter `CoupledEvolution.cpp` until a default-disabled evolution policy is reviewed; the evolution entry points keep refusing the grouped policies meanwhile.
4. **Before any of that is more than a candidate:** a digest for the evidence stronger than the sixty-four-bit fingerprint; a retained, recheckable certificate chain; a rule mapping the producer's growth and total radii to the accepted-step ledger, which the candidate only reports; a prefix proof for unequal retained starts; and a cost measurement.

## 7. What this establishes and what it does not

**Claim grade: measured**, by the scratch build and checker identified in the receipt, on the listed cases. In the solver's own types and arithmetic, a producer can certify validated steps whose tubes contain separately bracketed true states and decline otherwise, and the grouped route can consume those tubes with evidence, preserving its accounting, its two scheduling routes and exact fallback, without changing any existing fixture output.

It does not establish a solver capability. The candidate is serial, scratch, exercised on two and three paths, first order, and bound by an in-process record over a sixty-four-bit fingerprint. The grouped consumer was compared with the disabled route and with exact initial values, not with an independent truth for the tube case. The fourth failure above shows that the tube code inherited from the earlier preparation had an unreviewed error, and the consumer's radius has had no independent review in its corrected form. Agreement between the C++ candidate and the Python prototype is agreement of implementations. Falsifiers: a certified step whose correctly bracketed true state lies outside its tube; a group certified from tubes without matching evidence; a grouped total disjoint from the exact route's total; a refusal that publishes; or any existing fixture output changed by the candidate.
