# Generated-History Inclusion, Addendum 4: Binding Generated Segments to Their Ancestry

[Addendum 3](matched-pair-stage-3-addendum-3.md) reported version 2 of the prototype validated far-step carrier. Its [independent review](matched-pair-review-stage-3-addendum-3-2026-10-11.md) closed the two earlier findings and found a third, VFS-R3. This addendum records what that finding shows about hypothesis H0 and how version 3 answers it. Evidence is in the [version 3 receipt](../evidence/matched-pair-stage-3-validated-far-step-v3-receipt-2026-10-11.md). The prototype remains scratch evidence outside the solver, and Stage 3 of the [integration scope](../contracts/matched-pair-enclosure-integration-scope.md) is not released. Every numerical instance uses $c_f=1$.

## 1. The finding

Version 2 admitted a segment labelled as generated whenever a digest of that segment's own contents was one the module had published. The reviewer took a genuine first step for a static receiver at $0$ and a static source at $10$, then changed the source's prescribed past to $11$ while keeping the generated segment that begins near $10$. No continuous path can be at $11$ up to the join and inside a tube around $10$ immediately after it. Version 2 certified the next step all the same. **Claim grade: measured**, by the reviewer's instrument and reproduced by the author's probes.

## 2. What H0 actually requires of a generated segment

H0 says that each path has one true trajectory, continuously differentiable on the whole retained window, lying in every tube. For a prescribed segment that is an assertion about data. For a generated segment it is a conclusion: the theorem proves that the true continuation of a particular validated past, for a particular population under a particular coupling, lies in the published tube. The tube is true of that continuation and of nothing else. A generated segment therefore carries meaning only together with four things: the complete history of every path up to its start, since every path's past enters every other path's acceleration; the identity and charge of every path; the coupling; and its position in its own path. Checking the segment's contents alone confirms that the module once published those numbers. It does not confirm that they describe the history now presented. Version 2 confused the two, and so did the wording of addendum 3, which spoke of binding "the label".

The induction in the theorem makes the same point from the other side. The inductive step extends a validated history by one layer. It never licenses detaching a layer from the history it extended.

## 3. The correction

Version 3 treats the segments that one step publishes for all paths as one layer, and records for each certificate the population, charges, coupling, step and a chained digest of every path's history up to the step start. A history is admitted only if it is prescribed segments followed by complete layers, each of which was published together by the module from exactly the history beneath it, for these identifiers and charges, under the coupling of the present step. Appending a continuation applies the same test to the certificate and its base. The receipt states precisely what is bound and lists the refusals tested: altered ancestors in either path and at any depth, a genuine layer placed on a different genuine ancestor, segments swapped between paths or transplanted between populations, renamed and added paths, changed coupling, changed charge, and altered, forged or misapplied certificates.

Two properties of the correction should be stated plainly. It is content-bound, so an exact copy of a genuine history is admitted and any difference is refused. And it is a record in one process's memory. That is enough to make the prototype's admission rule the right rule and to test it; it is not a mechanism another program could recheck. A production carrier must retain the generating certificate with the history and let the consumer verify the chain, as the snapshot stages do for the grouped certificate.

## 4. Result and disposition

Run unchanged through an import adapter, the scientific qualification gives the same 45 result lines for version 3 as for versions 1 and 2. The version 3 admission probes pass all 45 rows, including the reviewer's three constructions for VFS-R1, VFS-R2 and VFS-R3, genuine inheritance through 512 layers, and blocking assertions on the long run. Exact rational receipts are retained for every step, now including the recorded ancestry.

**Claim grade: measured** for those results, on the listed cases. Three successive reviews each found a way to make the prototype certify something false at admission, and none found an error in the enclosure arithmetic. That is evidence about where the risk in a real carrier lies: in what it is willing to treat as validated history, more than in the mathematics of the step. It is not evidence that no fourth defect exists. The prototype's standing is otherwise unchanged: a bounded demonstration outside the solver. Stage 3 is not entered; evolution entry points still refuse the grouped policy and exact fallback remains in force.
