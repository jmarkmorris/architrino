## VFS-R1 — unequal starts erase a retained source root

`step` sets `s_min` to the latest first segment time among all paths. It then treats that as the complete emission domain for every source. This is not a certified exclusion of the older source history. The existing matched-pair scope requires complete member coverage or a separately proved prefix exclusion; selecting a later shared start cannot delete a retained causal event.

**Measured by the independent probe after known analytical controls:** take receiver fixed at zero with history `[-5,0]`, source fixed at z=10 with history `[-20,0]`, both charges one, $\kappa=10^{-3}$, $h=1/128$, $c_f=1$. The prototype certifies both cross pairs absent at `s_min=-5` and publishes zero acceleration. Yet the supplied source history contains the receiver's root $S=-10$ at reception zero, with exact acceleration $-1/100000$. The shorter receiver history does not erase that source emission. The prototype's implicit reinterpretation as a different common-cutoff problem is outside this selected integration contract.

Fix by refusing unequal retained starts for this bounded increment, or by proving and binding every discarded member prefix independently. Equal-start shallow histories may remain a clearly stated finite-retained-domain case. Do not reinterpret longer supplied source histories as missing inputs.

## VFS-R2 — cached speed is not bound to mutable history bytes

`past_speed_bound` stores `_speed_bound` in an ordinary mutable segment dictionary and later reuses it without comparing coefficients or error tokens. The comment says segments are immutable, but the API neither enforces immutability nor binds the cache to the data.

**Measured by the independent probe:** begin with a genuine static known step, which installs the source's computed cache. Then replace that source's z cubic, about start $-20$, by coefficients $(10,0,1/5,-1/150)$. Its derivative is zero at both endpoints and has exact maximum two at emission $-10$. With fresh data the prototype declines `VFS-H1-01`. With the stale cache the reported past speed remains $10^{-36}$; the budgeted request reaches `VFS-BUDGET-01`, and the same request without a budget certifies. Thus the H1 violation is masked, independently of whether a later budget happens to refuse a request. The all-sub-wake/no-self-root premise cannot be certified from that stale value.

Remove the cache, enforce an immutable carrier type, or use an internally owned cache whose key binds every coefficient, time and position/velocity radius affecting the calculation. Do not trust caller-supplied cached scalars. Test mutated coefficients and radii after a genuine known computation, and confirm the subject declines at the required history/speed obligation. Identity-bound provenance is a further required production step; the prototype's trusted labels remain explicitly provisional.
