## Reverse audit and direct algebra check

Every manuscript section maps back to the source dispositions above or to explicitly conditional explanatory algebra. Section 2.1 adds a finite signed sum of regular radial hits whose coefficients are already supplied by the dynamics. Finiteness and regularity avoid claiming convergence or assigning values at singular roots. For a unit reference vector, the matrix P = I − rrᵀ satisfies rᵀP = 0 and P² = P by direct substitution of rᵀr = 1. Therefore projecting the retained sum gives its transverse component. Collinear delayed directions or cancellation of weighted transverse parts give zero; differently directed contributions can give nonzero. This is a derived algebraic possibility, not evidence that a complete root inventory or accepted moving assembly realizes it.

Section 2.2 preserves the original half-difference diagnostic and makes its hidden comparison requirements explicit: matched receiver events, a common frame, the same unit direction and a declared full-history pairing. Exchanging the paired histories negates their difference; applying the common P makes it transverse. The half-sum supplies the complementary even component. A frame change requires a mapping before subtraction, and unmatched boundaries or instantaneous velocity replacement do not establish motion reversal. No dynamical time-reversal symmetry, existence of a reversed solution, unique apparatus projection or persistent nonzero signal is proven. Section 2.3 expressly supplies no accepted evolved example and requires an interval or statistical meaning before calling a response persistent.

The new proportional-pair implication in §5.2 is also direct algebra: e₁(λg₁) − g₁(λe₁) = 0. It checks the null of the defined pairing, independently of author prose. It does not verify the assumed transverse law, conserved J or cone orbit in the primary paper, and it does not import those quantities into primitive dynamics. The manuscript separately defines the pair coefficient q versus the individual charge q and keeps c, μ₀ and mechanical angular momentum inside the standard comparison.

Section 3.2 narrows the original scalar wave-speed comparison by declaring local linear homogeneous isotropic effectively nondispersive lossless conditions and warning about other response objects and signal observables. That is a conservative typing of the source comparison, not a new derivation from the proposed constitutive map. No primitive wake-speed number is instantiated. This review verifies the qualification and its role in the local argument; it does not independently derive a Maxwell constitutive theorem or validate an actual medium. The gravitational and driven-apparatus paragraphs likewise remain hypotheses, not an equivalence principle or optical-gravity recovery.

The added warnings about one family not certifying another, a single transverse fluctuation not establishing persistence, and equal readouts not identifying microstates make the source burdens explicit without replacing them. No reverse-direction unsupported physical upgrade was identified by the full manuscript-to-source comparison. A missing source object, a changed formula/convention, or promotion of any of these conditional possibilities into a measured or derived physical result would overturn this acceptance.
