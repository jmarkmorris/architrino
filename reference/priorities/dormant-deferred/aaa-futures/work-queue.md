## Rules

1. Keep only accepted, testable tasks here.
2. Rank live objects by marginal ROI; item 1 is the local winner.
3. Use `Queued`, `In progress`, `Awaiting verification`, `Verified`, `Superseded`, or `Withdrawn`.
4. Move durable results to the work log or a focused packet and remove verified rows from the live queue.
