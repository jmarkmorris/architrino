One declared pro/anti pair contributes (6,6), so two pairs balance these counts, without proving operator reachability or physical participation. Omitting electron and antineutrino instead gives products (15,21) and delta (3,−3), agreeing with the separate negative regression. Identical reactant/product requests permit the stated three identity pass-throughs at the declared law grade. These checks use direct componentwise arithmetic, independently of any source implementation.

**Cubic geometry.** For the standard cubic polynomial B(t)=(1−t)^3 P0+3(1−t)^2t P1+3(1−t)t^2 P2+t^3 P3, direct differentiation yields endpoint derivatives 3(P1−P0) and 3(P3−P2). The source fixes both control heights to the same midpoint-plus-offset. Both derivatives can be horizontal only if both endpoint heights equal that control height. Different-row endpoints therefore contradict the general horizontal-tangent requirement; equal-row endpoints with nonzero offset also fail it. Manuscript §8.2 states precisely that conflict and leaves the separate “lateral” versus control-y wording unresolved. No replacement curve or live renderer behavior is inferred.

**Orientation and family count.** Feed prose fixes W+ to anti-core and W− to pro-core, while its composite table uses h and ah respectively with opposite orientation labels; both transforms remain “no.” Manuscript §3.2 preserves both facts.
