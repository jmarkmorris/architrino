### Conditional reconstruction mathematics

Chapter 3 supplies one possible answer to the seed’s open question about a useful residual. It declares a common metric record space, indexed admitted carrier states, stage decoders, transition maps and a separately fixed source record. These maps are placeholders to be supplied by a worked system, not recovered apparatus dynamics.

The receiving writer corrected the inline metric and stage-index notation and made the finite integer stage sequence explicit. Endpoint tolerance is finite and nonnegative, permitting exact equality at zero; local bounds are finite and nonnegative. The triangle-inequality sum includes the source-record error as well as every local handoff error. The argument is pathwise and requires no independence among errors. A probabilistic claim needs its own joint law or justified bounds; none is constructed here.

All errors in that sum use the same decoded record space, units, calibration and alignment. The sum does not combine heterogeneous pressure, bit-error, latency or semantic scores. Different projections require explicit comparison/reconstruction maps, and discarded endpoint-relevant information cannot be recovered without another declared source. Exact symbol equality is not proof of truth, authorship or energy balance.

The receiving writer sharpened the comparison-reference condition. A held-out input needs a separately retained source record to support reconstruction evidence. A declared code constraint tests the error class it detects; a pass alone need not identify the intended message. A decoder fitted afterward to output the target is not an independent comparison. These conditions retain the source’s demand to distinguish a useful residual from superficial resemblance.

Timing, delivery/persistence windows and source lineage are separate required conditions in §3.3. Good metric accuracy cannot compensate for lateness, wrong provenance or an incomplete physical account. Categorical, ordering or multiple mandatory checks may be more suitable than a metric for some objectives. The manuscript therefore does not claim a universal minimal residual or general method validation.
