## Manuscript-to-source and added-reasoning audit

The manuscript's new explanatory material consists chiefly of logical conditions for making the original comparison meaningful. These conditions narrow the claims rather than introduce a new physical model. An index needs elementary objects, allowed operations, equivalence and reuse semantics; a selection probability needs a specified alternative process, exposure and sampling/survival/detection account; a history map needs compatible delayed boundary data. None claims that the required bridge already exists.

The concatenation example in §2.1 is independently checked by elementary counting under the stated rules. With primitive x and free reuse of constructed strings, one binary concatenation produces length two and a second produces length four. After k operations the maximum available length is at most 2 to the power k, so fewer than two operations cannot produce length four. Under the separately stated model without reuse, a binary tree with four primitive leaves has three internal joins. The upper constructions and lower bounds agree. This is a proof about that explicit toy model, not an external assembly-index theorem, molecular experiment, architrino history construction or inherited AT convention. No numerical physical parameters or scientific execution are involved.

The hardness explanation in §3.2 has the required direction: reducing a known hard decision problem A to B permits an algorithm for B to decide A. A reverse reduction only says solving A suffices to solve B. Finite encodings, preservation of yes/no instances and separately appropriate approximation guarantees are correctly required. A count of unspecified causal-root conditions has no declared input language, admissible condition grammar, error semantics or identification criterion, so no NP-completeness or approximation claim is established for it. The text also correctly avoids equating conceptual difference with complexity-class separation or shared hardness with identity of measures.

The dependence argument does not overcorrect the original into a claim that copying is irrelevant. Copies sharing an origin can inform replication, survival or selection under an appropriate model, while their correlation changes the evidence calculation. That is compatible with requiring independent references for computational verification and does not turn that repository rule into an asserted external AT rule.

The dynamical discussion limits its claim to acceleration consistency on a declared history/domain. A balanced prescribed configuration alone is not a perturbation theorem or a physically reachable retained population; a failed candidate alone does not imply universal dispersal. The manuscript preserves delayed-history geometry, polarity, Euclidean space and absolute time and imports no recipe law, selection postulate or complexity class into architrino dynamics.
