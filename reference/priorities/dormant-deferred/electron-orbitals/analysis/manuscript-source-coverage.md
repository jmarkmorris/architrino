## Explicit source tensions and author clarifications

1. The source gives the familiar diagonal fill order and later displays exceptions. Manuscript §3.1 scopes the sequence as typical neutral buildup and §5.3 preserves exceptions; neither original is repaired.
2. The source emphasizes highest-n valence but uses Fe 4s/3d participation. Manuscript §3.2 makes highest n a main-group guide and keeps transition-family participation. No new chemistry data is imported.
3. EOC is called a geometric ledger and possible dynamical/minimal state code while the source says its geometry-to-code map is imported from QM. Manuscript §§1.1, 6.3 keeps the strong proposal and the missing derivation, and explains absent orientation, spin, phase, and trajectory information.
4. Additive `1/C` tokens denote one electron each. Numerical summation yields occupancy fraction, not electron count. §4.2 defines both interpretations and preserves the source's full expansion in support; no fractional charge, probability, or persistent particle identity is inferred.
5. The formal complement of a closed row is empty, omitted in the printed table. §5.2 defines the extended domain and does not call the observed set symmetry-closed. Half-filling stability remains a source comparison, not a consequence of the fixed-point identity.
6. The source's vacancy invariant leaves its row domain implicit. §5.4 distinguishes an occupied-only set from a fixed comparison set, including core accounting. No infinite-state vacancy sum is asserted.
7. Source “almost monotone” successor language does not imply coordinatewise monotonicity or a physical path through one evolving atom. §5.3 uses matched subshell coordinates with zero padding and source-attributed counts; a minimal-path theorem would require additional definitions.
8. Recorded neutral-table absences are not prohibitions on other states. §5.3 gives the complete eleven-code list and scope. No compressed exclusion law or hidden number-theoretic discovery is reported.
9. The localized orbital picture offers four alternative geometries and a six-way effective reinterpretation without constructing atomic solutions. §6 keeps every alternative and explicitly retains absent sea response, exclusion, minimum approach, stability, and observation law. No Born/Pauli/Hund recovery follows from a code or picture.
10. Full/configuration/shorthand/EOC/additive columns can use different display orders. §§4.1–4.3 treat the rows as subshell-indexed occupancies and retain the source's marker conventions; no chronological significance is invented for line order.
