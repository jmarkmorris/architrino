## Mathematical qualifications checked separately from source copying

**C1 — Dilation and blackbody shape.** Substitution into the occupation denominator gives temperature divided by the dilation factor. Multiplication by the cubic frequency prefactor gives the different specific-intensity amplitude convention. For positive nonzero transport depth, scalar attenuation commutes with dilation but multiplies the occupation by a number strictly between zero and one. Equality with any Planck occupation across all frequencies would force the original temperature by the exponential tail, then force unit amplitude, a contradiction. This directly supports the manuscript's counterexample to sufficiency. It is not an exclusion theorem for every transport generator or a proof about an actual photon path; changing the function space, domain or physical family would require a new analysis.

**C2 — Energy signs and local law.** Under the declared redshift-energy comparison, emitted-minus-observed is positive while observed-minus-emitted is its negative. A positive sea deposit balances the latter. This algebra explains the source shorthand without changing its original signs. The volume-to-boundary implication is conditional on a regular local continuity law and a fixed region; it neither derives that law nor justifies a global infinite sum. An explicit alternative debit convention could preserve the source equation, but must be named.

**C3 — Measures and predictive fractions.** Lifting states to a space-time occupation/event space makes the conditioning intersection well typed. The finite positive denominator and inherited history law are necessary; neither is constructed by the ratio. The output fraction and initial fitted fraction refer to different spaces and fixed measures. The manuscript correctly avoids equating small burden with universal typicality. A nonmeasurable set, undefined history law or zero/infinite denominator blocks the intended quantitative interpretation.

**C4 — Residual and covariance scope.** A signed standardized residual can equal the negative of a positive compatibility penalty, giving zero while both component failures persist. Separate component conditions prevent that algebraic cancellation. The covariance expression remains conditional on a valid joint model, with singular directions and duplicated derived information explicitly named. No survey likelihood is independently reconstructed here.

**C5 — Effective regimes.** The manuscript labels canonical slow roll, thermal-relic abundance, linear adiabatic relations, separable growth and standard relic-neutrino estimates as comparisons under restricted assumptions. It does not use the compact formulas as general nonthermal or nonlinear laws. The BBN species warning preserves the distinction between total participating radiation and a declared neutrino-equivalent convention; no modern numerical convention is certified by this review.

**C6 — Entropy ownership.** With one nonoverlapping account, moving a negative ordered-subsystem term to the other side gives the non-strict compensation inequality. If that subsystem is outgoing, it must first be removed from the outgoing remainder. Equality is sufficient for the stated feasibility inequality. Correlation/access terms and actual positive exports remain unconstructed; the argument does not derive the effective second law from primitive dynamics or identify a low-entropy core.
