## Direct mathematical review

**Boolean conjunction.** With all four factors in the set {0,1}, their product is one precisely when every factor is one: any zero factor makes the product zero, and four ones multiply to one. This proof is pointwise and uses no probability distribution or statistical independence. The manuscript correctly treats real-valued scores as a different construction requiring a declared interpretation. Naming the ledger or persistence factor does not derive its physical content.

**Normalization.** For a finite or countable family of measurable eligible-basin intersections, set each weight to its nonnegative measure and let Z be their sum. If Z is finite and positive, each quotient is nonnegative and the sum of quotients is exactly one. This conclusion requires neither basin disjointness nor finite total measure of the entire history space. To interpret the denominator as the measure of the eligible set and the labels as mutually exclusive detector outcomes, the additional disjointness and eligible-set coverage assumptions stated in §3.2 suffice by countable additivity, allowing null exceptions. No stronger physical interpretation is silently inferred from normalization alone.

**Overlap and empty or infinite weight.** If one unit-weight eligible history belongs to two outcome basins, the normalized labels can each have weight one-half while counting the same history twice. Thus normalized numbers alone are not an exclusive outcome assignment, exactly as the manuscript says. Zero Z gives no conditional distribution; infinite Z does not license the displayed normalization or arbitrary equal weights. The source's invariant-measure notation supplies no existence, invariance, ergodicity/frequency or Born theorem, and the manuscript makes those absences explicit.

**The binary conditioning example.** At each fixed setting b, the declared unfiltered distribution assigns one-half to X=0 and one-half to X=1. Keeping only X=b leaves total retained weight one-half. At b=0 the retained X=1 numerator is zero; at b=1 it is one-half. Division therefore gives 0 and 1 respectively, despite equal retained fractions. This directly verifies the equation in §3.3 without code or scientific execution. The example specifies no separated devices, accessible local selection flag or transmission protocol; it proves a conditional-probability distinction, not physical signaling.

**Ensemble versus single-history status.** Operational no-signaling compares accessible marginals across contexts. The manuscript correctly leaves the single-history factor as notation awaiting a legitimate ensemble construction. Kastner's light-speed explanation is reported without either crediting an uninspected marginal proof or claiming no such proof exists. The same restraint applies to the assembly proposal. Eligibility does not provide a many-to-one event selector, and comparing record persistence windows with invariant spacetime events does not equate those distinct concepts.
