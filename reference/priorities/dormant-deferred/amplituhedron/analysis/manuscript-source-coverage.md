## Explicit qualifications and derivations

**Q1 — A probability measure is not automatically a canonical form.** Pushforward of a finite positive measure remains finite and positive. A canonical form has orientation and boundary-pole data and may not have finite total mass on its real interior. The displayed interval form has integral twice the logarithm of the cutoff ratio and diverges as the cutoff vanishes. This counterexample rules out an unqualified identification with a normalized full-domain probability, not every possible relation to histories. A regulated event measure or an independently derived amplitude-valued form needs its own construction and cannot be inserted as an established result.

**Q2 — Outcome data do not uniquely reconstruct amplitudes.** An arbitrary overall phase preserves a single amplitude's squared magnitude. Relative phase requires interference-sensitive data, and conventions still matter. The source's staged recovery arrows are obligations, not an inverse theorem from detector rates to a complex amplitude or from a finite window to an asymptotic S-matrix.

**Q3 — Factorization and charts.** The source's product of two three-point amplitudes is retained for a declared single channel with fixed conventions. General intermediate species/polarizations can require sums and normalization factors, and loop singularities need their appropriate comparison. Chart invariance applies to forms/measures with transformation laws, not raw equality of coordinate coefficients. A residual must have a common type, domain, norm, units and error bound. Positive causal-root magnitudes or generic stratification do not imply ordered-minor positivity.

**Q4 — Independent interval illustration.** Direct partial fractions split the interval form into endpoint terms; the two artificial interior terms have equal and opposite coefficients, so their pole cancels. Integrating the unit-interval coefficient gives the displayed logarithm. These two elementary derivations were authored independently of any numerical program and are not an evaluated amplituhedron or retained assembly. An algebraic failure of either identity would reopen the draft.

**Q5 — External and empirical scope.** The original planar theory, bibliographic program, realistic extension and cost/opportunity discussion are retained as historical source descriptions. No current literature, collider pipeline or performance test was independently audited. A representation that agrees with an existing amplitude need not create a new discriminator, and elegance is not evidence of lower measured cost. Scientific activation still belongs to the preserved revisit decision.
