## Why the disposition is bounded

A manuscript campaign can explicitly record why a target currently has no additional developed explanation. Here an extra manuscript would paraphrase the short retained remit and rules or introduce unsupported creative subject matter. The disposition remains part of campaign accounting and requires separate review; it does not imply that only scientific lanes deserve manuscripts.

A developed concept, substantial draft, selected brief or retained production result would reopen the decision. A newly found in-lane source or substantive asset would also overturn the current inventory premise. Content discovered elsewhere would require an explicit ownership and source review, not retroactive claims of having read it.
