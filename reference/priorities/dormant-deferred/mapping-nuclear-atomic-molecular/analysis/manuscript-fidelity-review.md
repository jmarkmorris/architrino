## Independent checks of source tensions and added reasoning

**Toy attraction and asymptotic fields.** The corridor source gives sampled values of an incomplete effective expression with chosen scales. It omits the short-core term and does not solve a spectral or retained-history problem. The manuscript correctly restricts its negative proton–neutron and positive proton–proton observations to the sampled reduced row. A Gaussian is nonzero at every finite argument with finite nonzero width, even though it decays, so its profile does not provide compact support. Nor does a small scalar residual sample determine the open-color projection. Both distinctions survive explicitly.

**Cusp type and fission term.** The source defines a pair-valued window but writes an argmax over a scalar mass number as its member. The manuscript instead requires a maximizing pair, a nonempty beta-stable set, domain and tie convention. Its symmetric Coulomb split follows directly by collecting the powers of two, yielding the factor 2 to the power −2/3. The roughly 63% comparison concerns only that term; surface creation, deformation, shell, products and sea accounts remain required. It does not become a fission theorem or a global graph optimum.

**Projection norm and uniformity.** For the manuscript's real matrix with rows (1,a) and (0,0), direct multiplication gives P squared equal to P and P times its transpose equal to diag(1+a²,0). Its Euclidean operator norm is therefore the square root of 1+a², greater than one for nonzero a. This independently validates the counterexample to the outside object's idempotence-to-unit-norm inference. Orthogonality or a separate declared bound would resolve that implication; neither is supplied by idempotence alone. The manuscript also correctly requires a bound uniform over the limit domain: finite constants at individual radii need not control the limit. With one finite K, a nonnegative open-field quantity bounded by K times a vanishing tail squared does vanish, but this elementary implication supplies none of its physical premises.

**Support, extraction and variational existence.** A locally accepted density provider, tail alias, singlet support or no-free-color row cannot bypass the top envelope, shared-event or no-open obligations.
