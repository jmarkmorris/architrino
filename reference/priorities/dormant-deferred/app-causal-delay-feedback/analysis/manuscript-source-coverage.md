## Conditional algebra, source conflicts, and claim limits

Manuscript §4 contains new explanatory algebra from prescribed causal-wake geometry, explicitly authorized by the coordinator after the source tensions were reported. It is not numerical experimentation or an EOM acceptance result. The geometry assumes a fixed receiver at the origin, source at (v u,b), v>0, b nonzero, positive c_f, full unbounded emission history and positive delays; R is smooth and positive. The arrival map t(u)=u+R/c_f is differentiated directly. The source-to-receiver unit vector gives projection −v²u/R, approaching +v as u→−∞ and −v as u→+∞. Roots design 52 and Roots log 19 instead claim +v at both infinities. That literal source error remains unchanged and is expressly identified; no external chapter or runtime was consulted to attribute or repair its cause.

For beta>1, t′ increases from 1−beta to 1+beta with positive t″, so it has a single minimum, its stated u* and t* follow from t′=0, and t tends to +∞ at both ends. Before/at/after the minimum there are zero/one double/two simple full-history roots. For beta<1, strict monotonicity with full real arrival range gives one full-history root. Finite histories can omit roots and finite observation windows can miss the fold. At beta=1 and nonzero b, derivative is positive at every finite u and arrival range is (0,∞), so an asymptotic threshold does not create a finite ordinary fold. At b=0, distance |vu| is nonsmooth at coincidence and the smooth-fold argument fails. These assumptions narrow the source's unqualified one-root/any-impact assertions without inferring a physical prohibition or resolving coincident same-source birth.

For the smooth positive-distance residual g=c_f(T_r−T_t)−R, manuscript §4.2 separately differentiates the two event coordinates: g_Tr=D_r=c_f−rhat·V_i and g_Tt=−D_t=−c_f+rhat·V_j, giving dT_t/dT_r=D_r/D_t along a simple root. This identifies the mathematical distinction between the signed playback derivative and nonnegative c_f/|D_t| acceleration weight. It is conditional notation, not a current producer-schema equivalence or permission to rename fields. The ordinary normal-form pair carries opposite emission-derivative signs, hence local signed-degree cancellation; tangent zero itself requires more than a sign-change marker. Nonzero quadratic/control coefficients, smooth interior history and no higher/coincident stratum are explicitly required.

The finite-impulse discussion derives only the elementary integral of 2C/sqrt(s) over (0,epsilon), which equals 4C sqrt(epsilon). Applying it requires the assumed finite-C bound on the norm of the paired physical contribution.
