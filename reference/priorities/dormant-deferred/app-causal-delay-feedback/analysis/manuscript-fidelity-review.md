## Independent mathematical assessment

For the newly defined smooth positive-distance residual, differentiating receiver time gives the receiver factor and differentiating emission time gives the negative transmitter factor. The chain rule along a simple root therefore gives the signed playback derivative stated in §4.2. The absolute value in the acceleration weight makes it a different quantity, even when the receiver is fixed. The manuscript neither identifies the two readouts nor authenticates a producer schema from this algebra.

Before reading the candidate I independently took a fixed receiver at the origin, a straight source path with constant speed v and nonzero transverse offset b, and distance R equal to the square root of v²u²+b². Arrival time is emission time plus R/c_f. The source-to-receiver unit vector gives projection −v²u/R; its limits are positive v at negative infinity and negative v at positive infinity. This directly contradicts the same-positive-limit sentence in Roots design line 52 and Roots log line 19. The manuscript's correction is explicit, and both source bytes remain preserved.

Direct differentiation gives arrival derivative 1+v²u/(c_f R) and strictly positive second derivative v²b²/(c_f R³) for positive v and nonzero b. Above threshold the derivative crosses zero exactly once. Solving that equation gives exactly the source-time minimum and receiver-time minimum printed in §4.3, including the absolute offset and all factors of speed. Both arrival-map ends tend to positive infinity, so the complete-history count is zero below, one double root at, and two simple roots above the minimum. This is a prescribed-path geometric derivation, not evidence that the path solves coupled dynamics.

The exclusions are material and present: a finite history or observation window can lose the relevant event; below threshold the stated full-history map is strictly increasing onto the real line; at threshold with nonzero offset its range is (0, infinity) in the chosen time origin and the limiting boundary is not a finite ordinary fold; zero offset makes distance nonsmooth at coincidence. The explicit positive-speed assumption excludes the stationary case from that proof without making a false statement about it. No universal all-impact, all-window or self-interaction conclusion survives unqualified. Symbolic wake speed is retained; no nonnormalized numerical experiment is introduced.

For an ordinary interior fold, nonzero quadratic and transverse coefficients give two simple roots on one side, with opposite derivative signs; the pair has zero net signed degree. At the event a double root may not be a sign-changing zero. The manuscript states the necessary smoothness, nondegeneracy and interior-domain hypotheses and excludes higher/coincident strata. A finite-window endpoint crossing is therefore not silently classified as a generic pair event.

The paired-contribution integral in §4.4 is also correctly conditional. An assumed norm bound 2C divided by the square root of positive time distance integrates over a one-sided interval of width epsilon to 4C times the square root of epsilon. This follows by an elementary antiderivative independently of the cited chapter. Neither that calculation nor source repetition proves the acceleration envelope, finite constant, transversality, nonzero separation, global continuation or coincident self-hit regularization. The manuscript explicitly leaves the full source proof outside its reading scope and does not upgrade integrability into physical branch acceptance.
