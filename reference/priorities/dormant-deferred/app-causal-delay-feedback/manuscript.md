# Causal History, Wake Geometry, and the Limits of a Teaching Display

## 1. A Reception Refers to an Earlier Event

### 1.1. The subject of the display

Causal Delay Feedback makes an earlier emission and a later reception visible together. The present receiver event is connected to an emission point in retained path history, rather than to an object's present location merely because it is nearby. Expanding wake surfaces provide the geometric connection. A circle on the two-dimensional canvas represents a section of a spherical wake, while a highlighted arc identifies the portion relevant to a displayed reception.

The app's purpose is explanatory. The retained product account reports eight ordered lessons, a Roots view, and Laboratory within one canvas-first application. Its numerical fixtures, replay projections, local drag previews, and browser comparisons remain display-only. A coherent picture of reception does not establish that the pictured history solves the coupled equations, that all physical roots have been certified, or that a branch binds or remains stable.

The [current product requirements](v1-product-requirements.md) preserve exact accepted learner copy and its interaction boundaries. The [advanced progression](causal-delay-feedback-advancement.md) and [Roots design](roots-requirements-and-design.md) describe deferred teaching work. Their proposals are not additional live modes or a second solver. This exposition explains their subject while preserving the distinction between the accepted surface, recorded evidence, and a proposed extension.

### 1.2. Emission time and receiver time

Let $T_t$ denote emission time and $T_r$ receiver time. The source path is evaluated at the former; the receiver event is evaluated at the latter. The role labels make clear why changing a time cursor can change which earlier event is relevant. The source packets relate these symbols to the master-equation chapter's receiver time $T$ and emission time $T_{\mathrm{em}}$; this notation correspondence is not a new dynamical law.

A causal root is an emission event whose expanding wake reaches the selected receiver event. The Roots proposal writes the delay-map condition as

$$
g(T_r;T_t)=0.
$$

The plot, wake intersection, history row, root identity, and selected branch must identify that same event pair. Agreement among those views is an internal consistency requirement because they consume the same state. It cannot serve as an independent proof of the evaluator that supplies them.

The retained documents disagree about whether new role labels should say source or transmitter, and the older plan uses $D_s$ where the later design uses $D_t$. Exact accepted lesson copy and existing producer fields are preserved. The mathematical discussion below defines its symbols explicitly; it does not resolve the interface disagreement by relabeling a field or changing learner copy.

## 2. The Accepted Lessons and Their Evidence Boundary

### 2.1. A fixed teaching surface

The accepted product sequence has eight lesson identities: meet, emission, meaning, motion, forward-buildup, inverse-square-spreading, superposition, and continuous-delayed-feedback, followed by Roots and Laboratory. Each has exact approved title and body text in the product source. Those strings, the two reciprocal relationship lines, the Laboratory title, and the lesson-local speed-selector labels are retained verbatim in the supporting coverage. This manuscript is not replacement app copy.

The accepted interactions are deliberately limited. A persistent lesson-and-tools list accompanies the standard navigation controls and one shared timeline with first frame, play/pause, last frame, and scrubber. Arcs and Full are mutually exclusive wake views. The learner surface has a fixed display rather than a settings panel, theme picker, animation-speed menu, general architrino-speed control, or reset-preset menu. The speed comparison belongs only to the designated lesson.

That local speed change returns its lesson to the first frame, stops playback, clears completion and resume state, and retains the chosen speed. Other transitions carry their own identity conditions: the second and third lessons meet at a shared handoff frame; the field-speed lesson enters at emission zero without inherited wake history; its fronts remain circular in screen space and none leads the body. These are source-specified display behaviors, not measured dynamics in this manuscript.

### 2.2. A fixture does not establish an interaction law

The spherical-spreading lesson holds both bodies fixed while equal-interval wakes expand. Its accepted inverse-square statement is scoped by the product source to that declared spherical fixture. The source's area expression and $1/R^2$ learner relation are not evidence that the app independently derives a general physical acceleration law. No mass, force law, or observer-level recovery principle is introduced as a substrate premise.

The superposition lesson fixes a three-body display, exactly two selected incoming wakes, two component arrows, and one net-acceleration arrow. Its accepted copy explicitly limits the scene: it establishes no physical acceleration law, measured magnitude, binding, stability, or solved trajectory. Showing a component sum can teach which displayed contributions are being combined while leaving the authority of those contributions unchanged.

The continuous-feedback lesson separates its sampled active pair from accumulated frozen screen history. The frozen arcs record earlier frames of the teaching animation; they are not a solved continuous trajectory or a new retained-history numerical certificate. Likewise, Laboratory's local direct manipulation remains a teaching preview, with endpoint ornaments and initial-velocity handles hidden even when internal interaction helpers exist.

### 2.3. Replaying a record without creating its evidence

The product account describes a default replay adapter that accepts a supplied recorded dataset. Without such an injection, the ordinary route falls back to a representative teaching replay. An explicit mock query also selects the teaching path. The representative replay is shaped like solver output, but it is not solver output or independent numerical evidence.

For a supplied record, the adapter projects recorded time-position samples and retains the carried engine, claim, evidence, run, time-window, and worldline-role provenance. It does not run or recompute the EOM solver, infer delayed hits from positions alone, or accept canvas edits as solver input. A record lacking delayed-hit rows explicitly disables causal evaluation and carries the reason that those rows are absent.

Two authorities therefore remain visible. The record's metadata describes what its producer claims. The viewer's use remains recorded-path display with display-only evidence and physics acceptance false. A faithful drawing of positions cannot fill in missing reception rows. The retained literal record contract is preserved in the supporting provenance; it is not silently updated or asserted compatible with a different live contract.

## 3. One History Across Simple and Advanced Views

### 3.1. Shared state and event identity

The reported architecture has one runtime, learner state, causal-root evaluator, timed-path sampler, and wake renderer. Story, Roots, and Laboratory are projections inside the same application shell. Query selection and browser Back/Forward restore the appropriate view without creating another application, and unrelated replay parameters and fragments remain intact. Internal history or branch helpers do not become live navigation entries simply because a module exists.

The deferred progression makes the state contract explicit. It carries or references stable source and receiver identities, their paths and geometry, retained source history, receiver and emission times, causal roots and stable root identities, accepted and rejected branch rows with reasons, wake geometry, selected root or ledger row, playback state, and replay provenance. Simple views can hide diagnostics; they cannot replace these objects with unrelated local examples while claiming continuity.

The common evaluator must supply both a prediction answer and the root shown after the reveal. A ledger selection must choose the same emission point, wake intersection, receiver event, root, and producer-carried contribution. A branch sum must consume the very rows displayed individually. This prevents a seemingly synchronized interface from quietly computing a different answer behind each panel.

### 3.2. The deferred learner progression

The advanced proposal proceeds through Story, Prediction, Path History, Roots, Self-Hit, Branch Lab, and Sandbox. Its first extension centers on one source, one wake, one receiver, and one reception, keeping the emission point visible as the objects move. Prediction pauses before reception and offers a small number of candidate points from that same history. The evaluator supplies the correct answer; an incorrect choice receives explanation and a retry. Moving-source, moving-receiver, curved-path, and hidden-wake rounds follow only after the simple-root round meets its checks.

Path History distinguishes inactive, currently causal, selected, rejected, and unavailable history, preserving producer-carried reasons. Roots links the delay-map zeros to wake intersections, root count, ordinal, times, and scene selection. If those counts disagree, the proposal requires a visible unavailable state and a failing focused check, rather than two plausible but inconsistent pictures.

Self-Hit compares below-, at-, and above-field-speed paths through geometry and the producer's actual diagnostics. Its states include absent, tangent, active, unresolved, and failed-floor. Branch Lab retains accepted, rejected, unresolved, and unavailable branches with stable identities and colors, individual contributions, their common-row vector sum, filters for age, magnitude, root kind and transversality, and visible reasons. Sandbox receives the same geometry, time, paths and selection; returning to a guided view does not silently reset them without a new lesson.

These stages remain proposed teaching work. The accepted navigation is still the eight lessons, existing Roots mode and Laboratory. Five deferred queue objects preserve the extension's order and completion boundaries, but none is executable while the owner remains dormant. The older instruction to inspect settings or reset controls cannot override the later accepted surface that excludes those menus.

## 4. The Ordinary Causal-Root Fold

### 4.1. A local root event with explicit assumptions

The Roots packet's central example is an ordinary interior fold. Two simple roots merge at a tangency and appear or disappear as the control crosses the event. Its local normal form is recorded as

$$
g\approx\alpha(T_t-T_{t,\ast})^2+\lambda(T_r-T_{r,\ast}).
$$

For an ordinary fold, the quadratic coefficient and the transverse control coefficient are nonzero, the relevant path and delay map are smooth, and the event lies inside the considered history domain. On the side where the local quadratic has real roots, there are two; on the other side there are none. The roots have opposite signs of the emission-time derivative. Thus the local count changes by $\Delta N=\pm2$, while the pair's signed-degree contribution cancels, giving $\Delta D=0$.

This is a statement about a generic pair event. At the tangent itself there is one double root, so simply counting sign-changing crossings can miss the event. Cusps and higher singularities require a different treatment. The sharp coincident same-source birth remains explicitly unresolved in the progression packet; the ordinary-fold lesson cannot supply its missing regularization or singular-stratum result.

### 4.2. Transversality is a projection, not a speed label

For this discussion, let $\hat{\mathbf r}_{ij}$ point from the earlier source position to the receiver event. The later Roots design defines

$$
D_{t,ij}=c_f-\hat{\mathbf r}_{ij}\cdot\mathbf V_j.
$$

A simple root requires this derivative factor to remain away from zero. A fold candidate occurs where it vanishes, together with the smoothness and nondegeneracy conditions just stated. The source velocity component toward the receiver then equals the wake speed. Total speed above $c_f$ does not, by itself, locate a root, prove that the retained history includes it, or establish a self-hit.

The source distinguishes two quantities that a readout must not conflate: the transmitter-side acceleration weight $W_{r\leftarrow t}^{\mathrm{acc}}=c_f/|D_t|$ and the signed playback derivative $D_r/D_t$. One is nonnegative and tied to the acceleration contribution's source factor; the other concerns how emission time changes with receiver time and includes the receiver factor. For the smooth geometric residual $g=c_f(T_r-T_t)-R(T_r,T_t)$, direct differentiation gives $D_r=\partial_{T_r}g=c_f-\hat{\mathbf r}_{ij}\cdot\mathbf V_i$, $\partial_{T_t}g=-D_t$, and hence $dT_t/dT_r=D_r/D_t$ along a simple root. This conditional algebra does not authenticate a producer field. Its actual names and definitions must remain visible. Missing or inconsistent fields are a documentation or data blocker, not permission to invent a value in the browser.

### 4.3. A straight-line example and a source correction

The deferred design says that any above-field-speed straight-line source has a fold regardless of impact parameter, justifying this with a projection limit of positive speed at both ends of the line. That two-ended sign statement is incorrect for an oriented line of sight. The following elementary geometry explains the useful restricted result and its exclusions. It is new explanatory algebra for a prescribed path, not an EOM solution or an independently certified physical branch.

Take a fixed receiver at the origin and a source at $(vu,b)$ at emission time $u$, with $v>0$, nonzero perpendicular offset $b$, an unbounded emission history, constant positive $c_f$, and only positive-delay receptions. Define the distance and arrival time by

$$
R(u)=\sqrt{v^2u^2+b^2},\qquad t(u)=u+\frac{R(u)}{c_f}.
$$

Here $u=T_t$ and $t=T_r$. The vector from source to receiver gives

$$
\hat{\mathbf r}\cdot\mathbf V=-\frac{v^2u}{R(u)}
\longrightarrow
\begin{cases}
+v,&u\to-\infty,\\
-v,&u\to+\infty.
\end{cases}
$$

Consequently, the arrival map satisfies

$$
t'(u)=1+\frac{v^2u}{c_fR(u)},\qquad
t''(u)=\frac{v^2b^2}{c_fR(u)^3}>0.
$$

With $\beta=v/c_f>1$, its derivative increases from $1-\beta<0$ to $1+\beta>0$. There is exactly one minimum, at

$$
u_\ast=-\frac{|b|}{v\sqrt{\beta^2-1}},\qquad
t_\ast=\frac{|b|\sqrt{\beta^2-1}}{\beta c_f}.
$$

The arrival time tends to positive infinity at both ends, so a fixed receiver time below the minimum has no root, at the minimum has a double root, and above it has two simple roots in this complete-history model. The nonzero second derivative makes the event an ordinary fold. Impact parameter changes its time and scale while preserving that conclusion under the stated assumptions.

For $\beta<1$, the arrival map is strictly increasing from negative to positive infinity, giving one full-history root for each receiver time. A finite retained history can contain fewer roots, and a restricted observation window can miss the fold altogether. At $\beta=1$ with nonzero $b$, the derivative remains positive at every finite emission time; the limiting arrival time as $u\to-\infty$ is zero, not a finite double root. At zero impact parameter, $R(u)=|vu|$ is nonsmooth at coincidence, so the preceding smooth ordinary-fold proof does not apply. These qualifications preserve the teaching comparison without extending it to coincident self-interaction.

All numerical teaching instantiations use $c_f=1$. Symbolic $c_f$ is retained here to show the geometric dependence; no numerical run or alternative wake-speed fixture is introduced.

### 4.4. A singular value can have finite accumulation

The source packet distinguishes a near-fold pointwise acceleration spike from accumulated velocity change. On the applicable side of an ordinary fold, its schematic magnitude grows with inverse square-root distance to the fold. This singularity is locally integrable: under a bound by $2C/\sqrt{s}$ for the norm of the paired contribution, with $s$ the positive receiver-time distance from the fold and $C$ finite,

$$
\int_0^\varepsilon\frac{2C}{\sqrt{s}}\,ds=4C\sqrt{\varepsilon}.
$$

This elementary integral illustrates the form of the packet's retained bound

$$
\int\|\mathbf A_{ij,+}+\mathbf A_{ij,-}\|\,dT_r\le4C\sqrt\varepsilon.
$$

It does not establish the required bound or its constant for an arbitrary physical history. The acceleration premise comes from the cited ordinary-fold treatment, whose full source is outside this manuscript's reading scope. Nor does a schematic plot prove a globally continued solution through a coincident or higher-order singularity. The teaching requirement is to show the pointwise spike and finite accumulation separately, without making the spike look like an infinite velocity kick.

## 5. Four Views of the Same Fold

### 5.1. A synchronized explanation

The deferred Roots design proposes a two-by-two arrangement. Pane A plots the delay map against emission time at the selected receiver time and marks roots. Pane B shows the source path, expanding circles or spheres, and receiver event. Pane C records active-root count over receiver time. Pane D shows the schematic acceleration spike and accumulated velocity change. Their common state identifies one event through algebra, geometry, counting, and a qualified integral.

Away from the tangent event, Pane A's simple-root count must equal the ledger count at the same receiver time. Tangency requires explicit treatment rather than a sign-change-only root marker. The progression also requires identity and time agreement with wake intersections and history rows, failing visibly when those quantities are unavailable or inconsistent. These are internal display checks. An independently known simple-root or fold fixture is still needed to check the evaluator's mathematics.

The proposed first control is a source-speed ratio with threshold one marked. A common time cursor drives instantaneous panes and moving markers on history panes. Impact parameter, path type, snap-to-fold controls, and optional diagnostic overlays provide further context. A compact numerical strip carries speed ratio, derivative factor, root count, and time to the next fold; it is not a fifth competing pane.

### 5.2. Decisions that remain open

The proposal leaves three design choices unresolved: two-dimensional circles or three-dimensional wake surfaces; whether a circular-orbit case belongs in the first version; and whether to show both the acceleration weight and signed playback derivative rather than only the simpler numerical strip. The circular case refers to a separate binary-root certificate; that certificate was not read or independently validated here.

The advanced control set is a proposal within the dormant lesson, not permission to add a general speed slider, reset menu, or settings panel to the accepted learner surface. Any reopening has to reconcile it with the product copy and control boundaries. The source/transmitter naming difference and older $D_s$ versus newer $D_t$ also remain documented prerequisites. The app must use actual producer fields and show unavailable data honestly.

The source prohibits mass or primitive force language and imported shock-front, Mach-cone, Cherenkov, relativistic, or quantum mechanisms as premises. An optional visual analogy would have to be clearly secondary. The proposed lesson does not adopt a new universal speed prohibition for individual architrinos, and it does not derive an observer-level limiting speed from its drawings.

## 6. Preserving the Meaning of Validation

### 6.1. Stage 0 has a narrower population

Two retained JSON files define a Stage 0 semantic baseline and a normal-browser transition matrix. They are source-described live regression inputs, but their baseline contains only the first five lessons and four previews. The matrix's final navigation case goes from lesson five directly to Laboratory. This is a narrower historical fixture population than the accepted eight lessons followed by Roots and Laboratory.

The fixture's claim grade and named source-test/browser instrument describe the authority it was intended to carry. The file is not itself a new test result. Its source copy is preserved, including selectors, shared display-rate token and desktop/portrait settings. Neither its five-lesson count nor its final transition can be adopted as the current complete navigation contract. Conversely, the eight-lesson requirements do not prove that the current fixture consumers correctly limit or adapt those tests. That relationship requires the unread executable owners, not an inferred repair to the JSON.

A pixel match or two views driven by the same evaluator can establish deterministic display conformance. It cannot establish independent solver correctness, a retained branch, physical acceptance, or launch readiness. Historical PNGs and contact sheets remain review artifacts rather than durable requirements.

### 6.2. Accessibility and producer completeness

The deferred progression requires keyboard access to modes, steps, choices, ledger rows, root rows and transport; real semantic controls and status regions; and a text-equivalent canvas summary keyed to the selected emission and reception. Color alone cannot identify source, receiver, acceptance, rejection, or uncertainty. Reduced motion must preserve comprehension, with visible focus, usable pointer targets, optional narration and no sound requirement.

The proposed checks cover desktop and portrait, keyboard-only operation, reduced motion and high contrast. They also include unresolved self-hit cases, branch reasons, the common-row vector sum and unavailable or incomplete EOM replay. A passing source test with a broken rendered interaction remains a feature failure. No such app checks were executed in preparing this manuscript; rendering these paragraphs is a different instrument.

### 6.3. The stronger solver remains a separate obligation

The source distinguishes guided teaching replay, representative mock replay, recorded EOM playback, constrained pair-interaction boundary replay, and the stronger physical causal-delay solver. Each can establish only its declared local contract. Their combination does not close the full coupled boundary-value problem, Noether sea response, retained physical branch, or theory-wide stability burden.

The immediate implementation lane is closed. The recorded reopening triggers are an operator-directed learner change, reproducible regression, accessibility/browser defect, or deliberate recorded-provenance/display-authority change. A new accepted task must name its visible request, preserved exact copy and exclusions, claim boundary, focused source/test coverage, and required browser confirmation. The five advanced teaching rows remain deferred, and the existing Roots direct link remains a mode in one application. Explaining the deferred geometry does not reopen that work or execute its historical build instructions.
