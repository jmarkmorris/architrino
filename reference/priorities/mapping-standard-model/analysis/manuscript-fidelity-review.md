## Independent checks of the qualifications

**Complex Gram convention (§4.2; coverage Q2).** With the first inner-product slot conjugated, A_ij is the transpose-indexed entry of M-dagger times W. Expanding normalized columns gives A'_ij = sum_kl (S_w)_ki A_kl conjugate((S_m)_lj), hence the printed transpose/conjugate formula. This direct index argument is independent of the source's shorthand and of any code. Positive-definite Grams make whitening meaningful; a null-space quotient needs separate admission. Two orthonormal sets need not span the same subspace, so their overlap is not automatically unitary. The manuscript states both conditions and preserves the frozen source rather than silently claiming its shorthand is generally correct.

**Weak geometry and measure (§4).** Under an orthogonal spatial transformation R, an axial vector transforms by det(R)R. The scalar triple product of three such vectors acquires determinant factor det(R)^4=1, making it parity-even. The symmetric second moment also cannot retain individually reversed directions and does not fix a frame at eigenvalue degeneracy. Neither fact excludes every weak geometry. Separately, multiplying all right-exposure weights by a positive constant multiplies their normalizer by the same constant and leaves the normalized measure unchanged. Empty support gives zero normalization, so a zero physical channel must be declared before normalization. The manuscript correctly retains destructive/orthogonal support and unnormalized-current alternatives.

**CP, gauge and topology (§§4.3, 6).** A nonzero complex transport ratio supplies magnitude and phase, but no algebra forces cosine of the phase to equal that magnitude. The stated target needs a bounded magnitude and nonzero denominators. The diagonal color-charge sum is not a Jacobi-numerator theorem. Chern integrality and specialized amplitude/positive-geometry comparisons require their own trace, domain, coupling, orientation, bundle and boundary assumptions. The manuscript's restrictions are supported missing-condition statements, not new substrate postulates.

**Confinement (§5).** Replacing an unproved attained minimum with an infimum is a valid weakening. The interior radial Euler–Lagrange equation follows formally under the source's fixed constitutive coefficients and regular variation, but does not establish an extremizer, origin regularity, a free-radius boundary condition or positive tension. The manuscript keeps these qualifications and requires the same derived functional for joint and isolated nuclear energies. Attraction over an interval does not establish a bound state or absence of every color far field.

**Scalar and detector (§7).** A zero numerator with a zero unregularized denominator evaluates to zero after adding positive epsilon; that convention cannot itself reject a degenerate scalar candidate. Explicit nondegenerate admission is therefore necessary. Likewise, substituting the definition of reconstructed missing momentum into the same object sum produces an identity. Calibration, independent controls and physical predictions provide evidence; duplicate visible/recoil names and nuisance categories cannot create new products. These are direct algebraic checks, not a new detector or scalar calculation.

**Covariance and bottom screen (§§7.3, 8).** A quadratic statistic needs an invertible admitted covariance block or a declared treatment of redundant directions. Parent masses, their derived angle and a global fit summary are not automatically separate evidence. For real bottom couplings, orthogonal rotation preserves their squared norm and directly gives the displayed double-angle asymmetry. Keeping the leading rate fixed additionally holds other widths/radiative rows fixed; the forward–backward map also fixes the electron row. The screen is attributed to source-time inputs and local statistical assumptions, while a branch prediction must independently establish direction and norm. No numerical fit was rerun.

**Hydrogen and inverse information (§1).** A supremum of individually acceptable perturbations may include distant disconnected accepted points; it is not automatically the radius of an entirely acceptable neighborhood. Separate-coordinate tolerances do not certify arbitrary simultaneous perturbations. Smooth population weights require additional conditions for exact membership exclusion. Sensitivity to deeper variables likewise does not establish an injective inverse map. These qualifications preserve the source's proposed constructions without upgrading them.

**Holonomy exclusions (§§2–3).** Independent additive increments commute under the stated complete-memory and fixed-detector hypotheses. A connected group cannot act nontrivially through only a discrete permutation image; adding diagonal phases does not recover off-diagonal color generators. The dimensional statement concerns continuous unitary effective response, not constituent count. The accessory identity subtracts a positive leakage term and preserves a desired representation only with the stated intertwiner and compatible readouts. The source's conditional proofs and possible escapes remain visible. No physical source-to-receiver transport, Born rule, spin-statistics result or gauge recovery follows.
