## Explicit qualifications for independent review

**Q1 — Tolerances, projectors and inverse information.** Supremum of individually acceptable perturbations is not necessarily the radius of an entirely acceptable neighborhood; the domain may be disconnected or nonmonotone. Separate-coordinate tests do not prove joint control. Smooth exclusion weights need exact admission conditions before claiming a locked branch cannot re-enter the sea. Non-decoupling sensitivity does not establish injectivity. These qualifications preserve the proposed targets without asserting completed proofs.

**Q2 — Complex Gram indexing.** The source defines A_ij as inner product of mass column j with weak column i, with the first slot conjugated. If normalized columns are W S_w and M S_m, expanding each inner product gives A'_ij=sum_kl (S_w)_ki A_kl conjugate((S_m)_lj), hence A'=S_w^T A conjugate(S_m). This is a direct algebraic derivation independent of any code. The source's untransposed shorthand is only compatible under additional conventions or special real Grams; the manuscript declares its convention rather than silently copying it. Positive-definite Grams and equal represented subspaces are separate requirements; whitening distinct subspaces does not supply a unitary mixing matrix. A contrary exact index expansion would overturn this correction.

**Q3 — Weak geometry and normalization.** Three axial-vector triple products are parity-even. A symmetric second moment loses directed information and may have degenerate eigenframes. Neither gives a complete weak-chirality selector. The final source explicitly proves that uniform exposure suppression cancels after separate normalization; its earlier positive-sounding language does not override this. Zero right-support must be handled before defining a probability measure. These are conditional mathematical restrictions, not exclusion of every geometry family.

**Q4 — Detector identity and covariance.** Reusing the definition of missing momentum in its balance residual produces an identity, not an independent hidden-particle test. Shared visible/recoil objects are not duplicate energy contributions. Full covariance or a valid likelihood must handle derived angles, fit summaries, detector/theory dependencies and singular directions. Positive and negative residual components may not compensate for omitted required conditions.

**Q5 — Specialized comparison domains.** Chern integrality needs geometric, trace, normalization and boundary assumptions; BCJ/positive-geometry proposals have specific amplitude scope. The source's diagonal color-sum identity is not a Jacobi or continuous-representation derivation. A transport ratio does not force cosine of its phase to equal its magnitude. The manuscript presents these as targets rather than universal imported premises.

**Q6 — Variational scope.** Writing a minimum and an interior Euler-Lagrange equation does not prove a minimizing carrier exists, is regular, attains a positive tension or satisfies the condition from varying the corridor radius. The manuscript uses an infimum and a conditional minimizer. Component source acquisition remains separate from top-level physical acceptance. Attractive potential windows do not establish bound states or the full no-open-color condition.

**Q7 — Scalar and historical precision claims.** A positive denominator regularizer alone allows a zero numerator/denominator candidate to score zero; nondegenerate admission is explicit. Scalar vector cancellation is a screen, not a representation theorem. ATLAS 2012, PDG/LEP/SLD screening vintages and source-reported anomaly preferences are not current refreshed measurements. The bottom rotation is real-coupling, leading-order, fixed-other-row comparison; nonzero vectors and norm agreement are needed in addition to its atan2 angle. Independent primary-source/fit reanalysis is outside this editorial pass.
