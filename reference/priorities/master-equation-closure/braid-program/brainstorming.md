## Current questions to think about

- **Open, unassigned:** What nonrigid complete history could persist outside the known exclusions? Breathing, changing relative phases and moving orbit planes are possible coordinates, not evidence of a new solution. A genuinely different history must satisfy its complete delayed acceleration ledger before stability or physical interpretation can follow.
- **Open, unassigned:** Can the three-member ceiling history be connected nonlinearly to its pursuit-limit spiral? The [current account](manuscript.md#16-three-member-ceiling-contact-and-the-pursuit-limit) explains why the linear result and symmetric reduced theorem leave a gap. Kick-dependent plane orientation and the two unrun screw preparations remain possible follow-ups, not selected runs.
- **Open, unassigned:** Can later actual eight-member history reach an independently admitted separation neighborhood? The [finite certificate](manuscript.md#1541-actual-original-history-on-the-certified-interval) and [conditional comparison](manuscript.md#1544-a-reciprocal-weight-removes-free-drift-amplification-from-a-conditional-comparison) give a starting point, but no later coverage follows merely from a better formal estimate.
- **Hypothesis, unselected:** Can root events produce a fixed assembly action increment? The [action-click proposal](analysis/action-click-mechanism-hypothesis.md) remains unproved: integer root counts and an auxiliary scale do not derive an action unit. A compatible delayed action and one retained history family would be required.
- **Deferred:** Weber investigations remain dormant under the [operator decision](../brainstorming.md#weber-research-disposition--dormant-2026-10-09). Their open questions do not authorize new research. The two-architrino outspiral proposal stays with [binary brainstorming](../binary-research/brainstorming.md#integer-frequency-stabilized-outspiral-investigation).

## Unresolved Ideas

- **[guessed] Universal two-braid fermion composition.** Compare single-braid-plus-accessory and conjugate two-braid architectures on matched retention, charge, wake, and assembly ledgers; a viable retained single-braid charged-fermion family falsifies necessity; likely destination: fermion assembly architecture.
- **[guessed] Symmetry-preserving breathing family.** Select one nonredundant internal-deformation coordinate and test its complete $c_f=1$ history under the Master Equation; a coordinate equivalence to a fixed spindle chart blocks the claim; likely destination: braid configuration space.
- **[closure target] Axis-structure invariant.** Determine whether the axis Gram matrix or another invariant classifies rank and limiting cases without relabeling persistent binaries; the next object is an equivalence-and-degeneration table; likely destination: braid taxonomy.
- **[guessed] Accessory-moment response map.** Test whether the lowest surviving polarity-signed moment of a complete six-site Accessory Configuration predicts independently computed response or coupling; matched moments with incompatible responses falsify the map; likely destination: Accessory Configuration mathematics.
- **[guessed] Alternating axial polarity stratum.** Declare the full coincident-axis three-binary configuration polarity and counterpart map and derive the complete $c_f=1$ axial acceleration sum before any stability calculation; a one-signed sum across the admissible noncoincidence chart closes the stratum negatively; likely destination: the coincident-axis three-binary configuration outward-search protocol.
- **[measured target] Four-path assembly-formation diagnostic.** On refined four-path runs, track minimum-cost opposite-polarity pairing, swaps, separation bands, relative speeds, within/between separation ratio, and persistence; promotion still requires the candidate-specific fate and independent-evidence gates.
- **[inferred] Campaign threshold-sensitivity margin.** Define the state-space distance and unresolved-perturbation norm for one live campaign before using a normalized event-boundary margin as a priority heuristic; any per-run retuning falsifies comparability.
- **[inferred] Wake-pattern return diagnostic.** Test whether a quotient wake-map return residual adds information beyond same-record root, phase, energy/action, speed-order, and stability records; block promotion if it merely restates a prescribed periodic input.
- **[closure target] Finite-memory assembly test.** Measure required history depth, omitted-tail contribution, active-root identity under refinement, and bounded assembly-plus-wake energy on one retained candidate; falsifier: no stable bounded memory envelope survives refinement.
- **[guessed] Coupled frequency-work ledger.** Define a branch-level account linking translation, transverse circulation, radius change, root-status change, and internal frequency increments without importing momentum or quantization as premises; the first test requires one retained branch and a closed same-record account.
- **[guessed] Excitation and reaction mapping.** Test whether internal frequency mismatch on retained assembly branches predicts association, dissociation, radiation, or heating channels under closed event ledgers; a shared mismatch with incompatible channels falsifies the proposed predictor.

These are retained proposals and targets for discussion. None is an additional accepted work item. Existing BP tasks and their blockers are maintained only in the queue.
