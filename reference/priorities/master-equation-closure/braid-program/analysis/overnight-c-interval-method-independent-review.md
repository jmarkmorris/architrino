## Independent acceleration-row derivation

Claim grade: derived. Rotate the coordinates so a positive receiver in pair $a$ is at $(a,0)$, writing $a$ here for its radius and $b$ for the source radius. Let $\sigma\in\{-1,+1\}$ be the source polarity, and let $\delta$ be its phase relative to the receiver, including an added $\pi$ for a negative source. At delay $\tau$ the source angle is $\theta=\delta-\omega\tau$. The delayed displacement and source velocity are

$$
\mathbf z=(a-b\cos\theta,-b\sin\theta),
\qquad
\mathbf v_j=\omega b(-\sin\theta,\cos\theta).
$$

Their scalar product is $\mathbf z\cdot\mathbf v_j=-\omega ab\sin\theta$. With geometric distance $d(\tau)=|\mathbf z|$, the source factor is therefore

$$
D(\tau)=1+\frac{\omega ab\sin\theta}{d(\tau)}.
$$

On a causal root, $d(\tau)=\tau$. Since the domain is strictly subfield, $D>0$ and the absolute source denominator in the law equals $D$. The per-hit radial and tangential rows are

$$
A_r=\frac{\sigma(a-b\cos\theta)}{\tau^2D},
\qquad
A_t=-\frac{\sigma b\sin\theta}{\tau^2D}.
$$

The sign in the tangential row follows directly from the displacement's second component. Exact circular motion requires total radial acceleration $-\omega^2a$ and zero tangential acceleration, so the evaluated necessary residuals are $R_r=\omega^2a+\sum A_r$ and $R_t=\sum A_t$. These are the expressions returned by `root_row` and accumulated by `residual_box`.

For the same pair, only the opposite endpoint contributes and its relative phase is exactly $\pi$. Keeping this exact relation avoids unnecessary interval dependency; it does not exclude a physical case. For different pairs, both polarities contribute with phases $\phi_b-\phi_a$ and $\phi_b-\phi_a+\pi$. The evaluator computes three positive receivers, five directed partner rows each. Under simultaneous endpoint exchange, all positions and velocities change sign and both polarities in each product change sign; hence every corresponding acceleration reverses while circular kinematics reverses with it. The negative receiver equations are therefore equivalent to the positive ones for this exact antipodal class. The six real residuals retain all independent member balance conditions.

An exact moving control checks the signs without using a numerical root search: take $a=b=1$, $\omega=1/(2\sqrt2)$, $\delta=\pi/2+1/2$, and $\sigma=1$. Then $\tau=\sqrt2$, $\theta=\pi/2$, $D=5/4$, and $(A_r,A_t)=(2/5,-2/5)$. The frozen evaluator's known-case code uses these values. This review derives them directly; it does not treat repeated execution of the same evaluator as an independent reference.

## Complete roots and contraction

Claim grade: derived. Every source speed is $\omega b\le9/10$, throughout its entire circular history. Every present separation is positive: an antipodal pair has distance at least two, and different radii are separated by at least $1/5$. For fixed parameters the causal residual $h(\tau)=d(\tau)-\tau$ satisfies

$$
h(\tau_2)-h(\tau_1)\le-(1-\omega b)(\tau_2-\tau_1)
\quad\text{for }\tau_2>\tau_1\ge0.
$$

It starts positive for a partner and is negative for $\tau>a+b$. Thus there is exactly one positive partner root. A self residual starts at zero and is strictly negative for every positive delay. The complete census is thirty partner roots and no positive-delay self root, with $D\ge1-\omega b\ge1/10$. Omitting the zero-delay endpoint implements the selected law; omitting positive self hits is justified here by the census, not by a changed self response.

Let $d_0$ be present partner distance and $v_{\max}$ an outward upper bound on source speed over a parameter box. At a root, the source chord bound gives $d_0\le(1+v_{\max})\tau$, while circular geometry gives $\tau\le a+b$. Any lower bound $d_{\min}\le d_0$ therefore initializes a valid enclosure

$$
\tau\in\left[\frac{d_{\min}}{1+v_{\max}},\,a+b\right]
$$

with endpoints enlarged outwards. The subject obtains $d_{\min}$ from an interval evaluation of the present distance and a radius-gap lower bound, retaining the larger available lower bound. Distinct-pair radius gaps keep this positive; the same-pair antipodal phase supplies positivity even when the independent interval copies of its radius overlap. All these bounds are consequences of the complete paths, not finite-window root observations.

Away from a root, differentiating $h$ yields

$$
h'(\tau)=-1-\frac{\omega ab\sin(\delta-\omega\tau)}{d(\tau)}=-D(\tau).
$$

The denominator is geometric distance. Substituting $\tau$ there before reaching a root would be unsound; the audited code uses `distance` during contraction and substitutes `tau` only for the final on-root acceleration factor. Globally, $1-v_{\max}\le D\le1+v_{\max}$. When the computed distance enclosure is strictly positive, intersecting this global interval with the trigonometric derivative enclosure retains every actual derivative. When that distance enclosure includes zero, the code uses only the global bound.

For any actual root $\tau_*$ already enclosed and any midpoint $m$ of the current delay interval, the mean-value relation gives

$$
\tau_*=m+\frac{h(m)}{\overline D},
\qquad
1-v_{\max}\le\overline D\le1+v_{\max}.
$$

Here $\overline D$ is the average source factor along the segment between $m$ and $\tau_*$. If geometric distance vanishes at an intermediate trial delay, distance is still Lipschitz and hence absolutely continuous, so the same integral average and global bounds remain valid even without differentiability at that point. When distance stays positive, the tighter derivative enclosure contains this average as well. Parameter intervals only enlarge the sets of possible $h(m)$ and $\overline D$. The update `intersect(tau, mid + hm/D)` therefore preserves every true root for every parameter value.

The computed midpoint can itself be a narrow outward interval. Its enclosure contains the exact midpoint; interval evaluation therefore contains the same valid mean-value image. No requirement that the computed midpoint be an exact floating-point singleton is needed for this inclusion argument. Twelve contraction steps and the floating-point width comparison affect only when narrowing stops. The width comparison never decides exclusion or replaces a rigorous endpoint, so early stopping can reduce efficiency but cannot remove a true root.

The final `D` intersects the on-root formula with the same positive global bound. Correlations among radius, phase, delay, and source speed may be lost under interval operations, enlarging the result; losing them does not narrow away valid values. An empty intersection raises an exception rather than returning a successful exclusion. Any such exception must be treated as a failed evaluation requiring investigation.

## Exact partition and continuation

A path therefore reconstructs a box from the original rational domain by successive exact midpoints. Both child boxes include the shared midpoint. They overlap only on their boundary and together cover the entire parent; overlap at a boundary is harmless because strict-sign exclusion is proved on each closed box.

The base run removes one pending leaf, then either records its exclusion, retains it as unresolved, or inserts both children. Each operation preserves the union of excluded and unresolved leaves. Time, node, memory, and depth limits retain all pending leaves as unresolved. Their effects are computational stopping conditions, not mathematical exclusions.

The continuation decodes the same paths against the stored domain. Its `check_partition` rejects duplicate or ancestor leaves and requires the two matching coordinate children at every internal node. Induction from the root proves complete structural coverage. A tree with siblings from different coordinates would not suffice, and the driver explicitly rejects it. The reviewer independently reconstructed the prefix-child sets, checked these properties, decoded every pilot path with exact fractions, and checked the normalized leaf volume $\sum_p2^{-|p|}=1$. Volume alone is not the proof of coverage; the matching-child and no-ancestor conditions supply that proof.

The subject's geometric weights determine which coordinate is bisected. They do not change midpoint exactness or completeness. Likewise, inherited depth-forty leaves remain unresolved and cannot silently become exclusions. Within uninterrupted execution, the continuation preserves the cover invariant.
