# Birth exclusion for complete comparison paths

## Purpose and status

The [accepted birth application](overnight2-d-birth-nonreturn-output-independent-review.md) excludes the original source birth from every later root of the actual original balance-1, seed-1 continuation. An error comparison can also evaluate roots on paths between the exact trajectory and its reference. This note derives the additional exclusion for those paths without identifying their roots with actual roots. The [independent review](overnight2-d-birth-comparison-independent-review.md) accepts the conditional corollary and its application to the declared constant complete-path mixtures; no computational producer or jump budget is changed. The selected law, original complete past and kick, and units $K=c_f=c_a=1$ remain unchanged.

## A common endpoint enclosure

Let $T>0$, and let $Q_i(T)$ and $Q_j(0)$ be fixed centers. Consider any family of complete paths $Y_i$ that are 1-Lipschitz in time on the interval needed for the roots, with

$$
|Y_i(T)-Q_i(T)|\le a_i,\qquad |Y_j(0)-Q_j(0)|\le b_j.
$$

Here complete means that the prescribed negative history is retained as well as the nonnegative path. Define a common lower bound

$$
\gamma\le\min_{i\ne j}\left[T-|Q_i(T)-Q_j(0)|-a_i-b_j\right],\qquad \gamma>0.
$$

Every path choice in this family then satisfies

$$
G^Y_{ij}(T):=T-|Y_i(T)-Y_j(0)|\ge\gamma.
$$

At a later reception $t\ge T$, the receiver speed bound gives $G^Y_{ij}(t)\ge G^Y_{ij}(T)$. For any causal root with source time $s$, the complete source speed bound gives

$$
t-s=|Y_i(t)-Y_j(s)|\le |Y_i(t)-Y_j(0)|+|Y_j(s)-Y_j(0)|\le t-G^Y_{ij}(t)+|s|.
$$

Hence $\gamma\le G^Y_{ij}(t)\le s+|s|$. If $s\le0$, the right side is zero, contradicting the strict common margin. If $s>0$, it equals $2s$. Every root therefore satisfies $s\ge\gamma/2$. This is the same geometric argument as the actual-path theorem, now with a common enclosure over a path family. It establishes neither an ordinary-factor lower bound nor unique roots or continued existence.

## Constant convex comparison paths

Let $X_i$ be any existing exact continuation of the original release and $Q_i$ the admitted complete reference. Assume both are 1-Lipschitz on their common time interval. For constants $\theta_i\in[0,1]$, allowed to differ between members, set

$$
Y_i^{\theta_i}(u)=(1-\theta_i)Q_i(u)+\theta_i X_i(u).
$$

The constants are fixed while the time variable $u$ varies. The triangle inequality proves that each mixed path is 1-Lipschitz, including across the continuous position join at the source kick. The independently admitted endpoint and initial allowances

$$
|X_i(T)-Q_i(T)|\le a_i,\qquad |X_j(0)-Q_j(0)|\le b_j
$$

give the same inequalities for every mixed path, since its difference from its reference center is multiplied by $\theta_i$ or $\theta_j$. Consequently the same $\gamma/2$ lower bound holds simultaneously for every root of every constant convex path combination. A time-varying interpolation parameter introduces an extra derivative and is not covered merely by endpoint convexity. Arbitrary receiver translations, auxiliary velocity fields, or independently selected pointwise state boxes also require their own speed and endpoint hypotheses.

## Application to the accepted mesh endpoint

The [first-400-cell admission](overnight2-d-third-prefix-admission-independent-review.md) supplies the actual allowances at $T=14.713681572972876$. The [birth instrument and output review](overnight2-d-birth-nonreturn-output-independent-review.md) used the exact increment-defined reference centers, the admitted memberwise weighted errors divided by the exact weight, and the literal initialization allowances to establish all 56 common triangle bounds at least $\gamma=2.492573310924761$. These same allowances contain the reference endpoints themselves, so they enclose every constant convex combination described above without additional numerical evaluation.

The accepted reference domain bounds its complete speed strictly below one through time 67. The exact continuation, wherever it exists, obeys the selected unit ceiling. Therefore the corollary supplies source time at least $1.2462866554623806$ for every root of every constant convex complete comparison path at reception times from the admitted endpoint through the common existing interval ending no later than 67. Beyond 67 the present reference certificate supplies no speed claim. The actual-only nonreturn theorem retains its separate conditional all-later statement.

This corollary excludes the original source-zero jump on this declared family of complete comparison paths. It does not validate later trajectory admission, remove ordinary interpolation-knot obligations, or establish all auxiliary constructions used by an arbitrary comparison algorithm. The current admission producer and its complete jump accounting remain unchanged. A later algorithm may use this fact only after showing that its comparison paths belong to the stated family or satisfy the more general common-enclosure hypotheses.

## Controls and falsifiers

An exact geometric control fixes the receiver at zero, takes $Q_j(s)=1$ for $s\le0$ and $Q_j(s)=1+s$ for $s\ge0$, and sets $X=Q$. At $T=t=2$, every convex combination is the same path, $\gamma=1$, and the root is $s=1/2$, attaining the factor $1/2$. This is a kinematic control, not a new master-equation preparation. A nontrivial receiver mixture between fixed positions 0 and $1/4$ with the same source gives at $t=2$ the root $s=(1+\theta/4)/2\ge1/2$; its common center 0 and allowance $1/4$ give the conservative common margin $3/4$ and floor $3/8$.

A root below the common half-margin on complete 1-Lipschitz paths satisfying the displayed endpoint bounds would falsify the corollary. A violated reference speed bound, wrong endpoint or initialization allowance, time-dependent interpolation without a separate speed bound, or a missing complete source history invalidates the application rather than demonstrating failure of the geometric theorem. The numerical values are inherited only from independently accepted receipts; their identities and the complete-reference domain must be checked in the independent review before treating this application as accepted.
