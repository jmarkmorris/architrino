## Independent geometric derivation

$$
T-|Y_i(T)-Y_j(0)|\ge T-|c_i-b_j^0|-a_i-b_j\ge\gamma>0.
$$

For $t_2\ge t_1\ge T$, the receiver's 1-Lipschitz property gives $|Y_i(t_2)-Y_j(0)|-|Y_i(t_1)-Y_j(0)|\le t_2-t_1$. Thus $G^Y_{ij}(t)=t-|Y_i(t)-Y_j(0)|$ is nondecreasing and is at least $\gamma$. At any causal root, with $s\le t$, the complete source's 1-Lipschitz property implies

$$
t-s=|Y_i(t)-Y_j(s)|\le|Y_i(t)-Y_j(0)|+|s|=t-G^Y_{ij}(t)+|s|.
$$

Therefore $\gamma\le G^Y_{ij}(t)\le s+|s|$. A nonpositive source time is impossible, and a positive source time obeys $s\ge\gamma/2$. The argument concerns every root; it does not require finding one, selecting a branch, differentiating a root or showing a transmitter factor positive. A root plateau or multiple root, if otherwise possible, cannot evade the same inequality. Complete history is essential because a putative root may initially have negative source time.

$$
