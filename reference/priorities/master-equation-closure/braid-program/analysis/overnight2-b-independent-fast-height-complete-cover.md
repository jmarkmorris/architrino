## Independent general-phase derivation

Fix receiver zero and rotate to its reception radial frame. Put $\phi=\kappa\tau$, $\psi=\phi-\kappa d$, $\alpha=j\pi/3-\beta d$, and $s=(-1)^j$. Direct position subtraction gives

$$
Q_r=1-\cos\alpha,\qquad Q_t=-\sin\alpha,\qquad
Q_z=HF,\qquad F=f(\phi)-s f(\psi).
$$

The source axial velocity is $v_z=sH\kappa f'(\psi)$, where $f'(\psi)=-\sin\psi-3\cos(3\psi)/8$. Thus $\partial_dF=s\kappa f'(\psi)=v_z/H$. Differentiating the squared causal gap at fixed reception yields

$$
G=2Q_r+H^2F^2-d^2,
\qquad G_d=2[-\beta\sin\alpha+HFv_z-d].
$$

Equivalently, $G_d=2Q\cdot V_s-2d$, with the source velocity rather than the receiver velocity. At a positive-delay root, $|Q|=d$ and

$$
D=1-\frac{Q\cdot V_s}{d}=-\frac{G_d}{2d}.
$$

Every ordinary acceleration contribution is $sQ/(d^3|D|)$. The prescribed dimensionless acceleration demand is $L_r=-\beta^2$, $L_t=0$, $L_z=H\kappa^2\ell$, where

$$
\ell=f''(\phi)=-\cos\phi+\frac98\sin3\phi.
$$

Because the physical acceleration demand is $L/R$ while the canonical sum is $A/R^2$, exactness requires $RL=A$. Consequently

$$
0=\frac{A_rL_z-A_zL_r}{H}
=\sum_{\text{all roots}}\frac{s[\ell\kappa^2Q_r+\beta^2F]}{d^3|D|}.
$$

All selected heights are strictly positive, so division by $H$ is legitimate. Factoring this numerator before interval evaluation retains useful correlation without changing the law. The independent evaluator intersects each row enclosure with the equivalent expression having denominator $d^2|G_d|/2$. It retains every negative-divisor root through $|D|$, not a signed replacement.
