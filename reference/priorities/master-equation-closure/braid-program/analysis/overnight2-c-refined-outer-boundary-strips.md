# Refined excluded neighborhoods of the outer-equal boundary

## Statement and scope

**Derived conclusions pending independent reconstruction:** the completed outer-equal radius and low-speed margins give three further explicit forbidden strips for exact strictly ordered, strictly subfield logarithmic three-binary configurations. Normalize $r_1=1$, retain $K_{\log}=c_f=1$, unchanged transmitter weighting, unit persistent polarities and complete circular histories. Write $R=35$ and let $\delta>0$ be the previously checked uniform simultaneous separation floor in this exact class. Set

$$
t_0=\frac\delta4,\qquad d_0=\frac{\delta^2}{512R^2},\qquad
\varepsilon(\mu)=\min\left\{\frac\delta4,\frac{\mu d_0^3t_0^2}{212}\right\}.
$$

The following necessary conditions apply independently and may overlap:

| Original strictly ordered sector | Uniform comparison component margin $\mu$ | Necessary outer-radius gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $r_3\ge5$ | $\mu_5=1/(240R)$ | $r_3-r_2>\varepsilon(\mu_5)$ |
| $r_3\ge2$, $\omega r_3\le1/40$ | $\mu_{\ell}=11/(3792R)$ | $r_3-r_2>\varepsilon(\mu_{\ell})$ |
| $r_3\ge3$, $\omega r_3\le1/8$ | $\mu_m=2819/(236808R)$ | $r_3-r_2>\varepsilon(\mu_m)$ |

These are quantified restrictions on unequal radii, not only on an equality surface. The very small widths establish mathematical separation; they do not establish a practical covering cost, global exclusion, an exact reference or stability. The older independently checked $r_3\ge9$ strip retains its larger margin $9/(320R)$ and is not replaced by the radius-five strip.

## Endpoint residual margins

The [completed paired-value certificate and reconstruction](overnight2-c-paired-value-full-independent-review.md) establish that an outer-equal configuration $r_1=1$, $r_2=r_3=b\ge5$, with distinct positions and $\omega b\le1$, has a tangential difference strictly exceeding $1/(120b)$. Therefore at least one tangential component has absolute value strictly exceeding $1/(240b)$.

The [independent low-speed reconstruction](overnight2-c-outer-low-speed-independent-review.md) establishes a difference at least $11/(1896b)$ for $b\ge2$, $\omega b\le1/40$, and at least $2819/(118404b)$ for $b\ge3$, $\omega b\le1/8$. The corresponding maximum absolute components are at least $11/(3792b)$ and $2819/(236808b)$. The speed variable here is the original outer speed $\omega b$, not the original angular rate. These inequalities include arbitrary noncollision phases and all thirty positive partner roots; no positive self root exists in their closed-subfield domain.

All required circular accelerations are radial, so a nonzero tangential component is already a full-balance residual. Because the original exact configuration has $b=r_3<R$, each listed endpoint component margin is greater than its corresponding constant $\mu$ in the table. The radius-five argument uses its previously completed interval certificate; the low-speed arguments are analytic. Neither is rerun here.

## A single radius comparison preserves the hypotheses

Start with a putative exact strictly ordered configuration $1<r_2<r_3<R$, $\omega r_3<1$, in any table sector, and put $h=r_3-r_2$. Suppose $h\le\varepsilon(\mu)$ for that sector. Keep the outer radius, inner radius, phases and angular rate fixed, and raise only the middle radius according to

$$
r_2(q)=r_2+qh,\qquad 0\le q\le1.
$$

Every intermediate radius lies in $[1,r_3]$. In particular, the increasing middle speed never exceeds the original outer speed $\omega r_3<1$. The defining sector inequalities involve only $r_3$ and $\omega r_3$, which remain fixed. Thus the comparison endpoint belongs to the corresponding outer-equal sector, with $b=r_3$.

Each present point moves by at most $h\le\delta/4$, so every intermediate simultaneous separation remains at least $\delta-2h\ge\delta/2$. The [independently reconstructed comparison estimate](overnight2-c-boundary-strips-independent-review.md) therefore applies along the whole path: there are thirty ordinary positive partner roots, no positive self roots, $\tau\ge t_0$ and $D\ge d_0$. Equal radii at the endpoint do not mean coincident positions because this separation bound persists. The endpoint need not be exact for these geometric and response bounds to hold.

For clarity, the sensitivity estimate retains the changing emission time in both the chord and source velocity. With $p=x-y(q,-\tau)$, $n=p/\tau$, and source velocity $v_s$, differentiation gives

$$
D\dot\tau=n\cdot(\partial_qx-\partial_qy),\qquad
\dot v_s=\partial_qv_s-\partial_t v_s\dot\tau.
$$

The checked bounds are $|\dot\tau|\le2h/d_0$, $|\dot p|\le4h/d_0$, $|\dot n|\le8h/(d_0t_0)$, $|\dot v_s|\le3h/d_0$ and $|\dot D|\le11h/(d_0t_0)$. They yield at most $21h/(d_0^3t_0^2)$ per signed acceleration row. Summing all five rows at a receiver and the required circular term gives a bound $106h/(d_0^3t_0^2)$ on the change of each scalar residual. Each local radial/tangential basis stays fixed because its phase stays fixed.

The starting residuals are zero. Consequently the endpoint maximum absolute residual is at most

$$
\frac{106h}{d_0^3t_0^2}\le\frac{\mu}{2}.
$$

Its independently established tangential margin is greater than $\mu$, a contradiction. Thus $h>\varepsilon(\mu)$ in each sector, including the proposed strip-width endpoint. No comparison changes the fixed logarithmic coupling, adds a receiver response, removes a root or assumes balance along the interpolating path.

## Evidence, controls and falsifiers

This note transfers existing checked margins through an existing checked sensitivity estimate. It adds no numerical instrument, target run, phase grid or enlarged subdivision budget. At zero displacement the path is the identity and the residual-change estimate vanishes. The exact factor $212=2\cdot106$ supplies the strict contradiction. The crucial direction check is that raising the middle radius leaves the largest radius and speed unchanged; this prevents the low-speed sector from being lost along the comparison.

Independent reconstruction must check the three component margins, the original-unit speed variable, the fixed-sector interpolation, complete-root preservation and the exact width inequality. A missing positive root, a violated separation or factor bound, an invalid endpoint margin, a dropped emission-time derivative or an exact configuration inside a declared strip would falsify the corresponding result. The unresolved interior, lower-radius equality regimes, higher-speed partial-equality configurations and general superfield configurations remain outside these conclusions.
