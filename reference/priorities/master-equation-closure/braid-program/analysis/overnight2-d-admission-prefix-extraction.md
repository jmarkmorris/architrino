# Retaining complete trajectory-admission steps from a bounded run

## Purpose and status

The reviewed delayed-admission producer appends a cell record only after all eight member trials have closed simultaneously. A later failed trial or resource limit can leave a complete earlier prefix in its partial evidence without a final aggregate JSON. The prepared [admission-prefix extractor](overnight2-d-admission-prefix.py) makes that prefix available for independent adjudication while preserving the original evidence. It never accepts the mathematics merely because a row exists, and it neither reruns the evolution nor changes the chosen law or preparation.

This follows the [residual-prefix extraction contract](overnight2-d-residual-prefix-extraction.md), with admission-specific whole-cell, saved-history and physical-error checks. Both source provenance and mathematical acceptance remain explicit. No scientific admission partial has been extracted. Shared-venv synthetic controls have passed, and the [independent implementation review](overnight2-d-admission-prefix-independent-review.md) accepts the repaired consumer for conditional evidence extraction. Actual producer association and full mathematical prefix acceptance remain separate requirements.

## Complete steps and unchanged history

The producer writes one record for each complete cell, in chronological order from cell zero, including the previously accepted prefix when resuming. Its header carries original arguments, dependency identities, initial envelope, front inventory, comparison-jump bounds and resumed-cell count. A parsed record is eligible only when it contains all eight members in order, their incoming values equal the preceding saved endpoint, and every endpoint is nondecreasing and strictly below its trial and the declared velocity limit. A record emitted after only some members pass is not part of the producer contract and is rejected by the complete-member check.

The extractor reads only newline-terminated records and reports any trailing incomplete fragment without parsing it. Duplicate JSON keys and nonfinite numbers are rejected by the reviewed shared parser. Requested selection must be a nonempty extension beyond the resumed prefix and no longer than the complete available prefix. Every selected endpoint must equal its exact encoded mesh node. When a resumed prefix exists, its bound prior JSON must match all retained earlier records, initial allowances, front brackets and comparison-jump bounds exactly. A fresh run starts from its bound initialization and requires the same full independent initialization review as the original consumer.

The selected records and their mathematical values are retained without alteration. The effective `cells` and `output` arguments identify the new extracted scope; all other arguments remain unchanged. The complete original arguments are retained separately as `source_arguments`. The extracted maximum weighted error is the maximum final member endpoint; its physical position allowance divides by the exact rational recorded weight and rounds upward with a checked rational comparison. This conversion does not turn a failed later trial into an accepted cell. It only summarizes the selected complete prefix.

## Lifecycle and provenance qualifications

A matching declared run must be terminal, have a closed process group and identify this checkout and the delayed-admission script. The extractor accepts only canonical separate option/value spellings and compares the effective parsed arguments, including defaults, against the partial header. Abbreviated, equals-form, duplicate and unknown options are rejected. Complete arguments prevent a silent switch of reference, domain, weight, residual inputs, trial limit or requested extent.

A terminal lease and matching command do not prove that the run created the partial file. As for residual extraction, the output explicitly labels that association unestablished until separate launch/progress evidence is checked. The file and lease must remain unchanged across extraction, and every original dependency must match its recorded identity before and after selection. Future changes invalidate later dependency checks. The output adds the extractor, shared extraction helper and original partial file to the scientific dependency inventory. An already inherited identical path/hash binding is preserved once, so an admission using an extracted residual, or resuming an extracted admission, remains composable; a conflicting identity is rejected. Output is created exclusively and does not overwrite any earlier artifact.

The observed closed run's elapsed time remains qualified operational metadata, not an independently established prefix cost or authenticated creator record. Per-prefix wall time and peak RSS are null. Control credentials from the operational lease are not copied or exposed. A completed output still needs independent provenance association and a full mathematical audit of the initialization, residual inventory, delayed source history, geometry, jump allowances, scalar propagation and strict existence induction. In particular, checking incoming values and trial inequalities alone cannot verify the root or matrix bounds.

## Known controls and falsifiers

Before any scientific target, a synthetic three-cell stream with eight members per cell and one resumed cell passed exact prefix selection: two cells are retained, one complete later cell and an eight-byte fragment remain unselected, and original parsed record objects match exactly. Controls reject an empty extension, overlong selection, Boolean member index, wrong incoming envelope, non-strict endpoint and nonfinite trial. Exact-rational upper conversion controls cover nonbinary fractions, exact integers and a positive subnormal value. Source-argument/default controls preserve an explicit distinct weight and reject a live or nonclosed run. The shared residual-parser and canonical-option controls also pass.

An accepted extracted cell with incomplete members, a changed saved incoming value, an omitted source interval, a bad matrix/root bound, an inward scalar or physical conversion, a changed bound dependency, or unestablished producer association defeats its scientific use. Failure of a later unselected step does not logically refute an earlier completed induction, but that earlier induction must be independently checked. This prepared consumer is not a new admission theorem, an independent solution, a successful whole-run receipt, or an automatic time-67 or tail conclusion.
