## Local radial demand and exact arithmetic

In the actual orthonormal radial/tangential frame,

$$
L_r=\ddot\rho-\rho(\beta+\dot p)^2,
\qquad L_t=2\dot\rho(\beta+\dot p)+\rho\ddot p.
$$

The tangential estimate is

$$
|L_t|\le(2\beta+1+3\eta)\eta\le5\eta.
$$

For the radial estimate, $\eta\le1/2000$ gives $\rho\ge1999/2000$ and

$$
\beta+\dot p\ge\frac{73}{40}-\frac1{2000}=\frac{3649}{2000}>0.
$$

Therefore, at the reception itself,

$$
-L_r\ge\frac{1999\cdot3649^2}{8000000000}-\frac1{2000}
=\frac{26613086799}{8000000000}>3.
$$

For an explicit integer audit, $3649^2=13315201$, $1999\cdot13315201=26617086799$, and subtracting $4000000$ gives the stated numerator. Its excess over $3\cdot8000000000$ is $2613086799>0$. These are direct arithmetic identities, requiring no numerical instrument.

Define $\epsilon=|E_0(\tau_*)|$. The actual radial projection is $E_r=RL_r-A_r$, so the identity $RL_r=A_r+E_r$ gives

$$
3R<R|L_r|=|A_r+E_r|\le|A_r|+|E_r|\le M+\epsilon.
$$

In particular $R\le(M+\epsilon)/3$, as claimed. This holds for every proposed $R>0$ and its actual residual. It assumes neither $\epsilon=0$ nor a small upper bound on $\epsilon$, nor exactness at another time. A large proposed scale simply requires a large radial residual. There is no circular inference from the desired residual conclusion.

## Pointwise tangential residual

Project the same residual vector onto the actual unit tangent. The reverse triangle inequality and the already basis-corrected acceleration comparison give

$$
\epsilon\ge|RL_t-A_t|
\ge|A_t^0|-|A_t-A_t^0|-R|L_t|
\ge m-73000000\eta-5R\eta.
$$

Substituting the radial bound at this same reception, with $\eta\ge0$, yields

$$
\epsilon\ge m-\left(73000000+\frac{5M}{3}\right)\eta
-\frac53\eta\epsilon.
$$

Since $5M/3=320000/147<3000$, with exact positive numerator difference $441000-320000=121000$, the conservative coefficient $C=73003000$ is valid. Moving the nonnegative multiple of $\epsilon$ to the left and dividing by a positive number gives

$$
\epsilon\ge\frac{m-C\eta}{1+5\eta/3}.
$$

Let $Q=30000000000$. The endpoint comparison

$$
\frac1{200}-\frac C Q>
\frac1{400}\left(1+\frac5{3Q}\right)
$$

is equivalent to

$$
3Q>1200C+5,
\qquad 90000000000>87603600005.
$$

The exact positive difference is $2396399995$. For every allowed $\eta$, one can avoid any unstated sign assumption in a quotient monotonicity argument by writing

$$
m-C\eta>\frac1{200}-\frac C Q
>\frac1{400}\left(1+\frac5{3Q}\right)
\ge\frac1{400}\left(1+\frac{5\eta}{3}\right)>0.
$$

Division proves $\epsilon>1/400$. At $\eta=0$, the sharper direct conclusion is $\epsilon\ge m>1/200$. The conclusion therefore includes the closed tolerance ball and its zero-tolerance center.

Finally, differentiating $X=Rx(t/R)$ gives $\ddot X=\ddot x/R$, while the coefficient-one canonical sum scales as $A/R^2$. Thus $\ddot X-A/R^2=E/R^2$ exactly. The normalized estimate does not assert the same numerical lower bound on that physical residual.
