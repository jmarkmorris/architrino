## A new all-phase inner-equal strip

Take $r_1=r_2=1$, $b=r_3\in[13/4,4]$, $0\le v=\omega\le1/8$, $0<\beta<\pi$, and arbitrary $\chi$. The joint-strip proof supplies the strictly stronger complete-circle lower bound

$$
L=\frac{Q_v(\beta)+Q_v(\pi-\beta)}2\ge Q_v(\pi/2)>\frac{15}{8}.
$$

The independently checked [common shifted pair comparison](overnight2-c-centered-response-independent-review.md) bounds the outer pair's opposing static tangential difference by $(b^2+1)/(b^2-1)$ and its two-receiver error by $2E$, where

$$
2E(b,v)=\frac{4v(2b-1)}{(b-1)^2(1-v)}.
$$

Both radius factors decrease for $b>1$, and the error increases with $v$. At $b=13/4$ and $v=1/8$ the static cap is $185/153$ and the error cap is $352/567$. Consequently the complete inner tangential difference satisfies

$$
A_{1,t}-A_{2,t}>\frac{15}{8}-\frac{185}{153}-\frac{352}{567}
=\frac{3473}{77112}>0.
$$

At least one component has magnitude greater than $3473/154224$. This excludes every noncollision phase in the declared radius/rate strip, including its endpoints. It extends the older all-phase result through $v=1/12$ on this narrower radius band. The thirty positive partner roots and no positive self roots are complete, since all speeds here are at most $1/2$. The other receiver equations remain necessary but cannot cancel a nonzero required-zero tangential component.

The rational subtraction can be checked over denominator $77112$: $15\cdot9639-185\cdot504-352\cdot136=3473$. The radius derivatives are $-4b/(b^2-1)^2$ for the static cap and $-2b/(b-1)^3$ for $(2b-1)/(b-1)^2$. These signed formulas and the endpoint substitution are analytic controls, not numerical target output.
