## Independent derivation of the finite-prefix exclusions

The weight is exactly $\alpha=1/5$. Thus $Y_i<10^{-3}$ bounds velocity error by $10^{-3}$ and position error by $0.005$. From the accepted comparison domain,

$$
|V_i|<0.724250801106246+0.001< U=0.726,\qquad
|X_i-X_j|>3.523614453346462-0.01>d_*=3.51.
$$

The original negative path is the complete prescribed rigid path, with speed bounded by the same complete reference speed. Its reference join translation does not change velocity. The actual initial position is continuous with its own original past; the prescribed kick changes velocity only. Combining the negative and admitted positive speed bounds therefore makes the complete actual position path $U$-Lipschitz across zero. No artificial derivative agreement at zero is needed.

For fixed reception $t$, set $F_t(\tau)=\tau-|X_i(t)-X_j(t-\tau)|$. For $\tau_2>\tau_1$,

$$
F_t(\tau_2)-F_t(\tau_1)\ge(1-U)(\tau_2-\tau_1).
$$

Also $F_t(0)=-d_0<0$ and $F_t(\tau)\ge(1-U)\tau-d_0\to\infty$. Complete negative-history coverage supplies the full delay domain, so exactly one positive root exists. At it, $d_0\le(1+U)\tau$. Hence

$$
\tau>\frac{3.51}{1.726}>2.03,\qquad
0.274\le D_r,D_t\le1.726.
$$

This uses both source velocity traces at zero. At smooth points implicit differentiation gives $S'=D_r/D_t$. A stronger two-time statement avoids differentiating at knots: monotonicity of the receiver-time causal gap first gives $S(t_2)>S(t_1)$ for $t_2>t_1$. Put $\Delta t=t_2-t_1$, $\Delta s=S(t_2)-S(t_1)>0$. The two causal equalities and the reverse triangle inequality give $|\Delta t-\Delta s|\le U(\Delta t+\Delta s)$. Therefore

$$
\frac{1-U}{1+U}\Delta t\le\Delta s\le\frac{1+U}{1-U}\Delta t.
$$

The lower constant is $137/863>0.1587$. This persists through the source-zero event and ordinary representation knots. It excludes a frozen clock or a lost partner root, but does not exclude reception of the prescribed source velocity jump.

The selected equation has zero self acceleration and unit-magnitude partner weights, $c_f=K=c_a=1$, with per-root row $\sigma_{ij}n/(\tau^2|D_t|)$. There is no extra receiver factor multiplying that row. Since actual speed stays strictly interior, the normal-cone reaction is zero after the complete signed sum. Seven partner rows give

$$

<\frac7{(203/100)^2(137/500)}<6.3.
$$

This is a positive-time almost-everywhere bound and a bound for both finite one-sided acceleration traces at subsequent source-zero receptions. It does not bound the imposed instantaneous velocity change at initialization or assert that the prescribed negative history solves this positive-time evolution equation.
