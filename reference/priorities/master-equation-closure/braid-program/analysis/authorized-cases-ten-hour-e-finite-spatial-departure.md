## A local polynomial extension across source seams

A source acceleration is continuous at an exact Hermite seam, while its derivative can jump. A fourth-order Taylor remainder for the response therefore cannot cross such a seam unchanged. Subdividing around every source knot is valid but may be expensive. The proposed alternative compares the actual piecewise trial source with the analytic continuation of its center piece only on the required local source window. This extension is a residual comparison device; it is never substituted for the physical prescribed past or coupled trajectory.

Let $P$ be that local extension and $Y$ the actual trial source. Independently enclose each local root and prove both relevant speeds below $b<1$ on their common source window. If the same-time differences satisfy $|P-Y|\le\epsilon_x$, $|P'-Y'|\le\epsilon_v$, $|P''-Y''|\le\epsilon_a$, the ordinary clock gap gives $|S_P-S_Y|\le\epsilon_x/(1-b)$. Let $A_0,J_0$ bound extension acceleration and jerk, and $\ell$ bound both positive ranges below. Then the source-root tuple differences obey

$$
\Delta R\le\frac{\epsilon_x}{1-b},\quad
\Delta n\le\frac{2\epsilon_x}{\ell(1-b)},\quad
\Delta v\le\epsilon_v+\frac{A_0\epsilon_x}{1-b},\quad
\Delta a\le\epsilon_a+\frac{J_0\epsilon_x}{1-b}.
$$

Here the receiver position and velocity agree on both sides. Enclose the partial derivatives $F_R,F_n,F_v,F_a$ of the full E+M row throughout the line segment between the two complete source-root tuples, retaining a strictly positive denominator there. The additional response error is at most

$$
P_x\epsilon_x+P_v\epsilon_v+P_a\epsilon_a,
\quad
P_x=\frac{|F_R|+2\|F_n\|/\ell+\|F_v\|A_0+\|F_a\|J_0}{1-b},
\quad P_v=\|F_v\|,\quad P_a=\|F_a\|.
$$

The [separate propagation helper](../evidence/authorized-cases-ten-hour-e-propagation.py) encloses these full-row partials. The source-acceleration coefficient remains present. Summing this error over the three partner hits and adding the analytic-extension Taylor residual gives a full trial residual, provided every source piece in the common window is covered.

For two quintic pieces, the same-time differences are exact degree-five polynomial differences after translating their coordinates to one center. For an analytic circle/patch versus a quintic, degree-five Taylor comparison includes the circle's sixth-derivative remainder; its component bound is $r\omega^6 |S-S_c|^{6-d}/(6-d)!$ for derivative order $d=0,1,2$. Both analytic old pieces share the same circle, so its derivatives cancel exactly when comparing those two pieces. Interval coefficient arithmetic encloses this cancellation rather than assuming rounded evaluations are identical.

Claim grade: derived local comparison inequality, with numerical implementation submitted for independent review. Falsifiers are an extension speed failure, a root outside the common window, an omitted source piece, a false same-time polynomial difference, or a partial-derivative box failing to contain either tuple and their interpolation.
