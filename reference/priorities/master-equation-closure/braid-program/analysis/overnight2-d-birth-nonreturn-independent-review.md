|X_i(T)-X_j(0)|

$$

$$
\gamma_{ij}\le T-|Q_i(T)-Q_j(0)|-E_i/\alpha-d_j^0

## Arithmetic and fail-closed boundaries

The component conversion helper rejects nonfinite encoded inputs. Squared displacement norms are sums of exact rational squares; there is no floating cancellation in that sum. `sqrt_upper` first obtains a floating proposal and then tests its exact rational square against the exact input. It returns only after the square is at least the exact squared distance. If the first proposal is too small, upward representable successors are tested. Thus rounding of the original squared value cannot silently admit an underestimated norm.

If $U\ge|Q_i(T)-Q_j(0)|$ is the verified returned norm, the rational value $T-U-E_i/\alpha-d_j^0$ is a lower margin. `lower` compares the proposed binary64 value back to the rational and moves it downward when necessary, then verifies the direction. The same operation is applied to each half-margin. The common minimum is taken before conversion, and `positive_floor` rejects a nonpositive exact margin or a zero/nonpositive representable common margin or half-margin. This intentionally fails closed if a positive mathematical half-floor is too small to represent positively.

The routine does not promise successful evaluation for every finite encoded geometry. An exact squared distance may overflow or underflow when converted to the initial floating square-root proposal even when its square root would be representable. Sixteen upward successor tests may then be insufficient. Such a case raises rather than supplies a false bound. The intended endpoint distances require their own successful verified proposals in the later target; no estimate is licensed after an exception. Likewise, missing files, malformed dimensions or incompatible accepted inputs must stop the application, not be replaced by nominal values.

Negative position allowances are rejected in `margin`; NaN or infinity in coordinates or allowances is rejected during encoded conversion. Empty or nonpositive margin inventories are rejected. The top-level command runs ordinary-Python controls before entering target mode and rejects optimized execution. These checks protect the declared consumer contract, while prior independent admission remains an explicit external premise.
