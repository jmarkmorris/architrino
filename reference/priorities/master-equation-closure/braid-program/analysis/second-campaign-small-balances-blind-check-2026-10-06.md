This record is the report of a checker that was given only the unchanged Master Equation row in words, the definition of a rigid balance, and seven-significant-digit data for two candidate balances, B1 (three members) and B2 (four members, zero net polarity). The checker wrote its own code and did not read the claim's code, results or documents. Units are $K=c_f=1$ throughout.

## Method

**Unknowns and equations.** The mirror symmetry and the freedom of overall rotation are used to reduce the system. The mirror pair is placed at angle 0 with heights $\pm h$ on radius $R_{\rm pair}$; each in-plane member $k$ has a radius $R_k$ and an angle $\phi_k$; $w$ is the common angular rate. B1 has 5 unknowns $(R_{\rm pair},h,R_3,\phi_3,w)$ and B2 has 7 unknowns $(R_{\rm pair},h,R_3,\phi_3,R_4,\phi_4,w)$. The equations are the components of summed acceleration plus $w^2(x,y,0)$: radial, tangential and $z$ on the upper pair member (3 equations) and radial and tangential on each in-plane member (2 each), giving $3+2=5$ for B1 and $3+2+2=7$ for B2. The lower pair member's equations are the mirror image of the upper member's, and the $z$ equation of an in-plane member is satisfied identically by the mirror symmetry, so nothing is dropped. The system is square because fixing the pair at angle 0 removes the rotation freedom and no other continuous freedom exists: with $K=c_f=1$ the equation has no free length scale (accelerations scale as inverse length under a joint rescaling of length and time, the kernel as inverse length squared). Because the motion is rigid, the equations are evaluated at receiver time 0 only.

**Delays.** For each ordered pair the delay is the zero of $g(\tau)=\tau-\lvert\mathbf x_i(0)-\mathbf X_j(-\tau)\rvert$. Since $g'(\tau)=1-\hat{\mathbf r}\cdot\mathbf V_j\ge1-R_jw>0$ and $g(0)<0$, there is exactly one causal root per ordered pair (derived). Point solves use bisection followed by Newton in mpmath.

**Refinement.** Newton iteration at 60 digits with an exact Jacobian from the checker's own forward-mode dual numbers; the delay's derivative with respect to the unknowns is obtained by implicit differentiation, $\partial\tau/\partial p=-(\partial g/\partial p)/g'$. Convergence was quadratic in four steps from the rounded data for both balances.

**Certification.** Krawczyk operator $K(X)=m-CF(m)+(I-CF'(X))(X-m)$ in mpmath.iv at 260 bits, with $m$ the refined point, $C$ a point approximation of $F'(m)^{-1}$, $F(m)$ evaluated in interval arithmetic and $F'(X)$ the interval Jacobian from dual numbers with interval values over the whole box $X$. Each delay is enclosed for the whole box by an interval Newton step $N(T)=t_0-g(t_0;X)/g'(T;X)$ with the check $N(T)\subset\operatorname{int}T$, and its gradient by the implicit formula evaluated on the enclosures; source speeds are certified below 1 on the box (B1 at most 0.84612, B2 at most 0.84457 on the $10^{-6}$ box), which makes the enclosed root the only causal root. $K(X)\subset\operatorname{int}X$ then gives existence and uniqueness of a zero in $X$. Endpoint comparisons are done at 320-bit point precision so that endpoint extraction is exact. Results: B1 certified at half-widths $10^{-50}$, $10^{-30}$, $10^{-12}$, $10^{-8}$ and $10^{-6}$, not at $10^{-5}$ (contraction bound 1.75, so the test is inconclusive there, not a refutation); B2 certified up to $10^{-5}$, not at $10^{-4}$. The $10^{-50}$ box yields an enclosure of width $1.3\times10^{-75}$ whose leading 40 digits are B1: $R_{\rm pair}=0.1567279039411990805366792549870211856156$, $h=0.1967345123153912766367163141550530315252$, $R_3=0.1726690032905393147885160463506067361002$, $\phi_3=5.315985632441111569553154593948816607475$, $w=4.900208320705234514778762969369813524135$; B2: $R_{\rm pair}=1.251601579690387362638475986203078294314$, $h=1.486061310051832827566304693012118419376$, $R_3=2.742584437060583779445756952138151541325$, $\phi_3=5.591732195917058713524748702490011736319$, $R_4=1.419661978652307876488060014850853654388$, $\phi_4=3.05028583657616680525784851425977551511$, $w=0.3079420171483580578218943368070445404012$.

**Sanity checks (measured, mpmath 60 digits, unreduced system).** Speeds $R_jw$: B1 0.767999378979 (pair) and 0.846114086652; B2 0.385420715116 (pair), 0.844556983748 and 0.437173573375; all below 1. All are positive, so the absolute value in the kernel is inactive. The $z$ acceleration on each in-plane member is exactly zero in the unreduced evaluation because the two pair contributions are equal and opposite (for B1 $\pm25.58443072$, for B2 $\pm0.1556281569$ and $\pm0.05492365256$) and in-plane sources contribute none; on the mirror pair the $z$ sum vanishes only at the balance (B1: $+4.004779841$ from the partner against $-4.004779841$ from the in-plane member; B2: $+0.1038161953-0.04549328808-0.05832290721$), with residual below $10^{-59}$.

**Linear stability, derivation.** Perturbed paths are $\mathbf x_j(t)=\mathcal R(wt)\,[\mathbf x_j^0+\mathbf u_je^{\lambda t}]$ with $\lambda=\mu w$, three components per member, no symmetry reduction. With $J$ the generator of rotation about the axis, the receiver side gives $(\lambda+wJ)^2\mathbf u_i$. Writing one row of the kernel as $\mathbf f(\mathbf r,\mathbf V)=\mathbf r/(\rho^2q)$, $\rho=\lvert\mathbf r\rvert$, $q=\rho-\mathbf r\cdot\mathbf V$ (equal to $\mathbf n/(\tau^2D)$ at $\rho=\tau$), and $E=e^{-\lambda\tau}$, $\mathcal R_-=\mathcal R(-w\tau)$, the variations at receiver time 0 are $\delta\tau=\mathbf n\cdot(\mathbf u_i-E\mathcal R_-\mathbf u_j)/D$, $\delta\mathbf r=(I+\mathbf V\mathbf n^{\mathsf T}/D)(\mathbf u_i-E\mathcal R_-\mathbf u_j)$ and $\delta\mathbf V=E\mathcal R_-(\lambda+wJ)\mathbf u_j-\mathbf A\,\delta\tau$, where $\mathbf A$ is the source's unperturbed acceleration at emission; the delay variation therefore enters both the emission position and the emission velocity. With $F_r=I/(\rho^2q)-2\mathbf r\mathbf n^{\mathsf T}/(\rho^3q)-\mathbf r(\mathbf n-\mathbf V)^{\mathsf T}/(\rho^2q^2)$ and $F_v=\mathbf r\mathbf r^{\mathsf T}/(\rho^2q^2)$, the $3N\times3N$ characteristic matrix is $M(\lambda)=\operatorname{blockdiag}((\lambda+wJ)^2)-\sum_{i\ne j}s_is_j\{K_{ij}[ii]+E_{ij}(-K_{ij}\mathcal R_-+F_v\mathcal R_-(\lambda+wJ))[ij]\}$ with $K_{ij}=F_rG-F_v\mathbf A\mathbf n^{\mathsf T}/D$, $G=I+\mathbf V\mathbf n^{\mathsf T}/D$. Characteristic roots are the zeros of $\det M(\lambda)$, an entire function.

**Bound on the search region (derived, with float64 coefficient norms).** The delayed terms are at most first order in $\lambda$ while the receiver side is second order, and $\lvert E\rvert\le1$ for $\operatorname{Re}\lambda\ge0$. Writing $M=\lambda^2I+\lambda L_1+L_0-\sum E(P_0+\lambda P_1)$, any root with $\operatorname{Re}\lambda\ge0$ satisfies $\lvert\lambda\rvert^2\le c_1\lvert\lambda\rvert+c_0$ with $c_1=\lVert L_1\rVert_2+\sum\lVert P_1\rVert_2$ and $c_0=\lVert L_0\rVert_2+\sum\lVert P_0\rVert_2$. This gives $\lvert\mu\rvert\le20.43$ for B1 ($c_1=90.43$, $c_0=970.6$) and $\lvert\mu\rvert\le8.404$ for B2 ($c_1=2.088$, $c_0=1.294$). No growing root can lie outside these discs.

**Count.** Argument principle for $h(\mu)=\det[M(\mu w)/(w^2(\mu+1)^2)]$ (the normalisation has no zero or pole in the region) on the boundary of $\{\operatorname{Re}\mu>0.0005\}\cap\{\lvert\mu\rvert<1.05\times\text{bound}\}$, sampled at spacing 0.002 in $\mu$ and refined adaptively until every adjacent phase step is below 0.25 rad. Winding numbers: B1 6.00000000 (55 235 points), B2 9.00000000 (22 766 points). Repeats with twice the radius and half the step gave the same integers, and moving the line to $\operatorname{Re}\mu=0.01$ gave the same integers, so no growing root lies in $0.0005<\operatorname{Re}\mu<0.01$. A Newton root search from a grid of seeds of spacing 0.15 covering $-0.6\le\operatorname{Re}\mu$ and $\lvert\operatorname{Im}\mu\rvert$ up to the smaller of the contour radius and 12 found exactly 6 and 9 growing roots, matching the winding numbers; at each the smallest singular value of $M$ is below $10^{-16}$ of the largest.

**Neutral roots (measured count, derived origin).** Moving the line to $\operatorname{Re}\mu=-0.0005$ raises both counts by exactly 6. Small-circle winding numbers give multiplicity 2 at each of $\mu=0$, $+i$ and $-i$, and the root search places them at $\lvert\operatorname{Re}\mu\rvert<2\times10^{-12}$. They are the rigid symmetries: rotation about the axis and shift along the axis at $\mu=0$; shift in the plane and tilt of the whole balance about a horizontal axis at $\mu=\pm i$. Each of the four mode shapes is annihilated by $M$ to $10^{-16}$ relative, and $M(0)$ and $M(iw)$ each have exactly two singular values at rounding level. These six are not counted as growing. The nearest non-symmetry roots on the damped side are $\mu=-0.018955\pm1.383593\,i$ for B1 and $\mu=-0.153287\pm1.884091\,i$ for B2.

## Controls

Controls 1, 2, 4 and 6, and the rotation, axial-shift and in-plane-shift modes of control 5, ran and passed inside the same script before the target output they guard was produced. Control 3 passed on its second attempt, as described there. The tilt mode of control 5 was checked afterwards, once the multiplicity count had shown a second neutral mode at $\mu=\pm i$.

1. **Hand-checkable kernel case (derived closed form against code).** Two opposite members diametrically opposite on one circle of radius $R$ at speed $v=Rw$. By hand: with $\theta=w\tau/2$ the delay satisfies $\theta=v\cos\theta$, $\tau=2R\cos\theta$, $D=1+v\sin\theta$, radial acceleration $-1/(4R^2\cos\theta\,(1+v\sin\theta))$ and tangential acceleration $+\sin\theta/(4R^2\cos^2\theta\,(1+v\sin\theta))$, which is nonzero for every $v>0$, so this pair is not a rigid balance. The checker's general row code reproduces $\tau$, $D$ and both components to $3\times10^{-61}$ at $R=0.7$ for $v=0.5$ (tangential 0.224922111267019), $v=0.8$ (tangential 0.321356166343808) and $v=10^{-6}$. At $v=10^{-6}$ the radial value $-0.510204081632398$ approaches the static value $-1/(2R)^2=-0.510204081632653$ and the tangential value is $5.1\times10^{-7}$.
2. **Jacobian.** Dual-number Jacobian against central differences at step $10^{-20}$: largest difference $1.0\times10^{-36}$.
3. **Krawczyk negative control.** A box of half-width $10^{-8}$ displaced by $5\times10^{-8}$ in every unknown, which excludes the zero, is not certified for either balance (the routine ran without exception and returned failure). An earlier version of this control passed vacuously because an exception was swallowed; that was found and removed before any certification result was used.
4. **Winding counter on a known function.** A product with zeros at $1$, $0.3\pm2i$ inside and at $0.0001$, $0.0004+4i$, $-1$ outside the region, times $e^{-\mu}$: count 3.000000, as constructed, including two zeros lying within 0.0005 of the line.
5. **Neutral modes.** The four rigid-symmetry mode shapes are annihilated by $M$ to $10^{-16}$; a deliberately mismatched pairing (in-plane shift vector of the wrong handedness) gives an order-one value, so the test can fail.
6. **Linearisation against the nonlinear delayed equation.** For random complex $\mathbf u$ and $\mu=0.3+0.7i$, $-0.2+1.9i$, $1.1$, the residual of the full delayed equation on the perturbed real paths, with delays re-solved on those paths, was differenced centrally in the amplitude and compared with $\operatorname{Re}(M\mathbf u)$: largest relative difference $2.3\times10^{-7}$ (B1) and $6.6\times10^{-8}$ (B2), the expected second-order size for the amplitude used. This tests the delay-variation terms directly.

## Limits

- **Independence.** The checker is the same model family as the author of the claim. Agreement is therefore not independent evidence in the strong sense: a shared misreading of the equation, of what a rigid balance is, or of how to linearise a delayed equation would pass unnoticed. What is independent: the code (written from the task statement, nothing reused), the choice of unknowns and equations, the dual-number Jacobian, the interval certification, the stability derivation and the counting procedure, and the controls against a hand closed form and against the nonlinear equation. What is not independent: the statement of the equation and the candidate data, both supplied by the claimant, and any habit of reasoning common to the model family. The checker was not told any expected growing-root count; whether its counts agree with the claim is for the claimant's record to state.
- **Grade of the existence result.** Computer-assisted derived rests on mpmath 1.3.0 interval arithmetic (including its interval sine, cosine and square root) being correctly outward-rounded, and on about 300 lines of unreviewed checker code. It is not a formally verified proof. Uniqueness is local to the stated boxes; nothing here excludes other balances elsewhere.
- **Grade of the stability result.** Measured, in float64, by argument principle and root search. The contour integrals are not interval-validated, and the region bound uses float64 matrix norms, although with wide margin. The count is of roots of the linearisation; no nonlinear evolution was run.
- **Scope.** Only the stated row with one causal root per ordered pair and no self-row was tested, at the stated symmetry. No statement is made about any other equation variant, about the neighbourhood of the balances in parameter space beyond the certified boxes, or about physical interpretation.
- **Reading of the threshold.** Roots are classed as growing when $\operatorname{Re}\mu>0.0005$. The six symmetry roots sit on the imaginary axis to $2\times10^{-12}$ and no other root lies in $-0.0005\le\operatorname{Re}\mu\le0.01$ for either balance, so the counts do not depend on the exact threshold within that band.
