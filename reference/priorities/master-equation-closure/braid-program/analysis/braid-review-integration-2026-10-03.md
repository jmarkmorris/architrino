## Conjugation and fixed-history symmetry

The member map uses sector-signed axial displacement and phase. Exchanging sectors while preserving their paths therefore sends $h_+\mapsto-h_-$, $\rho_+\mapsto\rho_-$ and $\theta_+\mapsto-\theta_-$. With $p_h=p_\theta=-1$ and $p_\rho=1$, the corrected even/odd variables are

$$
E_z=\frac{z_++p_z z_-}{2},\qquad
O_z=\frac{z_+-p_z z_-}{2}.
$$

They obey $(E_z,O_z)\mapsto(E_z,-O_z)$. This repair preserves the intended parity tests while changing which raw mean or difference belongs to each test. It is a derived coordinate correction, not a charge derivation. An explicit full member-map transformation disagreeing with it is its falsifier.

The direct spatial actions in the existing chart table fix a generic history; reflected phase actions generally relate another history. Orbit counting for pockets attached to one host must use its four-element stabilizer. Special histories or polarity-decorated additions can have different stabilizers; neither four nor eight supplies a capture theorem.
