## Independent rate derivation

Let $d=X_i(t)-X_j(t)$, $r=|d|>0$ and $u=d/r$. Fix a causal reception direction $n_i$ and its range $R_i$, and define the mean source velocity $m_i=R_i^{-1}\int_{t-R_i}^{t}V_j(q)\,dq$. Absolute continuity, complete source interval coverage and the speed bound give

$$
d=R_i(n_i-m_i),\qquad |m_i|\le1,\qquad
\eta_i=1-n_i\cdot m_i.
$$

Thus $n_i\cdot d=R_i\eta_i$. Also

$$
r^2=R_i^2(1-2n_i\cdot m_i+|m_i|^2)
\le2R_i^2\eta_i.
$$

When $\eta_i\ge\underline\eta_i>0$, division gives
$u\cdot n_i=R_i\eta_i/r\ge\sqrt{\eta_i/2}\ge\sqrt{\underline\eta_i/2}$, while $R_i\eta_i=n_i\cdot d\le r$ gives $R_i\le r/\underline\eta_i$. The reverse channel applies the same calculation to $-d$, so $u\cdot n_j\le-\sqrt{\underline\eta_j/2}$. These directions and signs are essential.

Writing $e_i=V_i+n_i$, $e_j=V_j+n_j$ and using the assumed directional bounds yields, almost everywhere on the positive-separation interval,

$$
\dot r
=u\cdot(V_i-V_j)
\le-\sqrt{\underline\eta_i/2}-\sqrt{\underline\eta_j/2}
+k_iR_i+k_jR_j
\le-\sigma+\lambda r.
$$
