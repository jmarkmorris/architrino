## Exact substitution, including all self rows

$$

Multiplying the chord equation by $\gamma^2$ gives exactly the stationary reference equation at $(R_0,\Omega_0)$, with the same source label and phase. At a mapped root, the separation direction and source velocity are

$$
\mathbf n=(\gamma\mathbf n_0,u),\qquad

Consequently

$$
D=1-\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf V_j
=\gamma^2(1-\mathbf n_0\cdot\mathbf V_{j,0})=\gamma^2D_0,

The per-hit baseline acceleration is therefore

$$
\frac{K\sigma_m\mathbf n}{\Delta^2|D|}
