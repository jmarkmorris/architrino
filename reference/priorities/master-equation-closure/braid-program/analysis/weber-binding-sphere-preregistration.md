## 1. The selected law

Six members $i=0,\dots,5$ with polarities $q_i\in\{+1,-1\}$, three of each sign. With $\sigma_{ij}=q_iq_j$, present separation $d_{ij}=\|\mathbf X_i-\mathbf X_j\|>0$ and $\mathbf e_{ij}=(\mathbf X_i-\mathbf X_j)/d_{ij}$,

$$
\mathbf A_i=\sum_{j\ne i}\frac{\sigma_{ij}K}{d_{ij}^{2}}\left[1-\frac{\dot d_{ij}^{2}}{2c_f^{2}}+\frac{d_{ij}\ddot d_{ij}}{c_f^{2}}\right]\mathbf e_{ij},
$$

with $\lambda_{\mathrm W}=-1/2$, $\mu_{\mathrm W}=1$, $K=1$, $c_f=1$, unit integration weights (architrinos have no mass), instantaneous distinct-partner support, no self term, no boundary response, no softening, and no speed ceiling enforced. Dots are absolute-time derivatives of present separations; $\ddot d_{ij}=\mathbf e_{ij}\cdot(\mathbf A_i-\mathbf A_j)+\|\mathbf w_{ij,\perp}\|^2/d_{ij}$ contains every member's unknown acceleration, so each evaluation solves the full $18\times18$ linear system $M\mathbf A=\mathbf b$ assembled pair by pair. Isolated-pair denominators $1-2\sigma K\mu_{\mathrm W}/(c_f^2 d)$ are not applied to six-body hits. A zero or non-finite pivot, $|\det M|<10^{-8}$, or a separation below $10^{-6}$ ends the regular case; no continuation is selected past such an event. Speed labels are recorded as unrestricted, strict ($v<c_f$) and inclusive ($v\le c_f$) coverage without enforcing a ceiling; a speed label never modifies an acceleration.

Section 9a, the Darwin comparison and the delayed canonical Master Equation are different laws and supply no premise. The orthogonal-plane exclusions recorded in the braid-program brainstorming under the delayed law are not transferred.

## 4. Binary frequency conventions

Cyclic frequency $f=1/P$, angular frequency $\Omega=2\pi f$, member orbital radius $\rho$, pair separation $d=2\rho$, individual centre-rest speed $v=\Omega\rho$. The reference angular frequency is $\Omega_0=c_f^3/K=1$, so $f_0=c_f^3/(2\pi K)=1/(2\pi)$, and the integer ladder is $\Omega_n=n\Omega_0$, $f_n=nf_0$ for $n=1,\dots,8$. Both the binary lane and the reference lane tabulate $\rho$, $d$, $v$, $\omega_r$ (radial perturbation frequency), $\Phi_{\mathrm{lin}}$ (linear apsidal angle), $\delta\varpi$ (precession per radial period), $\det M$ and the speed label at

$$
f\in\{0.01,\ 0.05,\ 0.1,\ 0.2,\ 1/(2\pi),\ 0.5,\ 2/\pi,\ 1,\ 2,\ 10\}\quad\text{and}\quad \Omega_n=n,\ n=1,\dots,8 .
$$

The expected equality-speed point is $\Omega=4c_f^3/K$, $f=2/\pi$; both lanes report it independently.

## 5. Candidate families, parameter boxes and controls

Equal velocity means equal constant speed magnitudes $\|\mathbf V_i\|=v$ for every member and time with independent directions. The centre $\mathbf C$ is fixed at the origin by preparing $\sum_i\mathbf X_i=0$ and $\sum_i\mathbf V_i=0$ (the sum of velocities is an invariant of the law because pair contributions are antisymmetric). A sphere candidate satisfies, for every member and time, $\|\mathbf X_i\|=R$, $\mathbf X_i\cdot\mathbf V_i=0$, $\mathbf X_i\cdot\mathbf A_i=-v^2$, $\mathbf V_i\cdot\mathbf A_i=0$, with the solved $\mathbf A_i$ equal to the path's actual acceleration in all three Cartesian components.

**Rigid reduction (to be stated and proved by the geometry lane, checked by the reference lane).** On any rigid rotation every $d_{ij}$ is constant, so $\dot d_{ij}=\ddot d_{ij}=0$ and the velocity terms cancel exactly in the solve; the balance condition is the instantaneous inverse-square one, $-\Omega^2\mathbf X_{i,\perp}=\sum_{j\ne i}\sigma_{ij}K\mathbf e_{ij}/d_{ij}^2$, with $\mathbf X_{i,\perp}$ the component perpendicular to the axis. Equal speed on a rigid rotation forces equal axial distance, so rigid sphere candidates lie on one circle or on two circles $z=\pm z_0$ of equal radius $a$, $R^2=a^2+z_0^2$.

## 7. Stability and fate protocol

Only a state that satisfies the full equation (residual $\le10^{-12}$) is linearized. Rigid relative equilibria use the rotating-frame Jacobian of the full 36-state system with the balance check refused above $10^{-10}$; periodic nonrigid candidates use the 36-state monodromy matrix over one primitive period. Symmetry and family directions are identified before any instability claim. Measured fate: unconstrained evolution of independently prepared perturbed states at two tolerances ($\mathrm{rtol}=10^{-12}$ and $10^{-10}$, GBS) and one RK4 fixed-step comparison, recording radius spread, minimum pair separation, $\det M$, speed labels, recurrence and first event. Finite survival is reported as the measured interval only.

## 8. Claim grades and falsifiers

Every claim is graded derived, measured, inferred or open where it is made. A measured claim names the script, the settings and the exact cases. A verified local fact does not license a global claim: a failed family is a negative for its box. The exact falsifier for a bounded negative is a state in the stated box whose independently evaluated residual is below the candidate threshold; the falsifier for an exact balance is an independent evaluation of the full $18\times18$ solve on that state with residual above tolerance.

### 11.3 Item 2: great-circle closure
