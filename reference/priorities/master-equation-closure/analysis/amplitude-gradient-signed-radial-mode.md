## 3. Explicit autonomous coefficient construction and the improved remainder

$$

## 5. Exact comparison potential and signed cycle correction

\tag{24}
$$

The subtracted linear part centers this scalar at zero even though the sixth-order moving center is not the static minimum of $P$. It is a mathematical radial norm, not physical energy. At zero parameter,

$$

\tag{25}
$$

Derivatives of the reference graph multiply differences of angular rate by at least $\delta^2$ or $\delta^3$, and enter (27) only at fifth or higher order. $$

All terms in $D$ are quadratic at the center. Its leading cycle sign can be computed without a small-amplitude approximation. At zero parameter the central comparison admits

$$

+e_c^3(3\cos^3\theta-\cos\theta).

Thus

$$

\qquad \oint\rho^{-3}d\tau=2\pi.
\tag{29}
$$

The ratio is exactly three for every fixed central energy below $1/2$. It is derived from this comparison equation, not imported as a physical eccentricity law.

Let brackets denote the time average over the frozen fast orbit. Define

\tag{30}
$$

The estimate is uniform on the compact cutoff, including $J\downarrow0$ by its smooth center limit. To construct the cycle correction explicitly, set $Q=\operatorname{sgn}(z)\sqrt{2v(z,\delta)}$.
