## C reconstruction prose and derivative sum

The [final rigidity audit](../binary-research/analysis/authorized-cases-ten-hour-c-rigidity-final-audit.md), SHA-256 `4374a43b730a93f2633c03307c768901b178fbd0baabf303e4a28db403018860`, records two transcription corrections to its author's audit notes. The supported conclusion is that positions converge in $C^2$, velocities in $C^1$, and accelerations in $C^0$, on compact positive scaled intervals. No second derivative of acceleration is required or established.

The earlier [compactness reconstruction](../binary-research/analysis/authorized-cases-ten-hour-c-compactness-audit-reconstruction.md), SHA-256 `2f3f299e1b1894649718ad0785fcd26b78d932dbd5fbf1ecc9983476b382ce8a`, omits separating plus signs between three terms in its jerk bound. Differentiating $Q''=-n/(LD)$, with $|n'|\le N_1$, $|L'|\le L_1$, $|D'|\le D_1$, $L\ge B$ and $D\ge d>0$, gives the intended sum

$$
|Q'''|\le\frac{N_1}{Bd}+\frac{L_1}{B^2d}+\frac{D_1}{Bd^2}.
$$

This finite bound supplies equicontinuity of acceleration. It requires delayed source acceleration already controlled by the preceding source generation, not a bound on delayed source jerk. The frozen mathematical subjects are unchanged; these two review-note corrections do not alter their scoped acceptance.

## Terminology and verification boundary

Geometric quantities written as a cross product of position and velocity in the frozen derivations are geometric angular-motion quantities. Occurrences of the conventional mathematical phrase “angular momentum” in those notes supply neither primitive mass nor a physical conservation law. The maintained synthesis and manuscripts use the geometric interpretation.

These clarifications are falsified by a mismatch to the identified source bytes, a different equation in the cited independent derivation, an accepted conclusion that still requires the withdrawn B transverse consumer or stronger C acceleration convergence, or a change in the reader's section-selection contract. They repair the reading and routing of retained evidence; the three original automated presentation findings, two C review-note transcription corrections and four reader-specific fragment findings remain recorded and must not be reported as a clean all-document pass.
