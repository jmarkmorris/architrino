# Binary research

## Scope

Investigate isolated electrino–positrino pairs under their complete delayed histories: circular and other noncollinear preparations, partner and self-root inventories, local evolution, perturbations, and eventual fate. A pair is two architrinos; their past histories are part of the dynamical datum. A prescribed path, a balance calculation, a finite prefix and a persistent evolved binary remain different claims.
