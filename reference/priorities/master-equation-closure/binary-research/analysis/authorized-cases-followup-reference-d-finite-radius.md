## Negative account

On the temporary region with member speed $\beta=1/100$, let $\mu=K/H\le2\epsilon<0.0007$. The exact relative decomposition and its transverse error give

$$
\frac{d|Q_\perp|}{kH}\le\frac{3\beta^2|U_\perp|}{4g_0^5}
+\frac{|N\cdot U|}{2g_0^3}<0.011,
\qquad
\frac{d|e_{\rm rel}|}{kH}\le6\mu<0.0042,
$$

where $k=K/d^2$ and $g_0=\sqrt{1-\beta^2}$. These bounds include the normal as well as planar component. The actual affine anisotropic tangential and normal coefficients each have absolute value at most

$$
\frac{KM^2}{dgH}\le\frac{k}{2g}<0.501k.
$$

Consequently

$$
H'>0.48kH,\qquad |H'/H|<1.52k,\qquad |\kappa|<0.52k,
$$

where $\kappa$ is the actual moving-plane rate in $T'=-HN/d^2+\kappa L$. These are full spatial estimates, with the actual delay error retained unfactored.

For the accepted corrected center $Y$ and its components $(A,B,C)$ in $(N,T,L)$, use $\mathcal S=|Y|^2+2\mu AB$. Its exact derivative is

$$
\mathcal S'=-2kC^2+2\mu\kappa AC-2\mu AB\frac{H'}H
+2[Y+\mu(BN+AT)]\cdot\mathcal R,
\qquad |\mathcal R|\le66k\chi M.
$$

Since $|Y|\le|W|\le M$, the preceding bounds and $2|AC|,2|AB|\le|Y|^2$ imply the conservative multiplicative estimate

$$
\mathcal S'\le(3\mu k+140k\chi)M^2.
$$

No additive $O(\epsilon^2)$ error floor is introduced. For $\chi\le1/100$, correction/coercivity gives $0.98|W|^2<\mathcal S<1.001|W|^2$. Since $\mu'=-\mu H'/H$, the positive angular derivative also gives

$$
\int\mu k\,dt\le\frac{\mu(T)}{0.48},\qquad
\int k\chi\,dt\le1.4\epsilon^2
$$

while the account is nonpositive. Running-supremum Gronwall therefore yields

$$
M(t)^2\le\frac{1.001}{0.98}m_0^2
\exp\left[\frac3{0.98}\frac{2\epsilon}{0.48}
+\frac{140}{0.98}1.4\epsilon^2\right]

$$

The exponent is below $0.005$ at $\epsilon\le1/2900$; $e^x\le(1-x)^{-1}$ verifies the last conservative comparison. The accepted scalar separation barrier and $\mathcal J\le0$ relative-speed bound then exclude the original speed and separation exits exactly as before, now with a smaller center allowance. The angular floor cannot be the first exit because $H'>0$.

## Positive account and finite radius

After the first account crossing, use the accepted $\beta=1/20$ continuation estimates. Here the actual relative error bound is $10K^2/d^3$, not the negative-stage constant six. Together with the transverse affine bound and $dM^2\le H_*/2$, it still gives $H'/H>0.4k$. $$
\int k\,dt\le2\pi\sqrt{\epsilon/18}.
$$

Thus the full running midpoint supremum satisfies

$$
M(t)\le1.1m_0\exp\left(5.2\,2\pi\sqrt{\epsilon/18}\right)<1.5m_0.
$$

This is valid on partial intervals before a possible region exit, not only after assuming an infinite tail. The exponent is below $0.144$ at the admitted endpoint, and $1.1e^{0.144}<1.5$. Member-speed and distance margins close by the accepted positive-account argument.

For $m_0>0$, set $D_*=H_*/(8m_0^2)$. Before first attainment of any prescribed radius $D\le D_*$ above the entry radius, the bound $M<1.5m_0$ gives $dM^2<9H_*/32<H_*/2$. Thus that extra temporary boundary cannot occur first. The other finite boundaries have already been excluded. If radius never reaches $D$, then $d$ remains bounded forever; this contradicts the negative-account finite integral of $k\chi$, or, after crossing, the finite integral of $k$. Hence first attainment is finite. This is a finite-distance theorem across both account signs, not an all-future angular-floor or terminal-fate theorem. For $m_0=0$ exact center symmetry must be handled separately rather than dividing by zero.
