## 1. Signed geometric quantity

Let $x_a(t)$ and $x_c(t)$ denote actual and comparison planar paths with strictly positive radii $r_a,r_c$ on one connected interval. Fix continuous angle lifts $\theta_a,\theta_c$. Put $\delta r=r_a-r_c$, $\delta u_t=u_{a,t}-u_{c,t}$ and $\phi=\theta_a-\theta_c$. Direct polar differentiation gives the exact identity

$$
\phi'=\frac{u_{a,t}}{r_a}-\frac{u_{c,t}}{r_c}
=\frac{N}{r_a r_c},\qquad
N=r_c\delta u_t-u_{c,t}\delta r.
\tag{A1}
$$

Thus the source-to-receiver error is $\phi(T)-\phi(S)=\int_S^T N/(r_ar_c)\,dt$. An equivalent numerator uses the geometric areal rates $h_a=r_au_{a,t}$ and $h_c=r_cu_{c,t}$:

-\frac{h_c(r_a+r_c)\delta r}{r_a^2r_c^2}.
\tag{A2}
$$
