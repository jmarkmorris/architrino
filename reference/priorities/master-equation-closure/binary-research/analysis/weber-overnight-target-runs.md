## 1. What was run

**The law.** For two members with present separation $r=\|\mathbf X_1-\mathbf X_2\|>0$, unit direction $\mathbf e=(\mathbf X_1-\mathbf X_2)/r$, polarity sign $\sigma=\operatorname{sign}(q_1q_2)$ and coupling $K$, the acceleration of member 1 caused by member 2 is

$$
\mathbf A_{1\leftarrow 2}=\frac{\sigma K}{r^2}\Big[1+\lambda_{\mathrm W}\frac{\dot r^2}{c_f^2}+\mu_{\mathrm W}\frac{r\ddot r}{c_f^2}\Big]\mathbf e,\qquad \mathbf A_{2\leftarrow 1}=-\mathbf A_{1\leftarrow 2},
$$

with the dots denoting absolute-time derivatives of the present separation. Support is instantaneous (no causal delay), there is no self term, and every member receives the full acceleration (architrinos carry no mass, so the integration weights are unity). The frozen coefficients are $\lambda_{\mathrm W}=-1/2$, $\mu_{\mathrm W}=1$, $c_f=1$, $K=1$; lengths are therefore in units of $K/c_f^2$ and the dimensionless separation is $x=r$. Because $\ddot r$ contains the unknown accelerations, the instrument assembles and solves the $6\times6$ linear acceleration system at every evaluation. The zero-coefficient control $\lambda_{\mathrm W}=\mu_{\mathrm W}=0$ is the instantaneous inverse-square comparison, not the delayed canonical Master Equation. Opposite polarity ($\sigma=-1$) is the attraction target; like polarity ($\sigma=+1$) is a control with its own singular radius $r_c=2K/c_f^2=2$, where the pair determinant $1-2\sigma K\mu_{\mathrm W}/(c_f^2 r)$ vanishes.

**Preparation conventions.** Every case starts with the centre of velocity at rest in the void frame unless the case says otherwise; member 1 (instrument index 0) sits at $+\boldsymbol\rho/2$ with velocity $+\mathbf w/2$ and member 2 at $-\boldsymbol\rho/2$ with $-\mathbf w/2$, where $\boldsymbol\rho$ is the relative position and $\mathbf w$ the relative velocity. A circle at separation $r$ uses the subject's circular relation $h^2=2Kr$, that is relative speed $\|\mathbf w\|=\sqrt{2K/r}$ and individual speed half of that. An apocentre release at $r$ with fraction $f$ of circular uses $\mathbf w$ tangential with $\|\mathbf w\|=f\sqrt{2K/r}$ and $\dot r=0$. The three like-polarity approaches start at the stated separation with the stated $\dot r<0$ (and, for WB-10, the stated tangential relative speed).

**Settings.** Gragg–Bulirsch–Stoer extrapolation with `rtol` $10^{-12}$ and `atol` $10^{-14}$ (the preregistered run), repeated at `rtol` $10^{-10}$ (the refinement run); `hmax` at most one fiftieth of the shortest expected period ($T/50$ for the circles, $0.5$ or less for the eccentric cases, $0.05$ for the approach, radial and collinear cases); exact condition number on. Events: contact at $r<10^{-6}$, escape at $r>10^{3}$, obstruction at $|\det M|<10^{-8}$, smallest pivot below $10^{-10}$ or condition number above $10^{10}$ (all three stop the run), every individual speed crossing of $c_f$ recorded, turning points of $r$ recorded with a floor of $10^{-9}$ on $|\dot r|$. Case files are under `.local-data/master-equation-closure/weber-overnight/binary/cases/` and `collinear/cases/`, trajectories and summaries beside them. The instrument source used has SHA-256 `3d16f3494c55fefa41b3d225e8e591501241f725a79965469f1dfb9d28109d18`.

**Controls first.** Before any target run the eleven known-case controls were rerun with `node … controls --receipt .tmp/weber-overnight/round2/targets/controls-rerun-receipt.json` at 12:50:56Z: 11/11 PASS, wall 0.43 s, Node v22.23.2. The committed receipt was not rewritten. This pass was recorded in the machine summary before the first target case was built.

**Probe runs.** Three measured quantities need a state that an event record does not carry. For these the same case was rerun under a separately named probe file: (i) pericentre speed, by integrating to the located first-pericentre time and reading the state there (WB-4, WB-5, WB-11, WB-12); (ii) the separation at WB-8's speed-equality crossing, by integrating to the located crossing time; (iii) the time at which WC-2 reaches $x=8$, by setting the escape radius to $8$ as a measuring stop. A probe changes no coefficient, preparation or tolerance.

## 2. Results by case

In the tables, "drift" of the candidate first integral $H$ means the largest relative departure from its initial value over the run; $H$ is the historical Weber-type expression $\sum_i\tfrac12\|\mathbf V_i\|^2+\sigma K r^{-1}(1-\dot r^2/2c_f^2)$ for the frozen coefficients and $\sum_i\tfrac12\|\mathbf V_i\|^2+\sigma K/r$ for the control; it is a mathematical invariant of an adapted law, not a physical energy account. The angular-momentum drift is the largest change of $\sum_i\mathbf X_i\times\mathbf V_i$ relative to its initial magnitude (for a centre at rest this sum is half the relative angular momentum $\mathbf h$). The integration-error estimate is the difference between the `rtol` $10^{-12}$ and `rtol` $10^{-10}$ runs for the quantity named.

## 4. Surprises and instrument limits met

1. **Contact stepped over at the refinement tolerance (WB-6, WC-1).** At `rtol` $10^{-10}$ with the preregistered `hmax`, one accepted step carried the state from $r=4.6\times10^{-5}$ on one side to $r=3.3\times10^{-6}$ on the other side of $r=0$; the event function $r-10^{-6}$ was positive at both ends and the contact event was not raised. The members passed through each other, the candidate $H$ then drifted by order unity, and the pair separated to $r\approx100$ with speeds above $c_f$ by $t=50$. The continuation beyond the pass-through is an artifact of integrating across the point $r=0$ where the law is undefined, not a history of the law, and nothing from it is used. The pass-through time, located as a sign change of $\dot r$ with $r\sim10^{-15}$, agrees with the extrapolated zero-separation time of the `rtol` $10^{-12}$ run to $4\times10^{-13}$ (WB-6) and $6\times10^{-13}$ (WC-1); that is the integration-error estimate reported for these cases. A rerun at `rtol` $10^{-10}$ with `hmax` reduced to $10^{-3}$ stepped over the window again (summaries `WB-6-refine-hmax0.001`, `WC-1-refine-hmax0.001`). The cause is the documented event-location assumption (one sign change per step), made likely because the frozen law reaches contact at finite speed and finite acceleration, so nothing forces the steps to shrink near $r=0$; the control WC-3, whose speed diverges, was caught at both tolerances. Falsifier of this reading: a `rtol` $10^{-10}$ step sequence for WB-6 whose accepted step ends bracket $r=10^{-6}$ yet no event is raised.
2. **WB-8 ends by step underflow, not by the determinant event.** The speed divergence outruns the step controller before $\det M$ reaches $10^{-8}$; the run stops at $r=2+1.3\times10^{-7}$ with a finite but unresolved state. The preregistration's "reaches $x=2$" prediction is met in time to $10^{-10}$, but the instrument does not certify the state at the stop and establishes no continuation.
3. **Round-off speed crossings on the WB-3 boundary circle.** Sixteen recorded crossings with $|\|\mathbf V_i\|-1|\le3.3\times10^{-16}$; harmless, but a consumer of the event list must not count them as dynamical crossings.
4. **Candidate drift near contact.** $H$ drifts by $10^{-6}$ in the last steps before the $r=10^{-6}$ stop in WB-6, WC-1 and WC-3, because the $1/r$ terms amplify a position error of order $10^{-12}$; the stop times themselves agree between tolerances to $10^{-12}$. The $10^{-9}$ drift tolerance is met on every regular run (all circles, WB-4, WB-5, WB-7, WB-9, WB-10, WB-11, WB-12, WC-2) with margin of at least three orders of magnitude.
5. **WB-7 centre velocity.** The kick on one member adds half of itself to the centre of velocity; the measured centre velocity is $(0.0525,0,0.005)$, which the speed-coverage entry uses.

## 5. Verdicts, grade and falsifiers

> Claim grade: measured by the subject instrument on exactly the preregistered cases, double-precision GBS with event location, two tolerances. Every predicted quantity in the preregistration table agrees inside its preregistered tolerance; the largest relative difference among them is $1.7\times10^{-10}$ (WB-4 radial period against the 10-digit printed value). Nothing disagreed, so no tolerance was widened and no case was altered. The binary decision inputs that this worker can speak to are met on the measured side: WB-1, WB-2, WB-4, WB-5 and WB-7 remained regular and bounded with invariant drift inside tolerance, and no opposite-polarity bound case reached contact, escape or an obstruction. Whether the class theorem survives adjudication is the PI's separate question.

What this does not establish: the instrument encloses nothing, so agreement on the control cases does not certify its error on the targets beyond the two-tolerance estimate; the subject reduction and this instrument were authored in the same lane, so their agreement is consistency rather than independent confirmation; finite bounded motion over five to twenty periods is a measured interval result, not persistent binding; WB-10 is consistent with, but does not prove, the like-polarity theorem; the withheld cases carry no verdict until the PI compares them with the blind reference.

Falsifiers, all operator-checkable from the files named above: a rerun of any case file under the same instrument source hash producing a termination, event time, turning radius or period that differs from the recorded summary by more than its integration-error estimate; a `controls` rerun that prints a `FAIL` line; a value in the machine summary that differs from the corresponding number in this account; or an independent reference value for a predicted case that differs from the measured value here by more than the preregistered tolerance, which would be a finding against one side rather than a reason to adjust either.
