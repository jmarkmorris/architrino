## 4. What this bound costs in eccentricity and phase

For $H>0$ set $L=\boldsymbol H/H$, $T=L\times N$, and define the geometric eccentricity vector

$$
E=\frac{U\times\boldsymbol H}{2K}-N,\qquad h=\frac{H}{4\epsilon},\qquad c=\frac Eh.
\tag{15}
$$

These agree with the accepted mirror variables in the fixed normalization. They are definitions on the actual nonmirror state, not assumptions of a central physical law. Write the exact relative acceleration as $-2kN+f$. Direct differentiation, with every component expressed in the actual moving frame, gives

$$
E'=\frac{Hf_T}{K}N-\frac{Hf_N+duf_T}{2K}T-\frac{duf_L}{2K}L,\qquad H'=df_T.
\tag{16}
$$

For an additive relative-row perturbation $r$ at that same state, its exact contribution to $(E/H)'$ is

$$
\mathcal L(r)=\frac{r_T}{K}N-
\left(\frac{r_N}{2K}+\frac{du r_T}{2KH}\right)T
-\frac{du r_L}{2KH}L-\frac{Ed r_T}{H^2}.
\tag{17}
$$

The normal component is retained. It is absent only for the nominal planar member. In particular,

$$
|\mathcal L(r)|\le |r|\left\{\frac1K+\frac{d|u|}{2KH}+\frac{|E|d}{H^2}\right\}.
\tag{18}
$$

Multiplying (18) by $4\epsilon$ and applying (14) gives the explicit extra budget in $c'$:

$$
160\epsilon M_t^2
\left\{\frac1{d^2}+\frac{|u|}{2dH}+\frac{K|E|}{dH^2}\right\}.
\tag{19}
$$

This is only the direct functional perturbation. A full comparison also has to control the centered relative history and the source-window remainders used in the mirror normal form. Granting those further obligations would not remove the last term in (19). The negative account supplies $\int k\chi\,dt=\int K^2d^{-3}\,dt$, whereas the last term requires a bound on $\int M_t^2|E|/(dH^2)\,dt$. Neither the scalar account nor the existing midpoint supremum implies that bound.

The [accepted mirror theorem](slow-binary-wider-regime.md) permits a zero-speed tail with finite positive limiting $H$. On its [conditional parabolic scale](authorized-cases-ten-hour-d-primary-parabolic-tail.md), $d\asymp t^{2/3}$ and $E\to-N_\infty$. A nondecaying midpoint bound in (19) then gives a divergent inverse-separation majorant. This is a failure of a sufficient estimate, not proof that the actual perturbation integral diverges.

The exact leading signed term shows what cancellation would be needed. In the planar affine decomposition the extra row is (4), with $b=b_TT$. Its contribution to $(E/H)'$ is

$$
\frac{2ab_T}{gd^2}N-
\left\{\frac{1-g}{d^2}+\frac{uab_T}{gdH}\right\}T
-\frac{2Kab_T}{gdH^2}E.
\tag{20}
$$

The last term has no relative-transverse-speed factor. If $ab_T$ has a nonzero limiting sign on a proposed bounded-$H$ parabolic tail, it is not absolutely integrable; the corresponding torque $2Kab_T/(dg)$ is already incompatible with that bounded-$H$ assumption. Alignment can eliminate this leading term, but alignment of the actual nominal midpoint with the actual terminal direction has not been proved. In angle variables $dt=d^2d\theta/H$, its coefficient becomes proportional to $d\,ab_T/H^3$. A bounded primitive for $ab_T$ alone therefore does not give the mirror's bounded normal-form correction: differentiating the coefficient retains the growing $d$ and the unknown midpoint and angular variation.

Thus a fixed small history norm does not uniformly preserve the mirror angular/eccentricity normal form at arbitrary radius. The required next input is a signed actual correlation or a new adapted coordinate estimate controlling (20), not an assertion of continuous dependence. The three-dimensional term in (17) and the moving-plane terms of the accepted spatial account remain additional obligations for the neighborhood.

## 7. Disposition, falsifiers and provenance

The bounded subject closes a local complete-history comparison obligation, not the actual branch-selection problem. Equations (14), (17)–(20), (26), and (32) are the new derived claims. No actual nominal outward witness has been proved. The exact remaining blocker is preparation-specific control of the signed midpoint/relative phase correlation at an attained state or over the later source windows, with sufficient resolution to decide (3), or an alternative actual return-exclusion argument that does not assume the mirror angular asymptotics. The accepted mirror seed enclosure alone is quantitatively too broad even if transferred without further loss.

Falsifiers are: a missed root or negative sampled time in the generated-window argument; an omitted source term in (10)–(11); a violation of the explicit constants in (8), (12) or (13); an incorrect moving-frame contribution in (17); either algebraic test state falling outside the asserted seed/account inequalities; or failure of the planar two-sided account estimate with its stated finite hypotheses. An independently proved actual signed cancellation making the last integral in (19) finite would remove that sufficient-bound obstruction. A preparation-specific phase enclosure excluding the turn state could remove the finite-state resolution obstruction. Neither possibility is ruled out by this subject.
