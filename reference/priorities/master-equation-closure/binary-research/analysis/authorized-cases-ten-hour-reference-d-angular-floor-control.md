# Independent affine release control for angular-floor invariance

Claim grade: exact analytical control of one release row. This reference is frozen before the proposed control subject. It is not the nominal past, an all-future affine solution or a nondispersal counterexample.

Prescribe complete affine histories with $Z(s)=dN+a^2sT$, $C(s)=Ws$, $W=a(N-T)$ and $d=H_*/a^2$, where $N,T$ are fixed orthonormal planar vectors and $a>0$ is sufficiently small. Their constant member velocities are $W\pm a^2T/2$. They are strictly subfield for small $a$, so the complete roots are the unique ordinary affine partner roots and there are no positive-delay self roots. At release, $H=d a^2=H_*$ and radial relative velocity is zero.

Put

$$
b_+=-a+a^2/2,\qquad b_-=-a-a^2/2,\qquad
g_\pm=\sqrt{1-b_\pm^2}.
$$

The exact affine pair row, including both distinct clocks, has tangential relative component

$$
(A_+-A_-)\cdot T=\frac K{d^2}
\left[a^2+a\left(\frac{b_+}{g_+}+\frac{b_-}{g_-}\right)\right].
$$

Hence its exact release angular derivative is

$$
H'(0)=\frac{Ka^2}{d}
\left[1-\frac{1-a/2}{\sqrt{1-a^2(1-a/2)^2}}
-\frac{1+a/2}{\sqrt{1-a^2(1+a/2)^2}}\right]
<-\frac{Ka^2}{d}<0
$$

for sufficiently small positive $a$. The strict inequality follows because each denominator is below one and the two positive numerators sum to two.

The account is

$$
\mathcal J(0)=\frac{a^4}{2}
-\frac{2K\sqrt{1-a^2}\,a^2}{H_*}<0
$$

whenever $a^2<4K\sqrt{1-a^2}/H_*$. The complete speed and $K/d$ can simultaneously be made arbitrarily small. Thus the instantaneous small-speed, nonpositive-account data alone do not point inward at the proposed angular boundary. The exact initial derivative points toward smaller angular magnitude.

This prescribed past is not generated before release and does not satisfy the later theorem's condition $t>3d(t)$ at time zero. Consequently the control disproves only automatic boundary invariance inferred from those instantaneous quantities. It does not falsify an additional generated-history theorem, establish that the exact nominal member reaches this boundary or demonstrate failure of dispersal. No numerical target or new authorized physical case is introduced.
