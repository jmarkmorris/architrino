# Blind physical companion reference for the Cartesian transverse method

**Derived before reading the companion subject.** The [Cartesian blind reference](authorized-cases-ten-hour-reference-a-cartesian-frame.md) fixes the exact coordinate identity and common frame. On a proposed receiving cell of width $h$, let constant nonnegative $b_x,b_w,b_f$ bound $\|EB_q\|,\|E\|,\|E\|f_q$. For the transformed norm use an independently certified logarithmic norm $\mu$ and forcing bound $f\ge0$.

The scalar comparison $Y'=\mu Y+f$, $Y(0)=W_0$, has endpoint $Y_h=e^{\mu h}W_0+f\phi(\mu,h)$, with $\phi=(e^{\mu h}-1)/\mu$ and $\phi(0,h)=h$. Because this constant-coefficient scalar solution is monotone or constant, $W_{\rm whole}\le\max(W_0,Y_h)$. For negative mu, one may not use the endpoint alone as a whole-cell bound. A deliberately larger nonnegative mu is admissible but loses contraction. Any exponential instrument needs outward mathematical enclosures and exact known controls, including mu zero and a negative case.

Physical velocity satisfies $V\le b_xX+b_wW+b_f$. Integrating the original identity $\xi'=\delta u$, and using $hb_x<1$, gives the sufficient whole-cell bounds

$$
X_*\le\min\left\{\frac{W_*}{\nu},\frac{X_0+h(b_wW_*+b_f)}{1-hb_x}\right\},
\qquad V_*\le b_xX_*+b_wW_*+b_f.
$$

The norm cap and integration cap each bound the same physical X; they may be minimized. Endpoint X can separately be bounded by $\min(W_h/\nu,X_0+hV_*)$. A second independent bound on velocity, if supplied by original acceleration integration, may likewise be minimized, but cannot create a circular proof unless all proposed whole-cell trials have strict closure. The transformed initial W is not a physical velocity error; substituting it for $V_0$ or old source velocity is invalid.

For future source use, retain the complete whole-cell Cartesian X/V/A bounds on closed bins, not endpoint-only values. The original E acceleration reconstruction must include the actual delayed source-acceleration inventory, full source displacement/root shifts, current velocity where present, and original comparison residual. Exact cancellation in H does not remove this obligation. Trial closure includes both receiving subfield speed and positive range, D and w throughout every independent source/position/velocity family. Same-history initial data and all inherited complete roots remain fixed. A metric change at a face costs $\max(1,\nu_{new}/\nu_{old})$ on W.

Known controls for the companion include $b_x=b_f=0,b_w=1,\mu=f=0$, where $W_*=W_0$ and $X_*\le\min(W_0/\nu,X_0+hW_0)$; and $b_x=1,h=1/2,b_wW_*+b_f=1,X_0=0$, whose integration bound is 1 before any norm cap. Reject $hb_x\ge1$, negative norms and nonpositive nu. Test whole versus endpoint with a strictly decaying scalar W. Every numerical trial remains subject to independent coefficient, scalar recurrence, strict trial and original-E reconstruction audits. This note introduces no physical target or computation.
