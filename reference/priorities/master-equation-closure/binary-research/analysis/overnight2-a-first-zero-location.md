## The cancellation at account zero

The exact zero-account identity is

$$

$$

$$

\qquad

\tag{3}
$$

This cancellation matters because an unrestricted $O(\alpha)$ error would become an order-one phase uncertainty after division by $\epsilon$. The first broad bounds in (3) give $|Ih^2-1|<.000689$. Equation (1) then implies

$$

\qquad |R|<1.55\times10^{-12},\qquad
\big||e|^2-1\big|<9.3\times10^{-7}.
\tag{4}
$$

Put $H=I^{-1/2}$, which also lies in $(1498,1501)$. Factoring (3) gives

$$
\frac{h-H}{\epsilon}+P
=P\left(1-\frac{2H^2}{h(h+H)}\right)
+\frac{RH^2}{\epsilon(h+H)}.
\tag{5}
$$

First $|h-H|<.00035$ follows from (3)–(4). In (5), use

$$
\left|1-\frac{2H^2}{h(h+H)}\right|
=\frac{|h-H|(h+2H)}{h(h+H)}.
$$

The two terms on the right of (5) are respectively below $3.6\times10^{-7}$ and $3.6\times10^{-6}$. Thus

$$
\left|\frac{h-H}{\epsilon}+P\right|<10^{-5}.
\tag{6}
$$
