## Exact angular balance with an explicit remainder

Let $Z=dN$, $U=Z'$, $W=(V_++V_-)/2$, $\boldsymbol H=Z\times U$, $H=|\boldsymbol H|>0$, $L=\boldsymbol H/H$, $T=L\times N$, and $W=aN+bT+cL$. Set $g=\sqrt{1-b^2-c^2}$ and $k=K/d^2$. Retain both actual partner clocks. The exact relative acceleration defines the *signed* remainder

$$
\boldsymbol r_H:=Z\times(A_+-A_-)-k\boldsymbol H-
\frac{2Ka}{dg}N\times W.
\tag{1}
$$

This definition is an identity, not an assumption that the current-affine approximation is the equation. The known actual-delay/relative-velocity estimates must independently bound it over the generated source windows. Taking the $L$ projection gives

$$
H'=kH+\frac{2Kab}{dg}+r_\parallel,
\qquad r_\parallel=L\cdot\boldsymbol r_H.
\tag{2}
$$

Writing $I(t)=\int_{t_0}^t k(s)\,ds$, the exact integrating-factor identity is

$$
e^{-I(t)}H(t)=H(t_0)+
\int_{t_0}^t e^{-I(s)}\left[\frac{2Ka(s)b(s)}{d(s)g(s)}+r_\parallel(s)\right]ds.
\tag{3}
$$

Consequently the maintained floor $H>H_*$, $H_*=2\epsilon R_0$, is equivalent, on an already regular chart, to the right side of (3) exceeding $H_*e^{-I(t)}$ at every time. A sufficient accumulated negative-torque criterion is

$$
\int_{t_0}^t e^{-I(s)}
\left[\frac{2K[-ab]_+}{dg}+|r_\parallel|\right]ds
<H(t_0)-H_* \quad\hbox{for every }t.
\tag{4}
$$

A stronger local barrier test at $H=H_*$ is

$$
\frac{2d[-ab]_+}{g}+\frac{d^2}{K}|r_\parallel|<H_*.
\tag{5}
$$

Continuity and the strict inward-pointing derivative then exclude the first floor crossing while the remaining domain stays valid. Equality-only versions require a separate viability argument; a sampled positive derivative is insufficient.

These formulas show the missing information precisely. A uniformly small midpoint speed gives $2[-ab]_+\le|W|^2$, but multiplies it by the unbounded separation $d$ in (5). A finite account budget for $\int K^2/d^3$ does not by itself bound the different signed integral in (4). Treating these weights as interchangeable would be an unsupported global implication. The old prescribed-affine release example remains only a local control; it says nothing decisive about (4) on generated nominal histories.

## The spatial frame term cannot be discarded

For the corrected midpoint $Y$ of the accepted reduction, write $Y=A N+B T+C L$ and $Y'=k(2NN^{\mathsf T}-I)Y+\mathcal R$. The actual frame has $N'=(H/d^2)T$ and $T'=-(H/d^2)N+\kappa L$, where $\kappa=L\cdot(A_+-A_-)/|U-(N\cdot U)N|$. Direct differentiation of

$$
\mathcal S=|Y|^2+\frac{2K}{H}AB
\tag{6}
$$

gives the exact identity

$$
\mathcal S'=-2kC^2+\frac{2K}{H}\kappa AC
-\frac{2K}{H^2}ABH'
+2\left[Y+\frac KH(BN+AT)\right]\cdot\mathcal R.
\tag{7}
$$

It is coercive with lower factor $1-K/H$ only while $H>K$. At the leading current-affine level, substituting the actual angular anisotropy yields the known positive term $4K^2A^2C^2/(dgH^2)$, in addition to the planar negative square; replacement of physical $W$ by $Y$ belongs in the retained remainder. Relative to $-2kC^2$, the dangerous coefficient is

$$
\Xi=\frac{2KdA^2}{gH^2}.
\tag{8}
$$

Thus a strict joint region $H>H_*$ and $\Xi\le1-\eta$, $\eta>0$, provides a usable normal-damping margin before the remaining errors. It does not follow from small $|W|$ and small $K/d$ separately. Proving this region invariant would require the actual derivative of $H^2-(2/(1-\eta))KdA^2/g$ at its boundary, retaining $d',A',g'$, frame motion and both source clocks. Assuming a fixed orbital plane would remove precisely the term under investigation.

On a hypothetical zero-relative-speed tail with $d\to\infty$, bounded $H$ and nonzero limiting radial midpoint component, (8) grows without bound. That is an obstruction to this damping estimate, not an actual counterexample or proof that the nominal trajectory has such a tail. An eventual angular growth law or directional midpoint alignment could repair it, but must be proved from the actual delayed equation.

## Reference controls and admission boundary

In a planar class $C=0$, the explicit spatial term vanishes, recovering the accepted planar identity. Rotating $N,T,L$ by one fixed orthogonal matrix must preserve (1)–(8). Two separately moving clocks, the recent essential-acceleration seam treatment and the generated-window restriction cannot be dropped to make these controls pass.
