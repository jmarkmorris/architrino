## Actual domain and criterion

Use the notation of the [exact scalar-account decomposition](authorized-cases-ten-hour-d-primary-anisotropic-account.md): $Z=dN$, $U=Z'$, $u=N\cdot U$, $v=U-uN$, $W=(V_++V_-)/2$, $g=\sqrt{1-|W-(W\cdot N)N|^2}$, $\chi=K/d$, $k=K/d^2$. This is ordinary three-dimensional geometry; no angular floor is imposed.

At an actual time $T$, assume a complete supplied strict speed bound $b_0<1$, generated member speeds below $\beta=1/100$, the existing locally Lipschitz velocity regularity, $T>3d_0$ with $d_0=d(T)$, $0<\chi_0=K/d_0\le1/100$, and $u_0>0$. Define

$$
F(u)=u-2\log(1+u/2),\qquad
P=\chi-F(u)+\frac32\chi^2,\qquad
c_\beta=\frac{(1+\beta)^2}{1-\beta}.
$$

Let $v_0$ bound both current member speeds. The sufficient entry inequalities are

$$
P(T)\le0,\qquad v_0+c_\beta\sqrt{\chi_0}<\beta.
\tag{1}
$$

**Conditional theorem.** Such an actual entry gives an all-future ordinary continuation with increasing separation, absolutely integrable member accelerations and convergent velocities. Its radial growth satisfies

$$
d(t)\ge\left[d_0^{3/2}+3\sqrt K\,(t-T)\right]^{2/3}.
\tag{2}
$$

If $P(T)=-\eta<0$, then additionally $u(t)\ge2\sqrt\eta$, so the relative terminal velocity is nonzero and separation has a positive linear lower rate. Equality in (1) allows a zero relative terminal velocity. Neither version requires a sign assumption on the auxiliary account $J=|U|^2/2-(2g+u)\chi$.

## Signed radial comparison

The accepted current-affine/actual decomposition gives the exact radial equation

$$
u'=\frac{|v|^2}{d}-k(2g+u)+Q_r+e_r,
\qquad Q_r\ge0,\qquad |e_r|\le6k\chi.
\tag{3}
$$

The error bound is the original actual-delay bound on the generated $\beta=.01$, $\chi\le.01$ region; it retains source acceleration and both independent clock shifts. No prescribed affine path is substituted for the coupled history. The [nested source-window argument](authorized-cases-followup-d-bootstrap-source-time-bound.md) remains valid since $(t-3d)'\ge.94$. It excludes the remote supplied past from the small-speed comparison windows without changing its own strict bound. Complete strict speed preserves one partner root per receiver and excludes positive-delay self roots.

Since $F'(u)=u/(2+u)$ and $\chi'=-ku$, differentiation of $P$ yields, while $u>0$,

$$
P'=-\frac{2ku(1-g)}{2+u}
-\frac{u}{2+u}\left(\frac{|v|^2}{d}+Q_r+e_r\right)
-3ku\chi\le0.
\tag{4}
$$

Indeed the only possibly positive term is bounded by $6ku\chi/(2+u)\le3ku\chi$. The remaining terms are nonpositive because $g\le1$ and $Q_r\ge0$. This is a signed inequality derived within the canonical row, not a new physical energy or response.

For $u\ge0$,

$$
0\le F(u)=\int_0^u\frac{s}{2+s}\,ds\le\frac{u^2}{4}.
\tag{5}
$$

Thus $P\le P(T)\le0$ implies $u^2\ge4\chi>0$. A finite first loss of $u>0$ is impossible because $P=\chi+(3/2)\chi^2>0$ at $u=0$, while finite speed keeps $d$ finite on a finite interval. Hence $d$ increases and $\chi$ decreases, preserving its ceiling. Integrating $d'=u\ge2\sqrt{K/d}$ proves (2).

The exact root/range bounds give $|A_i|\le c_\beta K/d^2$. On every partial outward continuation,

$$
\int_T^t |A_i|\,ds
\le c_\beta\int_{d_0}^{d(t)}\frac{K\,dr}{r^2\,2\sqrt{K/r}}
\le c_\beta\sqrt{\chi_0}.
\tag{6}
$$

The strict margin in (1) closes the provisional speed bound. Positive separation, complete root margins and regular generated histories allow ordinary continuation through every finite endpoint. Equation (6) gives absolute acceleration integrability and velocity limits. If $P(T)=-\eta<0$, then $F(u)\ge\chi+(3/2)\chi^2+\eta\ge\eta$, and (5) proves the stated positive lower radial speed.

## A nonempty negative-account algebraic control

This control checks the criterion's distinction from the older unsigned radial test; it does not prescribe or admit a new physical history. Set $W=0$, $v=0$, $\chi=x^2$ and $u=2x+(5/6)x^2$, with $0<x\le10^{-4}$. Direct substitution gives

$$
J=-\frac13x^3-\frac{35}{72}x^4<0.
\tag{7}
$$

The elementary inequality $s/(2+s)\ge s/2-s^2/4$ gives $F(u)\ge u^2/4-u^3/12$. Consequently

$$
F(u)-x^2-\frac32x^4
\ge x^3\left(\frac16-\frac{311}{144}x-\frac{25}{72}x^2-\frac{125}{2592}x^3\right)>0.
\tag{8}
$$

The final comparison follows by bounding the three negative coefficients by three, one and one respectively and substituting $x\le10^{-4}$. Here $v_0=u/2<.000101$ and $c_\beta\sqrt\chi<.000104$, so the impulse margin is strict. Thus the finite-state inequalities admit negative $J$ while the older unsigned radial criterion cannot do so. Compatibility and actual attainment remain separate history obligations.
