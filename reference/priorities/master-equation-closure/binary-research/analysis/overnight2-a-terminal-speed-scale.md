**Status: derived subject, conditional on the first-zero-location candidate and awaiting independent review.** The [first-zero subject](overnight2-a-first-zero-location.md) proposes an outward first auxiliary account zero with $2\times10^6<d_e<4\times10^7$. This note uses that actual entry to control the entire later transverse fraction and the accumulated account increase. The proposed consequence is a two-sided terminal relative-speed estimate, rather than merely a positive floor. The equation, coefficients, complete preparations and original spatial history neighborhood remain unchanged, as specified in the [main A report](overnight2-a-followup-and-research-2026-10-07.md#current-canonical-case-and-evidence-boundary). All numerical values use $c_f=1$.

## Entry and the inherited all-future solution

Use $Z=dN$, $U=Z'=uN+v$ with $v\cdot N=0$, midpoint velocity $W$, $g=\sqrt{1-|W-(N\cdot W)N|^2}$, $k=K/d^2$, $\chi=K/d$ and

$$
\mathcal J=|U|^2/2-(2g+u)\chi,
\qquad \Lambda=|v|^2/\chi.
$$

The accepted [outward theorem](authorized-cases-outgoing-d-reference-assessment.md) already gives complete ordinary continuation, $\mathcal J\ge0$, increasing unbounded $d$, whole accessible generated speeds below $1/20$, $T>3d$, individual terminal velocities and $\delta=|U_\infty|>0$. All source-window and actual-delay estimates below inherit that theorem. We are sharpening this proved future, not launching an independent evolution solver.

At the proposed first zero, $\mathcal J_e=0$, $2\times10^{-7}<u_e<5\times10^{-7}$ and $|W_e|<2.64\times10^{-20}$. The first-zero phase proof gives $1.5<\psi_e<2.5$ and $\big||e_e|-1\big|<10^{-6}$. The alternating upper cosine polynomial at $1.5$ gives $\cos(1.5)\le11/128<.09$. Consequently its phase coordinate satisfies $Q_e=1+|e_e|\cos\psi_e<1.1$. Since $\Lambda_e=2Q_e$, the exact zero-account identity yields

$$
\Lambda_e<2.2,
\qquad
\frac{u_e^2}{\chi_e}=4g_e+2u_e-\Lambda_e>1.79>\frac{16}{9}.
\tag{1}
$$

The exact coupling gives $4\times10^{-7}<K<4.5\times10^{-7}$, hence

$$
10^{-14}<\chi_e<2.25\times10^{-13}.
\tag{2}
$$

## Radial speed and smaller current member speeds

The inherited virial $\mathscr V=du$ obeys $\mathscr V'>(9/5)K/d$. Its monotone-radius identity gives

$$
d^2u^2>d_e^2u_e^2+\frac{18K}{5}(d-d_e).
$$

Dividing by $Kd$ and using (1) shows, for every later time,

$$
u>\frac43\sqrt\chi,
\qquad
\int_T^\infty k(t)\,dt<\frac32\sqrt{\chi(T)}.
\tag{3}
$$

No angular bound is needed for this step. At entry the zero account gives $|U_e|/2=\sqrt{(g_e+u_e/2)\chi_e}$, so $|V_i(T_e)|<1.01\sqrt{\chi_e}$. The full-history $1/20$ bound gives the actual acceleration estimate $|A_i|\le(441/380)k$. Integrating (3) then proves

$$
|V_i(T)|<2.76\sqrt{\chi_e}<b_*:=2\times10^{-6}
\quad(T\ge T_e).
\tag{4}
$$

The earlier accessible history is still bounded by $1/20$, not relabeled by (4). The smaller number bounds current generated states after entry and their current velocity interpolation segments. It may therefore sharpen the exact current-affine differential estimates while the previously established actual-delay errors retain their original constants.

## The transverse fraction stays below three

Use the exact affine decomposition and estimates in the [anisotropic-account source](authorized-cases-ten-hour-d-primary-anisotropic-account.md), with its $Q$ denoted $Q^{\rm aff}$ here to distinguish it from the earlier phase coordinate. The [positive-account source](authorized-cases-ten-hour-d-primary-positive-account-continuation.md) and its accepted descendants supply $|e_{\rm rel}|\le10k\chi$, $|e_{\rm ctr}|\le5k\chi$ on the full generated domain. Set $a=N\cdot W$, $b=W-aN$, and $g_*=\sqrt{1-b_*^2}$. The current velocity segment lies in the $b_*$ ball by (4), so

$$
|Q_t^{\rm aff}|\le k\left[\frac{3b_*^2|v|^2}{4g_*^5}+\frac{|u||v|}{2g_*^3}\right].
$$

For the physical angular vector $\mathbf H=Z\times U$, the exact relative row gives

$$
\mathbf H'=k\mathbf H+\frac{2kd,a}{g}N\times b
+dN\times Q^{\rm aff}+dN\times e_{\rm rel}.
\tag{5}
$$

This retains the normal and midpoint contributions. At a putative first boundary $\Lambda=3$, $H=d\sqrt{3\chi}>0$. Applying (5), (3) and $g_*^{-j}<1.1$ for $j\le5$ gives

$$
\frac{2|\mathbf H'|/H}{u/d}
\le\frac32\sqrt\chi+2b_*^2+(2.2b_*^2+10.1)\chi
<10^{-5}.
\tag{6}
$$

The five contributions in (6) come respectively from $k\mathbf H$, the midpoint term, the quadratic transverse term, the mixed $|u||v|$ term and the actual-delay error. Before combining, the last three bounds are $2.2b_*^2\chi$, $1.1\chi$ and $9\chi$. Their sum is displayed. Every comparison uses $\chi\le\chi_e<2.25\times10^{-13}$.

Since $\Lambda=H^2/(Kd)$, equation (6) implies $\Lambda'<0$ at that boundary. Its initial value is below $2.2$, so the first-exit argument proves

$$
\Lambda(T)<3\qquad(T\ge T_e).
\tag{7}
$$

No division by $H$ is made away from the positive boundary. Possible zero angular magnitude therefore does not invalidate the barrier argument, and no limiting orbital plane is assumed.

## The full account derivative has a uniform small relative error

The exact account identity independently reconstructed in the [quantitative reference](overnight2-a-reference-quantitative-terminal-speed.md) is

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal J'={}&k\chi\left(2g+u-\frac{2|b|^2}{g}\right)
+U\cdot Q^{\rm aff}-\chi Q_r^{\rm aff}
+\frac{2\chi}{g}b\cdot\Delta\\
&+(U-\chi N)\cdot e_{\rm rel}
+\frac{2\chi}{g}b\cdot e_{\rm ctr}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{8}
$$

Current-affine bounds use (4) and (7), while actual errors use the unchanged whole-history constants. The absolute deviation of (8), divided by $k\chi$, from two is at most

$$
2b_*+5b_*^2+4.95b_*+1.1b_*^2+4.4b_*^3
+20b_*+10\chi+11b_*\chi<.001.
$$

In particular the deliberately looser bounds

$$
1.99k\chi<\mathcal J'<2.01k\chi
\tag{9}
$$

hold throughout the actual future. Midpoint motion is not frozen in taking this derivative. The $\Delta$ and actual midpoint error in (8) are both included.

## Integrating the account yields a terminal speed scale

Because individual velocities converge, $d\to\infty$ and $\chi\to0$, the account has limit $\mathcal J_\infty=\delta^2/2$. Its entry value is zero. Equations (3) and (9) give

$$
0<\mathcal J(T)\le\mathcal J_\infty
<2.01\frac34 K^{3/2}\int_{d_e}^\infty r^{-5/2}\,dr
=1.005\chi_e^{3/2}.
\tag{10}
$$

Thus $\delta<1.5\chi_e^{3/4}$. On the first interval $d_e\le d\le2d_e$, the exact account identity and (10) imply

$$
\frac{u^2}{\chi}\le\frac{|U|^2}{\chi}
=\frac{2\mathcal J}{\chi}+4g+2u
<4.02\sqrt{\chi_e}+4+4b_*<(2.001)^2.
$$

Using this upper speed in the lower integral from (9) gives

$$
\mathcal J_\infty>
\frac{1.99}{2.001}\frac23(1-2^{-3/2})\chi_e^{3/2}
>.405\chi_e^{3/2}.
$$

For the last strict comparison, $1.99/2.001>.99$ and $1-2^{-3/2}>.64$, whose product with $2/3$ is $.4224>.405$. Therefore

$$
\boxed{.9\left(\frac K{d_e}\right)^{3/4}
<|U_\infty|<1.5\left(\frac K{d_e}\right)^{3/4}.}
\tag{11}
$$

Combining (2) with (11) yields the simpler uniform proposal

$$
\boxed{2\times10^{-11}<|U_\infty|<5\times10^{-10}.}
\tag{12}
$$

The upper numerical comparison follows by raising positive quantities to the fourth power: $1.5^4(2.25\times10^{-13})^3<(5\times10^{-10})^4$. The lower comparison uses $\sqrt{10}>3$. With dimensions restored, (11) reads $.9[K/(c_f^2d_e)]^{3/4}<|U_\infty|/c_f<1.5[K/(c_f^2d_e)]^{3/4}$.

## The small midpoint velocity survives the outgoing tail

The [exact nonlinear midpoint estimate](authorized-cases-ten-hour-d-primary-nonlinear-center.md), used in the accepted [inward assessment](authorized-cases-two-hour-d-reference-inward-assessment.md) and [preparation-specific midpoint assessment](authorized-cases-gain-d-reference-subject-assessment.md), retains every interpolated source clock and its sampled acceleration. On the generated window it gives $|W'|\le5.2kM(T)$, where $M(T)$ is the supremum of midpoint speed over the whole accessible prefix through $T$. The original remote history is not assigned this smaller bound; its exclusion follows from the inherited root and source-window proof.

At entry that whole-prefix supremum is below $m_e=2.64\times10^{-20}$. For the later interval, the old generated speed $1/20$ and $\chi\le\chi_e$ give the source-acceleration factor $\eta\le(1.05)^2\chi/(.85)^2$ in the midpoint formula. Its coefficient remains below $5.2$. Using (3) in the scalar integral inequality therefore gives

$
M(T)\le m_e\exp\left(5.2\int_{T_e}^T k\,dt\right)
<m_e e^{7.8\sqrt{\chi_e}}<2.65\times10^{-20},
$
$
|W_\infty-W(T_e)|\le\int_{T_e}^\infty|W'|\,dt
<m_e(e^{7.8\sqrt{\chi_e}}-1)<10^{-25}.
\tag{13}
$

The last comparisons follow from $\sqrt{\chi_e}<4.75\times10^{-7}$ and $e^x-1\le x/(1-x)$ for $0<x<1$. This uses a prefix supremum, not the current midpoint speed substituted for source history. The source-window acceleration is bounded on generated times; no remote acceleration or seam derivative is imported.

Writing $v_\pm=W_\infty\pm U_\infty/2$ for the individual terminal vectors, (13) proves

$
\left||v_\pm|-\frac{\delta}{2}\right|<2.65\times10^{-20}.
\tag{14}
$

Thus both members have nonzero terminal speeds in this particular preparation, once (11) is admitted. Equation (14) does not identify their vector directions or replace their small common velocity by zero.
