## 1. The actual late passage is outward

The actual negative passage has $d>8\times10^{16}$, $K>4\times10^{-7}$, $|u|<4.6\times10^{-12}$, $M<m=2.64\times10^{-20}$, and $\chi<5\times10^{-24}$. If the angular subject is accepted, then

$$
\Lambda=\frac{H^2}{Kd}
<\frac{81}{(4\times10^{-7})(8\times10^{16})}
<2.532\times10^{-9}.
\tag{1}
$$

The exact radial equation, retaining both clocks in its error, is

$$
u'=k(\Lambda-2g-u)+Q_r+e_r,\qquad
0\le Q_r\le\frac{k|v|^2}{4g_0^3},
\qquad g_0=\sqrt{1-.01^2}.
\tag{2}
$$

The source assessment gives $|e_r|\le k\chi(7\sqrt\chi+3m_-)$, even with the older conservative $m_-=1.65\times10^{-17}$. Hence $|e_r|/k<10^{-34}$. Also $|v|^2\le81/d^2$ gives $Q_r/k<10^{-32}$, and $g\ge\sqrt{1-m^2}$. Substitution into (2) proves

$$
\boxed{u'<-1.9k}
\quad\hbox{throughout }[T_D,T_L].
\tag{3}
$$

Because first attainment gives $u_L\ge0$, strict decrease implies $u(t)>0$ for every $T_D\le t<T_L$. Thus there are no radial returns on this late passage. This does not claim absence of returns before $T_D$ or after $T_L$. It uses a bounded actual angular theorem; it is not a consequence of large separation alone.

Since $d'=u>0$ in the open passage, integrate $u\,du/dd=u'$ to get

$$
u_D^2-u_L^2>3.8K(D^{-1}-L^{-1})=2.85\chi_D.
\tag{4}
$$

In particular $u_D>\sqrt{2.85\chi_D}$. This is an actual lower bound within the remaining nominal branch, but is weaker than the approximately $2\sqrt{\chi_D}$ radial witness threshold.

## 2. The actual late gain is small

Use $P=\chi-F(u)+\tfrac32\chi^2$, $F(u)=u-2\log(1+u/2)$ and the accepted exact identity

$$
P_L-P_D=-\int_{T_D}^{T_L}
\left\{\frac{u}{2+u}
\left(\frac{H^2}{d^3}+2k(1-g)+Q_r\right)
+3ku\chi\right\}dt+\delta,\qquad |\delta|<9\times10^{-59}.
\tag{5}
$$

All geometric terms now have nonnegative integrands. Using $u\,dt=dd$, $2+u\ge2$ and $H<9$,

$$
0\le\int_{T_D}^{T_L}\frac{uH^2}{(2+u)d^3}\,dt
\le\frac{81}{4}(D^{-2}-L^{-2})
=\frac{1215}{64D^2}<2.11\times10^{-58}.
\tag{6}
$$

The other terms are much smaller. The exact nonnegative quadratic radial row satisfies

$$
\int_{T_D}^{T_L}\frac{uQ_r}{2+u}\,dt
\le\frac{81K}{24g_0^3}(D^{-3}-L^{-3})
<7\times10^{-95}.
\tag{7}
$$

Since $1-g=|b|^2/(1+g)<.501m^2$,

$$
\int_{T_D}^{T_L}\frac{2ku(1-g)}{2+u}\,dt
\le .501Km^2(D^{-1}-L^{-1})<6\times10^{-76}.
\tag{8}
$$

Finally,

$$
\int_{T_D}^{T_L}3ku\chi\,dt
=\frac32(\chi_D^2-\chi_L^2)<5\times10^{-72}.
\tag{9}
$$

Thus the whole actual late geometric gain is less than $2.12\times10^{-58}$. Equation (5) yields the useful asymmetric and absolute bounds

$$
-3.02\times10^{-58}<P_L-P_D<9\times10^{-59},
\qquad
|P_L-P_D|<3.1\times10^{-58}.
\tag{10}
$$

No sign of $P_D$ has been derived. For example $P_D>3.1\times10^{-58}$ would imply $P_L>0$ for this actual branch, while $P_D<-9\times10^{-59}$ would imply $P_L<0$. These are discrimination scales, not an assertion that the nominal preparation lies in either subcase. The bound (4) still permits $P_D$ of order $\chi_D$, approximately nineteen orders above the correction in (10). Establishing the signed preparation deficit at entry to $D$ remains load-bearing.
