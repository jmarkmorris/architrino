## Limits, controls and falsifiers

This proves a finite first-attainment conclusion and preservation of the two previously missing estimates up to $D_*$. It does not prove a uniform all-future neighborhood: the sufficient smallness condition contains $dM^2$, so the bound is exhausted at a scale proportional to $m_0^{-2}$. The [outgoing angular/spatial theorem](authorized-cases-followup-d-outgoing-angular-spatial.md) supplies a separate tail continuation test if a strict outgoing witness and terminal-geometric margins are later established. No such witness is inferred from first attainment alone. A positive signed angular floor is itself stronger than dispersal.

The exact mirror control $m_0\downarrow0$ removes midpoint motion, and the derivative identity (15) has zero normal component when $C=0$. These are analytical identity controls, not new physical preparations.
