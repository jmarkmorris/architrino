## Clarifications that must travel with the theorem

The accepted neighborhood result needs a fixed matching buffer near the left endpoint of the supplied finite history. The earlier flow subject's requirement that the cutoff vanish for $s\le-1$ alone does not imply such a buffer: a smooth function may start changing immediately to the right of $-1$. The neighborhood adjudication explicitly chooses the allowed cutoff to vanish for $s\le-1/2$ and equal one for $s\ge-1/4$. All release delays are below $1/5$, so this retains every sampled characteristic datum and supplies membership in the stated physical preparation class. This is a necessary accepted clarification, not a new modification introduced by this audit.

The flow subject's quadratic rigid-orbit projection statement is not automatic in an arbitrary $C^1$ manifold chart. The prior circle adjudication makes the same distinction found independently here: either use a linear graph chart, whose restricted rigid orbit is smooth, or use the weaker $o(\|z\|)$ estimate furnished by differentiability. The neighborhood subject already uses only the resulting small linear bound. Thus no quadratic regularity of the general solution map is needed, and the accepted departure result does not depend on that stronger phrasing.

The endpoint-defect sign also matters. The circle adjudication gives the explicit coefficient used in the table above; the opposite convention would double rather than repair the discrepancy. The chosen correction is root-free and leaves both present velocity and the common nonmirror velocity margin unchanged.

The distance is measured in the defined $C^1$ history norm for positions and velocities. On physical histories that norm is equivalent, with fixed rotation-dependent constants, to a $C^2$ physical-position history norm. Departing in this norm need not mean the instantaneous positions have a fixed positive separation from every translated/rotated circle. No such stronger instantaneous configuration statement follows here. The remote matching condition specifies the initial preparation; it is not required to hold near the left endpoint of every later moving history window.

## Disposition and falsifiers

No accepted N02 conclusion needs withdrawal after this audit. The accepted statement is local nonlinear orbital departure for the specified relatively open nonmirror preparation sets, using a leading mirror-sector instability. It does not classify arbitrary nonmirror histories, establish a transverse unstable sector, preserve asymmetry indefinitely, settle the primary D historical-source dispersal question, or determine later fate.

A failed root complement or remote bound, wrong ambient clock denominator, hidden derivative of a variation, incompatible or nonphysical preparation, cutoff overlap with the relevant source interval, unjustified spectral norm, uncontrolled continuous-time step, or growing rigid-symmetry tangent would overturn the respective conclusion. Numerical radius/time claims without the listed constants would exceed the established scope even if this qualitative theorem remains valid.
