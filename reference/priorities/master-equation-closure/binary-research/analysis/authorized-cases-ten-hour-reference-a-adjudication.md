## 1. Independent reconstruction

From the admitted intrinsic relation $\delta u_t=z_t-Q_t\rho-f_t$ and the elementary polar identity, the exact angular difference is

$$
\delta\omega=\frac{z_t-k\rho-f_t}{r_c+\rho},\qquad
k=Q_t+u_{c,t}/r_c.
$$

Here $\rho=r_a-r_c$, $z_t$ is the tangential transformed-velocity difference and $f_t$ is its source contribution. The old whole-cell norm gives $\nu^2\rho^2+z_t^2\le W^2$, after dropping the nonnegative radial transformed component. This is an actual joint constraint, not an inferred covariance between separately stored magnitudes. The extra whole-cell radius bound $|\rho|\le R$ may safely intersect it.

Let $a=\min(R,W/\nu)$, $B=\max|k|$, $m\le r_c$ and $|f_t|\le F$, with $m>R$. For $\lambda\ge0$, both desired inequalities follow if

$$
\sqrt{W^2-\nu^2s^2}+(B+\lambda)s+F\le\lambda m
\quad\text{for all }0\le s\le a.
\tag{1}
$$

This follows by multiplying each signed inequality by the positive actual radius and maximizing over $|\rho|=s$ and $z_t$. Thus the subject's support optimization is sound even if its coefficient and source terms are independently varied over their intervals. Dependence discarded in those intervals enlarges the admissible set conservatively.

For an independent executable check, define $b=B+\lambda$, $C=\lambda m-F$ and

$$
P(s)=(b^2+\nu^2)s^2-2Cbs+C^2-W^2.
\tag{2}
$$

Condition (1) is equivalent to $C-ba\ge0$ and $P(s)\ge0$ on $[0,a]$. The latter is checked at the exact clipped vertex $s_v=\min(a,\max(0,Cb/(b^2+\nu^2)))$. This uses neither the subject's square-root evaluator nor its support-branch decision. All operations are rational. It provides a separately derived certificate for every proposed density rather than merely repeating the producer's branch calculation.

The [independent audit instrument](../evidence/authorized-cases-ten-hour-reference-phase-audit.mjs) implements (2) with its own rational arithmetic and no subject imports. It reconstructs each coefficient interval from the raw old row, checks containment by the subject input interval, verifies the proposed density, preserves fallback rows, and rebuilds both cumulative primitives from the original exact faces. Original joint-norm and coefficient-family validity remain independently admitted premises; the new audit does not purport to reprove them from their numerical values.
