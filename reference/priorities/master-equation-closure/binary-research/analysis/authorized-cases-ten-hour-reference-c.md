## 3. Independent curved-birth checks

For any simple same-label root, let $r=T-S>0$ and $\mathbf n=(\mathbf X(T)-\mathbf X(S))/r$. Exact differentiation yields

$$
S'=\frac{D_r}{D_t},\qquad
r'=\frac{\mathbf n\cdot(\mathbf V(T)-\mathbf V(S))}{D_t},\qquad
D_r=1-\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf V(T).
\tag{1}
$$

**Derived conditional obstruction:** suppose a branch $S(T)<T_*$ approaches birth from the incoming subfield history, so $D_t>0$, $r\downarrow0$, and $\mathbf n\to\mathbf e=\mathbf V(T_*)$ with $|\mathbf e|=1$. Suppose all remaining contributions have an integrable lower bound in projection on $\mathbf e$, and every other potentially divergent contribution has nonnegative projection. Continuity supplies $\mathbf e\cdot\mathbf n\ge c>0$ and a finite positive upper bound $B$ on $\mathbf n\cdot(\mathbf V(T)-\mathbf V(S))$. Then (1) gives

$$
\frac{cK}{rD_t}\ge\frac{cK}{B}(\log r)',
$$

even where $r'$ is negative. Integrating between two positive reception times and taking the earlier one to birth yields an unbounded positive projected velocity increment. Thus monotonic playback or monotonic delay is unnecessary once the branch and sign/lower-bound hypotheses have been proved. The canonical $r^{-2}$ comparison similarly uses $(-1/r)'$. This argument leaves the branch's existence, completeness and the sign/lower bound for all other rows as explicit obligations.

Under the stronger hypothetical $C^2$ crossing with $a=\mathbf e\cdot\mathbf X''(T_*)>0$, Taylor expansion independently predicts the newborn source $S(T_*+t)=T_*-t+o(t)$, $r=2t+o(t)$ and $D_t=at+o(t)$. Hence the logarithmic self magnitude is asymptotic to $K/(2at^2)$ and the canonical magnitude to $K/(4at^3)$. Both are incompatible with that regular continuation. These asymptotics alone say nothing about weaker finite-velocity continuations and cannot replace the integral proof.

Known adversarial controls are: straight constant subfield motion has no positive-delay self root; straight speed-one motion has a nonsimple continuum and fails the ordinary-root chart; curved constant unit-speed motion has strict chord length less than arc length and may have no positive-delay self root; the explicit local path $\mathbf X(t)=\mathbf e(t+at^2/2)$ has exact near-birth root $S=-t$, range $2t$ and $D_t=at$.
