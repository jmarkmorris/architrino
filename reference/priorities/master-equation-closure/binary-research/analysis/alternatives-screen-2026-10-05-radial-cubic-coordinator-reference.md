## An exact monotone clock closes the whole future

The complete-window estimate gives $H'_s=\eta h r^{-3}[1+O(\epsilon)]>0$. Thus $H$ is an actual monotone clock as long as the provisional box holds. Divide the exact equations by this positive derivative. Since $h^2-1=2\eta^2H+\eta^4H^2$ and $\epsilon/\eta^2=\eta$,

$$
\frac{dU}{dH}
=\frac{2H+\eta^2H^2+O(\eta)}{h[1+O(\epsilon)]}

$$

$$
\frac{dz}{dH}=-\frac{2U}{h[1+O(\epsilon)]}
