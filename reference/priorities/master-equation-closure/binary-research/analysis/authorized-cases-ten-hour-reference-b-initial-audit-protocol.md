# Independent initial-coordinate audit protocol

Prepared after known controls and before target use. The [independent instrument](../evidence/authorized-cases-ten-hour-reference-b-initial-audit.py), SHA-256 `24f65e7f1bf0765ec8bcf3f781675c69b02c647d880c73129d44babeec89f4bb`, uses SymPy Gaussian univariate series and imports no producer helper. It independently evaluates the original final layer polynomial and its derivative at scaled time 200, constructs the bilinear physical variables, and checks all three full forward-map residuals of the emitted inverse coordinates through degree sixteen. Exact leading seed and coefficient norms are additional obligations.

Known mode passed the independently soluble circular coordinate conversion, square-root and reciprocal identities, the triangular inverse map, and rejection of an altered inverse. Its receipt `reference/b-initial-audit-known-v1.json` has SHA-256 `c062e95090877535deee631add34263982427d58ff8e5553402c586b439b6ce5`. Known-only development exposed and corrected comparisons of Gaussian-domain elements with bare Python integers before target use.

The target is coordinator initial-map `coefficients-v1.json`, SHA-256 `bb454c74e947e3b53ce4796639cdf26213eae539d0e2ed88e136595176fa8a51`; the layer and normal-map inputs are bound to their separately accepted digests. The run uses the shared venv, a 150-second supervisor and 120-second internal alarm, and is limited to the finite coefficient/residual audit. Output is a small receipt; no actual phase, source history or physical trajectory is computed. A failed assertion blocks acceptance and is preserved.
