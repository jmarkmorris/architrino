## 1. Two complete clocks and one shared speed budget

Choose $\beta<1$ strictly above the complete supplied-past speed supremum of both labels. Let the present separation be $d_0=|X_1(0)-X_2(0)|>0$, and let

$$
w_0=\frac{X_1(0)-X_2(0)}{d_0}\cdot[V_1(0)-V_2(0)]>0.
$$

On a future trial with both speeds below $\beta$, each receiver has its own unique partner source $S_{ij}$ and range $R_{ij}=t-S_{ij}$. The complete old chord bound excludes all positive-delay self roots; the remote-past sign and source monotonicity supply each partner root. For the instantaneous equal-time separation $d(t)$,

$$
\frac d{1+\beta}\le R_{ij}\le\frac d{1-\beta},\qquad
D_{ij}=1-n_{ij}\cdot V_j(S_{ij})\ge1-\beta.
$$

Thus each original canonical acceleration has the bound

$$
|A_i|\le\frac{C_\beta}{d^2},\qquad
C_\beta=\frac{(1+\beta)^2}{1-\beta}.
\tag{1}
$$

No equality between the two delays, source velocities or acceleration vectors was used. In particular, opposite polarity does not make the actual delayed accelerations equal and opposite at one reception time.

## 2. An explicit sufficient outgoing criterion

The following strict inequalities suffice:

$$
d_0w_0^2>8C_\beta,\qquad
\max_i|V_i(0)|+\frac{2C_\beta}{w_0d_0}<\beta.
\tag{2}
$$

Together with the fixed supplied-past bound, bootstrap both future speeds below $\beta$ and the outward radial speed $w=d'$ above $w_0/2$. Then $d\ge d_0+w_0t/2$, and each total velocity change is at most

$$
\int_0^\infty |A_i|dt\le\frac{2C_\beta}{w_0d_0}.
\tag{3}
$$

The relative radial equation contains a nonnegative transverse-velocity term, hence $w'\ge-2C_\beta/d^2$. Since $d'>0$, integration with respect to radius gives

$$
w^2\ge w_0^2-4C_\beta\left(\frac1{d_0}-\frac1d\right)
>\frac{w_0^2}{2}.
\tag{4}
$$

Both trial inequalities improve strictly. Positive separation, complete speed margin and the two regular delayed-root charts then continue the same solution on every finite interval. The pointwise acceleration bound controls each sampled source's regularity; delayed velocities are locally Lipschitz. This is a method-of-steps argument on the exact prescribed history, not a replacement tail. The explicit old fixed-direction impulse criterion is another sufficient form; (2) uses the moving separation direction and a radial squared-speed estimate.

## 3. Terminal motion and center motion

Each $|U_i-V_i(0)|$ is bounded by (3), but neither individual terminal speed is asserted nonzero unless an additional present-speed margin exceeds that budget.

For the center $M=(X_1+X_2)/2$, its acceleration is $(A_1+A_2)/2$, not assumed zero. It obeys the same per-member bound (1), so $M'$ converges to $(U_1+U_2)/2$ with the corresponding impulse budget.
