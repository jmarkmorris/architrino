The period parameter covers every sufficiently nearby physical period, rather than a restricted subset: with $x=\beta\cos x$, $D=1+\beta\sin x$ and $\omega=4\beta^3\cos x D$,

\frac{d\log\omega}{d\beta}=\frac3\beta+\frac{\beta\cos^2x}{D^2}>0.

## Symmetry of the uniquely reconstructed complement

$$
(Y_1,Y_2)\mapsto(-Y_2,-Y_1),\qquad
Y_i\mapsto\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-1)Y_i,
$$

$$
Y_i(s)\mapsto\operatorname{diag}(1,-1,1)Y_i(-s).
$$

Each is an $L^2$ isometry, fixes the base solution, preserves the exact full equation and its linearized kernel, and hence commutes with $P_K$.
