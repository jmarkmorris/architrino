$$
a=c\cdot N,\qquad q=\sqrt{1-|c|^2+a^2}>0.
\tag{1}
$$

For each receiver the partner gap is strictly monotone on the complete source past and has exactly one root. The positive delays and transmitter factors are

$$
R_+=\frac{d}{q-a},\qquad R_-=\frac{d}{q+a},\qquad
D_+=q(q-a),\qquad D_-=q(q+a).
\tag{2}
$$

Indeed, the two root equations are $R_+=|dN+cR_+|$ and $R_-=|-dN+cR_-|$. The identity $(q-a)(q+a)=1-|c|^2>0$ selects their positive roots and proves that all factors in (2) are positive. The emission-to-reception directions are $n_+=(q-a)N+c$ and $n_-=-(q+a)N+c$, both unit vectors.

Substitution into the complete canonical partner rows gives

$$
A_+=-\frac{K}{d^2q}\{(q-a)^2N+(q-a)c\},

A_-=-\frac{K}{d^2q}\{-(q+a)^2N+(q+a)c\}.
\tag{3}
$$

Consequently the common-center and relative acceleration outputs are exactly

$$
\frac{A_++A_-}{2}=\frac{K}{d^2}(2aN-c),
\tag{4}
$$

$$

\{(1-|c|^2+2a^2)N-ac\}.
\tag{5}
$$
