# Completed source-window search proposals

Derived method observation: an initial source-window radius and its expansion factor are search proposals, not premises of the interval response theorem. On each trial cylinder the driver must still establish a complete closed guard strictly earlier than the receiving left face, include every nonmonotone completed history bin and negative-time seam, compute angular transport on that entire guard, and then prove that both its initial source window and every response/root-family source interval lie inside it. Only after those tests may it accept strict whole-cell error improvement. No guessed radius can waive any test or root.

Consequently, replacing the former radius proposal $4\,dt+0.01+10(e_R+e_{R,prefix})$ by $4\,dt+0.01+2(e_R+e_{R,prefix})$ does not change the eventual coefficient/error theorem. Likewise, when a required source window does not fit, the next proposed radius may be $1.05$ times the maximum of the current radius and the absolute distances of its two required faces from the fixed seed. Both are positive finite search proposals; the latter expands strictly and contains the presently required window. The next angular/field evaluation must nevertheless repeat the full inclusion tests, since its source family can change with the guard.

This is not a theorem that a fixed point exists, that the actual source window has the guessed size, or that fewer guards improve physical error. Search may still fail by noncompletion, domain loss or exhaustion. The driver retains every failure and the unmodified final source/root-family proof obligations. Its falsifier is any accepted receiving cell without the complete final guard, angular/source inventory or root-family inclusions. A larger failed proposal reaching the present proves only that this search failed, not a dynamical event.
