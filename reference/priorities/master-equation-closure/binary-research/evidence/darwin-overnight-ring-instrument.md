## 1. What the wrapper is

[darwin-overnight-ring-cases.mjs](darwin-overnight-ring-cases.mjs) imports `runCase`, `invariants`, `K` and `CF` from the frozen pair instrument and adds no integration arithmetic. It builds $N=4$ preparations: member $k$ at $\mathbf X_k=R(\cos\phi_k,\sin\phi_k,0)$ with $\phi_k=k\pi/2$ (the unit vectors at multiples of $\pi/2$ are written as exact integers so that no $\cos(\pi/2)=6\times10^{-17}$ enters the preparation), polarities $(+,-,+,-)$, velocity $v\,(-\sin\phi_k,\cos\phi_k,0)$, with optional perturbations (per-member radius factors, per-member $z$ offsets, a common velocity). The speed is $v=\sqrt{2(2\sqrt2-1)/(8R-1-\sqrt2)}$ for the full law and $v_0=\sqrt{(2\sqrt2-1)/(4R)}$ for the zero-coupling control, the period is $2\pi R/v$, and every run uses the frozen settings of preregistration Section 2 (`rContact` $10^{-3}$, `rEscape` $10^4$, `speedBound` $0.1$, `epsBound` $0.05$, `detTol` $10^{-12}$, `condMax` $10^{12}$, `tTol` $10^{-9}$, output cadence period$/200$) at settings P (`dp54` rtol $10^{-10}$, atol $10^{-12}$), C (rtol $10^{-12}$, atol $10^{-12}$) and R (`rk4`, $h=5$). The instrument ran $N=4$ without any change: the $12\times12$ velocity Hessian is assembled, factored and condition-checked by the same code that handled $N=2$.

Each run record is reduced to ring observables: the instantaneous centre (mean position), each member's distance from the centre and the relative radius deviation $(d_k-R)/R$ (minimum and maximum over all samples and events, plus its history per sample), adjacent separations (pairs $01,12,23,03$; expected $R\sqrt2$) and diagonal separations ($02,13$; expected $2R$), the tilt of the best-fit plane (normal from the smallest eigenvector of the centred scatter matrix, angle against $z$), the centre displacement and mean centre velocity, invariant drifts $|\Delta E/E|$, $|\Delta\mathbf P|$, $|\Delta\mathbf J|/|\mathbf J|$ (final and maximal), events in order, the running supremum of member speed, the maximum of $K/(c_f^2r)$ over pairs, the minimum $\det H$ and maximum $\operatorname{cond}_2H$, the member return error at each whole period against the preparation, the first sample at which the radius deviation exceeds $10^{-9},10^{-6},10^{-3},10^{-2},10^{-1}$, and a least-squares exponential-growth fit of the radius deviation over a stated window. The reduction is written next to each record as `<ID>-<setting>.reduced.json`; the compact receipt [darwin-overnight-ring-instrument-controls.json](darwin-overnight-ring-instrument-controls.json) holds the known cases first and one summary row per target run.
