# Intrinsic comparison-curve triangle transfer

Derived prospective rule for the same original exact physical E preparation; mathematical assessment precedes any target error transfer. It changes a mathematical test curve only. Let two complete comparison curves $q_0,q_1$ have the same literal negative-time preparation, and let an already independently admitted physical solution certificate relative to $q_0$ cover a finite closed positive-time interval. Suppose separately established continuum bounds on that interval satisfy $|q_1-q_0|\leq d_x$, $|v_1-v_0|\leq d_v$, $|a_1-a_0|\leq d_a$, $r_1\geq m_1>0$, $r_0\geq m_0>0$, $|v_0|\leq B_0$, $|a_0|\leq A_0$, $|q_0|\leq R_0$. Every bound includes all closed source/receiving bins and every physical seam; there is no endpoint-only transfer.

The reverse triangle inequality gives $|r_1-r_0|\leq d_x$. For $n_j=q_j/r_j$,

$$
|n_1-n_0|\leq\frac{2d_x}{m_1}.
$$

Indeed insert $q_0/r_1$: the two differences have norms $d_x/r_1$ and $|r_1-r_0|/r_1$. The same bound holds for the planar tangential rays. Direct projection differences therefore give

$$
|u_{r,1}-u_{r,0}|,\ |u_{t,1}-u_{t,0}|\leq d_u:=d_v+\frac{2B_0d_x}{m_1},
$$

$$
|A_{r,1}-A_{r,0}|,\ |A_{t,1}-A_{t,0}|\leq d_A:=d_a+\frac{2A_0d_x}{m_1}.
$$

These are physical acceleration components, not jerks. The planar cross-product expansion gives

$$
|h_1-h_0|\leq d_h:=R_0d_v+B_0d_x+d_xd_v.
$$

Finally $\omega_j=u_{t,j}/r_j$ yields

$$
|\omega_1-\omega_0|\leq d_\omega:=\frac{d_u}{m_1}+\frac{B_0d_x}{m_1m_0}.
$$

All these scalar quantities are invariant under a constant planar rotation; the physical solution's unknown absolute phase need not be bounded. The triangle inequality adds $d_x,d_u,d_h,d_A,d_\omega$ to the corresponding old whole-cell intrinsic errors. With any retained positive metric $\nu$, the stronger joint radial/radial-velocity certificate transfers as

$$
Z_1\leq Z_0+\sqrt{\nu^2d_x^2+d_u^2}.
$$

Its new radius and radial-velocity errors can be bounded by $Z_1/\nu$ and $Z_1$. The new h error is $H_0+d_h$. Independently transferred physical radial/tangential acceleration bounds remain available for completed-source inventories; full velocity and acceleration norms are recomputed from their complete component bounds. Every angular primitive and source inventory is rebuilt on the full transferred history using the new bounds. The initial/past mismatch is recomputed against the unchanged complete physical preparation and the new test, and exact literal negative-time agreement permits the old negative-time triangle bounds without a spurious infinite phase integral.

A future receiving comparison may choose a new cell metric only with the already assessed metric-jump rule. It must recompute its own full mean-value response/root coefficients and use its own complete continuum residual. It cannot reuse old coefficient families or old residual caps after changing the test. A transferred old prefix is justified by the old admitted physical certificate and the separately established curve bounds; it is not a new trajectory solve and must not be audited as if its inherited rows satisfied the new response recurrence. A distinct transfer audit must bind every old certificate row, the complete difference/source geometry evidence, and all rebuilt scalar/component/angular states before continuation.

Falsifiers are a missed interval or seam, an invalid global difference/geometry bound, a zero radius, nonidentical physical past, missing physical source acceleration, inappropriate reuse of old coefficient or residual families, or treating a numerical curve difference as an actual-history error without the old admitted certificate. The rule alone establishes neither an event nor efficacy; it provides an independently checkable route for a finer mathematical comparison on the same original physical launch.
