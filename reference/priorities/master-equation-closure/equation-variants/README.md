## Operator approval for deviations

**Explicit operator approval is required before investigating a new Master Equation variant or any deviation from an approved variant's specified equation and research scope.** A proposal, registered formula, historical result, agent-authored launch specification or mathematically successful comparison is not approval. General instructions to search broadly, continue working, use more agents, spend available credits or pursue an agenda do not approve additional equation changes unless the operator explicitly selects the named variants or clearly identified fixed specifications.

Before requesting that approval, state the baseline and the proposed change in plain language, then identify the exact specification: added or removed terms; coefficients or parameter ranges outside those already approved; distance dependence; past/future support; self and partner channels; root exclusions; reception width or spatial core; memory or receiver response; speed-domain and event/continuation rules; and any enlargement of the approved geometry or history domain. Link the defining passage and record the operator's actual instruction and date. An agent's statement that a case was selected is not evidence of selection. Freeze the approved specification before target work and pass this approval boundary to delegated workers.

Continue routine mathematical and numerical work within an existing explicit selection without asking again. Any new departure remains a proposal until approved; stop only that departure and continue unaffected authorized work. When the selected equation fails or leaves its domain, report that result without adding a remedy. Listing a candidate in this registry permits description and discussion, not execution or adoption into canon.
