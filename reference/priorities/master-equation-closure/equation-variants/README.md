# Authorized Master Equation variations for research

The [canonical Master Equation](../../../../content/markdown/aaa/dynamics/master-equation.md#the-master-equation-canonical-form) remains the baseline. The operator authorizes research into the field-speed ceiling and logarithmic potential, separately or together. These are the only authorized changes to the Master Equation for research. Authorization to investigate does not adopt an alternative into canon or select it for every scenario.

| Equation choice | Shared definition | Research boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Canonical Master Equation | [Canonical form](../../../../content/markdown/aaa/dynamics/master-equation.md#the-master-equation-canonical-form) | Default baseline; variations must be named explicitly |
| Field-speed ceiling | [Definition and general results](field-speed-ceiling/definition.md) | Distinguish strict and inclusive speed domains, self response and any explicitly selected boundary response |
| Logarithmic potential | [Equation before and after replacement](logarithmic-potential/manuscript.md#master-equation-before-and-after-a-logarithmic-replacement) | Inverse-distance reception candidate with its specified causal geometry and transmitter weighting |
| Both variations | [Combination procedure](combined-variations.md) | Specify the logarithmic kernel and exact ceiling clauses together; an inequality alone supplies no boundary response |

This directory owns shared definitions, general mathematical consequences and preserved cross-geometry provenance. It has no independent geometry queue. The parent [queue](../work-queue.md) owns shared equation questions; each [configuration directory](../README.md#research-directories) owns all research for its configuration, including authorized equation comparisons, supporting derivations, reviews, evidence and local instruments. A result spanning several configurations has one shared comparative source linked from the affected owners.

Every result identifies its equation by a stable defining passage, the selected modifications, complete histories, self-channel treatment, population summation where applicable, evidence grade and continuation scope. The [scenario-assumption procedure](../../../op/theory-orientation.md#scenario-assumptions) prevents an investigated response from becoming an implicit later assumption. Existing quarantined or withdrawn studies remain records, not additional research authority.

The [migration account](../analysis/equation-ownership-migration-2026-10-03.md) records destinations, frozen-source routing and validation. Directory organization changes no scientific acceptance or task lifecycle.
