# Photon Research: Candidate Histories, Propagation, and Environmental Response

## 1. The object being investigated

The current photon construction is a coaxial contra-rotating polarity-conjugate planar pair. Each planar braid contains three two-architrino binaries. The full candidate therefore contains twelve architrinos, organized into two six-member components with a common propagation axis. Coaxial placement, opposite circulation and polarity conjugation are distinct relations that must be specified in the candidate history.

This construction is a theorem target. The [canonical referent statement](../../../../content/markdown/aaa/assemblies/bosons/electroweak-bosons.md#photon-referent-status) states that no independently certified retained branch of the proposed assembly has been exhibited. A retained branch would be a history that continues under the equation while preserving the declared carrier identity and properties over its stated domain. A picture of twelve moving points, or twelve prescribed circular paths, establishes the chosen geometry rather than its dynamical persistence.

Several levels of research consequently enter the photon problem. An individual binary contains two architrinos. A three-binary braid contains six. A candidate planar pair combines two such braids and adds photon-specific requirements on propagation and response. Results about a smaller constituent must be checked again in the complete assembly because the other constituents change the received histories and acceleration contributions. The ambient population and any emitter or receiver add further interactions; twelve is the candidate's internal inventory, not a declaration that every physical calculation contains only twelve histories.

## 2. Absolute histories and three speed questions

Let $T$ be absolute time, let $\mathbf C(T)$ be a declared carrier-center path, and let $\boldsymbol\xi_a(T)$ locate constituent $a$ relative to that center. Its absolute path is

$$
\mathbf X_a(T)=\mathbf C(T)+\boldsymbol\xi_a(T),
\qquad a=1,\ldots,12.
$$

This is a decomposition of positions, not an evolution law. For straight translation along a fixed unit vector $\hat{\mathbf e}$, write $\dot{\mathbf C}=c_\gamma\hat{\mathbf e}$. If the internal velocity is transverse to that direction, elementary Euclidean geometry gives

$$
\|\dot{\mathbf X}_a\|^2
=c_\gamma^2+\|\dot{\boldsymbol\xi}_a\|^2,
\qquad
\hat{\mathbf e}\cdot\dot{\boldsymbol\xi}_a=0.
$$

The equality is a kinematic identity under the stated orthogonality condition. It shows why carrier speed $c_\gamma$, constituent path speed $\|\dot{\mathbf X}_a\|$ and primitive wake speed $c_f$ are separate quantities. Equality between an observer's measured light speed and a candidate's transport speed is a further recovery question. A provisional display control or a constituent crossing of $c_f$ does not decide that equality. Numerical instantiations use $c_f=1$.

The absolute history matters even when the relative geometry appears fixed. At reception time $T$, a contribution from constituent $a$ to constituent $b$ comes from an earlier emission time $\tau$ satisfying

$$
\|\mathbf X_b(T)-\mathbf X_a(\tau)\|
=c_f(T-\tau),
\qquad \tau<T.
$$

The [Master Equation](../../../../content/markdown/aaa/dynamics/master-equation.md) determines which causal contributions enter the acceleration and the limits of the admitted update at singular cases. A complete candidate calculation must retain its admissible source–receiver rows, including any admissible past self-history hits, and justify any omitted exterior or symmetry reduction. Geometric symmetry at one instant does not by itself justify cancellation between contributions sampled at different emission times.

The [prescribed moving-pair analysis](analysis/prescribed-moving-pair-and-self-hit-geometry.md) develops this issue for a moving apparatus, and the [speed analysis](analysis/carrier-and-constituent-speeds.md) states the orthogonality boundary explicitly. The no-catch-up and old-root regimes constrain that diagnostic preparation. The Photon app supplies internal paths and uses its declared display regularization; it does not establish that the unmodified equation produces those paths.

## 3. From candidate dynamics to photon identification

A complete history is the first requirement. Photon identification additionally connects that history to observed transport, polarization and interaction. The corpus [closure interface](../../../../content/markdown/aaa/assemblies/bosons/electroweak-bosons.md#photon-closure-interface) separates these requirements into three gates.

| Gate | Physical question | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A: kinematics and optics | Does a retained propagating branch yield consistent phase, energy, momentum and transport readouts, with no residual photon rest branch and acceptable dispersion? | Observer-level photon relations are recovery targets; they cannot generate the constituent paths or replace the missing branch. |
| B: polarization and spin | Does the same carrier support exactly two transverse channels, no free longitudinal channel, the required helicity and a derived material-analyzer response? | A transverse plot or fitted ellipse does not establish helicity, an invariant detector measure or single-photon statistics. |
| C: vertices and transitions | Do emission, absorption, scattering and other admitted events close the source, carrier, receiver, wake, medium and remnant accounts? | A source-free diagram does not establish event transfer, reaction thresholds or ensemble radiation behavior. |

These gates are linked through one history and its accounts. They are not independent formulas to fit. In particular, the phase wavelength along the propagation direction, the transverse braid radii, the leading–trailing separation and the total packet length are distinct quantities. The energy–frequency recovery target alone does not equate any two of them.

The [EQ-12 packet](../../mapping-equations/analysis/eq-12-16a-photon-quantum-gauge-neutrino-packet.md#eq-12-photon-energy-null-condition-and-eikonal-propagation) keeps these source, path and receiver requirements on one proposed transfer record. Its stated first blocker is an accepted photon packet. Passing an attempt-level residual therefore cannot establish the physical carrier that the residual assumes.

## 4. Formation, propagation and environmental response

A photon preparation includes its earlier source interaction and the ambient histories relevant to later propagation. A source-coupled release state may have different geometry from a later freely propagating state. Calling the visible release shape an equilibrium would require a separate dynamical argument.

The existing conditional formation question asks whether admissible source and Noether sea states select distinct retained geometries. Candidate observables include pair spacing, braid scale, shape ratios and relative phase offsets. That question becomes physically meaningful only after a retained free-photon branch and explicit environmental inputs exist. The current [formation-geometry proposal](work-queue.md#pho-009--conditional-formation-geometry-relaxation) remains guessed and deferred / blocked.

An environmental response cannot be inferred from a change in a speed slider. It must follow from reciprocal interaction between the candidate and the specified surrounding histories. The [shared sea response](../noether-sea-research/manuscript.md#4-one-shared-pressure-response) also requires clocks, material assemblies and signal consumers to use the same population record. A separately fitted photon response would leave that common constitutive question unresolved.

A proposed geometry map must distinguish dependence on the final environment from dependence on the path used to reach it. Hysteresis, branch switching or transition-rate dependence would refute an endpoint-only map. A converged null response would bound the declared tested family rather than rule out every environment. An observed change in geometry would still need an explicit account before being interpreted as a frequency shift, energy transfer or gravitational redshift.

The current sources provide a candidate inventory, prescribed-history diagnostics, causal-root requirements and observer-level recovery targets. The [same-transmitter analysis](analysis/prescribed-moving-pair-and-self-hit-geometry.md) and [unchanged sweep receipt](evidence/helical-self-hit-phase-lock-sweep.receipt.v1.json) retain a bounded historical negative for the app's tested prescribed cases. The [F6c photon proposal](analysis/f6c-photon-source-and-carrier-candidate.md) considers an emitter or receiver role and a possible propagating continuation of an eight-architrino geometry. Its photon assignment remains guessed. That assembly's transverse response plane supplies a search clue, not an accepted free two-channel carrier.

The decisive missing object is a complete, independently checked retained photon history on a declared domain. A future bounded result must state its preparation, equation, causal-root coverage, environmental assumptions and interval. Claims of stability or long-time persistence require additional evidence over the corresponding disturbance family and duration. A failed equation residual, omitted causal contribution, lost carrier identity or failed declared recovery would overturn the relevant candidate claim. No conclusion about the lifetime, stability or relaxation of physical photons is supplied by organizing these research sources.
