# Photon research brainstorming

## From a specified pair to a retained carrier

The current photon construction is a coaxial contra-rotating polarity-conjugate pair of planar three-binary braids. A prescribed version supplies twelve trajectories and permits causal-root diagnostics. The unresolved dynamical question is whether a complete interacting history retains an appropriate propagating carrier under the unchanged Master Equation. This is an existing theorem target, not a newly activated search.

The geometric variables include braid radii and phase relations, leading–trailing separation, propagation direction and carrier speed. A useful candidate must specify the entire required past and the surrounding population as well as these instantaneous coordinates. A visible phase pattern cannot substitute for the complete history. The [manuscript](manuscript.md) explains the relation to the existing corpus construction and app diagnostics.

## Carrier speed and constituent speed

The [speed analysis](analysis/carrier-and-constituent-speeds.md) distinguishes the motion of a carrier center from the absolute motion of each architrino. Transverse internal motion contributes to constituent speed even when the center moves along one fixed axis. This kinematic distinction does not impose a constituent speed ceiling or establish a photon transport law.

The resulting question is which self-history and partner-history roots exist in a candidate translating preparation, and whether the resulting acceleration is compatible with the supplied internal motion. A count of roots or a repeated phase-at-hit family alone does not establish phase locking, persistence or photon identity.

## Formation and environmental geometry

The existing [PHO-009 proposal](work-queue.md#pho-009--conditional-formation-geometry-relaxation) asks whether admissible source-coupled and ambient sea states select different retained geometries after a free-photon branch has been certified. Its grade remains **guessed conditional hypothesis**, and its status remains **Deferred / blocked**.

A release transient and an asymptotic propagation state are different objects. A prospective geometry response could involve pair spacing, braid scale, shape ratios or phase offsets. It would need forward and reverse environment paths, declared stability and relaxation criteria, refinement and an independent theorem or oracle. Hysteresis, branch switching or transition-rate dependence would defeat a claim that only the final environment determines the geometry. A null response over a declared family would constrain that family; an absent retained branch would leave the question without a physical referent.

Geometry response would not by itself establish a frequency shift, energy transfer or gravitational-redshift law. Those readouts require the corresponding source, path, receiver and environmental accounts. The [shared sea response](../noether-sea-research/analysis/pressure-dependent-noether-sea-constitutive-response.md) and mapping owners retain those dependencies.

## Rigid screen and modulated-history proposal

The [root and axial analysis](analysis/translating-carrier-root-and-axial-constraints.md) derives a phase-bijection constraint for regular alternating hexagons with equal and opposite angular frequencies: their complete cross-ring acceleration is anti-periodic in a constituent's rotating frame. A nonzero constant cross correction is impossible in that chart. This does not prove that every contra-rotating layered history fails or that a periodic solution exists.

**Claim grade: guessed search proposal.** After a rigid prescribed-history screen, seek a history with periodic internal modulation in the translating chart, supplying its full past and solving the acceleration equation rather than imposing circular motion. A boundary-value formulation would need an unknown period, phase convention, admitted root/event treatment and a same-record independent check. It should preserve twelve constituent identities while allowing the shape and, if declared, axial offsets to vary. The latter change requires re-deriving the fixed-plane constraints. Co-rotating controls remove the relative-phase symmetry obstruction but are not thereby balanced.

The proposal is unactivated. Replacing or supplementing the queued residual-screen completion has a [discussion-scoped decision entry](work-queue.md#decide-whether-to-add-a-modulated-periodic-orbit-criterion); the rigid screen remains binding until that decision. Its falsifier is failure of the resulting declared history to satisfy the complete equation or maintain the proposed carrier identity; a nonzero rigid residual does not prove that a modulated branch exists.

## Geometry and neutral-history comparisons

**Claim grade: guessed research leads.** The following questions are fresh statements of ideas in the mixed Braid mining notes, retained there as historical provenance. They supply possible comparisons, not accepted dynamics or new executable tasks.

The [AM-12 geometry lead](../braid-program/mining/2026-07-15-assembly-and-mass-map.md#12-the-photon-as-separator-mediated-surgery-not-flattening-pair-separation-proportional-to-wavelength) suggests varying axial gap, radii, circulation and relative phase as independent coordinates of a complete planar-pair history. An admitted transition from a volumetric source assembly could then be examined for changes in its causal-root structure. A gap proportional to wavelength and a transition mediated by a separating component remain unproved hypotheses; neither relation may be imposed as an entry condition. A candidate relation would fail if a retained history in its declared family had incompatible spacing, or if the proposed transition failed the unchanged equation or complete root accounting.

The [MC-12 neutral-history lead](../braid-program/mining/2026-07-15-medium-and-conservation.md#12-neutrality-is-not-silence-internal-relative-motion-radiates) suggests comparing admissible, collision-free neutral histories in common translation with matched histories that also contain internal relative motion. The comparison would compute received acceleration contributions from the same declared source inventory and complete causal-root census. Net neutrality alone decides neither cancellation nor emission, because the receiver samples different constituent positions at their respective emission times. A proposed cancellation is falsified by an independently checked nonzero received sum within its stated domain; a residual by itself still does not establish a photon or radiation channel. The [prescribed moving-pair analysis](analysis/prescribed-moving-pair-and-self-hit-geometry.md) supplies an explicit chart from which such questions can be formulated, without supplying a retained carrier.
