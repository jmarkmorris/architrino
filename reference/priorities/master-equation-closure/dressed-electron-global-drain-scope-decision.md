# Dressed-Electron Global Angular-Momentum Drain Scope Decision

**Decision:** authorized for focused planning on 2026-07-11.
**Claim level:** derivation / simulation target with seed-grade null controls.
**Authority boundary:** this decision fixes the comparison object and retained-record contract. It does not authorize a new solver, a native release, branch retention, corpus promotion, or selection of a new payload geometry.

## Closure Question

Test whether a complete charged-electron candidate changes the bare neutral scaffold's two independent obstructions:

1. the gyroscopic-circulatory axis flutter; and
2. the sign-definite axial angular-momentum pump whose self-consistent global drain remains open.

The comparison is not allowed to promote a favorable seed geometry. It must evaluate the bare and dressed objects on retained records carrying the same root, wake, action, stability, and boundary-exchange identities.

## Existing Seed-Grade Null Controls

Two coarse pilots already narrow the placement space without supplying retained evidence:

- Section 88 tests a spinless on-axis column. It opens the $-1e$ channel and partially reduces the seed axial pump, but it has no payload angular momentum, does not solve axial equilibrium, and only damps rather than dissolves the flutter. It is a rejected electron placement and a retained comparison control.
- Section 89 tests co-rotating, off-axis, single-binary equilibrium sites. The payload enters the gyroscopic sector and sources an orbital magnetic moment, but the sites are linearly unstable, the assembled 12-body ledgers do not close, the pump grows, and the flutter is aggravated. It is a rejected minimal docking realization and a retained comparison control.

These results establish neither that every spin-carrying payload fails nor that a sense-split or counter-rotating payload succeeds. They require the next candidate to be off-axis and spin-carrying, and they require its placement to be solved self-consistently for the assembled 12-body object. Candidate sense choices remain hypotheses until a separate implementation decision selects one.

## Conclusions That Require Dressed Retest

The retained 12-site program must re-evaluate:

- self-consistent payload docking and full-object radial support;
- the common-cadence or declared multi-cadence representability condition;
- the full 12-site gyroscopic-circulatory spectrum, including payload-relative deformation modes rather than only rigid layer tilts;
- the cycle-averaged axial pump, including scaffold, payload, cross-sector, wake, and Noether sea contributions;
- whether a large-distance medium response carries the pumped angular momentum without introducing an unclosed local brake, drag term, or hidden external torque;

- accelerated or transition radiation as a finite ledger event rather than an appended acceleration-force term;

No current result licenses a stable electron branch, a solved global drain, the claim that payload dressing cures the bare no-gos, a mass derivation, a $g$-factor derivation, or corpus retention language.

## Retained-Record Output Contract

One accepted comparison record must expose at least:

1. **Inventory and identity:** all 12 constituent ids, polarities, scaffold/payload membership, attachment or phase relation, source/receiver mapping, and the charge-conjugate control map.
2. **History binding:** one retained-history id shared by causal-root, path-history, receiver-normal, wake-history, action, stability, and boundary-exchange rows.
3. **Geometry and kinematics:** positions, velocities, cadence variables, layer and payload phases, drift state, payload-relative coordinates, and the declared representability witness.
4. **Force and torque partitions:** per-constituent radial and tangential rows plus scaffold, payload, scaffold-payload, wake, and Noether sea subtotal rows.
5. **Angular-momentum balance:** internal, wake, medium, and outgoing-channel contributions on one finite event window,

   $$
   \Delta \mathbf{J}_{12}
   +\Delta \mathbf{J}_{\mathrm{wake}}
   +\Delta \mathbf{J}_{\mathrm{NS}}
   +\Delta \mathbf{J}_{\mathrm{out}}
   =\mathbf{0},
   $$

   with every term finite and independently populated. A small total residual cannot substitute for a missing row.
6. **Stability:** the full linearized spectrum, gauge/null-mode deflation, payload-relative modes, perturbation-return trials, separatrix or escape diagnostics, and native-release survival time.
7. **Electromagnetic and radiation routing:** charge-current multipoles, magnetic moment, photon-channel event rows when present, recoil, remnant, and non-radiative medium routing.
8. **Numerical credibility:** regulator sweeps, step-size and history-window convergence, bare-drop recovery, charge-conjugate recovery, and explicit failure codes for missing or synthetic evidence.

## Decision Gates

1. **Representability:** the 12-site object is a closed retained assembly rather than a snapshot or independently shearing set of layers.
2. **Self-consistent support:** the payload and scaffold close their force and cadence ledgers together; prescribed-worldline balance is only a seed locator.
3. **Stability:** no un-deflated growing mode remains, and finite perturbations return within a declared basin.
4. **Global angular-momentum balance:** the pump is absent, internally exchanged, or exported through populated wake / Noether sea / outgoing rows on the same retained record.
5. **Observer-facing handoff:** charge, magnetic moment, drift response, and any photon output are derived from the retained record at their honest comparison grade.

Failure at any gate rejects that realization; it does not reject the 12-architrino inventory or authorize a different geometry automatically.
