## 1. The three method additions

(iii) Generalized first-cell bound: if $F$ is monotone of either direction and positive on $[0,u_1^2]$, then $U(\psi)/\psi=\tfrac1\psi\int_0^\psi F$ lies between $F(0)$ and $F(\psi)$, hence between $F(0)$ and $F(u_1^2)$, and $I=2/\sqrt{2gU/\psi}$ lies between $I_0$ and $I_0/\sqrt{F(u_1^2)/F(0)}$; the code takes the outer hull of the two and requires $\inf F>0$ on the cell: derived. ## 2. Residual monotonicity (derived, with its scope)

$d\,\text{chord}_j/dt=(\mathbf T\cdot\hat{\mathbf r}_j)\dot q$ with $|\mathbf T\cdot\hat{\mathbf r}_j|\le1$, so on an interval where $\dot q\le v_{\max}<1$ every chord is Lipschitz in $t$ with constant $v_{\max}$, the minimum of such functions is too, and $r(t)=t-\min_j\text{chord}_j$ increases by at least $(1-v_{\max})\Delta t$: strictly increasing, unique root, rigorous bracketing — derived, whatever the chord signs. Scope: the argument holds only while the motion is sub-wake up to the root. For the crossing-first cells the fixed-image window ends (antiprism 30° at speed $1.93$, 45° at $1.37$, 60° at $1.05$, six poles at $1.15$, twist 40 at $1.03$) lie past the crossing, where the argument does not apply; those brackets still contain a root by the intermediate value theorem (sign-definite endpoints, $r$ continuous) but the first-root property is not established there.
