Hence on this interval both runs read only $h$: for each class the delay $\tau_c(t,x)$ is the unique root of $\tau=r_c(x,h(t-\tau))$ (unique because $h$ is sub-wake: $\partial_\tau(\tau-r_c)=D_c>0$), a function of the CURRENT coordinate $x$ and $t$ only, and the delayed state is $(h,\dot h)(t-\tau_c(t,x))$. Both runs are therefore delay-free non-autonomous second-order equations with no dependence on the current velocity:
$$\ddot q=g\,[\Phi(t,q)+A(t,q)],\qquad \ddot q_h=g\,[\Phi(t,q_h)+H(q_h)],$$
where $\Phi(t,x)=2a_U+2a_L$ evaluated at $(x,h(t-\tau_U),\dot h(t-\tau_U))$ and $(x,h(t-\tau_L),\dot h(t-\tau_L))$ is the SAME function in both runs, $H(z)=\sin\tfrac z2/(4\cos^2\tfrac z2)$ is the fixed antipode hit, and $A(t,x)=H(x-p)/D_A$ with $p=h(t-\tau_A(t,x))\ge0$, $D_A=1+\dot h(t-\tau_A)\sin\tfrac{x-p}2\ge1$ is the moving antipode hit (for $t<t_a$: $p=0$, $D_A=1$, $A=H$).

**Exact decomposition.** With $\Psi(t,x)=\Phi(t,x)+H(x)$ (the held right-hand side as a function of the current coordinate),
$$\ddot\delta=g[\Psi(t,q)-\Psi(t,q_h)]-g[H(q)-A(t,q)]=k(t)\,\delta-\varepsilon(t),$$
$k(t)=g\,\partial_x\Psi(t,\xi(t))$ at an intermediate $\xi(t)$ between $q_h(t)$ and $q(t)$ (mean value theorem; $\Psi(t,\cdot)$ is smooth on the segment by the lemma), and the deficit is evaluated at ONE state, the canonical one:
$$\varepsilon(t)=g\Big[H(q)-\frac{H(q-p)}{D_A}\Big]=g\Big[H'(\zeta)\,p+H(q-p)\,\frac{D_A-1}{D_A}\Big]\ \ge0,\qquad \zeta\in[q-p,q],\quad H'(z)=\frac{1+\sin^2\tfrac z2}{8\cos^3\tfrac z2}>0,$$
zero for $t\le t_a$ and strictly positive for $t>t_a$ (then $p>0$ and $\dot h>0$). The second form (mean value in $H$) is the one evaluated, because the direct subtraction loses the sign on a step box (its interval widths, about $5\times10^{-5}$, exceed the deficit; recorded per step as `eps_naive_subtraction`). There is no $\dot\delta$ term and no delayed-state Lipschitz term: the delayed states are functions of the current coordinate on the common history, and their variation is inside $\partial_x\Psi$: for a meridional class $\partial_x[a(x,h(s),\dot h(s))]_{s=t-\tau(t,x)}=a_q-(a_{q'}\dot h+a_{v'}\ddot h)\,\partial_x\tau$, $\partial_x\tau=r_q/D$, with $\ddot h=gF(h)$ (the delayed arguments lie in the fixed-image window).

**Comparison lemma.** Let $\ddot\delta=k\delta-\varepsilon$ on $[0,T]$, $\delta(0)=\dot\delta(0)=0$, $\varepsilon\ge0$, $|k|\le K$, $\kappa=\sqrt K$, $I_\varepsilon(t)=\int_0^t\varepsilon$, $E_2(t)=\int_0^t(t-s)\varepsilon(s)\,ds$. From $\delta(t)=\int_0^t(t-s)[k\delta-\varepsilon]\,ds$, $|\delta|$ is dominated by the solution $M$ of $\ddot M=KM+\varepsilon$, $M(0)=\dot M(0)=0$ (monotone Picard iteration of the Volterra inequality), $M(t)=\int_0^t\varepsilon(s)\,\tfrac{\sinh\kappa(t-s)}{\kappa}\,ds\le\sigma\,t\,I_\varepsilon(t)$, $\sigma=\sinh(\kappa T)/(\kappa T)$. Therefore
$$\dot\delta(T)=-I_\varepsilon(T)+\int_0^Tk\,\delta\ \in\ -I_\varepsilon(T)+[-1,1]\,\frac{K\sigma T^2}{2}\,I_\varepsilon(T),\qquad \delta(T)\in-E_2(T)+[-1,1]\,\frac{K\sigma T^3}{6}\,I_\varepsilon(T),$$
i.e. $\dot\delta(T)\in-I_\varepsilon(T)\,[1-\eta,1+\eta]$ with $\eta=K\sigma T^2/2$. The same bound at any $t\in(t_a,T]$ gives $\dot\delta(t)\le-(1-\eta)I_\varepsilon(t)<0$ and $\delta(t)<0$ whenever $\eta<1$: **a trajectory ordering, not only a same-state inequality** (the same-state theorem supplies $\varepsilon\ge0$; the lemma converts it into an ordering of the two trajectories on this interval).
