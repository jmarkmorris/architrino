## 1. How the dimensionless quantities scale with $g$ (derived)

The unit-sphere kernels are $g$-free: the five-fixed-image field $F(q)$, its potential $U(q)$ with $F=U'$, the per-hit tangent $N_c/(r_c^3D_c)$ and radial $\sigma_{\rm hit}/(2r_cD_c)$ kernels, the chords, the root equations $\tau=r_c(q(t),q(t-\tau))$ and the transmitter factors depend on $(q,q',v')$ only. The coupling enters as the overall factor in the law, $\ddot q=g[\,F(q)-\sum_{\rm read}a^{\rm tan,fixed}_c+\sum_{\rm read}a^{\rm tan}_c\,]$ (unit bracket), and in $a_{\rm rad}=g[1/(r_LD_L)-1/(r_UD_U)-1/(2r_AD_A)]$; the support is $\hat\lambda=-\dot q^2-a_{\rm rad}$, physical $\lambda=g\hat\lambda$. Release: $a_{\rm rad}(0)=-g/4$, $\hat\lambda(0)=+g/4$ ($1/32$ at $R=8$, $1/40$ at $R=10$, $1/48$ at $R=12$), physical $\lambda(0)=g^2/4=K/(2R)^2$ ($1/256$, $1/400$, $1/576$): the sphere must push outward by exactly the antipode's pull at distance $2R$. In the model control of section 4, where the history is prescribed, $a_{\rm rad}$ and $da_{\rm rad}/dt$ are exactly proportional to $g$ (measured: $R\,a_{\rm rad}$ and $R\,da_{\rm rad}/dt$ identical to 25 digits across $R=8,10,12$).

Fixed-image window (all sources read as their stationary release images, $s\le0$): $\ddot q=gF(q)$ from rest, so with $\theta=\sqrt g\,t$ the window solution is $q(t;g)=Q(\sqrt g\,t)$ where $Q''=F(Q)$, $Q(0)=Q'(0)=0$ is one $g$-free profile; $\dot q=\sqrt g\,Q'(\theta)$, $\dot q^2=2gU(q)$ (the energy identity used by the wave-14/15 machinery, which indeed takes $g$ as a parameter). Window-end equation (first unlike reading): $t=\sqrt{2-\sqrt2\sin q(t;g)}$, i.e. $t=\sqrt{2-\sqrt2\sin Q(\sqrt g\,t)}$: the chord side is $g$-free, the coordinate side slows with $\sqrt g$. Leading order ($Q(\theta)\approx\theta^2/2$, $F(0)=1$): $t^2(1+g/\sqrt2)\approx2$, $T^*(g)\approx\sqrt{2/(1+g/\sqrt2)}$, giving $1.3667$ at $R=10$ (the certified $T^*=1.36668$, so the leading order is accurate to $10^{-4}$ there), $\approx1.3555$ at $R=8$ and $\approx1.3743$ at $R=12$, with $q(T^*)\approx gT^{*2}/2$: $0.1149$, $0.0934$, $0.0787$ (inferred; the continuation's enclosure decides). The like reading $t=\sqrt{2+\sqrt2\sin q}$ and the antipodal reading $t=2\cos(q/2)$ follow the same pattern: nearly $g$-free times, shifted by the coordinate reached.

Beyond the window the moving-source corrections break the exact $\sqrt g$ scaling only through the delayed states: at a given $q$ the delays are $g$-free chords, but $q'=q(t-\tau)$ and $v'$ depend on $g$ through the history, and $D_c-1\propto v'\propto\sqrt g$; the tangent and radial brackets remain $g$-free functions of $(q,q',v')$. So the scaling $q(t;g)\approx Q(\sqrt g\,t)$ is exact on the window and approximate (corrections of relative order $\sqrt g\times$ small) afterwards.
