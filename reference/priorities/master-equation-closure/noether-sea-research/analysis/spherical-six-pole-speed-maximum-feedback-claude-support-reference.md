## 2. Speed-maximum criterion (own derivation)

Derived. All six poles share $q(t)$ on congruent great circles of the unit sphere, so each pole's speed is $|\dot q|$ and $d|\dot q|/dt = \mathrm{sign}(\dot q)\,\ddot q$ wherever $\dot q \ne 0$. While $\dot q > 0$ is proved, speed and $\dot q$ have the same derivative $\ddot q$, the total tangent acceleration (all classes with multiplicities). Certificate of a local speed maximum: (a) $\ddot q > 0$ proved at one certified step end and $\ddot q < 0$ proved at a later certified step end; (b) every step between is certified (actual motion on both sides, no extrapolation); (c) $\dot q > 0$ proved on every step of the bracket; then by continuity of $\ddot q$ on the bracket (the delayed arguments stay inside continuous certified history, with separate one-sided treatment if a delayed argument crosses a record where the history rate jumps) there is a zero of $\ddot q$ with a sign change from positive to negative in the event-time bracket [earlier step end's LOWER time endpoint, later step end's UPPER time endpoint]; the speed at the maximum is enclosed by the hull of the step speed boxes over the bracket steps. Uniqueness: only if $d\ddot q/dt < 0$ is proved on every step of the bracket (then $\ddot q$ is strictly decreasing there and has exactly one zero); extended to a neighbourhood only as far as the proved negative rate extends. Distinctions kept: negative-to-positive crossing is a minimum; $\dot q$ reaching zero is a coordinate turn (there the speed derivative is $\mathrm{sign}(\dot q)\ddot q$ and the criterion above does not apply); a step end whose $\ddot q$ box contains zero is UNDECIDED and only widens the bracket to the nearest proved ends; a run that ends undecided or positive certifies no maximum. Detector controls (measured, slice5-iv E1 to E7 on synthetic step sequences, all passed before any target): maximum detected with the full-uncertainty bracket and speed hull; minimum reported as minimum; zero-containing boxes reported undecided and never as an adjacent crossing; qdot through zero reported as a coordinate turn; uniqueness refused when one rate box is undecided.

Which terms drive $\ddot q$ toward zero (derived from section 1 plus the step-214 values, hence an inference about the future): at the step-214 end the three tangent contributions are all positive: unlike in $[0.0099074, 0.0115724]$, like in $[0.0093788, 0.0096450]$, antipode in $[0.0074559, 0.0075697]$ (whole-step boxes of step 214, my frozen evaluation). The unlike numerator $N_U$ is about 0.054 and falling at about $-0.5$ per unit time ($\partial_q N\,\dot q + \partial_{q'} N\, v' \dot s$ with both partials negative), which accounts for nearly all of $d\ddot q/dt \in [-0.12030, -0.11331]$. So the expected mechanism is: the unlike tangent contribution passes through zero (a reversal, $N_U = 0$) and becomes negative; the speed maximum occurs later, when the negative unlike contribution cancels the still-positive like and antipode contributions (about 0.017 together).

## 3. Account identities and derivatives (signed general form)
