## (1) Structured search for tangent equilibria (independent of the symmetry census)

**Problem (derived).** Six members at rest on the unit sphere, polarities $(+,+,+,-,-,-)$ by index, static field $\mathbf A_i=\sum_{j\ne i}\sigma_{ij}(\mathbf X_i-\mathbf X_j)/|\mathbf X_i-\mathbf X_j|^3$ (the release law with a stationary past, $K=R=1$); a tangent equilibrium is a placement with $P_i\mathbf A_i=0$ for all $i$. Objective $f=\max_i|P_i\mathbf A_i|$ over the twelve spherical angles. Method: Nelder–Mead (adaptive, up to 6000 iterations) on $f$, then Levenberg–Marquardt (Newton-type least squares) on the twelve tangent residual components (two per member in a local orthonormal tangent basis). Endpoints are compared up to rotation and polarity-preserving permutation through the labelled chord multiset (PP, PE, EE classes, rounded to $10^{-4}$). Script `evidence/spherical-curvature-claude-coverage-wave7-equilibrium-search.py`, output `.out`, `.json`.

**Starts (recorded).** (a) The alternating hexagon perturbed by $0.1$ rad on every member (random tangent directions from `numpy.random.default_rng(20261011)`); (b) octahedron class A (canonical six poles: antipodal pairs of opposite polarity) and class B (one $++$ antipodal pair, one $--$, one mixed); (c) twelve random placements from the same generator stream under the $0.3R$ chord rule.

**Controls (all pass, recorded before the target starts were interpreted).** Octahedron class A is not an equilibrium: $f=1$ exactly ($|P\mathbf A|=K/R^2$ at every pole, wave-1 F1).
