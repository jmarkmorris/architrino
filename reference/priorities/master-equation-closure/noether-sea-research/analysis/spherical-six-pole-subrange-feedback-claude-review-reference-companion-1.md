So the true rate jumps at the three first-reading times: the unlike class at $T^*$ (the window end), the like class at the time $t=r_L(q(t),0)$ (near $1.46$, inside the wave-16 records, inferred location), and the antipode class at $t=r_A(q(t),0)$ (near $1.99$, inside the antipodal records, inferred location). The sub-range rule needs only this weaker premise: on the step, $\ddot q$ is Lipschitz and its derivative, where it exists, lies in the stored $a_p$. Then $\ddot q(t_s+\sigma)-\ddot q(t_s)=\int_0^\sigma\dddot q\in a_p\sigma$ still holds and the three inclusion lines follow unchanged.
