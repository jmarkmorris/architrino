## (2) D1 on its stationary-source window (derived; quadrature measured)

**Window equation.** For $T$ below the first reception of a post-release emission, every root of every member reads a partner at its initial site (emission time $T-\tau<0$ with $\tau$ the chord), so the images are fixed, not co-moving: each member moves in the static field of the five fixed initial images of the others. The order-12 symmetry of the complete history keeps the motion in the family positrinos at $120k^\circ+\psi(t)$, electrinos at $50^\circ+120k^\circ-\psi(t)$, twist $50^\circ-2\psi$. For a receiver at azimuth $\psi$ on the equator and a fixed source at relative azimuth $\delta$ (chord $2\sin(\delta/2)$), the azimuthal component of the per-hit acceleration is $\sigma_{\text{rel}}G(\delta)$ with $G(\delta)=\cos(\delta/2)/(4\sin^2(\delta/2))$ (positive toward increasing azimuth for an attracting source ahead; $G$ is odd about $\delta=\pi$). Hence
$$
\ddot\psi=g\,F(\psi),\qquad F(\psi)=\sum_{j=0}^{2}G\!\left(50^\circ+120j^\circ-\psi\right)-\sum_{j=1}^{2}G\!\left(120j^\circ-\psi\right),
$$
with $F(0)=0.668064615$ (my value; the definition's closed form $S(50^\circ)=0.66806$ agrees, and the instrument's $|P\mathbf A|=0.668065$ is the same number). **First integral.** $G$ is the derivative of the inverse chord: $\int G\,d\delta=-1/(2\sin(\delta/2))$, so $F=-d\Phi/d\psi$ with $\Phi(\psi)=\sum_{\text{sources}}\sigma/\text{chord}(\psi)$ (energy in the fixed field), and $\tfrac12\dot\psi^2=g\,[\Phi(0)-\Phi(\psi)]=:g\,U(\psi)$ with $\dot\psi(0)=0$. **Quadrature.** $t(\psi)=\int_0^\psi d\psi'/\sqrt{2gU(\psi')}$; with $\psi=u^2$ the integrand is smooth and has the analytic limit $2/\sqrt{2gF_0}$ at $u=0$; $U$ is evaluated cancellation-free through $\text{chord}(\delta-\psi)-\text{chord}(\delta)=-4\cos\!\big((2\delta-\psi)/4\big)\sin(\psi/4)$. **Window end (self-consistent).** The first post-release emission is read when $T$ equals the chord from the receiver to its nearest fixed image, $T^*=2\sin\!\big((50^\circ-\psi(T^*))/2\big)$, solved by bracketing on $\psi$. Script `evidence/spherical-curvature-claude-coverage-wave9-d1-window.py`, outputs `.out`, `.json`.

**Controls (all pass; PI item 1: no integrator anywhere).** The $60^\circ$ twist is stationary ($F=1.4\times10^{-15}$); $dU/d\psi=F$ by finite differences at three points and the cancellation-free $U$ equals $\Phi(0)-\Phi(\psi)$ to $10^{-12}$; the sign convention is fixed by the requirement that positrinos accelerate toward their nearer electrino (a flipped $G$ gives $-0.668$ and is rejected); the small-$\psi$ series $t=\sqrt{2\psi/(gF_0)}\,(1-F_1\psi/(12F_0)+O(\psi^2))$ is reproduced by the quadrature with ratios $1.00000040$, $1.0000000041$, $1.00000000004$ at $\psi=10^{-3},10^{-4},10^{-5}$ ($O(\psi^2)$ remainder); and two independent rules, adaptive Gauss–Kronrod and Gauss–Legendre with 200 and 400 nodes, agree to $4\times10^{-16}$ at $\psi=5^\circ,10^\circ,15^\circ$ (a first version with $\Phi(0)-\Phi(\psi)$ evaluated by subtraction agreed only to $1.5\times10^{-10}$, which is why the cancellation-free form was adopted; recorded in the receipt).
