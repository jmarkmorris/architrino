## (b) The last right strip, closed

The strip $[-0.05,4]\times[-40,40]$ for out-of-plane $m=\pm1$ at $g=1/10$ could not be justified within the sample cap ($2.048\times10^6$ samples on the left edge) because $|h|$ is of order $10^{-3}$ there between the exact zero root and the damped root at $-0.1147$ while the Lipschitz bound is $80$. The remedy is a smaller strip justified by a modulus bound: for $\mathrm{Re}\,\Lambda\ge-0.05$ every exponential has modulus at most $e^{0.05\tau_k}$, so a root needs $|\Lambda|^2\le g\big(|a_0|+\sum_k|a_k|e^{0.05\tau_k}\big)$, that is $|\Lambda|\le0.4967$; hence every root with $\mathrm{Re}\,\Lambda\ge-0.05$ lies in the box $[-0.05,0.997]\times[-0.997,0.997]$. On that box the Lipschitz bounds are $2.2$ to $3.0$, $2000$ samples per side justify every segment, the winding count is exactly $1$, and the single Newton root in the box is the exact zero root. So the out-of-plane tilt mode at $g=1/10$ is certified: no root with $\mathrm{Re}\,\Lambda>-0.05$ other than the rigid-tilt zero mode. With this, every dominant root reported in waves 3–6 (Family A, in-plane $m=0,\pm1,\pm2,3$, out-of-plane $m=0,\pm1,\pm2$) is certified under the justified contour criterion. Claim grade: derived for the box bound, measured for the count. Falsifier: a root of the $m=1$ function with $\mathrm{Re}\,\Lambda\ge-0.05$ and $|\Lambda|>0.4967$ (impossible by the bound) or a segment record marked unjustified (none).
