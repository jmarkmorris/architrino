## (1) Outward-rounded four-step enclosure of A3-v0.5 (six poles, V1, $R=1$, $v=1/2$, H1)

**Design (derived).** The method is the frozen wave-2 (d) method unchanged in structure: four fixed Taylor steps of the decoupled static-field ODE $\ddot{\mathbf X}=\mathbf F(\mathbf X)-(\mathbf X\cdot\mathbf F)\mathbf X-(\dot{\mathbf X}\cdot\dot{\mathbf X})\mathbf X$ (on the sphere the selected law), each with the Cauchy-majorant remainder bound and Gronwall propagation between steps; no step is added. What changes is the arithmetic: every quantity is an `mpmath` interval (`iv`, 40 digits, directed rounding) — the source positions ($\sqrt3/2$ and the like are enclosed), the release tangents, every Taylor coefficient of position **and** of velocity ($V_k=(k+1)X_{k+1}$ is formed from the enclosed $X_{k+1}$), the series of $(\mathbf d\cdot\mathbf d)^{-3/2}$, the majorant constant $M$ (upper end taken), the step length $h=\theta\rho/M$ (lower end), the Cauchy tail $\rho\theta^{N+1}/(1-\theta)$ (upper end), the Lipschitz constant $L$ (upper end), the propagated error $\epsilon_{k+1}=\epsilon_ke^{Lh}+\text{tail}$ (upper end), the coefficient-domination self-check ($|c_k|$ upper end against $\rho(M/\rho)^k$ lower end), the ball-consistency condition, and the evaluation of the Taylor polynomial at $t=h$ (interval-valued). The reported speed enclosure is the interval $|\mathbf V_N(h)|-\epsilon$ to $|\mathbf V_N(h)|+\epsilon$ computed in interval arithmetic and then rounded outward to the printed digits. The only non-interval operations are the choice of the integer $N$ (any sufficiently large integer is valid; the tail is then bounded in intervals with that $N$) and the grid search for $\rho$ (any admissible $\rho$ is valid). Script `evidence/spherical-curvature-claude-coverage-wave6-enclosure-iv.py` (sha256 `77ca3416…`); it takes the cell name and member list and writes a JSON.
