## 2. Adjudication of the path-map angle and sign issue (derived, with a numerical spot check)

Componentwise, with $\mathbf X_1(q)=(\cos q,-\sin q/\sqrt2,-\sin q/\sqrt2)$: the inherited map puts $E_1$ at $-\mathbf X_1(p)=(-\cos p,+\sin p/\sqrt2,+\sin p/\sqrt2)$ (map A). The only unit-sphere map on the same great circle that yields a sum-of-angles chord is $-\mathbf X_1(-p)=(-\cos p,-\sin p/\sqrt2,-\sin p/\sqrt2)$ (map B), which differs from map A in the sign of the $\mathbf e_2,\mathbf e_3$ components for every $p\ne0$, i.e. it rotates $E_1$ in the opposite sense to $P_1$. Map B is not the inversion-with-polarity-swap image of the common $q(t)$ and would break the order-12 symmetry (the inversion must commute with the common rotation); so the sum chord belongs to a different history and cannot be substituted. For map A, $\mathbf X_1(q)\cdot(-\mathbf X_1(p))=-\cos q\cos p-\sin q\sin p=-\cos(q-p)$, $r^2=2+2\cos(q-p)$; for map B the product is $-\cos q\cos p+\sin q\sin p=-\cos(q+p)$, $r^2=2+2\cos(q+p)$. The transmitter sign follows the same split: for map A the velocity is $-v\mathbf T_1(p)$ and $\mathbf V\cdot\hat{\mathbf r}=-v\sin\tfrac{q-p}2<0$ (receding); for map B it is $+v\mathbf T_1(-p)$ and $\mathbf V\cdot\hat{\mathbf r}=+v\sin\tfrac{q+p}2>0$ (approaching). So the meridional reference's "sum, approaching" pair is internally consistent with map B and simply describes the wrong path.

Spot check (measured; instrument [pathmap-adjudication.py](../evidence/spherical-six-pole-antipodal-claude-support-pathmap-adjudication.py), output [.json](../evidence/spherical-six-pole-antipodal-claude-support-pathmap-adjudication.json), PID 18032, 0.06 s, peak RSS 20.2 MB, receipt in [support-commands.txt](../evidence/spherical-six-pole-antipodal-claude-support-commands.txt)): at $(q,p,v)=(0.2,0.1,0.2)$ map A gives $Y=(-0.995004,+0.070593,+0.070593)$, chord $1.9975005207$, $D_t=1.0099958338$, $a_{\rm tan}=0.0124021119$, $a_{\rm rad}=-0.247835503$, equal to my closed forms with the difference argument to the digits shown; map B gives $Y=(-0.995004,-0.070593,-0.070593)$, chord $1.9775421558=2\cos(0.15)$, $D_t=0.9701123735<1$, $a_{\rm tan}=0.0393901728$, $a_{\rm rad}=-0.260628683$. At $(0.175,0,0)$ both maps coincide (the fixed antipode, chord $1.9923486336$, $D_t=1$), which is why the error was invisible before any antipodal history was read. At the equal-angle state $(0.19,0.19,0.2)$ map A gives chord $2$, $D_t=1$, $a_{\rm tan}=0$, $a_{\rm rad}=-1/4$ exactly (the exact antipode), map B gives chord $1.9640$, $D_t=0.9622$. Verdict: the inherited family is map A; the chord argument is $q-q'$; $D_t>1$ (receding) for $q>q'$, $v'>0$; the meridional reference section 6 last sentence is refused. Falsifier: a componentwise expansion showing the inversion-with-polarity-swap image of $\mathbf X_1(p)$ is not $-\mathbf X_1(p)$ (it is, trivially), or any direct computation on map A yielding $D_t<1$ at $q>q'$, $v'>0$.
