## 2. The own-antipode root chart after the reading (derived)

Source $E_1$ at $\mathbf Y(s)=-\mathbf X_1(q(s))$, emission time $s=t-\tau$, $q'=q(s)$, $v'=\dot q(s)$. Emitter-to-receiver vector $\mathbf r=\mathbf X_1(q)+\mathbf X_1(q')$. Componentwise: $\mathbf X_1(q)\cdot\mathbf X_1(q')=\cos q\cos q'+\sin q\sin q'\,|(\mathbf e_2+\mathbf e_3)/\sqrt2|^2=\cos(q-q')$. Hence
$$r^2=2+2\cos(q-q')=4\cos^2\!\big(\tfrac{q-q'}{2}\big),\qquad r=2\cos\!\big(\tfrac{q-q'}{2}\big),$$
and the chord argument is the DIFFERENCE $q-q'$, not the sum. (A sum would make the chord shrink with a stationary source as $q'$ grows, which is not the geometry: the source sits at the antipode of $\mathbf X_1(q')$, and the chord from $\mathbf X_1(q)$ to the antipode of $\mathbf X_1(q')$ is the chord of the angle $\pi-(q-q')$.) Root equation: $\tau=2\cos((q(t)-q(t-\tau))/2)$. At $q'=0$ it reduces to the fixed chord $2\cos(q/2)$: the chart is continuous at the reading.

Source velocity: $\dot{\mathbf Y}=-v'\,\mathbf T_1(q')$. With $\mathbf T_1(q')\cdot\mathbf X_1(q')=0$ and, componentwise, $\mathbf T_1(q')\cdot\mathbf X_1(q)=-\sin q'\cos q+\cos q'\sin q=\sin(q-q')$:
$$\dot{\mathbf Y}\cdot\mathbf r=-v'\sin(q-q'),\qquad \dot{\mathbf Y}\cdot\hat{\mathbf r}=-\frac{v'\sin(q-q')}{r}=-v'\sin\!\big(\tfrac{q-q'}{2}\big),\qquad D_t=1-\dot{\mathbf Y}\cdot\hat{\mathbf r}=1+v'\sin\!\big(\tfrac{q-q'}{2}\big).$$
Sign: after the reading the emission is post-release with $q'\ge0$, $v'\ge0$ and $q>q'$ (the receiver's coordinate at the later time exceeds the source's at the earlier time because $\dot q>0$ on the certified history), so $\sin((q-q')/2)>0$ and $D_t>1$: the antipode RECEDES along the line of sight (its velocity has a negative component along $\hat{\mathbf r}$, the direction from emission point to receiver). This is the opposite of the "approaching" wording at the end of the meridional reference section 6 and that wording is refused by this derivation. Geometric reading: the antipode of $E_1$'s position is $\mathbf X_1(q')$, which lies behind $P_1$ on $P_1$'s own circle; $E_1$ moving so that its antipode advances toward $P_1$ is itself moving away from $P_1$. The weight $1/D_t$ is below one, so the moving antipode hit is weaker than the fixed-image value at equal chord, while the shrinking chord strengthens it; the net sign of the correction relative to the fixed term is not decided here (unresolved; the continuation's enclosure decides it).

Tangential hit on $P_1$ (unlike, attracting): $\mathbf A=-\hat{\mathbf r}/(r^2D_t)$; $\mathbf T_1(q)\cdot\mathbf r=\mathbf T_1(q)\cdot\mathbf X_1(q')=-\sin q\cos q'+\cos q\sin q'=-\sin(q-q')$, so
$$a_A(q,q',v')=\mathbf T_1\cdot\mathbf A=\frac{\sin(q-q')}{r^3D_t}=\frac{\sin(q-q')}{8\cos^3((q-q')/2)\,(1+v'\sin((q-q')/2))}>0,$$
which at $q'=v'=0$ is the fixed antipode term $\sin q/(8\cos^3(q/2))$ (continuity of the right-hand side at the reading). Post-reading law: $\ddot q=g[F(q)-2a_U^{\rm fixed}-2a_L^{\rm fixed}-a_A^{\rm fixed}(q)+2a_U+2a_L+a_A(q,q',v')]$ with $a_A^{\rm fixed}(q)=\sin q/(8\cos^3(q/2))$.

Root function and uniqueness: $h(\tau)=\tau-2\cos((q(t)-q(t-\tau))/2)$; $\partial_{q'}r=\sin((q-q')/2)$ and $dq'/d\tau=-v'$, so $dh/d\tau=1-\partial_{q'}r\cdot(-v')=1+v'\sin((q-q')/2)=D_t$, which is positive for every sub-wake history (indeed $D_t\ge1-|v'|>0$ in general, and $D_t\ge1$ on the post-release range with $q\ge q'$). $h$ is therefore strictly increasing on the whole admissible delay axis, including the pre-release part where $q'=0$ and $h=\tau-2\cos(q/2)$; $h(0)=-2\cos(q/2)<0$ and $h(\tau)\to+\infty$, so the antipode has exactly ONE root at every $t$, pre-release before the reading and post-release after it; no second root can appear while the history is sub-wake. Falsifier: a sub-wake history with two admitted antipode roots, or a sign of $dh/d\tau$ below zero at any admissible delay.

Self-root exclusion: a receiver with speed below $c_f=1$ on its complete history cannot receive its own emission (the distance from any past position is less than the elapsed time); the sphere theorem used in adjudications 14 and 21 applies unchanged while $\sup\dot q<1$ (the accepted history has $\dot q\le0.18875$ and the envelope of section 1 keeps it below $0.21$).

Root census after the reading on the sub-wake domain (derived): five partner images, each with exactly one root, each reading post-release generated history: two like ($P_2,P_3$, receding, $D>1$, repelling, read since $t_{\rm like}\in[1.46704,1.46775]$), two unlike ($E_2,E_3$, approaching, $D<1$, attracting, read since $T^*$), one antipode ($E_1$, receding, $D>1$, attracting, read from $t_{\rm anti}$); no self root; no pre-release root remains once the antipode's release emission has been read, because each class's root function is strictly increasing in $\tau$ and its unique root moves continuously to post-release emissions after the class's reading time. Post-release emissions of a class before its reading are excluded by the same monotonicity (the unique root is the pre-release one). The brief's six-source census (five partners) is complete: no other transmitter exists in the cell.

## 4. Radial support (derived identity; release control)

For every source on the unit sphere, $\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf r=\mathbf X_1\cdot(\mathbf X_1-\mathbf Y)=1-\mathbf X_1\cdot\mathbf Y=r^2/2$ (unit vectors), so the canonical radial part of one hit is $\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf A=\sigma_{\rm hit}\,(\mathbf n\cdot\hat{\mathbf r})/(r^2D_t)=\sigma_{\rm hit}/(2rD_t)$, independent of where on the sphere the source is, moving or not: the whole dependence is through the delayed chord and the transmitter factor. Dimensionless, with the coupling: $a_{\rm rad}=g\,[\,1/(r_LD_L)-1/(r_UD_U)-1/(2r_AD_A)\,]$ (two like sources at $+1/(2r_LD_L)$ each, two unlike at $-1/(2r_UD_U)$ each, one antipode at $-1/(2r_AD_A)$). Signed additional normal support, outward positive: $\hat\lambda=-\dot q^2-a_{\rm rad}$; physical $\lambda=(c_f^2/R)\hat\lambda$. Falsifier: any configuration at which the direct vector evaluation of $\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf A$ differs from $\sigma_{\rm hit}/(2rD_t)$ (checked in section 5).

Release control ($q=0$, all sources fixed, $D=1$): $r_L=r_U=\sqrt2$, $r_A=2$, so $a_{\rm rad}(0)=g\,[1/\sqrt2-1/\sqrt2-1/4]=-g/4=-0.025$ (inward), $\dot q=0$, $\hat\lambda(0)=+g/4=+0.025$ outward (physical $+0.0025$ with $c_f^2/R=1/10$): the like and unlike pairs cancel radially at release and the antipode's attraction alone must be supported. Expected order at the accepted endpoint (hand estimate, to be enclosed in section 5): $\dot q_0^2\approx0.03562$, $a_{\rm rad}\approx g[0.657-0.778-0.251]\approx-0.0372$, so $\hat\lambda\approx+0.0016$: close to zero relative to the terms, so the sign at and beyond the endpoint is a genuine question for the enclosures and may be undecided.

Acceptance criterion per step: the step must enclose $\hat\lambda$ over the whole step interval from the enclosed $\dot q$ range and the per-class enclosed delayed chords and transmitter factors (not point values), using the identity above or an independently derived equivalent; a sign is proved only if the whole-step enclosure excludes zero; an enclosure containing zero is reported UNDECIDED; a proved zero crossing requires a proved sign at one step end and the opposite proved sign at a later step end with the intermediate steps certified. An inward $a_{\rm rad}$ alone proves nothing about $\hat\lambda$. No physical provider is selected.
