## 1. Signed geometry on the new box (receiver $q \in [1.04, 1.50]$, source $q' \in [0, 1.20]$, source signed velocity $w' \in [-0.05, 0.45]$)

## 2. Radial account and its derivatives (signed general form)

Derived. Per class $c$ with multiplicity $m_c$ (2, 2, 1) and radial sign $\sigma_c$ ($-1$ unlike, $+1$ like, $-1$ antipode), the canonical radial component is $m_c\,g\,\sigma_c/(2 r_c D_c)$ (from $n \cdot (X - Y) = r^2/2$ on the unit sphere), so $a_r = g\,[\,1/(r_L D_L) - 1/(r_U D_U) - 1/(2 r_A D_A)\,]$. Its time derivative per class is $\frac{d}{dt}\frac{\sigma_c}{2 r_c D_c} = -\frac{\sigma_c}{2 (r_c D_c)^2}\,(\dot r_c D_c + r_c \dot D_c)$ with $\dot r_c = \partial_q r_c\, w + \partial_{q'} r_c\, w'\dot s_c$ and $\dot D_c = \partial_q D_c\, w + (\partial_{q'} D_c\, w' + \partial_{w'} D_c\, a')\,\dot s_c$, $\dot s_c = (1 - \partial_q r_c\, w)/D_c$; the source acceleration $a'$ enters only through $\partial_{w'}D_c = -c M/r_c$ (meridional) or $\sin((q-q')/2)$ (antipode). Then $dl/dt = -2 w a - da_r/dt$. These are the forms in my frozen instrument (contributions: `d_arad_dt`, `dlambda_dt`); nothing in them assumes a sign of $w$, $w'$, $M$ or $N$. The second derivative $d^2 l/dt^2$ (needed for uniqueness of a support turn) is NOT derived in this reference: it requires the derivative of the source acceleration $a'$ along the history, i.e. a jerk of the stored records, which my lookup does not enclose; any uniqueness claim for a support turn is therefore unresolved unless a later controlled instrument supplies it.

Measured controls on the NEW box (geomw-iv, before its target): (H1) exact hand case for the tangent rate (antipode, static source, $q = \pi/3$, $w = 2/5$: $dh_A/dt = 1/(6\sqrt3)$); (H2) EXACT HAND-KNOWN RADIAL case: all three classes with static sources at $q' = 0$, receiver $q = \pi/3$, $w = 2/5$, $a = 0$: $a_r = g\,[1/r_L - 1/r_U - 1/(2 r_A)]$ with $r_{U,L}^2 = 2 \mp \sqrt2\sin q$, $r_A = 2\cos(q/2)$, and $da_r/dt = g\,w\,[-\sqrt2\cos q/(2 r_L^3) - \sqrt2\cos q/(2 r_U^3) - \sin(q/2)/(2 r_A^2)]$: the instrument returns $a_r$, $da_r/dt$ and $dl/dt = -da_r/dt$ equal to the hand values to 35 digits ($da_r/dt = -0.026493490610981258205681880$ in this case); (K2, K3) NUMERICAL prescribed-history controls (Richardson differences of an exactly known two-segment history with a generated segment) at $t = 4.4$ ($q = 1.168$) and $t = 4.8$ ($q = 1.378$): $dl/dt$, $da_r/dt$, each $dh_c/dt$ and $da/dt$ agree to better than $10^{-28}$; (K2b) NUMERICAL control on a smooth history that TURNS, $q(s) = 1 + 0.3\sin(0.5(s-2))$, at $t = 6.6$ where the receiver $w < 0$ and the unlike source $w' < 0$: $da_r/dt$, $dl/dt$ and each $dh_c/dt$ agree to better than $10^{-30}$; negatives, each rejected: (W2) pre-seam source acceleration used beyond the seam, (W4) and (W7) source acceleration omitted (in the tangent rate and in $da_r/dt$), (W5) and (W6) delayed velocity sign flipped (in the tangent rate and in $da_r/dt$).

## 3. Event criteria (own derivation)

PATH TURN (derived). All six poles share $q(t)$, so each pole's path coordinate is $q$ and its speed is $|w|$. If $w > 0$ is proved at one certified step end and $w < 0$ at a later one, with all steps between certified, continuity of $w$ gives a zero of $w$ in the event bracket [earlier end's lower time endpoint, later end's upper time endpoint]. At that zero $q$ stops increasing and starts decreasing: a local MAXIMUM of $q$ (if the zero is isolated). The speed $|w|$ is zero there, which is its smallest possible value: a local MINIMUM of speed, not a speed maximum; the speed derivative is $\mathrm{sign}(w)\,a$, undefined at $w = 0$, so the speed-maximum criterion of the last campaign does not apply. Uniqueness: only if $a = dw/dt$ is proved negative on every step of the bracket (then $w$ is strictly decreasing and has one zero). Enclosures to report over the whole bracket: $q$ (hull of the step path boxes) and $|w|$ (from 0 to the largest $|w|$ of the step boxes). A $w$ box containing zero, flat or shrinking boxes, or a trend prove nothing. If $w$ becomes negative the law continues unchanged (signed forms of section 1; $D_c$ labels then follow the sign of the source velocities, which turn later).

SIGNED-SUPPORT TURNING POINT (derived). $l$ is continuous; where $dl/dt$ exists, a proved negative $dl/dt$ at one certified step end (or over a whole step) and a proved positive one at a later end gives a point in the bracket where $l$ stops decreasing and starts increasing: a local MINIMUM of the signed support. While $l < 0$ (support inward) that is a local MAXIMUM of the inward magnitude $|l|$. A positive-to-negative crossing is a local MAXIMUM of $l$. Not support turns: a zero of $l$ (sign change of the support itself), a zero of $a$, a zero of the geometric term $-w^2$'s derivative $-2wa$, an undecided $dl/dt$ box. Seams: $dl/dt$ contains $da_r/dt$, which contains the source acceleration $a'$; $a'$ is continuous along certified history except at the release seam ($T^*$), where only one-sided values exist; at such a seam I compare the left and right one-sided signs and report a sign change across it as a one-sided (corner) extremum of $l$ at that instant. Uniqueness: only from a proved one-signed $d^2 l/dt^2$ over the bracket (not available, section 2) or another monotonicity proof; otherwise the turning point is certified as existing in the bracket, uniqueness unresolved.

Detector controls (measured, slice6-iv E9 to E17 on synthetic sequences, all passed before any target): path turn detected with its bracket and NOT called a speed maximum; reverse crossing reported as a reverse turn; flat or zero-containing $w$ boxes undecided; $dl/dt$ negative-to-positive reported as a signed-support minimum and, with $l < 0$ on the bracket, as a maximum of inward magnitude; positive-to-negative as a signed-support maximum; a support zero and a zero of $a$ (with $dl/dt$ one-signed) not reported as support turns; undecided $dl/dt$ stays undecided; a seam handled one-sidedly. The earlier controls E1 to E8 (speed maximum, wrong boundary, uniqueness, join intersection) rerun unchanged.
