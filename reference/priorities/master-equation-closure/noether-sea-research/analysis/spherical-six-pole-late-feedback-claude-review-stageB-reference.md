## 1. Window-end equation and the first reception (derived)

From rest with the stationary complete past, the fixed-image reduction is $\ddot q=gF(q)$ with the five-image field $F$ ($F(0)=1$, $F$ slowly increasing on $[0,0.16]$; interval bounds $[F_{\rm lo},F_{\rm hi}]$ evaluated per cell in section 5), $q(0)=\dot q(0)=0$. Envelope on the window: $q(t)\in[gF_{\rm lo}t^2/2,\ gF_{\rm hi}t^2/2]$, $\dot q(t)\in[gF_{\rm lo}t,\ gF_{\rm hi}t]$ (derived from $\ddot q\in g[F_{\rm lo},F_{\rm hi}]$ while $q$ stays in the box). The window ends at the first reception of a post-release emission: the unlike images at chord $r_U(q,0)=\sqrt{2-\sqrt2\sin q}$ (decreasing in $q$), the like at $\sqrt{2+\sqrt2\sin q}$ (increasing), the antipode at $2\cos(q/2)$; the unlike chord is the smallest, so $T^*$ solves $t=\sqrt{2-\sqrt2\sin q(t)}$ (the wave-15 definition), and the like and antipode chords at $T^*$ exceed $T^*$ (checked per cell). Because $q$ grows like $gt^2/2$, a larger $g$ gives a slightly smaller $T^*$ and a larger $q(T^*)$; $T^*$ stays near $1.37$ for all three cells (the dependence enters only through $\sin q(T^*)\approx g\,T^{*2}/2$). Bracketing: $f(t)=r_U(q(t),0)-t$ is strictly decreasing; evaluating it with the upper $q$ envelope proves "before" and with the lower envelope proves "after"; bisection gives $[T^*_{\rm lo},T^*_{\rm hi}]$ and the state brackets there.

## 3. Ordering criterion (the criterion the review will apply)

An ordering verdict "support zero before antipodal reception" (or the reverse) is admitted only when BOTH events are certified by opposite proved signs at adjacent certified step ends: the zero by $\hat\lambda$ proved positive at one step end and proved negative at the next (step-end enclosures with delays re-enclosed at the end state), the reception by the residual $2\cos(q/2)-t$ proved positive then proved negative; each event then has a certificate interval of one step width; the verdict holds only if the two certificate intervals are DISJOINT (the later event's interval lies entirely above the earlier's); overlapping intervals give UNDECIDED, to be narrowed only by a finer fixed sub-plan stated before its run (as in the antipodal campaign's $0.001$ sub-plan), never by diagnostics. Uniqueness of the zero on the certified range requires the pre-target-controlled derivative enclosure; without it the verdict is about the certified zero, not "the" zero.
