It is a mathematical inclusion argument, not a new evolution instrument or a claim of measured width reduction. The same trajectory and uncertainty remain.

Let a previously certified slab start at the true time $\theta\in[a,b]$, with fixed duration $d>0$. Its actual initial coordinate, velocity and acceleration are contained respectively in $Q_0,V_0,A_0$, and its scalar acceleration derivative is bounded throughout by the interval $J$. The latter is a bound on $q'''$, not a replacement evolution law. These hypotheses must belong to the original slab certificate and initial-state provenance. A stored whole-slab coordinate box alone does not supply them.

For any true query time $s\in[u,w]$ that belongs to this slab, put $h=s-\theta$. Its possible offsets belong to

$$
H=[\max(0,u-b),\ \min(d,w-a)].
$$

If the lower endpoint exceeds the upper, this slab cannot contribute. Otherwise every possible offset is nonnegative and lies in $H$. The uncertain origin is preserved by subtracting the interval $[a,b]$ before clipping to the certified duration. Setting $\theta=a$ would exclude possible smaller offsets and is not justified.

Repeated integration of the acceleration derivative gives

$$
q''(\theta+h)=q''(\theta)+\int_0^h q'''(\theta+z)\,dz,
$$

$$
q'(\theta+h)=q'(\theta)+h q''(\theta)+\int_0^h(h-z)q'''(\theta+z)\,dz,
$$

$$
q(\theta+h)=q(\theta)+h q'(\theta)+\frac{h^2}{2}q''(\theta)+\frac12\int_0^h(h-z)^2q'''(\theta+z)\,dz.
$$

All integration weights are nonnegative. Thus, with outward interval operations and powers evaluated on the nonnegative $H$, valid enclosures are

$$
\mathcal A(H)=A_0+JH,\qquad
\mathcal V(H)=V_0+A_0H+\frac12 JH^2,
$$

$$
\mathcal Q(H)=Q_0+V_0H+\frac12 A_0H^2+\frac16 JH^3.
$$

The initial intervals and $J$ can be correlated. Dropping those correlations only enlarges the result; it does not invalidate containment. The formulas do not allow a narrower $Q_0,V_0$ than the original certificate. Reconstruct initial enclosures from actual saved starts or the preceding certified endpoint, with printed-string padding and exact time alignment, rather than using a final state as the initial one. Each candidate slab uses its own origin and data. A query that may belong to several slabs is enclosed by the hull of their valid local enclosures. At the uncertain original-window boundary, the original window and every potentially applicable slab contribute. Complete coverage must be proved independently; an empty candidate set or an uncovered part of a query is refusal, not a zero contribution.

Where the old whole-slab boxes $S,V_S,A_S$ are independently known to contain the same true coordinate, velocity and acceleration, intersections with the corresponding new enclosures are valid. This requires matching the exact physical history/slab, not just overlapping numeric values. An empty intersection is a contradiction in the premises or implementation and must not be repaired by choosing one side. The inclusion proof establishes no promised width improvement: dependence losses, inherited endpoint widths and uncertain joins may still dominate.
