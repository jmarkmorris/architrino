## D1: twisted planar hexagon at rest, twist $\phi=50^\circ$

Definition: $P_k$ at azimuth $120k^\circ$, $E_k$ at $50^\circ+120k^\circ$ on the equator, all at rest for $s\le0$, released from rest.

Symmetry (derived): $C_3$ about $\hat{\mathbf z}$ (polarity preserving); the reflection in the vertical plane at azimuth $25^\circ$ composed with the polarity swap ($\theta\to50^\circ-\theta$ maps $P_0\leftrightarrow E_0$, $P_1\leftrightarrow E_2$, $P_2\leftrightarrow E_1$); the equatorial reflection; order 12, transitive. Consequences while the ordinary-chart continuation is unique: motion planar, six equal speeds, and the configuration stays in the one-parameter family $P_k$ at $120k^\circ+\psi(t)$, $E_k$ at $50^\circ+120k^\circ-\psi(t)$, twist $\Phi(t)=50^\circ-2\psi(t)$. Falsifier: a group element preserving the history that this list omits, or one listed that fails on a label.

Release census (derived, stationary history): from a positrino, electrinos at relative azimuths $50^\circ,170^\circ,290^\circ$ at chords $2\sin25^\circ=0.845237$, $2\sin85^\circ=1.992389$, $2\sin145^\circ=1.147153$; positrinos at $\pm120^\circ$ at $\sqrt3$; delays equal chords, $D_t=1$, weight $1$; no self root.

Release acceleration (derived, closed form). Per-hit azimuthal component from a source at relative azimuth $\delta$ (source minus receiver, in $(0,2\pi)$) with polarity sign $\sigma$: $\sigma\cos(\delta/2)/(4\sin^2(\delta/2))$ along increasing azimuth, with the sign convention that for an unlike source ($\sigma=-1$) at $\delta=50^\circ$ the component is positive (attraction toward the nearer electrino); the two like partners at $\pm120^\circ$ cancel azimuthally. Hence at every positrino the azimuthal acceleration is $g\,S(50^\circ)$ with $S(\phi)=\sum_{j=0}^2G(\phi+120^\circ j)$, $G(\delta)=\cos(\delta/2)/(4\sin^2(\delta/2))$: $G(50^\circ)=1.268584$, $G(170^\circ)=0.021956$, $G(290^\circ)=-0.622475$, $S(50^\circ)=0.668065$; at every electrino $-0.668065$ (mirror-swap); $z$ component $0$; discarded radial part $-0.701017$ at every member. ## D2: hexagon in rigid rotation about the axis $\hat{\mathbf n}=(\sin30^\circ,0,\cos30^\circ)$ at $\omega=1/2$, prescribed for all $s\le0$

Definition: $\mathbf x_k(s)=\mathrm{Rot}(\hat{\mathbf n},\omega s)(\cos\theta_k,\sin\theta_k,0)$, $\theta_k=k\pi/3$, polarity $(-1)^k$. A prescribed path; its residual is the diagnostic.

Kinematics and symmetry (derived). Correction to the designer's symmetry statement: inversion with polarity swap preserves this history, because $\mathrm{Rot}(\hat{\mathbf n},\omega s)(-\mathbf x)=-\mathrm{Rot}(\hat{\mathbf n},\omega s)\mathbf x$ maps $k\to k+3$ with reversed polarity and the velocity $\omega\hat{\mathbf n}\times(-\mathbf x)=-\mathbf v$ consistently; the reflection in the $xz$ plane reverses the rotation sense and is not a symmetry. So the label symmetry group has order 2 with the three orbits $\{0,3\},\{1,4\},\{2,5\}$: speeds (and every residual) are equal within each antipodal pair, not across pairs; the instrument confirms the pairwise equality to $10^{-15}$.
