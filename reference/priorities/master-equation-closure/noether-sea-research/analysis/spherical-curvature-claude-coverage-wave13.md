## Affine-in-time model (derived)

On a step $[t_0,t_0+\Delta]$, $s=t-t_0$, the right-hand side $a(t)=g[F(\psi)-G(50^\circ-\psi)+Q(t)]$ of the wave-12 equation is enclosed as $a(t_0+s)\in a_0+a'_{\text{range}}[0,s]$ (mean value theorem), where $a_0=a(t_0)$ is evaluated at the narrow start data (the root $\tau(t_0)$ by bisection of the monotone residual at the point time, then $\delta_0$, $D_t$, $Q_0$), and $a'_{\text{range}}$ encloses $da/dt$ over the step from $da/dt=g[(F'(\psi)+G'(50^\circ-\psi))\dot\psi+Q']$ with $Q'=[G'(\delta_0)\delta_0'D_t-G(\delta_0)D_t']/D_t^2$, $\tau'=-\cos(\delta_0/2)(\dot\psi(t)+\dot\psi(t-\tau))/D_t$ (from differentiating the root condition), $\delta_0'=-\dot\psi(t)-\dot\psi(t-\tau)(1-\tau')$, $D_t'=-[\ddot\psi(t-\tau)(1-\tau')\cos(\delta_0/2)-\dot\psi(t-\tau)\sin(\delta_0/2)\delta_0'/2]$ and $\ddot\psi(t-\tau)=gF(\psi(t-\tau))$ from the window equation; $\tau$ over the step is enclosed as $\tau(t_0)+\tau'_{\text{range}}[0,\Delta]$ by a short fixed-point iteration, the delayed argument range and the history hulls ($\psi,\dot\psi$ monotone on the window, zero before release) follow. Then $\psi(t_0+s)\in\Psi_0+V_0s+a_0s^2/2+a'_{\text{range}}[0,s^3/6]$ and $\dot\psi(t_0+s)\in V_0+a_0s+a'_{\text{range}}[0,s^2/2]$; the step closes when these sets over $s\in[0,\Delta]$ lie strictly inside the candidate sets $S$, $VS$ used to evaluate the ranges (first-exit argument), and is certified when $\sup VS<1$ (sub-wake: $E_0$ root unique, no self root) and every other partner's chord over $S$ exceeds the step end (fixed images), as in wave 12. The gain over wave 12 is that the step's width growth is now of order $\text{width}(a_0)\Delta$ plus $\text{width}(a'_{\text{range}})\Delta^2/2$ in speed instead of $\text{width}(A)\Delta$ with $A$ the crude zeroth-order acceleration set.
