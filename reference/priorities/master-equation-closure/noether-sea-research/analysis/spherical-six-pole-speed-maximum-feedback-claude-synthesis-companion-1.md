1. **The geometry-box adequacy flag is recorded AND acts as a software refusal (measured by reading the code).** Finding F6 and item (2) of the closeout limits say that per-step adequacy of the wider geometry box "is recorded by the instrument, not imposed by a software refusal", following review adjudication 3 item 7. That is wrong for the target path. In the subject instrument version 3, [steps8-iv.py](../evidence/spherical-six-pole-speed-maximum-feedback-claude-continuation-steps8-iv.py) (`6d7bd1adb3cf2b972289d8512ce8b035cbbee4c51e7170aa03f2885db1b10653`), line 315 builds a list `fails` whose last entry is the flag `receiver_and_source_candidates_inside_geometry_box`, and line 316 reads: if that list is non-empty the step is marked uncertified, appended with the failure reason, and the loop breaks before the step's end is published or appended to the source history. So a step whose receiver or source candidates left the controlled box would have stopped the run uncertified. The flag is true on all 60 steps, so no result changes; the correction concerns the description of the software. The one place where the flag is recorded but not required is the restart-regression control step from step 213, which lies below the new receiver box by design (the control's own field says so). The frozen review bytes stand as written; this companion and the coordinator's record the narrower true statement. Falsifier: a target path in that file that publishes a step end without passing through line 316.

2. **Scope of the algebraic uniqueness argument (derived; premises stated).** Finding F6 says the coordinator's algebraic companion makes each tangent reversal unique "independently of any stored box", following review adjudication 4 item 5. The correct scope is narrower. The argument is independent of the stored ACCELERATION-RATE enclosures: negative partial derivatives of the tangent numerator on the chart, positive coordinate velocities of receiver and source, and a positive source-time rate make the numerator strictly decreasing along each meridional class, and a positive denominator gives the contribution the same zeros. But its premises (that the actual receiver and source coordinates stay inside the chart, that both velocities are positive, that the transmitter factor and separation are positive) are taken from the admitted state and history enclosures of this same certified run. It is therefore a separate analytical route to uniqueness, conditional on those attained premises; it is not independent trajectory data and says nothing about motion outside the chart or beyond $t\approx4.6067$. The rate-enclosure route to uniqueness (reviewer and support) and this algebraic route share the admitted states and differ in everything else. Falsifier: the actual motion leaving the chart, or either velocity or the source-time rate failing to be positive, on the interval where uniqueness is claimed.
