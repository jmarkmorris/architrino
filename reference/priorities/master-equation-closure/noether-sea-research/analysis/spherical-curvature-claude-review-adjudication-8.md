## 1. The fixed-image reduction and its scope for the seven rest releases

Derivation (review). A release from rest with a history identically stationary for $s\le0$ reads every partner at its initial site until the first reception of a post-release emission; on that window the representative receiver moves in the static field of the fixed images. If the release state has a symmetry group that is transitive on the labels and whose stabilizer of a member fixes exactly one tangent direction at that member, the member's path is the great circle in that direction and all six share one arc-length coordinate $q(t)$; the reduction is $q''=gf(q)$ with $f=\mathbf t\cdot\sum_j\sigma_j(\mathbf x-\mathbf s_j)/|\mathbf x-\mathbf s_j|^3$, and since the kernel is a gradient, $f=dW/dq$ with $W=\sum_{\mathrm{unlike}}1/|\mathbf x-\mathbf s_j|-\sum_{\mathrm{like}}1/|\mathbf x-\mathbf s_j|$ and $\tfrac12q'^2=g[W(q)-W(0)]$, $t(q_1)=\int_0^{q_1}dq/\sqrt{2g(W-W(0))}$, times scaling as $g^{-1/2}$: derived (the same structure as adjudication-6 for D1; the first integral is an identity of the fixed-source field, not an energy). Symmetry and images per family, checked by the review: six poles at rest, group of order 12 (axis permutations with inversion-and-swap; reference 4.2 and symmetry wave 1), stabilizer of $P_x$ the mirror through $\mathbf e_x$ and $\mathbf w$, invariant tangent direction $-\mathbf c_x$, path the great circle through $\mathbf e_x$ and $\mathbf w$; images $\mathbf e_y,\mathbf e_z$ (like), $-\mathbf e_x,-\mathbf e_y,-\mathbf e_z$ (unlike); $f(0)=1$ (the reference's $K/R^2$). ## 2. The two derivation boundaries

Window end $T_w$: the first reception at which the shortest root reads a post-release emission, $T_w=\min_j|\mathbf x(q(T_w))-\mathbf s_j|$ solved self-consistently; derived by the monotone root-residual argument of adjudication-6 section 3 (for $T<T_w$ every root is the chord to a fixed image, at least $T$). Wake crossing $t_c$: the first time the window speed $\sqrt{2g(W(q)-W(0))}$ reaches $c_f$; at $t_c$ the sub-wake census certificate lapses, and the ordinary chart ends there by the theorem reference's Step 4 applied at $T_1=t_c$ (the history up to $t_c$ is sub-wake-or-equal and on the sphere, chords bounded below): any continuation with uniformly bounded acceleration would keep the speed at most $c_f$ on an initial interval after $t_c$, on which the reduction is still the law (if $t_c<T_w$) and gives $q''=gf(q_c)>0$, so the speed exceeds $c_f$, a contradiction; hence no ordinary-chart continuation past $t_c$: derived, with the premise $f(q_c)>0$ (true in every case below) and $t_c<T_w$. The hard boundary of the derivation is $\min(T_w,t_c)$: derived (beyond $T_w$ the law becomes a delay equation whose continuation is open; beyond $t_c$ no ordinary chart exists). The subject states both correctly and calls them boundaries of the derivation, not properties of later motion. Falsifier: a lawful continuation with bounded acceleration past $t_c$ (would contradict the theorem reference), or a root reading a post-release emission before $T_w$.
