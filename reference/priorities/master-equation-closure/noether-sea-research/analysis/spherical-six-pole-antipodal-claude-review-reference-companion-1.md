## 1. Correction to section 2: the root function at zero delay (derived)

The frozen text states $h(0)=-2\cos(q/2)$. That is the value of $\tau-r$ at $\tau=0$ on the PRE-release chart only, where $q'=0$ is held; it is not the value of the actual root function. On the actual history, at $\tau=0$ the source coordinate is $q'=q(t-0)=q(t)$, so the chord is $r=2\cos((q-q)/2)=2$ and $h(0)=-2$. Corrected premise: $h(0)=-2<0$ for every $t$; $h$ is continuous and strictly increasing in $\tau$ (its derivative is $D_t=1+v'\sin((q-q')/2)>0$ on the post-release part of the delay axis and exactly $1$ on the pre-release part where $q'$ and $v'$ vanish, with $h$ continuous at the seam $\tau=t$ because $q(0^+)=q(0^-)=0$), and $h(\tau)\to+\infty$; hence exactly one root at every $t$, pre-release while $\rho(t)>0$ and post-release once $\rho(t)<0$. The sign and the uniqueness conclusion of the frozen section 2 survive unchanged; only the stated value of $h(0)$ is corrected. Falsifier unchanged.
