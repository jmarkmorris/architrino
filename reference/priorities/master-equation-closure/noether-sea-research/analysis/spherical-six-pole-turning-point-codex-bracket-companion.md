# Coverage-safe delay brackets on the same sphere

Derived October14,2026 after the v3b bracket repair and its independent adjudication, before root sees a proposed new search implementation. This is a reference for a bounded repair of the existing analytical delay lookup, not a new evolution solver, law or physical event rule. Its applicability depends on the same admitted sphere/history and ordinary sub-wake hypotheses.

In the campaign's scaled time and unit-radius coordinates, every chord rho between two points of the sphere is at most2. At fixed current receiver coordinate q define h(tau)=tau-rho(q,p(t-tau)). Where the source path is differentiable,

$$h'(\tau)=1+\rho_pv(t-\tau)\ge1-|v(t-\tau)|.$$

The chord is 1-Lipschitz with respect to source arc length. If the true source speed is bounded strictly below1 on the ENTIRE search interval, h is strictly increasing there. Piecewise smooth seams are allowed if the path is continuous and the same strict Lipschitz bound holds over the union. No positive-quadrant sign of a tangent quotient is needed. A strictly negative lower residual and a strictly positive upper residual enclose exactly one ordinary root. They do not license discarding an additional root in a domain where the speed premise fails.

Let the receiver time lie in the full uncertain interval[t_minus,t_plus], and let S_max be the guaranteed LOWER end of accepted source-history coverage, including all newly admitted slabs and their origins/padding. Choose a positive lower delay tau_minus satisfying tau_minus>t_plus-S_max. Then every lower-bracket read t-tau_minus lies strictly inside guaranteed coverage for every admissible t. Choose tau_plus=2+epsilon, epsilon>0, and require tau_minus<tau_plus. For every admissible receiver/source position on the sphere, h(tau_plus)>=epsilon>0. The source interval[t_minus-tau_plus,t_plus-tau_minus] must be covered by the exact stationary past and accepted generated history without any gap; no new source extrapolation is supplied by this argument. The upper bracket's positive residual can still be checked by the implementation independently of the geometric theorem.

The remaining obligation is h(tau_minus)<0 on the whole current receiver/time box, with a genuine covered-source lookup. If that residual is undecided or nonnegative, this construction does NOT certify a root and must refuse or use a separately justified covered lower delay; it must not clamp the root into the bracket. If tau_minus>=tau_plus, history coverage is inadequate for this bracket. A positive lower delay also does not silently remove an attained zero-delay collision: the required negative residual will fail in that case under the uniqueness premise. Every source acceleration/jerk used after finding the root keeps the existing inclusion/rate premises.

A lower endpoint above d must fail the strict-negative-residual check. A lower delay reading into a possible but unguaranteed history end must be refused. An artificial gap between retained records must be refused even when both outer endpoints are covered. A changed transmitter sign is not a repair of bracket failure and remains a negative control. Replay of an older root enclosed by both brackets is consistency evidence, not an independent check of this theorem. A zero chord belongs to a separate singularity/regularity analysis and is not repaired here.

This proof suggests replacing repeated fixed-chord offsets with a coverage-safe root bracket after its independent review and controls, if the existing narrower bracket becomes too short.
