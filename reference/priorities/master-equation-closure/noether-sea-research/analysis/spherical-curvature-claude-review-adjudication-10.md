## 2. Lipschitz certificate (Codex (a)); closure of the adjudication-8 item

Constant: for a unit-rate great circle, $\mathbf t'=-\mathbf x$ gives $|\mathbf t'\cdot\mathbf r|/d^3=(d^2/2)/d^3=1/(2d)\le1/d^2$ on the unit sphere ($d\le2$), and $\mathbf t\cdot\partial_q(\mathbf r/d^3)=1/d^3-3(\mathbf r\cdot\mathbf t)^2/d^5\in[-2/d^3,1/d^3]$; so $L=\sum_j(1/d_j^2+2/d_j^3)$ with $d_j$ the continuous per-image chord minimum is a Lipschitz constant of $f$: derived (the first term could be sharpened to $1/(2d_j)$). Defect, as Codex states: `lipschitz()` takes $d_j$ from the grid minimum, not the continuous minimum, and bounds no evaluation error, so the wave's $f_{\rm lb}$, $f_{\rm ub}$ and everything built on them are conditional as written. Repair (review run): $d_j^*=\min_{\rm grid}d_j-h/2$ (each chord is 1-Lipschitz in $q$ since $|d'|\le|\mathbf t|=1$), $E_f=64\epsilon\sum_j1/d_j^{*2}$ as a stated floating model (a few dozen correctly rounded operations per image term; `cos`/`sin` within 2 ulp assumed, as (A1) of adjudication-9), $f_{\rm lb}=\min_{\rm grid}f-Lh/2-E_f$. Repaired window values at $g=1$ / $g=1/10$: six poles $0.999363/0.999491$; antiprism 30° $1.673497/1.977741$; 45° $1.152639/1.167244$; 60° $0.945443/0.945586$; twist 40 $1.587464/1.588436$; twist 50 and 70 (in the direction of motion) $0.667316/0.667529$; Family C 5° $0.030020/0.055846$; 15° $0.091242/0.176271$. All positive, changed from the wave's values only in the fourth decimal. Ruling: after repair, "$f$ keeps its sign on $[0,q_{\rm hard}]$" is derived for all seven wave-9 cases and both Family C cells at both couplings, which closes the adjudication-8 item "positivity between samples unproved" for the non-D1 cases on their windows (the path beyond the window, including the measured first zeros and turning points, remains measured). The twist-50 term-by-term bound on $[0,25^\circ]$ rechecked by hand ($G(50^\circ)=1.2686$, $G(170^\circ)=0.0220$, $G(290^\circ)=-0.6225$, $-G(95^\circ)=-0.3107$, $-G(215^\circ)=+0.0827$; sum $0.440$): derived.
