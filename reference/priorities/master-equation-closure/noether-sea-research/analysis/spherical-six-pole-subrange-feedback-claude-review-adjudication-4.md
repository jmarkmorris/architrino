1. **How the whole-step boxes are constructed on a first-reading record (derived from the code).** In v5, `sub_box` returns `R["S"], R["VS"], R["acc"]` unchanged for a record flagged as containing a first reading (line 108). `R["S"]` and `R["VS"]` are the record's stored strings padded outward; `R["acc"]` is built in steps6 `rec_from_step` (line 59) as `a0 + ap*[0, Delta]`, the stored start acceleration plus the stored rate bound times the whole step. The stored `S` and `VS` are themselves the frozen step's Taylor images over the whole step: `S_new = Q0 + V0*s + a0*s^2/2 + ap*[0, s^3/6]` and `VS_new = V0 + a0*s + ap*[0, s^2/2]` with $s=[0,\Delta]$ (steps3-iv.py, function `step`; the same two lines are at wave-16 lines 128 and 129 and wave-17 lines 150 and 151), accepted when they lie strictly inside the candidate sets on which `a0` and `ap` were evaluated.

2. **Ruling: control (A) is NOT premise-free, for any of the three quantities.** The acceleration box is explicitly $a_0+a_p[0,\Delta]$, and the coordinate and speed boxes are the step's own Taylor images with the same $a_p$.
