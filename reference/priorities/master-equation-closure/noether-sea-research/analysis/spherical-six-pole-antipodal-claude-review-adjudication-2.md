## 1. Sections 1 and 2 (geometry and radial identities): derived, agreement

Chord $2\cos((q-q')/2)$ with the difference argument, $D_t=1+v'\sin((q-q')/2)>1$ (receding), tangential hit $\sin(q-q')/(r^3D_t)$, radial identity $\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf r=r^2/2$ hence per-hit $\sigma_{\rm hit}/(2rD_t)$ for every class, release $a_{\rm rad}(0)=-g/4$, $\hat\lambda(0)=+g/4$: identical to the review's frozen sections 2 and 4 and to the coordinator's reference (adjudication 1). The support's argument that the sum chord fails the equal-angle test ($q'=q$ must give the diameter $2$) is a second, independent refutation of the meridional reference's last sentence, and its wave-1 section 2 identifies the sum form with the map $Y=-\mathbf X_1(-p)$ that breaks the order-12 symmetry; the review agrees (the review's own reason was the antipode-of-$\mathbf X_1(q')$ geometry; the two reasons are equivalent). The support's sign claim $a_{\rm rad}<0$ on the admitted range rests on $r_L>r_U$, which for the moving chords requires $\sqrt2\sin(q+q')>\sin q\sin q'$; that holds for every $q,q'\in[0,\pi/2]$ with $q+q'>0$, so the claim is derived on the whole admitted range (the review adds the explicit condition). Ruling: admitted.
