## Families (derived from the law and the configurations)

On the stationary-source window every root reads a partner at its initial site (emission before release), so each member moves in the static field of the five fixed images, $\mathbf A=\sum_j\sigma_j(\mathbf X-\mathbf X_j)/|\mathbf X-\mathbf X_j|^3=-\nabla\Phi$ with $\Phi=\sum_j\sigma_j/|\mathbf X-\mathbf X_j|$. For a unit-speed path $\mathbf X(q)$ on the sphere with unit tangent $\mathbf T=d\mathbf X/dq$ the tangential acceleration is $F(q)=\mathbf T\cdot\mathbf A=-d\Phi/dq$, so $\dot q^2=2gU(q)$ with $U(q)=\Phi(0)-\Phi(q)$ ($=W(q)-W(0)$, $W=-\Phi$), and $F'(q)=-\mathbf X\cdot\mathbf A+\mathbf T^{\mathsf T}J\mathbf T$ with $J=\sum_j\sigma_j(I/r_j^3-3\mathbf r_j\mathbf r_j^{\mathsf T}/r_j^5)$ (since $\mathbf T'=-\mathbf X$). The wake crossing is where $\dot q=1$: $U(q_c)=1/(2g)$, which fixes $q_c$ exactly from the first integral; $t(q_c)$ follows from the quadrature. The window end is $T^*=\min_j\text{chord}_j(q(T^*))$ over the fixed images.
