## Question and selected law

Six architrinos, three positrinos and three electrinos, move on a fixed sphere of radius $R$ centered at the origin. Sphere curvature is $\mathcal C=1/R$, so a smaller sphere has higher curvature; the reference case is $R=1$, $\mathcal C=1$, initial speed $v=c_f=1$. The interaction is the canonical transmitter-weighted all-root Master Equation. Writing $\mathbf A_i$ for the complete canonical acceleration at receiver $i$ (sum over every admitted causal root of every transmitter, including lawful self roots), $\mathbf n_i=\mathbf X_i/R$ and $P_i=I-\mathbf n_i\mathbf n_i^{\mathsf T}$, the selected equation of motion is

$$
\ddot{\mathbf X}_i=P_i\mathbf A_i-\frac{|\mathbf v_i|^2}{R}\mathbf n_i .
$$

The first term is the tangent part of the interaction acceleration. The second term is the geometric centripetal bookkeeping that keeps a tangent path on the sphere in Cartesian coordinates; it is not the discarded radial interaction and not a physical confinement mechanism. The canonical radial interaction component $(\mathbf n_i\cdot\mathbf A_i)\mathbf n_i$ is discarded. Speed is free to change: dotting the equation with $\mathbf v_i$ gives $\tfrac{d}{dT}\tfrac12|\mathbf v_i|^2=\mathbf v_i\cdot\mathbf A_i$, the tangent work, so speed is constant only where the tangent work vanishes. Numerical instantiations use $c_f=1$ and $K=1$ for the physical fixed-coupling comparison, where $K=\kappa|q_iq_j|$ is the per-pair acceleration coefficient of the inverse-square kernel. No damping, cap, smoothing, polarity change, root omission, event law, contact law or tangential controller is selected.
