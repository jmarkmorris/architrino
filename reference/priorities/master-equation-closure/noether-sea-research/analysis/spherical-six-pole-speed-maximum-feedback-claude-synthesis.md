## 5. Account of results

This section says in plain words what the campaign found. The exact numbers and their grades are in section 3; nothing here adds a claim.

**The question.** Six architrinos start at rest at the poles of a sphere of radius 10 and move along great circles with a common speed that changes over time. Each one's tangent acceleration comes from where its partners were at earlier emission times. The preceding campaign certified the motion to dimensionless time $t\approx4.0067$, with the speed still rising but its growth nearly gone. This campaign asked whether the speed actually reaches a maximum, and when a partner's emission from the latest certified stretch of motion (beginning near $t\approx3.4067$) is first received.

**The speed reaches a maximum.** The total tangent acceleration is proved positive at one certified instant and negative two steps later, with the velocity positive throughout. So the speed peaks at a time between $4.2467$ and $4.2667$ (physical time between about $42.47$ and $42.67$), at a value between $0.38797$ and $0.39065$ of the field speed. The acceleration falls steadily across the whole stretch studied, so this is the only speed extremum between $4.0067$ and $4.6067$. After it the speed declines, and by the end it is provably below its peak level. The velocity stays near $0.38$, so the motion does not turn around.

**What causes it.** Three partner classes act on each architrino: the unlike pair on the neighbouring axes, the like pair, and its own antipode. Near $t\approx4.11$ the unlike pair's tangent contribution changes from pushing forward to pushing backward. This is a change of sign of the geometric numerator, not a passage of the transmitter factor through one; that factor stays above one throughout. The backward unlike contribution then grows until, at the maximum, it exactly balances the forward contributions of the like pair and the antipode. Later, near $t\approx4.56$, the like pair's contribution also turns backward, by a thin but proved margin. From then on only the antipode pushes forward.

**The latest stretch of history is received after the maximum, not before.** The unlike pair's emission time crosses the start of the latest stretch at a receiver time between $4.4267$ and $4.4367$, and for the following seventeen steps every unlike read lies wholly inside that stretch. The launch had guessed about $4.28$; both independent roles separately estimated about $4.43$ before seeing any result, because the unlike history age there is about $1.02$. The four events have disjoint brackets, so their order is certain: unlike reversal, speed maximum, reception of the latest stretch, like reversal. The maximum therefore happened while every partner was still reading older history. The like pair and the antipode have not reached the latest stretch at all; they read history that is about $1.75$ and $1.89$ time units old.

**Radial support.** The extra normal support needed to keep the architrino on the sphere, counted outward positive, stays negative and keeps strengthening through and after the maximum, from about $-0.085$ to about $-0.101$ dimensionless (one tenth of that in physical units). It has no zero and no turning point on the stretch. The discarded canonical radial part is inward but weakening toward zero. The support keeps growing anyway because the bending requirement, set by the square of the speed, is large and nearly constant near the peak. The support's rate of change is itself shrinking, so a turning point later is possible; it was not reached and is not claimed.

**How firmly this is known.** The maximum's sharpest bracket rests on step-end acceleration values that are much narrower than the next step's starting value for the same instant. The PI raised this; the reviewer then evaluated those instants with its own delay solver, lookup and formulas, with controls first, and reproduced the values on every step end. The narrow values are valid; the wider ones lose an exact cancellation. Two weaker brackets that do not use those values contain the sharp one and stand as fallbacks. Every statement depends on the interval-arithmetic library behaving as specified and on the previously accepted 264-record history. Agreement between the subject and replays of its own records is consistency, not independent evidence; the independent content is the two references frozen before any result, the reviewer's own endpoint evaluation and audit, and the support's separate evaluation.

**Not concluded.** Nothing beyond $t\approx4.6067$. No later extremum, no support zero, no turnaround, and no recurrence, confinement, energy or Noether-sea conclusion. No physical provider of the normal support is selected.
