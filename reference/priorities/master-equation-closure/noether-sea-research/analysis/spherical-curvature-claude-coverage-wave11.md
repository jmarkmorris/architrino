## Object and method (derived)

Challenge cell D1 at $g=K/(Rc_f^2)$, $R=c_f=1$: on the stationary-source window (wave 9) the motion is $\ddot\psi=gF(\psi)$ with $F(\psi)=\sum_EG(\delta_E-\psi)-\sum_PG(\delta_P-\psi)$, $G(\delta)=\cos(\delta/2)/(4\sin^2(\delta/2))$, relative azimuths $\delta_E=50^\circ,170^\circ,290^\circ$ and $\delta_P=120^\circ,240^\circ$; first integral $\dot\psi^2=2gU(\psi)$ with $U(\psi)=W(\psi)-W(0)$, $W=-\Phi$, $\Phi=\sum\sigma/\text{chord}$; $t(\psi)=\int_0^{\sqrt\psi}I(u)\,du$, $I(u)=2u/\sqrt{2gU(u^2)}$; window end $T^*=2\sin\big((50^\circ-\psi(T^*))/2\big)$. Script `evidence/spherical-curvature-claude-coverage-wave11-d1-window-iv.py`, mpmath `iv` at 30 digits (`iv.dps=mp.dps=30`), every operation directed-rounded; $U$ in the cancellation-free interval form $\sigma[\text{chord}(\delta-\psi)-\text{chord}(\delta)]/(\text{chord}(\delta)\text{chord}(\delta-\psi))$ with $\text{chord}(\delta-\psi)-\text{chord}(\delta)=-4\cos((2\delta-\psi)/4)\sin(\psi/4)$; $F'$ from $G'(\delta)=-(1+\cos^2(\delta/2))/(8\sin^3(\delta/2))$.

Enclosure of $t(\psi)$ on a partition $0<u_1<\dots<u_N=\sqrt{\psi_{\max}}$ of $u=\sqrt\psi$: on the first cell $[0,u_1]$, $u_1=0.005$, interval arithmetic verifies $F'>0$ on $[0,u_1^2]$, so $U(\psi)/\psi\in[F(0),F(u_1^2)]$ there and $I(u)\in[I_0/\sqrt{F(u_1^2)/F(0)},\,I_0]$ with $I_0=2/\sqrt{2gF(0)}$, a two-sided bound that replaces the series at the inverse-square-root endpoint (the substitution $\psi=u^2$ makes $I$ smooth with limit $I_0$). On every other cell the midpoint rule with the rigorous error $(\Delta u)^3\max|I''|/24$, where $I''=-2S'S^{-3/2}-uS''S^{-3/2}+\tfrac32uS'^2S^{-5/2}$ with $S=2gU(u^2)$, $S'=4guF(u^2)$, $S''=4gF(u^2)+8gu^2F'(u^2)$ is bounded by interval evaluation on the cell; the cell enclosures of $U$ and $F$ use monotonicity ($F>0$ on the cell makes $U$ increasing, $F'>0$ makes $F$ increasing, each verified in intervals), falling back to direct interval evaluation otherwise (the first validation run showed that direct evaluation of $U$ over a cell is too loose near $\psi=0$ by dependency; this is why the monotone enclosures were adopted, recorded in the receipt). Node bounds $t_{\text{lo}}(u_k)\le t(u_k^2)\le t_{\text{hi}}(u_k)$ accumulate the cell contributions with directed endpoints. Inversion: for a given $t$, $\psi(t)$ lies between the largest node whose upper time bound is at most $t$ and the smallest node whose lower bound is at least $t$; within that bracket $\psi(t)\in[\psi_a+(t-t_{\text{hi}}(\psi_a))v_{\min},\,\psi_a+(t-t_{\text{lo}}(\psi_a))v_{\max}]$ with $v=\sqrt{2gU}$ enclosed on the bracketing cell, intersected with the bracket; $\dot\psi(t)=\sqrt{2gU(\psi(t))}$ is then enclosed. Window end: the residual $r(t)=t-2\sin((50^\circ-\psi(t))/2)$ is strictly increasing because $\psi(t)$ is increasing ($\dot\psi>0$) and the chord decreases in $\psi$ on $[0,50^\circ)$, so its root is unique; it is bracketed by interval evaluations of $r$ at trial times and narrowed by bisection until the residual enclosure straddles zero. Floating premises: mpmath `iv` provides outward-rounded $+,-,\times,\div$, `sqrt`, `sin`, `cos`, `pi` at the set precision; partition nodes and trial times are exact decimal or dyadic numbers; no other arithmetic is used.
