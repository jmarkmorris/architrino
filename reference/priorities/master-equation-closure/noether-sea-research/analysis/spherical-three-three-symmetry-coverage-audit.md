## One useful selected unfinished derivation

**Proposed next task, not executed by this audit:** test a small initial positional phase offset of one member of the already accepted actual equatorial hexagon release, with all six initial speed magnitudes still $1/4$, old stationary sites fixed and a complete recent preparation. This fills the nearby-phase actual-motion gap without another prescribed-history pilot, a new solver, or arbitrary extension of an existing endpoint.

A concrete preparation candidate is to add $\delta H(u)$ to one member's recent phase, where $u=1+4T$, $H(u)=3u^2-2u^3$ on $[-1/4,0]$, zero earlier, and $0<|\delta|\le1/1000$. It changes the release position by $\delta$ while retaining the same release angular speed because $H'(1)=0$. Its preparation speed contribution is bounded by $6|\delta|$ and its velocity matches the stationary past at the join. This candidate is $C^1$ at the old join; if matching the earlier $C^2$ preparation regularity is desired, use a quintic endpoint-flat interpolation and derive its explicit derivative bound instead. These are proposed data, not yet an admitted target.

In the old-source interval, the unperturbed members and offset member should obey the same separately indexed static five-source angular function, evaluated at their own angles. The required proof is to reconstruct that statement with the correct omitted self label, give complete root/collision margins for the changed history, and bound their angle and speed differences until a common declared angle event. The accepted bound $f'>3/2$ near zero offers an analytical comparison route: a positional phase offset should generate a signed acceleration and finite-time speed difference even though the initial velocities match. For negative offsets the proof must extend the derivative strip by reflection and keep the common event/root interval explicit. This is a candidate route with a falsifier, not a completed sensitivity theorem.

The decisive deliverable would be a two-sided small-phase sensitivity bound, distinguishing spatial phase mismatch, speed splitting and persistence of equatorial planarity, together with an admitted common event endpoint. It would complete an actual-motion phase comparison selected by the plan. Independent reconstruction would remain necessary. No claim of recurrence, chaos, attraction, physical energy or uniform surface coverage would follow.

## Exact blockers and completion boundary

**Recurrence:** the current exact autonomous reductions cease when moving preparation arrives. The meridional return to its starting latitude has reversed velocity and a different recent history, so it is not a full-state return. A rotating geometric shape alone also does not give a period when its rate varies. A useful recurrence claim needs a complete ordinary-root continuation to a delayed-state return (with any rotation/permutation quotient declared), or a separately constructed periodic solution passing the full residual. Repeating the short-step existence proof to another arbitrary endpoint would not settle this selected question.

**Irregular surface coverage:** the accepted symmetric trajectories remain on meridians or the equator, and the small symmetry-breaking proof lasts only a short controlled interval. No theorem or retained actual trajectory supplies global root regularity, occupation statistics or a long observation window for a generic nonsymmetric release. The EOM capability inspection in [the dynamics owner](spherical-three-three-dynamics.md#scope-and-executable-capability) found no normal-constrained route in its named request/construction paths, and its governing contract excludes artificial guidance from accepted canonical evolution. The audit inherits that scoped finding; it does not claim a new whole-repository capability search. A new production solver or second evolution instrument is outside the present authorization.

**Energy mapping:** the accepted mathematical first integrals apply only to fixed old-source reductions and are not a full delayed-history energy account. Supplying $E\mapsto(R,s)$ requires both an admissible energy definition including the appropriate history bookkeeping and a branch/geometry-selection relation; an energy label alone need not select a unique history. Parameter rescaling and the pending six-cell comparison cannot fill those missing premises.

**Physical support:** the normal multiplier is an explicitly added acceleration and now demonstrably changes direction for the meridional release. No source medium, boundary mechanism, constitutive response, or free-confinement proof supplies it. Quantifying its sign does not derive such a provider. The plan explicitly leaves neighboring spheres and constitutive/Standard-Model interpretations to later investigations. A one-sided support mechanism would be a new scenario, not a reinterpretation of the accepted signed constraint.

**Singular prescribed charts:** the accepted $\beta=3$ fold and ordinary-side divergence are precise chart obstructions; no event continuation law was selected. They do not establish fate of an actual evolved assembly or license root deletion. More evaluations approaching the same fold would duplicate a resolved obstruction.

The useful immediate dependencies are therefore the already active six-cell independent comparison and, if selected by the coordinator, the bounded actual phase-perturbation derivation above. This audit does not declare the campaign complete, dispatch another agent, accept provisional results, or extend its scope or deadlines. Once those selected dependencies are disposed of, recurrence, irregular occupation, energy mapping and physical confinement remain substantive open questions with the stated missing evidence; idle time would not close them.
