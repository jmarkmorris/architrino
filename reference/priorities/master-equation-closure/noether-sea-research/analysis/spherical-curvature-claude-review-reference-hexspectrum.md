## Linearization (review derivation)

Tangent perturbations $\boldsymbol\xi_i$ in the bases $\{\mathbf t_i,\hat{\mathbf z}\}$ about the stationary hexagon with its identically stationary history. Per hit from $j$ at unperturbed delay $\tau=r_{ij}$: the kernel $\sigma\hat{\mathbf r}/r^2$ varies as $\sigma[(I-3\hat{\mathbf r}\hat{\mathbf r}^{\mathsf T})/r^3](\boldsymbol\xi_i(T)-\boldsymbol\xi_j(T-\tau))$; the transmitter factor $1/|D_t|\approx1+\hat{\mathbf r}\cdot\boldsymbol\xi_j'(T-\tau)$ gives $\sigma(\hat{\mathbf r}\hat{\mathbf r}^{\mathsf T}/r^2)\boldsymbol\xi_j'(T-\tau)$; the root-time shift is first order but enters only multiplied by the unperturbed transmitter velocity, which is zero, so it drops out at linear order; the projection varies as $\delta(P_i)\mathbf A_i^0=-a_0\boldsymbol\xi_i$ with $a_0=\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf A^0=-5/4+\sqrt3/3$; the curvature term is second order. Blocks are circulant in the rotating frames and $t$/$z$ decouple because every line of action is in-plane. Fourier mode $k$ (polarity sign absorbed by $\sigma_m=(-1)^m$):

$$
\Delta_k^t(\Lambda)=\Lambda^2-gD_t+g\sum_{m=1}^{5}\sigma_me^{ikm\pi/3-\Lambda\tau_m}(c^M_m-\Lambda c^N_m),\qquad
\Delta_k^z(\Lambda)=\Lambda^2-gD_z+g\sum_{m=1}^{5}\sigma_me^{ikm\pi/3-\Lambda\tau_m}r_m^{-3},
$$

with $\tau_m=2\sin(m\pi/6)$, $D_t=J_{\phi\phi}-a_0=29/8-5\sqrt3/18=3.143875$, $D_z=J_{zz}-a_0=-7/8-\sqrt3/9=-1.067450$, and (computed from the geometry, signs included) $\sigma_mc^M_m=(1.75,-0.240563,0.125,-0.240563,1.75)$, $\sigma_mc^N_m=(-0.75,1/12,0,1/12,-0.75)$, $\sigma_mr_m^{-3}=(-1,0.192450,-0.125,0.192450,-1)$ for $m=1..5$. Hand check of $m=1$: $\mathbf t_0=(0,1,0)$, $\mathbf t_1=(-\sqrt3/2,1/2,0)$, $\hat{\mathbf r}=(1/2,-\sqrt3/2,0)$, $c^M_1=[1/2-3(3/4)]/1=-7/4$, $c^N_1=3/4$, $\sigma_1=-1$.

Identifications. $(k=3,z)$: $\sigma_me^{im\pi}=+1$ for every $m$, so $\Delta_3^z=\Lambda^2-g[D_z-2e^{-\Lambda}-\tfrac{2}{3\sqrt3}e^{-\sqrt3\Lambda}-\tfrac18e^{-2\Lambda}]$, which is exactly the alternating latitude mode of the frozen [hexagon-mode reference](spherical-curvature-claude-review-reference-hexmode.md) (positrinos up, electrinos down): the symmetry worker's Family A mode is the $(k=3,z)$ block, not $(k=0,z)$, which is the uniform lift of all six (Family C's displacement pattern) with static coefficient $-a_0=+0.672650$. $(k=3,t)$: $\Delta_3^t=\Lambda^2-gD_t-g[(\tfrac72+\tfrac32\Lambda)e^{-\Lambda}+(\tfrac{5}{6\sqrt3}+\tfrac{\Lambda}6)e^{-\sqrt3\Lambda}+\tfrac18e^{-2\Lambda}]$ (static sum $D_t+\tfrac72+\tfrac5{6\sqrt3}+\tfrac18=7.25$); on the real axis every bracketed term is positive for $\Lambda\ge0$, so $\Delta_3^t(\sqrt{gD_t})<0$ and $\Delta_3^t\to+\infty$: a real growing root exists strictly above $\sqrt{gD_t}$, that is above $1.773$ at $g=1$, $2.508$ at $g=2$, $3.546$ at $g=4$ (derived). $(k=0,t)$: static sum $0$ (rigid rotation about $\hat{\mathbf z}$ is a zero at $\Lambda=0$); $(k=\pm1,z)$: rigid rotations about $\hat{\mathbf x},\hat{\mathbf y}$ are zeros at $\Lambda=0$.
