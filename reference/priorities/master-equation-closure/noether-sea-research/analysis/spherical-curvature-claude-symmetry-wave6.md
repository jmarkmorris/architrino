## (3) Census follow-up

**Nonconverged endpoints.** Of the 400 recorded seeds, 46 were skipped for an initial chord below $0.2$, 88 converged (all to the alternating hexagon: like chords $\sqrt3$, unlike $1$ and $2$, order 24, three zero singular values) and 266 did not reach residual $10^{-9}$. The 266 endpoints do not approach collisions: their minimum unlike chord lies in $[0.914,0.929]$ (quantiles 10/50/90 all $0.927$), minimum like chord in $[1.430,1.439]$, and the residual norm in $[0.975,0.977]$; none has an unlike chord below $0.5$ and none has residual above $1$. They all stall at one and the same place up to symmetry: a configuration at which the squared tangent residual has a nonzero stationary value ($|P\mathbf A|\approx0.975$ summed over members), a critical point of the least-squares objective that is not an equilibrium. Its positions were not recorded by this part (only the chord statistics), so its symmetry and shape are not identified here; that is a cheap follow-up. The practical consequence for the census is that two thirds of the random seeds are captured by this one stall, so the effective sample of the equilibrium search is the 88 converged seeds, not 400. Claim grade: measured (chord and residual statistics over 266 endpoints). Falsifier: a nonconverged endpoint with an unlike chord below $0.5$ or a residual outside $[0.97,0.98]$ in the results files.

**Planar-restricted census.** Six members on the equator, five free angles, polarity sequences by bracelet up to rotation, reflection and global swap: there are exactly three, $---+++$, $--+-++$ and $-+-+-+$ (enumerated by the instrument). Sixty recorded seeds per bracelet (master seed 20261012), least squares on the six azimuthal components. Alternating bracelet: 60 of 60 converge to the regular hexagon. Non-alternating bracelets: 58 of 60 and 59 of 60 do not converge; the 2 and 1 that converge end with the members re-ordered along the circle so that the spatial polarity sequence is alternating again (angles $0,120,240,300,180,60$ degrees for the labels of $---+++$), that is, the solver let members pass each other and found the same regular hexagon. No planar equilibrium with a non-alternating spatial polarity sequence was found. Every found planar equilibrium also has zero full-sphere tangent residual ($2\times10^{-15}$), order 24 and three zero singular values. Claim grade: measured (this instrument, recorded seeds). Falsifier: a seed in the recorded lists whose converged planar endpoint has a non-alternating spatial sequence with all chords above $0.05$.

## (4) Scope of the census

The census of waves 5–6 supports only this: within the searched sample (88 converged random seeds on the sphere, 60 per bracelet on the equator) and the analytic families treated in wave 5 (every two-ring polarity-segregated configuration, every planar D$_3$ twist, both octahedral polarity classes), the only stationary equilibrium found or admitted is the alternating equatorial hexagon. It does not establish global uniqueness of spherical equilibria of three positrinos and three electrinos: a least-squares search from random seeds can miss basins, two thirds of the seeds were captured by a single non-equilibrium stall, and the analytic families are a small part of configuration space. A uniqueness statement would need either a complete critical-point count for $U$ on $(S^2)^6$ or a certified global search, neither of which exists here.
