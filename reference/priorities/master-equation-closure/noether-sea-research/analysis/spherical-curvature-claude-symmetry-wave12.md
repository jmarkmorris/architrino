## The reduction for a moving release

By the transitive symmetry of each pattern (six poles V2: $S_3\times\mathbb Z_2$, order 12; antiprism V2: $D_{3d}$ type, order 12) all six members share one coordinate $q$, the arc along the receiver's great circle in the direction of its release velocity: for the six poles from $\mathbf e_x$ toward the $+\mathbf w$ cap along $\mathbf c_x$, for the antiprism pole-ward along the azimuth-$0$ meridian from colatitude $60^\circ$. Under the H1 preparation of duration $1/4$ every member is at rest at its site for $s\le-1/4$ and displaced by at most $\delta=27\nu/1024$ radians (chord) inside the preparation. On the stationary-source window every root reads the partner at its initial site, so the law is $q''=gf(q)$ with $f(q)=\mathbf t(q)\cdot\mathbf B_{\rm images}(\mathbf x(q))$, $f(0)=-1$ (six poles, the wave-1 value $-K/R^2$ along the cap direction) and $f(0)=-0.94603$ (antiprism, the wave-2 value); with $q'(0)=v$ the first integral is

$$
\tfrac12q'^2=\tfrac12v^2+g\,[W(q)-W(0)]=\tfrac12v^2-g\,V(q),\qquad V(q)=\int_0^q|f| ,
$$

$V>0$ ahead of the release site and $V<0$ behind it on the certified range, where $f$ keeps its sign. The member decelerates to a turning point $q^*$ with $V(q^*)=v^2/(2g)$ at $t^*=\int_0^{q^*}dq/\sqrt{v^2-2gV}$, returns through the release site at $2t^*$ (the autonomous reduction is time-reversible about the turning point; this is a property of the reduction, valid only while the window lasts), and continues behind it with growing speed, reaching wake speed where $V=-(1-v^2)/(2g)$. Window criterion: a root reads a moving emission only if its chord to the actual partner position at emission equals $T-s<T+1/4$; that chord is at least the chord to the site minus $\delta$, so the window holds while $T+1/4\le D-\delta$ with $D$ the continuous minimum chord to any image over the path visited; hence $T_w\ge D_{\rm seg}-\delta-1/4$ with $D_{\rm seg}$ over the whole segment $[q_{\min},q^*]$ provided the member stays in it (checked), and $T_w\le\max_{\rm seg}d_{\min}+\delta-1/4$. Brackets by the wave-11 step envelopes of $|f|$ (continuous chord minima, floating model), the integrand $1/\sqrt{v^2-2gV}$ being increasing in $V$. Controls recorded before the targets, all passed: the constant-field toy $f=-f_0$ from speed $v$ gives $q^*=v^2/(2gf_0)$, $t^*=v/(gf_0)$ and return at $2t^*$ to $10^{-7}$; the stationary hexagon gives $f(0)=0$; the release fields reproduce $-1$ and $-0.9460319$; a polarity flip changes $f(0)$. Claim grade: derived (reduction, first integral, window criterion with $\delta$); measured (envelope evaluations), conditional on the floating premises stated below.

## Codex items 19(1)–(4), applied here and as a dated companion to the frozen wave 11

1. **Partial-cell suffix maximum.** Wave 11's upper bound $T^{**}$ used the running maximum of $d_{\min}$ over the grid plus half the spacing; a maximum inside the partial cell between a mid-grid point and the next included sample can lie up to a full spacing from that sample, so the correct continuous bound adds the full spacing (or includes the boundary sample). In wave 11 this affects only the upper ends $T^{**}$ by at most one grid spacing ($Q/2000$, below $10^{-3}$ in time), which changes no verdict there (every "window ends before the crossing" margin exceeds $0.18$); the wave-12 upper bounds above use the full spacing. The correction is recorded here as a companion; wave 11's printed $T^{**}$ values are to be read with that enlargement.
2. **Floating model extended, kept separate from the theorems.** The wave-11 model bounded only the field evaluation. The wave-12 instrument states, as modeling premises: chord evaluations carry relative error at most $10^{-15}$, absorbed by lowering every chord lower bound by $10^{-12}$; accumulated time sums over at most $200$ cells, square roots and the $80$-step bisections carry relative error at most $10^{-12}$, absorbed by widening every time, position and speed bracket by a relative $10^{-10}$. Every certified number in this wave is conditional on those premises; the Lipschitz theorems (chord $1$-Lipschitz, field Lipschitz constant, monotone envelopes) are independent of them. Wave 11's certificates are, retroactively, conditional on the same premises, which were not stated there.
3. **Simultaneous separation.** Wave 11's phrase "actual member separations stay above $1$" was false as a general statement (the $30^\circ$ antiprism has same-polarity chord $\sqrt3/2$ at release; the twisted $40^\circ$ and $50^\circ$ sites are closer than $1$). The correct route is the one used above: with every member on a known great circle under the reduction, the simultaneous chords are explicit functions of $q$, $2$-Lipschitz in $q$, and their minimum over the segment visited is certified from a grid minus a full spacing. For wave 11's seven rest releases this certificate was not computed; the wave-11 statement is withdrawn for them and replaced by "positive simultaneous separation on the certified segment is to be established by the same route before the actual-motion bound is used" (a sub-second computation per case, not run in this wave to keep the targets as assigned).
4. **Superseded brackets and byte preservation.** The rev-3 (run 2) printed brackets of wave 11 are superseded by wave 11's correction block, not silently valid; their output bytes, previously held only in scratch, are preserved in the repository as `spherical-curvature-claude-symmetry-wave12-superseded-wave11-rev3-run2.json` under this wave's prefix.
