## 1. Method (derived)

Each step rechecked. Envelopes: with the per-sub-interval Lipschitz constant (adjudication-10 §2, continuous chord minima $d_j^*=\min_{\rm grid}d_j-h/2$ by the 1-Lipschitz chord along a unit-rate great circle, $|dd_j/dq|=|(\mathbf x-\mathbf s_j)\cdot\mathbf x'|/d_j\le1$) the step functions $W_{\rm lb}\le W-W(0)\le W_{\rm ub}$ follow by integrating $f_{{\rm lb},k}\le f\le f_{{\rm ub},k}$. Time bounds: the integrand $1/\sqrt{2gW}$ is decreasing in $W$, so $t_{\rm lb}=\int dq/\sqrt{2gW_{\rm ub}}\le t\le t_{\rm ub}$; the closed form $2(\sqrt{W_{\rm end}}-\sqrt{W_{\rm start}})/(f_k\sqrt{2g})$ on a linear piece is correct. Consequence 1 ($q_c$ and $t_c$ brackets): correct, since $W_{\rm ub}$ and $t_{\rm lb}$ are increasing. Consequence 2 ($T_w\ge T^*$): for $t<T^*$, $q(t)\le q_{\rm ub}(t)<q^*$ and $d_{\min}(q(t))\ge D(q_{\rm ub}(t))\ge D(q^*)=T^*>t$ with $D$ the running minimum: correct, and the uniqueness statement ($t_{\rm lb}-D$ strictly increasing) is a theorem. Consequence 3 ($T_w\le T^{**}$ and "no crossing before the window end" when $\sqrt{2gW_{\rm ub}(q_{\rm ub}(T^{**}))}<1$): correct as an inequality on the reduction. Ruling: the three consequences are derived; the Lipschitz constant and the chord theorem are derived; the floating model $E_f=200\epsilon\sum_j1/d_j^{*2}$ is acceptable as a stated model for the grid values of $f$ (a few dozen correctly rounded operations per term plus `cos`/`sin` within 2 ulp, assumption (A1) of adjudication-9), but see §3 for what it does not cover.
