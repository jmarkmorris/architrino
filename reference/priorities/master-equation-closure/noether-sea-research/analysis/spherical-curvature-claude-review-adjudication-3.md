Method (derived). For a block function $h(\Lambda)=\Lambda^2-gD+g\sum_m(A_m-\Lambda B_m)e^{-\Lambda\tau_m}$ (with $B_m=0$ for the $z$ blocks), on any set with $\mathrm{Re}\,\Lambda\ge x_{\min}$ and $|\Lambda|\le\Lambda_{\max}$,

$$
|h'(\Lambda)|\le M:=2\Lambda_{\max}+g\sum_m e^{-x_{\min}\tau_m}\big[\tau_m(|A_m|+\Lambda_{\max}|B_m|)+|B_m|\big],
$$

because $h'=2\Lambda+g\sum_m[-\tau_m(A_m-\Lambda B_m)-B_m]e^{-\Lambda\tau_m}$ and $|e^{-\Lambda\tau}|=e^{-\tau\,\mathrm{Re}\Lambda}$. Each rectangle edge is cut into pieces of length at most $1$ with the piece's own $x_{\min}$ and $\Lambda_{\max}$. Along a piece, samples $\Lambda_j$ at spacing $d$ are accepted only if $d\,M<0.9\min(|h(\Lambda_j)|,|h(\Lambda_{j+1})|)$; then for every point between, $|h(\Lambda)-h(\Lambda_j)|\le dM<|h(\Lambda_j)|$, so $h$ stays in an open disk about $h(\Lambda_j)$ that excludes $0$, no zero lies on the contour, and the argument moves by less than $\pi/2$ relative to $h(\Lambda_j)$; hence the principal-value increment $\arg(h(\Lambda_{j+1})/h(\Lambda_j))$ is the true increment and the winding number is the exact sum of increments divided by $2\pi$. Failing pairs are refined by halving, up to fourteen rounds; a piece still failing makes the block uncertified (never silently counted). Evaluations are binary64; the $10\%$ margin exceeds rounding by more than ten orders, which is the stated rounding allowance. The derived outside bound (adjudication-2 H.2) $|\Lambda|\le\tfrac12[gB+\sqrt{g^2B^2+4g(|D|+A)}]$ for $\mathrm{Re}\,\Lambda\ge0$ ($9.667$ for $t$, $3.783$ for $z$ at $g=4$, smaller below) places every root with nonnegative real part inside the right strip $\mathrm{Re}\,\Lambda\in[-0.01,10]$, $\mathrm{Im}\,\Lambda\in[-10,10]$, which is the certified region.

Controls, run and recorded before the targets (order in `_order`): K1, the no-delay blocks are quadratics whose roots are known in closed form; the certified winding on the full rectangle $[-4,10]\times[-10,10]$ equals the number of quadratic roots inside for $(3,t)$, $(3,z)$ (the Family A no-delay pair $\pm1.891388i$), $(0,t)$ and $(2,z)$ at $g=1$. N1, negative control: for $(3,t)$ at $g=1$ the full rectangle certifies $5$ roots and the rectangle cut at $\mathrm{Re}\,\Lambda\le1.9$, which excludes the known root $2.010858$, certifies $4$. N2, negative control: with refinement disabled and coarse sampling the instrument returns "uncertified" rather than a number.
