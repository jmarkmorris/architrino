## Stage A design

### 1. The representation (derived)

State at a step start: $x=(q,w)=c+\sum_i M_i\,\xi_i$ with point centre $c\in\mathbb{R}^2$, point coefficient columns $M_i\in\mathbb{R}^2$ and latent symbols $\xi_i\in[-1,1]$. Symbols and their meaning: $\xi_{\rm aq}$, $\xi_{\rm aw}$ are the anchor's $q$ and $w$ uncertainty, two INDEPENDENT symbols because the accepted rectangle licenses independent variation (they are never tied; control E3 shows a tie would exclude a known corner); for each reconstructed step $k$, $E_k$ is the error of the acceleration's mean-value enclosure (one quantity, entering the $q$ row with coefficient $\Delta^2/2$ and the $w$ row with $\Delta$, so it is one shared symbol), and $Rq_k$, $Rw_k$ are the Taylor remainders of the two rows plus all rounding of that row (independent). Dimension after $n$ reconstructed steps: $2+3n$ symbols, joint domain $[-1,1]^{2+3n}$, no other constraint. Centres and coefficients are exact binary numbers; every operation is done in interval arithmetic and the rounding radius is moved into $Rq_k$ or $Rw_k$, so the stored set is a rigorous superset.

Step map (the frozen Taylor form of the method of steps, unchanged premise): $q^+=q+w\Delta+A(q)\Delta^2/2+\rho_q$, $w^+=w+A(q)\Delta+\rho_w$, with $\rho_q\in a_p[0,\Delta^3/6]$, $\rho_w\in a_p[0,\Delta^2/2]$ and $a_p$ the frozen whole-step rate enclosure (stored-rate premise kept). $A(q)=g\sum_c m_c\,N/(r^3D)$ evaluated with the true history at the source time $t-\tau_c(q)$; it does not depend on the receiver's $w$. Mean-value form: $A(q)=A(\hat q)+A'(\eta)(q-\hat q)$ with $\hat q=c_q$. The end-state sensitivity on the current state is the $2\times2$ matrix $J=\begin{pmatrix}1+j\Delta^2/2 & \Delta\\ j\Delta & 1\end{pmatrix}$ with $j$ the centre of the enclosure of $A'$; the radius of that enclosure times $|q-\hat q|$, the width of $A(\hat q)$ and the remainders become the new symbols. New coefficients $M_i^+=JM_i$ (exactly the linearised flow on the latent parameters, which removes the box-wrapping that the frozen box iteration suffers).

Delayed reads and the clock origin: in this design the delayed reads and the uncertain clock origin enter as BOXES at each evaluation, i.e. treated as independent of the latent symbols, and their whole effect goes into the radius of $E_k$ (the "omitted dependence put into the remainder" option). Their widths are small because, after the anchor, the reader consumes only the RECONSTRUCTED records (appended before consumption), and before the anchor the frozen records of steps up to 154 (width at most $3.3\times10^{-4}$ in $q$). This loss of the history correlation is stated, not hidden; carrying the history sensitivity jointly would be the next refinement if the band stays undecided.

Mean-value justification (derived): along the segment from $\hat q$ to the true $q$, $\tau_c(q)$ is $C^1$ by the implicit function theorem ($1+r_{q'}w'>0$ by the sub-wake bound), the source position is $C^1$ and the source velocity Lipschitz (bounded source acceleration with jumps only at record seams), so $A$ is Lipschitz and absolutely continuous in $q$ with a.e. derivative $\frac{dA}{dq}=g\sum_c m_c\left[h_q-(h_{q'}w'+h_{w'}a')\,\frac{r_q}{1+r_{q'}w'}\right]$; at a seam the one-sided source accelerations both lie in the history box consumed (whole-step box on the three first-reading records, $a_0+a_p[0,\Delta]$ elsewhere), so the integral mean-value inclusion $A(q)-A(\hat q)\in\mathrm{hull}(A'(Q_d))\,(q-\hat q)$ holds with $Q_d=\mathrm{hull}(\hat q, Q_s)$ (control E6 checks the one-sided case on a known path).

Each step also runs the FROZEN step (steps9 v3c1, unchanged) on the narrowest proved start box $Q_s$ (the intersection of the affine hull and the previous proved end), which supplies the closure, the whole-step boxes, $a_p$, every frozen check (sub-wake, coverage, chart, trace identity) and the record. The record's end is the intersection of the frozen-step end and the affine hull (both proved; an empty intersection refuses).

### 6. Seams in the $j$ enclosure, and the per-step width budget (added at the PI's request)

A seam is either a join between two records, where the hull contains both records' boxes and hence both one-sided source accelerations, or the interior of a first-reading record, where the whole-step box contains both sides by construction.
