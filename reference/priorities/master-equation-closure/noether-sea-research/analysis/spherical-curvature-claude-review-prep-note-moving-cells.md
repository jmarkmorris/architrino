## A. Rigid rotation of an arbitrary six-member configuration about a unit axis $\hat{\mathbf w}$ at rate $\Omega$

Input class: prescribed complete history $\mathbf X_j(T)=\mathcal R_{\hat{\mathbf w}}(\Omega T)\mathbf X_j(0)$ for all $T$ (H2-type, never motion). Member $j$ moves on the latitude circle about $\hat{\mathbf w}$ at polar angle $\alpha_j$ ($\cos\alpha_j=\hat{\mathbf w}\cdot\mathbf X_j$), radius $\rho_j=\sin\alpha_j$, speed $\beta_j=\Omega\rho_j$, azimuth $\varphi_j(T)=\varphi_j(0)+\Omega T$ about $\hat{\mathbf w}$. Everything below is time independent in the co-rotating frame because time translation is a rotation of the whole history; so the release-instant evaluation is the whole diagnostic, and a phase scan adds nothing (derived from the invariance of the kernel under common rotations).

1. Root equation (scalar, per ordered pair). With $\Delta\varphi_{ij}=\varphi_i(T)-\varphi_j(T)$ and $\mathbf X_i(T)\cdot\mathbf X_j(T-\tau)=\cos\alpha_i\cos\alpha_j+\sin\alpha_i\sin\alpha_j\cos(\Delta\varphi_{ij}+\Omega\tau)$,
   $$\tau^2=2-2\big[\cos\alpha_i\cos\alpha_j+\sin\alpha_i\sin\alpha_j\cos(\Delta\varphi_{ij}+\Omega\tau)\big].$$
   For $\beta_{\max}=\Omega\rho_{\max}\le1$ the sphere certificate (adjudication-1 A.2) gives exactly one root per noncoincident pair and no self root; the reference will state the bracket $(0,2]$ and solve by bisection on the monotone residual $\tau-\mathrm{chord}(\tau)$. The equatorial hexagon is the case $\alpha_j=\pi/2$, which reduces to the frozen moving reference's lag equation.
2. Transmitter factor in closed form. $\mathbf V_j(T-\tau)=\Omega\,\hat{\mathbf w}\times\mathbf X_j(T-\tau)$, and $\hat{\mathbf r}=(\mathbf X_i(T)-\mathbf X_j(T-\tau))/\tau$, so $\mathbf V_j\cdot\hat{\mathbf r}=\Omega\,\hat{\mathbf w}\cdot(\mathbf X_j(T-\tau)\times\mathbf X_i(T))/\tau=\Omega\sin\alpha_i\sin\alpha_j\sin(\Delta\varphi_{ij}+\Omega\tau)/\tau$ (sign to be fixed at the freeze by one Cartesian control), hence $D_t=1-\mathbf V_j\cdot\hat{\mathbf r}$ and $W=1/|D_t|$, with the sphere lower bound $D_t\ge1-\beta_j\sqrt{1-\tau^2/4}$ as the certified margin.
3. Kinematic tangent acceleration of the rigid rotation itself: $\ddot{\mathbf X}_i=-\Omega^2\rho_i\hat{\boldsymbol\rho}_i$ (toward the axis), whose tangent part is $\Omega^2\rho_i\cos\alpha_i\,\mathbf e_{\alpha,i}$ along the pole-ward meridional direction about $\hat{\mathbf w}$ and zero azimuthal; the normal part $-\Omega^2\rho_i^2$ is the curvature term (consistent with $|\mathbf v_i|^2=\Omega^2\rho_i^2$).
4. Diagnostic: the residual $\mathbf F_i=P_i\ddot{\mathbf X}_i-gP_i\mathbf B_i$ in the co-rotating tangent basis $(\mathbf e_{\alpha,i},\mathbf e_{\varphi,i})$; exact solution iff both components vanish for all six members. Necessary conditions the reference will carry: azimuthal component of the delayed sum zero at every member; meridional component equal to $\Omega^2\rho_i\cos\alpha_i/g$. For a two-ring polarity-segregated configuration about its own axis the sign lemma (adjudication-2 B.5) excludes this at every rate; about a tilted axis the configuration is not two-ring with respect to $\hat{\mathbf w}$ and the lemma does not apply, so the reference will evaluate the two components directly, with the tangent work per member $\mathbf v_i\cdot\mathbf A_i=g\beta_i B_{\varphi,i}$ reported.
5. Controls to run before the target: $\Omega=0$ reproduces the static field; the equatorial alternating hexagon at $\hat{\mathbf w}=\hat{\mathbf z}$ reproduces $B_\phi(\beta)$ of the frozen moving reference ($0.807163$ at $\beta=1/2$); one Cartesian evaluation of $D_t$ at a solved root; a negative control at $\beta_{\max}>1$ refused.

## B. Twisted planar hexagon released from rest

Input class: positrinos at azimuths $0,2\pi/3,4\pi/3$ and electrinos at $\phi,\phi+2\pi/3,\phi+4\pi/3$ on the equator, all at rest for every $T<0$ (identically stationary history, no preparation), released at $T=0$ with zero velocity; $\phi=\pi/3$ is the alternating hexagon (equilibrium), $\phi\to0$ the coincident singular limit.

1. Symmetry: $C_3$ about $\hat{\mathbf z}$ (polarity preserving) and the equatorial reflection hold for every twist; the meridian reflections hold only for $\phi\in\{0,\pi/3\}$ and inversion-with-swap only for $\phi=\pi/3$. Hence the generated motion (while unique) stays planar with each polarity triangle rigid: two degrees of freedom, the rotations $\psi_+(t)$ and $\psi_-(t)$ of the two triangles, equivalently the twist $\Phi=\phi+\psi_--\psi_+$ and the mean $\bar\psi$. Equal speeds hold within each triangle, not across polarities (derived from the group).
2. Release field (stationary sources, exact): at a positrino the like partners at $\pm2\pi/3$ cancel azimuthally by the vertex reflection of the positrino triangle; the unlike partners give the azimuthal acceleration $gS(\Phi)$ with $S(\Phi)=\sum_{j=0}^{2}G(\Phi+2\pi j/3)$, $G(\delta)=\cos(\delta/2)/(4\sin^2(\delta/2))$ (sign convention: positive toward increasing azimuth, to be fixed at the freeze against the symmetry wave-5 convention and one hand evaluation); at an electrino the same with $\Phi\to-\Phi$ and the sign reversed. $S$ is strictly decreasing on $(0,2\pi/3)$ with its only zero at $\pi/3$ (adjudication-4, 1.1). Radial parts are discarded; the $z$ components vanish.
3. First step from rest (derived expectation to be proved at the freeze): every partner root at reception $T$ reads emission $T-\tau$ with $\tau\ge d_{\min}(T)$, so all roots read the stationary past while $T<d_{\min}$; with release from rest the displacement by time $T$ is at most $\tfrac12gA_{\max}T^2$ with $A_{\max}$ the largest static tangent magnitude on the configurations reachable (a chord bound as in the symmetry theorem's Step 4), so a stationary-source window $[0,T_s]$ with $T_s$ of order $d_{\min}$ follows, far longer than the H1 preparation's $R/4$; on it the law is the two-degree-of-freedom static-field ODE $\psi_+''=gS(\Phi)$, $\psi_-''=-gS(-\Phi)$ (signs at the freeze), hence $\Phi''=-g[S(\Phi)+S(-\Phi)]$ with $\bar\psi''=\tfrac g2[S(\Phi)-S(-\Phi)]$; $S(\Phi)+S(-\Phi)$ and $S(\Phi)-S(-\Phi)$ have closed forms from $G$. No self root while speeds stay below $1$ (sphere certificate); the speeds start at zero.
4. Deliverables of the freeze: the exact release azimuthal accelerations for a named set of twists (closed form in $G$), the window $T_s(\phi)$ with its chord bound, the sign of $\Phi''$ at release (toward or away from the alternating hexagon; expected toward it for the static field, since the hexagon is the unique zero of $S$ and the Hessian direction is the $(k=3,t)$ pairing mode with static sum $7.25>0$, which is the destabilizing direction, so the expectation must be checked, not assumed), and the first-step reduction's validity conditions; controls: $\phi=\pi/3$ gives zero azimuthal acceleration; $\phi\to$ small reproduces the leading singular term $G(\phi)\approx1/\phi^2$; a negative control with a non-planar velocity rejected.
