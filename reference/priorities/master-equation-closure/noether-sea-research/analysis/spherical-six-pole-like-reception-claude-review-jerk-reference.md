## 1. Derivation (derived; the review's own quotient derivative)

Tangent hit of a meridional class ($\sigma=+1$ unlike, $-1$ like): $h=N/(r^3D)$ with $N=\cos(q+q')/\sqrt2-\cos q\sin q'/2$, $M=\cos(q+q')/\sqrt2-\sin q\cos q'/2$, $r^2=2-\sigma\sqrt2\sin(q+q')+\sigma\sin q\sin q'$, $D=1-\sigma v'M/r$, delayed state $(q',v')=(q,\dot q)(s)$, $s=t-\tau$. From the root equation $\tau=r(q(t),q(t-\tau))$: $\dot\tau=(r_q\dot q+r_{q'}v')/D$ and the source-time rate $\dot s=1-\dot\tau=(1-r_q\dot q)/D$ (using $1+r_{q'}v'=D$). Then $\dot N=N_q\dot q+N_{q'}v'\dot s$, $\dot r=r_q\dot q+r_{q'}v'\dot s$, $\dot M=M_q\dot q+M_{q'}v'\dot s$, $\dot v'=a'\dot s$ with $a'$ the SOURCE acceleration at $s$, $\dot D=-\sigma[\dot v'M/r+v'(\dot Mr-M\dot r)/r^2]$, and
$$\dot h=\frac{\dot N}{r^3D}-\frac{3N\dot r}{r^4D}-\frac{N\dot D}{r^3D^2},$$
with $N_q=-\sin(q+q')/\sqrt2+\sin q\sin q'/2$, $N_{q'}=-\sin(q+q')/\sqrt2-\cos q\cos q'/2$, $r_q=\sigma(-\sqrt2\cos(q+q')+\cos q\sin q')/(2r)$, $r_{q'}=\sigma(-\sqrt2\cos(q+q')+\sin q\cos q')/(2r)$, $M_q=-\sin(q+q')/\sqrt2-\cos q\cos q'/2$, $M_{q'}=-\sin(q+q')/\sqrt2+\sin q\sin q'/2$. Own antipode, $h_A=\sin d/(r^3D)$ with $d=q-q'$, $r=2\cos(d/2)$, $D=1+v'\sin(d/2)$: $\dot s=(1+\sin(d/2)\dot q)/D$, $\dot d=\dot q-v'\dot s$, $\dot r=-\sin(d/2)\dot d$, $\dot D=\dot v'\sin(d/2)+v'\cos(d/2)\dot d/2$, $\dot h_A=\cos d\,\dot d/(r^3D)-3\sin d\,\dot r/(r^4D)-\sin d\,\dot D/(r^3D^2)$. With all three classes read from generated history, $\ddot q=g(2h_U+2h_L+h_A)$ and the tangent jerk is
$$\frac{d\ddot q}{dt}=g\,(2\dot h_U+2\dot h_L+\dot h_A),$$
each term being that class's contribution derivative with its multiplicity. Continuity: every term is continuous in $t$ on the reached interval, including at the like and antipode seams and at every record join, because the delayed coordinate, speed AND acceleration of the common history are continuous everywhere (only the source jerk jumps at reading instants); so a sign change of a contribution derivative between two times implies a zero of it between them.
