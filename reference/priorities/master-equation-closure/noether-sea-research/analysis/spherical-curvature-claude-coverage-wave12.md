## Equation on $[T^*,T^*+\Delta]$ (derived)

With the symmetry family of wave 9 (positrinos at $120k^\circ+\psi(t)$, electrinos at $50^\circ+120k^\circ-\psi(t)$), take the positrino $P_0$ at azimuth $\psi(t)$. After the window end its nearest electrino $E_0$ is read at a post-release emission $t-\tau>0$, where $E_0$ sits at azimuth $50^\circ-\psi(t-\tau)$; the root is $\tau=2\sin(\delta_0/2)$ with $\delta_0=50^\circ-\psi(t)-\psi(t-\tau)$, unique while sub-wake because $\tfrac{d}{d\tau}[\tau-2\sin(\delta_0/2)]=1-\cos(\delta_0/2)\,\dot\psi(t-\tau)=D_t>0$. The source velocity at emission is $-\dot\psi(t-\tau)$ along its azimuthal tangent and the unit vector from source to receiver has component $-\cos(\delta_0/2)$ along that tangent, so $D_t=1-\dot\psi(t-\tau)\cos(\delta_0/2)$: an approaching source, weight $1/D_t>1$. The per-hit azimuthal acceleration of that hit is $G(\delta_0)/D_t$ with $G(\delta)=\cos(\delta/2)/(4\sin^2(\delta/2))$ and chord $\tau$. The other four partners keep their fixed images as long as their emission times stay before release, i.e. while $t<\text{chord}_j(\psi(t))$: $E_1$ at $170^\circ$ (chord $2\sin((170^\circ-\psi)/2)$, decreasing in $\psi$), $P_1$ at $120^\circ$ ($2\sin((120^\circ-\psi)/2)$, decreasing), $E_2$ at $290^\circ$ (shorter arc $70^\circ+\psi$, chord $2\sin((70^\circ+\psi)/2)$, increasing), $P_2$ at $240^\circ$ ($2\sin((120^\circ+\psi)/2)$, increasing). Hence on the step
$$
\ddot\psi=g\Big[F(\psi)-G(50^\circ-\psi)+\frac{G(\delta_0)}{D_t}\Big],
$$
with $F$ the fixed-image field of waves 9 and 11. At $t=T^*$, $\tau=t$ and $t-\tau=0$ with $\psi(0)=\dot\psi(0)=0$, so $\delta_0=50^\circ-\psi(T^*)$ and $D_t=1$: the equation is continuous with the window equation. The next root-class change is the first $t$ at which some other partner's chord over the enclosed $\psi$ equals $t$; with the enclosed $\psi$ below $0.30$ rad the minimum chords are $E_2$: $1.289\to1.30$, $P_1$: $1.631\to1.62$, $P_2$: $1.81$, $E_1$: $1.97$, so no other root reads post-release history before $t\approx1.3$, consistent with the PI's expectation of none before about $1.1$; the continuation below stops earlier for a different reason.

**Step enclosure (derived).** On a step $[t_0,t_0+\Delta]$ with $\psi(t_0)\in\Psi_0$, $\dot\psi(t_0)\in V_0$ (intervals) and a candidate set $S\supset\Psi_0$: the delayed argument $t-\tau$ ranges over an interval obtained from $\tau$ enclosed by interval bisection of the monotone residual over the whole box ($t$ in the step, $\psi(t)\in S$, history values from the window enclosure, which is monotone in $s$ and vanishes for $s\le0$); $\delta_0$, $D_t$ and $Q=G(\delta_0)/D_t$ follow as intervals; with $\psi\in S$, $\ddot\psi\in A=g[F(S)-G(50^\circ-S)+Q]$, so $\psi(t)\in\Psi_0+V_0[0,\Delta]+A[0,\Delta^2/2]$ and $\dot\psi(t)\in V_0+A[0,\Delta]$; if the $\psi$ set lies strictly inside $S$ the step closes (first-exit argument: the solution cannot leave $S$ before leaving the computed set, which it cannot), the end values are $\Psi_0+V_0\Delta+A\Delta^2/2$ and $V_0+A\Delta$, and the step is certified if in addition $\sup(V_0+A[0,\Delta])<1$ (sub-wake throughout: no self root and the $E_0$ root unique) and every other partner's chord over $S$ exceeds $t_0+\Delta$ (fixed images). Otherwise $S$ is inflated and retried, and failing that $\Delta$ is halved. This is a zeroth-order Picard set: its widths grow with $\Delta$ and with the width of $Q$ (dominated by the width of $S$ through $G$), which is the price of treating the delayed term as interval forcing.
