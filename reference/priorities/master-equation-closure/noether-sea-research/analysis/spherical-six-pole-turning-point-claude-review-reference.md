## 2. Event criteria (my own; derived)

**Path turn.** Certified only by: $w>0$ proved at a certified step end $k_1$ and $w<0$ proved at a later certified step end $k_2$ (end boxes of the certified state, which are certificates of the true end state), every step between certified, bracket $t\in(\underline t_{k_1},\,\overline t_{k_2})$; there $q$ has a local MAXIMUM and $|w|$ a local MINIMUM (zero); it is NOT a speed maximum. Enclosures over the bracket: $q$ by the hull of the step coordinate boxes, $|w|$ by the hull of $|V_S|$ (which contains zero). Uniqueness only from a proved negative sign of $a$ on every step from $k_1+1$ to $k_2$ (then $w$ is strictly decreasing there), or another independently justified monotonicity proof. A flat, zero-containing or merely shrinking $w$ box proves nothing; undecided ends are listed and widen the bracket. A negative-to-positive crossing of $w$ is a path turn the other way ($q$ local minimum). Falsifiers: a step inside a claimed bracket that is not certified; an end sign of $w$ not excluding zero.

**Signed-support turning point.** $l=-w^2-a_r$, $a_r=\hat n\cdot A$, $\dot l=-2wa-\dot a_r$. A step-end sign of $\dot l$ counts only under THREE-WAY agreement: the subject's end value (if it records one), my forms on the subject's end boxes, and my independent end evaluation (own delay bracket over the whole end box and uncertain end time, own lookup giving $q'$, $w'$, $a'$, own signed forms). A NEGATIVE-to-POSITIVE crossing of $\dot l$ between certified ends (or over whole steps whose own enclosures exclude zero) is a local MINIMUM of signed support; it is a maximum of INWARD magnitude only while $l<0$ there (stated with the $l$ enclosure). A POSITIVE-to-NEGATIVE crossing is a local maximum. NOT turning points: a zero of $l$ (support zero), a zero of $a$ (the geometric term $-2wa$ changing sign), a zero of $-2wa$ alone, an undecided derivative box. Seams: where a delayed acceleration $a'$ jumps (a class reading source time $0$ or a record whose rate bound has a two-regime hull), $\dot l$ may jump; the one-sided signs are retained and a sign change across such a seam is reported as a one-sided change, not a smooth turning point (no class reads such a seam in this campaign: all source times exceed $2.7$). Uniqueness only from a valid enclosure of $\ddot l$ of one sign over the bracket, or another monotonicity proof; an endpoint trend is not one. Falsifiers: an end sign not in three-way agreement; an $\ddot l$ enclosure containing zero used for uniqueness.

**Order** is decided only by disjoint certified brackets or by signs at a common certified instant.
