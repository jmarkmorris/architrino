### Geometric occupation and wake overlap

Absolute smallness does not determine packing. For braid number density $\rho_{\mathrm{NS}}>0$ and a consistently defined representative envelope radius $R$, define a spacing scale $d=\rho_{\mathrm{NS}}^{-1/3}$ and the dimensionless ratio $R/d$. The spacing scale is volume per member to the one-third power, not a measured nearest-neighbor distance. Small $R/d$ describes envelopes small relative to that spacing; an order-one ratio makes their geometric interaction relevant, with the actual clearance depending on shape and correlations. If both $R$ and $d$ are reduced by the same factor, $R/d$ is unchanged. This is a derived geometric identity, not a dilation symmetry of the equation. For a broad size distribution, one radius cannot describe all pair clearances.

The [envelope chapter](../../../../content/markdown/aaa/noether-braid/braid-envelope-geometry.md#dynamic-exclusion-envelope) defines a dynamic exclusion region rather than a material wall. Consequently, a gap between declared envelopes does not imply absence of coupling, and a high number density alone does not determine exclusion, stress or coherent response. Near space-filling envelope occupation remains a guessed preparation property; a measured clearance distribution on an accepted population history would test it.

### Many interactions and coherent resonance

An isolated calculation includes the histories assigned to that calculation. It cannot establish the response of omitted neighbors. In a specified population, neighbor contributions can alter internal cadence, orientation, envelope shape and persistence. ## Hexavalent geometry and changing neighbor couplings

The proposed organizing picture combines a deformable spatial arrangement with temporary, phase-sensitive interactions between neutral assemblies. A persistent assembly need not retain rigid orbits, permanent internal binary partnerships or the same dominant external neighbor. The explanatory target is persistence of an identifiable organization while its internal geometry and received contributions change. This extends the packing and collective-resonance question above; it does not supply a demonstrated population history.

### Six connections, six directions and six constituents

A graph with exactly six incident edges at every vertex is 6-regular, also called six-valent or hexavalent. The infinite simple-cubic nearest-neighbor graph is one three-dimensional periodic example. Stretching or shearing its cubic cells by one invertible linear map produces parallelepipeds that still tile volume, while preserving the six declared edges at each vertex. The resulting geometry is generally a deformed cubic net, rather than a strictly simple-cubic lattice. The cells fill volume; their one-dimensional edges do not occupy every point of space. Groups of cells can describe rods, branches and shapes with holes without changing the underlying graph. Replacing such groups by new vertices is a different operation and need not preserve degree six. The [Lattice Lab treatment](../../app-lattice-lab/manuscript.md#24-six-valent-connectivity-and-deformed-cubic-nets) develops these geometric distinctions.

The proposed physical interpretation begins with three spinning binaries within a tri-binary assembly, possibly with distinct radii and cadences. Their three axes suggest six directional poles or coupling opportunities. This is a guessed geometric correspondence: it does not establish six persistent exposed poles, six occupied neighbor directions or exactly six physical connections. Valence here counts graph connections; the chemical analogy supplies only the idea of combining capacity, without importing chemical bonding laws. A neutral group of six architrinos, three of each polarity, is a separate statement about constituent inventory. Six constituents, six candidate directions and six external connections must not be identified with one another.

The broader candidate allows those six constituents to precess, rearrange and change internal partnerships without remaining three permanently paired binaries. This is a proposed search class, not a demonstrated flexible six-member assembly or a withdrawal of assumptions selected for other cases. Its membership convention must state whether the same six architrinos persist or whether constituent exchange is allowed. If exchange is allowed, an independently defined pattern identity must distinguish survival from replacement by a similar-looking organization.

### Temporary dominance and internal feedback

One nearby assembly may contribute especially strongly to a receiving binary for several internal cycles, after which another neighbor becomes dominant. The discussion's image of couplings flashing like lightning refers to changing influence, not a new interaction law. Several neighbors can contribute simultaneously, and a rapid change in which contribution is largest can arise from continuous motion. Temporary coupled alignment or correlated motion is the intended meaning of “fuse”; actual assembly merger is unestablished.

The proposed feedback sequence starts when relative orientation, internal phase and delayed wake timing favor a neighbor. Its arriving acceleration contributions alter the receiving binary's motion. Changes in cadence, phase, radius or orientation then change subsequent emissions and received alignment, potentially favoring another neighbor. Internal coupling can transmit that response to the other constituents. Whether this feedback bounds deviations, produces repeated changes of dominance or disrupts the assembly is open. Equal source-polarity counts do not establish cancellation of the distinct delayed contributions received on those histories.

Three descriptions therefore remain separate: the spatial arrangement of assemblies, a possible six-direction geometry, and the time-dependent pattern of dominant received contributions. The last need not be periodic, a lattice or exactly 6-regular at any instant. Exact six-valence would require an occupancy and connection condition supported on the histories. Under the unchanged canonical Master Equation, all admitted delayed causal-hit contributions remain present. Selecting a dominant contribution for diagnosis does not delete weaker neighbors, impose a bond-switching event, or add damping, pressure or another response term.

### Transparency, fluctuations and persistence

The stability puzzle is how a larger persistent organization survives surrounded by smaller rapidly changing assemblies and their wakes. An approximately neutral-fluid picture suggests largely transparent relative passage, but supplies no microscopic law. Possible occupation of an assembly's envelope region requires actual trajectories and clearance analysis; neither material contact nor collisionless interpenetration is assumed. ### Analytical method before expensive searches

For the first experiment, fix the center $\mathbf C$ and radius $R>0$ in the Euclidean void. Write $\mathbf r_i=\mathbf X_i-\mathbf C$, $\mathbf n_i=\mathbf r_i/R$ and $\mathbf v_i=\dot{\mathbf X}_i$, with initial tangency $\mathbf n_i\cdot\mathbf v_i=0$. Let $\mathbf A_i^{\mathrm{ME}}$ be the canonical acceleration evaluated on the actual constrained histories. Define the outward-signed scalar constraint acceleration $\lambda_i$ by

$$
\ddot{\mathbf X}_i=\mathbf A_i^{\mathrm{ME}}+\lambda_i\mathbf n_i,
\qquad
\lambda_i=-\frac{|\mathbf v_i|^2}{R}-\mathbf n_i\cdot\mathbf A_i^{\mathrm{ME}}.
$$

Differentiating $|\mathbf r_i|^2=R^2$ twice gives $\mathbf n_i\cdot\ddot{\mathbf X}_i=-|\mathbf v_i|^2/R$, which yields the stated multiplier. Thus $\lambda_i<0$ means artificial inward support is needed; $\lambda_i>0$ means an outward correction is needed because the interaction's inward acceleration exceeds the surface requirement. Zero support throughout an admitted history makes that history a candidate of the unmodified equation, but does not establish stability. These are derived, self-reviewed kinematic identities. No intrinsic architrino mass or substrate force is introduced.

Equivalently, the constrained evolution is $\ddot{\mathbf X}_i=(I-\mathbf n_i\mathbf n_i^T)\mathbf A_i^{\mathrm{ME}}-(|\mathbf v_i|^2/R)\mathbf n_i$. The matrix projects the interaction acceleration onto the tangent plane; the remaining term is the inward curvature acceleration. Tangential interaction is retained, so paths turn and speeds evolve under mutual influence. The ideal constraint is perpendicular to velocity and directly contributes zero to $d(|\mathbf v_i|^2/2)/dT$; this kinematic statement is not a total energy conservation law for the delayed system. Its normal reaction changes the paths and therefore future wakes. Silent position projection would lose the support measurement and can introduce numerical artifacts; a numerical scheme must record normal reactions and independently control radius and tangency errors.
