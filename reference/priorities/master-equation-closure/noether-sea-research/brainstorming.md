## Packing, collective resonance and envelope deformation

The operator asks whether extremely small, closely packed assemblies with different internal cadences could undergo enough mutual resonance to change the interpretation of isolated braid calculations, and whether neighbor loading explains a spherical-to-ellipsoidal envelope. This is a proposed collective mechanism under the conditional assumption that suitable persistent assemblies and their observer-level particle mapping can be established. Neither that assumption nor free scalability is established by this discussion. The [scale analysis](analysis/preparation-controls-and-population-scales.md#a-scaled-history-needs-an-equation-check) already explains why arbitrary scaled copies are not generic solutions at fixed coupling and wake speed.

### Geometric occupation and wake overlap

Absolute smallness does not determine packing. For braid number density $\rho_{\mathrm{NS}}>0$ and a consistently defined representative envelope radius $R$, define a spacing scale $d=\rho_{\mathrm{NS}}^{-1/3}$ and the dimensionless ratio $R/d$. The spacing scale is volume per member to the one-third power, not a measured nearest-neighbor distance. Small $R/d$ describes envelopes small relative to that spacing; an order-one ratio makes their geometric interaction relevant, with the actual clearance depending on shape and correlations. If both $R$ and $d$ are reduced by the same factor, $R/d$ is unchanged. This is a derived geometric identity, not a dilation symmetry of the equation. For a broad size distribution, one radius cannot describe all pair clearances.

The [envelope chapter](../../../../content/markdown/aaa/noether-braid/braid-envelope-geometry.md#dynamic-exclusion-envelope) defines a dynamic exclusion region rather than a material wall. Wake fronts also extend beyond that region. Consequently, a gap between declared envelopes does not imply absence of coupling, and a high number density alone does not determine exclusion, stress or coherent response. Near space-filling envelope occupation remains a guessed preparation property; a measured clearance distribution on an accepted population history would test it.

### Many interactions and coherent resonance

An isolated calculation includes the histories assigned to that calculation. It cannot establish the response of omitted neighbors. In a specified population, neighbor contributions can alter internal cadence, orientation, envelope shape and persistence. The proposed benefit is environmental support of an assembly that fails in isolation; the equally open alternative is collective disruption of a branch that persists in isolation. Neither outcome follows from population size alone.

Resonance here means a sustained response selected by a relation between received wake timing and an assembly's internal motion. Counting sources or internal cycles does not measure it. Contributions depend on polarity, orientation, phase, transmission delay and the admitted causal roots; reinforcement, cancellation, dephasing and collective growth are distinct possibilities. A mixed-cadence population must therefore retain cross-assembly phase information long enough to determine which possibility occurs. No statistical independence, random-phase cancellation, imposed damping or pressure law is assumed.

### Spherical and ellipsoidal response

For a rotationally symmetric preparation with an isotropic response branch, equal loading in all directions supplies no preferred axis. It can change size or cadence while preserving spherical symmetry. Unequal directional response can instead support an ellipsoidal envelope: an oblate spheroid has a shorter symmetry axis, a prolate spheroid a longer one, and a general ellipsoid can have three unequal axes. The shape need not remain ellipsoidal under strong deformation. Spontaneous selection of an axis is another possibility, but requires demonstrating symmetry breaking rather than attributing it to scalar pressure alone. These are conditional symmetry deductions and proposed response channels, not a derived constitutive law.

General relativity supplies an observer-level recovery target: pressure contributes to its gravitational source, as explained in [Einstein Online, Mass and more](https://www.einstein-online.info/en/spotlight/mass_and_more/). That relationship supplies no microscopic packing fraction or assembly shape. In the present substrate account, the required derivation runs from interacting histories to medium response, then to the behavior of clocks, rulers and signals; agreement with effective curved spacetime must be demonstrated at the last step. The Euclidean void is unchanged.

A retained population record yielding common stress, clock and signal responses would supersede the current scientific blocker. This remains a proposal, with no queue activation. Possible later promotion belongs to the Noether sea and envelope chapters only at the evidence grade actually established.
