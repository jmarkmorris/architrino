## Scientific motivation

The purpose of this investigation is to discover fundamental properties of the Master Equation that determine whether it supplies a mathematically complete dynamics. Given explicitly admitted complete past histories, does it determine an evolution, is that evolution unique, how does it respond to changes in the histories, and what events prevent it from continuing? These questions guide closure by separating properties already implied by the equation from genuinely missing assumptions or mechanisms. A proposed repair must address a demonstrated failure and earn its own derivation and review; making a selected example run is insufficient.

The operator's immediate motivating question is whether two distinct architrinos, initially separated, can occupy the same Euclidean position at the same absolute time under the equation's own evolution. The preferred diagnostic uses two opposite-polarity architrinos with a specified complete history, initially stationary or moving along one line. Their initial positions and velocities alone do not specify the delayed problem. This small system makes the physical question and any obstruction easier to identify; a larger population is relevant when a stated claim requires an environment, not automatically a prerequisite for every finite-system result.

A no-coincidence theorem would establish an exclusion property only for its stated class of histories and evolutions. It would not prove that the theory is free of singularities. Present coordinate separation, delayed emission-to-reception range, degeneracy of a causal root, and convergence of an infinite population sum are different mathematical quantities. Conversely, coordinate coincidence does not by itself prove an infinite acceleration: the retained delayed roots determine the actual contributions. The accepted finite four-particle construction establishes distinct-label contact within its scope, while the stationary two-particle analysis encounters an unchanged-law self-interaction continuation obstruction at positive separation. These results already show why contact and singular behavior must be investigated separately. Their proofs and limits are recorded in the [contact adjudication](analysis/mec-008-independent-adjudication.md) and [mirror boundary analysis](../collinear-research/analysis/mirror-close-approach-causal-root-boundary.md).

The broader closure objective is a well-defined causal evolution with justified regularity and continuation on a declared physical domain, together with independently derived accounts on the same update. Finite instantaneous acceleration, locally integrable acceleration sufficient for continuous velocity, and a unique continuation are distinct requirements; an integrable divergence must not be called bounded acceleration. No local control, excluded collision, or candidate continuation alone establishes global regularity or completeness.

Research value is measured by what a result decides: an allowed event, an invariant exclusion, a precise failure of the existing equation, a missing assumption, or a proved limitation of a proposed repair. The [motivation and interpretation guide](brainstorming.md#how-the-small-system-question-guides-closure) connects the parallel efforts to those decisions. The [execution queue](work-queue.md) tracks the next tests; task completion is not itself theory closure.

Scenario interpretation distinguishes absent external sources, exact environmental cancellation or symmetry, and disturbances of specified histories. Isolation permits changes to the pair's own histories; an infinite stationary lattice has balanced contributions, not an absent environment. The [scenario comparison](manuscript.md#31-three-distinct-questions) states when this changes a conclusion, and the [analytical scope](analysis/coincide-or-not.md#environment-and-perturbation-assumptions) distinguishes prescribed past disturbances from the environment's later EOM response. Population alone establishes neither symmetry-breaking nor a contact probability. Future scenario comparisons retain these explicit assumptions without changing existing acceptance grades.
