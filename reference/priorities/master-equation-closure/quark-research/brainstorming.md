# Quark research — open questions

These are proposed investigations, not dispatched calculations. The [accessory analysis](analysis/accessory-geometry.md) supplies their geometry and failure boundaries. The candidate-preparation recommendation now has its single execution entry in the [work queue](work-queue.md#specify-one-complete-quark-candidate-geometry-and-past-history).

## Mixed-polarity association

Examine how accessory placement changes when opposite polarities can approach each other. The shared interior, boundary and exterior placement families offer controlled comparisons. Distinguish temporary residence against a prescribed host from association that survives full mutual evolution.

## Selection among down-type patterns

The [published color chapter](../../../../content/markdown/aaa/assemblies/fermions/color-charge-su3.md) admits two down-type pattern families at counting level. Determine whether delayed dynamics retains either, both or neither under explicitly comparable preparations. Counting does not decide the dynamical selection.

## Interacting candidates and color response

After a candidate history is established, compare its isolated and interacting behavior through the [vortex-coupling target](analysis/quark-vortex-coupling-target.md). Effective singlet and octet constructions need a response map from those histories. A fitted confinement term or an imposed color constraint cannot establish that map.
