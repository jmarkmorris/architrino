## Ownership and dependencies

This owner covers quark-specific constituent preparations, polarity decoration, candidate retention and interactions between those assemblies. General host qualification remains with the [Braid Program](../braid-program/priorities.md); the shared law remains with [Master-Equation Closure](../priorities.md). A prescribed host or a color-counting identity cannot supply a retained assembly by itself.

The [geometry-first mapping program](../../mapping-standard-model/analysis/geometry-first-program.md) retains mass-basis and weak-basis constructions, flavor overlap and effective confinement response. Their observer targets constrain eventual recovery; they are not new constituent acceleration laws. Published quark and color exposition remains in the corpus and is linked from this owner.

## Evidence and promotion boundary

A physical result must identify its constituents, complete past, environment, equation, causal-root coverage, interval and independent support. Missing contributions, a failed equation residual or loss of the declared assembly identity reject the proposed realization. Finite retention does not establish long-time stability or quark identity. The [work log](work-log.md) distinguishes this filing change from scientific progress.
