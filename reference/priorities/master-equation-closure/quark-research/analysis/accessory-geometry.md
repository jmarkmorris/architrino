# Quark Accessory Geometry

## Scope And Host Inventory

The quark-specific question is whether a candidate braid can retain six additional architrinos with the required polarity and axis organization while every member follows the unchanged Master Equation. The six accessories are separate declared members of the proposed assembly. Their locations and histories remain unknown; a six-site drawing supplies neither binding nor a retained quark.

Two host geometries must remain distinct. The Generation I construction in the [published quark catalogue](../../../../../content/markdown/aaa/assemblies/fermions/quarks.md) proposes a six-member braid plus six accessories, giving twelve members. The separate eight-member asymmetric counter-breathing geometry studied in [the F6c analysis](../../braid-program/analysis/f6c-geometry.md#asymmetric-counter-breathing-representative-accessory-inventory-and-generation-search-questions) would give fourteen members if it retained six additional architrinos. It is an alternative host experiment, not a redefinition of the catalogue candidate. The [shared candidate registry](../../braid-program/configurations/candidate-registry.md#accessory-bearing-continuations) records both continuations without claiming a retained base braid or accessory-bearing assembly.

## Polarity Assignment And Axis Partitions

The unpublished [polarity inventory](../../braid-program/analysis/f6c-geometry.md#six-member-polarity-inventory) and [morphology analysis](../../../mapping-equations/analysis/inferring-braid-requirements.md#discrete-morphology-coordinates-already-present-in-the-corpus) distinguish a count of each polarity from its assignment to particular paths. Opposite sites define three candidate axial pairs, or dyads. Assignments with the same total polarity count can place like-polarity and mixed-polarity dyads differently and therefore produce different delayed interactions.

In the up-type catalogue proposal, one mixed dyad identifies the exceptional axis. The down-type inventory permits two different dyad partitions; the dynamics must select a physical family or explain their equivalence. Counting alone cannot discard an extra surviving family. Nor can a spatial rotation of a drawing establish an internal color transformation. The [published color chapter](../../../../../content/markdown/aaa/assemblies/fermions/color-charge-su3.md) retains that separate recovery problem. These are candidate bookkeeping constraints, not established quark dynamics.

## Interior, Boundary, And Exterior Placements

The shared F6c geometry provides a [two-plus-four reference arrangement](../../braid-program/analysis/f6c-geometry.md#strongest-six-seat-seed-the-central-octahedral-axes): two sites along its distinguished body axis and four along the two transverse axes. The axial pair and transverse quartet may have different radii. At the equal-scale reference they form an octahedron inside both polarity-sector envelopes. That location is derived geometry; no capture region has been established there. A polarity assignment can also reduce the symmetry of the undecorated arrangement.

The same angular arrangement admits interior, boundary-crossing, just-exterior, and remote-exterior histories. F6c additionally distinguishes paths between its two breathing envelopes. As the [envelope analysis](../../braid-program/analysis/f6c-geometry.md#inside-between-or-outside-the-sector-envelopes) explains, these surfaces classify location; they are not material walls. A retained six-member pattern could occupy six separate regions or one connected region containing six separated paths. It need not consist of six stationary points.

The [accessory-placement analysis](../../../mapping-equations/analysis/inferring-braid-requirements.md#accessory-placement-relative-to-the-braid) identifies competing mechanisms rather than a preferred proven location. Greater separation can reduce individual acceleration contributions but can also weaken association. Symmetry may cancel disruptive contributions inside the braid. Mixed polarities can pair attractively, so a placement proposed for six like-polarity lepton accessories cannot be transferred to quarks without checking its complete histories. Lower accessory activity supplies no primitive reduction of coupling.

## Complete Mutual History Requirement

A fixed-host probe calculation measures residence or escape for a declared polarity, velocity, past history, and host phase. Evolving six mutually interacting accessories against a prescribed host adds useful information but still omits the host's response. Conversely, prescribing the accessories while evolving the host measures only that direction of response. None of these diagnostics establishes the complete assembly.

A dynamical claim requires all twelve or fourteen members, as appropriate to the declared candidate, to respond together. The record must include host–host, host–accessory, and accessory–accessory contributions, all applicable causal roots including delayed self-history roots, and the source histories needed to account for them. Numerical instantiations use $c_f=1$. This requirement supplies no added speed ceiling or continuation rule.

The [shared calculation requirements](../../../mapping-equations/analysis/inferring-braid-requirements.md#required-calculation-order) distinguish common translation and rotation, changes within an admitted braid family, and changes that disrupt the member relationships. A claim of robust association requires a nonzero range of nearby position, velocity, and history preparations, together with bounded relative motion, complete root accounting, and member clearance. A single retained history does not establish that wider claim. A claim that accessories can transfer while the braid survives additionally requires a complete reaction history and recovery of the host branch.

## Failure Boundaries And Present Grade

Escape, collision, growing internal deformation, or loss of association when mutual response is included would reject the tested candidate within its declared preparation and observation interval. Unresolved roots or near-contact histories instead limit what the calculation can conclude. Neither outcome rejects every quark geometry or disproves the Master Equation.

The current material supplies derived reference geometry and candidate selection conditions. It establishes no retained quark assembly, color dynamics, confinement mechanism, or generation assignment. Even a retained accessory-bearing branch would still need the independently stated particle-recovery requirements; association alone would not identify it as a quark.
