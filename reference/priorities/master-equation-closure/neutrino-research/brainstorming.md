# Neutrino research — open questions

These questions develop the [existing candidate account](manuscript.md). They are proposals, not accepted calculations or claims of physical realization.

## Candidate geometry and complete histories

Specify what “near-photon” means for both braid histories, including the constituent identities, relative placement and internal mismatch. A spatial picture alone does not define the past needed by the delayed equation. The current photon reference is unexhibited, so an equilibrium perturbation calculation first needs a demonstrated equilibrium.

## From six binaries to collective modes

The candidate contains six constituent binaries. The proposed three-mode description therefore needs a reduction from the complete assembly, with the omitted motion controlled. Three flavor channels do not by themselves identify three constituent binaries.

## Source, environment and detector

Determine how one retained candidate couples differently to production and detection channels while propagating through a specified environment. Small net polarity and near-planarity alone do not establish weak response, shielding or a mass value. The common-clock and oscillation questions remain linked to their [equation-mapping owner](../../mapping-equations/analysis/eq-16a-neutrino-common-clock-phase-packet.md).
