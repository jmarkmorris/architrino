# Neutrino research priorities

## Objective

Establish whether specified interacting architrino histories support the proposed neutrino assembly, then derive its propagation and coupling to other assemblies. The [manuscript](manuscript.md) separates this dynamical question from the downstream recovery of observed neutrino properties.

## Current

**Status: candidate construction; retained branch unresolved.** The [canonical referent statement](../../../../content/markdown/aaa/assemblies/fermions/neutrinos.md#referent-status) identifies the unexhibited photon reference branch inherited by the near-photon construction. The assembly identification remains guessed. A geometric neighborhood, phase model or prescribed path is not a retained solution or a stability result.

The directory is a current subject owner with no independently ranked evidence object. Its setup assigns no numerical score and activates no calculation. The [queue](work-queue.md) links existing execution owners; [brainstorming](brainstorming.md) holds proposed questions until a bounded investigation is selected.

## Ownership and dependencies

This owner covers neutrino-specific candidate geometry, admissible complete histories and physical dynamics. [Photon research](../photon-research/priorities.md) owns the proposed reference carrier; the [Braid Program](../braid-program/priorities.md) owns generic assembly qualification. A neutrino candidate still needs its own coupled history even if a photon reference branch is established.

[Equation Mapping](../../mapping-equations/analysis/eq-16a-neutrino-common-clock-phase-packet.md) owns the common-clock phase operator and its observer readout. [Benchmark Mapping](../../mapping-benchmarks/analysis/neutrino-oscillations.md) owns the oscillation comparison. Their sources, task states and acceptance conditions remain in place. Source/detector coupling and ambient response must use the same retained histories and the common [Noether sea account](../noether-sea-research/priorities.md).

## Promotion boundary

A result must identify its preparation, equation, root coverage, interval and independent support. A failed equation residual, missing causal contribution or lost candidate identity would reject that proposed realization. Stability, mass, flavor response and reaction claims each require their corresponding derivation. The [work log](work-log.md) records this organizational setup separately from scientific progress.
