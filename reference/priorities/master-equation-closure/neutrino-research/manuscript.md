# Neutrino Candidates and Their Histories

## 1. The proposed assembly

The [current neutrino construction](../../../../content/markdown/aaa/assemblies/fermions/neutrinos.md#near-photon-neutral-core-pairing) pairs two near-planar Noether braids with opposite polarity assignments. Each component contains three binaries. The complete candidate therefore contains six binaries and twelve architrinos, with no additional charged-fermion-style six-site layer. Internal mismatch distinguishes the intended neutrino configuration from the proposed coherent photon pair.

Claim grade: guessed for the neutrino identification. The constituent count describes the proposed internal inventory, not an accepted physical neutrino. An emitter, detector or ambient population adds further interacting histories to a complete preparation.

The phrase “near-photon” identifies a proposed relation between constructions. The [photon reference branch](../photon-research/manuscript.md#1-the-object-being-investigated) has not been exhibited as a retained solution, and the neutrino construction inherits that unresolved starting point. A retained branch is a complete history that satisfies the equation and preserves the declared assembly identity over its stated domain. A set of prescribed paths supplies a geometric input; it does not establish that the equation produces or retains that motion.

## 2. Geometry and delayed dynamics

An instantaneous arrangement does not specify a neutrino state. The [Master Equation](../../../../content/markdown/aaa/dynamics/master-equation.md) computes acceleration from causal intersections with earlier source paths. A preparation must therefore state constituent identities and polarities, complete relevant histories, equation assumptions and the surrounding population. Contributions from another binary or an ambient assembly can change the motion of a pair even when that pair would be balanced in isolation.

Zero summed polarity is an inventory statement. It does not cancel every exterior acceleration contribution, because the sources generally have different positions, emission times and velocities. Likewise, proximity to a planar geometry alone does not derive shielding, a propagation speed or an observer-facing mass. Those are questions about the retained motion and its interaction with other assemblies.

Numerical instantiations use $c_f=1$ for the primitive wake speed. The assembly's propagation speed and an observer's calibrated light speed remain distinct quantities until a derived map relates them. A stability calculation about an equilibrium requires that equilibrium to be established first; otherwise its spectrum cannot describe a state that the candidate occupies.

## 3. Internal motion and flavor response

Flavor denotes the electron, muon or tau charged-lepton channel associated with production or detection. The proposed account relates changing channel response to collective internal motion. Its three-mode description is a reduction of the complete six-binary candidate; it is not a one-to-one labeling of three constituent binaries by flavor.

The [common-clock phase packet](../../mapping-equations/analysis/eq-16a-neutrino-common-clock-phase-packet.md) separates a common phase rate from relative phase evolution. A common phase multiplying all reduced modes equally does not change their relative phases. Recovering flavor probabilities additionally requires the mode reduction and its coupling to the source and detector. A fitted matrix or prescribed phase beat does not supply those dynamical constructions.

The [oscillation benchmark](../../mapping-benchmarks/analysis/neutrino-oscillations.md) constrains the eventual observer description. Its measured targets are recovery conditions, not constituent-level laws from which to prescribe architrino motion. The [corpus account](../../../../content/markdown/aaa/assemblies/fermions/neutrinos.md#pmns-mixing-as-an-effective-recovery-target) keeps the conditional mathematics separate from the unestablished map to a retained assembly.

## 4. Evidence boundary

The present sources define a candidate family and downstream recovery questions. They do not exhibit a retained neutrino branch. A successful candidate would need a complete coupled history and independently supported causal-root and acceleration calculations on a declared interval. Its source, ambient response and detector readout would then need to follow from that same physical account.

A failed equation residual, an omitted causal contribution or loss of the declared assembly identity rejects the corresponding proposed realization. A finite retained result would support its stated history and interval; stability and long-time persistence require additional evidence. Establishing those dynamics would still leave the specific mass, flavor and reaction recovery obligations to be demonstrated.
