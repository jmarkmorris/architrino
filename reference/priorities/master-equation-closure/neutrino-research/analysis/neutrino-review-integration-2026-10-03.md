## A near-reference coordinate requires a real reference

The [photon constraints](../../photon-research/analysis/translating-carrier-root-and-axial-constraints.md) apply to histories with fixed axial offsets or, for the anti-periodicity result, regular alternating hexagons with equal opposite angular frequencies. They do not force every proposed photon history into that geometry. Periodic modulation remains a proposed search route. The neutrino reference has not acquired a proved new shape.

If a reference history $X_i^*$ becomes available, one possible proximity coordinate on a declared history window of length $h$ is

$$
d_h=\inf_{Q,a,\pi,\vartheta}\max_i\sup_{-h\le s\le0}
\left[
\frac{|X_i(T+s)-QX_{\pi(i)}^*(\vartheta+s)-a|}{L_*}
+\frac{|V_i(T+s)-QV_{\pi(i)}^*(\vartheta+s)|}{c_f}
\right].
$$

Here $L_*>0$ is a frozen reference scale; $Q$ is a permitted fixed rotation, $a$ a fixed translation, $\pi$ a declared polarity-preserving persistent-label permutation, and $\vartheta$ a common time/phase origin. A periodic reference permits restricting its phase to one cycle modulo its declared spatial return. The same spatial action is used throughout the window. No velocity boost or history-dependent relabeling is allowed. The history length must meet the actual causal-delay requirements; small $d_h$ alone does not certify root coverage or preserve topology. This is a proposed coordinate choice, not a measured neutrino mismatch or a metric already accepted by the mapping owner.

## A defensible mode-reduction target

After a periodic reference and an admissible differentiable history flow exist, a return map advances disturbances by one period $P$. Eigenvalues of its derivative are Floquet multipliers. First declare which translation, rotation and phase directions are removed and which retained motion remains. For relative-periodic translation, the spatial return must be included explicitly.

One possible three-oscillation reduction selects an isolated six-real-dimensional spectral subspace consisting of three complex-conjugate pairs. Its amplitude time scales must be compared with the intended observation window and with the omitted spectrum. Slow retained directions can lie near, rather than exactly on, the unit circle. For example, each real block $e^{-\epsilon P}Q(\theta_j)$ has multipliers $e^{-\epsilon P}e^{\pm i\theta_j}$; three such blocks are slowly decaying when $\epsilon P\ll1$, even though every modulus is below one. Additional blocks contracting at $e^{-\gamma P}$ with $\gamma\gg\epsilon$ provide separation. This elementary example refutes the proposed exact-unit-circle necessity; it is a mathematical comparison, not a neutrino model.

A retained subspace also needs an invariant-manifold or finite-window error estimate for discarded histories. Fast unstable directions cannot be dismissed as harmless fast modes. Degeneracies, generalized eigenvectors and additional symmetry directions require their own treatment. A selected six-real-dimensional subspace does not establish the corpus's three-component normalized effective state: the source/detector projection, common-phase convention, inner product and exposure account must still be derived. The [EQ-16A packet](../../../mapping-equations/analysis/eq-16a-neutrino-common-clock-phase-packet.md) explicitly preserves that same-branch/readout boundary.

No spectrum is computed without a reference. This proposal remains in brainstorming, with no claim of unitarity, attraction, flavor identification or absolute mass recovery.
