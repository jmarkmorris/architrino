## Working boundary

Keep all new logarithmic-potential derivations, candidate equations, local instruments, experiment definitions, reviews, and substantive findings here until the operator decides whether the approach is promising. Develop the explanation in `manuscript.md`; place longer supporting mathematics and local research instruments in `analysis/`, evidence records in `evidence/`, and any later model contracts in `contracts/` when those files are needed.
