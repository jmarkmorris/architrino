Begin with a two-moving-constituent collinear approach, then distinguish instantaneous comparison results from complete-history causal dynamics and any effective collective interpretation.

## Ownership and scope

This directory owns all new logarithmic-potential research until its promise is assessed. The parent retains authority over the accepted law and any eventual adoption. Unchanged-law collinear controls, existing reviewed evidence, and broad electromagnetic comparisons retain their original owners. The present proposal must not be inserted into the EOM solver or corpus as an accepted law.

Every new calculation uses $c_f=1$. Each candidate specifies its polarity and sign convention, coupling units, reference length, complete initial history where required, causal-root weighting, self-reception rule, boundary population, and domain of continuation. A changed potential zero must not change a predicted event. Mass, conventional force laws, a speed cap, hard-core radius, reflection, or an event impulse are not implicit ingredients.

## Decision criteria

Call a model promising only at the level supported by its evidence: an independently checked first-event result, a proved obstruction, or a defined continuation with a stated advantage over its control. A finite stable numerical trace alone is insufficient. A useful negative result identifies exactly which response or history assumptions fail and prevents repeating the same blocked route under a new name.

Broader work depends on resolving the first encounter's dominant uncertainty without hidden retuning. A gauge-dependent event, missing causal contributions, unacknowledged self-birth divergence, nonconvergent instrument, or undefined contact update prevents that candidate from advancing. Electromagnetic angular structure and a common source–receiver account remain later requirements; an inverse-square magnitude alone does not satisfy them.

## Promotion boundary

Keep mathematical derivations, numerical measurements, and physical hypotheses separately graded. No substantive logarithmic material is promoted or distributed to other workstreams until the operator reviews the lane's promise. The plan's final disposition can be to continue, revise a named assumption, or park the proposal with its obstruction preserved.
