# A conditional grazing continuation for the unchanged pair equations

**Status: derived candidate, awaiting independent assessment.** This complements the [positive-projection obstruction](authorized-cases-ten-hour-curved-birth-obstruction.md) for the existing mirror pair questions in the [ten-hour assignment](../../analysis/authorized-cases-ten-hour-codex-investigation.md). It selects no new preparation and asserts no grazing arrival for a specified disturbed spiral. Its purpose is to identify an endpoint hypothesis that rotating geometry can satisfy differently from the established incoming line cases.

## Fixed equation and endpoint assumptions

Use the canonical inverse-square row with $p=2$, or the registered coefficient-one logarithmic row with $p=1$, separately. Write the mirror paths as $X_+(t)=x(t)$ and $X_-(t)=-x(t)$, and use $c_f=1$. For the canonical row retain its fixed $K$; for the admitted logarithmic case take $K=1$. Before the endpoint the complete ordinary root sum has one partner root and no positive-delay self root, so

$$
x''(t)=-\frac{K n(t)}{R(t)^p D(t)},\qquad
R=t-s=|x(t)+x(s)|,\quad n=\frac{x(t)+x(s)}R,
\quad D=1+n\cdot v(s),\quad v=x'.
\tag{1}
$$

Place the endpoint at zero. Assume the complete supplied/evolved past is strictly subfield at every negative time, has a uniform strict speed margin on each sufficiently remote tail, and reaches a positive-separation endpoint with a finite velocity trace. Assume the earlier partner source is regular, its root $s_0<0$ is ordinary, and the source is $C^2$ near that emission. The endpoint equation and acceleration trace agree. Put $R_0=-s_0$, $n_0=n(0)$, $v_0=v(0)$ and $b_0=v(s_0)$. Suppose

$$
|v_0|=1,\qquad n_0\cdot v_0=0,\qquad
\Lambda_p:=1+(n_0+v_0)\cdot b_0-KR_0^{1-p}>0.
\tag{2}
$$

The middle equality means zero tangential acceleration projection at unit speed. It does not mean zero acceleration. For the registered logarithmic coefficient-one case the last condition reduces to $(n_0+v_0)\cdot b_0>0$. For the canonical case it retains the range-dependent term. These conditions must be checked on the actual incoming history if this theorem is used.

## Exact curvature at a grazing endpoint

The ordinary partner clock and direction obey

$$
s'=\frac{1-n\cdot v}{D},\qquad
n'=\frac{I-nn^{\mathsf T}}R\left(v+v(s)s'\right).
\tag{3}
$$

At (2), $s'=1/D$ and

$$
\frac{d}{dt}(n\cdot v)
=-\frac{K}{R^pD}+\frac{1+v\cdot v(s)/D}{R}
=\frac{\Lambda_p}{RD}.
\tag{4}
$$

Let $b(t)=|v(t)|$. Because $b'=v\cdot x''/b$ and $n\cdot v=0$ at zero, differentiating the scalar prefactor contributes nothing there. Thus

$$
b'(0)=0,\qquad
b''(0)=-\frac{K\Lambda_p}{R_0^{p+1}D_0^2}<0.
\tag{5}
$$

This calculation retains the moving source clock and its velocity. Treating the source as fixed when it moves changes (4). As an algebraic control, a stationary source gives $\Lambda_p=1-KR_0^{1-p}$; for the coefficient-one logarithmic row its exact unit-speed grazing curvature is zero. The condition is therefore strict and does not classify the degenerate stationary control.

## The partner construction is the complete unchanged-law continuation

For a sufficiently short interval after zero, the partner root remains in a fixed neighborhood of $s_0<0$. The source path in (1) is consequently already supplied. The implicit root derivative has a fixed nonzero denominator there. The partner equation is a regular local second-order equation in the current receiver position and time; it gives a unique mirror continuation with continuous acceleration and a continuously differentiable acceleration near zero. This first construction uses no newly evolved source values and imposes no cap or endpoint impulse.

By (5), its speed has the expansion

$$
b(t)=1-\frac{K\Lambda_p}{2R_0^{p+1}D_0^2}t^2+o(t^2).
\tag{6}
$$

It is strictly below one for every sufficiently small positive time. Together with the incoming assumptions, the complete joined path has speed at most one, with equality only at the isolated endpoint in this neighborhood. Every positive-length time interval contains a set of positive length on which the speed is strictly below one. Hence for any distinct times $a<b$ in that complete history,

$$
|x(b)-x(a)|\le\int_a^b|v(u)|\,du<b-a.
\tag{7}
$$

There are no positive-delay self roots. The partner gap is strictly monotone in source time, apart from a possible isolated zero derivative, since the complete source speed is at most one and is strictly below one away from the isolated touch. Its reception value and remote sign are opposite; the remote-tail margin supplies the latter. Thus the already constructed ordinary partner root is the unique complete partner root. Its emission remains strictly earlier than the unit touch and its transmitter denominator remains positive. The full unchanged equation therefore agrees with the partner construction throughout this local continuation, including at equality itself. No root has been suppressed.

The actual acceleration value at equality is justified by this complete root census and the finite ordinary partner row. It is not inferred merely from an incoming limit or the word inclusive. The continuation is a grazing touch followed by return to strict subfield speed, not a crossing to superfield speed. The theorem makes no later-fate claim.

## Domain dispositions and proof boundary

A strict-domain comparison ends when its membership condition first reaches equality, even though the underlying equation has the local continuation just constructed. An inclusive-domain-only comparison permits this particular continuation because it stays at speed at most one and its unchanged acceleration is defined by the proved complete root census. This supplies no general equality response, projected acceleration, contact selector or continuation for positive-projection arrival.

If $\Lambda_p<0$, (5) gives a local speed minimum at one, contradicting a regular strictly subfield incoming branch with the other grazing assumptions. The case $\Lambda_p=0$ requires higher-order analysis and is unresolved here. If $n_0\cdot v_0<0$, the endpoint instead has positive acceleration along the velocity and belongs to the separate self-root-birth obstruction when that theorem's remaining assumptions hold.

In the established mirror incoming line cases the velocity points toward the partner while the separation direction points outward, so $n\cdot v<0$ at unit arrival; the grazing hypothesis fails. A rotating history can have $n\cdot v=0$, but this geometric possibility is not evidence that the frozen perturbed spiral reaches such an endpoint. A proof of that member's arrival and the sign of $\Lambda_p$ remains necessary.

Falsifiers are a wrong clock sign in (3), an omitted term in (4), insufficient source regularity for (6), a nonordinary earlier partner root, failure of the complete remote-tail condition, or another time interval with unit speed almost everywhere that defeats (7). No numerical instrument, target trajectory or new physical history was used. Maxwell and amplitude-gradient equality behavior is outside this theorem.
