# No later unit-speed return for the admitted original width escapes

The [complete braking-work theorem](alternatives-screen-2026-10-05-width-entry-braking-work.md) gives, after contact and up to a hypothetical first loss of outward monotonicity,

$$
u(t)^2\ge u_c^2-\frac2\rho.
$$

Its continuation argument excludes that loss whenever the right side is positive. A stronger strict contact margin $u_c^2>2/\rho+1$ therefore gives $u(t)>1$ for the entire postcontact future. This uses the same complete self and partner integrals, including the stationary old past and every truncated reception band.

Thus, for each of the three admitted exact preparations, the first upward unit event is the only unit-speed event on its whole future, and the unchanged softened law remains superfield thereafter. This supplements the already derived eventual unbounded-speed escape; it does not add a speed ceiling to the equation.
