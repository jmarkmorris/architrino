# Frozen endpoint case for the original held-tail memory family

## Question and unchanged input

Fix any parameter in the finite-inward-unit set of the [critical-memory family proof](alternatives-screen-2026-10-05-memory-critical-existence-independent.md), using exactly its [frozen preparation](alternatives-screen-2026-10-05-memory-critical-preparation.md): $a=100$, release velocity $b\in[0,3/4]$, unique compatibility coefficient $(13/12)A=b/2+(200+b/2+A/12)^{-2}$, held tail and the fixed unit polynomial ramp. No parameter is chosen from a numerical event.

The selected law retains $K=c_f=\lambda=\tau=1$, all ordinary canonical self and partner roots, and the full unit uniform velocity memory. Consider its first inward unit endpoint, already proved to occur at positive separation for this parameter. Move its time to $t=0$ and orient the right receiver's coordinate inward: $y(t)=-x(T_*+t)$, $u=y'=-v(T_*+t)$. The complete incoming history is retained. It satisfies

$$
-5/6<u(t)<1\quad(t<0),\qquad u(0-)=1.
$$

It need not have monotone position or velocity throughout its supplied and generated past. In particular the original outward ramp is not replaced by a monotone inward preparation.

The partner root at the endpoint is unique, ordinary and at positive delay; it samples a compact strictly earlier regular segment. Let its positive inward acceleration magnitude be $P_*>0$. The exact regular partner-plus-memory incoming trace is

$$
B_*=P_*-1+\int_{-1}^{0}u(s)\,ds.
$$

The target is to determine what can be proved about this trace and about continuation, distinguishing:

1. endpoint transversality $B_*>0$;
2. unchanged-law continuous or bounded-variation velocity across the endpoint;
3. a genuine immediate superfield crossing, $u(t)>1$ throughout some $(0,\eta)$;
4. merely punctured equations that omit the event itself.

The continuation class to be tested keeps position continuous, remains collinear for both labels, and retains positive separation locally. It must preserve all old roots and all newly born self roots. For bounded-variation claims, the velocity derivative must equal the unmodified acceleration measure through the event, without an added atom or boundary rule. Any extension beyond ordinary isolated roots with nonzero transmitter factor must be stated separately; an unspecified generalized root prescription is not silently admitted.

The existing [class-level first-exit theorem](class-level-first-exit-independent-adjudication-2026-10-03.md) is an antecedent proof method, not a directly applicable theorem: its complete monotone-inward past and pure partner incoming acceleration hypotheses are absent here. A new proof must retain the bounded memory contribution and use the actual complete subfield geometry in place of those premises.

This source fixes the case and regularity questions before the new endpoint obstruction proof. It selects no outgoing trajectory, speed cap, jump, reflection, self deletion or coupling change. No numerical target or executable instrument is proposed.
