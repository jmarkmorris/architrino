# Entire-history exclusion for the equal past/future collinear law

The [initial collinear checkpoint](alternatives-screen-2026-10-05-collinear.md#equal-pastfuture-radial-response-a-boundary-exclusion) froze a periodic or time-reflection boundary formulation for the selected equal-weight canonical radial law. Its exclusion extends to every entire separated uniformly subfield two-label collinear history satisfying the equation at all real times. This is a boundary-solution exclusion, not a causal initial-value theorem.

## Complete equation and domain

Take two persistent opposite-polarity labels, $K=c_f=1$, canonical inverse-square radial response, and equal weights $1/2$ on every past and future root. Retain every self and partner channel. Let $x_1,x_2\in C^2(\mathbb R)$ obey

$$
r(t)=x_1(t)-x_2(t)>0,\qquad |x_i'(t)|\le1-\eta,
\qquad \eta>0,
$$

for every real time, and require the selected equation at every time. No lower bound on $r$ uniform in time and no periodicity assumption are needed.

The uniform speed bound makes each complete past and future partner clock strictly monotone with a nonzero margin and forces its range to cross the receiving time exactly once. The chord bound gives one partner root on each time side, with direction $+1$ at label one and $-1$ at label two. It excludes every positive-delay or positive-advance self root. All transmitter factors $1-nv_j(s_-)$ and $1+nv_j(s_+)$ are at least $\eta$. Thus every complete partner acceleration contribution points toward the other present label. In particular,

$$
x_1''(t)<0,\qquad x_2''(t)>0,\qquad r''(t)<0
$$

for all $t\in\mathbb R$.

## Derived nonexistence

A positive differentiable concave function on the entire real line is constant. Indeed concavity gives the tangent bound $r(t)\le r(t_0)+r'(t_0)(t-t_0)$. A positive tangent slope makes this upper bound negative sufficiently far in the past; a negative tangent slope makes it negative sufficiently far in the future. Positivity therefore forces $r'(t_0)=0$ at every $t_0$, contradicting the strict inequality $r''<0$.

Consequently there is no entire separated uniformly subfield collinear two-label solution of this equal past/future law. Every periodic-up-to-drift or time-reflection candidate in the initially fixed boundary class is included. A possible solution outside this domain must fail at least one named assumption: it may have contact, lose the uniform speed margin, lack complete roots or regularity, contain additional labels, or have noncollinear geometry. This theorem chooses no such extension and supplies no initial-value evolution or perturbative stability claim.

The same concavity reasoning applies to any full past/future radial response whose two partner weights are nonnegative and not both zero and whose per-root scalar magnitude is strictly positive on this domain. That is a mathematical class observation, not selection of another law for this campaign.
