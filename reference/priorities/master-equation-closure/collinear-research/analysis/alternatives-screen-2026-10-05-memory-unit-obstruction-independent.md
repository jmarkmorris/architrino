# Unit-endpoint crossing obstruction and the remaining memory tangency case

## Verdict and exact scope

**Grade: derived obstruction with a named unresolved condition.** For every finite inward-unit endpoint of the [unchanged held-tail memory family](alternatives-screen-2026-10-05-memory-critical-preparation.md), the full ordinary-root law excludes a genuine immediate superfield crossing with continuous finite velocity and finite accumulated acceleration. This remains true when the incoming acceleration trace is zero. The unchanged measure equation also excludes a velocity jump at the endpoint.

If the actual incoming acceleration trace $B_*$ is strictly positive, these facts strengthen to exclusion of every local bounded-variation collinear velocity continuation satisfying the equation through the event. For the entire held-tail family, this source proves $B_*\ge0$ and a restrictive necessary condition for equality; it does not prove $B_*>0$ for every inward parameter. A zero trace with a strictly negative next derivative would instead admit a local tangential touch and subfield return under the unchanged equation. Existence of such a tangential member in this family is not established.

The [case specification](alternatives-screen-2026-10-05-memory-unit-obstruction-specification.md) was frozen before this proof, SHA-256 d67a1a164de53311c1110d47990c03ba52eb0802a852b5bae2f4b0036deee268. It fixes the original canonical-plus-unit-uniform-memory law, $K=c_f=\lambda=\tau=1$, all old and newly born self/partner roots, positive separation and collinear continuation. No cap, reflection, jump, self deletion, impulse or outgoing rule is introduced.

The existing class-level obstruction is not applied by relabeling its hypotheses. That theorem assumes a complete monotone-inward past and pure partner incoming acceleration. The present family's supplied outward ramp violates the first premise, and its memory violates the second. The proofs below replace them with complete strict-subfield characteristic clocks and the actual partner-plus-memory trace.

## Endpoint data and the regular trace

Take any parameter in the finite inward-unit set proved in the [critical-memory source](alternatives-screen-2026-10-05-memory-critical-existence-independent.md). Shift its endpoint to $t=0$, orient the right receiver's position inward as $y=-x$, and put $u=y'=-v$. The complete past satisfies

$$
-5/6<u(s)<1\quad(s<0),\qquad u(0-)=1.
$$

Position and velocity need not be monotone over that entire past. The endpoint partner root has positive range $R_*>0$ and source time $s_*<0$ on a compact regular past segment. With the mirror incoming source speed denoted by the same $u$,

$$
P_*=\frac1{R_*^2[1-u(s_*)]}>0.
$$

The incoming equation is exactly

$$
u'=P-u+\overline u,\qquad
\overline u(t)=\int_0^1u(t-\theta)\,d\theta.
\tag{1}
$$

All terms have finite limits at the endpoint. The acceleration trace is

$$
B_*:=u'(0-)=P_*-1+\int_{-1}^{0}u(s)\,ds\ge0.
\tag{2}
$$

The inequality follows from first arrival at $u=1$ from below and existence of this derivative limit. Strict positivity does not follow from $P_*>0$: the memory contribution in (2) is strictly negative. Neither $M<0$ nor its monotonicity independently determines the sign strictly.

The regular partner contribution persists locally for any continuous-position continuation. Its old root is unique and ordinary at the endpoint. On a compact old interval, the incidence residual is separated from zero outside a neighborhood of that root; in the remote held tail the residual grows linearly. Emissions near or after the endpoint cannot reach the receiver before the positive pair gap is crossed. Thus, after shrinking the neighborhood, there is still exactly one regular partner root, at a strictly earlier time, with bounded locally Lipschitz coefficient.

The memory equals $-u(t)+y(t)-y(t-1)$. For bounded velocity it is bounded; for a continuation with right trace $u(0+)=1$ it is continuous at the endpoint and its limit is the memory term in (2). Therefore the regular partner-plus-memory part tends to $B_*$ and is locally bounded. The partner's future may also reach unit speed; positive separation keeps its new emissions outside this short receiver neighborhood.

## Full self-root geometry does not need monotone position

Define the two scalar characteristic clocks

$$
h(t)=y(t)-t,\qquad \ell(t)=y(t)+t.
$$

On the entire old past,

$$
h'=u-1<0,\qquad \ell'=u+1>0.
\tag{3}
$$

Thus $h$ is strictly decreasing and $\ell$ strictly increasing, even where the supplied path moves outward. An inward self chord requires $h(s)=h(t)$; an outward self chord requires $\ell(s)=\ell(t)$. At $t=0$ neither equation has a solution $s<0$. There is no positive-delay self root at or before this first unit endpoint. The zero-delay diagonal remains excluded by the original convention.

For a continuous velocity with right trace one, $u>0$ on some outgoing interval. There are then no outward self chords between two outgoing times. Nor can there be an outward root from the old past: $\ell(t)>\ell(0)>\ell(s)$ for $s<0<t$. Every possible local self contribution therefore has the inward sign. Its canonical acceleration weight uses the absolute transmitter factor, so an outgoing source does not reverse that sign. This conclusion uses (3) and local positive velocity, not monotone position over the entire old history.

## No event atom and no velocity jump

Consider a continuation with continuous position and locally bounded-variation velocity satisfying the unchanged acceleration-measure equation through $t=0$. On ordinary root charts that measure must equal the canonical root sum times reception time, with the transmitter-side absolute Jacobian and no receiver multiplier. No independently assigned event atom is allowed.

The partner root is regular and supplies a bounded density near zero. The complete old self-root set at reception zero is empty by (3); no interval of old self emissions is received there. The zero-delay diagonal is excluded. The finite unit memory gives an ordinary locally integrable density, even if a bounded velocity had a jump. Consequently the unmodified acceleration measure has no atom at zero, whenever it is locally finite. If it is not locally finite, it cannot equal the derivative of a bounded-variation velocity in the first place.

A jump would give the distributional derivative the nonzero atom $[u(0+)-1]\delta_0$. Hence every such continuation must satisfy

$$
u(0+)=1.
\tag{4}
$$

This excludes a reset, finite reversal or superfield velocity jump through the event. It says nothing about a different generalized equation that adds a singular event response.

## No immediate genuine superfield crossing, including zero incoming curvature

Suppose a candidate has continuous position and finite right velocity trace one, is locally absolutely continuous in velocity on every punctured interval $[\delta,t_1]$, and satisfies the complete ordinary-root equation there. Assume it makes a genuine immediate crossing:

$$
u(t)>1\qquad(0<t<t_1).
\tag{5}
$$

Then $h$ increases strictly on the outgoing side while it decreases strictly on the entire old past. For every small $t>0$ there is exactly one self root $s(t)<0$ with $h(s)=h(t)$, and $s(t)\to0$ as $t\downarrow0$. There is no root between two outgoing times because both $h$ and $\ell$ increase there, and no outward old root by (3). The whole self census is therefore this one inward root, not a selected subset of a larger sum.

Set

$$
\rho=t-s(t),\qquad w_-=1-u(s(t))>0,\qquad
w_+=u(t)-1>0.
$$

The exact self row and source-clock derivative are

$$
A_{\rm self}=\frac1{\rho^2w_-},\qquad
\frac{ds}{dt}=-\frac{w_+}{w_-},\qquad
\frac{d\rho}{dt}=\frac{w_-+w_+}{w_-}.
$$

These hold almost everywhere for the stated punctured regularity: the old inverse of $h$ is $C^1$ at every negative source, and the outgoing path is locally absolutely continuous. Consequently

$$
A_{\rm self}\,dt
=\frac{d\rho}{\rho^2(w_-+w_+)}.
\tag{6}
$$

The bounded finite velocity traces give $w_-+w_+\le C$. Also $\rho\downarrow0$ toward the event. Thus for any fixed small $t>0$,

$$
\int_\delta^t A_{\rm self}(r)\,dr
\ge\frac1C\left[\frac1{\rho(\delta)}-\frac1{\rho(t)}\right]
\longrightarrow\infty\qquad(\delta\downarrow0).
\tag{7}
$$

The partner and memory contributions have bounded integrals on this interval. They cannot cancel (7), while integrating the actual equation would give a finite difference $u(t)-u(\delta)\to u(t)-1$. This is a contradiction.

No positive incoming acceleration or quadratic crossing jet was used. Thus even a zero-curvature or flatter genuine immediate crossing is excluded. In particular the usual $C^2$ transverse crossing is excluded, and so is an immediate crossing with bounded-variation velocity. The proof also applies to a finite superfield right trace $u(0+)>1$ in a merely punctured equation; the all-time equation already excludes that trace by the no-atom argument.

The qualification “immediate” is explicit: (5) must hold on a right neighborhood. A hypothetical velocity with infinitely interlaced subfield and superfield intervals accumulating at the endpoint is not excluded by this single monotone root-branch calculation alone. It must not be reported as an admitted continuation.

## Positive trace excludes every bounded-variation continuation

If $B_*>0$, suppose a bounded-variation continuation satisfies the all-time measure equation. Its right trace is one by (4), so all nearby self contributions are nonnegative inward, as proved after (3). Its regular partner-plus-memory contribution is at least $B_*/2>0$ after shrinking the interval. Integrating the equation gives

$$
u(t)-1\ge(B_*/2)t.
$$

Thus (5) follows rather than being assumed. The divergent self integral (7) contradicts bounded variation. This proves the named obstruction:

**A positive partner-plus-memory acceleration trace at the first unit endpoint admits no continuous-position, bounded-variation collinear velocity continuation satisfying the unchanged equation through the event.**

A nonordinary self set cannot repair this conclusion by an opposing contribution: its chord sign is inward. A proposed extension that assigns it an unrelated finite counterterm is a changed equation, outside the theorem. The statement uses the same ordinary-root and locally finite acceleration-measure interpretation as the existing class-level owners, now with the actual bounded memory retained.

## What a tangent endpoint would have to satisfy

The incoming regular row is differentiable once more at the endpoint, because its partner samples a compact $C^{2,1}$ source segment. Let

$$
u_s=u(s_*),\qquad a_s=u'(s_*).
$$

The exact incoming source equations are

$$
S'=\frac{1+u(t)}{1-u(S)},\qquad
R'=-\frac{u(t)+u(S)}{1-u(S)}.
$$

Differentiating $P=[R^2(1-u(S))]^{-1}$ gives the endpoint formula

$$
P_*'
=2P_*\left[
\frac{1+u_s}{R_*(1-u_s)}
+\frac{a_s}{(1-u_s)^2}
\right].
\tag{8}
$$

From (1),

$$
u''(0-)=P_*'-B_*+1-u(-1).
$$

If $B_*=0$, first arrival from below requires $u''(0-)\le0$. Therefore a tangency can occur only if

$$
P_*'\le-[1-u(-1)]<0,
$$

or, equivalently,

$$
a_s\le
-\frac{1-u_s^2}{R_*}
-\frac{(1-u_s)^2[1-u(-1)]}{2P_*}.
\tag{9}
$$

This is a quantitative necessary condition on the actual delayed source acceleration. In particular $a_s\ge0$ is a sufficient condition for $B_*>0$ and hence for the full bounded-variation obstruction. More generally, any strict violation of (9) rules out $B_*=0$.

Another directly checkable sufficient condition is

$$
P_*>1-\int_{-1}^{0}u(s)\,ds.
$$

The conservative condition $P_*\ge11/6$ implies it, since the complete old and incoming history has $u>-5/6$. These are endpoint conditions, not established bounds for every member of the frozen family.

No argument supplied here establishes that every inward member's emission acceleration violates (9). The earlier negative-$M$ witness proves arrival at unit speed, but neither fixes the sign of $a_s$ nor removes the negative memory term in (2). This is the exact remaining transversality obligation.

## The regular tangential alternative and why the limitation matters

The local equation with the self contribution absent is a well-defined diagnostic initial-value problem at the event:

$$
y'=u,\qquad
u'=P(t,y)-u+y-y(t-1),\qquad
y(0)=y_*,\quad u(0)=1.
\tag{10}
$$

For a sufficiently short interval, the partner source and the position sample $t-1$ are in the retained old history. The regular partner function is locally Lipschitz in $(t,y)$, so (10) has a unique local regular solution. It is a trial equation only until its complete self-root census is checked.

If $B_*=0$ and

$$
C_*:=P_*'+1-u(-1)<0,
\tag{11}
$$

that trial has $u(t)=1+\tfrac12C_*t^2+o(t^2)<1$ for small positive $t$, and remains above $-1$. Its complete joined history is strictly subfield except at the isolated event. Every positive-duration chord is still strictly shorter than its duration: an isolated unit value cannot make its speed integral equal the interval length. Thus it has no self root, and its partner census is unchanged. Reflecting the trial for the other label gives the paired local continuation; positive separation keeps both partner inputs in their retained old histories on the short interval. In this conditional case the trial is an actual unchanged-law local continuation, touching unit speed and returning subfield. This is not a selected reflection rule; it follows from the ordinary equation if the actual endpoint data satisfy (11).

Whether (11) occurs for any inward parameter of this fixed family is unresolved. If $B_*=C_*=0$, higher-order information or a direct examination of the regular trial is needed. A unit-speed plateau is not an ordinary self-root chart: it creates a continuum of positive-delay self incidences. No plateau prescription is supplied here. A trial that genuinely crosses superfield cannot be promoted to a solution, by (7).

A simple prescribed-input control shows why positivity of the partner term alone cannot settle tangency. Take $u(t)=1-t^2$ on $[-17/16,1/16]$, which contains every unit memory window used for $|t|<1/16$. Then $\overline u=2/3+t-t^2$, so the exact memory equation (1) is satisfied with the positive prescribed input $P(t)=1/3-3t$ for $|t|<1/16$. It has $B_*=0$ and $u''=-2<0$. This is an algebraic control of the inference from bounded positive input, not a solution of the selected canonical partner-root law and not a counterexample within the family. That missing geometric constraint is precisely what a universal transversality proof must use.

If one imposes the equation only for $t>0$ and assigns an arbitrary finite subfield right trace $-1<U<1$, the corresponding regular memory initial-value problem has a local solution with no self roots. Such punctured restarts are mathematically distinct from continuations through the event: they omit the missing atom $(U-1)\delta_0$. They do not choose an outgoing state under the unchanged all-time equation.

## Falsifiers, exclusions and validation boundary

The geometric obstruction is refuted by an additional outward self root under (3) and local positive outgoing velocity, by a finite self integral despite (6), or by an admitted opposing singular contribution that cancels (7). The no-jump claim fails if the unchanged law supplies an event atom despite the empty old self census and regular partner root. The positive-trace theorem fails if its regular contribution does not stay positive under a right trace of one. Equations (8)–(9) are separately checkable by differentiating the actual partner root and memory window; a sign error there would affect the stated transversality test, not the curvature-free crossing integral.

The unresolved universal claim has a precise target: establish $B_*>0$ for every inward member, for example by proving that its actual source acceleration cannot satisfy (9), or exhibit a compatible family member with $B_*=0$ and determine its regular trial. No result here proves such an exhibit, a universal subfield return, or absence of every oscillatory generalized continuation. Noncollinear departures, velocities without a finite right trace in a punctured equation, externally assigned atoms and modified self regularizations are outside scope.

The independent validation route is complete old clock monotonicity, regular partner isolation, zero-event-atom census, the exact self-measure change of variables, the separate trace derivative and the conditional trial-root check. No numerical target or new instrument was used. The source does not transfer the old complete-monotone-inward theorem or a memory-free acceleration sign into this family.
