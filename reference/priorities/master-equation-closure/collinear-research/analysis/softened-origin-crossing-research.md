## Mathematical idea and its limit

The auxiliary family uses two different modifications. A normalized function $\delta_\eta$ replaces the exact causal delta distribution by a narrow band of distance-gap values, with width $\eta>0$. A spatial scale $\epsilon_c>0$ replaces the inverse-square vector kernel by $\mathbf r/(r^2+\epsilon_c^2)^{3/2}$, where $\mathbf r$ joins the emission point to the receiver event and $r=\|\mathbf r\|$. The full definition remains in the corpus as an [auxiliary mathematical technique](../../../../../content/markdown/aaa/dynamics/master-equation.md#auxiliary-dual-mollified-regulator-for-proof-and-computation).

At positive regulator values, the spatial kernel is finite at zero separation. This makes an auxiliary local crossing problem easier to formulate, but it supplies no physical prescription for the sharp law. At finite width, the integral samples a band of emissions rather than only the exact causal roots. A crossing, rebound, periodic return, or attractor of that family would therefore first be a result about the family. It would become a Master Equation result only through an argument that recovers the complete sharp dynamics for the same initial and boundary histories.

The regular-domain recovery statement in the Master Equation first sends $\eta$ to zero on a fixed history with isolated simple roots, then removes $\epsilon_c$ while all causal separations remain positive. That statement neither proves convergence of self-consistently evolved trajectories nor resolves coincidence or self-root birth. It must not be extended across those boundaries solely because a finite-regulator trajectory exists.

## What would make a result relevant to the unchanged equation

A result must specify the complete incoming history, retained wake and boundary data, regulator family, solution class, and limiting order. It must then establish convergence of the complete evolved histories on a common time interval, recover every contributing causal root or otherwise prove equivalence of the full acceleration measure, and control the omitted history and singular-event contributions. If an event carries a finite impulse, finiteness alone does not supply a unique outgoing history: the event equation and uniqueness in the claimed class must also hold. Dependence on an arbitrary regulator choice cannot be used to select a physical outcome without a selection rule derived from the Master Equation.

Any claimed conservation or recapture result needs independent justification for its action and wake-history charge. Defining a wake-energy entry as the negative integral of the same realized acceleration power makes the resulting total constant by construction; it does not independently establish conservation or rule out unbounded acceleration-driven motion.

These are mathematical requirements for transferring a proposed result, not a claim that the transfer exists. Until they are met, this program supplies no passage, rebound, recurrence, or coincidence prediction under the unchanged Master Equation.
