# Collinear research

This directory owns the study of architrinos moving along one line: approach, coincidence, passage, braking, turning and recurrence. It compares the Master Equation with separately specified alternatives, preserving their different histories and assumptions.

Start with the [encounter comparison](manuscript.md#1-collinear-encounter-comparison), then [current results and blockers](priorities.md). The [queue](work-queue.md) records existing task authority; [discussion](brainstorming.md) holds provisional material and [work log](work-log.md) records changes.

[Binary research](../binary-research/README.md) owns the single-pair problem across geometries, including the combined Campaign 1. The [Braid Program](../braid-program/README.md) owns multi-binary and other multi-architrino collections. Collinear analyses remain here as the shared line-geometry controls. [Master-Equation Closure](../priorities.md) owns shared equation definitions and general continuation questions. The [former ceiling investigation](../field-speed-ceiling/README.md) is historical.
