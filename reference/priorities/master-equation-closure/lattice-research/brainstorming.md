## A spatially localized self-consistent disturbance

The exact ancient staggered branch proves incoming growth and a first wake-speed event for one coherent infinite-support pattern. It does not establish the response to a disturbance confined to a bounded region. A proposed next investigation is a spatially localized preparation whose complete past also satisfies the unchanged equation, together with a controlled exterior and an admitted summation rule.

The scientific distinction is the preparation. The earlier two-target pulse is valid forward data but is not an unforced solution throughout its supplied past. The coherent ancient branch is self-consistent throughout the past but changes every lattice label. A localized self-consistent construction would answer a different question; neither accepted experiment supplies its trajectory or eventual behavior.

**Grade:** proposed follow-up, not an accepted execution task. Its first useful result would identify a complete admissible local preparation or a precise obstruction to the proposed class. Any claimed continuation would need complete causal reception, controlled tails and environmental response. A proof that only changes the present positions while leaving the required history unspecified would not resolve this preparation question.
