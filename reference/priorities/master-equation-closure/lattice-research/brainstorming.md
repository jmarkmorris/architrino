## A spatially localized self-consistent disturbance

The exact ancient staggered branch proves incoming growth and a first wake-speed event for one coherent infinite-support pattern. It does not establish the response to a disturbance confined to a bounded region. The [queued preparation investigation](work-queue.md#specify-a-spatially-localized-self-consistent-lattice-preparation) owns the construction question; this section retains the distinction between the candidate preparations.

The scientific distinction is the preparation. The earlier two-target pulse is valid forward data but is not an unforced solution throughout its supplied past. The coherent ancient branch is self-consistent throughout the past but changes every lattice label. A localized self-consistent construction would answer a different question; neither accepted experiment supplies its trajectory or eventual behavior.

**Grade:** unproved research target. The task and its acceptance condition are recorded in the queue. Any claimed continuation would need complete causal reception, controlled tails and environmental response. A proof that only changes the present positions while leaving the required history unspecified would not resolve this preparation question.
