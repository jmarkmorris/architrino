# The chart radius does not carry the localized-family estimate further: an exact obstruction for route (a) (2026-10-20)

## Result

**In words.** The handover named a larger chart as a possible route to a later barrier for the localized disturbance of the alternating lattice ("route (a)"): the chart condition $d+2Kd^2\le1/16$, which keeps every member inside the region where the lattice facts are proved, was close to saturation. The [band-envelope note](saturday-lattice-growth-2026-10-20.md) and its [receiver-cubic companion](saturday-lattice-growth-2026-10-20-receiver-cubic.md) lowered the barrier coefficient and made the barrier inequality itself, not only the chart, a limit. This note settles the route for the present estimate. Every constant of the estimate grows with the chart radius, so a larger chart relaxes the chart condition but tightens the barrier inequality, and a smaller chart does the reverse. Exact certificates over a partition of every admissible chart radius show that the two effects never combine to pass seed size $0.034$.

1. **No chart reaches seed size $0.034$ (derived; exact certificates).** For the assembled estimate with the band forward factor and the signed receiver constant, and for every chart radius $\rho$ with $\bar a\rho<1$, there is no barrier coefficient $K$ at any seed size $d\ge0.034$ (Proposition C.2). For $\rho\le1/16$ this concerns the estimate as derived in the accepted notes; for $\rho>1/16$ it concerns the same formulas, whose underlying chart lemmas are not re-derived here.
2. **On the chart $1/16$ the estimate reaches $0.0334$ (derived; exact arithmetic).** With coefficient $13$ the barrier holds at $d=0.0334$ and the chart condition holds with $d+2Kd^2=0.06240456\le1/16$ (Corollary C.3). The same solution therefore exists to $\ln(0.0334/(0.0983\varepsilon))/2.8033$, which exceeds the receiver-cubic endpoint by at least $0.0042$ and the accepted $T_N$ by at least $0.1619$ for every admitted amplitude.
3. **Consequence for route (a).** The largest seed size this estimate can reach on any chart lies in $[0.0334,0.034)$. A chart other than $1/16$ can add at most $\ln(0.034/0.0334)/2.8033<0.0064$ of guaranteed time. Route (a) is closed for this estimate; further progress needs a smaller estimate. The largest of its three parts is the summed odd remainder, and every part carries constants evaluated at the chart radius rather than at the sizes the solution actually has.
4. **What is not obtained.** No statement about the solution: the obstruction bounds what this sufficient estimate can certify. Growth uniform in $\varepsilon$, order-one displacement, the ending of regular evolution and wake-speed arrival remain open.

Claim grade: derived for Lemma C.1, Proposition C.2 and Corollary C.3, by the hand arguments below; the certificates and the barrier check are exact rational arithmetic, with interval exponentials, by a session instrument that passed known cases first. The declared lattice sums and contour constants keep their interval-library conditionality, and the signed receiver constant is the enclosure of the receiver-cubic note, which is under independent review. Falsifiers are in Section 4.

The scenario is unchanged: the Master Equation at $g=16$, unit spacing, $c_f=1$, every member dynamical, every causal root and tail retained, the original eight-source block summation, the contour of radius $1/10$, the accepted seed class, and the complete-past family $u^\varepsilon=\varepsilon\phi+v^\varepsilon$, $0<\varepsilon\le0.00646$.

## 1. The estimate on a chart of radius $\rho$

A chart of radius $\rho$ is the set of histories whose displacements stay at most $\rho$ and whose speeds stay at most $\varpi=\bar a\rho$, with $\bar a=2.6953$. The barrier argument applies the assembled estimate at times where the seed bound $d=\tilde\Delta(t)$ and the correction bound $y=y(t)$ satisfy $d+2y\le\rho$, which keeps the solution in the chart. On such a chart the estimate is the polynomial $F_\rho(d,y)=g[c_{E3}^{\rm s}A^3+\Sigma_{\rm br}+\tfrac12\mathcal O]$, $A=d+y$, of the summed signed note's Theorem L.4, with the band factor $\tilde\mu(\rho)=e^{-(\underline\lambda-a)(1-2\rho)}$ of Corollary B.3 and the signed receiver constant $c_{E3}^{\rm s}(\rho)=c_3+6Z_7(1-\rho)^{-7}\rho^2$ of the receiver-cubic note, $c_3\le14.3469$, $Z_7\le7.46706$.

**Lemma C.1 (the constants grow with the chart; derived).** Every chart-dependent constant entering $F_\rho$ is a nondecreasing function of $\rho$ on $0<\rho<1/\bar a$, and enters every coefficient of $F_\rho$ with a nonnegative sign. The lattice sums do not depend on $\rho$. Hence each coefficient of $F_\rho$ is nondecreasing in $\rho$.

*Proof.* The chart-dependent constants are: the row constants $c_{20},c_{11},c_{02}$ of Lemma Q1 and $c_{30},c_{21},c_{12},c_{03}$ of Lemma L.1, which increase in $\mathsf m\le(1-\varpi^2)^{-1/2}$ and in $1/D$ with $D\ge1-\varpi$, both increasing in $\varpi=\bar a\rho$; the reciprocal range factor $1/\kappa=1/(1-2\rho)$ and the delay factor $\vartheta_0=1/(1-\varpi)$; $\eta=e^{2\bar a\rho}$ and $\mathsf s_1=\eta e^{-a}$; $c_{E3}^{\rm s}(\rho)$; and $\tilde\mu(\rho)$, which increases because $\underline\lambda>a$. In the per-offset bounds of Lemmas L.2 and L.3 and in the summation over offsets, each appears in products and sums with nonnegative coefficients (inspection of the displayed bounds; the instrument checks the summed coefficients for one pair of radii). $\blacksquare$

The constants are finite only while $\varpi<1$, that is $\rho<1/\bar a=0.37101\ldots$; the transmitter-factor bound $D\ge1-\varpi$ fails beyond.

## 2. The obstruction over all chart radii

**Proposition C.2 (derived; exact certificates).** For every chart radius $0<\rho<1/\bar a$, every $d\ge0.034$ and every $y\ge0$ with $d+2y\le\rho$,
$$
y-C_LF_\rho(d,y)<0 .
$$
So no coefficient $K$ gives a barrier at a seed size $d\ge0.034$ on any chart. For $\rho\le1/16$ the estimate is the derived one; for $\rho>1/16$ the statement is about the same formulas.

*Proof.* If $\rho<0.034$ the chart condition already fails, since $d+2y\ge d\ge0.034>\rho$. Cover $[0.034,1/\bar a)$ by finitely many intervals $[r_0,r_1]$. On a chart $\rho\in[r_0,r_1]$, Lemma C.1 gives $F_\rho\ge F^{\rm low}_{r_0}$ coefficientwise, where $F^{\rm low}_{r_0}$ is built from every chart-dependent constant evaluated at $r_0$ and rounded down, with the receiver coefficient lowered to $c_3$ (dropping the nonnegative term in $\rho^2$); and the chart condition gives $y\le(r_1-d)/2$. The polynomial $\varphi(y)=y-C_LF^{\rm low}_{r_0}(0.034,y)$ is concave in $y$, so its maximum on $[0,(r_1-0.034)/2]$ is bounded exactly as in Proposition B.8: at the right endpoint if $\varphi'$ is nonnegative there, at $0$ if $\varphi'(0)\le0$, and otherwise by the tangent at the left end of an exact bisection bracket of the maximizer. The instrument finds a partition into $81$ intervals, of widths from $0.005$ down to $0.000625$, on each of which this bound is negative; the largest of the $81$ bounds is $-8.97\times10^{-6}$, on $[0.054,0.05525]$. For $d>0.034$ the function $y-C_LF^{\rm low}_{r_0}(d,y)$ is smaller at each $y$ (nonnegative coefficients) and the admissible range of $y$ is shorter, so the conclusion holds for every $d\ge0.034$. $\blacksquare$

The tightest bound lies on $[0.054,0.05525]$, slightly below $1/16$, where the chart condition and the barrier inequality bind together. A larger chart does not help because the constants grow faster than the chart condition relaxes: the factor $1/\kappa^5$ alone grows from $1.95$ at $\rho=1/16$ to $2.39$ at $\rho=0.08$. A scratch search, which is exploration and not evidence, found the largest passing half-grid seed size $0.0334$ at $\rho=1/16$, $0.0328$ at $1/15$, $0.0322$ at $0.07$, $0.0310$ at $0.075$ and none at $0.08$, $0.085$ or $0.09$.

**Corollary C.3 (derived, relying on the band-envelope and receiver-cubic notes).** On the chart $\rho=1/16$, with $c^{\rm s}_{E3}\le14.622$ and $\tilde\mu\le0.92274$, the pair $(d,K)=(0.0334,13)$ satisfies, in exact arithmetic: $d+2Kd^2=0.06240456\le1/16$; $C_LF(0.0334,0.01450228)\le0.0143449<0.01450228$; and the start condition $C_LF(d_0,31.9d_0^2)<31.9d_0^2$ at $d_0=0.0006351$. By the proof of Theorem B.5, for every $0<\varepsilon\le0.00646$ the same solution exists to $T_C(\varepsilon)=\ln(0.0334/(0.0983\varepsilon))/2.8033$, with $\lvert u-\varepsilon\phi\rvert<13\,\tilde\Delta^2$ on $[0,T_C]$, displacements at most $0.047903$, speeds at most $0.16820$, accelerations at most $0.66406$, transmitter factors at least $0.83180$, separations at least $0.904195$, each delay within $0.095805$ of the anchor distance, one causal root per ordered pair and no root on a member's own path. $T_C-\hat T=\ln(0.0334/0.033)/2.8033\ge0.0042$, and at $\varepsilon=0.00646$, $T_C-T_N\ge0.1619$; this difference grows with $\ln(1/\varepsilon)$ as in Theorem B.5.

*Proof.* The three conditions are exact evaluations. The rest is the argument of Theorem B.5 with the receiver-cubic polynomial, whose coefficients are nonnegative; the bounds follow from $d+Kd^2=0.04790228$, $\bar a(d+2Kd^2)=0.168199\ldots$, $\bar a^2(d+4Kd^2)=0.664054\ldots$ and the chart facts with radius $0.04790228$. $\blacksquare$

Together, Proposition C.2 and Corollary C.3 bracket the reach of this estimate over all charts between $0.0334$ and $0.034$. The central growth bound of Proposition B.7 applies at $T_C$ with the coefficient $13$; it is not tabulated here.

## 3. Instrument, evidence and the reference boundary

[`chart_route.py`](../evidence/saturday-lattice-growth-2026-10-20/chart_route.py), described in the [evidence README](../evidence/saturday-lattice-growth-2026-10-20/README.md), imports the band-envelope instrument unchanged and through it the frozen accepted instruments. It builds the lower-rounded polynomials $F^{\rm low}_{r_0}$ from the frozen per-offset routines, with every chart constant taken from lower interval endpoints, and runs the adaptive partition for candidate seed sizes $0.0340,0.0345,\dots$, stopping at the first fully certified one; the first, $0.034$, succeeded. Known cases, run before any target: the lower-rounded chart-$1/16$ constants lie below the declared ones and within $0.1$ percent of them; the chunk-1 certificate value $-8.2514\times10^{-5}$ at $d=0.0317$ is reproduced; the bounded certificate is right on two hand polynomials; and the constants and all summed coefficients increase from $\rho=0.07$ to $\rho=0.08$, a sample of Lemma C.1. It computes no history and encloses no lattice sum.

## 4. Falsifiers

- Lemma C.1: a chart-dependent constant of the estimate that decreases with $\rho$, or one that enters a coefficient with a negative sign.
- Proposition C.2: a chart radius $\rho<1/\bar a$, a seed size $d\ge0.034$ and $y\ge0$ with $d+2y\le\rho$ and $y\ge C_LF_\rho(d,y)$; or an interval of the recorded partition whose exact bound is not negative.
- Corollary C.3: an exact evaluation in which one of its three conditions fails with the declared constants; a complete-past solution of the family that violates one of its bounds before $T_C$.

## 5. Limits

The obstruction concerns this sufficient estimate, with its declared constants and its chart condition $d+2y\le\rho$; another estimate, another way of keeping the solution in a chart, or a different norm is not covered. In particular it does not cover an estimate whose row constants, range factor and delay factor are evaluated at the actual receiver and source sizes on the barrier set rather than at the chart radius; that refinement, which removes the chart radius from the constants, is the subject of the [tube-constant note](saturday-lattice-growth-2026-10-20-tube.md). For chart radii above $1/16$ the chart lemmas themselves are not re-derived here, so the statement there is about the formulas, not a theorem on those charts. Every limit of the band-envelope and receiver-cubic notes is inherited.
