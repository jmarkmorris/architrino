### EPRB-001 — Decide the scope of “decided at entanglement”

- **Status:** discussion-scoped
- **Priority object:** `decided_at_entanglement_assumption_scope`
- **Question:** Does the intended phrase preserve both local response and
  statistical independence of the later detector settings from the pair's
  complete past state?
- **Evidence / blocker:** If both conditions are preserved, a passive source
  record is inside $\lambda$ and obeys the CHSH bound under valid sampling. If
  either condition is rejected, the actual changed assumption and physical
  mechanism must be named before the phrase has determinate content. A
  one-particle result decided before a local detector click is not yet this
  two-wing claim; the intended pair statement must supply a response law over
  independently selected setting pairs. Determinism and “realism” do not add
  separate mathematical premises, and a deterministic setting generator does
  not by itself violate measurement independence. See the focused
  [Maudlin EPR source-mining packet](../source-mining/tim-maudlin-epr-bell-video-source-mining.md)
  and [Maudlin Bell-foundations packet](../source-mining/tim-maudlin-bell-foundations-2022-video-source-mining.md).
- **Completion:** The operator answers the question and the lane records the
  selected assumption scope without yet promoting a mechanism.
