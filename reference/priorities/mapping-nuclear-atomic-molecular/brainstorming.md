# Nuclear, Atomic, and Molecular Recovery Synthesis

This synthesis organizes the recovery hierarchy from nucleon assemblies through nuclei, atoms, molecules, and biomolecular records. Each level consumes accepted lower-level records and must not import its effective description as substrate ontology.

## Shared Recovery Form

Every target combines a declared assembly state, path-history or reaction transition, conserved-account ledger, Noether sea or material response where required, independent observer-level benchmark, and explicit falsifier. A higher-level label such as nucleus, orbital, bond, or enzyme names an effective assembly pattern rather than a new primitive.

Plainly: the hierarchy adds larger physical organizations, not new fundamental substances or laws.

## Atomic Structure and Orbitals

Atomic orbitals are effective occupancy and readout patterns for localized electron and nuclear assemblies, not ontological probability clouds. The first bridge must distinguish standard orbital mathematics and spectra from candidate assembly geometry, exclusion envelopes, resonance states, transition ledgers, and ambient Noether sea coarse-graining.

Hydrogen and helium provide the cleanest initial boundary cases. Periodic families and chemical trends remain downstream summaries and cannot be inserted into the assembly model as organizing premises.
