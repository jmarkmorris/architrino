## Classification Rule

Each chapter receives exactly one of the four levels already defined by the paper:

| Level | Audit rule |
| --- | --- |
| `architecture-ready` | The chapter names a native $\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}$ mechanism and a discriminating test surface. Derivations or coefficients may still be open. |
| `direction-ready` | A plausible native route is named, but a mechanism, theorem, transfer function, retained branch, or quantitative bridge is still too incomplete for architecture-ready treatment. |
| `appendix-watch` | The topic is useful as comparison pressure or a secondary stress test, but it is not a central closure route with a mature native mechanism. |
| `exclude-for-now` | The topic lacks a shared native mechanism and discriminating physics test, or lies outside the paper's current physics scope. |

The primary owner is the one current priority lane responsible for the next unresolved burden. Supporting lanes may contribute, but they do not create a second owner in this audit. For excluded chapters, Mapping Open Problems owns the exclusion boundary; no scientific proof burden is activated until a later scope decision supplies a native mechanism and test.
