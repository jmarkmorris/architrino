## Inherited tensions and required interpretation

**I1 — Confirmed muon falsifier scope tension; retain the narrow qualification.** Canonical lines 821 and 823 say convergence to the Standard Model expectation falsifies the proposal, while line 827 explicitly retains architecture-ready precision-benchmark status and denies anomaly-driven promotion. The claim audit's chapter 38 requires a common electron/muon/tau response grammar without anomaly-specific fitting. Test-contract row 16 is more precise: failure occurs if a correction disappears while the proposed mechanism still predicts it. Thus Standard Model convergence falsifies a registered nonzero discrepancy correction whose uncertainty excludes zero; it does not falsify every candidate assembly record that reproduces the standard observable values. The coverage already identifies that distinction at line 45. This review confirms it as the necessary acceptance interpretation, rather than leaving the question unresolved. The literal broader canonical sentence remains an inherited editorial tension; this campaign disposition does not repair or certify it. A later canonical wording repair can restrict “the proposal” to the nonzero correction route, but no new scientific task or source edit is authorized by this report.

**I2 — Cutoff and shielding claims remain proposed, and “field-speed limit” is not adopted.** Canonical line 318 uses that phrase in a candidate cosmological-constant route. Other entries also propose maximal-curvature cutoffs, terminal alignment, near-field-speed rows and bounded exposure. The coverage correctly refuses to infer a universal architrino speed ceiling or ultraviolet completeness from this language. Finite geometry, a shielding label, or a taxonomy member does not establish the requested numerical suppression, retained branch, stability or effective law. These are inherited scientific burdens, not new results of retaining the chapter.

**I3 — Entropy and reversibility need a declared comparison domain.** Canonical lines 515 and 517 use broad microscopic time-symmetry language, line 525 uses “largely,” and the intervening paragraphs distinguish windowed measure/flux accounting and dynamical relaxation from typicality. The chapter and audit's macroscopic-reversal falsifier can test a particular wake-memory prediction only after the closed-system boundary, measure, observation window, meaning of large record growth, tolerance and predicted reversal behavior are specified. This review does not certify universal time-reversal invariance, a global entropy theorem, primitive self-hit dissipation or absolute impossibility of reversal. The coverage's qualification is appropriate; instantiating the contract remains scientific work.

**I4 — Readiness and the strength of a contract are editorial classifications.** A named mechanism and a discriminating test surface do not certify a retained source, mathematical theorem, numerical recovery or experimental fit. Some public architecture paragraphs use declarative shorthand for candidate geometry or response, while their detailed routes and claim levels keep derivation open. The detailed test audit is supporting contract specification, not evidence that all gates have run or all public paragraphs spell them out. The source work log's earlier maintenance counts are historical instrument reports and were not re-executed here.

**I5 — External freshness and broader scientific correctness are unverified.** The full paper contains dated cosmological and precision-particle statements, comparative claims about other frameworks, and links to scientific owners not read here. This assignment did not verify those dates, numerical values, significance levels, theoretical generalizations or the adequacy of every primary citation. No currentness or physics-certification claim follows from full local reading or from a correct coverage map. The paired Crisis cross-map was not audited; its routing remains an outside dependency.

No additional defect in the new coverage proposal was found beyond the inherited matters it already exposes and the supporting-detail distinction clarified above. The smallest acceptance action is to record that I1 is confirmed and retain I2–I5, not to create a second manuscript or silently treat the canonical paper as scientifically closed.
