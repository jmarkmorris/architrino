## Inherited tensions requiring explicit review

1. **Muon precision recovery and a nonzero anomaly proposal are different claims.** The paper's secure record and classification retain precision recovery when a Standard Model discrepancy recedes. Its detailed-route and resolution-test paragraphs nevertheless say the proposal fails if observations converge to the standard expectation. The independent review confirms the necessary qualification: that falsifier rejects a preregistered nonzero anomaly correction whose prediction excludes zero; it cannot by itself reject every underlying assembly response that reproduces the standard result. The test-contract row likewise concerns a predicted correction disappearing. The broader canonical wording remains an inherited scope tension, explicitly bounded here. No experiment or scientific owner was reanalyzed here.
2. **Cutoff and shielding routes are unproved.** The cosmological-constant entry uses “field-speed limit” wording while discussing a proposed constraint. This cannot establish a universal primitive velocity ceiling. Other passages keep maximal-curvature, shielding, finite-mode and early-state mechanisms at target grade. This coverage does not elevate those proposals or equate finite geometry with an ultraviolet-completion proof.
3. **Entropy needs its declared measure and boundary.** The paper includes broad time-symmetry language, a narrower “largely” statement and an explicit windowed entropy/flux qualification. Its mechanism remains an unproved wake-memory/relaxation route. Neither the audit nor this disposition certifies universal time-reversal symmetry, a global entropy theorem or microscopic dissipation.
4. **Classification is editorial maturity.** Architecture-ready names a mechanism and test surface; it does not certify a solution, mathematical theorem or admitted branch. The chapter and both audits state this limit. Source claims about historical maintenance checks are preserved as reports, not repeated as present scientific verification.

These items distinguish the campaign's coverage task from a new scientific investigation or unauthorized canonical rewrite. An independent finding of substantive omission, unsupported strengthening, or an inadequate existing developed owner defeats the proposed acceptance until it is explicitly dispositioned. Existing source preservation does not excuse concealing such a finding.
