## Explicit qualifications and review questions

1. **Area normalization.** The older strategy asks whether raw per-block log growth tends to one quarter. The later complete entropy contract derives alpha=s/a and requires independent area calibration. Section 4.3 follows that more precise result and exposes the missing limit/calibration rather than silently importing a unit area density.
2. **Horizon support and residual units.** Finite-window interface admission cannot establish a global event horizon; a scalar weighted or maximum summary cannot waive component checks. A distance or boundary norm in an alignment maximum needs declared units/normalization beside dimensionless speed ratios. No evaluator is instantiated here.
3. **Event compatibility is not automatically transitive.** Source wording calls the existence of a common fitting family an equivalence relation. Section 6.2 supplies the logical counterexample structure: A/B can share P and B/C share Q without a common A/C family. A fixed predeclared family and accepted domain, or a separate transitivity proof, is required before grouping. This qualifies the proposed rule without changing its original bytes or creating an astrophysical classification.
4. **Exact surface intersection.** The holographic ratio uses area of an exact intersection, not distance to the horizon. Near-horizon approach does not by itself imply a positive intersection area. Section 4.4 preserves the proposed comparison with measurable-surface and nonzero-denominator conditions, without claiming the suggested limiting behavior has been proved.
5. **Release angular momentum.** The strategy's reduced jet-recoil J sum has no separately named radiation-J term. Section 5.2 requires explicit ownership of that contribution inside a declared channel or the complete event account. It does not silently set radiation J to zero or alter the source diagnostic.
6. **Primitive language and inherited editorial damage.** The terminal decision's primitive-level force wording is expressed as acceleration in the manuscript under current canon. The nested brainstorming heading is visibly malformed; its actual equation-coverage table is integrated without reproducing the damaged heading. No causal attribution or source repair is claimed.
7. **External and carrier gaps.** Full local source reading does not validate EHT dates/numbers, Kerr/RT/Maldacena primary-source attribution, all domain-specific benchmark validity assumptions, or the global absence of a carrier. The manuscript reports the live owners' missing-carrier boundary. Other owners or a changed snapshot could require revision; no broad repository absence claim was measured.

The independent reviewer should compare both directions, including whether supporting links retain every omitted detailed equation/negative control, and whether the new logical qualifications are correctly bounded.
