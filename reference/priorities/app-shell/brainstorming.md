# Shell Open Ideas

Accepted design work lives in [the queue](work-queue.md); the developed explanation lives in [the manuscript](manuscript.md). These extensions remain provisional.

## Energy, size and frequency

**Theory level:** assembly-level speculation. **Claim grade: guessed.** The operator proposes that increasing energy selects smaller, faster assemblies whose paths reorganize across scales. Initial speed, shape and coupling can be varied now as preparation variables, but identifying them with physical energy requires the same-history account and branch-selection equations. A verified branch with opposite size trend would falsify a universal shrinkage rule. The next useful artifact is the scientific owner's energy/account derivation, not an energy-labeled app control. A future reader-facing result belongs with the relevant assembly dynamics only after that derivation and verification.

## Near-surface motion and changing boundaries

**Theory level:** experimental model design. **Claim grade: guessed.** A finite thickness band may reveal whether exact surface organization survives small normal excursions. A band needs a specified restoring or boundary rule, and changing the shape in time adds boundary-account questions. Results dominated by that added rule would falsify an interpretation as free assembly organization. The next artifact, if selected, is a separate equation specification with its support account; neither extension is implicitly included in fixed-surface evolution.

## Other closed shapes and populations

**Theory level:** geometric comparison. **Claim grade: guessed.** Other smooth closed surfaces and polarity distributions may reveal dependence on curvature and shape symmetry. Evidence requires equivalent preparation classes, actual evolving histories and separately declared readouts. If a proposed curvature dependence disappears under controlled preparation comparisons, that interpretation fails. Sphere and ellipsoid support are the first scope; additional shapes remain proposals rather than an executable broad search.
