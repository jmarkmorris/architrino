# Shell — Closed-Surface Path Research

**Shell** is the proposed application name; `app-shell` is its priority-folder identity. The app studies distributions of architrinos whose future paths are calculated from their supplied histories and delayed interactions while an explicit artificial constraint keeps them on a fixed closed surface. The first case is three electrinos and three positrinos on a sphere; ellipsoids follow.

This folder opens the app design workstream. It contains no executable app or new simulation result. The name is a working proposal for operator review.

- [Priorities](priorities.md) — purpose, current disposition, dependencies and ownership.
- [Manuscript](manuscript.md) — research questions, constrained equation and interpretation.
- [Requirements and design](contracts/requirements-and-design.md) — first-version scope and solver boundary.
- [Work queue](work-queue.md) — next design object and dependent implementation stages.
- [Brainstorming](brainstorming.md) — unresolved extensions and interpretations.
- [Work log](work-log.md) — authorization, inspection and validation records.
