## Candidate Statement

Use generic binary labels first. The proposed common-frequency family is

$$
(f_1,f_2,f_3)=(f,f,f),
\qquad
f\in\mathbb Z_{>0}.
$$

Equivalently,

$$
\omega_1=\omega_2=\omega_3=\omega_f,
$$

while the phase offsets remain free branch data in the input row order:

$$
\Theta_{123}
=
\left(
\phi_2-\phi_1,
\phi_3-\phi_2
\right).
$$

The first high-priority phase profile is the triadic spacing suggested by the equal-frequency clock:

$$
\left(\phi_1,\phi_2,\phi_3\right)
=
\left(0,\frac{2\pi}{3},\frac{4\pi}{3}\right),
\qquad
\Theta_{123}
=
\left(\frac{2\pi}{3},\frac{2\pi}{3}\right).
$$

Equivalently, the three binaries are separated by $120^\circ$ or one third of a turn in the displayed input order. All $S_3$ permutations of this profile are the same quotient-sector candidate but should remain valid repeated solver rows unless an explicit quotient-sector view is being used. Other phase offsets remain searchable, but this triadic profile is the first candidate because it uses the common clock without privileging one binary phase.

The speed rows are not frequency rows. In a role-assigned chart one targeted speed profile is

$$
s_I=\rho_I\omega_f>c_f,
\qquad
s_M=\rho_M\omega_f\approx c_f,
\qquad
s_O=\rho_O\omega_f<c_f.
$$

Here $\rho_\ell$ is the retained effective lever-arm projection entering the local binary speed row. Earlier shorthand $R_\ell$ in this packet should be read as this effective lever arm unless a retained branch radius or projection map has been derived. Thus the candidate requires an energy-radius law that admits distinct retained lever-arm projections

$$
\rho_I>\rho_M>\rho_O
$$

in the simple monotone speed-row model after the retained branch supplies the $I:M:O$ role map. This inequality is not a pre-search ordering of the three binaries. It is not automatically the global nested-shell coordinate or the radius of a common sphere. If the retained shell geometry uses a different radial ordering, the proof must exhibit the corresponding effective-lever-arm map rather than assuming the simple ordering. The middle-tagged binary remains on the $c_f$ edge by lever arm and energy placement, not by having a different frequency.

## General Tri-Binary Noether Braid Geometry

The baseline object is the retained all-layer tri-binary Noether braid branch state. It is not a fixed sphere by itself, not a flattened chart by itself, and not three independent orbits chosen before the branch is solved. The low-drift state is a spherical or near-spherical branch with retained angular-momentum / principal-direction rows; as the branch carries drift velocity, the same object deforms through an oblate spheroidal envelope toward a flattened limit. In the Lorentz/return-cycle bridge notation, a branch state has the form

$$
B_{3B}(q,v)
=
\left(
R_{\parallel},R_{\perp},\xi;\,
\omega_a,J_a,\phi_a,\mathbf{N}_a;\,
\rho_a,s_a;\,
\mathbf{P}_{\mathrm{branch}},\mathbf{J}_{\mathrm{branch}};\,
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{root}};\,
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{wake}};\,
E_{\mathrm{branch}};\,
\mathrm{role\ map};\,
\mathrm{coupling\ rows}
\right)_q,
$$

where $a\in\{1,2,3\}$ names the generic binaries before any $I:M:O$ role map is attached. Here $\mathbf{N}_a$ denotes a plane-sector normal or angular-momentum bivector when that sector chart is certified, $\rho_a$ is the retained effective-lever-arm projection emitted by the branch state, and the coupling rows are whatever external assembly, field, or retained event interacts with the tri-binary Noether braid. The branch-total momentum and angular-momentum ledgers include retained wake contribution. A return-map pass is not enough for promotion if the reported principal-direction rows fail to decompose $\mathbf{J}_{\mathrm{branch}}$ or if the moving-envelope axis is inconsistent with $\mathbf{P}_{\mathrm{branch}}$. The retained replay must solve a velocity-deformation map

$$
\mathcal D_v(B_{3B}):
\text{spherical or near-spherical support with retained principal-direction rows}
\longrightarrow
\text{oblate spheroidal envelope}
\longrightarrow
\text{flattened or planar limit},
$$

and emit $\rho_\ell(v)$ only as a projection of that branch state. The velocity-dependent geometry must include the known deformation sequence:

1. low-drift or rest branch: spherical or near-spherical three-dimensional support with retained angular-momentum / principal-direction rows;
2. moving branch: oblate spheroidal envelope with shape channel $\xi=R_{\parallel}/R_{\perp}$;
3. field-speed alignment limit: flattened or planar branch support.

The current executable row is therefore a flattened-limit projection view of this general case, not the model itself. It is one member of a deformation projection-chart family that also contains low-drift spherical-envelope retained-row and moving oblate-incidence rows. The current view can populate causal roots, self-hit spans, phase rows, and speed-row projections. It proves only that the algebraic speed relation can be populated with a common $\omega_f$ and different effective lever-arm projections.

For a retained replay, the proof must choose how $\rho_\ell$ is produced by the tri-binary Noether braid branch:

1. Low-drift spherical or near-spherical support, with small-circle or latitude lever arms:

$$
\rho_\ell=R_{\mathrm{sph}}\sin\theta_\ell,
\qquad
s_\ell=\rho_\ell\omega_f.
$$

In this case equal frequency can still give different speeds if the retained branch energy selects different $\theta_\ell$ or an equivalent retained-plane incidence. A hinge row becomes $\rho_h\omega_f\approx c_f$ only after the retained branch identifies which binary plays that role.

2. Moving oblate spheroidal support, where the branch supplies $\rho_\ell$ through envelope axes, retained-plane incidence, retained-row continuation, and the shape/scale channels:

$$
R_{\parallel}(v,E,n)=\frac{\lambda(v,E,n)R_0}{\gamma_\star(v)},
\qquad
R_{\perp}(v,E,n)=\lambda(v,E,n)R_0.
$$

Here $\rho_\ell$ is a projected lever arm inside the deformed envelope, not a raw shell radius.

3. Common-sphere great-circle control:

$$
\rho_O=\rho_M=\rho_I=R_{\mathrm{sph}}.
$$

In this case equal frequency forces equal speed magnitude in the simple kinematics, so it cannot by itself realize the outer/middle/inner speed ordering. A proof using great circles would need another speed definition, projection, or causal-delay row.

4. Nested shell, branch-radius, or flattened-alignment support:

$$
\rho_\ell=R_\ell^{\mathrm{eff}}\left(J_\ell;\lambda_\ell\right),
$$

where the retained energy/radius law supplies different effective lever arms. This is closest to the reduced runner only if $\rho_\ell$ is treated as a branch projection, not as the nested shell radius itself. The sampled equal-frequency speed rows force $\rho_I>\rho_M>\rho_O$ only inside the displayed role map, so a direct nested-shell-radius reading with $\rho_I<\rho_M<\rho_O$ is incompatible unless the retained replay derives a projection, incidence, envelope-deformation, or retained branch map that separates lever arm from shell radius.

The working target is therefore not a completed flattened-radius, fixed-sphere, or single-chart geometry claim. It is: equal-frequency binaries may have different retained effective lever-arm projections inside the velocity-deforming tri-binary Noether braid, and those projections may produce different speed relations while the same branch continues from spherical or near-spherical support through oblate support toward the flattened limit.

## Why This Is A Distinct Hypothesis

The current offset candidate

$$
(I,M,O)=(f+2,f,f-1)
$$

is one role-assigned subfamily of the unordered three-binary search. It tests whether the inner-tagged layer's doubled self-hit burden appears as a phase-lock or resonance-index offset. The equal-frequency candidate tests the opposite branch mechanism: the transaction may have a common clock, while any doubled-burden role appears through causal-root multiplicity, phase offset, branch incidence, action partition, wake routing, or energy/radius placement.

This matters because the present branch-certificate ranking privileges the index proxy

$$
n_I-n_M=2.
$$

For $(f_1,f_2,f_3)=(f,f,f)$, this proxy is zero by construction. A naive replay that scores the equal-frequency family only by self-root parity index coverage would reject the wrong question. The equal-frequency packet needs separate rows:

1. common-frequency closure;
2. phase-offset row-set identity;
3. energy-radius or lever-arm speed-regime placement;
4. branch-incidence or root-multiplicity support for the doubled inner burden;
5. retained angular-momentum and wake-energy routing on the same event or positive-width retained domain.

## Energy-Radius Proof Target

The branch energy/radius claim must be proved from the retained dynamics, not assigned. A clean proof target is:

$$
\omega_f
=
\frac{\partial H_a}{\partial J_a}
\left(J_a;\lambda_a\right),
\qquad
\rho_a
=
\rho_a\left(J_a;\lambda_a\right),
\qquad
a\in\{1,2,3\},
$$

where $H_a$ is the reduced branch Hamiltonian or energy functional, $J_a$ is the branch angular momentum, and $\lambda_a$ denotes retained layer data such as root sheet, wake charge, polarity assignment, or branch geometry. The proof must exhibit three retained branch states with the same $\omega_f$ but different $J_a$, $\rho_a$, and $s_a=\rho_a\omega_f$. Only after that row is retained should the solver attach a role map such as slow/hinge/fast or outer/middle/inner.

A minimal sanity check is the reduced circular-path kinetic proxy

$$
J_a=\mu_a \rho_a^2\omega_f,
\qquad
E_{\mathrm{kin},a}
=
\frac{1}{2}\mu_a \rho_a^2\omega_f^2
=
\frac{1}{2}J_a\omega_f.
$$

This proxy does not prove the retained branch. It only shows why the idea is physically coherent: at fixed $\omega_f$, different effective lever arms give different speeds, angular momenta, and energies. The real proof must replace the proxy with the delayed branch action, wake-history terms, and root-ledger constraints.

## Relation To The Angular-Momentum Unit

This candidate may simplify the one-unit angular-momentum ledger. The existing packet often writes the unit as $\hbar$ in energy-frequency rows such as

$$
\Delta E=\omega_{\text{tx}}\hbar.
$$

For a four-substep transaction with weights $1:1:2$, the current weighted frequency target is

$$
\omega_\ast
=
\frac{\omega_O+\omega_M+2\omega_I}{4}.
$$

Under the equal-frequency family this collapses to

$$
\omega_\ast=\omega_f.
$$

That is the main attraction: the angular-momentum unit can remain one conserved action increment, while energy differences are carried by the common transaction frequency, tri-binary Noether braid lever arms, phase offsets, wake routing, envelope deformation, and branch energy placement. The candidate therefore may reduce the need to explain a distinct weighted transaction frequency before the angular-momentum unit is accepted.

This is not yet an acceptance claim. The proof burden is to show that the same retained event can carry one angular-momentum unit through the common-frequency branch without hiding missing angular momentum in the wake, recoil, transport, or endpoint-provider rows.
