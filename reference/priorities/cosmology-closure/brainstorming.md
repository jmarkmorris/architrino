### Shedding + Ordered-Core Recycling — Candidate Cyclic Mechanism (2026-07-11)

Claim level: **speculation**. Directional cosmology hypothesis; not a derivation, not promotable to `content/markdown/aaa` until the entropy obligation below is discharged and it is checked against observed cosmology.

- **The idea.** Assemblies may be created relatively high-energy and shed toward equilibrium over cosmic time. A slow, universal shedding of this kind is a candidate *microphysical driver* of the assembly-scale evolution the corpus already identifies with expansion (the effective scale factor $a(t)\propto\langle R_\perp\rangle$ in [expansion-mechanism](../../../content/markdown/aaa/cosmology/expansion-mechanism.md), tied to the Lorentz scale channel $\lambda$). It is **not** tired light: the redshift lives in the *emitters/scale evolving over time*, not in photon energy-loss *in transit*.
- **Falsification guardrails (must be respected).** Photon-transit energy loss (tired light) is refuted by Type Ia supernova light-curve time dilation $\propto(1+z)$ and the Tolman surface-brightness test; any version here must reproduce both, plus CMB blackbody, BBN, and structure formation. The AAA photon is the $\xi\to0$ null endpoint and is plausibly *non-radiating by construction*, so it is naturally exempt from transit loss — consistent with those tests.
- **The heat-death problem.** Pure shedding runs down to a maximum-entropy floor where nothing sheds further (classical heat death) — which would halt the redshift engine. A source is required to refill the supply.
- **The proposed refill: black-hole ordered cores as recyclers.** In AAA there is no true singularity — the interior is a finite, ultra-dense, *ordered* core (candidate single-microstate / low-entropy condensate, the $\xi\to0$ coherent limit; see [singularity-resolution](../../../content/markdown/aaa/spacetime/singularity-resolution.md), [black-holes](../../../content/markdown/aaa/spacetime/black-holes.md)). An ordered low-entropy core is the kind of "fresh fuel" reservoir a recycler needs, which a standard maximum-entropy black hole cannot supply. Infalling assemblies could be reprocessed at the ordered core and re-emitted, closing a shedding/refill loop that avoids heat death. This is the AAA-specific loophole: the recycler is possible *because* the core is ordered, not a max-entropy dead end.
- **The single make-or-break obligation — a closed entropy ledger.** A perpetual shedding/refill loop is one step from perpetual motion. The hypothesis is viable only if a concrete accounting shows the ordered core returns lower-entropy assemblies than it swallowed, *and* names where the exported disorder goes (radiated to infinity? stored? balanced globally). Without that ledger it is ruled out on the second law alone.
- **Owning destinations / cross-links:** cosmology-closure (this lane); [expansion-mechanism](../../../content/markdown/aaa/cosmology/expansion-mechanism.md) ($a(t)=\langle R_\perp\rangle$), [cross-theory-mapping/cosmological-redshift-distance-ladder](../cross-theory-mapping/cosmological-redshift-distance-ladder.md), [singularity-resolution](../../../content/markdown/aaa/spacetime/singularity-resolution.md), [black-holes](../../../content/markdown/aaa/spacetime/black-holes.md).
