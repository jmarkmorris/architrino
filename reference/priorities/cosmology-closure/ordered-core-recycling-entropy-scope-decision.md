# Ordered-Core Recycling And Entropy-Ledger Scope Decision

**Decision:** retain ordered-core recycling as speculation and formalize only its entropy-ledger feasibility target (operator option (a), 2026-07-12). **Claim level:** thermodynamic closure target plus speculative mechanism. **Authority boundary:** this decision does not establish an ordered low-entropy core, a recycling cycle, a cosmological source term, an entropy decrease, or a redshift mechanism. It authorizes no simulation, corpus promotion, or new validation infrastructure.

## Retired Route

Free-steady-particle radiative shedding is not an admissible source for this mechanism. The corrected steady-braid result finds bound far fields for the steady velocity-field channel, and the Master Equation contains no separately appended acceleration-field term. The recycling hypothesis therefore cannot use a universal slow radiation loss from freely propagating stable particles as either:

- a cosmological redshift driver;
- the entropy-export channel that pays for core ordering; or
- a replenishment loop that keeps stable particles running.

Non-radiative scale or mass evolution remains a distinct cosmology program. It is not evidence for ordered-core recycling.

## Speculative Mechanism Retained

The retained hypothesis is narrower:

1. strong-field processing may transform infalling assemblies or Noether sea states;
2. an outgoing assembly, photon, gravitational, reaction, or medium-relaxation channel may carry processed energy and entropy away; and
3. the outgoing state may be more ordered than the infalling coarse-grained assembly only if the full event exports at least the compensating entropy elsewhere.

The words `ordered core` name a candidate state class. They do not establish that a black-hole interior is a single microstate, low entropy, or capable of returning fresh low-entropy assemblies.

## Formal Entropy-Ledger Target

For one declared strong-field event window $W$, separate the coarse-grained entropy changes into core/interior, outgoing resolved carriers, surrounding Noether sea or material medium, horizon-access partition, and unobserved remainder rows:

$$
\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}(W)
=
\Delta S_{\mathrm{core}}
+\Delta S_{\mathrm{out}}
+\Delta S_{\mathrm{med}}
+\Delta S_{\mathrm{hor}}
+\Delta S_{\mathrm{rem}}.
$$

The feasibility condition is

$$
\Delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}(W)\ge0.
$$

If the proposed processing orders the core or the returned assembly so that its local contribution is negative, the populated export rows must overpay that decrease:

$$
-\Delta S_{\mathrm{ordered}}
\le
\Delta S_{\mathrm{out}}
+\Delta S_{\mathrm{med}}
+\Delta S_{\mathrm{hor}}
+\Delta S_{\mathrm{rem}}.
$$

This is an observer-level coarse-grained ledger. Fine-grained record preservation does not by itself supply a local entropy decrease, erase the second law, or populate any export row.

Every entropy term must be tied to the same energy, momentum, angular-momentum, architrino-inventory, polarity, wake-history, and identity-routing event record. A numerical entropy balance assembled from different source histories does not establish feasibility.

## Admissible Export Sources

An export row may be considered only when it is sourced by a non-steady event already admitted by the radiation or strong-field architecture:

- acceleration, deceleration, curved transport, or a sharp Noether sea gradient;
- gravitational or horizon-interface evolution;
- a reaction, capture, merger, dissociation, or branch transition;
- a resolved photon, gravitational-wave, particle, jet, diffuse-outflow, or medium-excitation channel;
- irreversible medium relaxation or thermalization with a named source depletion row.

Naming an allowed channel does not prove that it occurs. The event must populate a finite source loss, outgoing or medium gain, recoil/remnant row, and entropy contribution on the same record.

## Required Feasibility Record

The smallest useful event packet must expose:

1. the infalling assembly, radiation, and Noether sea state;
2. the claimed ordered-core or ordered-output coarse-graining and its state count;
3. all outgoing carrier identities and energy / momentum / angular-momentum ledgers;
4. local medium heating, wake storage, recoil, remnant, and unresolved rows;
5. horizon-access and observer coarse-graining conventions;
6. entropy before and after under the same partition;
7. the full inequality above with no missing positive export term inferred from the desired result;
8. a failure verdict when the ordered state, export source, or same-record binding is absent.

This target should reuse the existing strong-field, radiation, and Noether sea ledgers. It does not justify another standalone gate or checker until a concrete event record exists to consume it.

## Cosmological Gate

Even a thermodynamically legal local recycling event does not establish a cosmological mechanism. A population-level source requires formation, feeding, processing, release, and Noether sea transport histories, plus a rate and spectrum. The resulting effective history must preserve at least:

- Type Ia supernova light-curve time dilation;
- Tolman surface-brightness behavior;
- CMB blackbody quality, anisotropy, and distance-to-last-scattering constraints;
- BBN abundance constraints;
- BAO and structure-formation / growth constraints;
- ordinary accretion, merger, dust, jet, and environmental alternatives.

The recycling mechanism remains speculation until both the local entropy ledger and this population-to-observation transfer close.

## Failure And Rejection Conditions

Reject the ordered-core recycling mechanism if any of the following holds:

- no ordered state is defined by an explicit coarse-graining or state-count reduction;
- the required negative local entropy contribution is asserted rather than computed;
- no accelerated, gravitational, reaction, or medium-relaxation source populates the export row;
- total coarse-grained entropy decreases on the declared closed event record;
- the only proposed driver is free-steady radiative shedding;
- the population history spoils the cosmological constraints above or is unnecessary after ordinary astrophysical explanations close the data.

Failure of this speculative mechanism does not challenge the standard second law or the already-promoted effective discussion of heat-death openings.
