# Cosmic Civics Work Queue

This is the canonical execution ledger for accepted Cosmic Civics work. [priorities.md](priorities.md) owns strategy and status, [concept-and-scope.md](concept-and-scope.md) owns the stable boundary, and [brainstorming.md](brainstorming.md) holds proposals and open questions.

## Rules

1. Promote an item here only after it has an accepted, testable completion condition.
2. Keep legal and philosophical questions in question form until an appropriate authority and review method are declared.
3. Keep maps, coordinate records, and hypothetical claims separate from the validity or enforceability of any claimed right.
4. Do not turn `Passage` or `Claim` into canonical $\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}$ terminology without an explicit terminology decision.
5. Do not infer a reference-frame-selection law from an operational map, moving-system chart, or relative-location record.
