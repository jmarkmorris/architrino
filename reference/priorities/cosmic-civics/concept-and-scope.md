# Cosmic Civics Concept And Scope

- Kind: `priority-concept`

- Legal status: `no legal opinion or validity claim`
- Theory status: `no change`

## Purpose

Cosmic Civics asks how civilization maps human activity and asserted rights over nature, beginning with familiar land, water, and air questions and extending the inquiry toward outer space, moving systems, relative operational locations, and hypothetical future claims over passage through declared regions.

The original impetus is a GIS-like stack of thematic maps. Possible layers include rights, jurisdiction, proximity, liability, use, access, and exclusion. A layer records what a source, institution, observer, or scenario says about a region or activity. It does not by itself establish that the statement is legally valid, physically realizable, enforceable, exclusive, or authorized.

## Working Chain

> Absolute Timespace → Assembly Architecture → Passage → Claim

The chain is a proposed organizing lens, not a derivation or doctrine.

| Link | Classification | Bounded meaning in this packet | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Absolute Timespace** | Established corpus terminology | The formal product background of absolute time and the Euclidean void, used only when that full product structure matters. | A legal geography, ownership substrate, curved relativistic spacetime, or reference-frame-selection rule. |
| **Assembly Architecture** | Proposed conceptual bridge | A packet-local reminder that any actor, vehicle, habitat, instrument, institution, or record is represented through physical and organizational structure rather than as a disembodied claim. The phrase does not add a new canonical layer to the Architrino Assembly Architecture. | A new assembly theorem, actor taxonomy, rights-bearing ontology, or change to the theory name. |
| **Passage** | Proposed concept | A source-bound description of movement or occupancy through a declared region over a declared interval, with explicit chart, scale, resolution, and provenance. | Permission, trespass, ownership, liability, a privileged operational frame, or proof that the passage can occur. |
| **Claim** | Open legal/philosophical concept | An assertion associated with a region, activity, passage, actor, or record, labeled by its source and declared authority. | Recognition, validity, enforceability, priority, exclusivity, title, duty, liability, fee, or toll. |

Plainly: the first link is established physics vocabulary. The later links are a proposed way to organize questions about activity and assertions. The arrows do not prove that physics generates law or that a path generates a right.

## Original GIS-Like Impetus

The starting picture is a layered map in which each thematic surface answers a different question:

| Illustrative layer | Question carried by the layer | Required caution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rights | What right is asserted, by whom, and from which source? | The layer records an assertion; it does not validate the right. |
| Jurisdiction | Which institution or rule set is asserted to apply? | Boundaries and conflicts remain jurisdiction-specific legal questions. |
| Proximity | Which actors, routes, regions, or events are near one another under a declared metric and time window? | Nearness depends on declared coordinates, scale, uncertainty, and operational purpose. |
| Liability | What responsibility or exposure is alleged after an event or interaction? | No legal conclusion follows without governing law, facts, causation, and competent review. |
| Use | What activity is recorded or proposed in a region and interval? | Use does not automatically create title, priority, or exclusion. |
| Access | What entry, transit, observation, or resource interaction is asserted to be allowed? | A map cannot grant permission. |
| Exclusion | What boundary or restriction is asserted? | A displayed boundary is not proof of authority or enforceability. |

The layers may overlap without agreeing. Their value is analytic separation: a route can be physically described while access remains disputed; an institution can assert jurisdiction while another contests it; a proximity map can show operational risk without resolving liability.

## Domain Bridge

The inquiry remains anchored to ordinary domains before it expands:

- **Land:** How do maps distinguish physical occupation, use, access, boundaries, asserted rights, and jurisdiction?
- **Water:** How do moving boundaries, shared routes, resource use, environmental effects, and multiple authorities complicate the same layers?
- **Airspace:** How do altitude, time, route, proximity, safety, access, and jurisdiction make a static parcel map insufficient?
- **Outer space:** Which parts of the mapping method remain useful when objects move continuously, operational positions are relative to declared charts or observers, and legal authority is unsettled or domain-specific?

These are framing questions, not summaries of applicable law. The broader universal framing is exploratory and must not erase the differences among land, water, air, and outer-space law.

## Moving Systems And Relative Operational Location

A moving-system map cannot rely on a bare point or boundary. A candidate record would need, at minimum:

- the mapped object or actor identity as declared by the source;
- a spatial chart or operational frame and its provenance;
- an absolute-time or observer-time interval, with the level named explicitly;
- position, region, route, uncertainty, scale, and resolution;
- the activity or interaction being mapped;
- the source of any asserted right, jurisdiction, restriction, duty, or claim;
- whether the record is observed, reconstructed, predicted, proposed, or hypothetical.

This list is a candidate information structure. It does not select a privileged operational reference frame, derive one from $\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}$, or say that all domains share one legally meaningful coordinate system.

## Illustrative Hypotheticals

The following scenarios are thought experiments only:

- A future institution publishes a corridor map and asserts a toll for passage through a defined region. The map can record the assertion, route, interval, source, and dispute; it does not show that the institution exists, has authority, or may lawfully collect a toll.
- Two moving habitats publish overlapping proximity or exclusion layers in different operational charts. The overlap raises questions about coordinate reconciliation, notice, risk, and conflicting assertions; it does not create a physical or legal resolution.
- A transit route crosses land, water, air, and an outer-space segment. A multi-domain map may expose where the asserted rule source changes, but it cannot merge those legal regimes or settle their boundaries.
- A resource-use layer and a passage layer overlap. The overlap can identify a potential conflict for study; it does not establish ownership, damage, liability, priority, or a duty to pay.

## Non-Goals

This packet does not:

- create a legal definition of territory, passage, ownership, access, exclusion, liability, or jurisdiction;
- advise any person or institution about current law;
- assert that future ownership, passage-fee, or toll scenarios are real, accepted, desirable, or feasible;
- derive rights from ontology, movement, proximity, occupancy, or physical capability;
- claim that one coordinate frame determines legal priority;
- alter $\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}$ ontology, dynamics, recovery targets, or acceptance status;
- authorize an app, database, registry, governance system, enforcement mechanism, or implementation program.

## Open Questions

- Is `Passage` best treated as a map feature, an event record, a path-history summary, or a family of domain-specific records?
- Is `Claim` only an assertion record, or should later work distinguish claim, evidence, authority, objection, adjudication, and outcome?
- Which distinctions survive across land, water, air, and outer-space mapping, and which must remain domain-specific?
- How should a record express moving boundaries, changing operational charts, uncertainty, latency, and competing sources without implying a universal legal frame?
- What ethical or civic principles, if any, could justify access, exclusion, shared use, stewardship, liability, or a passage fee in a hypothetical future regime?
- What would falsify the usefulness of the four-link chain as an organizing method?

## Promotion Rule

The umbrella name and chain may remain useful even if no legal or physical theory follows from them. Any proposed promotion must identify the destination, source authority, claim classification, and failure test. Until then, all material stays priority-only.
