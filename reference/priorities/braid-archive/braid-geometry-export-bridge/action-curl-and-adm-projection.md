# Action Curl And ADM Projection

Promotion status: `priority-only`. This third geometry-bridge packet joins the branch-local response object to the action-compatibility test and then states the downstream ADM/Cartan projection that may consume a closed response record. It refines the `virtual_work_curl_test` and `adm_cartan_projection` rows in [priorities.md](priorities.md) without editing that bucket index.

The theory advance is the level separation:

$$
\text{response geometry}
\not\Longrightarrow
\text{action-derived geometry}
\not\Longrightarrow
\text{Noether-closed observer geometry}.
$$

A branch may therefore make real mathematical progress as a geometry-export response even before its delayed-force one-form is proved exact. What changes is the claim level: a curl-open branch can export kinematic or constitutive response rows, but it cannot be promoted as an action-derived branch or used as a Noether-closed observer-metric source.

---

## 1. Shared Branch Chart

Fix a root-regular branch chart $B$ with finite retained coordinates

$$
a=(a^1,\ldots,a^m)
$$

after translation, rotation, phase, period, root-label, and branch-family gauge directions have been quotiented or bordered. Let

$$
z_B(a;\theta)
=
\left(
Y,\nu,\eta,J,\Lambda,\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{hist}},
Q_{ab},\mathcal{Z}^{ab},\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{sea}}^{ab}
\right)
$$

be the retained branch state over a local Noether sea/environment record $\theta$. Root-regular means that the active causal-root ledger

$$
\mathcal{A}_B(a;\theta)
$$

has fixed labels, isolated roots, positive inactive-root gaps, noncollision floors, support floors, and branch Jacobian floors

$$
|J_r|\ge\epsilon_J
\qquad
(r\in\mathcal{A}_B).
$$

The response object is still the geometry-export packet

$$
\mathcal{K}_{B}^{\mathrm{geom}}
:
\delta\theta
\mapsto
\left(
\delta\ln T_B,\,
\delta Q_{ab},\,
\delta\mathcal{Z}^{ab},\,
\delta\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{sea}}^{ab},\,
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{pf}},\,
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{ledger}}
\right),
$$

but this packet adds a second question: whether the force response that produced the row is the exterior derivative of a scalar branch action on the same retained root stratum.

## 2. Branch-Local Virtual-Work One-Form

Let $\partial_p z_B$ denote the retained non-gauge tangent generated by $a^p$. The branch-local response force row is collected as

$$
\mathfrak{F}_B(z_B;\theta)
=
\left(
P^\perp\widetilde{\mathbf{F}}_i,\,
\Pi_\nu,\,
\Pi_{\mathrm{supp}},\,
\Pi_{\mathrm{evt}},\,
\Pi_{\mathrm{sea}}
\right),
$$

where the entries are, respectively, the transverse delayed-force row, speed-clock work row if $\nu$ is retained, support-work row, event-endpoint row, and Noether sea exchange row. The finite-mode virtual-work one-form is

$$
\omega_B
=
\sum_{p=1}^{m}W_p(a;\theta)\,da^p,
$$

with coefficients

$$
W_p(a;\theta)
=
\int_0^{L_B}
\sum_i
P_i^\perp
\widetilde{\mathbf{F}}_i(\lambda;z_B,\theta)
\cdot
\frac{\partial Y_i^\perp}{\partial a^p}(\lambda)
d\lambda
$$

$$
\quad
+
\left\langle
\Pi_\nu,\frac{\partial\nu}{\partial a^p}
\right\rangle
+
\left\langle
\Pi_{\mathrm{supp}},\frac{\partial\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{supp}}}{\partial a^p}
\right\rangle
+
\left\langle
\Pi_{\mathrm{evt}},\frac{\partial\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{hist}}}{\partial a^p}
\right\rangle
+
\left\langle
\Pi_{\mathrm{sea}},\frac{\partial\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{sea}}}{\partial a^p}
\right\rangle .
$$

The last four pairings are zero only when the corresponding variables are not retained by the branch chart. They must not be silently omitted when speed factors, support endpoints, event variables, or Noether sea exchange terms participate in the residual. Otherwise the curl test is testing a truncated one-form rather than the branch response.

Dimensionful normalization may multiply $\omega_B$ by the same scale used by the action row, for example $R_*E_\epsilon(R_*)/c_f$. The curl test is unchanged by a nonzero constant normalization, but the reported branch action must state the chosen normalization.

## 3. Root-Sensitive Curl Test

The action-compatibility curl is the exterior derivative of the one-form on the retained root stratum:

$$
\mathcal{C}_{pq}^{B}(a;\theta)
=
\frac{\partial W_q}{\partial a^p}
-
\frac{\partial W_p}{\partial a^q}.
$$

Every derivative in this expression is root-sensitive. For an active root $r=(i,j,\lambda,\mu)$ with delay $\eta_r(a;\theta)$, write the causal constraint as

$$
G_r(\eta_r;a,\theta)=0.
$$

For each chart coordinate,

$$
\partial_p\eta_r
=
-
\frac{\partial_pG_r}{\partial_\eta G_r}
=
\frac{
\widehat{\mathbf{R}}_r\cdot
\left[
\partial_p\mathbf{Y}_i(\lambda)
-
\partial_p\mathbf{Y}_j(\lambda-\eta_r)
\right]
}
{J_r}
+\Delta_{r,p}^{(\nu,\theta,\mathrm{end})},
$$

where $\Delta_{r,p}^{(\nu,\theta,\mathrm{end})}$ collects speed-clock, environment, and endpoint contributions when the causal selector or support convention depends on those rows. Therefore

$$
\frac{\partial W_q}{\partial a^p}
=
\left(\partial_p W_q\right)_{\mathrm{explicit}}
+
\sum_{r\in\mathcal{A}_B}
\frac{\partial W_q}{\partial\eta_r}\,
\partial_p\eta_r
+
\left(\partial_p W_q\right)_{\mathrm{end/supp/sea}}.
$$

A finite-difference evaluation must recompute the active roots after each perturbation, or must differentiate the bracketed root solver using the same root labels and memory policy. A curl value computed with frozen roots has status

$$
\texttt{frozen-root-curl-invalid}.
$$

The residual reported by this packet is

$$
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{curl}}^{B}
=
\frac{
\|\mathcal{C}^{B}\|_{\mathrm{F}}
}
{
1+\|W\|_{\mathrm{F}}
}.
$$

The branch is action-compatible on the tested chart only if

$$
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{curl}}^{B}
\le
\epsilon_{\mathrm{curl}}
$$

under refinement of chart step size, root tolerance, memory depth, support convention, and retained endpoint variables.

## 4. Pass/Fail Consequence

The curl result changes the permitted claim level.

| Status | Mathematical condition | Claim allowed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `response-geometry-open` | $\mathcal{K}_{B}^{\mathrm{geom}}$ is computed but $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{curl}}^{B}$ is not computed | Branch-local kinematic/response geometry only |
| `response-geometry-curl-open` | $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{curl}}^{B}>\epsilon_{\mathrm{curl}}$ on the retained root stratum | Geometry export may remain response-level, but no scalar branch action, action-derived scale, or Noether closure is claimed |
| `action-compatible-response` | $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{curl}}^{B}\le\epsilon_{\mathrm{curl}}$ on one shared root ledger | A local scalar action $S_B$ may be reconstructed up to a constant on a simply connected chart |
| `action-noether-export-ready` | curl passes and the charge, energy, momentum, angular-momentum, event, source, and Noether sea rows close on the same ledger | The response can feed observer-level ADM/Cartan projection |

Equivalently, curl failure implies

$$
\nexists\,S_B(a;\theta)
\quad\text{with}\quad
dS_B=\omega_B
$$

on the tested chart. The branch response can still be useful: it can report period stretch, shape response, exposure response, medium-response tensors, and preferred-frame leakage. It cannot be described as action-derived, and any conserved-current statement is at most diagnostic until the rows in [action-and-noether-closure-row.md](../braid-retained-branch-closure/shell-braid/action-and-noether-closure-row.md) close on the same ledger.

If the curl passes and the conservation rows close, the response reaches

$$
\omega_B=dS_B,
\qquad
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{A/N}}=0,
$$

and may be consumed by the observer-level metric bridge as an action-derived constitutive source. This is stronger than a fitted geometry export but still weaker than full GR recovery; PPN, photon, lensing, clock, and preferred-frame residuals remain downstream tests.

## 5. ADM/Cartan Constitutive Projection

The ADM/Cartan projection consumes the geometry-export rows as observer-level constitutive data. It does not curve the Euclidean void and does not insert $c_{\text{eff}}$ into the primitive branch calculation.

Define the local export vector

$$
\kappa_B(\mathbf{x},t)
=
\left(
\delta\ln T_B,\,
\delta Q_{ab},\,
\delta\mathcal{Z}^{ab},\,
\delta\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{sea}}^{ab},\,
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{pf}},\,
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{ledger}}
\right)_{(\mathbf{x},t)}
$$

after coarse-graining over the declared observer cell. Let $\kappa_0$ be the homogeneous reference record, and write

$$
\Delta\kappa_B=\kappa_B-\kappa_0.
$$

The constitutive projection is a local map

$$
\Pi_{\mathrm{ADM/Cartan}}:
\Delta\kappa_B
\mapsto
\left(
N,\,
u^i_{\mathrm{sea,eff}},\,
e^a{}_i,\,
\gamma_{ij}^{\mathrm{eff}}
\right)
$$

with weak-response expansion

$$
\ln N
=
A_T\,\delta\ln T_B
+
A_Q^{ab}\,\delta Q_{ab}
+
A_Z{}_{ab}\,\delta\mathcal{Z}^{ab}
+
A_M{}_{ab}\,\delta\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{sea}}^{ab}
+
O(\|\Delta\kappa_B\|^2)
+
O(\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{ledger}}),
$$

$$
u^i_{\mathrm{sea,eff}}
=
B^i_T\,\delta\ln T_B
+
B^{i,ab}_Q\,\delta Q_{ab}
+
B^i{}_{Z,ab}\,\delta\mathcal{Z}^{ab}
+
B^i{}_{M,ab}\,\delta\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{sea}}^{ab}
+
B^i_{\mathrm{pf}}\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{pf}}
+
O(\|\Delta\kappa_B\|^2)
+
O(\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{ledger}}),
$$

$$
e^a{}_i
=
\delta^a{}_i
+
E^a{}_{i,T}\,\delta\ln T_B
+
E^a{}_{i,Q}{}^{bc}\,\delta Q_{bc}
+
E^a{}_{i,Z,bc}\,\delta\mathcal{Z}^{bc}
+
E^a{}_{i,M,bc}\,\delta\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{sea}}^{bc}
+
O(\|\Delta\kappa_B\|^2)
+
O(\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{ledger}}).
$$

The spatial compliance metric is then assembled by the Cartan frame:

$$
\gamma_{ij}^{\mathrm{eff}}
=
\delta_{ab}e^a{}_i e^b{}_j.
$$

The observer-level line element read from this projection is

$$
ds_{\mathrm{eff}}^2
=
-N^2c_0^2dt_{\mathrm{eff}}^2
+
\gamma_{ij}^{\mathrm{eff}}
\left(dx_{\mathrm{eff}}^i-u^i_{\mathrm{sea,eff}}dt_{\mathrm{eff}}\right)
\left(dx_{\mathrm{eff}}^j-u^j_{\mathrm{sea,eff}}dt_{\mathrm{eff}}\right).
$$

This line element is a reconstruction surface for clocks, rulers, and signal paths. It is not substrate geometry. The Euclidean void remains the primitive bookkeeping space in which the branch roots and response rows were computed.

For the projection to be admissible as an action-derived observer source, the same retained ledger must satisfy

$$
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{curl}}^{B}\le\epsilon_{\mathrm{curl}},
\qquad
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{A/N}}\le\epsilon_{\mathrm{A/N}},
\qquad
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{ledger}}\le\epsilon_{\mathrm{ledger}}.
$$

If only $\mathcal{K}_{B}^{\mathrm{geom}}$ is available, the same formulas may still define a response-level fit, but the status must remain

$$
\texttt{response-level-adm-cartan-fit}
$$

rather than `action-noether-export-ready`.

## 6. Advancement Summary

This packet removes a false dependency in the geometry bridge. The theory does not have to wait for full Noether closure before studying how branch-local delay geometry deforms periods, shape tensors, exposure tensors, and medium-response tensors. Those rows form response geometry.

The action curl then decides whether that response geometry is generated by a scalar branch action:

$$
\mathcal{C}_{pq}^{B}=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\omega_B\ \text{is locally exact}
$$

on a simply connected root-regular chart. Only after that exactness and the conservation rows close can the response be promoted to action-derived observer export.

The ADM/Cartan projection is therefore downstream and constitutive:

$$
\mathcal{K}_{B}^{\mathrm{geom}}
\xrightarrow[\mathrm{if\ curl/Noether\ close}]{\Pi_{\mathrm{ADM/Cartan}}}
\left(
N,u^i_{\mathrm{sea,eff}},e^a{}_i,\gamma_{ij}^{\mathrm{eff}}
\right),
$$

while a curl-open branch remains valuable as

$$
\mathcal{K}_{B}^{\mathrm{geom}}
\quad
\text{response-level geometry only}.
$$

This separation is the practical bridge: it lets the geometry program advance with computable response tensors while preserving the stricter action and Noether claims for the branches that actually pass the finite-mode curl and conservation tests.
