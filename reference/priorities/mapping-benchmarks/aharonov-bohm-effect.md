# Aharonov-Bohm Effect

## Standard-Theory Concept

The Aharonov-Bohm effect shows that a charged quantum system can acquire a phase from electromagnetic potential even when the local magnetic field vanishes along its path. The phase shift is

$$
\Delta\phi
=
\frac{q}{\hbar}\oint \mathbf{A}\cdot d\mathbf{l}
=
\frac{q\Phi_B}{\hbar}.
$$

It is a benchmark for gauge potential, topology, and path-dependent phase.

## Existing $\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}$ Signals

This case pressures $\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}$ to recover gauge-like holonomy without treating the vector potential as primitive ontology. The useful substrate objects are causal-wake path history, excluded or shielded source regions, boundary conditions, and effective phase accumulation.
