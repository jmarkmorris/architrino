## Exact conflicts and non-promotion decisions

1. **Lensing normalization — derived comparison conflict.** `gravitational-lensing.md:70–78` prints $\alpha_\theta^i=(1+\gamma_{\mathrm{eff}})c_0^{-2}\int_{\Gamma_0}2\nabla_\perp^i\Phi_{\mathrm{eff}}\,ds+O(c_0^{-4})$ and then claims the point-mass value $4GM/(bc_0^2)$ at $\gamma_{\mathrm{eff}}=1$. For the declared complete straight ray and $\Phi=-GM/\sqrt{b^2+s^2}$, the integral of $\partial_b\Phi$ is $2GM/b$, so the printed formula gives $8GM/(bc_0^2)$. Section 4.2 shows the independent elementary integral and the comparison result from the line element; it neither changes the source nor claims a native derivation. A different explicitly declared potential normalization or integration domain would change the audit and is its checkable falsifier.
2. **Clock direction — operational definition conflict.** `effective-metric-deformation.md:65–79` proposes $P_{\mathrm{core}}\sim R_\perp f(\xi)/c_f$, then prints $d\tau/dt\propto1/\omega_{\mathrm{core}}\sim R_\perp f(\xi)$, while saying shrinkage speeds the clock. `gravitational-redshift-clock-tests.md:165` defines the clock observable as normalized tick frequency. The canonical clock passage inspected at `content/markdown/aaa/spacetime/proper-time-and-time-dilation.md:252–298` defines $C_N=\Omega_N/\Omega_{N0}=\Gamma_N^{-1}$ and the necessary material-clock mismatch. Section 4.3 preserves the period estimate and explains the inverse-rate problem. A definition showing that the source's $\omega_{\mathrm{core}}$ is not tick frequency could overturn this specific conflict; no such definition was present in the full source read.
3. **Photon-frequency direction is not the same conflict.** The opening stationary-metric frequency ratio in `gravitational-redshift-clock-tests.md:13` can describe transported-photon versus local-clock comparison depending on emitter/receiver convention. The manuscript does not condemn every reciprocal frequency ratio. It defines the actual clock-ratio experiment and preserves source, receiver, and transport distinctions. Endpoint signs in cosmology are conditional on the declared factorization and clock-to-sea identification.
4. **Shapiro path convention remains incomplete.** `shapiro-time-delay.md:5–15` calls a printed $2GM/c^3$ logarithmic expression round-trip but allows endpoint-convention factors without fixing the path. Section 4.1 uses the explicit baseline-subtracted path integral and states that the traversal and endpoints determine the coefficient. No universal round-trip normalization is certified.
5. **Deformation comparisons are not physical theorems.** `effective-metric-deformation.md:23–26`, `51–59`, `101–109`, and the comparison table at `113–126` state Kerr/singularity, Petrov-sequence, simultaneous shape/scale endpoint, and cosmological-relaxation claims more strongly than the candidate header and absent derivation support. Section 4.3 preserves the proposed mechanism and withholds those analogies as evidence. This manuscript does not complete a separate scholarly audit of Kerr, Petrov, fluid-equilibrium, or Gordon-metric conventions. Their equations remain preserved in the source and require dedicated verification before use.
6. **Finite tolerance does not define an equivalence relation automatically.** `spacetime-structure-experiment-suite.md:62–73` appends “within the declared uncertainties” to its equivalence definition. Pairwise distance at most $\epsilon$ is not transitive: $0$, $0.75\epsilon$, and $1.5\epsilon$ give two nearby pairs and a separated endpoint pair. Section 12.2 uses exact equal projected outputs for an equivalence relation and tolerance-based admissible sets for experimental constraints. This is a logical clarification, not a new physical assumption.
7. **Unstated statistical premise.** The entropy log measure needs normalization; the thermal detailed-balance formulas need actual transition/occupation structure before Planck statistics follow. Sections 7.1 and 8.1 make those burdens explicit and do not certify them from a tuple or ratio alone. Moving-window entropy flux also needs a relative-boundary convention. Original equations remain unchanged.
8. **Syntax and level normalization.** The clock source contains `$Gamma_N$`, `$mathcal ...$`, and `$Theta...$` strings at lines 65, 108, 114 and 118 that do not express their intended TeX macros. The new manuscript writes proper TeX symbols. Bare source time is translated into $T$ or $t_{\mathrm{eff}}$ according to role, temperature is subscripted, the material refractive factor avoids density $n$, and the two-flavor comparison's natural-unit assumption is stated explicitly. These are explained notation choices, not silent changes to preserved sources.
