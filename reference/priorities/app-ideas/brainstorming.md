## Portfolio Rules

- Prefer a capability inside an existing app when it shares that app’s data, visual grammar, and teaching sequence.
- Create a new app only for one coherent learner journey that does not fit an existing surface.
- Prefer a central animation, a small control set, and a live diagnostic or puzzle readout.
- Preserve the actual authority of prescribed geometry, display-only records, candidate mappings, measured rows, and derived results.
- Defer concepts whose lesson depends on an unclosed constitutive law, recovery map, or physical branch.
- Promote a concept only when it has a teaching mechanism, corpus authority, implementation boundary, acceptance condition, and owner.

## Concept Families

The accepted task-shaped capability packets for Causal Delay Feedback, Borg, Standard Model, and the proposed Interaction Ledger Lab now live in [work-queue.md](work-queue.md). Conceptually:

- Causal Delay Feedback is one progressive suite over a shared source, receiver, retained-history model, causal-root evaluator, and wake renderer.
- Borg extensions must consume sealed prescribed or EOM records and keep display transforms separate from source-carried analytical rows.
- Standard Model learning should contrast observer-level classification and bookkeeping with still-open assembly, confinement, mass, and reaction mechanisms.
- Interaction Ledger Lab would make conserved-account routing a visual puzzle while keeping any material constitutive response explicitly unresolved.

## Deferred Ideas

These concepts remain worth preserving, but their learner journeys depend too heavily on unresolved recovery or constitutive work. They are not executable tasks.

### Emergence And Measurement Lab

Candidate concepts:

- Threshold Crossing Map: delayed wake input moves a state across a basin separatrix while the underlying motion remains continuous.
- Born Rule as Basin Measure: ensemble frequencies are compared with deterministic basin measures.
- Record Autonomy and Restartability: visually similar intermediate states diverge because retained histories differ.
- Emergence Layer Lens: a resolution control moves among path histories, assembly records, and observer-level summaries.

Deferral reason: a record-backed example must first supply the basin, preparation measure, retained variables, and falsifiable recovery rows.

### Lorentz Recovery Lab

Candidate concepts:

- Clock-Cadence Retuning: compare identical assembly clocks in declared Noether sea states.
- Two-Way Signal Recovery: synchronize moving emitter, reflector, receiver, clock, and ruler readouts.

Deferral reason: clock, ruler, and two-way signal behavior must be recovered together from one accepted record before an app can teach the result as more than adjustable recovery knobs.

### Noether Sea Response Lab

Candidate concepts:

- Reversible Retuning: distinguish stored response from excitation, heating, radiation-like shedding, or branch transition.
- Directional Response: use response ellipses or ellipsoids and directional probes to visualize anisotropy.
- Mass-Map Exposure: compare exposed, shielded, and partially exposed assembly contributions.

Deferral reason: without independently accepted constitutive and mass-map rows, a parameterized display would mainly report what its author built into it.
