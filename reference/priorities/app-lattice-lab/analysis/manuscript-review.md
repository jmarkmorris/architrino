## Required clarification

### LL-F1 — Reference shell membership is not compressed distance rank

At initial manuscript line 108, §3.3 calls the displayed members the “first six neighbors” and the “next twelve neighbors” while discussing the entire positive compression range. The actual preserved sets are the undeformed first and second shells. Their transformed distances are correctly given, and their pairs correctly cancel, but they cease to be the first two distance-ranked shells in general.

For example, at $\lambda=1/4$, offsets $\pm2\mathbf e_x$ have distance $d/2$ and therefore precede the four members of the undeformed nearest shell that remain at distance $d$. This direct comparison uses the declared coordinate map; no scientific simulation or app enumeration is involved. The unqualified wording would incorrectly imply a current nearest-neighbor ranking even though §2.2 already warns that transformed canonical relationships need not remain current-shortest.

Required repair: explicitly identify the six and twelve members as the undeformed/reference nearest and next shells. Preserve all counts, distances, parity, pair cancellation, eighteen-row example scope and infinite-exhaustion boundary.
