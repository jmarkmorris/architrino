# Lattice Primer Removal Audit

## Scope and evidence boundary

This audit closes the paragraph-inventory requirement of LAT-095 without changing geometry, calculations, certificates, controls, or evidence authority. The source inventory is the last pre-removal `Lattice Primer` case data in the parent of commit `2b4126c2a`. The current canonical app has no Primer or `What You Are Seeing` presentation; introductory duplication was therefore resolved by retaining facts in their narrower surviving owners rather than migrating prose into another overview.

The finite Random 50/50 case is not listed below because it was added after the Primer removal and never had `primerTitle` or `primerParagraphs` fields. Its population, finite scope, and result authority remain in the case summary, Shared Display Conventions, Boundary treatment, and Site Ledger.

The audit compares two surfaces: the exact pre-removal paragraphs preserved in the table and the narrower owners present in the canonical app after removal. A disposition of `already stated` means the fact survives in a named owner; `retain as non-Primer authority` means the evidence boundary remains elsewhere; and `drop` means the removed sentence added only repetition, an unnecessary analogy, or a duplicated disclaimer.

Claim grade: `measured` by comparing the last pre-removal case data in the parent of commit `2b4126c2a` with the canonical post-removal sources and their owner fields. Falsifier: a former Primer fact required by the current app that is absent from every named surviving owner, or any live Primer or `What You Are Seeing` source, runtime, style, or accessibility hook missed by the audit.

## Reconciliation result

No former Primer sentence qualifies for migration into an introductory destination: every necessary fact is already stated in a surviving, narrower presentation, while density repetitions, analogies, and broad disclaimers fall under LAT-095's explicit non-migration categories. The former Primer DOM, case-data fields, runtime hooks, style hooks, and the later `What You Are Seeing` replacement surface are absent from the canonical implementation. The removed-surface copy rows are therefore superseded rather than implemented against dead UI.

Plainly: removing the Primer did not remove the facts the Lab still needs. Geometry stays with Geometry, repeating-pattern facts stay with the repeat panel, and calculation authority stays with the Ledger.
