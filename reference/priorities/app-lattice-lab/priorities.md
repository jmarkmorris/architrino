## Current

This folder owns planning for **Architrino Lattice Lab**, a single-page, geometry-first explorer of idealized red/blue architrino arrangements.

The intended educational question is narrow: for a declared arrangement held initially at zero velocity, does its complete declared acceleration ledger cancel at every displayed site? The first case, the simple-cubic checkerboard, now has a [derived stationary-release cancellation certificate](simple-cubic-checkerboard-cancellation-certificate.md) under receiver-centered inversion-symmetric exhaustion. Its result comes from the canonical acceleration rows and an inversion-pair theorem, not from rendered balance or the finite visual neighborhood. This workstream does not claim a physical void filling, a realized architrino medium, stability, conservation, or an EOM-evolved configuration.

Accepted executable work lives in [work-queue.md](work-queue.md). The product brief, including reuse and potential Borg migration, lives in [requirements-design.md](requirements-design.md). Provisional visual and geometry ideas live in [brainstorming.md](brainstorming.md). Dated planning and handoffs live in [work-log.md](work-log.md).

## Objective

Extend the educational, single-page arrangement explorer only with cases that have a declared geometry, polarity assignment, boundary convention, and independently checkable zero-acceleration or non-cancellation result.

## Current Blocker

No second case has been accepted into the live queue. The verified simple-cubic result is limited to the exact stationary retained history and receiver-centered inversion-symmetric exhaustion; perturbative stability and later delayed-history evolution remain untested.

## Boundaries

- Treat cubic, body-centered cubic, face-centered cubic, and other color/polarity patterns as candidate geometry cases, not as established equilibria.
- Use acceleration-first $\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}$ language; do not introduce mass or force framing.
- A finite display must name its boundary treatment. Visual cancellation cannot stand in for an infinite-lattice or retained-history argument.
- Keep all displayed results labeled by their evidence grade and certificate reach.
- Do not promote a display result into a physical, stability, conservation, or void-medium claim.
