# Legacy WordPress Operations Notes

## Status

The WordPress site is a historical source archive, not a current development or publication workspace. Its searchable post inventory, mining queue, and archive-level dispositions are owned by this source-mining lane. See [priorities.md](priorities.md) for the current archive policy.

The notes below are retained as historical operating context. They do not authorize current publishing, glossary, asset-regeneration, or backup work. Any newly needed public communication work must be opened as an explicit priority item with its current owner and validation route.

## Historical Notes

- WordPress was no longer the place to develop new ideas.
- The operator preferred em-dash typography in WordPress copy, with whitespace around the dash despite the usual house-style convention.
- A Chrome administrative search view was used for older post lookup: `https://johnmarkmorriscom.wordpress.com/wp-admin/edit.php?s=CDM&post_status=all&post_type=post&action=-1&m=0&cat=0&paged=1&action2=-1n`.
- Historical terminology and draft ideas included `æther`, article enhancement for scripts, a glossary list, blog-home slide graphics, backups, and WordPress galleries. Those are archive-era notes, not live tasks.
