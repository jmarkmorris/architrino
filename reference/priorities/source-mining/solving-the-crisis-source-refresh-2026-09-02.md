## Selection Rule

Each retained chapter has at least one source selected for a local purpose: defining the standard problem, recording a direct observation or measurement, documenting an experiment or data release, or bounding a comparison framework. A source may support more than one chapter only when the inspected record actually spans those roles. Review sources are used for mature synthesis; primary papers and official releases are used for original results and current measurements. Wikipedia, public problem lists, news summaries, and unsourced anomaly compilations are absent.

Standard-physics equations and interpretations remain recovery targets, observational constraints, or comparison tools. They are not premises at the architrino or assembly level. The source ledger therefore records what must eventually be recovered or distinguished, not what the substrate is assumed to contain.
