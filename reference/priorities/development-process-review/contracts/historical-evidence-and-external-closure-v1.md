The new result names retained scientific byte verification, recorded-provenance verification, and full original-environment verification separately. The first two concern the retained records and their accepted scope; they do not establish a fresh observation of the old process table, permit replay of archived code, or promote the scientific claims of the original calculation.

## External completion

The canonical coordinator's conditional record remains pending. Completion requires a separate observer to retain the exact invocation, successful process exit within the original bound, completed stdout and stderr, absence of the original process group and its owned descendants, and absence of the original lock. A worker-thread guard closes with the observed death of its owning process; the guard's in-process pending record alone proves no such closure.

The observer also verifies the pending record's coordinator, plan, source and output identities, the corresponding operation record, stage count and successful admissions, and source/output stability after process exit. Missing observations, unresolved descendants, late exit, incomplete logs, file replacement, and changed output invalidate completion. A generic ownership lease alone is insufficient.

The completion result grants operational closure only. Independent mathematical comparison and historical scientific acceptance retain their own requirements. Old receipts and the frozen old closure checker are preserved; the new live closure boundary has a distinct version and never translates pending fields into the old accepted record.

Real bounded synthetic invocations then exercise successful closure and failure. No historical numerical replay or new scientific batch is part of this implementation authorization.
