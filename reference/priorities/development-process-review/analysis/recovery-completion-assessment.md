# Recovery completion assessment

This assessment answers the operator's question about why Monday-am still has partial work after repeated completed batches. It is a review of the current plans, not a fresh execution of their tests or authorization to expand the campaign.

## Finding

The campaign combines operational repair, historical reconstruction, scientific execution and general test maintenance under one completion label. This makes real completed work difficult to distinguish from obligations that need different inputs or separate decisions. The original [operator concerns](operator-concerns.md#time-retries-and-expanding-scope) explicitly sought to avoid another unbounded investigation. The [repair plan](review-and-repair-plan.md#priority-and-execution-order) retains a broad steps 1–5 boundary; the [queue](../work-queue.md) now includes ten within-cap circular candidates, six resource-blocked candidates, owner-specific failures, missing original objects and final cross-runtime reconciliation. This supports a scope-management concern; it does not establish that individual repairs were unnecessary or that the remaining tests pass.

## Reporting defects observed

Reading the current queue alongside the complete reconciliation table exposes stale summaries: the queue and owner recovery record report actual SDK HTTP success, while the reconciliation table still describes its snapshot failure. The queue records the second circular candidate's accepted eight-phase rung, while the broader table still says nine candidates are deferred. The retained repair proposal also lists the authored deployment identity as blocked although the newer owner table records its completion. These disagreements are documentation lag, not evidence that the completed work failed again.

## Recommendation

Before another broad run, reconcile one finite operational closeout list with three explicit outcomes: repaired and verified, blocked on a named external input or decision, and separately owned scientific continuation. Preserve every existing obligation and evidence link while making its actual purpose visible. Ask which remaining circular cases test a distinct repaired behavior before scheduling their full ladders. Do not label the original recovery complete until its selected defect coverage and final reconciliation are demonstrated; do not require open-ended scientific exploration merely to stop reporting operational recovery as perpetually partial.

No remaining defect was waived, no test was rerun, and no execution or resource policy was changed by this assessment. Its conclusion would need revision if contract review demonstrates that the entire remaining candidate ladder is necessary for distinct recovery acceptance conditions rather than broader execution coverage.
