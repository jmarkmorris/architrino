# Independent review of recovery necessity

Review date: September 8, 2026. **✓ Done — bounded independent audit. ○ Not done — proposed scope decisions and remaining implementation.** This review answers whether continuing the two-day recovery effort is justified. It changes no acceptance rule, scientific record, source implementation, or other task's queue. The reviewed Git state is `16c25e9e9`; live authored closeout documents were also read. No tests, scientific runs, regeneration, or publication were performed.

## Recommendation to the operator

**Continue a finite set of real corrections, but stop treating the whole remaining list as damage from the original incident.** Several remaining failures demonstrably existed before the seven audited repair commits. Some were ordinary content changes whose dependent fixtures or reviewed bindings were not updated. Others are missing rendering assets and old presentation mismatches. The circular continuation also contains new implementation work and a scientific campaign, not repeated failure of the already accepted run.

The repair work was not all wasted. The retained independent process controls exposed actual normal-return, startup and cleanup defects, and the historical-source audit exposed a genuine confusion between old evidence and current executable inputs. Those corrections protect meaningful behavior. But a broad discovery sweep also brought unrelated backlog into a single recovery label. That label now prevents an intelligible finish line.

The next decision should be what the recovery will certify: restoration of the affected supported execution paths and honest disposition of the discovered failures, or universal current acceptance of every scientific campaign and application test. The latter is a substantially larger program. Keeping its obligations recorded does not require completing that program before ending the incident investigation. The [closeout preservation map](recovery-closeout-review.md) already retains those obligations; this review proposes their purpose and priority, not their deletion.

## Why the effort has kept growing

**One failure family can produce many failed tests.** The retained [analytical log](../evidence/owner-failure-recovery/analytical.stdout.log) repeats the same methodology-admission error in the sixteen failed assertions. The three Archie items share the snapshot/response chain. Counting those outputs as independent broken systems exaggerates the number of repair causes, although every affected assertion still needs a final result.

**Historical identity and current usability require different work.** The [consequential audit](consequential-repair-audit.md) records 194 commit/file changes, 177 digest-only, including repeated changes to the same file; its historical binding comparison shows some historical pins were already wrong and then advanced again. This is concrete metadata churn, not an estimate of time wasted. The later separation of archived originals from current transport was a necessary correction. Future source edits should not reactivate old scientific calculations merely because current byte identities changed.

**The scientific scope is real but distinct.** The [circular predeclaration](../../braid-program/evidence/2026-08-27-subfield-circular-h3-pilot-predeclaration.md#cost-pilot-and-fixed-ladder) calls 8/32/128 a proposed scientific ladder after the all-sixteen cost pilot. The recovery plan later incorporated cohort completion. The accepted first ladder and second initial rung protect specific behaviors; this audit has found no evidence that every remaining geometric ladder is needed to prove the same process fix. A particular geometry may reveal a distinct numerical problem, so those runs remain valuable scientific work with their original limits. They should not silently define the incident's finish line.

**Unobtainable evidence cannot be repaired by another ordinary batch.** I1–I3 identify exact missing utility objects, I5 a missing sealed database, and I6 an unexplained past disappearance without a terminal cause. The search boundaries and negative results are already retained. Do not repeat the same search absent a new accessible source. I4's refined transport remains blocked on those inputs or a separately reviewed contract decision. No “all recovered” claim is justified; an explicitly bounded administrative closeout can leave these scientific/provenance obligations open.

**The finish line moves when final verification means every current check.** V1–V8 combine ordinary tests, original-input reproduction, scientific commands requiring selected data, supported-host checks and discovery of further commands. V9–V10 add publication on a concurrently changing checkout. That is a useful repository-health program but an unstable definition of one incident. The existing S1–S8 and N1–N6 records should remain with their scientific owners and reopen conditions. A final recovery statement must name its actual selected universe and residuals, not imply all possible checks passed.

## Finite route forward

1. **○ Proposed — agree one closeout claim.** Prefer restoration of the affected supported operational paths, completion of the connected current-fixture updates and semantic binding reviews, and an explicit residual inventory. Keep “all repository tests healthy” and “all scientific ladders complete” as separate named goals. This requires operator acceptance; the present wider obligations are not waived by this report.
2. **○ Proposed — finish connected changes, not repeated rediscovery.** G1–G3 form one deliverable; G4 and G5 have concrete bounded maintenance procedures; G9 and G10 are distinct semantic reviews. G7/G8 belong at a stable candidate. G6 and C4 can remain recorded maintenance/hardening unless selected. C1–C3 need an explicit supported-interface and coverage decision before more execution.
3. **○ Proposed — use a thirty-minute checkpoint for decisions and evidence.** Each batch should end with one closed obligation or one precise blocker. Do not launch a non-resumable long scientific run to consume the timebox. A fresh failure must be linked to its requirement and causal evidence before it becomes another recovery package.
4. **○ Proposed — perform final reconciliation once on the selected stable state.** Preserve failed, blocked, skipped and inapplicable results separately. Reuse unchanged historical evidence only within its declared applicability; rerun required current controls. No arithmetic subtraction of old failures from later passes, and no claim of universal health from a green publication gate.

## Limitations and falsifiers

This is a source/history and retained-evidence audit, not a fresh health measurement, complete semantic review, or exhaustive chronology of all sessions. It does not establish every first mismatch, whether each source change was scientifically correct, exact labor time, or agent motive. G1/G7/G8 attribution remains unresolved beyond their observed freshness class; most G6 rows were not visually inspected. A demonstrated failed requirement in an excluded operational path, an earlier matching state that changes an attribution, or a coverage argument requiring an additional live circular case would revise the corresponding recommendation.

The correct answer is neither “stop all repairs” nor “continue all seven packages.” Necessary repairs remain, but a substantial part of the apparent endless recovery is inherited maintenance, preserved scientific work and unavailable history sharing one label. Preserve all of it; execute only the finite accepted closeout obligations now.
