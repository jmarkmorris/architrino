Inspection of `classifyLease` showed that loss of the group leader alone produced `stale_closed`; the implementation did not check for surviving descendants or a surviving sidecar. The foreground waiter also consulted only the durable terminal status, so a lost sidecar could leave it waiting until the original workload deadline. Neither observation establishes why the sidecar disappeared in the original incident.

The current implementation checks group existence and sidecar absence before classifying an abandoned run as closed. Missing ownership identities, a surviving group, or a surviving sidecar retain the identity-uncertain classification and prevent closeout or automatic signalling.
