### Borg geometry and summary delivery

The geometry failures came from comparing quantized display-record coordinates at a tighter tolerance than their declared grid. `canonicalizePrescribedBraidRecord` rounds each initial cubic coefficient to the nearest multiple of `positionQuantum = 2e-11`. At a segment's initial endpoint, each coordinate error is at most half that quantum. By the triangle inequality, one pair distance can move by at most `sqrt(3) * positionQuantum`; comparison of two recorded pair distances requires twice that bound. The repaired assertions use those independently derived bounds and retain the exact tetrahedral length and all five independently known vertex/edge/degree inventories. They also require the recorded quantum to equal `2e-11`, so a coarser generator cannot silently weaken the test. No geometry record, generator or playback implementation changed.

The home-entry failure was a source-text assertion requiring an obsolete inline call. Live `bootBorgApp` already computes the summary in the selected-record branch and supplies it through the `librarySummary` variable. Its test now calls the actual boot path and checks the selected assembly and model revision delivered to `mountApp`, retaining the existing renderer assertions. Run `7a210014-9d8b-4587-a550-a28e0bb54402` passes the initial eleven geometry/home tests; see [output](../evidence/owner-failure-recovery/borg.stdout.log). Review then added a negative control that displaces one actual tetrahedral endpoint by `1e-6` along its edge and verifies that the same bound rejects it. The tests explicitly check an initial segment boundary at zero and unit-scale vertex coordinates; the additional `1e-14` allowance covers binary64 subtraction/norm arithmetic in this scope, not arbitrary-scale or interior interpolation errors. Final run `0a4403cd-2283-4119-b152-199dd7eceb75` passes all twelve tests with zero skips, exit zero and closed group; see [final output](../evidence/owner-failure-recovery/borg-final.stdout.log).
