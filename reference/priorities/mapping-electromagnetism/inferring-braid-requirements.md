## Requirement And Realization Grades

Every row in this packet must distinguish four stages:

1. **Deduced requirement.** A necessary property of any native realization if
   the declared effective behavior is recovered. This is a deduction from the
   recovery target, not yet a derivation within $\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}$.
2. **Inferred requirement.** A strongly motivated native property that is not
   logically unique because another native mechanism may project to the same
   effective behavior.
3. **Candidate realization.** A specified matter braid, sea-braid population,
   wake organization, or coupled complex that could meet the requirements.
4. **Selected realization.** A geometry derived from the Master Equation or an
   accepted retained record that predicts the full behavior bundle, survives
   controls, and outperforms competing realizations without private retuning.

The repository-wide evidence labels still apply independently: a statement is
derived, measured, inferred, or guessed according to the evidence supporting
it. A deduced effective requirement may therefore coexist with a guessed
microscopic realization.

The phrase `could be caused by` belongs to a candidate realization. It must not
be used to make a speculative braid picture read like a necessary requirement.

Plainly: first state what nature requires. Then state what geometry might
supply it. Only a successful forward derivation and independent comparison can
select the geometry.

## Matter-State, Sea-State, And Scale Must Remain Distinct

Inverse mapping must keep four operations distinct: changing an observer-level
scale coordinate, changing a persistent matter assembly, producing a transient
event-level retuning, and changing the surrounding sea. None implies another
without a derived relation.

For a matter transition $A\rightarrow B$, record separately:

- internal matter cadence, radii, envelope scale and shape, phase, exposure,
  and wake organization;
- near-sea retuning coupled to the matter transition;
- environmental and extended sea differences;

- effective electric and magnetic projections; and

This separation preserves four possibilities: a fixed-background projection,
matter-state retuning, coupled local matter--sea retuning, and an extended
sea-state difference. Agreement with a conventional running law constrains the
combined projection but does not directly reveal which native coordinates
changed.

Plainly: a higher experimental scale does not by itself prove a smaller or

the nearby sea, both, or neither; the record must show which.

## Direct-Wake And Sea-Mediation Controls

For each candidate realization, evaluate at least these mathematical controls:

1. **Direct-only:** preserve the resolved source history while suppressing the
   authored collective sea response.
2. **Frozen-sea:** retain the reference sea record while changing the source
   history.
3. **Responsive-sea:** evolve or recompute the declared sea degrees of freedom
   with no coefficient change.
4. **Source-removed relaxation:** remove or reverse the maintaining source and
   track what relaxes, remains, or propagates away.
5. **Randomized orientation:** preserve scalar population data while destroying
   polar, axial, or alignment order.
6. **Polarity and circulation reversal:** change one signed native feature at a
   time while preserving the remaining geometry.
7. **Near/environment/far knockout:** suppress one spatial sea domain at a time
   to determine whether the response is local, environmental, extended, or a
   boundary artifact.
8. **Receiver substitution:** change the receiver class while holding the
   source, sea, carrier, and boundary record fixed.

An authored sea model swept over its own parameters is evidence about that
model, not evidence that nature uses the model. These controls become physical
evidence only when the candidate sea response is derived from resolved braid
histories or compared with an independent instrument.

Plainly: the controls ask whether the source acts directly, through the sea,
or through both, and whether the inferred field state exists independently of
one specially chosen detector.

## Correct Work Order

Use this sequence for each new behavior family:

1. State the raw observer behavior without explaining it through a primitive
   field.
2. Extract magnitude, direction, covariance, symmetry, reversal, range,
   temporal, and conservation requirements.
3. Grade each statement as a deduced requirement or inferred native
   requirement.
4. Populate every relevant role in the absolute native record, including any
   sea domain whose contribution is presently unknown.
5. Enumerate the smallest candidate realization levels: one causal-root
   contribution, one retained matter braid, a local matter--sea complex, a
   collective sea or material response, and the effective observer projection.
6. Derive the effective output forward from each candidate.
7. Apply direct/sea, reversal, null, receiver, and refinement controls.
8. Intersect the surviving candidate sets across independent equations and
   benchmarks.
9. Call a geometry selected only after it is retained or otherwise accepted,
   passes the full requirement bundle, and survives comparison with competing
   realizations.

Simple devices or idealized situations may be introduced when a particular
ambiguity needs a clean discriminator. They do not replace this work order and
do not become new priority programs merely because they are easy to visualize.

Plainly: requirements first, candidates second, forward calculation third,
selection last.
