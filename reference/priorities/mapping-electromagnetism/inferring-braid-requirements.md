## Direct-Wake And Sea-Mediation Controls

For each candidate realization, evaluate at least these mathematical controls:

1. **Direct-only:** preserve the resolved source history while suppressing the
   authored collective sea response.
2. **Frozen-sea:** retain the reference sea record while changing the source
   history.
3. **Responsive-sea:** evolve or recompute the declared sea degrees of freedom
   with no coefficient change.
4. **Source-removed relaxation:** remove or reverse the maintaining source and
   track what relaxes, remains, or propagates away.
5. **Randomized orientation:** preserve scalar population data while destroying
   polar, axial, or alignment order.
6. **Polarity and circulation reversal:** change one signed native feature at a
   time while preserving the remaining geometry.
7. **Near/environment/far knockout:** suppress one spatial sea domain at a time
   to determine whether the response is local, environmental, extended, or a
   boundary artifact.
8. **Receiver substitution:** change the receiver class while holding the
   source, sea, carrier, and boundary record fixed.

An authored sea model swept over its own parameters is evidence about that
model, not evidence that nature uses the model. These controls become physical
evidence only when the candidate sea response is derived from resolved braid
histories or compared with an independent instrument.

Plainly: the controls ask whether the source acts directly, through the sea,
or through both, and whether the inferred field state exists independently of
one specially chosen detector.
