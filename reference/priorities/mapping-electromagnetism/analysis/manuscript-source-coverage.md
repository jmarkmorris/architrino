## Conflicts, qualifications, and editorial decisions

1. **Sea necessity remains unresolved.** Some fine-structure prose describes a responsive sea as a required carrier. The current strategy and read inverse `BR-EB-07` require changing sea geometry only if sea-essential or mixed controls survive. Manuscript §§1.3, 3.2 and 9 preserve a declared reference sea and permit a null nontrivial correction. This is a source-tension disposition, not silent deletion or a claim that direct wakes already pass.
2. **Stationary cancellation is conditional.** The homogeneous affine identity uses complete stationary histories and inversion-paired exhaustion. It does not establish absolute convergence, finite crop equilibrium, nonuniform elasticity, orbiting retention, or propagation.
3. **Moving-seed negatives are scoped separately.** Global-plane symmetry leakage, rigid finite-ladder release mismatch, and site-local orientation-balanced release mismatch are three different experiments. The manuscript preserves their distinct controls and does not infer an infinite-medium no-go.
4. **Report completion is not physics completion.** EMAP-001 is complete only as the source/moment/field matrix. EMAP-002–004 remain deferred; historical implemented evaluators do not reactivate them.
5. **Historical terminology is not universal restriction.** Seed speed guards, circle/period language, apparent scalar return, and reflected shape/current return retain their local assumptions. They do not prove a primitive speed ceiling, retained cycle, or spinor restoration.
6. **Action notation is locally defined.** The continuation packet's finite-difference `h` becomes $\delta_\zeta$ in manuscript §10.1 so it cannot be mistaken for $h_\vartheta$. This explanatory notation change is confined to the new manuscript; original mathematics and the discussion-scoped canon question are unchanged.
