### Causality Insight

"One assembly event" does not mean instantaneous action around the circuit.
At substrate level, every change must propagate through causal-wake and medium
histories. The quasistatic Maxwell equality is the coarse observer limit in
which the circuit's propagation delays are resolved into one consistent event
window. A native calculation must compare delay-aligned records, not impose
$I(T)=\epsilon_{mathrm{eff}}d\Phi_E(T)/dT$ at unrelated simultaneous substrate
locations by assumption.

Plainly: Maxwell's compact time label hides travel time. The assembly map must
recover the compact equality from delayed records rather than erase the delay.

### No Photon Is Required In The Minimal Case

Slow charging changes the near-field and medium state without requiring a
detached photon assembly on every update. Continuous causal-wake transmission
and reversible Noether sea/plate response are the candidate carriers. Rapid or
non-adiabatic charging can open a radiation branch, but that is an extension of
the experiment, not part of the minimal capacitor identity.

This separates ordinary field updating from photon emission. If the mapping
requires a free photon for every infinitesimal change in capacitor charge, it
has collapsed continuous causal-wake response and resolved radiation into one
incorrect channel.

### Energy-Flow Check

The minimal case has one useful companion constraint. With electric response
across the gap and magnetic response around the axis, the effective Poynting

$$
\mathbf S_{\mathrm{eff}}
=\frac{1}{\mu_{\mathrm{eff}}}
\mathbf E_{\mathrm{eff}}\times\mathbf B_{\mathrm{eff}}
$$

points into the capacitor storage region from its surroundings, with sign set
by the charging orientation. The assembly map must therefore route source
work, plate recoil/stress, causal-wake transfer, and Noether sea storage through

the gap without a carrier ledger is insufficient.

Plainly: the capacitor does not merely acquire charge; the region between the
plates acquires stored response. The physical account must show how that
account arrives.

### Claim Grades And Falsifiers

- **Effective-grade derivation:** the two Maxwell surfaces give the same
  $\Gamma_B[C]$ when charge continuity and the ideal comparison assumptions
  hold. Falsifier: a calculation of the declared Maxwell problem gives
  different circulation for the same boundary loop.
- **Assembly-level inference:** wire transport, plate accumulation, and the gap
  update are projections of one closed circuit event. Falsifier: the source and
  plate ledgers cannot share one conserved charge and event history.
- **Derivation target:** changing plate histories drive a Noether sea
  polarization/circulation update that supplies the gap-side magnetic
  projection. Falsifier: the sea record remains unchanged, violates delay, or
  requires a response coefficient retuned away from the wire-side result.
- **New geometric synthesis:** magnetic circulation is the boundary-dual
  projection of an oriented flux-change record. Falsifier: a native map passes
  the scalar flux magnitude but fails loop orientation, reversal parity, or
  radial dependence.

The minimal experiment fails immediately if it invents material current across
the ideal gap, makes the receiver depend on the imaginary surface, treats
changing electric flux as an uncaused substance, forces photon emission during
quasistatic charging, or omits the energy-transfer ledger.
