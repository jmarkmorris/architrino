## Receiver-Orbit Action

Use receiver $1+$ as the representative row. The receiver orbit is

$$
\{1+,1-,2+,2-,3+,3-\}.
$$

The relevant receiver-orbit action is generated by two maps:

$$
C:\;1\mapsto2\mapsto3\mapsto1,
\qquad
S:\;i+\leftrightarrow i-.
$$

In carrier coordinates this can be represented by

$$
Q_C(x,y,z)=(z,x,y).
$$

The antipodal relation is

$$
Y_{i-}(u)
=
Y_{i+}(u+\pi)
=
-Y_{i+}(u).
$$

Thus $S$ can be read as a sign flip at fixed signed label phase, or equivalently as a half-period phase shift in the unsigned carrier convention.

The reduction is not a claim of unrestricted full signed-octahedral invariance. It applies to the scalar receiver clock/length row under the stated all-root fold-aware convention.

## Candidate Equivariance Lemma

Let

$$
f_i(u)=T_i(u)\cdot F_i^{\mathrm{fold}}(u)
$$

be the fold-aware all-root tangential forcing for receiver $i$ at the representative zero-ray point. If the coarea/root chart is closed under the signed-cyclic receiver maps, then for each receiver-orbit element $g$ there is a phase shift $\sigma_g$ and a source-row relabeling such that

$$
\boxed{
f_{g i}(u+\sigma_g)=f_i(u).
}
$$

The source relabeling preserves:

$$
\text{root multiplicity},
\qquad
|J|,
\qquad
\operatorname{sign}(q_iq_j),
\qquad
\text{physical delay}.
$$

With the same mean-subtracted primitive convention,

$$
A_i(u)
=
\int_0^u
\left(f_i(q)-\overline f_i\right)\,dq,
$$

the same relation gives

$$
A_{g i}(u+\sigma_g)-A_{g i}(0)
=
A_i(u)-A_i(0).
$$

Since every receiver in this period-rescaled carrier has the same path-length ratio

$$
\frac{L_{g i}}{H}=\frac{L_i}{H},
$$

the clock/length criterion is transported with the same numerical bounds:

$$
\nu_{g i,0}^{\mathrm{clk}}
=
\nu_{i,0}^{\mathrm{clk}},
\qquad
\nu_{g i,\min}^{\mathrm{clk}}
=
\nu_{i,\min}^{\mathrm{clk}},
\qquad
\nu_{g i,\max}^{\mathrm{clk}}
=
\nu_{i,\max}^{\mathrm{clk}}.
$$

Thus a future interval/chart-partition certificate does not need six independent receiver searches if it proves both:

1. the representative $1+$ interval profile bounds; and
2. the signed-cyclic chart-closure identity above, including source relabeling and fold-aware all-root preservation.

Until that chart-closure proof exists, this packet cannot replace the six receiver rows of the sampled orbit scan.

## Equivariance Proof Skeleton

For a receiver $i$ and source $j$, define the causal-root equation

$$
\Phi_{ij}(u,\eta)
=
\|Y_i(u)-Y_j(u-\eta)\|-\eta.
$$

For a signed-cyclic receiver-orbit map $g$, let $Q_g$ be its orthogonal carrier map and $\sigma_g$ its phase convention, so that

$$
Y_{g i}(u+\sigma_g)=Q_gY_i(u),
\qquad
\dot Y_{g i}(u+\sigma_g)=Q_g\dot Y_i(u),
$$

and likewise for every relabeled source $j$. Orthogonality gives

$$
\Phi_{g i,g j}(u+\sigma_g,\eta)
=
\|Q_gY_i(u)-Q_gY_j(u-\eta)\|-\eta
=
\Phi_{ij}(u,\eta).
$$

Thus every positive root $\eta$ on the representative row is transported to the relabeled row with the same delay value. The delayed displacement unit vector and Jacobian obey

$$
\widehat R_{g i,g j}(u+\sigma_g,\eta)
=
Q_g\widehat R_{ij}(u,\eta),
$$

$$
J_{g i,g j}(u+\sigma_g,\eta)
=
J_{ij}(u,\eta),
$$

because dot products are preserved by $Q_g$. The charge product is also preserved by the receiver-orbit source relabeling:

$$
q_{g i}q_{g j}=q_iq_j.
$$

Therefore the fold-aware all-root force transforms as

$$
F_{g i}^{\mathrm{fold}}(u+\sigma_g)
=
Q_gF_i^{\mathrm{fold}}(u),
$$

provided the chart includes all positive roots with the same coarea convention at fold phases. Taking the receiver tangent inner product gives the scalar identity

$$
T_{g i}(u+\sigma_g)\cdot F_{g i}^{\mathrm{fold}}(u+\sigma_g)
=
T_i(u)\cdot F_i^{\mathrm{fold}}(u).
$$

This is the exact reason a future interval proof can be a one-receiver proof plus chart closure. It is also the exact reason this packet avoids full tensor or retained-branch claims: the proof only transports the scalar clock/length forcing row under the stated receiver-orbit convention.
