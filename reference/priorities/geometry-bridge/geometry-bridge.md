## Scope

This priority folder owns the focused mathematics and geometry attack plan for moving from the Master Equation of Motion to emergent assembly geometry without jumping directly to full observer-level GR. The workstream exists because the current stack has strong pieces in the Master EOM, Noether swarm architecture, nested shell swarm geometry, and emergent metric map, but it lacks one controlled intermediate object:

$$
\text{Master EOM}
\longrightarrow
\text{branch-local assembly response}
\longrightarrow
\text{Noether-Sea / observer-geometry export}.
$$

The central target is a branch-local response theorem. For a candidate assembly branch $B$, define a retained state

$$
z_B
=
\left(
Y,\nu,\eta,J,\Lambda,\mathcal{E}_{\mathrm{hist}},
Q_{ab},\mathcal{Z}^{ab},\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{sea}}^{ab}
\right),
$$

and a local environment or Noether-Sea record $\theta$. The first bridge object is a residual equation

$$
\mathcal{R}_B(z_B;\theta)=0
$$

on a root-regular, ledger-consistent branch chart. The first variation is

$$
D_z\mathcal{R}_B[\delta z]
+
D_\theta\mathcal{R}_B[\delta\theta]
=0.
$$

After quotienting gauge directions and fixing the root-ledger convention, a valid response row has the form

$$
\delta z
=
-
\mathcal{G}_B
D_\theta\mathcal{R}_B[\delta\theta],
$$

where $\mathcal{G}_B$ is the inverse or bordered generalized inverse of $D_z\mathcal{R}_B$ on the retained non-gauge subspace.

The output is not a metric yet. The output is the geometry-export packet

$$
\mathcal{K}_{B}^{\mathrm{geom}}
:
\delta\theta
\mapsto
\left(
\delta\ln T_B,\,
\delta Q_{ab},\,
\delta\mathcal{Z}^{ab},\,
\delta\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{sea}}^{ab},\,
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{pf}},\,
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{ledger}}
\right).
$$

Only after this packet is branch-certified should [Emergent Metric](../../../content/markdown/aaa/spacetime/emergent-metric.md) consume it as candidate input for $N$, $u^i_{\text{sea}}$, $e^a{}_i$, and $\gamma_{ij}$.

## Core Theory Advance

The first actual mathematical advance of this bucket is the **branch-response reduction**:

$$
\text{emergent geometry recovery}
\quad\leadsto\quad
\text{a gauge-quotiented response problem on one retained assembly branch}.
$$

Instead of asking immediately for a full effective metric, fix one root-regular branch chart and treat the surrounding Noether-Sea or assembly environment $\theta$ as a perturbing record. The branch response is obtained from the linearized equation

$$
D_z\mathcal{R}_B[\delta z]
=
-
D_\theta\mathcal{R}_B[\delta\theta].
$$

Let $\mathfrak g_B$ be the finite-dimensional or finite-mode gauge space generated by translations, rotations, phase shifts, period choice, root-label relabeling, and any declared branch-family neutral direction. Let $\Pi_B^\perp$ project onto a chosen complement. If

$$
\Pi_B^\perp D_z\mathcal{R}_B\Pi_B^\perp
:
\Pi_B^\perp T_{z_B}\mathcal Z_B
\longrightarrow
\Pi_B^\perp\mathcal F_B
$$

has a bounded inverse $\mathcal{G}_B$ on the retained root stratum, then the non-gauge first-order assembly response is

$$
\boxed{
\delta z_\perp
=
-
\mathcal{G}_B
\Pi_B^\perp D_\theta\mathcal{R}_B[\delta\theta].
}
$$

This is stronger than another checklist item. It turns the geometry problem into a computable derivative problem. The branch chart either supplies $\mathcal{G}_B$ and therefore a local geometry response, or it fails with a mathematical obstruction: non-invertible quotient derivative, root-stratum failure, missing response variable, or ledger mismatch.

The first observer-facing check can also be stated before full PPN recovery. If the geometry export gives a weak local lapse perturbation $\delta N$ and a spatial-compliance perturbation $\delta\gamma_{ij}$ in a local homogeneous Noether-Sea frame, then the isotropic weak-field equality between dynamical and lensing potentials has the branch-response form

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{Weyl=dyn},B}
=
\delta N
+
\frac{1}{6}h^{ij}\delta\gamma_{ij}
=0.
}
$$

This follows from the comparison forms

$$
N=1+\frac{\Phi_{\mathrm{dyn}}}{c_0^2}+O(c_0^{-4}),
\qquad
\gamma_{ij}
=
\left(
1-\frac{2\Psi_{\mathrm{sp}}}{c_0^2}
\right)h_{ij}
+O(c_0^{-4}),
$$

for which $h^{ij}\delta\gamma_{ij}=-6\Psi_{\mathrm{sp}}/c_0^2$. Thus $\Psi_{\mathrm{sp}}=\Phi_{\mathrm{dyn}}$ is equivalent to the displayed residual vanishing. This condition is not yet a GR proof, but it is a concrete branch-response equation that can reject a lapse-only geometry bridge before the wider metric program is attempted.
