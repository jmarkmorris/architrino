## 1. General Neutral Branch

Let

$$
I=\{1,\ldots,6\}
$$

index the Noether braid architrinos. A neutral branch supplies a polarity map

$$
\sigma:I\to\{+1,-1\},
\qquad
\#\{i:\sigma_i=+1\}=3,
\qquad
\#\{i:\sigma_i=-1\}=3,
$$

so that

$$
Q_{\mathrm{core}}
=
\epsilon\sum_{i\in I}\sigma_i
=0.
$$

No binary relation is part of the base definition. The branch variables are six closed arclength curves

$$
\mathbf{Y}_i:\mathbb{R}/L_i\mathbb{Z}\to\mathbb{R}^3,
\qquad
\|\mathbf{Y}_i'(\lambda)\|=1,
$$

positive bounded speed factors

$$
0<\nu_-\le\nu_i(\lambda)\le\nu_+<\infty,
$$

and causal-time maps

$$
\chi_i(\lambda)
=
\int_0^\lambda\frac{d\xi}{\nu_i(\xi)},
\qquad
\Lambda_i=\chi_i^{-1},
\qquad
H_i=\chi_i(L_i).
$$

The physical trajectory in the normalized center-time chart is

$$
\mathbf{x}_i(u)=\mathbf{Y}_i(\Lambda_i(u)).
$$

The equality or winding period row is still required:

$$
H_i=H_*,
$$

or

$$
m_iH_i=H_{\mathrm{com}}.
$$

The base branch is therefore

$$
\mathfrak{K}_\nu
=
\left(
I,\sigma,
\mathbf{Y},
\nu,
\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{supp}},
\mathcal{A}_\nu,
\mathsf{Action}^{\nu},
\mathsf{Event}^{\nu},
\mathsf{Inventory}
\right).
$$

The old binary-index notation $i=(a,\sigma)$ with $a\in\{1,2,3\}$ is allowed only after a packet declares a partition of $I$ into three two-site binaries. Without that partition, the correct source sums range over all $j\ne i$.

## 2. Hollow Support Band

Let $\mathbf{C}$ be the center-gauge point and define

$$
r_i(\lambda)=\|\mathbf{Y}_i(\lambda)-\mathbf{C}\|.
$$

A general neutral braid support descriptor is an annular or hollow support band

$$
\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{supp}}^{\mathrm{neutral}}
=
\left(
\mathbf{C},
R_{\mathrm{in}},
R_{\mathrm{out}},
B_i^-,
B_i^+,
\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{cent}},
\mathcal{S}_{\mathrm{case}}
\right),
$$

with

$$
0<R_{\mathrm{in}}<R_{\mathrm{out}}.
$$

The support inequalities are

$$
B_i^-(\lambda)
=
r_i(\lambda)-R_{\mathrm{in}}
\ge0,
\qquad
B_i^+(\lambda)
=
R_{\mathrm{out}}-r_i(\lambda)
\ge0.
$$

For the example band suggested by the operator,

$$
R_{\mathrm{in}}=\frac12R_*,
\qquad
R_{\mathrm{out}}=3R_*.
$$

The hollow center condition is the exclusion row

$$
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{hole}}
=
\max_i
\sup_\lambda
\left(R_{\mathrm{in}}-r_i(\lambda)\right)_+.
$$

This row says only that the six neutral braid architrinos do not enter the central hollow. It does not decide what lives inside the hollow. A charged branch, central-inventory representative, or other inner occupant must carry its own noncollision, action, root, event, and provenance rows.

The band-width ratio is

$$
\beta_{\mathrm{band}}
=
\frac{R_{\mathrm{out}}}{R_{\mathrm{in}}}.
$$

A shell braid case has $\beta_{\mathrm{band}}\approx1$ after radius normalization. A general neutral braid branch may have $\beta_{\mathrm{band}}\gg1$ while still remaining bounded and noncolliding.

## 3. Occupancy Measure

The relevant mathematical object is the causal-time occupancy measure of the closed paths. For a Borel region $A\subset\mathbb{R}^3$, define

$$
\mu_i^\nu(A)
=
\frac1{H_i}
\int_0^{H_i}
\mathbf{1}_A(\mathbf{Y}_i(\Lambda_i(u)))\,du.
$$

The signed and unsigned coarse-grained densities at smoothing scale $\delta$ are

$$
n_{\mathrm{occ},\delta}(\mathbf{x})
=
\sum_{i\in I}
\frac1{H_i}
\int_0^{H_i}
K_\delta(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{Y}_i(\Lambda_i(u)))\,du,
$$

and

$$
n_{\mathrm{sgn},\delta}(\mathbf{x})
=
\sum_{i\in I}
\sigma_i
\frac1{H_i}
\int_0^{H_i}
K_\delta(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{Y}_i(\Lambda_i(u)))\,du.
$$

These are occupancy densities, not probabilities unless a separate normalization and observer rule is declared. A branch may look like a smooth support distribution to a coarse observer when $n_{\mathrm{occ},\delta}$ varies slowly compared with the path recurrence time, even though the primitive branch is six deterministic closed paths.

A coverage claim is optional. If the branch claims that the Noether braid forms an enclosing support distribution over an annular region

$$
\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{supp}}
=
\{\mathbf{x}:R_{\mathrm{in}}\le\|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{C}\|\le R_{\mathrm{out}}\},
$$

it must emit coverage rows such as

$$
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{cover}}^{\delta}
=
\sup_{\mathbf{x}\in\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{supp}}}
\left(
n_{\min}-n_{\mathrm{occ},\delta}(\mathbf{x})
\right)_+,
$$

and signed-balance rows such as

$$
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{sgn}}^{\delta}
=
\sup_{\mathbf{x}\in\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{supp}}}
|n_{\mathrm{sgn},\delta}(\mathbf{x})|.
$$

These rows are not required for every branch. They are required only when the branch claims cloud-like containment, shielding, or near-neutral coarse exposure.

## 4. Optional Binary Partition

A binary involution is a derived grouping map

$$
\iota:I\to I,
\qquad
\iota^2=\mathrm{id},
\qquad
\iota i\ne i,
\qquad
\sigma_{\iota i}=-\sigma_i.
$$

It is a derived row, not base ontology. If a packet declares binary behavior, it must emit binary residuals. For exact antipodality around $\mathbf{C}$,

$$
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{anti},i}
=
\sup_\lambda
\left\|
\mathbf{Y}_{\iota i}(\lambda+\Delta_i)
+
\mathbf{Y}_i(\lambda)
-2\mathbf{C}
\right\|,
$$

with the speed parity row

$$
\mathcal{R}_{\nu\mathrm{pair},i}
=
\sup_\lambda
|\nu_{\iota i}(\lambda+\Delta_i)-\nu_i(\lambda)|.
$$

A binary partition is a partition

$$
\mathcal{P}
=
\{P_1,P_2,P_3\},
\qquad
|P_a|=2,
\qquad
\sum_{i\in P_a}\sigma_i=0.
$$

In a nested shell braid case, each block $P_a$ is the binary contained in one shell. Thus $a$ indexes that binary, and the same index names the shell when the nested shell context is clear.

Once $\mathcal{P}$ is declared, the binary-index notation $i=(a,\sigma)$ becomes available. The partition-indexed radii are then derived from [Nested Shell Braid Radial Support Functional](../nested-shell-braid/nested-shell-braid-radial-support-functional.md), not assumed:

$$
R_a=\mathscr{R}_a(\mathbf{Y},\nu,\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{supp}},\mathcal{P}).
$$

The binary-partition reduction row is

$$
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{slot}}
=
\left(
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{partition}},
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{anti}},
\mathcal{R}_{\nu\mathrm{pair}},
\mathcal{R}_{R\mathrm{def}},
\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{slot\text{-}ledger}}
\right).
$$

If $\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{slot}}$ is absent or fails, the branch remains a general neutral braid branch. It is not a failed Noether braid branch merely because no clean binary partition exists.

## 5. Topological And Knotted Support Data

Let

$$
\Gamma=\bigcup_{i\in I}\operatorname{im}\mathbf{Y}_i
$$

be the labeled path union. A topological statement is meaningful only when the branch has a tubular noncollision floor:

$$
d_{\min}
=
\inf_{i\ne j,\lambda,\lambda'}
\|\mathbf{Y}_i(\lambda)-\mathbf{Y}_j(\lambda')\|
>2\epsilon_{\mathrm{tube}}.
$$

Then the branch may attach a labeled link, braid, or framing invariant

$$
\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{top}}
=
\left(
\operatorname{Link}_{ij},
\operatorname{Wr}_i,
\operatorname{Braid}_{\Pi},
\operatorname{Framing}_i,
\operatorname{Provenance}
\right),
$$

with the understanding that these are branch classifiers until the dynamics closes. The topology cannot replace the force, action, root, support, or event rows.

An event that changes the labeled link class must be ledgered. Typical topology-changing events are:

| Event | Required row |
| --- | --- |

| self-hit window | self-hit return and exchange closure |
| root-sheet fold | bounded-speed root-fold normal form |
| support-boundary contact | support multiplier or event exchange row |
| central-hollow contact | central exclusion event and provenance row |

## 6. Root Ledger And Dynamics

For every ordered source pair $i\ne j$, retained causal roots solve

$$
G_{ij,\alpha}^{\nu}(u,\eta)
=
\left\|
\mathbf{Y}_i(\Lambda_i(u))
-
\mathbf{Y}_j(\Lambda_j(u-\eta))
\right\|
-\eta
=0.
$$

The bounded-speed Jacobian is

$$
J_{ij,\alpha}^{\nu}
=
1-\nu_j^-\mathbf{T}_j^-\cdot\widehat{\mathbf{R}}_{ij,\alpha}.
$$

The general force ledger is

$$
F_i^\nu(u)
=
\sum_{j\ne i}
\sum_{\alpha\in\mathcal{A}_{ij}^{\nu}(u)}
\sigma_i\sigma_j
W_{ij,\alpha}^{\mathrm{rec},\nu}(u)
\frac{\widehat{\mathbf{R}}_{ij,\alpha}(u)}
{\eta_{ij,\alpha}(u)^2}
+
F_{i,\mathrm{self}}^\nu
+
F_{i,\mathrm{med}}^\nu
+
F_{i,\mathrm{supp}}^\nu.
$$

Here $W_{ij,\alpha}^{\mathrm{rec},\nu}(u)$ is the same-record
receiver-normal branch strength. The $|J_{ij,\alpha}^{\nu}(u)|$ row remains a
root-chart diagnostic, not receiver-normal branch strength.
If a derivative-consuming force/action packet cannot emit this row and its
required first derivative on the same root record, it exits as
`receiver-normal-restart-required`.

The structural attraction/repulsion inventory survives without binary partition. For each receiver $i$,

$$
N_{\mathrm{attr},i}
=
\#\{j\ne i:\sigma_i\sigma_j=-1\}
=3,
$$

and

$$
N_{\mathrm{rep},i}
=
\#\{j\ne i:\sigma_i\sigma_j=+1\}
=2.
$$

The weighted force implication is still a delayed projection problem:

$$
F_i^\nu
=
F_{i,\mathrm{attr}}^\nu
+
F_{i,\mathrm{rep}}^\nu
+
F_{i,\mathrm{self}}^\nu
+
F_{i,\mathrm{med}}^\nu
+
F_{i,\mathrm{supp}}^\nu.
$$

The bounded-speed dynamics rows are

$$
R_{T,i}^\nu
=
\nu_i\nu_i'
-
\Gamma_B^\nu\mathbf{T}_i\cdot F_{i,\mathrm{tot}}^\nu,
$$

and

$$
R_{N,i}^\nu
=
\nu_i^2\mathbf{K}_i
-
\Gamma_B^\nu P_i^\perp F_{i,\mathrm{tot}}^\nu.
$$

The speed ODE must close over the declared period:

$$
\int_0^{H_i}
\mathbf{T}_i(u)\cdot F_{i,\mathrm{tot}}^\nu(u)\,du
=0,
$$

with primitive excursion inside the speed band.

## 7. Containment And Central Inventory

The word `containment` is not a primitive force law. In this packet it means one or more of the following certified rows:

1. hollow support exclusion for the six neutral-core paths;
2. support-band viability under the dynamics and support multipliers;
3. coarse-grained occupancy coverage if cloud-like enclosure is claimed;
4. Noether sea exchange and response rows if the branch claims a medium-supported boundary;
5. central-inventory provenance rows if charged or inner architrinos occupy the hollow region.

Let $\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{cent}}$ denote any central inventory placed inside the hollow region. Its exclusion from the neutral-core paths is

$$
\inf_{i\in I,\,a\in\mathcal{I}_{\mathrm{cent}},\,u,v}
\|\mathbf{x}_i(u)-\mathbf{x}_a(v)\|
\ge
d_{\mathrm{cent}}>0.
$$

Its charge and provenance rows remain separate:

$$
Q_{\mathrm{total}}
=
Q_{\mathrm{core}}+Q_{\mathrm{cent}},
\qquad
Q_{\mathrm{core}}=0.
$$

Thus a neutral braid can provide a hollow support band around a central inventory, but it does not by itself explain a charged fermion. The charged branch must still supply the central-inventory ledger in [central-inventory-and-event-ledgers.md](../shell-braid/central-inventory-and-event-ledgers.md).

## 9. Optional Reductions

The old architectures are recovered as reductions of the general branch:

| Reduction | Added rows |
| --- | --- |
| exact-antipodal binary | involution $\iota$, antipodal geometry, speed parity, binary-root parity |
| shell braid | partition $\mathcal{P}$, small partition-indexed radius spread, common support band |
| nested shell braid | partition $\mathcal{P}$, ordered partition-indexed radii, persistent radius gaps |
| nested shell braid transition case | partition $\mathcal{P}$ plus radius functional and transition rows |
| neutral braid without binary partition | no partition; hollow support band, occupancy density, topology, and all-pairs root ledger |

The proof direction is one-way:

$$
\text{general retained branch with optional reduction rows}
\Longrightarrow
\text{branch satisfying the added reduction rows}.
$$

The reverse implication is false unless the reduction certificate also proves that no required general-case rows were hidden by the symmetry ansatz.
