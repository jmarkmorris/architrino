# Pair-Provenance Local Compliance Scope Decision

**Decision:** reject the ER=EPR ontology analogy and retain only a tightly local provenance-compliance coarse-graining target (operator option (a), 2026-07-12). **Claim level:** priority-only constitutive probe. **Authority boundary:** this decision does not establish provenance-dependent Noether sea compliance, modify the Bell program, create a wormhole or nonlocal bridge, authorize implementation, or promote the idea into corpus prose.

## Rejected Interpretation

The following claims are rejected as a route for this workstream:

- entanglement or pair provenance creates the Euclidean void, the Noether sea, or observer-level spacetime;
- a correlated pair is connected by a substrate wormhole;
- a detector choice at one wing changes the distant Noether sea before causal-wake contact;
- shared information acts as energy, stress, compliance, or a force without a local physical carrier;
- an ER=EPR analogy supplies evidence for a local constitutive coefficient.

ER=EPR may remain external comparison history, but it has no ontological or evidential role in the retained target below.

## The Allowed Local Question

A pair-creation event leaves a retained provenance record $\Pi_{AB}$. A local Noether sea region may later contain a population of causal histories whose records include members of such pairs. The allowed question is whether a local statistic of that physically present record population helps predict a reversible Noether sea response already measured by the medium-response program.

This requires two separate maps:

$$
\{\text{locally available pair records}\}
\longrightarrow
C_{\Pi}^{ab}(\mathbf{x},t)
\longrightarrow
\delta\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{pair}}^{ab}(\mathbf{x},t).
$$

The first arrow is a coarse-graining. The second is a constitutive derivation. Neither arrow follows merely from the existence of pair correlations.

## Local Provenance Statistic

Let $\chi_{\mathrm{loc}}(\Pi_{AB};\mathbf{x},t)$ equal one only when the retained carrier history contributing to $\Pi_{AB}$ has entered the declared local causal-support window around $(\mathbf{x},t)$. A candidate statistic is

$$
C_{\Pi}^{ab}(\mathbf{x},t)
=
\int
\chi_{\mathrm{loc}}(\Pi_{AB};\mathbf{x},t)
\Pi_{\mathrm{loc}}^{ab}(\Pi_{AB};\mathbf{x},t)
\rho_{\mathrm{pair}}(\Pi_{AB})
\,d\Pi_{AB}.
$$

Here $\Pi_{\mathrm{loc}}^{ab}$ may use only locally present orientation, phase, wake, angular-momentum, and carrier-state data. It may not read a remote detector setting, a future record, or an abstract pair label whose physical carrier never reached the local window.

The tensor should be decomposed into its scalar and trace-free parts before comparison with the existing symmetric medium-response tensor:

$$
C_{\Pi,0}
=\frac{1}{3}h_{ab}C_{\Pi}^{ab},
\qquad
C_{\Pi,\mathrm{tf}}^{ab}
=
\left(
\delta^a{}_c\delta^b{}_d
-\frac{1}{3}h^{ab}h_{cd}
\right)C_{\Pi}^{cd}.
$$

This decomposition makes isotropic population density distinct from directional pair alignment. It does not yet make either component a compliance coefficient.

## Constitutive Consumption Target

The existing Noether sea medium-response program owns $\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{sea}}^{ab}$. Pair provenance may enter that program only through a derived, local response map such as

$$
\delta\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{pair}}^{ab}
=
\mathcal{L}^{ab}{}_{cd}
C_{\Pi}^{cd},
$$

where $\mathcal{L}^{ab}{}_{cd}$ is obtained from retained local perturbation records rather than fitted to a desired quantum or gravitational result. The reversible response must remain symmetric and must pass the positivity, isotropy, birefringence, dispersion, and preferred-frame bounds already owned by the medium-response probe.

If no retained acceleration, gradient, pressure, or strain probe consumes $C_{\Pi}^{ab}$ with predictive value beyond ordinary local density and orientation variables, the compliance bridge is rejected as redundant.

## Causality And Quantum Guards

### Causal-Support Guard

For a remote setting change $\delta\hat{\mathbf m}_A(t_A)$ and a local statistic evaluated near wing $B$, the required pre-contact response is

$$
\frac{
\delta C_{\Pi}^{ab}(\mathbf{x}_B,t)
}{
\delta\hat{\mathbf m}_A(t_A)
}
=0
\qquad
\text{for}
\qquad
t-t_A
<
\frac{\|\mathbf{x}_B-\mathbf{x}_A\|}{c_f}.
$$

The same condition applies to $\delta\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{pair}}^{ab}$. A violation rejects the construction; it is not repaired by calling the pair history shared.

### Measurement-Independence Guard

The source measure remains independent of later detector settings:

$$
\rho_{\mathrm{src}}
(\Pi_{AB}\mid
\hat{\mathbf m}_A,
\hat{\mathbf m}_B,
P_{\mathrm{src}})
=
\rho_{\mathrm{src}}
(\Pi_{AB}\mid P_{\mathrm{src}}).
$$

A compliance fit that requires setting-dependent provenance is a measurement-independence failure.

### Program Separation

The Bell source-measure program asks whether local apparatus and joint record basins recover the observed correlation tables. The compliance probe asks whether a local population statistic predicts a Noether sea response. Passing one does not help the other unless both consume the same retained records without changing their respective guards.

## Minimal Comparisons

Before the constitutive map can advance, compare at least:

1. an isotropic local pair population;
2. a directionally aligned local pair population with the same scalar density;
3. a provenance-scrambled control preserving the same one-point carrier density and orientation distribution;
4. a non-pair local population with matched ordinary medium variables;
5. causal-window cuts before and after the physical carrier history reaches the probe region.

The bridge has incremental value only if the retained response distinguishes a genuine provenance statistic from all matched local controls and the difference survives regulator, window, and probe-direction convergence.

## Failure Conditions

Reject the local compliance bridge if:

- the statistic depends on remote settings before causal contact;
- the source measure becomes setting-dependent;
- the response can be reproduced entirely by ordinary local density, orientation, strain, or wake variables;
- the constitutive tensor loses symmetry or positivity in a reversible channel without a separately routed loss term;
- the result requires treating information as a physical carrier;
- no retained medium-response consumer exists;
- the only motivation left is the ER=EPR analogy.

Rejection of this bridge does not reject pair provenance as a quantum source ledger.
