## Deliberate qualifications and source tensions

**T1 — Speed window versus physical metastability.** The agency source's opening states universal middle-binary metastability strongly, while its mathematical object only constrains speed proximity and its later tests require an actual metastable branch and control mechanism. Manuscript §8.1 preserves the architectural proposal and explicitly denies a derivation from the displayed window. This is necessary to retain its own open-proof status; no universal physical assertion is accepted.

**T2 — Transfer notation and error units.** The source first calls the transfer operator a measure pushforward and later places a star on its action on measures. Manuscript §5.1 consistently defines a new local notation, $\mathcal P_\tau\mu=(\Phi_\tau)_*\mu$, rather than silently choosing incompatible density/adjoint conventions. The source's proposed additive metastable estimate includes an observable-distance coarse error. Such an error needs an additional measure-of-boundary-neighborhood or equivalent probabilistic control before it can bound basin weights. Section 5.2 makes that condition explicit and routes the uncompleted estimate to support. No new quantitative probability bound is claimed.

**T3 — Cost and reversible limits.** The agency scaffold initially uses a strictly positive cost inequality, but its minimality gate permits reversible ideal limits with a prepared state. Section 8.2 retains the later exception. Section 8.3 keeps the action-step bracket as an illustrative reduced model with undeveloped physical dimensions and normalization; its symbols do not constitute a Planck-constant derivation or a new action convention.

**T4 — Pair-coordinate units and phasor sign.** The early diagnostic uses fractional-cycle phases; the later source map uses radians and division by $2\pi$. Section 7.4 declares the latter convention and the equivalent cycle conversion. The candidate minus sign is preserved as part of the proposed singlet-like phase convention; the manuscript does not infer the complete phasor map from angular-momentum balance alone. Gauge invariance, nonzero phasors and source-derived wake phase remain unproved conditions.

**T5 — Table residuals versus complete-record evidence.** Section 7.3 distinguishes the declared-baseline product residual from an infimum over all local models. Exact target curves, zero marginal residuals and the source's historical generated counts are not independent source-dynamics evidence. The completed-record parity failure and missing local-response records override any earlier positive-sounding table description. A nonrestartable pre-record stage does not establish a lawful Bell mechanism.

**T6 — Input density versus invariant resolved measure.** The abstract map's input density generally evolves; the resolved atomic mixture is invariant. Section 5.4 states this distinction. A toy map cannot certify a native return map, doubling-frequency lock, root chart or physical measure.

**T7 — Quantum/effective conventions and source-time claims.** Radian phases, effective natural units in the vacuum neutrino benchmark, spinor versus photon angle laws, positive transverse inner product, and the normalized simple-root wake expression are declared locally. Absolute substrate time is written $T$; source lower-case time notation is not copied into an ambiguous substrate formula. Historical external particle/experimental claims and source-time candidate surveys remain attributed, not refreshed. The chapter does not assume every advertised benchmark or candidate premise has been derived.
