## Objective

Design a hierarchical acceleration algorithm for large populations of architrinos, the equal and opposite point transceivers of $\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}$, that reduces the cost of evaluating the Master Equation's delayed causal-root sum below the exhaustive ordered-pair count while preserving the repository's certification discipline: no contribution is dropped, every approximation carries a certified remainder, and the result gains authority only through independent references. The design begins from Barnes-Hut and its rivals and departs from them wherever the architrino law differs from instantaneous gravity or electrostatics.

Five differences between the architrino problem and the classical treecode problem organize the design and are developed in the manuscript's [departure analysis](manuscript.md#where-the-architrino-problem-departs-from-the-treecode-problem): signed polarity of exactly two equal magnitudes, so neutral clusters have zero unweighted monopole and a position-dipole-led spatial far field, while the velocity weight can still leave a term at the monopole's distance power; delayed interaction through causal roots on retained histories, so a source cell must be aggregated on the receiver's past cone rather than at one instant; a transmitter-velocity weight on every hit, so cell moments are moments of a weighted emission measure; the absence of a mutual-interaction symmetry, because the reverse hit occurs at a different time; and a self-hit term that no cell may absorb. The candidate algorithm, a [certified space-time multipole route](manuscript.md#a-candidate-algorithm-certified-space-time-multipoles-on-the-past-cone), extends the EOM solver's existing enclosed-pair route from zero-centered enclosure to moment-expanded value plus certified remainder. Its cheapest new route, the matched-pair enclosure, was jointly reviewed with Codex on 2026-10-11 in the [review conversation](../../op/agent-chats/2026-10-11-barnes-hut-matched-pair-review/chat.md). The review confirmed the pair radius under explicit hypotheses and corrected three things: the velocity-weight part of the bound decays only as one over distance squared, so the one-power gain holds for the position part alone; both members' roots must be certified present in the common slab, since equal magnitudes cannot cancel an absent hit; and admission must solve the summed inequality against the pair's two budget slices rather than take the larger single-term cutoff. The [self-contained theorem and outward-bound construction](analysis/matched-pair-enclosure-theorem.md), authored by Codex, was accepted after Claude reviewed the frozen proof. The [execution conversation](../../op/agent-chats/2026-10-11-bhl-001-execution/chat.md) records ownership and review. The full moment-remainder theorem and BHL-002 remain deferred.
