## Search Coverage And Claim Boundary

The $N=2$ through $N=6$ regular campaign enumerates all symmetry-inequivalent balanced polarity words and retains bounded negatives for every non-alternating class on the declared interval. Its nonuniform phase searches are unresolved. The $N=12$ row covers only the regular alternating class. The binary row uses its separately owned circular all-root simple-chart scan.

All positive rows establish prescribed acceleration balance only. They establish no binding, retention, stability, release survival, physical identity, score increase, or scientific acceptance. A bounded negative excludes only its declared geometry and speed interval.

Plainly: this registry records what was tested and where each result belongs. It does not turn relative numerical comparisons into a physical ranking.

## Maintenance Rule

Add a row when a circular-path geometry has an exact reproducible coordinate record and durable evidence. Update the Braid Candidate Registry only when the same record separately passes an admitted braid taxonomy gate. Every measured row must name its instrument, domain, independent reference, claim grade, and falsifier in the evidence owner.

The next evidence step for a balanced row is a separately predeclared EOM-solver evolution with complete retained prehistory and guarded causal-root continuity. Geometry closure requires additional bounded or covering searches of unresolved nonuniform phase charts; planar three-binary closure also requires unequal-radius coverage.
