- `applicable-threshold-crossing`: complete primary/refined inventory, finite $P,Q$, refined `P <= 6`, and maximum score change `<= 0.05`;

An `applicable-threshold-noncrossing` is only a failure to cross this diagnostic handoff line; it is not a configuration-wide or physical negative.
