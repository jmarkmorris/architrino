## Exact orbit census

Let $m=2N$. The acting finite group is generated by cyclic rotation, global polarity conjugation, and the checked reflection-with-circulation action. Burnside averaging over its $4m$ elements gives the number of balanced binary words containing exactly $N$ plus signs and $N$ minus signs.

For a rotation by $k$ sites, put $d=\gcd(m,k)$ and $r=m/d$. An ordinary rotation fixes

$$
\begin{cases}
\displaystyle {d\choose N/r},&r\mid N,\\
0,&r\nmid N,
\end{cases}
$$

while a rotation followed by conjugation fixes $2^d$ words when $r$ is even and fixes none when $r$ is odd. Among the $m/2$ edge-axis reflections and $m/2$ vertex-axis reflections, the ordinary fixed-word counts are

$$
E=\mathbf 1_{2\mid N}{m/2\choose N/2},
$$

and

$$
V=
\sum_{\substack{0\leq a\leq2\\a\equiv N\pmod2}}
{2\choose a}{(m-2)/2\choose(N-a)/2}.
$$

An edge reflection followed by conjugation fixes $2^{m/2}$ words; a vertex reflection followed by conjugation fixes none. Dividing the complete fixed-point sum by $4m$ gives:

## Shared circular-kernel reduction

For a fixed regular $2N$-gon and fixed $\beta_f$, every causal-root equation depends on a receiver-transmitter pair only through the phase separation $s=j-i\pmod{2N}$. It is independent of the polarity word. Let $K_s^{(r)}(\beta_f)$ and $K_s^{(t)}(\beta_f)$ be the complete radial and tangential acceleration kernels obtained by summing every admitted root at separation $s$. For a polarity word $\sigma_i\in\{-1,+1\}$, every receiver row is then the signed circular convolution

$$
A_i^{(r)}
=
\sigma_i\sum_{s=0}^{2N-1}\sigma_{i+s}K_s^{(r)}(\beta_f),
\qquad
A_i^{(t)}
=
\sigma_i\sum_{s=0}^{2N-1}\sigma_{i+s}K_s^{(t)}(\beta_f).
$$

Thus the causal topology and root ledger need be computed once per $(N,\beta_f)$, not once per polarity orbit. The existing full evaluator and unchanged generic/self-root references remain the required independent checks at every accepted candidate and declared negative control.

Plainly: changing the polarity pattern changes only the signs used to combine one shared geometric root ledger. Re-solving the same causal equations 40,952 times at every speed would add cost without adding evidence.
