## Shared metric-contract adjudication

For a frozen seed event $t_0$ with $N$ persistent, equally weighted members, define the centered seed and centered-RMS ruler

$$
\mathbf C_0=\frac1N\sum_i\mathbf X_i(t_0),
\qquad
L_0^2=\frac1N\sum_i\lVert\mathbf X_i(t_0)-\mathbf C_0\rVert^2
=\frac1{2N^2}\sum_{i,j}\lVert\mathbf X_i(t_0)-\mathbf X_j(t_0)\rVert^2.
$$

With $c_f=1$ in every numerical instantiation, the associated native units are

$$
T_0=\frac{L_0}{c_f},
\qquad
\widehat{\mathbf X}=\frac{\mathbf X}{L_0},
\qquad
\widehat{\mathbf V}=\frac{\mathbf V}{c_f},
\qquad
\widehat{\mathbf A}=\frac{L_0}{c_f^2}\mathbf A.
$$

Plainly: $L_0$ is the equal-member radius of gyration at release. One time unit is the time a wake moving at $c_f$ takes to cross that radius. Moving or rotating the entire candidate does not change these units, and a uniform size change rescales the ruler and clock together.

**Derived:** $L_0$ is invariant under translation and proper rotation. Under $\mathbf X\mapsto\lambda\mathbf X$ and $t-t_0\mapsto\lambda(t-t_0)$ with fixed $c_f$, all hatted quantities remain unchanged. A nonzero dimensional target common to every candidate fails this scaling test, so `M07` may use only a common dimensionless target; the accepted future convention is $\tau_{\mathrm{target}}=1$.

**Proposed and accepted for future predeclared packets:** `M05` and `M06` use member-level acceleration mismatch in units $c_f^2/L_0$; `M08` uses the horizon supremum of the full position-and-rate distance to the exact predeclared invariant surface; `M10` and `M11` use member RMS position and rate mismatch in units $L_0$ and $c_f$ at one eligible event and one frozen exact action; `M12` uses the same action and event over a predeclared dimensionless retained-history depth with bijective root ownership; `M13` uses the largest full state-history distance across materially different prehistories after certified seed-influence clearance.

Plainly: future packets now have one ruler, one clock, and one way to keep position, rate, and earlier-history return separate while forcing them to refer to the same event. These definitions do not make an old record eligible when that record did not measure the required fields.

The exact residual formulas, including the member-acceleration norm, full-state surface distance, same-action endpoint residuals, endpoint-orthogonalized retained-history residual, normalized causal-root-time term, and multi-prehistory pair/window selector, are frozen in the [score packet](../candidate-weighted-score-packet.md#common-ruler-clock-and-exact-action-boundary). That packet is the implementation contract; this closeout records the adjudication and consequences.
