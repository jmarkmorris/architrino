### Derived continuous-error argument

For one coordinate, let $f$ be the analytic F5 path, $Hf$ its exact endpoint-position-and-tangent cubic Hermite interpolant on $[a,b]$, and $q$ the actual serialized cubic. Write $h=b-a$, $e_0=q(a)-f(a)$, $e_1=q(b)-f(b)$, $d_0=q'(a)-f'(a)$, and $d_1=q'(b)-f'(b)$. The Bernstein control values of $q-Hf$ are

$$
e_0,\quad e_0+\frac h3d_0,\quad e_1-\frac h3d_1,\quad e_1,
$$

and those of its derivative are

$$
d_0,\quad \frac3h(e_1-e_0)-d_0-d_1,\quad d_1.
$$
