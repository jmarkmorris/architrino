## Exact Reduction

For $|u|<1$, write

$$
\gamma=\sqrt{1-u^2}.
$$

The accepted screw-path reduction maps a stationary balance at transverse speed $b_n$ and radius $R_n$ to the only possible translated tangential-balance coordinates

$$
\beta_f(u)=\gamma b_n,
\qquad
R(u)=\frac{R_n}{\gamma}.
$$

Its axial residual is

$$
\mathcal R_z(u)=uS(b_n),
$$

with

$$
S(b_n)=
\sum_m
\frac{(-1)^m}
{4\sin^2v_m\,|b_n\cos v_m-1|}.
$$

Because every certified interval below has $S(b_n)<0$, $\mathcal R_z(u)=0$ if and only if $u=0$ on the declared speed interval. The conclusion is uniform in $u$; no speed sampling is used.

Plainly: changing the translation speed only multiplies a mode-specific signed weight by $u$. Once that weight is proved nonzero, every nonzero upward or downward speed fails the axial balance test.
