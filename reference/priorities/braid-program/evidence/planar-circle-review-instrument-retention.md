No retained instrument was executed on its target during this integration. All are self-contained Node files that write to stdout; source inspection found no file import, filesystem input or hardcoded `.tmp` dependency. `departure-cross.mjs` takes its step from the `DT` environment variable; `growth-refit-run.mjs` takes an optional first positional step argument. The remaining inputs are hardcoded. Future replay must first use a genuinely known input on the same checking path, record an explicit pass and stop on failure. Retaining the source neither supplies that control nor repairs the historical evidence ordering.

The [analytic confinement proof](../analysis/planar-circle-growing-mode.md#31-analytic-confinement-of-nonnegative-real-part-roots) independently bounds all roots with nonnegative real part by $|z|<3$. Together with $F'(0)=1$ and the positive-axis sign argument, it establishes a simple phase root and at least one positive real root. The reported winding near two remains an uncertified diagnostic. A complete count needs a nonvanishing enclosure for the entire contour image and a validated total argument change, including control between samples. Neither an analytic outer bound, a Newton search, nor pointwise interval values alone supplies that count. Uniqueness and multiplicity of the positive root, absence of other center roots and the dimension of the unstable manifold remain unproved by these instruments.
