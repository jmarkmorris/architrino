## Composition and Taxonomy

Candidate classification separates assembly composition, individual braid structure, and persistent binary identity. Neutral member identifiers carry no mechanism or geometry by themselves; coordinate tables, worldline maps, symmetries, and retained histories supply the content. Persistent indices must not be reassigned by instantaneous radius, frequency, speed, or exposure.

The universal two-braid fermion proposal is an economical composition hypothesis, not canon. Its value is comparative: it puts single-braid-plus-axial and two-braid architectures in one configuration chart so persistence and charged-sector bookkeeping can decide between them. A surviving single-braid charged-fermion construction would remove the need for universal two-braid composition; a systematic failure of single braids paired with retained conjugate-braid assemblies would support it.

## Geometry and Deformation

Several apparently distinct symmetric six-site constructions reduce to existing coordinate families when their axes, radii, frequencies, and antipodality are written explicitly. A genuinely new family therefore requires an invariant or a time-dependent coordinate absent from the existing chart, such as a controlled radial breathing mode. Prescribed breathing, spherical constraints, or planarization are chart definitions and comparison instruments until the Master Equation supplies the required acceleration contributions and retained history.

Plainly: a new drawing is not necessarily a new braid. The coordinate record must show which mathematical freedom is actually new.

Accessory Configuration claims require all six sites, including polarity and path or position. Lower moments may organize comparisons only after the complete site record is known; a reduced four-site or two-site surrogate cannot stand in for the current six-site architecture.

## Analytical Structure

Winding, causal-delay moments, angular approximations, and static-balance identities can provide exact or bounded diagnostics on declared charts. They do not certify a braid. In particular, a controlled approximation is useful only when its remainder is bounded on the same domain, and a stationary or prescribed rotating geometry is not a retained branch until delayed roots, acceleration, continuation, and stability close.

The explored symmetric neutral six-point static arrangements supply scoped negative results on their declared strata. ## Coordinate Coincidence

Coordinate coincidence is an event class in which persistent labeled architrinos occupy the same Euclidean coordinate at one absolute time. It is not, by itself, a contact, merger, rebound, annihilation, or identity-loss rule. Ordinary positive-separation formulas are inadmissible at the event; a candidate passage requires a declared regularized or quarantined chart that preserves labels, polarities, incoming states, causal-wake histories, and a refinement-stable outgoing history.

Plainly: two paths meeting at one coordinate does not tell the dynamics what happens next. The continuation law and its convergence evidence must do that work.
