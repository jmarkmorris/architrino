A role assignment fails when another persistent index gives the same diagnostic within tolerance, when the maximizing index changes without being recorded, or when the role was inferred only from a radius or frequency label.

## Integer Frequency and Assembly Transitions

## Coordinate Coincidence and Continuation

Coordinate coincidence means that two persistent labeled architrinos occupy the same Euclidean coordinate at one absolute time. It does not introduce contact, rebound, merger, annihilation, or identity loss. A candidate with such an event must transfer from the ordinary positive-separation formula to a declared coincidence-capable chart while preserving labels, polarities, incoming velocities, causal-root provenance, and retained path histories.

For each pair, define

$$
s_{ij}(T)=\|\mathbf X_i(T)-\mathbf X_j(T)\|^2,
$$

so that closest approaches are enclosed through

$$
\dot s_{ij}
=
2(\mathbf X_i-\mathbf X_j)\mathbin{\cdot}(\mathbf V_i-\mathbf V_j)
=0.
$$

Because $s_{ij}$ touches zero without changing sign, a sign-change detector is insufficient. Coordinate-coincidence limit closure requires one finite, refinement-path-independent event-integrated acceleration contribution and one complete outgoing retained history. Regulator-dependent outcomes, provenance loss, or an unowned causal-root family falsify the proposed closure. The current gate and next protocol are owned by [candidate-registry.md](candidate-registry.md).

Plainly: sharing a coordinate is an event to classify, not a verdict about what the paths do next.
