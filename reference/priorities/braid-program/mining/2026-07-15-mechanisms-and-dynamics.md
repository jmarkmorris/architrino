## 1. Discrete action clicks as root-topology transitions; rectification as the confinement enabler

**The idea.** The action quantum may be implemented as a discrete topological event — one causal-interaction root opening or closing as a trajectory crosses the field-speed boundary — so action arrives in integer clicks because root counts are integers. The inversion worth testing: a continuous self-interaction cannot rectify a breathing cycle (it only slows a leak), while discrete finite-size events can fire on one phase and coast on the other; stable matter would then exist *because* action is quantized. Beneath the integer ledger sits an analog tipping point where an arbitrarily small external wake decides whether a transaction fires — a candidate substrate for measurement-adjacent physics.
**Why it matters.** Mechanism-level target for the action quantum, and a structural reason fully continuous confinement searches might fail.
**Confirm/kill.** On the validated retained-history engine, compare confinement of a breathing state under continuous self-interaction versus discrete root-transition events; rectification appearing only in the discrete case confirms.
**Source:** `braid-archive/braid-ideal/brainstorming.md` (~L2785, 3019, 3039).
**Recommended disposition:** brainstorming entry; the fold-crossing treatment (item 2) is its instrument prerequisite. Note: the fold-set quantization hypothesis survives in corpus at hypothesis level; this adds the rectification argument and the analog tipping point.

## 7. Round-trip wake memory as the relaxation channel; stability questions have a dependency order

**Why it matters.** A candidate damping mechanism the fresh relaxation campaigns can test, plus two diagnosis rules.

## 8. Stabilizers must lock phase, not just shape

**The idea.** A confining response that presses constituents back onto a preferred surface can hold size and still fail to make the object recur, because the failure lives in the tangential/phase direction. Screen any proposed binding mechanism for whether it supplies phase or cadence locking along the orbit, not only normal support.
**Why it matters.** A cheap triage criterion before spending evolution budget on a candidate stabilizer.
**Confirm/kill.** Project a candidate mechanism's response into surface-normal and surface-tangent components; a null tangent component disqualifies it as a recurrence-closer.

## 9. Three-regime completeness as the reason for three layers — posed as a persistence theorem

The sharp version: under joint drift and medium-gradient stress, does the stable slow manifold persist for three or more layers while losing normal hyperbolicity for two or fewer?
