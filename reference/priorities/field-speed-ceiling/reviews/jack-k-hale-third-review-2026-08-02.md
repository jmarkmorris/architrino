## 1. Executive verdict

The construction does four things correctly and one thing it does not know it is doing.

Correct: (i) the event map itself — zero matched impulse, position/velocity continuity, labeled splice, single ownership — is a well-formed proposed event law once the state contract and guard are tightened as specified below; (ii) the isolated straight-trace compatibility census is complete and correct as a conditional trace verification (independently reverified here, algebraically and numerically); (iii) immediate exact rebound is correctly excluded in the declared class, subject to one missing atom-ownership clause; (iv) the single-ownership rule is not merely defensible — a short triangle argument proves the owned family can **never** re-activate on any ceiling-admissible right trace, so double booking is structurally impossible (finding JKH3-4, a strengthening the documents do not yet claim).

What it does not know: the anticipated upgrade path — "compatibility plus a no-Zeno census-stability lemma gives local uniqueness" — appears to be **blocked, not merely open**. The straight separating trace carries a continuum of co-moving trailing partner fronts whose activation margins $2c_f(s-T_{\mathrm c})$ accumulate at zero. Because unlike polarities attract, this margin-zero boundary supports a candidate one-parameter family of *delayed spontaneous-braking* continuations: solutions that coincide with the straight trace on $[T_{\mathrm c},u_*]$ for an arbitrary $u_*>T_{\mathrm c}$ and then brake self-consistently on newly activated ordinary partner roots, in apparent full compliance with every declared clause of the restart law, the swept-source law, and the corrected admission rule. I verify the branch on the reduced collinear mirror system numerically (Verification log, check 3). If it survives full-system scrutiny, the restart law defines a multivalued continuation relation, not a locally unique restart — and census stability is not a missing hypothesis to be proved but a false property of this chart that can only be *imposed* as a new selection postulate. Finding JKH3-6 is therefore the centerpiece of this review, and the promotion recommendation is conditioned on its disposition.

Plainly: the event itself is clean — nothing kicks, everything is remembered, nothing is billed twice. The trouble starts a moment later. After the pair separates, each one runs exactly even with a whole train of the partner's outgoing wakefronts, none of which has quite caught it. If either one slows by any amount at any time, the nearest front catches it, the attraction pulls it back further, and the slowdown feeds itself. The mathematics as written does not forbid a solution from starting that slide spontaneously, and I could not find a clause that breaks the tie between "keep flying straight" and "start sliding at time $u_*$" for any chosen $u_*$.

### JKH3-4 — DERIVED with a new lemma (Question 4). The single-ownership rule is consistent, and more: the owned family can never re-activate, so the rule's re-billing escape clause is provably vacuous and double booking is structurally impossible.

**Owned-family permanence lemma.** Let $c_a\le c_f$, let $(j,s_0)$ be any owned emission ($s_0\le T_{\mathrm c}$ in the matched cap, including the endpoint), and let the receiver satisfy the ceiling on $T>T_{\mathrm c}$ with $\mathbf X_i(T_{\mathrm c})=\mathbf x_{\mathrm c}$. The causal gap
$$
\gamma(T)=c_f(T-s_0)-\|\mathbf X_i(T)-\mathbf X_j(s_0)\|
$$
satisfies $\gamma(T_{\mathrm c})=0$ (the receiver sits on the front at the event) and $\gamma'\ge c_f-\|\mathbf V_i\|\ge0$ almost everywhere, and by the triangle inequality through $\mathbf x_{\mathrm c}$, $\gamma(T)=0$ forces the exact straight full-speed recession ray from $\mathbf X_j(s_0)$ through $\mathbf x_{\mathrm c}$. Hence on any ceiling-admissible right trace, each owned emission is either exactly ridden — the frozen endpoint on the straight trace, or the rigid $D_t=D_r=0$ characteristic interval on the exact rebound ray, both silenced by clause 4 — or strictly interior, and once strictly interior it remains strictly interior forever. **No owned emission can ever return as an isolated positive-delay crossing root.** (Verification log, check 2; the equality case reproduces the disposition theorem's dichotomy.)

Consequences: (i) the sets "event-aggregated at $E$" and "later ordinarily billed" are provably disjoint — the rule cannot double-book even in principle; (ii) the rule cannot suppress a later isolated transverse reception, because any such reception necessarily comes from an *unowned* emission (post-event, or third-party); (iii) the wording ambiguity in clause 4 ("without sweeping new source history") is resolved — re-sweeping *owned* history is geometrically impossible, so the clause never has to decide that case; (iv) the restart note's sentence "If a retained emission later becomes an isolated positive-delay root with $D_t,D_r>0$, it is again an ordinary reception" is correct but can only ever apply to unowned emissions, which the text should say.

**Formal owned-silence criterion** (the membership rule Question 4 requests). For channel $c=(i\leftarrow j)$ at $T>T_{\mathrm c}$, a causal root or root set $\Sigma\subset\{s:g_c(T,s)=0\}$ is event-silenced iff $\Sigma\subseteq\overline{\mathfrak O_E(c)}$ and the channel's received-history clock is locally constant at $T$ (frozen singleton) or $\Sigma$ is a nondegenerate characteristic interval (necessarily the rigid chord). Otherwise the root is ordinary iff it is isolated, positive-delay, $D_t>0$, with locally increasing clock (the JKH2-1-corrected rule). This decides the five requested cases: the owned noncrossing family (silenced); the frozen partner endpoint $S(T)=T_{\mathrm c}$ (silenced; also inactive under SSR independently); the co-moving same-transmitter family (characteristic, silenced by the separate same-transmitter convention, ownership not even needed); the $D_t=D_r=0$ inherited interval on the rebound ray (silenced as owned characteristic); a later ordinary root with $D_t,D_r>0$ (necessarily unowned by the lemma; billed normally).

Grade: lemma `derived` (three-line triangle argument, numerically spot-checked); criterion `derived` restatement of clause 4. Falsifier: one ceiling-admissible right trace and owned $s_0$ with $g(T,s_0)=0$ at some $T>T_{\mathrm c}$ off the exact recession ray; the inequality chain forbids it.

Plainly: once the collision has been paid for, geometry itself tears up the invoice. Every wakefront in the paid family either stays exactly under the receiver's feet — the two rigid cases the rule already silences — or falls behind forever. Nothing paid can ever be re-billed, and nothing that can be billed later was ever paid. The ownership rule is not just careful bookkeeping; it is enforced by the speed ceiling.

### JKH3-6 — PROPOSED COUNTEREXAMPLE / UNRESOLVED QUESTION (Questions 6, 7). Compatibility does not upgrade to selection: the straight trace appears to be one member of a continuum of admissible continuations, because the trailing-front margins vanish and unlike polarities attract.

This is the decisive finding. Structure of the argument:

**(a) Margin-zero boundary.** On the straight trace, a post-event partner emission at $s>T_{\mathrm c}$ has $g(T,s)=2c_f(s-T_{\mathrm c})$ for all $T$ — a constant positive margin, co-moving, never caught. The margins accumulate at zero as $s\downarrow T_{\mathrm c}$. There is no inactive gap: the straight chart sits on the boundary of activation at every post-event time. Grade of (a): `derived` (two-line algebra, reverified numerically).

**(b) Ignition mechanism.** Suppose a candidate continuation coincides with the straight trace on $[T_{\mathrm c},u_*]$, then develops a symmetric collinear slowdown, $v(w)=c_f-m(w)$, $m\ge0$, with deficit integral $E(w)=\int(c_f-v)$. The partner-channel root equation becomes $2c_f(s-T_{\mathrm c})=E(w)+E(s)$: a root **exists** whenever $E(w)>0$, detaching continuously from the owned frozen endpoint. At the root, $D_t=c_f+v(s)\approx2c_f$ (bona fide transversality), $D_r=c_f-v(w)=m(w)>0$, clock strictly increasing — an isolated positive-delay ordinary crossing root under the corrected admission rule, sweeping genuinely *new* (post-event) source history, hence explicitly returned to the ordinary ledger by clause 4. Its row has bounded magnitude at detachment, $A_0=K/[2c_f^3(u_*-T_{\mathrm c})^2]$, and — by the foundations' polarity law, unlike polarities attract — points **backward**. The self-consistency loop closes: $m(w)=A_0(w-u_*)+o(w-u_*)$, $E(w)=\tfrac12A_0(w-u_*)^2>0$ activates the root for every $w>u_*$, and the pair $(m,E)$ solves the restart law with the activated row. This is the classic spontaneous-ignition nonuniqueness of a right-hand side that switches on with **full bounded magnitude at zero threshold** (compare $\dot x=\mathrm{sign}$-type dry-friction models): the history-to-ledger map is discontinuous at the straight trace, so no Osgood or Lipschitz criterion applies. The straight trace and the $u_*$-branch share the *complete identical history* through $u_*$ and both appear to satisfy every declared clause afterward. Verification log check 3 integrates the branch on the reduced collinear system: fully self-consistent, no clause violated, $v$ falling smoothly from $c_f$.

**(c) What is excluded.** Immediate sustained ignition at $u_*=T_{\mathrm c}$ **is** excluded in the declared class: the detachment row scales as $K/[2c_f^3(w-T_{\mathrm c})^2]$, whose time integral diverges at $T_{\mathrm c}$, incompatible with an absolutely continuous ball-valued velocity. So the answer to the assignment's Zeno question splits: activations with positive density accumulating at $T_{\mathrm c}$ are excluded (`derived`); thin Zeno cascades at $T_{\mathrm c}$ (activation bursts of vanishing measure) are not yet excluded (`unresolved question`, though the total-variation budget $\int_{\text{active}}(w-T_{\mathrm c})^{-2}dw\le c_f^2\cdot\mathrm{const}$ forces the active set's density to vanish at $T_{\mathrm c}$, a strong constraint); and delayed ignition at every $u_*>T_{\mathrm c}$ is not excluded at all — it is candidate-realized.

**(d) Consequences.** (i) The strongest justified statement is: *the straight trace is an exact solution of the isolated restart problem, and no immediate-jump alternative exists; the event law does not select it.* Compatibility must not be promoted. (ii) The anticipated "no-Zeno census-stability hypothesis" cannot be *proved* for this chart — census stability in the FSC-007 sense (uniform margins, fixed census on a history neighborhood) is **false** here, since the margins $2c_f(s-T_{\mathrm c})$ vanish. It can only be *imposed*, e.g. as a uniform inactive-gap floor at activation, and imposing it is a new physical selection postulate (no spontaneous rebinding), not a technical lemma. (iii) If the branch family is instead accepted, the restart law defines a multivalued continuation relation, and — because the branching solutions share identical complete microstates with the straight trace — this contradicts the determinism claim of the foundations (deterministic multistability presumes distinct microstates select distinct branches; here the microstate is the same). Either outcome is a foundational decision above this reviewer's authority; the review's job is to establish that the decision exists.

**Caveats and grade.** The branch is established at reduced-system rigor: exact mirror symmetry imposed, collinear, ledger verified to contain exactly the one detached root per receiver (owned families strictly interior by JKH3-4; self co-moving family silent; no self root once $v<c_f$). Remaining verification burdens: existence of an exact solution of the reduced state-dependent-delay ODE through the ignition instant (the right-hand side is discontinuous exactly there; after ignition it is locally Lipschitz, so this is a boundary-case Carathéodory argument, not a deep one); and embedding in the full three-dimensional class. Grade: **(a) `derived`; (b) `plausible inference` at full-system level, `derived modulo the ignition-instant existence argument` at reduced-system level; (c) first part `derived`, second `unresolved question`; (d) `derived consequences of (b) if (b) stands`.** Falsifiers: exhibit a declared clause of the restart law, the swept-source law, the corrected admission rule, or the Section 5 obligations (as currently quantified) that the branch violates — I checked each and found none, but one demonstrated violation kills the family; or prove the reduced branch does not extend to a solution of the full system; or prove a uniqueness theorem in a class provably containing both candidate continuations. Conversely the finding is *confirmed* by a Carathéodory existence proof for the reduced branch plus a full-system embedding.

Plainly: the document hoped the missing uniqueness proof was a lemma nobody had written yet. This review's claim is sharper and worse: on this chart the lemma is false as usually stated, because the separating pair runs dead even with an infinite ladder of almost-caught wakefronts, and falling off that ladder is self-consistent at any moment.
