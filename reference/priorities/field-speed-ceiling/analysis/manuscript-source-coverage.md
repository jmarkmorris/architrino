## Extracted scientific content and final dispositions

### Motivation, primitives and the proposed response

The canonical acceleration-first master equation distinguishes finite wake propagation from unrestricted architrino path speed. A path ceiling is additional dynamics, not a consequence of finite wake speed. The proposed ceiling `c_a` and wake speed `c_f` must remain distinct; all new numerical instances use `c_f=1`. The proposal retains Euclidean void, absolute time, labels, polarity, delayed paths and the ordinary inverse-square causal-root kernel, then applies a normal-cone constraint once to the complete finite ordinary acceleration ledger. A speed inequality alone does not define the response. Forward radial reaction is removed at the ball boundary; inward and transverse components survive. Summing separately projected rows fails. Finite-ledger response is Lipschitz at fixed direction but discontinuous from the interior to the boundary; supplied-input well-posedness uses monotonicity, not a fictitious smooth vector field.

### Causal geometry and measures

At or below wake speed the root function is nondecreasing in emission time and nonincreasing in reception time. Ordinary branches carry `S'=D_r/D_t`. Empty, simple, degenerate and characteristic fibers must be distinguished; receiver tangency at one instant is not a frozen interval. Characteristic and frozen intervals are rigid exact-speed straight motions. A strict speed gap gives one partner root on complete all-past histories with distinct present positions and excludes self roots, but does not remove zero-range singularity. At equality a same-label root requires an exact straight wake-speed chord; a curved circle has no self reception. Cubic degeneracy needs a nonzero third derivative; genericity and singular-continuous-clock exclusion do not follow from weak topology. An event catalogue is not yet an exhaustive stratification. The velocity ball and sphere supply no nontrivial fundamental-group protection.

The receiver-time/source-time transfer identity cancels a small transmitter factor only with the branch, injectivity, range and receiver-factor hypotheses. The mirror incoming simple branch has finite accumulated raw acceleration before coincidence despite a divergent pointwise row; that result is not the cap-family endpoint measure. Conditional compact-source convergence requires eventual branch coverage, collapse to the event, moving-trace and kernel convergence, positive receiver factor and separated competing strata. The endpoint variation grows as `K/(2 c_f^2 rho)` and has residue `K/(2 c_f^2)` in the stated iterated order; no finite vector-Radon ordinary endpoint measure follows. Principal-value cancellation on disjoint times cannot repair infinite total variation. A finite normalized projection of a divergent forward sequence is not a canonical infinite-ledger response; rotating transverse residues provide a counterexample, and removing the leading term does not control its remainder.

The exact uniform-translation spatial measure theorem is distinct: the three-dimensional source-provenanced marginal converges in local total variation to the wake-speed trailing density, with zero singular residual. Its lifted characteristic fiber has zero angular measure. This is neither a point-receiver self-action limit nor a contact or event law; lower-dimensional slices and all-space finiteness are outside the result.

### Coincidence, continuation and recurrence

The proposed event cancellation uses a finite signed labeled source-history carrier, equal matched weights, a common event support and a common linear event-to-acceleration map. Opposite ordinary rows do not cancel: polarity and direction both reverse. The finite source event construction does not derive an extension of the singular ordinary kernel. The event returns immediate continuous position/velocity data and typed ownership, clocks and measure records. It does not return a uniquely determined future. Complete incoming coverage is checked by the sign of each retained causal gap; unarrived external fronts remain in the remainder. Owned fronts can only remain ridden or pass permanently inside, never return as an ordinary crossing. Zero event atom excludes immediate rebound in the atom-owned solution class, not later continuous braking.

The isolated straight right trace is compatible but has trailing-front margins with infimum zero. For every positive waiting time `u`, an exact local mirror branch shares the complete straight past and then brakes on one newly activated ordinary partner root per receiver. The ordinary acceleration determines evolution after an onset is supplied; no admitted wake/action mechanism supplies that onset. The no-cascade theorem excludes ordinary activation-component accumulation at the event in the stated mirror class without restoring uniqueness. Regular-chart positive-gap theorems cannot contain this margin-zero event neighborhood.

The complete-lobe result has explicit sufficient bounds, not necessary thresholds: a finite turnaround requires a formal `K/u>3/2` balance, while stronger bounds control which source history is sampled, positive-separation inward-cap arrival and return-event recovery. The theorem's `K>=7u/2` completion bound is sufficient; historical numerical evidence about sharper thresholds has a missing-instrument gap recorded by the live queue. Equal prescribed waiting times produce a spatial two-cycle. The future-equivalence quotient upgrades this to a controlled quotient-state cycle only after complete owned, consumed records have a strict permanent passage margin; current zero-gap families, clocks, ownership and cap length remain live. Equality means equality of future sets, not a selected future. Literal all-past history grows.

For the cap-reset convention `u_next=G(K,u)`, the closed cap-ratio formula is restricted to `alpha=K/u>=6`: `G/u=(1+z^2)(1-z atan(z))`, `z=sqrt(2/(alpha-2))`. It is strictly between zero and one; there is no positive fixed point. The shrinking waits are of inverse-square-root order and event periods have divergent sum, excluding finite event accumulation in that controlled sequence. This convention is not autonomous onset physics. Option A explicitly preserves the multivalued relation; no-bootstrap, an invariant waiting-time functional, a regularization selection, an action trigger and minimal-acceleration choices are additional proposals rather than inferred laws.

### Circular binaries, perturbations and six-path tests

The complete isolated antipodal circle has one partner root and no positive-delay self root. Its half-delay angle solves `xi=lambda cos(xi)`, `lambda=c_a/c_f`, and its normal-cone compatibility radius is `R=K/[4 c_a^2 cos(xi)(1+lambda sin(xi))]`. In the normalized equality case `D=cos(D)`, `D≈0.7390851332151606`, `R/K≈0.2021113735152611`. The family radius decreases as the speed ratio increases at fixed coupling/wake speed; this is a family-specific minimum, never a universal minimum radius. Interior uniform circles retain a forward residual and fail; boundary projection is essential to this ansatz. Constant-speed rigid translation must be normal to the circle plane, and the resulting helix retains a negative axial residual for every nonzero translation. The row magnitude remains finite and independent of the translation split even as individual root factors collapse. This excludes that full rigid constant-boundary-speed translation class, not arbitrary moving assemblies.

The small-speed expansion, chord-exchange condition for `D_t=D_r`, derivative floor and interval-Newton-ready form are conditional analytic consequences. The retained numerical receipts are reproducibility witnesses, not independent interval proof. The geometric `W^{2,infinity}` circle tube gives explicit root brackets, range/delay/transversality floors, collision margin and self-root exclusion. It does not prove that coupled evolution stays in the tube. The regular history-to-ledger theorem uses finite slots, a complete delay window, selected BV representatives, atom-free bounded acceleration, preserved strata, explicit positive floors and an invariant response cylinder. Its method-of-steps contraction gives unique local continuation for the exact circle; the whole perturbative tube still needs an invariant response regime and compatible right-acceleration trace. No stability, capture or formation follows.

Three coupled binaries cannot be assembled by superposing isolated projected circles. A six-path boundary-speed chart requires six nonnegative complete-ledger parallel scalars and twelve perpendicular scalar residual equalities for the whole period. Exact speed equalities and inequality margins have different tangent rules. Common-period winding, phase quotient and frame redundancy need explicit treatment; naive relative phases fail for some winding integers. Homothetic scaling gives at most one positive scale for a fixed curved shape, not existence. A small-radius obstruction assumes bounded external rows; unbounded cross rows or leading cancellation change the hypotheses.

The quarantined orthogonal equal-radius six-path reference has a proved complete root census but fails the response test for all four polarity orientations after global reversal: negative parallel components or nonzero binormal residuals remain. Sampled all-time numerical extrema are not interval bounds; a high-precision time-zero receipt is not a whole-cycle certificate. The paths form a time-configuration loop, not six disjoint spatial link components. Alternative transverse redirection requires an additional geometric direction and magnitude law and has not rescued the chart.

### Accounts, stability and remaining obligations

The isolated circle's raw cycle integral of `V dot A` is positive while its effective integral is zero. That is a velocity-squared diagnostic, not an energy or action account; no law stores the removed forward component. A native action pair, cycle action, an energy-frequency identity and quantization each need separate derivations. Radius/frequency retuning preserves the boundary-speed product but requires a full event guard, outgoing retained history and accounts. Finite standoff, sea-driven return, adiabatic stability and effective observer analogies remain exploratory. Stability requires a constructed solution, a declared differentiable evolution and return map, correct symmetry reduction and spectral conditions; no spectrum about a non-solution is meaningful. Local finiteness of a background does not ensure absolute convergence of an inverse-square infinite wake sum; ordering and tail cancellation must be justified.

The numerical contract needs independent all-root certificates, rounding and quadrature budgets, method-of-steps delay floors, response-cylinder control and propagated breaking-point detection. Supplied-input discrete projection is nonexpansive; an order-one rate and some historical numerical suggestions are still proposed. Reordered finite-precision sums are compared within a declared accumulation tolerance. High precision, step refinement and replay of a shared formula establish neither independent correctness nor scientific adoption.
