$$
\dot{\mathbf V}
+
\mathbf n
=
\mathbf A_{\mathrm{ord}},
\qquad
\mathbf n\in N_{\mathcal B_{c_f}}(\mathbf V)
\quad\text{a.e.}
$$

Under those proposed solution-form clauses, the minimal-selection theorem derives

$$
\dot{\mathbf V}
=
\Pi_{T_{\mathcal B_{c_f}}(\mathbf V)}
\left(\mathbf A_{\mathrm{ord}}\right)
\quad\text{a.e.}

It is the identity below $c_f$. At $\|\mathbf V\|=c_f$, it removes only the net forward speed-increasing component and retains transverse and backward components. The derivation does not follow from the bare speed inequality.

The first equality says that the nonisolated contact family is not an ordinary positive-separation isolated-reception row.
