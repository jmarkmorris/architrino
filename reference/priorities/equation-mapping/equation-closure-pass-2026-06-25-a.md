- `bidirectional`: the row already supports a useful map from $\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}$ carriers to the standard equation and inverse clues from the standard equation back to missing native rows.
- `bulk/effective`: the row is currently best treated as an observer-level, continuum, statistical, or family-level residual.
- `building-block`: the row should primarily become a reusable sub-equation carrier consumed by many rows.

The lane is the best closure route, not a score decision. Every row except the native root row remains blocked from score movement until accepted retained evidence replaces attempt, toy, comparison-only, or checker-only rows.
