Their present motion depends on path history because received influence arrives from prior source positions, not from instantaneous action at a distance.

It is an effective description of a stable or transient architrino assembly. A field is not the default substrate substance; it is an effective way of summarizing many source-provenanced causal wakes. Second is assembly and medium behavior: binaries, neutral braids, nested shell braids, particles, Noether sea density, stress, flow, and response.
