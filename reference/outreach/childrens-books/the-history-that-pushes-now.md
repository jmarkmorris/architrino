Book: 15

## Book Promise

This first formal dynamics book teaches:

1. Present motion depends on admissible path-history.
2. Field speed selects which past emissions can arrive.
3. Self-action can occur when an entity meets its own causal history.

Parent/teacher terms: `state`, `path-history`, `causal root`, `causal-root ledger`, `field speed`, `self-hit`, `master equation`.
