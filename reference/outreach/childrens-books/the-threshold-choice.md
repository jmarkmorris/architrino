# The Threshold Choice

Book: 13

Age interval: `12-13`

Series frame: **The Wonder of Nature and the Universe**

Wonder question: Why can tiny differences matter?

Discovery sentence: Near a threshold, a tiny difference can choose between lawful outcomes.

Text status: text-only manuscript draft; no image prompts in this pass.

## Book Promise

This book teaches:

1. Small differences can matter.
2. Thresholds choose among lawful paths.
3. Settling basins record outcomes.

Parent/teacher terms: `threshold`, `basin`, `deterministic multistability`, `sensitive dependence`, `record`.

## Parent Note

This book should not teach that sensitive systems are magical or uncaused. The point is sharper: lawful dynamics can turn tiny differences near thresholds into stable, readable outcomes.
