# Nature Remembers Motion

Book: 3

Age interval: `2-3`

Series frame: **The Wonder of Nature and the Universe**

Wonder question: How can we tell something moved?

Discovery sentence: Motion can leave a trace.

Text status: text-only manuscript draft; no image prompts in this pass.

## Book Promise

This older-toddler / first-preschool book teaches:

1. Motion leaves traces.
2. Before and after order a path.
3. A path can be followed.

Parent/teacher terms: `trace`, `path`, `before`, `after`, `path-history`.

## Read-Aloud Manuscript

### Cover

Read-aloud title:

> Nature Remembers Motion

### Spread 1: The First Line

Read-aloud text:

> Electra pulled a ribbon.
>
> The ribbon made a line.

Editor note:

A moving thing can leave something behind.

### Spread 2: The Ball Goes

Read-aloud text:

> Poz rolled the red ball.
>
> The ball went away.

Editor note:

Motion carries the ball from one place to another.

### Spread 3: The Trail Stays

Read-aloud text:

> The ball was over there.
>
> The trail was still here.

Editor note:

A trace can remain after motion has moved on.

### Spread 4: Before

Read-aloud text:

> This part came first.
>
> That part came next.

Editor note:

A trail can show order.

### Spread 5: Near And Far

Read-aloud text:

> Some parts were close.
>
> Some parts were far.

Editor note:

Spacing helps tell the story of a path.

### Spread 6: The Water Remembers

Read-aloud text:

> Electra tapped the water.
>
> Circles grew from the tap.

Editor note:

Water can show where motion began.

### Spread 7: The Sound Remembers

Read-aloud text:

> Poz clapped.
>
> Electra heard it after.

Editor note:

Some traces travel before they are noticed.

### Spread 8: The Shadow Walks

Read-aloud text:

> Poz walked past the light.
>
> The shadow walked too.

Editor note:

Motion can be shown in more than one way.

### Spread 9: Back Again

Read-aloud text:

> Electra turned around.
>
> The new trail met the old trail.

Editor note:

Returning can cross an earlier path.

### Spread 10: Follow It

Read-aloud text:

> "Can we find where it went?"
>
> Poz followed the trail.

Editor note:

A trace can guide later attention.

### Spread 11: The World Shows Us

Read-aloud text:

> Ribbon.
>
> Water.
>
> Sound.
>
> Shadow.
>
> They all showed motion.

Editor note:

Nature gives clues.

### Spread 12: The World Remembered

Read-aloud text:

> It moved.
>
> The world remembered.

Editor note:

Motion can leave readable history.

## Parent Note

This book keeps `path-history` at the level of visible traces. It does not yet teach the child that delayed influences are physical causes; it prepares that idea by making earlier motion visible and ordered.
