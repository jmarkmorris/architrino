# The Tiny Transceivers

Book: 9

Age interval: `8-9`

Series frame: **The Wonder of Nature and the Universe**

Wonder question: What are the tiny sender-receivers in nature?

Discovery sentence: An architrino is a tiny point entity that keeps a path, sends, and receives.

Text status: text-only manuscript draft; no image prompts in this pass.

## Book Promise

This first explicit architrino-facing book teaches:

1. There are two tiny kinds.
2. Each keeps a path.
3. Each can send and receive.

Parent/teacher terms: `architrino`, `polarity`, `transmission`, `reception`, `path`.

## Read-Aloud Manuscript

### Cover

Read-aloud title:

> The Tiny Transceivers

### Spread 1: The Message Lab

Read-aloud text:

> Electra and Poz opened the message lab.
>
> Their question was small and enormous:
>
> What sends the deepest messages?

Editor note:

The story moves from everyday messages to fundamental senders.

### Spread 2: A Point With A Path

Read-aloud text:

> Electra placed one red point on the screen.
>
> It moved.
>
> They could follow its path.

Editor note:

The tiny entity has persistent identity through motion.

### Spread 3: A Second Kind

Read-aloud text:

> Then Poz placed one blue point.
>
> "This is the other kind,"
>
> Poz said.

Editor note:

The ontology has two fundamental polarities.

### Spread 4: The Name

Read-aloud text:

> A card on the screen said:
>
> architrino.
>
> Electra read the word slowly.

Editor note:

The child meets the canonical term.

### Spread 5: Sender

Read-aloud text:

> The red architrino moved.
>
> From its old place, a message began.

Editor note:

An architrino transmits from its path-history.

### Spread 6: Answer

Read-aloud text:

> The blue architrino crossed the screen.
>
> When the message reached it,
>
> its path changed.

Editor note:

Reception changes future motion.

### Spread 7: Keeps Its Path

Read-aloud text:

>
> Poz said.
>

Editor note:

The entity is tracked through its worldline-like path.

### Spread 8: Two Answers

Read-aloud text:

Editor note:

Polarity matters.

### Spread 9: Toward

Read-aloud text:

### Spread 10: Away

Read-aloud text:

> Another pair bent outward.
>

Read-aloud text:

>
> Electra said.
>

Polarity is physics, not personality.

### Spread 12: Many Tiny Paths

Read-aloud text:

>

### Spread 13: Send And Receive

## Parent Note

This book introduces `architrino` explicitly. Keep the story disciplined: red and blue are intrinsic polarities of point transceivers, never human identities or social metaphors.
