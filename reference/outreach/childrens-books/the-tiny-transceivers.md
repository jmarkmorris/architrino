# The Tiny Transceivers

Book: 9

Age interval: `8-9`

Series frame: **The Wonder of Nature and the Universe**

Wonder question: What are the tiny sender-receivers in nature?

Discovery sentence: An architrino is a tiny point entity that keeps a path, sends, and receives.

## Book Promise

This first explicit architrino-facing book teaches:

1. There are two tiny kinds.
2. Each keeps a path.
3. Each can send and receive.

Parent/teacher terms: `architrino`, `polarity`, `transmission`, `reception`, `path`.
