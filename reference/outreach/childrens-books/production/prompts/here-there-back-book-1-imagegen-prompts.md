# Book 1 Image-Generation Prompt Set: Here, There, Back

Generation mode: built-in `image_gen` tool.

All prompts used the same shared style block:

> Use case: illustration-story
>
> Asset type: 3:2 landscape source illustration for a toddler board book, text-free source art.
>
> Character consistency: Electra is a toddler girl with medium-brown skin, dark curly hair in two small puffs, and simple white-and-lavender play clothes. Poz is a toddler boy with light-brown skin, short straight dark hair, and simple white-and-purple play clothes. They are ordinary human children, not symbols.
>
> Style: approved children's-book exemplar style: natural human skin and hair, informal children-at-play energy, generous white paper space, black expressive linework, soft board-book simplicity, gentle warm expressions.
>
> Palette: non-human visual systems, scenery, blanket trim, clothing, balls, paths, and shadows use only white, black, pure red, pure blue, and red-blue purples. Human skin and hair may use natural tones only.
>
> Constraints: no arrows, no labels, no captions, no readable text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no logo, no extra characters. No tiled floor, no grid, no busy background.

## Cover

Primary request: Single continuous white playroom scene for a toddler board book cover. Electra and Poz sit on a simple white blanket with purple trim. A red ball and a blue ball sit nearby, with a soft dotted purple path loop showing here, there, and back again.

Geometry: restricted red/blue/purple geometry only on the balls and soft dotted path loop. The path loop is subtle and integrated into the scene.

## Spread 1

Primary request: Electra and Poz sit close together on a white blanket with purple trim. A red ball rests in front of them, clearly visible and uncovered. The composition makes three simple places readable: Electra here, Poz here, ball here.

Geometry: no formal diagram; simply make three clear visual positions on the page: Electra, Poz, and the red ball.

## Spread 2

Primary request: A red ball and a blue ball sit on the white floor a short distance from Electra and Poz. Electra looks toward the red ball while Poz looks toward the blue ball. The balls are pure red and pure blue; the room remains white and pale purple with black linework. Keep the page very simple.

Geometry: the red and blue balls are the only strong colored geometry. Keep them separated and easy to point to.

## Spread 3

Primary request: Electra and Poz both reach toward the red ball on the floor. Their hands point toward the ball without touching it yet. Use a very faint purple path glow from their hands toward the ball, subtle enough for a baby board-book page.

Geometry: faint purple path glow toward the ball, integrated into the floor as a gentle visual hint, not a diagram.

## Spread 4

Primary request: The red ball rolls a short distance across the white floor. A few soft purple dots behind the ball show the route it followed. Electra and Poz watch with delight from the blanket. Keep the red ball as the focal object, with no heavy grid.

Geometry: a short sequence of soft purple dots behind the red ball, clearly showing where it rolled, subtle enough for a baby board-book page.

## Spread 5

Primary request: The red ball rests farther away on the white floor. Electra and Poz remain together on the blanket, looking toward the ball. Leave clear open space between the children and the ball so here and there are easy to see.

Geometry: no formal diagram; composition itself teaches near/far and here/there through empty space.

## Spread 6

Primary request: Poz crawls on hands and knees from the blanket toward the red ball while Electra watches from nearby. A few faint purple floor dots behind Poz show earlier body positions. The red ball stays visible ahead. Gentle toddler-board-book composition.

Geometry: faint purple floor dots behind Poz show a simple crawl route through space. Keep the geometry subtle and child-friendly.

## Spread 7

Primary request: The blue ball rolls back toward Electra and Poz on the blanket. A soft dotted purple path curves from far away back to the children. Electra reaches toward the returning ball while Poz watches. Keep the blue ball pure blue and the path subtle.

Geometry: a curved dotted purple return path from far away back to the children; it should feel like motion memory, not a diagram.

## Spread 8

Primary request: Electra and Poz sit together on a simple white blanket with purple trim. A red ball and a blue ball sit nearby in front of them on the white floor. Both children are clearly looking down and forward at the balls, with their heads and eyes aimed directly toward the red and blue balls, not off-page and not away from the balls. Two faint dotted purple floor loops show here, there, back, and again, with one loop softer and one fresher. The scene should feel calm and complete, with generous white space and pale purple shadows.

Geometry: two faint dotted purple floor loops around the balls and children, one older/softer and one newer/fresher; no arrows, no diagram labels.

## Backmatter 1

Primary request: A clean, text-free activity scene for the idea "I am here" without showing words. Electra sits on the white blanket and gently touches her own chest while looking at a red ball nearby. Poz sits beside her and looks at the same ball. The red ball is close enough to compare child-here and ball-there. Keep the scene simple, calm, and easy for a grown-up to use during read-aloud play.

Geometry: no formal diagram; composition itself teaches child-here and ball-there. Optional one or two faint purple floor dots are allowed, but keep them very subtle.
