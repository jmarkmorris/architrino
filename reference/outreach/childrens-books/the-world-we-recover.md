# The World We Recover

Book: 16

Age interval: `15-16`

Series frame: **The Wonder of Nature and the Universe**

Wonder question: How does the familiar universe appear from the deeper architecture?

Discovery sentence: Clocks, rulers, light paths, mass behavior, records, and effective spacetime geometry must be recovered from deeper dynamics, not assumed at the start.

## Book Promise

This capstone book teaches:

1. Assemblies can support observer tools.
2. Familiar physics is recovered, not assumed.
3. Claim levels matter.

Parent/teacher terms: `assembly`, `Noether sea`, `effective metric`, `effective spacetime geometry`, `clock`, `ruler`, `photon channel`, `mass-map`, `observer-level record`, `claim level`.

## Parent Note

This final book should feel like a capstone, not a victory lap. It teaches that familiar physics must be recovered from the deeper architecture, while separating observed, simulated, derived, and open claims. The endpoint should be concrete: clocks, rulers, light-like channels, mass behavior, records, and effective spacetime geometry are recovery targets built from path-history, interactions, assemblies, and Noether sea response.
