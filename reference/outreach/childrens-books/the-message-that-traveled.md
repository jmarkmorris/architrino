# The Message That Traveled

Age band: 6-8

Format: illustrated picture book, 16 story spreads plus back matter

Core concept: causal influence travels. In $\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}$ language, motion leaves causal wakes, and receivers respond only when admissible wake history reaches them.

## Book Promise

This book introduces causal wakes without making the child carry adult mathematics. It teaches four ideas:

1. Nature has a shared order of before and after.
2. Messages from motion take time to arrive.
3. Direction and distance matter.
4. Stable patterns can form when many lawful influences fit together.

The parent/teacher layer should use the canonical terms `absolute time`, `Euclidean void`, `causal wake`, `line of action`, `Noether sea`, and `assembly`. The read-aloud layer should use ordinary language first: beat, place, message, path, near, far, together.

## Art Bible

Use case: illustration-story, scientific-educational.

Style: warm picture-book gouache and colored-pencil texture, clear geometry, friendly classroom and outdoor scenes, soft natural light, visually rich but not cluttered.

Recurring characters:

- **Mira:** a thoughtful six-year-old with a yellow raincoat in outdoor scenes and a green sweater indoors.
- **Tomas:** an energetic eight-year-old with red sneakers and a blue hoodie.
- **Dr. Sol:** their mother, a particle physicist and child psychologist, calm and playful, with a field notebook and simple experiment kit.

Recurring geometry:

- causal wakes appear as translucent pale-gold expanding arcs or rings;
- earlier source positions appear as small glowing dots with faint footprint-like trails;
- line-of-action moments appear as a single clean arrow or luminous thread from the relevant earlier source position to the receiver;
- Noether sea background appears as faint neutral specks arranged in gentle nested patterns, visible only when the scene invites "zooming in";
- assemblies appear as small groups of luminous points held in balanced geometric relation.

Image-generation rule: do not include book text, captions, labels, equations, or watermarks inside the illustration. Any labels should be added later by layout.

## Back Matter For Adults

### What The Child Just Learned

This book teaches causal structure before terminology. The child has seen that:

- events have an order;
- positions and distances matter;
- influence travels rather than appearing everywhere at once;
- a receiver responds when a message reaches it;
- earlier source positions can matter now;
- many influences can add;
- stable patterns can hold together;
- observation is a physical interaction.

### Classroom Activity Sequence

1. Clap across the room and ask which child hears first.
2. Drop a pebble and point to the splash center after the pebble has moved.
3. Roll a marble and mark old positions with sticky notes.
4. Stretch a string from an old source mark to a receiver mark.
5. Add two arrows from two sources to one receiver and ask which way the receiver should move.
6. Build a bead mobile and look for patterns that keep returning.
7. Shine a flashlight on the mobile and ask what changed because of looking.
8. Use an AI or simulation only after children make a prediction.
