# The Measure That Makes A Record

Book: 14

Age interval: `13-14`

Series frame: **The Wonder of Nature and the Universe**

Wonder question: How does looking make a record?

Discovery sentence: Measurement is a physical interaction that changes matter into a readable record.

Text status: text-only manuscript draft; no image prompts in this pass.

## Book Promise

This book teaches:

1. Measurement is physical interaction.
2. Records form in matter.
3. The surrounding medium is not empty.

Parent/teacher terms: `measurement interaction`, `record`, `device`, `branch`, `Noether sea`, `observer-level description`.
