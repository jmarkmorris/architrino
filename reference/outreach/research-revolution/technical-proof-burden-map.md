# Technical Proof-Burden Map: Research Revolution

This packet maps the technical and governance burdens behind the research-revolution memo. It is written for readers who need to see how institutional actions are tied to evidence thresholds, proof obligations, and failure conditions.

## Status Key

**Evidence threshold** means the maturity level that controls what kind of institutional action is justified.

**Reviewable object** means the first technical packet that can be evaluated without accepting the candidate ontology first: primitives, update laws, equations or algorithms, assumptions, benchmark targets, and failure conditions.

**Proof burden** means the specific derivation, simulation, residual comparison, classification table, or negative control owed before the next threshold can be claimed.

## 1. Object Formation

**Current state:** required before serious intervention.

**Source inputs:** primitive variables, update laws, conserved quantities, candidate ontology terms, simulation assumptions, benchmark targets, and claim-level labels.

**Target outputs:** conventional review language, equations or algorithms, reproducible examples, failure modes, and a translation key for skeptical experts.

**Acceptance conditions:**

- state the primitive ingredients and laws without relying on diagrams alone;
- identify which inherited results are preserved as benchmarks;
- state which assumptions are challenged and which observations remain untouched;
- supply at least one computation, bridge equation, simulation target, or residual table;
- name what would falsify the candidate at the current stage.

**Failure mode:** if the candidate cannot be turned into variables, equations, algorithms, residuals, or failure conditions, it remains a signal or provocation, not a transition object.

## 2. Benchmark Recovery

**Current state:** required for benchmark pressure.

**Source inputs:** candidate dynamics, Noether sea response, retained path-history machinery, classification hypotheses, source-to-effective maps, and simulation outputs.

**Target outputs:** recovery or domain-labeling of GR, QM/QFT, Standard Model phenomenology, thermodynamics, conservation laws, precision benchmarks, Lambda-CDM-era observational constraints, and direct detector records.

**Acceptance conditions:**

- recover known successes without hidden parameter drift;
- separate exact recovery, effective approximation, computation tool, and narrative replacement;
- show negative controls where the mechanism is absent or altered;
- preserve detector-facing records and uncertainty discipline;
- publish benchmark packets that independent skeptics can rerun or attack.

**Failure mode:** if each benchmark family needs a separate rescue mechanism, the program has not shown a deeper implementation basis.

## 3. Classification Discipline

**Current state:** high-value work package, not evidence by itself.

**Source inputs:** particle-family candidates, reaction channels, detector signatures, conserved quantities, symmetry exposures, stability rules, and missing or failed entries.

**Target outputs:** table-grade classification atlases that help researchers find failures as well as apparent regularities.

**Acceptance conditions:**

- each row names primitive ingredients, conserved quantities, reaction permissions, known exceptions, and falsification points;
- the table compresses known families rather than redrawing the inherited catalogue;
- missing rows and failed rows are recorded;
- chemistry and materials-science style classification pressure is allowed to challenge physics-only narrative defense;
- classification claims remain subordinate to benchmark recovery.

**Failure mode:** a visually persuasive atlas without table discipline creates confidence without obligations.

## 4. Governance And Funding Escalation

**Current state:** conditional on evidence threshold.

**Source inputs:** portfolio maps, assumption-risk registers, technical-decision records, grant disclosures, facility bills of materials, and written review dispositions.

**Target outputs:** preserve/convert/stress-test/pause/sunset/investigate classifications for grants, facilities, departments, publication tracks, and workforce programs.

**Acceptance conditions:**

- every disruptive recommendation names its evidence threshold;
- inherited assets are preserved before narrative authority is removed;
- students and junior researchers receive teach-out and migration protection;
- review authority is reassigned only after requalification standards are declared;
- suspected misconduct is separated from honest historical error.

**Failure mode:** aggressive action detached from evidence maturity becomes institutional overreach; institutional inaction after threshold movement becomes governance failure.

## 5. Workforce Requalification

**Current state:** required before major transition action.

**Source inputs:** current expertise, benchmark stewardship needs, translation needs, validation capacity, teaching roles, student pipelines, and program value.

**Target outputs:** retain/retrain/redeploy/sunset/separate decisions with due process.

**Acceptance conditions:**

- legacy experts can renew authority through baseline stewardship, translation, validation, teaching, or applied rebuild work;
- students are not admitted into programs whose future has become structurally doubtful;
- senior authority becomes non-veto unless requalified against the new implementation basis;
- narrative revision is treated as a legitimate scholarly contribution;
- personnel actions follow ordinary legal and contractual constraints.

**Failure mode:** a transition that humiliates useful experts or traps students will create denial, sabotage, and bad review incentives; a transition that leaves unrequalified authorities in control will not correct the false-prior risk.

## 6. Capital And Application Gating

**Current state:** downstream of benchmark pressure and safety review.

**Source inputs:** facility bills of materials, collider data value, detector expertise, application mechanisms, safety cases, radiation accounting, medium-state accounting, and public-interest governance.

**Target outputs:** preserve/convert/pause/sunset/shut-down decisions for capital infrastructure, plus staged application roadmaps.

**Acceptance conditions:**

- capital facilities state their benchmark, recovery, or falsification value under the candidate implementation;
- application work remains subordinate to mechanism, safety, radiation, and governance requirements;
- corporate and defense capital cannot define scientific acceptance;
- public-private work keeps open benchmark packets and conflict-of-interest controls;
- far-future application horizons are labeled as conditional, not near-term promises.

**Failure mode:** moving money from obsolete narratives into uncontrolled application hype repeats the same governance error in a new form.

## Stop Conditions

The transition should slow, pause, or reverse if any of the following happens:

- the candidate cannot form a reviewable object;
- benchmark recoveries depend on unrelated ad hoc mechanisms;
- independent replication fails without a bounded explanation;
- classification tables hide missing or failed rows;
- application claims outrun mechanism, safety, and benchmark evidence;
- institutional action proceeds without threshold labels, due process, or student protection.
