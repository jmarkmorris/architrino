# Research Revolution Review Companions

These companion packets adapt [Research Revolution](../../priorities/future-physics-cosmology/research-revolution.md) for targeted review. They are review surfaces, not replacements for the source memo, and they do not certify any foundation-level transition.

| Packet | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [Executive And NSF Review Packet](executive-nsf-review-packet.md) | A compact decision brief for NSF-scale leaders, funders, institutional executives, and transition planners. |
| [External Reviewer Packet](external-reviewer-packet.md) | A review packet for skeptical technical readers who need to evaluate the transition claim without accepting the ontology first. |
| [Technical Proof-Burden Map](technical-proof-burden-map.md) | A structured map connecting evidence thresholds, proof obligations, governance actions, and stop conditions. |

The core distinction used across these packets is simple: an **evidence threshold** controls what kind of institutional action is justified, and a **reviewable object** is the minimum technical packet that lets a skeptical expert judge primitives, update laws, equations or algorithms, assumptions, benchmark targets, and failure conditions.

Each packet should preserve the same status boundary: the research revolution is priority-only planning material until open equations, reproducible benchmark packets, skeptical review, negative controls, public evidence packets, and safety governance move the work across stated thresholds.

## Architrino Corporation Role

Architrino Corporation belongs at the end of each packet as the company-side implementation layer. Its role is consulting, specification, education, software, specialized AI support, application design, due diligence, workforce training, and transition planning. It does not certify scientific acceptance, and its services should always be labeled by claim level: exploratory, candidate, benchmark-pressure, transition-evidence, or institutional-transition.
