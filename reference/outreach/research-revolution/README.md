# Research Revolution Review Set

Research Revolution needs separate review materials for decision makers, skeptical technical readers, and proof-burden planners. The shared task is to judge a possible foundation-level transition without treating conditional planning as validation.

| Document | Use |
| --- | --- |
| [Science And Technology Leaders Review](science-technology-leaders-review.md) | A compact decision brief for science and technology leaders, funders, institutional executives, and transition planners. |
| [External Reviewer Brief](external-reviewer-brief.md) | A review brief for skeptical technical readers who need to evaluate the transition claim without accepting the ontology first. |
| [Technical Proof-Burden Map](technical-proof-burden-map.md) | A structured map connecting evidence thresholds, proof obligations, governance actions, and stop conditions. |

The core distinction is simple: an **evidence threshold** controls what kind of institutional action is justified, and a **reviewable object** is the minimum technical object that lets a skeptical expert judge primitives, update laws, equations or algorithms, assumptions, benchmark targets, and failure conditions.

Each document should preserve the same status boundary: the research revolution is priority-only planning material until open equations, reproducible benchmark artifacts, skeptical review, negative controls, public evidence records, and safety governance move the work across stated thresholds.

## Architrino Corporation Role

Architrino Corporation belongs at the end of each document as the company-side implementation layer. Its role is consulting, specification, education, software, specialized AI support, application design, due diligence, workforce training, and transition planning. It does not certify scientific acceptance, and its services should always be labeled by claim level: exploratory, candidate, benchmark-pressure, transition-evidence, or institutional-transition.
