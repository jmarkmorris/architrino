# Single-Panel Comic Prototypes

These prototypes test one-panel $\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}$ outreach comics with bubble-first lettering, visible public humor, and hidden academic humor. They are script and art-direction seeds, not final images.

Each panel should keep the main cast composed and credible. The satire lives in background artifacts, academic rituals, captions, version numbers, and institutional language.

## Prototype Format

Each prototype records:

- `Surface joke`: the joke a general reader should get immediately.
- `Hidden academic joke`: the quieter joke for physicists, mathematicians, graduate students, open-source contributors, or frequent journal readers.
- `$\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}$ point`: the $\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}$ idea the comic should preserve.
- `Claim level`: established explanation, working hypothesis, proof target, open problem, app/tutorial instruction, or playful analogy.
- `Bubble text`: the actual speech or thought bubble text.
- `Background text`: small readable labels, notes, or artifacts that carry hidden humor.

## 1. Future Work, Obviously

| Field | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Series | `Future Work, Obviously` |
| Main cast | Rachel Stone |
| Surface joke | A claim tries to hide in the phrase `future work`, and Rachel refuses to let it leave the room. |
| Hidden academic joke | `Future work` is shown as a literal escape hatch under a seminar podium. |
| $\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}\mathbb{A}$ point | Open proof burdens should be named directly rather than polished into vague confidence. |
| Claim level | Proof target. |

### Panel

Rachel stands beside a whiteboard headed `Claim Level`. A large arrow points from `established` toward `future work`; Rachel has stopped it halfway at `proof target`. A trapdoor in the floor is labeled `future work`, with a stack of papers trying to slide into it.

### Bubble Text

- Rachel speech bubble, sharp angled projection: `Nice try. You are not "future work" until you tell us what would prove you.`
- Paper stack thought bubble, dot trail: `Maybe if I cite three reviews...`

### Background Text

- Whiteboard rows: `established`, `working hypothesis`, `proof target`, `open problem`.
- Small note on podium: `Seminar policy: unresolved claims may not use emergency exits.`
- Paper title on stack: `A Brief Note On Deferred Closure, v67`.

- Small caption box: `Effective summaries are useful after the ledger is paid.`
- Preprint note in background: `Instantaneous Intuition, revised under delay pressure, v67`.
