## Overall Finding

Exact Lorentz-shaped contraction and period dilation remain **unlikely** for the stated delayed acceleration kernel, and the case is sharp in both directions.

**The negative side hardened into an exact result.** The programme's implicit hope is that the translating branch is the rest branch acted on by a contraction along the drift composed with a uniform slowing of absolute time. That map fails to carry causal-root pairs to causal-root pairs (Finding 1). More decisively, **no real map of that class exists at all**: requiring any map of the repaired form

$$
T\mapsto\alpha T+\lambda x_\parallel,
\qquad
x_\parallel\mapsto\frac{x_\parallel}{\gamma_f}+\beta T,
\qquad
\mathbf x_\perp\mapsto\mathbf x_\perp
$$

to preserve the root relation for every pair forces $c_f^2\lambda^2=\gamma_f^{-2}-1=-u^2/c_f^2<0$, which has no real solution for $u\neq0$ (Finding 2). Once transverse lengths are left alone, an active longitudinal contraction by $1/\gamma_f$ cannot be completed into any real root-preserving linear map. No symmetry argument can generate the desired family; if it exists, it exists by dynamical accident and must be found by solving the delay equations.

*Plainly: there is no way to repair the shrink-and-slow recipe by adding a position-dependent time shift. The arithmetic returns an imaginary number. Whatever the moving orbit is, it is not the rest orbit in disguise.*

**Two things the programme did not previously have are now in hand.** The transmitter weight $c_f/|D_t|$ — its first power *and* its absolute value — is **derived**, not postulated, from a constant positive emission measure through the root-counting identity (Finding 9). And fold births are **not** a well-posedness catastrophe: the acceleration singularity at a generic transverse fold is integrable in absolute time, the impulse across a shrinking window vanishes, and velocity stays continuous (Finding 10).

**One hoped-for cheap falsifier is closed.** The first-order period coefficient $a_1$ is forced to vanish for the exactly circular planar binary, by ordinary spatial symmetries of that orbit together with the kernel's invariance under global polarity reversal (Finding 4). There is no linear-in-drift period shift to look for. The first-order *shape* correction $\boldsymbol\rho_1$ need not vanish, however, and it feeds three additional terms into the second-order solvability equation (Finding 5).

**One foundation is missing outright.** Euclidean and time-translation covariance do **not** by themselves supply conserved energy or momentum; Noether's theorem needs an action or an independent balance identity, and none has been given for a strictly delayed, receiver-local acceleration law. So $E_{\mathrm{wake}}$ and $\mathbf P_{\mathrm{wake}}$ cannot presently be written down at all, and the mass-shell programme with a drift-independent $M_0$ is an unresolved target rather than available bookkeeping (Finding 15).

*Plainly: the law looks the same if you move or rotate the whole experiment, but that alone does not mean anything is conserved. Until someone writes down what the wake carries, the energy story has nothing underneath it.*

**What to do next, in order.** The second-order programme is legitimate only after three certificates close: a finite fixed root inventory, a strictly positive Jacobian floor $\inf|D_t|>0$ on the rest ledger, and a solution of the full first-order shape equation. Only then does the corrected second-order adjoint solvability condition mean anything. Two of those three are cheap and can be attempted immediately; none requires a long forward evolution.

Balance of judgment: exact closure is unlikely; approximate closure over a restricted fold-free low-drift chart remains **undecidable at present**; and no exact no-go has been proved against the two scalar observables agreeing by accident. What would change the judgment is a converged, corrected $O(b^2)$ adjoint calculation returning $a_2=\tfrac12$ and $g_2=-\tfrac12$ with a vanishing phase-resolved vector residual and no fitted parameters.

## Finding 1: The Contract-And-Slow Map Does Not Preserve The Causal-Root Relation

Take any rest-branch emission and reception pair with separation $\Delta\mathbf x_0=x_\parallel\hat{\mathbf e}+\mathbf x_\perp$ and absolute-time gap $\Delta t_0>0$ obeying $\|\Delta\mathbf x_0\|=c_f\Delta t_0$. Under the active map

$$
\mathbf X_u(T)=uT\hat{\mathbf e}+A_u\mathbf X_0\!\left(\frac{T}{\gamma_f}\right),
\qquad
A_u=\operatorname{diag}\!\left(\gamma_f^{-1},1,1\right),
$$

the image pair has $\Delta T=\gamma_f\Delta t_0$ and $\Delta\mathbf X=u\gamma_f\Delta t_0\hat{\mathbf e}+(x_\parallel/\gamma_f)\hat{\mathbf e}+\mathbf x_\perp$. Using $\gamma_f^2(c_f^2-u^2)=c_f^2$ and eliminating $c_f^2\Delta t_0^2=x_\parallel^2+\|\mathbf x_\perp\|^2$,

$$
\|\Delta\mathbf X\|^2-c_f^2\Delta T^2
=2u\Delta t_0\,x_\parallel-\frac{u^2}{c_f^2}x_\parallel^2
=\frac{u\,x_\parallel}{c_f^{2}}\left(2c_f^2\Delta t_0-u\,x_\parallel\right).
$$

Since $c_f\Delta t_0=\|\Delta\mathbf x_0\|\ge|x_\parallel|$ and $u<c_f$, the bracket is strictly positive, so the defect vanishes **if and only if** $x_\parallel=0$.

*Plainly: the recipe maps wake hits correctly only when the emitter and receiver were side by side. Every other hit lands off the wake surface, so it is not a hit at all in the moving configuration.*

The quadratic terms do not cancel: an expression of the form $2u\Delta t_0x_\parallel$ alone is incomplete. Numerical instantiation at $c_f=1$, $u=0.6$, $\gamma_f=1.25$, $x_\parallel=1$, $\mathbf x_\perp=\mathbf 0$, $\Delta t_0=1$:

| quantity | value |
| --- | --- |
| $\Delta T$ | $1.25$ |
| $\Delta X$ | $0.75+0.8=1.55$ |
| $\Delta X^2-\Delta T^2$ | $2.4025-1.5625=0.84$ |
| corrected form $2ux_\parallel\Delta t_0-u^2x_\parallel^2$ | $1.2-0.36=0.84$ |
| incomplete linear-only form $2ux_\parallel\Delta t_0$ | $1.2$ |

Verified additionally against direct evaluation over $2\times10^5$ randomly sampled causal pairs, maximum discrepancy $2\times10^{-11}$.

> Claim grade: derived, with independent symbolic and numerical checks. Falsifier: any pair with $x_\parallel\neq0$ and $u\in(0,c_f)$ for which direct evaluation disagrees with the closed form.

## Finding 2: Exact No-Go — No Real Repaired Map Of That Class Exists

The natural repair is to add the position-dependent time term the active map lacks. Consider the general real linear class

$$
T'=\alpha T+\lambda x_\parallel,
\qquad
x_\parallel'=\frac{x_\parallel}{\gamma_f}+\beta T,
\qquad
\mathbf x_\perp'=\mathbf x_\perp,
$$

and require it to send the root quadratic $Q=x_\parallel^2+\|\mathbf x_\perp\|^2-c_f^2T^2$ to a multiple $kQ$. The transverse coefficient is untouched, so $k=1$, and coefficient matching gives exactly three conditions:

$$
\frac{1}{\gamma_f^2}-c_f^2\lambda^2=1,
\qquad
\beta^2-c_f^2\alpha^2=-c_f^2,
\qquad
\frac{\beta}{\gamma_f}-c_f^2\alpha\lambda=0 .
$$

*Plainly: demand that the repaired recipe turn every wake surface into another wake surface, and matching the terms leaves exactly three algebraic conditions on the four coefficients.*

The first alone is fatal. Since $\gamma_f>1$ for $u\neq0$,

$$
c_f^2\lambda^2=\frac{1}{\gamma_f^2}-1=-\frac{u^2}{c_f^2}<0,
$$

so $\lambda^2=-u^2/c_f^4$ and no real $\lambda$ exists. Confirmed symbolically; at $c_f=1$, $u=0.6$ the required $\lambda^2$ evaluates to $-0.36$.

*Plainly: the equations that would make the repaired recipe work demand the square of a real number be negative. Holding transverse lengths fixed while shrinking longitudinal ones by the Lorentz factor is simply the wrong sign of deformation to be completed into a root-preserving map.*

> Claim grade: derived exact no-go for this class of maps. Falsifier: exhibit a real solution of all three coefficient equations with $u\neq0$, or show that the class is too narrow and name a wider one — for instance one that also rescales transverse lengths, at the cost of contradicting the assumed $L_\perp$ invariance.

The consequence for the programme is structural. Any translating solution, if one exists, cannot be obtained by transporting the rest solution. It must be found by solving the delayed root equations afresh, and the two target ratios have no shared origin unless the dynamics supplies one.

## Finding 3: What A Genuine Root-Preserving Map Would Demand Of The Kernel

This finding is an **external algebraic comparison**, not a substrate symmetry claim, and is recorded only to exhibit the structure a closing kernel would need. A real linear map preserving the null quadratic uses a longitudinal coefficient $\gamma_v$, not $1/\gamma_v$. Writing $k_r=1-vV_{r\parallel}/c_f^2$, $A_n=1-v\hat r_\parallel/c_f$, and $k_t=1-vV_{t\parallel}/c_f^2$, direct differentiation gives

$$
\frac{dT_r'}{dT_r}=\gamma_vk_r,
\qquad
A_{r\parallel}'=\frac{A_{r\parallel}}{\gamma_v^3k_r^3},
\qquad
\mathbf A_{r\perp}'
=\frac{k_r\mathbf A_{r\perp}+\dfrac{v}{c_f^2}\mathbf V_{r\perp}A_{r\parallel}}{\gamma_v^2k_r^3},
$$

and, using invariance of the null form and differentiating in transformed emission time,

$$
D_t'=\frac{D_t}{\gamma_v^2A_nk_t},
\qquad\text{hence}\qquad
\frac{c_f}{|D_t'|}=\gamma_v^2\left|A_nk_t\right|\frac{c_f}{|D_t|} .
$$

All four identities were verified symbolically here.

*Plainly: even under a map that does move wake surfaces onto wake surfaces, the transformed acceleration depends on how fast the receiver is moving and mixes the along-motion component into the sideways one, while the weight picks up angle and emitter-velocity factors. The two sets of factors are different.*

A closing kernel would therefore need an equivariant tensorial structure reproducing receiver-velocity dependence and longitudinal–transverse mixing simultaneously. A scalar radial kernel depending only on transmitter data cannot do that universally.

> Claim grade: derived as external comparison only. It is *not* evidence that the substrate should have these factors, and it must not be cited as a requirement on the substrate law. Falsifier: demonstrate that the postulated vector kernel satisfies the displayed transformed-acceleration identities for arbitrary simple-root histories.

## Finding 4: The First-Order Period Coefficient Is Forced To Vanish

Let $\hat{\mathbf L}$ be the orbital-axis direction and decompose the drift as $\mathbf u=\mathbf u_\perp+u_\parallel\hat{\mathbf L}$. Two spatial operations act on the exactly circular planar rest branch:

- **Rotation by $\pi$ about $\hat{\mathbf L}$.** This carries each constituent to its partner's position; combined with **global polarity reversal** it returns the original configuration. The kernel is invariant under global polarity reversal because both $\sigma_{tr}=\operatorname{sign}(q_tq_r)$ and $|q_tq_r|$ are unchanged when every charge flips sign. The drift transforms as $(\mathbf u_\perp,u_\parallel)\mapsto(-\mathbf u_\perp,u_\parallel)$.
- **Reflection in the orbital plane.** The trajectory lies in that plane and is unchanged, and so is its sense of circulation; the orbital axis, being a pseudovector built from two in-plane vectors, is also unchanged. The drift transforms as $(\mathbf u_\perp,u_\parallel)\mapsto(\mathbf u_\perp,-u_\parallel)$. This operation is available **because the kernel contains no cross product and no pseudovector anywhere**, so it is invariant under the full orthogonal group and not merely under rotations.

Composing them sends $\mathbf u\mapsto-\mathbf u$ while preserving the rest branch up to phase. If the nearby translating branch is unique modulo phase and smooth in $\mathbf u$, then $T(\mathbf u)=T(-\mathbf u)$, so

$$
T(\mathbf u)=T_0+T^{(2)}_{ij}u_iu_j+O(\|\mathbf u\|^4),
\qquad
a_1=0\ \text{for every drift orientation}.
$$

*Plainly: reversing the drift can be achieved by ordinary spatial operations that leave this particular orbit alone. A single number like the period therefore cannot tell forward drift from backward drift at first order.*

The candidate pseudoscalar $\mathbf u\cdot\hat{\mathbf L}$, which would otherwise permit a linear period shift, is odd under the orbital-plane reflection and is therefore forbidden. The hoped-for cheapest falsifier — a linear-in-drift period response — **does not exist for this orbit**.

> Claim grade: derived, conditional on exact circular planar geometry, polarity antisymmetry, smoothness in $\mathbf u$, and local uniqueness of the translating branch modulo phase and center. Falsifier: two inequivalent translating branches exchanged by the symmetry, a nonplanar or chiral rest record, or a retained-history rule that is not spatially equivariant would each permit a branch-specific linear term.

The two orbital-shape families to watch are exactly those that break a hypothesis: a rest branch with any out-of-plane structure, and any retained-history truncation applied in a way that distinguishes drift directions.

## Finding 5: The Second-Order Solvability Problem Carries First-Order Shape Feedback

Write $b=u/c_f$ and pose the periodic-branch equation in orbital phase, with $\boldsymbol\rho$ the relative coordinate and $\Omega$ the phase rate:

$$
\mathcal F(\boldsymbol\rho,\Omega,b)(\theta)
\equiv
\Omega^2\boldsymbol\rho''(\theta)-\mathcal A[\boldsymbol\rho,\Omega,b]\!(\theta)=0,
$$

with $\mathcal A$ the total delayed acceleration contribution assembled from the causal-root ledger, each root solving the root condition and carrying weight $c_f/|D_t|$. Let $L$ be the linearization about the rest branch, including the variation of every root through implicit differentiation.

*Plainly: write the moving orbit as the rest orbit plus a small correction, substitute into the delayed law, and collect terms by powers of the drift.*

Finding 4 gives $a_1=0$, so the first-order problem reduces to a pure shape equation with gauge conditions:

$$
L\boldsymbol\rho_1+\mathcal F_b=0 .
$$

**$\boldsymbol\rho_1$ need not vanish**, so the second-order equation is

$$
L\boldsymbol\rho_2-a_2\Omega_0\mathcal F_\Omega
=-\tfrac12\mathcal F_{bb}
-\mathcal F_{\rho b}\boldsymbol\rho_1
-\tfrac12\mathcal F_{\rho\rho}[\boldsymbol\rho_1,\boldsymbol\rho_1],
$$

and the adjoint projection, for $\psi\in\ker L^\ast$, becomes

$$
a_2=\frac{\bigl\langle\psi,\ \tfrac12\mathcal F_{bb}+\mathcal F_{\rho b}\boldsymbol\rho_1+\tfrac12\mathcal F_{\rho\rho}[\boldsymbol\rho_1,\boldsymbol\rho_1]\bigr\rangle}
{\bigl\langle\psi,\ \Omega_0\mathcal F_\Omega\bigr\rangle},
$$

provided the denominator is nonzero.

*Plainly: the period has no term linear in the drift, but the orbit's shape can lean linearly, and that lean feeds back into the period at second order. A shortcut that sets the lean to zero changes the answer.*

> Claim grade: derived formal reduction, conditional on Finding 6's chart hypotheses. Falsifier: an evaluated ratio differing from $\tfrac12$ outside numerical and regulator uncertainty refutes exact period dilation at second order — but only once $\boldsymbol\rho_1$ has actually been solved for, since omitting it moves the answer.

The practical consequence is an ordering constraint: **the first-order vector problem must be solved before the second-order coefficient means anything.** It is a prerequisite, not an optional refinement.

## Finding 6: Fredholm Legitimacy Holds Conditionally, And Only On A Fold-Free Chart

The solvability reduction of Finding 5 requires the linearized delayed operator to be Fredholm, which holds under explicit hypotheses and fails at a fold. Take $X=C^2_{\mathrm{per}}([0,2\pi];\mathbb R^m)$ after center and phase gauges are fixed, and $Y=C^0_{\mathrm{per}}$. Assume:

1. only finitely many roots occur in the declared retained-history interval;
2. every root is simple;
3. a uniform Jacobian floor $|D_{t,q}(\theta)|\ge d_J>0$ holds;
4. root labels retain fixed cardinality across the neighbourhood;
5. the orbit and kernel stay bounded away from collisions, $\inf_{q,\theta}r_q(\theta)>0$.

*Plainly: under those hypotheses, the delay terms are controlled additions to the periodic second-derivative operator. That makes the adjoint solvability test legitimate on this chart, but says nothing at a fold where the root labels cease to vary smoothly.*

The implicit function theorem then makes each delay functional locally smooth, and the operator takes the form

$$
L=\Omega_0^2\frac{d^2}{d\theta^2}-\mathcal K_0-\sum_q\mathcal K_q\mathcal E_q,
$$

with $\mathcal E_q$ evaluating perturbations at shifted phases including the induced root displacement. The principal periodic second-derivative operator is Fredholm; the delay and evaluation terms are bounded $X\to Y$ and relatively compact through the compact embedding $C^2_{\mathrm{per}}\hookrightarrow C^1_{\mathrm{per}}$. So $L$ is Fredholm, of index zero after gauge fixing.

*Plainly: away from a fold, each old wake can be tracked as its own smooth labelled channel, so the equation behaves locally like an ordinary problem with a fixed finite list of delays.*

For a simple root the drift sensitivity is

$$
\delta\tau_q=\frac{\delta G_q}{D_{t,q}},
\qquad
\partial_b\tau_q\big|_{b=0}=\frac{\tau_q\,\hat{\mathbf e}\cdot\hat{\mathbf r}_q}{J_q},
$$

which is singular as $J_q\to0$ — the same limit in which hypothesis 3 fails.

> Claim grade: derived under the five explicit hypotheses. **At a fold the reduction is not merely harder, it is unavailable**: root maps behave as square roots rather than differentiable functions, so $\mathcal F_b$, $\mathcal F_{bb}$, and the Fréchet derivative may fail to exist. Falsifier: if the rest orbit itself has $\inf|D_t|=0$, the ordinary second-order programme cannot start at all — which is why measuring $d_J$ is the first certificate.

Four replacements are available at a fold, none free: a fold-resolved coordinate with the control parameter quadratic in the root offset; piecewise-smooth continuation on each fixed-ledger chart; a weak integral formulation; or a regulator with a demonstrated removal limit.

## Finding 7: The Existence Question Is An Unknown-Versus-Condition Count

The autonomous delayed system is invariant under time translation, so the phase mode lies in $\ker L$; on a circular branch that mode coincides with the rotational one. If the rest benchmark belongs to a continuous family — for instance one indexed by orbital radius — each additional neutral direction contributes a further element of $\ker L^\ast$ and therefore a further solvability condition.

At second order the free constants are $a_2$ together with whatever parameters of the neutral family drift with $u$, such as a radius response. The translating branch exists at that order only if the number of independent solvability conditions does not exceed the number of free constants. **The same count must be run at first order**, where Finding 5 now requires $\boldsymbol\rho_1$ to be solved rather than assumed away.

*Plainly: count the equations the drift forces on you, then count the dials the rest orbit gives you. More equations than dials means there is no nearby moving orbit, and no simulation is needed to find that out.*

> Claim grade: plausible inference, resting on Finding 6's hypotheses and on an unmeasured $\dim\ker L^\ast$. Falsifier: compute $\dim\ker L^\ast$ on the accepted rest ledger; a value exceeding the free-constant count is a local nonexistence result, and a value equal to it converts the existence question into a solvable linear problem.

## Finding 8: A Denominator Power Alone Cannot Restore Covariance

For the scalar-weight family $\mathcal K_{n,p}=\hat{\mathbf r}r^{-2}(1-\hat{\mathbf r}\cdot\boldsymbol\beta_t)^{-n}(1-b^2)^p$ with $\mu=\hat{\mathbf r}\cdot\hat{\mathbf e}$, the expansion and the symmetric forward/backward average a closed orbit samples are

$$
(1-b\mu)^{-n}=1+n\mu b+\frac{n(n+1)}{2}\mu^2b^2+O(b^3),
$$

$$
\frac12\left[(1-b\mu)^{-n}+(1+b\mu)^{-n}\right]\!(1-b^2)^p
=1+\left[\frac{n(n+1)}{2}\mu^2-p\right]b^2+O(b^4).
$$

Verified numerically at several $(n,p,\mu)$. The postulated first-power kernel gives coefficient $\mu^2$; a third power gives $6\mu^2$.

*Plainly: the exponent on the denominator sets one angular weight and the numerator exponent sets one direction-independent weight. That is two dials.*

The acceleration still points only along the delayed radial line, and Finding 3 shows what a closing structure would additionally require: receiver-velocity dependence and longitudinal–transverse mixing, neither producible by scalar reweighting of radial contributions.

> Claim grade: the two-dial count and the expansion coefficients are derived. That the defect's independent harmonic content exceeds two dials is a plausible inference, unverified without the rest ledger. Whether an orbit-specific $n$ closes second order for this binary specifically is unresolved. Falsifier: project the second-order residual onto the radial $m=0$, radial $m=2$, and tangential $m=2$ harmonics; a single $(n,p)$ zeroing all independent projections at every phase — not merely after orbital averaging — would leave the scalar family alive at that order.

## Finding 9: The Transmitter Weight Is Derivable, But Only Under One Interpretation

Let the transmitter emit wake labels with constant positive measure $\nu\,dT_t$. At a fixed reception event, with $g(T_t)$ the root function,

$$
\frac{dg}{dT_t}=c_f-\hat{\mathbf r}_t\cdot\mathbf V_t=D_t
$$

— verified symbolically here — so representing the density of wake surfaces at the event as a delta distribution gives

$$
\mathcal N(T_r,\mathbf X_r)=\nu c_f\int dT_t\,\delta\bigl(g(T_t)\bigr)
=\nu\sum_{T_t\in\mathcal C}\frac{c_f}{|D_t|}.
$$

*Plainly: equal amounts are emitted in equal stretches of emission time. When many neighbouring emission times land on nearly the same wake surface, those surfaces pile up. Counting positive surfaces necessarily produces the absolute value of the Jacobian.*

This **derives** three previously postulated features: the first denominator power, the absolute value, and the sum over all roots. It does *not* derive the radial inverse-square amplitude, its sign, or the claim that receiver motion leaves per-event strength alone.

The interpretive burden is now precise. The **temporal arrival rate at a moving receiver** is a different quantity:

$$
\frac{dN}{dT_r}=\nu\sum_q\left|\frac{D_r}{D_t}\right|,
\qquad
D_r=c_f-\hat{\mathbf r}_t\cdot\mathbf V_r(T_r),
$$

which reduces to $c_f/|D_t|$ only for a stationary receiver. So the postulated law must be read as **event-local wake-surface density**, not as arrivals per unit receiver time. That reading is exactly what makes the packet's structural fact — receiver velocity does not enter the strength of a hit — consistent rather than arbitrary.

*Plainly: counting how many surfaces are sitting on you right now gives the emitter factor alone. Counting how fast you sweep through them also depends on how fast you are moving. The law uses the first count, and that choice should be stated rather than assumed.*

Finally, the absolute value is now justified rather than suspect: a signed $1/D_t$ would be the Jacobian of an *oriented flux*, a different microscopic coupling rule under which fold-born pairs cancel instead of adding. The earlier concern that the absolute value might be a transcription artifact is resolved — it is the correct choice for a positive count.

> Claim grade: derived, conditional on constant positive emission measure and event-local shell-density coupling. Falsifier: a declared microscopic rule in which each crossing supplies a fixed acceleration increment per unit receiver time would require $|D_r/D_t|$ and would contradict the stated law for moving receivers. Choosing a different emission measure, or a signed oriented flux, also breaks the derivation.

## Finding 10: Fold Singularities Are Integrable In Time

Near a generic fold crossed transversely at $T=T_\ast$, write $G(\tau,T)=A(T-T_\ast)+\tfrac{B}{2}(\tau-\tau_\ast)^2+\cdots$ with $AB\neq0$. On the two-root side,

$$
\tau_\pm-\tau_\ast=\pm\sqrt{-\frac{2A}{B}(T-T_\ast)}+O(T-T_\ast),
\qquad
|D_{t,\pm}|\sim\sqrt{2|AB|}\;|T-T_\ast|^{1/2},
$$

so each contribution behaves as $|T-T_\ast|^{-1/2}$ and the impulse over a one-sided window of width $\varepsilon$ is

$$
\Delta\mathbf V_\pm=\int_{T_\ast}^{T_\ast+\varepsilon}\mathbf A_\pm(T)\,dT
=\frac{2\mathbf C_\pm}{\sqrt{2|AB|}}\sqrt{\varepsilon}+O(\varepsilon)
\;\xrightarrow[\varepsilon\to0]{}\;0 .
$$

Verified symbolically. Velocity stays continuous, though generally only Hölder-$\tfrac12$ differentiable there; position stays $C^1$.

*Plainly: the acceleration does blow up at the instant a pair of hits is born, but the area under it is finite and shrinks to nothing as you narrow the window. Nothing gets an infinite kick.*

Two consequences, in opposite directions. **For the branch-quantized response:** a regulator-free weak or Carathéodory-type reading is plausible for isolated transverse crossings, which partly rehabilitates that proposal. But the finite change in an observable comes from the *newborn branch persisting afterwards*, not from the singular instant, whose contribution vanishes. **Against it:** the argument does not cover tangential time crossings, repeated fold accumulation, simultaneous collision singularities, nonunique root-ledger continuation, or numerical schemes that sample the singular point directly. Parameter averages need separate analysis, since a quadratic tangency in phase can generate logarithms.

> Claim grade: derived local integrability for a generic transverse time crossing. Global well-posedness and uniqueness of the weak continuation remain unresolved. Falsifier: a local normal form with $A=0$, a higher degeneracy, or feedback producing a nonintegrable exponent at or above one defeats the conclusion; so does exhibiting regulator-dependent weak continuations.

## Finding 11: Delay Divergence Does Not Imply Period Divergence

A periodic delay equation may retain roots emitted many periods earlier, so a forward delay scaling as $(c_f-u)^{-1}$ does not imply a period scaling the same way. The missing companion is the weight: on a chasing forward root, $r\sim c_fd_{\min}/(c_f-u)$ and $D_t\sim c_f-u$, so

$$
\frac{1}{r^2}\cdot\frac{c_f}{|D_t|}
\sim\frac{(c_f-u)^2}{c_f^2d_{\min}^2}\cdot\frac{c_f}{c_f-u}
\sim\frac{c_f-u}{c_f\,d_{\min}^{2}} .
$$

*Plainly: the oldest forward hit becomes both arbitrarily delayed and arbitrarily weak. The orbit does not wait for it — it simply stops mattering.*

Finite memory changes the reading. If retained history is physical, the forward channel disappears at or below the critical drift. If it is a numerical truncation, disappearance there is a numerical failure, not physics. The corpus currently states the delay divergence and the memory obligation without this companion, which invites the stronger unsupported reading.

> Claim grade: plausible asymptotic inference; the exact scaling depends on orbital phase and transmitter velocity at the root. Falsifier: compute the forward-root contribution against $c_f-u$ on a converged ledger; failure of the predicted linear decay, especially growth from a simultaneous approach of $J_q$ to zero, invalidates the smooth-shedding picture.

## Finding 12: The High-Drift Regime Yields A Sharp Dichotomy

No universal axis-ratio asymptotic is derivable from rear and self roots alone: a retained forward root has large delay and weakening contribution, a rear-partner root can stay at order-one delay and strength, and self-root geometry depends on internal speed, curvature, phase, and branch index. No small parameter forces these into a universal ratio.

There is nevertheless a conditional positive signature. If a rear-and-self-supported branch has a nondegenerate limiting orbit with $0<L_{\parallel,\ast}<\infty$ and $0<L_{\perp,\ast}<\infty$, then

$$
\frac{L_\parallel}{L_\perp}\longrightarrow\xi_\ast>0,
\qquad\text{whereas}\qquad
\frac{1}{\gamma_f}=\sqrt{1-\frac{u^2}{c_f^2}}\longrightarrow0 .
$$

*Plainly: any moving orbit that keeps a finite width as the drift approaches the wake speed disagrees flatly with Lorentz contraction, which demands the width go to zero.*

> Claim grade: derived dichotomy under nondegeneracy; the actual high-drift branch is unresolved, and longitudinal collapse, loss of periodicity, or a fold cascade all remain open. Falsifier: a converged branch with $L_\parallel/L_\perp\sim C\sqrt{1-u^2/c_f^2}$ defeats the finite-ratio alternative.

This is the programme's most distinctive *positive* falsifiable signature, and it is worth more than the mere absence of the Lorentz law.

## Finding 13: The Closed-Return Argument Needs A Named Root Itinerary

Built from actual causal roots rather than an assumed round trip, a two-hit return requires

$$
G_{-+}(\tau_{+-};\theta_1,u)=0,
\qquad
G_{+-}(\tau_{-+};\theta_1+\Omega_u\tau_{+-},u)=0,
$$

with phase closure $\Omega_u(\tau_{+-}+\tau_{-+})=2\pi k+\Delta\theta_{\mathrm{return}}$ for a declared integer itinerary, and weights $c_f/|D_{t,+-}|$ and $c_f/|D_{t,-+}|$ along it.

*Plainly: a real return cycle must name two actual wake hits, the phases at which they land, and how hard each kicks. Equal travel times alone close nothing.*

For fixed, non-orbiting, co-moving endpoints this reproduces the kinematic selection $g(b)=\sqrt{1-b^2}$. For the orbiting binary the deformed shape appears inside both root equations, so both delays depend on orbital phase and on the super-wake-speed internal motion, and no phase-independent scalar equation for the axial factor survives.

> Claim grade: derived ledger formulation; survival of the Lorentz axial factor for this binary is unresolved and not implied. Falsifier: evaluate the two-root return bracket on the Lorentz-deformed ellipse; residual phase dependence at second order after optimizing the period rejects the closed-return argument as an exact reduction of that itinerary.

## Finding 14: The Coframe Reciprocity Product Is Normalization-Dependent

Under independent rescaling $e^0\mapsto a e^0$, $e^\parallel\mapsto b e^\parallel$, $e^\perp\mapsto c e^\perp$, the reciprocity product $e^0(\partial_T)e^\parallel/e^\perp$ changes by $ab/c$, so it can be forced to one by rescaling a single leg.

*Plainly: as written the test can be passed by choosing units, which is exactly the hidden tuning it was invented to rule out.*

The repair is to declare six normalizations in ledger quantities only, frozen before any moving branch is evaluated: a clock normalization tying one named internal return event to the rest period; longitudinal and transverse ruler normalizations on named equal-time boundary events selected by a declared rule; axis fixing from the center record; rest calibration of all three legs to one standard; and no post hoc rescaling. With these the product becomes an observable comparison against a rest standard — but then it is no more fundamental than the clock and ruler ratios it is built from, so it is a packaging test rather than an independent circularity-proof theorem.

> Claim grade: derived gauge objection; proposed operational repair. Falsifier: two admissible ledger-based normalization rules giving different reciprocity values on the same branch confirm the objection; a proof that the declared construction fixes all three scales dissolves it.

## Finding 15: No Conservation Law Has Been Derived, So The Mass-Shell Programme Has No Foundation

Euclidean and time-translation covariance hold: the equations look the same after moving or rotating the entire history. But covariance alone does not supply a conserved ledger. Noether's theorem additionally requires an action, or an equivalent independently proved balance identity, and neither has been given.

An ordinary single-copy path action is obstructed by three features of the stated law: strict delay rather than time-symmetric pair coupling; a receiver-local acceleration with no reciprocal simultaneous reaction; and self-hits with root creation carrying an absolute Jacobian weight. A symmetric pair kernel varied over both paths generically generates reciprocal advanced *and* delayed contributions, which the law does not have.

*Plainly: a delayed law can be perfectly symmetric under moving the whole experiment while still handing energy and momentum to its own history with nothing tracking where they went.*

The consequence for the shell programme is severe. No explicit wake dynamics has been supplied, so $E_{\mathrm{wake}}$ and $\mathbf P_{\mathrm{wake}}$ **cannot presently be computed at all** — they can only be fitted after the moving branch is known, which is precisely the construction that carries no evidential weight. A drift-independent $M_0$ and a mass-shell relation are therefore unresolved targets, not available bookkeeping.

Two further points follow from the earlier analysis. Matching $\gamma$-shaped targets order by order forces

$$
E_{\mathrm{wake}}(u)-E_{\mathrm{wake}}(0)
=\left(\frac{M_0}{2}-\mu_{\mathrm{arch}}\right)u^2+\frac{3M_0}{8c_f^2}u^4+\cdots-\bigl[K_{\mathrm{int}}(u)-K_{\mathrm{int}}(0)\bigr],
$$

$$
\mathbf P_{\mathrm{wake}}(u)=\left(M_0-2\mu_{\mathrm{arch}}\right)u\,\hat{\mathbf e}+\frac{M_0}{2c_f^2}u^3\,\hat{\mathbf e}+\cdots,
$$

and the shell relation itself is one scalar identity implied by the pair of $\gamma$-shaped readouts but not implying them — any reparameterization applied to both preserves the shell while destroying both target shapes, as verified numerically. A shell residual is a necessary check, never a sufficient one.

> Claim grade: derived failure of the Noether inference and derived weakness of the shell relative to the pair. Absolute nonexistence of any enlarged conserved ledger is unresolved. Falsifier: supply an action, or a direct history-space balance theorem, whose variation reproduces exactly the all-root delayed acceleration including self-hits and the transmitter weight, and whose boundary terms yield explicit conserved ledgers.

## Finding 16: The Explicit No-Fold Bound, And Why Its Constants Resist Measurement

Let $d_J=\min_{q,\theta}|D_{t,q}(0,\theta)|$ on the complete rest ledger, with dimensions of speed. Suppose throughout a candidate neighbourhood $0\le|u|\le u_0$ that

$$
\bigl|D_{t,q}(u,\theta)-D_{t,q}(0,\theta)\bigr|\le M|u|+Cu^2
$$

for every retained root and phase, with $M$ dimensionless and $C$ of dimension inverse speed. Then a sufficient no-fold condition is $d_J-M|u|-Cu^2>0$, that is

$$
|u|<u_{\mathrm{safe}}\equiv\min\left[u_0,\ \frac{\sqrt{M^2+4Cd_J}-M}{2C}\right]
\qquad(C>0),
\qquad
|u|<\min\left(u_0,\ \frac{d_J}{M}\right)
\qquad(C=0,\ M>0).
$$

Here $M\ge0$ and $C\ge0$ because they are bound constants. In the degenerate case $M=C=0$, the estimate is independent of drift and gives $u_{\mathrm{safe}}=u_0$. The root formula was verified symbolically. This supersedes the earlier self-referential form.

*Plainly: measure how far the resting orbit's hits are from the edge of being born or dying, then subtract the largest certified change a drift can cause. What is left must stay positive.*

Evaluating it requires measuring, on the complete rest ledger: $d_J$; the first drift sensitivity $M$; a second-order remainder bound $C$ covering changes in root time, line-of-action direction, transmitter velocity, orbit shape, and period; and a chart radius $u_0$ over which root labels stay complete and collisions are excluded.

> Claim grade: derived sufficient bound, not a necessary fold criterion. **Important caveat:** because $D_t$ depends on the unknown first-order orbit correction, $M$ cannot generally be obtained from frozen rest geometry alone — it must be bounded jointly with the first-order continuation problem of Finding 5. Falsifier: a root fold inside the certified interval demonstrates that $M$, $C$, the root inventory, or their numerical bounds were wrong.

Because the rest benchmark has super-wake-speed internal motion, there are already phases at which the internal projection approaches the wake speed with no drift at all, so $d_J$ could be small or zero. **No positive fold-free drift interval is currently certified.**

## Finding 17: The No-Retune Witness Is Frozen-Source Held-Out Parallelism

Curvature, loop holonomy, and the cocycle law are automatically trivial on the one-dimensional drift base, which has no noncontractible loops, so a drift-only holonomy reading cannot certify that a connection was built without tuning to its desired outputs. The current source of truth is frozen-source held-out parallelism: a connection constructed only from declared source-side retained data must predict an independently extracted section over a drift block that did not shape the construction.

*Plainly: transporting something back and forth along a single line always returns it unchanged, so that check proves nothing. The real test is to freeze the rule, then predict values it never saw.*

> Claim grade: derived. This conclusion is reached independently under two review lenses applied to the same statement; per the evidence-independence rule that is corroboration of a reading, not independent evidence about the substrate. Falsifier: an enlarged parameter base — drift crossed with relative phase, or with a second retained physical modulus — on which a genuine curvature or loop-holonomy test becomes nontrivial would restore a role for the loop form on that larger base.
